🦝 Give Me Five! 1 (нулевой уровень) · Unit 1 “Ready for school!” · Lesson 3 — Story “Toby goes to school”
Pre-A1 young learners (~6–7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · Story: look → predict → listen → sequence → act out (drama) + Phonics · carpet + desks
Week 02.11–08.11 · Lesson 18 of the year📖 STORY~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.15 · диграфы ng·oo (b) закрепление · there·once
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have looked at, predicted, listened to, put in order and acted out the story “Toby goes to school” — following the picture-story and re-using the classroom language Where's my (pencil case)? — It's here!, The (book) is in the bin., Put the (pencil case) on the desk.
  • Subsidiary. Review classroom objects + Where's my…? — It's here! (L1–L2, уроки 16–17) woven into the story; prepositions in · on · under in context; colours black · white · purple; phonics — digraphs ng · oo (recognise → blend → read short words) + tricky words there · once; listening for detail (sequence story pictures).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; one clear hinge-check after the story before moving on; run a stirrer→settler rhythm with songs, signals and drama.
By the end, pupils can…
  • follow & retell the gist of “Toby goes to school”;
  • put the 5 story pictures in order by listening;
  • act out story lines with a partner/team;
  • say where a thing is: It's in / on / under the…;
  • blend & read short ng / oo words + read the tricky words there · once.
Target language
Story
“Toby goes to school” (Pupil's Book p.10–11, CD1 15) — Toby the dog rides the magic bike into class and makes a mess; the children ask where things are and tidy up. (В КТП с-нуля историю назвали «Classroom chaos» — по учебнику это одна история юнита «Toby goes to school»; берём фактический сюжет книги.)
Lexis
bookpencil casecrayonbagbinshelfdesk · colours blackwhitepurple
Structure
Where's my (pencil case)? — It's here!  ·  The (book) is in/on/under the (bin).  ·  Put the (book) in the (bag). / on the (desk).
Form
Where + 's (is) + my + noun? → It + 's + prep + the + noun. Prepositions in · on · under + the.
Pron. watch
connected Where's /weəz/; final /n/ in bin vs. /f/ in shelf; long /uː/ in school & short /ʊ/ in book · Look · good (both oo).
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.15: диграфы ng · oo (тетрадь стр.39–42) — закрепление (b): read the two-letter sounds → blend into short words (ring · king · song · long · moon · zoo · boot · book · look · good) → read them «fast». Tricky (sight) words: there · once — «read as one».
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is Toby a boy or a dog?” → a dog. “Do dogs go to school?” → no! (that's the joke)
“Where is the book — in the bin or on the bin?” → in the bin.
Hinge check (after the story): “Is the classroom tidy or messy at the end?” → tidy → “Well done, children!” (all show thumbs at once — if half get it wrong, replay frames 6–8.)

Assumptions: objects & Where's my…? — It's here! are known from L1–L2 (уроки 16–17); colours & greetings known from the Genki intro block + Starter. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → they follow the pictures + audio, not decode the text; “read” = point & join in. Carpet & drama routines are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Lost in the story — too many picturesPoint to each frame; pause the audio; ask a one-word CCQ per frame.
Confuse in / on / underOne fixed gesture each (in = tuck hands, on = flat on top, under = duck down); use them every time.
Act-out = chaos on the carpetModel roles first; assign 4 roles per team; Freeze! to reset.
Only 2 children “act”, rest watchWhole team says the chorus line together; rotate the Toby role every scene.
Sequence cards: teams rush & misorderPlay the audio in chunks; pause after each scene so they lay one card at a time, left→right.
Ask & Grab: everyone lunges at onceOne runner per team, the rest chant the question & point; rotate the runner each round.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset (used a lot in drama).

Retrieval — objects + “Where's my…?” (L1–L2, уроки 16–17, now feeding the story). Spread the 6 story-object A4 cards face-up on the carpet (book · pencil case · crayon · bag · pencil · ruler).

ICQ: Do you run or walk? — What do you shout? (→ run · “It's here!”). These are the very things Toby loses in a minute — hook the story.

10–18′
Phonics Hero — digraphs ng · oo (ур.15 b)
board → desks

Speed reading. Hub «⚡ Speed reading» / sound strip: Read with me — fast! Point to the two-letter friends ng and oo: ng… like a bell — riiing! oo… like a ghost — oooo! (whole class, choral, there & back). Then blend on the board: r-i-ng → ring! m-oo-n → moon! l-oo-k → look! — sound it, then say it whole.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: flash short-word cards — a real ng/oo word (ring · king · song · moon · zoo · boot · book) → stand up tall; a nonsense word (zong · voo · nук) → crouch small. 8–10 cards, fast — every child moves every time.

Tricky (sight) words. Flash there · once: Don't sound it — read it! “there”… “once”. Chorus, air-write. Tie-in to today's story: Once there was a sad dog… and THERE is the magic bike! — the story words, now in print.

🎮 Phonics Hub (projector): speakandsmile.ru/gmf1/phonics/ — «Диграфы ng · oo». A word/short line appears — class reads it together; a rotating child taps the picture it matches. ✅ Offline: word cards on the board ledge.

Write — тетрадь стр.39–42 (desks). Read & match the ng/oo words to pictures; trace & read the tricky words there · once. Colour the star «Я молодец!». Finishing in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… pencils down.

20–23′
Story ① Look ② Predict
carpet, books p.10–11

① Look — pictures only, NO sound. Books open at p.10–11, cover the text with your hand. 🦝 Speaky Look at the pictures — no sound yet. What can you see? Give 20–30 sec of silent looking; children just explore the frames.

② Predict — ask about the pictures, children describe & guess. Fire quick picture questions; pupils point, name, predict (English + gestures welcome): Who is Toby? (a dog!) · Is Toby happy or sad? · What is Toby riding? · What classroom objects can you see? (a book · a pencil case · a crayon…) · Oh no — is the classroom tidy or messy? · What will Toby do? Accept all guesses — we check them by listening. EAL frame on the board for answers: I can see a … / Toby is … .

ICQ: Now — eyes on the pictures, ears open. Listen!

 Pupil's Book p.10–11 · Speaky · «I can see a…» frame card
23–27′
Story ③ First listen
carpet

③ First listen (CD1 15) — whole story. Play it through; you point to each frame so all follow. Children just listen & watch — and do the in/on/under gestures whenever they hear them (on = flat hand · in = tuck · under = duck). Frames: Toby is sad — “Poor Toby. Dogs don't go to school.” → Toby is on the magic bike → “Look! He's in the classroom now.” → “Stop, Toby! Oh no! My pencil case!” → “Where's my black and white book? Where's my purple pencil case?” → the book is in the bin, the pencil case is on the shelf — “Bad dog, Toby! Tidy up.” → “Put the pencil case on the desk. Put the book in the school bag. Good dog!” → “What a tidy desk. Well done, children!”

Hinge check — the guesses (all at once). Was Toby happy or sad at the start? Is the classroom tidy at the end? Thumbs up / down — quick, everyone shows; replay frames 6–8 if the room splits.

27–33′
▶ Story ④ Sequence by ear
carpet, teams

④ Put the shuffled story in order — by listening (teams). Give each team the 5 story-frame cards SHUFFLED, face-up on the carpet (Toby is sad · rides the magic bike · comes to school · makes a mess · tidies up). ▶ Sequence race: play CD1 15 again in chunks — pause after each scene. As they hear a scene, the team finds that card and lays it next in line, left→right (1→5). Roles inside the team: a “card-mover”, a “checker”, and everyone points to the card they think is next before it's placed — all engaged.

Group check (all at once). Show me card 1! … card 5! Teams hold up each card in turn — you scan for a match; replay any scene that split the room.

41–48′
▶ Story ⑦ Phrases + Act it out
carpet, teams

⑦ Work the story phrases, then act (I do → we do → you do). I do: you + 🦝 Speaky model two lines — Speaky (Toby) knocks a book down — Oh no! Where's my book? — you find it — It's in the bin! Put it in the bag! Big gestures, big voices. We do: whole class choruses the key lines with the gestures using the frame The (book) is (in) the (bin). — Put it (on) the (desk)! then Bad dog, Toby! — Good dog!

▶ Act out (teams of 4): You do — roles: Toby the dog (barks + rides), Beth, a child, the teacher. Give each team 3 real objects (book · pencil case · crayon). They act the mess-and-tidy using the phrases; the whole team says Bad dog, Toby! Tidy up! together. Rotate the Toby role so everyone acts. 1–2 teams perform; everyone claps: Well done, children! Reset with Freeze! if loud.

 3 real objects per team · Speaky · role cards (Toby/Beth/child/teacher)
48–54′
▶ Ask & Grab · in/on/under
carpet, teams

Where things are — race for it. Spread 6 A4 «object-in-place» cards on the carpet: book in the bin · pencil case on the shelf · crayon under the desk · book in the bag · pencil case on the desk · crayon in the bag. Teams stand in lines, one runner each.

▶ Ask & Grab: the whole team chants the question to messy Toby — Where's the book? 🦝 teases: Hmm… is it on the shelf? No!… It's IN THE BIN! On “yes”, runners race to grab the matching card; the grabber shouts the whole sentence — The book is in the bin! — to keep it. Non-runners point & echo the sentence — everyone plays every round. Rotate the runner; 6–7 rounds.

ICQ: Who asks the question? — Everyone! One word or a whole sentence to keep the card? (→ whole sentence!).
