📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, every learner has performed for a real audience (parents/guest group): «My year» talk + the season's stations; parents see the progress with their own eyes.
  • Subsidiary. MCs run the programme in English; the acts follow the plan laid out in P8; after the guests leave — a team season debrief and a bridge to the Final block (test strategy without fear).
  • Personal. My day is a producer's day: timing, backing up the MCs, rescuing anyone who stumbles (in interview mode), photos/video for absent parents. Certificates are NOT today — at the very last meeting (Final P3): the intrigue stays.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«What if a parent doesn't understand English?» → Show them with hands/an exhibit + the translation line at the bottom of the invitation; the main thing is the parent SEES: their child is speaking.
«Forgot the words in front of Mum — is that a disaster?» → No: a pause, keywords, carry on. Parents see courage, not mistakes.
«Are certificates today?» → No — at the last meeting: today is the show, that's the ceremony.

Assumptions: Invitations have been handed over; the plan of acts and roles was drilled in P8. If there are few/no guests (force majeure) — an invited neighbouring group/admin becomes the audience; the show goes on regardless, everything is filmed for parents.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Guests are late/arrive in wavesAct 1 starts on the timer +5 min maximum; the registrar quietly hands late guests a programme; talks run in blocks — entry happens between blocks.
A child freezes in front of their parentsI come and stand beside them, interview mode: I turn their own keywords into questions; the room sees a dialogue, not a failure.
Parents speak Russian to the children at the stationsThat's fine for the parents; the children answer in English + with a show — the guest cheat sheet in the programme helps.
Timing creepsThe timekeeper has 5/1-min cards for the MC; acts are trimmed to plan: the fair is cut first (it's the most flexible).
Technology fails (the video won't play)The technician tests it BEFORE the doors open; the fallback — the showreel from a phone in the MC's hands, or skipping the act: the live stations matter more than the recording.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
