Week 10.05–16.05 · Lesson 68 (L4)🎵 Song focus · We want to get some honey · Ask the (ladybird), please~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH finale · tricky-words marathon · Genki: Where Is The Spider?(74) · game: Boat Race(46)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act the story song «We want to get some honey» (CD4 T23) with full actions, and ask politely: “Where are the bees? — I don't know. Ask the ladybird, please.”
  • Subsidiary. Re-tell the story; take song roles (Mimi, Dylan, the bugs, Bruno); play We want to get some honey — act it out & the dynamic Boat Race (46); sing Genki «Where Is The Spider?» (74); follow the maze & colour the bees (PB p.94); Phonics Hero finale — tricky-words marathon (all parts).
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions, then words. Repeat the song several times without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (giant voice, whisper, super-fast). Give everyone a role or an action job so all are moving. After the big acting, a calm reset before the table.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing «We want to get some honey» with the actions;
  • act a bug or a character in the song;
  • ask “Where are the bees? Ask the ladybird, please.”

Form
Repeated request pattern in a musical frame: Ask the + bug + please. Recycled: Where are the bees? I don't know.
Pron. watch
sing on the beat; polite falling Ask the ladybird, please; delighted Yummy, yummy, yummy!
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — FINALE. A tricky-words marathon: the whole tricky-words wall (all parts — the, was, said, you, they…) read as whole words, fast, as a happy race. The words that can't be sounded out — and the children just KNOW them now.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“In the song, who has the honey at the end?” → Bruno & the bees!
“Do we say Give me honey! or Ask the ladybird, please?” → please — we ask politely!

Assumptions: The story is met in L3 — today deepens it through the song. Children know the bugs & the polite-request pattern. Reading finishes the tricky words. Pre-readers follow the song by pictures & actions, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by repetition of the songChange the mode each time: giant voice, whisper, super-fast, slow-motion.
Only a few singGive everyone an action job (flap / wiggle / slide); sing the actions even without the words.
Not everyone gets a roleRotate roles fast; children without a bug card do the Where are the bees? chorus & actions.
Over-excited after actingBreathing reset — breathe in… breathe out… four times before the table.
Forgets pleasePuppet always adds a big polite …please! with a bow; children copy.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

24–31′
Game · Boat Race (46) ⚡
carpet, teams in lines

▶ Boat Race Two teams in lines; bug mini-cards spread in front of each line. Speaky calls a line: Ask the… snail! the first child reads/says the bug, mimes it, runs to the back — next child continues. First team through the bugs wins a cheer. Everyone moves.

 bug mini-cards (a set per line) · Speaky
31–40′
Perform · act the story song «We want to get some honey»
carpet, standing

Act & sing. Assign roles: Mimi, Dylan, worm, ladybird, snail, butterfly, Bruno. Give out the puppet & bug cards. The actors sing at the front; everyone joins the chorus We want to get some honey. Where are the bees? and the actions.

Round 1 — actions only (flap, wiggle, slide, rub tummy on Yummy!); Round 2 — add the words; Round 3 — new actors, giant voice. Everyone moves throughout.
