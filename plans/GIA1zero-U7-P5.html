🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): No racing and no competition today. The test is quiet, the review is kind. The main goal of this pair: everyone leaves with a list of “I can already”, not a feeling of failure. We close the verb wall for the duration of the test.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Don't open yet.” “Nobody has to finish first. You have to finish CALM.”

0–6′
Opening · a calm entry
desks · calm

WHAT WE DO: Inside-Outside Circle for the review: quiet music; a mini-review of 5 items they'll definitely solve; a reminder “we did all of this”

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Quiet music before the bell. At the door: Hi! One word — how do you feel? Fingers 1–5.
  2. I build Kagan Inside-Outside Circle: two circles facing each other, a question → both answer → Outside circle, rotate two steps! — a new partner, a new question; 3 rotations, everybody speaks.
  3. A silent mini-review on a draft, 5 lightning items: 1 Laika ___ a street dog. 2 Gladiators ___ (−) boring. 3 watch → ? 4 go → ? 5 12.03 in words.
  4. The key on the screen, everyone checks themselves. Five out of five? Look — the test is exactly this.
  5. I cover the verb wall with a sheet: The wall has a rest. But it's in your head now.
📺 On screen: quiet music · a slide of 5 items → key · drafts · a sheet for the wall (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they come in calmly, solve, check themselves. Expected: 4–5 out of 5 — the anxiety drops before your eyes.

6–12′
The strategy step by step
desks

WHAT WE DO: we go through one example from every part together; the strategy card stays on the desk

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I hand out the tests cover-up: Don't open yet.
  2. We go through one example from every part — the class answers in chorus, I show where to write.
  3. The strategy card (stays on the desk for the whole test): 1 Read the example. 2 Don't know? Skip it, dot in the margin, come back. 3 Finished? ONE sweep: is every verb in the past?
  4. Nobody has to finish first. You have to finish CALM.
📺 On screen: tests · slide of examples · strategy cards on every desk (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they say the strategy aloud, put the card in front of themselves. Expected: a calm mindset. Typical error: they open early — I repeat the rule calmly.

12–27′
Test · parts 1–2
desks · solo

WHAT WE DO: parts 1–2 in writing, in silence: jobs, dates, feelings; was/were, questions, -ed, irregulars

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Timings on the board: Part 1 — 7 min, Part 2 — 8 min; I warn 2 minutes before the end of each.
  2. Silence; a question — with a raised hand, I answer in a whisper at the desk.
  3. Content: P1 — 6 jobs by definition + 4 dates in words + 4 feelings in context; P2 — 6 was/were(n't) gaps · 4 was/were questions + a short answer · 4 regulars with spelling · 4 irregulars.
📺 On screen: a visible timer · timings on the board · strategy card
👦 Students do: they work by the strategy, put dots in the margin and come back. Expected: dates and jobs go confidently. Typical error: they freeze on an irregular verb — a dot in the margin, they move on.

27–35′
Test · part 3 Listening
desks

WHAT WE DO: we read the task aloud TOGETHER before the audio; two passes + a third on the disputed ones

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. We read the task aloud all together — this is part of the support, not a hint.
  2. Read ALL the sentences first. Not sure? Question mark — fix it on the second listen.
  3. Track (or I read the script in the TB p.135 format, slowly, in three voices) — two passes with a pause.
  4. A third pass only on the items where hands went up.
  5. Pens down. Last part.
📺 On screen: audio or a script in the TB p.135 format · a grid in the test (Backup: no track — I read the script aloud at listening speed.)
👦 Students do: they read the task in advance, listen two or three times. Expected: 5–6 out of 8 — a good result for this line.

35–47′
Test · part 4 Writing + handing in
desks

WHAT WE DO: a description of a fictional character using a support on paper; we hand it in, a standing warm-up

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I announce: A description of a character — three paragraphs. The frames are ON the paper, like in class.
  2. The supports are printed on the paper: §1 My favourite character is … §2 He/She was born in … §3 I like him/her because …
  3. I remind them: Paragraph two — the past. And put ‘also’ somewhere in the right place.
  4. For finishers — a quiet bonus sheet (match 8 verbs with their forms + one date in words).
  5. I collect them calmly, 2 minutes of a standing warm-up.
📺 On screen: test p.4 with supports · bonus sheets
👦 Students do: they write using the supports on paper. Expected: 5–6 sentences from everyone — the supports in the test do their job. Typical error: they forget also — the criterion on the paper reminds them.
paper p.4 · bonus sheets
47–57′
Self-checking against the key
desks · solo

WHAT WE DO: we check OUR OWN work with a coloured pen against the key on the screen; we count ticks, not crosses

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I hand back the papers (except the writing part — I take that home).
  2. The key by parts on the screen; rule: Green tick for every correct answer. Count the TICKS, not the crosses.
  3. I ask them to say the correct answer to themselves as they put the tick: Marking is learning round two.
  4. I go round and help anyone confused by the key.
  5. Everyone writes at the bottom: My best part was …
📺 On screen: key slides by part · coloured pens (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they check their own work, count correct answers, name their best part. Expected: “my best part is dates/jobs” for almost everyone. Typical error: they get upset over the irregular verbs — “that was the hardest part, for everyone”.
key slides · pens
57–70′
Fix-it clinic · fixing it together
desks · teams

WHAT WE DO: teams get cards with typical errors (anonymous, from the papers) and fix them together; I go round and help

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ⚡ Stand up for 60 seconds: shake out your hands, three deep breaths — the test is behind you.
  2. I gather the most common errors into 8 cards, rephrase them — no names: Laika were a street dog. · Yes, she wasn't. · I goed to the cinema. · She studyed medicine. · We stoped at a café. · Where you were yesterday? · She's my favourite character also. · She taked photos.
  3. Clinic rule: Every card gets a FULL correct sentence — write it out. Help each other. No timer, no points.
  4. I open the verb wall back up: The wall is back. Use it.
  5. Teams take turns reading out their corrections; the class confirms with a thumbs up.
  6. These eight cards — that's the whole unit's mistakes. Eight. You know the rest.
📺 On screen: 8 error cards per team · verb wall open · markers
👦 Students do: they fix the cards as a team with no timer, read out the corrections. Expected: a calm, confident “oh, I get it”. Typical error: they fix just the word — we ask for the WHOLE sentence.

WHAT WE DO: Timed RoundRobin: an answer to WDYT in one phrase, applause, homework (SEL pp. 80–81), a Unit 8 teaser

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Back to WDYT round the circle: What does it mean to be famous? One sentence each — or one word if you like.
  2. This is Kagan Timed RoundRobin: strictly round the circle against the timer, one turn — one person, no skipping: Clockwise, one each — keep it moving, no skips!

  4. Applause for the whole group; we hang the best comics on the classroom wall.
  5. Homework: WB SEL pp. 80–81 (a calm spread about emotions) + “my 2 goals for Unit 8”. For the brave — p. 88 Exam trainer, Writing Part 1.
  6. Teaser: Unit 8 — travel. And the OTHER half of the past: how to say ‘I DIDN'T go’ and ask ‘DID you go?’

Unit 7 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: a job from its definition (I work in a hospital → doctor; I have ideas and make new things → inventor) — mirrors SB p.84 ex.2 and WB p.58 Vocabulary review. B) 4 items: dates in words (27.05 → the twenty-seventh of May; 01.10.2015 → the first of October, twenty fifteen). C) 4 items: feelings in context (I drink water when I'm ___ / Students are often ___ before a test) — SB p.89 ex.3.
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 6 gaps: was/were/wasn't/weren't in a mini-text about a celebrity of the past (format SB p.88 ex.3 “The Beatles”). B) 4 items: make a question with was/were from the given words + give a true short answer (half Wh-, half Yes/No) — SB p.88 ex.5–6. C) 4 items: past simple of regular verbs with spelling traps (study · stop · plan · play). D) 4 items: past simple of irregular verbs (go · take · write · meet) — list SB pp.126–127.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A news item in the style of SB p.89 ex.6–7 (three teens about a week without a phone; the teacher reads it twice if there’s no track): 3 items — match the speaker to the opinion (good / bad / mixed) + 5 items — match phrases a–e to speakers 1–3.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A description of a fictional character (35+ words, 3 paragraphs) — mirrors SB p.92 Skills boost. Marked for: past simple forms, regular and irregular (4) · position of also (2) · three-paragraph structure (2) · task complete + overall clarity (2).

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.93 — every format was met in class during the unit. The two lines: 🚀 higher (+ Wh-questions in writing, more spelling traps) · 🐣 standard.

They forget the strategy under stressThe strategy card lies ON the desk for the whole test, not just said aloud once.
They freeze in the listeningWe read the task aloud together BEFORE the audio; two passes + a third on the disputed items.
Self-checking hurtsWe check OUR OWN work, not someone else's; a coloured pen; rule: “a tick for every correct one, not a cross for every error”.
An energy crash after the testThe review isn't a points quiz but a “clinic”: teams fix error cards together, I go round and help.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
