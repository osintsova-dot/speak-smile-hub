Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, read and act out the story «A surprise party» and correct false statements in the past: “No, he didn't. He arrived at three o'clock. / No, she wasn't. She was surprised.”
  • Subsidiary. Break Luke's code again («Have a great CELEBRATION!») — recycles the alphabet code; Values — doing nice things for others; story skills: predict → listen → read along → Read & correct → act out; notice clock times (“ten to four”) to prepare telling-the-time in L4.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, prediction; the times (twenty-five to four…) are NOTICED today, taught properly in L4. Give every child a story role.
By the end, pupils can…
  • retell 2–3 things Josh, Ravi and Dad did for Lily;
  • read their role's lines from the book;
  • correct false statements: “No, he didn't. He arrived at three o'clock.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“It's a SURPRISE party — does Lily know about it before, or is it a secret?” → a secret!
“Josh fell down and made a mess — was that on purpose or an accident?” → an accident!

Assumptions: Irregular past & 12 attractions are active from L1–L2; the alphabet code (A=4) was learnt in the Starter. Readers follow the text with a finger. Act-out routine is familiar from earlier units.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Code pre-reading too hardSolve the first two letters together (12=C, 20=E); give the A=4 key card — it's a warm hook, not a test.
Read & correct — pupils just say “false”Model the full correction: No, he didn't. He arrived at THREE o'clock.; sentence frame stays up.
Times in the story confuseJust point & read the clock in each frame (“ten to four”) — noticing only; the system comes in L4.
Acting groups off-taskRoles printed (Josh · Ravi · Dad · Lily); strongest reader narrates; rotate once only.
Story audio too fast for someSecond listening with pauses per frame; readers follow the text with a finger.

Read & correct · comprehension
carpet → desks

Read and correct (act. 3, Talk Partners). Statements: Ravi arrived at two o'clock… true or false? Pairs correct the false ones fully: No, he didn't. He arrived at three o'clock. 5 statements (Josh decorated the cake ✗ / painted a picture ✗ / Dad made a pizza ✗ / Lily was angry ✗) — every pair speaks.

Who did it? (Showdown). Mini-whiteboards: you read a clue — I painted a message. Who am I? — all write the name (Josh), Show me! 4 rounds.

✔ Assess: the boards are your check — who follows the story? The rest sit with you in the board game.

💪 Support: point to the right frame in the book. ⭐ Extension: add a second true fact about that character.

28–40′
Act out
carpet, groups of 4

Model a frame. You + 3 pupils play the “What a mess!” frame with big drama: Are you OK, Josh? — Yes, I am. But look at our clothes and look at the cake!

Groups of 4 (Josh · Ravi · Dad · Lily). Rehearse 2 frames with books as scripts. Rotate roles once.

Showtime. Each group performs ONE frame (30–40 sec — fast carousel, no long waits). Audience task: shout Happy Birthday, Lily! at the end.

💪 Support: shortest lines / the “Happy Birthday!” chorus for the shyest child. ⭐ Extension: add an extra line from the frame.
