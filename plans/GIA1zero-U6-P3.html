WHAT WE DO: input through the P2 text (TB p.87 Warmer): why isn't Sherlock at home → is travelling → the formula → ex.1 table → notebook

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

Discover 2 · -ing spelling with gestures
board · desks

WHAT WE DO: ex.3 three rules = three gestures; teams sort 12 verbs; choral /ɪŋ/ drill

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Gesture 1 “scissors” (drop the -e): make → MAKING · write → WRITING · dance → DANCING. Chorus + gesture.
  2. Gesture 2 “double fist” (cons.+vowel+cons. → double it): sit → SITTING · run → RUNNING · swim → SWIMMING.
  3. Gesture 3 “plus” (just +ing): go → GOING · read → READING · play → PLAYING.
  4. Ex.3: teams sort 12 verbs into three columns, 4 min. Key: a going, walking, playing, reading — rule 3 · b making, riding, dancing, writing — rule 1 · c chatting, running, shopping, swimming — rule 2. Reinforcement (TB p.87): I write the second verb of each column on the board in the -ing form — as a model.
  5. /ɪŋ/ drill (TB p.87): pinch your nose — sitting · running · writing ×3.
📺 On screen: board: 3 columns with gestures (stay up) · SB p.76 ex.3
👦 Students do: learn the rules with their bodies and sort the verbs as a team. Expected: the gestures stick instantly. Typical error: reading → “readding” — the “plus” gesture: two vowels, we don't double.

WB Unit 6 (pp. 44–51) · notebook · a phone photo of the board — allowed
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can make present continuous affirmative and negative sentences (I'm sitting · He isn't wearing a coat) with correct -ing spelling — a new structure introduced through a full cycle.
  • Subsidiary. Contrast with present simple (U4) — one pair of examples only, no theory; -ing spelling through 3 gestures; written reinforcement in portions; after 45′ — 11 clothes items + carry/put on/take off/wear + listening step by step (subskill: photo captions).
  • Personal. ⚠️ THE MAIN THING: give the new structure ROOM TO BREATHE. Present continuous is the course's second big verb system. Don't rush, choral drills, a cheat-sheet table in the notebook, a check after every step. Success today = clean sentences, not speed.
By the end, students can…
  • say the formula: be + verb-ing
  • spell -ing by the three rules
  • make negatives with isn't/aren't + -ing
  • name 11 clothes items and catch answers in a radio programme

Structure
I'm sitting on a chair. · She's wearing a red dress. · They aren't listening to music. · He isn't watching TV at the moment.
Form
am/is/are + verb-ing. Spelling: most verbs: + -ing → going · walking · playing · reading (rule 3) · verbs ending in -e: drop the -e + -ing → making · riding · dancing · writing (rule 1) · 1 consonant + 1 vowel + 1 consonant: double the last letter → chatting · running · shopping · swimming (rule 2). Time expressions: today · now · at the moment.
Pron. watch
-ing = nasal /ɪŋ/ (TB p.87: pinch your nose — the sound “gets stuck”): sitting · running. Clothes: coat /əʊ/ · skirt and shirt — /ɜː/, the r isn't pronounced.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Present continuous — about every day or about now?” → about NOW (now, today, at the moment).
“He wearing a jacket — is that enough?” → no, you need be: He IS wearing.
“sit → siting or sitting?” → sitting (consonant+vowel+consonant — we double).
“My trousers is blue?” → no: trousers ARE blue.

Assumptions: They've HEARD the form in Pairs 1–2 (in yellow on the board), but never built it. Present simple was done in U4 — we give the contrast carefully, with one pair of examples, so as not to mix them up. Clothes are partly familiar from life.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
