Structure
She's giving him some milk. · They aren't wearing old clothes. · I'm not sending the other pictures. · The students are using spray paint.
Form
be (am/is/are) + verb-ing; negative — be + not + -ing. Spelling: most verbs: + -ing → going · walking · playing · reading (rule 3) · verbs ending in -e: drop the -e + -ing → making · riding · dancing · writing (rule 1) · 1 consonant + 1 vowel + 1 consonant: double the last letter → chatting · running · shopping · swimming (rule 2). Time expressions: today · now · at the moment.
Pron. watch
-ing = nasal /ɪŋ/ (TB p.87: pinch your nose — the sound should “get stuck”): sitting · writing · running. Clothes: /əʊ/ in coat · /ɜː/ in skirt and shirt (don't pronounce the r).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“They siting — OK?” → No: sit → sitTING (1 consonant + 1 vowel + 1 consonant = doubling).
“He wearing a jacket — is that right?” → No, the be is lost: He IS wearing.
“Present continuous — is it about every day or about now?” → about now/today/at the moment.
“My trousers is blue?” → No — trousers ARE blue (always plural).

Assumptions: Spoken PC is fluent for them (GMF3–4), the written form is a real gap: the missing be and -ing spelling. Clothes are partly familiar, but the system (+ carry/put on/take off/wear and plural nouns) is new. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (-ing spelling / missing ‘be’ / question word order), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.
