Structure
She's giving him some milk. · They aren't wearing old clothes. · I'm not sending the other pictures. · The students are using spray paint.
Form
be (am/is/are) + verb-ing; отрицание — be + not + -ing. Спеллинг: most verbs: + -ing → going · walking · playing · reading (правило 3) · verbs ending in -e: убираем -e + -ing → making · riding · dancing · writing (правило 1) · 1 согласная + 1 гласная + 1 согласная: удваиваем последнюю → chatting · running · shopping · swimming (правило 2). Time expressions: today · now · at the moment.
Pron. watch
-ing = носовой /ɪŋ/ (TB p.87: зажать нос — звук должен «застрять»): sitting · writing · running. Clothes: /əʊ/ в coat · /ɜː/ в skirt и shirt (r не читаем).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“They siting — OK?” → No: sit → sitTING (1 согласная + 1 гласная + 1 согласная = удвоение).
“He wearing a jacket — правильно?” → No, потерян be: He IS wearing.
“Present continuous — про каждый день или про сейчас?” → про сейчас/сегодня/at the moment.
“My trousers is blue?” → No — trousers ARE blue (всегда множественное).

Assumptions: Устно PC у них беглый (GMF3–4), письменная форма — реальная дыра: пропавший be и -ing spelling. Clothes частично знакомы, но система (+ carry/put on/take off/wear и plural nouns) — новая. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (-ing spelling / missing ‘be’ / question word order), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
