📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. The official unit test: vocabulary (clothes & accessories + describing clothes), present continuous, present simple vs continuous, time expressions, listening, a short piece of writing — the Standard level (Higher only if a strong student wants it).
  • Subsidiary. The “easy first” strategy; a practice mini-run before the test (to take the fear out of the format); feedback the same day; the unit celebration and a gentle way into the Midterm.
  • Personal. The tone is gentle: marks stay private, the comparison is only with yourself (the P3/P5 mini-tests). After the feedback — the unit ceremony with a nomination for everyone.
By the end, students can…
  • start with easy items and mark hard ones
  • apply the unit language in test format
  • check against the key and sort own slips
  • see their own growth across the unit

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
