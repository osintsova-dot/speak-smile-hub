Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to follow & enjoy the story «Mimi's kite» (CD1 T41) — a windy day, a kite stuck in a tree, the gardener helps — and to ask & answer about place: “Where's Mummy? — Under the tree / On the bench!”
  • Subsidiary. Predict from the pictures; listen (CD1 T41) with the story cards; circle the weather (windy) & add the story sticker (swing); play Chocolate Monster «Look in the trees!»; act tiny bits; sing Genki «What's The Weather Like?» (10) & «Where Is It?» (82); Phonics Hero — sounds ue · er · ar, speed-read p.49–51.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, a gasp when the kite gets stuck. Don't test comprehension; celebrate joining in. The TB tip is about feelings — name how Mimi feels (worried → happy).
By the end, pupils can…
  • point to what happens in each story picture;
  • say the weather — “It's windy!”;
  • answer “Where's Mummy? — Under the tree / On the bench!”

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — sounds ue · er · ar (Jolly gesture) → blend (ar → star, er → her, ue → blue) → speed-read p.49–51. Park link: p-ar-k · flow-er!
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“The kite flies — is it windy or sunny?” → windy!
“Mummy sits — is she ON the bench or UNDER the bench?” → on the bench!

Assumptions: Park words & There's a / There are are active from L1–L2; reading continues in the Phonics Hero workbook. The story-time routine (listen · point · join in) is set from earlier units. windy · under · on are new but very actable.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Story audio a bit fastPlay once for fun, then again pausing on each picture; point as you go.
Children lose the threadUse the big story cards frame by frame; ask a picture question after each.
under / on get mixed upDo it with bodies: hands ON your head, hands UNDER the table; puppet models.
Everyone shouts the weather at onceShow four weather cards; children POINT to windy, then chorus once.
Wriggling during listeningGive a job: wave when you hear the KITE! — active listening.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Whoosh — it's a WINDY day! Feel the wind… and up goes the kite! Sway like wind, fly an invisible kite, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 18 — sounds ue · er · ar. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: ar → car / star, er → her / fern, ue → blue / glue — arm-sweep + a picture. Park link: p-ar-k · flow-er! ▶ Blend relay — Speaky shows a word; teams blend it and race to touch its picture.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.49–51 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read old · only together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · Story «Mimi's kite»
carpet

Predict. Show the story cards — What has Mimi got? What's the weather? What happens to the kite? Take 2–3 guesses (Russian OK). TB tip: ask how Mimi feels (worried → happy).

Listen 1 — for fun. Play the story (CD1 T41) with the cards. Pause on each picture: It's windy! There's the pond! Oh no — the kite is in the tree! Children point & join the words they can.

Listen 2 — spot the kite. Wave when you hear KITE! (wave for the kite) — active listening; then answer place questions: Where's Daddy? — Under the tree!

 story cards · «Mimi's kite» (CD1 T41)
26–33′
Game · Chocolate Monster (32) → Look in the trees! ⚡
carpet

▶ Look in the trees! Genki Chocolate Monster, park-skinned: put park cards on the board; a child picks one and asks Is it in the tree? Is it under the bench? Everyone looks — is the kite there? 🦝 Speaky reveals with a big “Ooh!”. Rotate fast so lots of children ask; the rest point & chorus.

Links straight to the story — the kite is hiding in the tree! Everyone points & guesses each round.

💪 Support: point & copy in the tree? ⭐ Extension: ask the full question with a place word.

 park cards on the board · kite card · Speaky
33–41′
Perform · act the story + Genki «What's The Weather Like?»
carpet, standing

Act it out. Play the story again; children mime: sway (windy), fly the kite, gasp (stuck!), reach up, cheer (rescued!). Pick 2 to be Mimi & Dylan and 1 tall child to be the gardener; the class narrates.

Weather song. Sing Genki «What's The Weather Like?» (10) — the windy weather of the story with big actions.

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Ask your partner “Where's Mummy?” — answer with under / on! — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I know the story! I can say “It's windy!” colour one star.

Wonderful storytellers today! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
