🎡 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Unit 6 “Meals” · Lesson 2 — Language · What do you have for breakfast?
Pre-primary / early years (~5–6 y.o., pre-readers) · 60 min · Genki recipe · Disco warm-up → phonics → curiosity input → Genki games → song → craft · TPR + puppet · Phonics Hero 2.0 (reading: recognise → blend → speed-read) · carpet + tables
Week 08.02–14.02 · Lesson 2✨ Focus · +fish · pizza · soup · spaghetti · What do you have for breakfast?~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 29 (/aʊ/ ou ow · /ɔɪ/ oi oy · p.79–82) · Genki: What Would You Like For Breakfast?(67)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name four MORE foods — fish · pizza · soup · spaghetti — and talk about meals: “What do you have for breakfast? — I have pancakes.”
  • Subsidiary. Sort food into breakfast / lunch / dinner; sing the language song «What do you have for breakfast?» (CD3 T5) with mimes; play Breakfast, lunch or dinner? & Genki Dinosaur Danger! (12) re-skinned; Phonics Hero — /aʊ/ ou ow · /ɔɪ/ oi oy (consolidation), speed-read p.79–82; listen & find the new foods, match to Alex & Lucy (PB p.38).
  • Personal. Model What do you have for breakfast? many times with the puppet before children say it. Slow, exaggerated drilling for tricky words (ssss-pa-ghetti). Keep speaking short & choral first, then paired — no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name four new foods (fish · pizza · soup · spaghetti);
  • ask & answer “What do you have for breakfast? — I have pancakes”;
  • say what I have for a meal.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions

“I ask What do you have for breakfast? — do you tell me a colour or a food?” → a food I eat!

Assumptions: The six foods from L1 are fresh (checked in the warm review) and re-used on the wheel. The four new foods are new; the question What do you have for breakfast? is understood receptively — producing it is today's stretch. Reading continues from L1.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuses breakfast / lunch / dinnerAnchor to the sun: breakfast = sun up, lunch = sun high, dinner = sun down; sort each food under the right sun.
Says only the food, not the sentencePuppet models the frame I have… PANCAKES; child echoes with the taste-mime.
New words hard to saySlow, silly drilling with the puppet — sssspa-ghetti! — mouth wide, then normal speed.
Same partner every timeMusic on = walk, music off = new partner (Mix-Pair).
Waiting = wrigglingEveryone holds a food card and answers chorally, then pairs — nobody just watches.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning! What do YOU have for breakfast? Mime your breakfast… your lunch… your dinner! Mime three meals fast, then freeze.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 29 · /aʊ/ & /ɔɪ/ (again) — sounds ou · ow  (/aʊ/)  ·  oi · oy  (/ɔɪ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: h-ou-se → house, n-ow → now, b-oi-l → boil, j-oy → joy — sweep the sounds together + a picture. ▶ Sound hop — children hop to the spelling Speaky calls (ou / ow / oi / oy), then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.79–82 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read they · eight together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–25′
Curiosity input · four new foods + meals
carpet

Guess the new ones! Slide out the four new cards one by one: What else do I eat? Guess! Reveal + taste + echo (fish · pizza · soup · spaghetti). 🦝 Speaky Which words are new?

Sort by meal. Three «sun» hoops — breakfast (sun up) · lunch (sun high) · dinner (sun down). Do you have soup for breakfast or dinner? Children place each food card under a meal.

Choral first. Point to the flashcards and model, then ask: What do you have for breakfast? — I have pancakes! I have toast! children answer chorally with you.

Game · Dinosaur Danger! (12) → «Cross if you have it»
carpet / floor space

▶ Cross if you have it Genki Dinosaur Danger!, re-skinned: children line up at one wall, each holding a food card. Speaky (the hungry monster) holds up a food & says I have pizza for dinner! — every child whose card matches makes the sentence and races to the safe wall; the monster tries to tag them. Swap cards, repeat — everyone crosses several times.

💪 Support: run & say the food. ⭐ Extension: say the whole I have pizza for dinner!
