Give Me Five! 4 · Unit 1 “Let's eat!” · Lesson 1 — Vocabulary
A1+ pre-teens (~10–11 y.o., confident readers & writers) · 60 min · PPP + team challenges + song · carpet/board + desks
Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 4✨ NEW UNIT~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4📖 Pupil's Book p. 8
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 baking ingredients and ask & answer about what's in the kitchen using “Is there any (flour)? — Yes, there is.” / “Are there any (nuts)? — No, there aren't.”
  • Subsidiary. Distinguish countable vs uncountable nouns (any + is / any + are); listen for detail in the song; spell tricky ingredient words; retrieve Level 3 food language (fluency).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk lean; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; hide every drill inside a team challenge (nobody just watches).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name & spell the 12 ingredients;
  • ask & answer “Is there any…? / Are there any…?” with correct short answers;
  • sort ingredients into countable / uncountable and explain the choice.

Structure
Is there any (flour) in the cupboard? — Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.  ·  Are there any (nuts) in the fridge? — Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
Form
any + uncountable (flour, sugar, oil…) → Is there…? / there is.   any + countable plural (nuts, biscuits, raisins, chocolate chips) → Are there…? / there are.
Pron. watch
biscuits /ˈbɪskɪts/ (silent middle) · chocolate /ˈtʃɒklət/ (2 syll.) · coconut /ˈkəʊkənʌt/ · connected “Is there any…” /ɪzðərˈeni/.
Phonics / spelling
Unit phonics i_e–ie (Spike likes rice, Mike, pies, tie) is taught in L5 — today = spelling awareness of the new words: double tt in butter; ai in raisins; tricky biscuits · chocolate · coconut.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Can I count nuts? One, two, three…” → yes → “Are there any nuts?”
“Can I count flour? One flour, two flours?” → no → “Is there any flour?”
“Where do we keep butter — cupboard or fridge?” → fridge.

Assumptions: pupils know some food words from Level 3 (Starter revised them) and can read/write short words. There is / there are is known; the new step is any + the countable/uncountable split. Teams: max 4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Are there any flour?” (wrong verb)Colour-code on the word map: uncountables one colour → Is; countables another → Are. CCQ “Can we count it?”
Short answer dropped (“Yes.” only)Insist on full form: Yes, there is. / No, there aren't. Model on the board strip.
biscuits / chocolate mis-speltSpelling Showdown: syllable-clap + point to the silent/tricky letters.
Relay gets loud & chaoticOne runner at a time; team must whisper the full sentence before the runner may go. “Freeze!” to reset.
Faster pupils finish firstChallenge card: “Add 2 ingredients we don't have — say them.”
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
Scoreboard — team points on the board; keeps challenges fair & fast.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pens down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset for relays.

TimeStage / whereProcedureMaterials
0–4′
Lead-in · retrieval
carpet, teams

Warm up. Welcome back! Today — a brand-new unit. Look… I'm making a cake! Hold up a mixing bowl / spoon.

▶ 60-second food dash (retrieval — Level 3): teams race to write every food word they already know on one mini-whiteboard. Go! Count aloud, award points. Nice — but which of these go INTO a cake? Circle them. → funnels straight into today's topic.

 mini-whiteboards + pens · bowl/spoon prop · scoreboard
4–10′
Meet the words · chant
board

Present (Pupil's Book p. 8, Act 2). Play the vocabulary chant (CD1 10): Listen, point and say. Pupils point to each picture and echo the word.

Tier & check. Sort the 12 into “I knew it” (salt, sugar, oil, jam) vs “brand-new” (coconut, raisins, chocolate chips, biscuits…). Drill the new four extra: choral ×3, then 2–3 pupils. Contrast the tricky pair: raisins (dried grapes) vs nuts.

 GMF4 PB p. 8 · CD1 10 chant · A4 flashcards ×12
10–20′
Presentation · 12 ingredients
carpet → board
📓 Interactive notebook — “What goes into a cake?” word map (A5). Glue the map on a clean page (it stays open all unit). Pupils point to each picture as it's presented, then colour countables one colour, uncountables another. Drill the question strip Before you shop, ask: Is there any…? / Are there any…? (~5 min).

Establish the target question. Point to a coloured word: Can we count it? No → Is there any flour?   Yes → Are there any nuts? Board strip with the two frames + four short answers.

▶ Slow-Reveal Race (teams, on the board): cover a flashcard, reveal it slice by slice. First team to call a full sentence — It's coconut! Are there any coconuts? — wins the point (a bare word = no point, next team). Kagan · Numbered Heads: only the called number may answer for the team → everyone stays ready.

 📓 word-map A5 ×pupils · scissors · glue · 2 coloured pencils · flashcards · cover card
20–28′
🎵 Song · Let's go shopping!
desks
📓 Interactive notebook — “Let's go shopping!” song takeaway (A5). Glue it opposite the word map: LOOK (cupboard: flour, oil, nuts, biscuits, chocolate chips, sugar, salt, raisins / fridge: butter, coconut, cream, jam) → ASK (Is there any…? Are there any…?) → answers → MAKE A LIST box (~4 min).

Listen & sing (PB p. 8, Act 3 · CD1 11). First listen: Where does each word live — cupboard or fridge? Second run: pupils point CUPBOARD / FRIDGE on their takeaway as they hear each word; join in the chorus Let's go shopping! Make a list!

Pupils tick what their own kitchen has and write three items in the MAKE A LIST box.

 📓 song-takeaway A5 · CD1 11 “Let's go shopping!” · PB p. 8
28–37′
▶ Concentration
desks, teams

▶ Memory / Concentration (teams of 4): a face-down grid of picture cards + word cards. On your turn flip two — if picture matches word, you must ask & answer the target question to keep the pair: Is there any butter? — Yes, there is! Wrong verb (is/are) → pair goes back. Most pairs wins.

Kagan · RallyCoach: partners take turns, one flips & speaks, the other checks the is/are choice & praises Nice — it's countable! Rotate every turn → all four active.

ICQ: Do you keep the pair for just a match, or for a match AND a correct question? (→ both.)

 3 memory sets (12 picture + 12 word cards each)
37–46′
▶ Shopping-list relay
open floor, teams

Stand up — team relay! Two hoops/boxes on the floor: CUPBOARD and FRIDGE. Each team has a stack of ingredient cards.

▶ Relay challenge: the whole team whispers the full sentence, then the runner sprints, drops the card in the right hoop and says it out loud: Are there any biscuits in the cupboard? Yes, there are! Correct place + correct is/are = point; then tag the next runner. Freeze! resets if it's too loud.

Fast finishers' challenge card: Name 2 ingredients we DON'T need for a cake.

 2 hoops/boxes (CUPBOARD / FRIDGE labels) · 3 card stacks · scoreboard
46–53′
▶ Spelling Showdown
desks, teams

Settle & spell (KTP “spelling activity”). ▶ Kagan Showdown: you say a tricky word — biscuits… chocolate… coconut… raisins… butter — teams write it on a mini-whiteboard, hide it; on Showdown! all boards up at once.

Point out the trap letters: silent middle in bis-cu-its, double tt in butter, ai in raisins, two syllables in choc-late. Syllable-clap each word.

 mini-whiteboards + pens
53–57′
Reflection
carpet

▶ Timed Pair Share (20 sec each): Tell your partner one ingredient that's countable and one that's uncountable — and why.

Quick self-rate on the word map: Tick the words you can already ask about. Team scoreboard total → winners' round of applause.

 📓 word map (self-tick) · scoreboard

door
