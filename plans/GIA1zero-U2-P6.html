WHAT WE DO: Showdown for revision: high-five; P5 blitz revision: the letter skeleton in chorus + 1 apostrophe fix + a volley of questions; bridge to the finale

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. At the door — a high-five + one word: project mood?
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: everybody writes at the same time, boards face down, on the call No peeking… Boards up! they all hold them up together — I see every answer, nobody can hide.
  3. P5 revision: the skeleton in chorus (Move one? — Hello! Move two? — The favour!); a fix on boards: my grandmas glasses → grandma's.
  4. Volley: Have you got any cousins? — in chorus, full short answers.
  5. Bridge: You've met the Canes, the Putmans, the twins… Today — the most important family: the people around YOU. Final boss of Unit 2!
📺 On screen: mini-boards
👦 Students do: they high-five, fix it, answer. Expected: up to speed in 6 minutes. Typical error: apostrophe in the wrong place — the houses are still up.

WHAT WE DO: announcing the “map of important people” project; the inclusivity rule out loud; the prize breakdown

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I announce: Five circles. YOU in the centre. Around you — your people: with names, faces and facts. Poster + presentation!
  2. The rule out loud (TB): Family, friends, teams, teachers, pets — IMPORTANT people. Your map, your choice. An invented hero-map is fine too.
  3. Prize breakdown: points for captions by the frame · for “everybody spoke 3+” · for creativity.
  4. Support groups of 4 (for PRESENT), we work individually.
📺 On screen: TASK slide (or on the board) · board (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they listen to the rules, relax at the inclusive rule. Expected: “what about my dog?!” — “Alex took his basketball team. Take your dog.”

WHAT WE DO: ex.2–3: the centre, orange/blue circles, the anatomy of a caption; Super skill: “don't marry the first idea”

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Ex.2 at a sprint: Who's in the centre? Which circle = most important? Who besides family — and why? (TB key: friends — school together; Sarah — music; the team — basketball).
  2. Caption anatomy on the board: who + age · looks (have got!) · why important / what we do together. The Uncle Daniel example out loud.

Step 2: a list of people + notes
PLAN · list + notes

WHAT WE DO: RallyRobin: ex.4–6: a personal list of 8+ → pair discussion with Key phrases → choose 6 + notes by the formula

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Ex.4 solo (3 min): a list of 8+ important people/groups — more than will fit.
  2. I run the pair as Kagan RallyRobin: we alternate one answer at a time — Ping-pong it: A — one, B — one, back to A. No monologues, no lists!
  3. Ex.5–6 in pairs with Key phrases tents: What do you think? Have you got any cousins? Are you sure about that? — choose 6.
  4. Notes by the formula in WORDS: uncle Dima · 40 · beard · fishing together.
  5. ICQ: Sentences in notes? — No! Words!
📺 On screen: Key phrases tents · formula cards on the desks · note sheets
👦 Students do: they browse ideas, discuss in English, write notes. Expected: lists with coaches and pets. Typical error: discussing in Russian — the tent reminds: decide in English.

CREATE · the map (20′)
desks · solo

WHAT WE DO: a personal A4/A3 map: 5 colour-coded circles, names, captions by the formula; a sync bell; drawings instead of photos

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. 20-min countdown: CREATE! Five circles, colour code, names, captions by the formula. Photos = drawings today; beauty is homework!
  2. Caption frames on the desks: ___ is my ___ . He's/She's ___ . He's/She's got ___ . We ___ together.
  3. Minute 10 — the bell: Sync! Centre done? Orange circle captioned? Support: a caption skeleton with gaps.
  4. I walk round with a notepad: 2 typical errors — into the revision games; with 3 min left: Captions FINISH now!
📺 On screen: A4/A3 sheets · markers · caption frames · bell
👦 Students do: they draw circles, write captions by the frame. Expected: maps with dogs and teams. Typical error: a one-word caption — the frame on the desk demands 3 lines.

WHAT WE DO: How to p.118 → a 2-min rehearsal → everybody presents their map in a foursome (3+ sentences); the audience with the peer card ex.9

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. How-to tips (p.118) in 1 min: Speak to PEOPLE, not paper. A groupmate holds your map!

  3. Foursomes: everybody presents their map, 3+ sentences per person is not required — 3+ in total, as much as they can manage; the audience with peer card ex.9: who's in the map? how is it like yours?

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners create and present a circle map of important people (scaffolded captions, groups of 4, everyone speaks 3+ sentences) — and warm up for the test with revision games.
  • Subsidiary. Project cycle THINK→PLAN→CREATE→PRESENT with Alex's video model; Super skill: Critical thinking («don't choose your first idea»); Key phrases; Peer review; Final reflection (smileys); revision games block.
  • Personal. You are the timekeeper, not the designer: loud timers per step, praise process. The map is INDIVIDUAL (it's about their life) — but friends/teams/pets count: say the inclusive rule out loud. Presentation success = everyone speaks 3+ sentences.
By the end, students can…
  • choose people thinking twice (not first idea)
  • write scaffolded captions with have got
  • present their map in 3+ sentences
  • self-assess with smileys

Structure
The people in the orange circle are the most important. · My mum's very important to me. She's got brown hair and blue eyes, like me. · We play video games together.
Form
circle map: Me! centre; orange = most important, blue = less; caption: who + age + looks (have got) + why/what we do together.
Pron. watch
presentation voice: loud, slow, eyes up; important — stress on POR.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Only family in the map?” → No — friends, teachers, teams, pets: IMPORTANT people.
“First idea = final choice?” → No: write it, then think who ELSE.
“One person presents the group's maps?” → No — everyone presents their OWN, 3+ sentences.

Assumptions: All unit language in place; the challenge is captions depth + speaking stamina. Second project of the year — structure still heavy: timers, frames, trackers. Personal topic → inclusive rule + invented-person option.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
