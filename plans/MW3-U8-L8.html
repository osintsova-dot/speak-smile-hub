Week 26.04–02.05 · Lesson 8🎨 Project · Our class band · big band poster~8 pupils · make + presentPH free reading Part 3 (p.53–65) · songs: Welcome to our show(T8) · What Are You Doing?(58)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a big “Our class band” poster — each child draws themselves playing an instrument and says: “I'm playing the guitar!” — then the whole band performs together.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle all six instruments & the activities through a creative product; sing «Welcome to our show» (CD4 T8) & Genki «What Are You Doing?» (58); take turns presenting; Phonics Hero — free reading, Part 3 (p.53–65); a happy end-of-unit band show.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the presenting sentence first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (one instrument each) so no long waiting — then the whole band plays together.
By the end, pupils can…
  • draw myself playing an instrument for the class band;
  • present my part — “I'm playing the drum!”;
  • perform in the band & say “Well done!” to a friend.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do we draw ourselves playing ONE instrument, or all of them?” → one — my band part!
“Your friend shows their picture — what do we say?” → Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 8 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can name the instruments, say I'm playing the… & answer Do you like…?; reading is fluent free reading. They know the making & presenting routines from earlier projects.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Everyone wants the same instrumentThat's fine — a band can have five drummers! Or share so the band has variety; celebrate every choice.
Making takes over the lessonPre-drawn instrument outlines / a ready big poster to add to; time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; showing the picture + one instrument is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone claps & says Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentAdd pictures to the poster in a fast carousel, then the WHOLE band plays together — no long solos.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Make it. Children draw themselves playing their chosen instrument (colour outlines ready) and add it to the class band poster. Write/trace the instrument word if they can.

Rehearse as they work. go round: What are you playing? — I'm playing the…? help each child rehearse their line quietly at the table.
