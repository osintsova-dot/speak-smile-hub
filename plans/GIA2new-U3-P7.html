🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): With new students the first big project of the unit follows the coursebook steps exactly (THINK→PLAN→CREATE→PRESENT), with an organiser, ready-made questions and discussion phrases on cards. The unit review is built in as stations, framed as “the checks before the exhibition opens” — test preparation without the word “test”. For the presentation the whole crew stands at the front and the speaker keeps the sheet; success = you went through all the steps, not “the best project”.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Your crew makes a real book today. A small one — but a real one: paper, colour, a title, photos. It's about traditional clothes: a region, what men wear, what women wear, and when. You'll see a video of a girl making one, you'll read a model page about Bolivia, and then you make yours. Before the exhibition opens — four quick checks of everything we've learnt. Let's build!”
In simpler words, if the class needs support: The crew makes a real mini-book about traditional clothes: the region, what men and women wear, and when. First the video and the model page, then four checks before the exhibition opens, then building it and presenting it.

WHAT WE DO: the video about making a mini-book (ex.1) → the Bolivia model page (ex.2) → the project phrases

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ex.1 video: What do you need to make a mini-book? Key: coloured card, paper, coloured pens and pencils, scissors, glue, a ruler, a rubber, photos, a stapler. I hold up a finished mini-book.
  2. ex.2 the model page: Which of these things can you see? Key: everything except information about the history of the region. We tick them in the book itself.

WHAT WE DO: 5-minute stations with support: clothes · adjectives · present continuous · choosing the tense

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Four checks. Five minutes each. Station cards help you!
  2. Station 1 — clothes: 21 words, pictures → words, the head-to-feet route (the P1 cards).
  3. Station 2 — adjectives: the seven pairs of opposites (the P4 cards).
  4. Station 3 — present continuous: -ing forms, negatives, short answers (the P3 poster).
  5. Station 4 — the two clocks: time expressions into the columns + two stative traps (the P5 posters).
  6. At every station there is a support card from the pair it came from. The station finishes with Kagan Numbered Heads Together: at the first station I show how it works myself, after that the crew checks the answers together and makes sure every member could say them; I call a number — that student answers, and the support card stays open while they do.
  7. A personal “superpower list”: whatever did not work, I write into my own notebook.
📺 On screen: the station cards · the posters off the walls · timer (backup — the stations move along the rows and I pass the cards round myself)
👦 Students do: they revise the unit with support. Expect: they recognise their own materials on the walls — that gives confidence. Error: they do not write the list — I walk round and remind them personally
📓 Interactive notebook — Get_Involved_A2_Unit3_Translation_Match_It_2up_A4 + Get_Involved_A2_Unit3_Translation_Translate_It_2up_A4
two 2-up sheets (one A4 = 2 pupils each): MATCH IT! — 12 Russian sentences to English A–L (clothes, present continuous, simple against continuous, buying an item, because and so), then TRANSLATE IT! — the same 12 from memory
  • Glue MATCH IT! in and join the pairs in pencil — first alone, then check with a partner.
  • Glue TRANSLATE IT! on the next page and write the English without looking back.
  • Self-check against the matched sheet; every mistake is rewritten as a full sentence.
  • Write your score out of 12 in the margin — that is the unit's exit line.

WHAT WE DO: fold the book following the video → write the text on the skeleton → the photo caption → one run-through

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can make and present a mini-book about traditional clothes, following the project steps, and revise the whole unit at four stations.
  • Subsidiary. Super skill: creativity (two layout ideas before choosing — in a simplified form); the project phrases from the cards; unpacking the model page (Bolivia); Quick review p.45 as stations with support; Final reflection p.47.
  • Personal. The project follows the coursebook steps; I do not cut it. At the stations they have the support cards from the earlier pairs. I call the gap list “the superpower list”; the test is not mentioned until the end of the pair.
By the end, students can…
  • choose a region and collect three facts about its clothes
  • use project phrases in the crew (What about…? That's a good idea.)
  • write facts in present simple and a photo caption in present continuous
  • present a mini-book with the crew (speaker with notes)

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The discussion collapses into RussianThe phrase cards on the table; the rule: a decision is only made with a phrase from a card.
The stations get squeezed out by the projectThe stations sit firmly in the first half of the pair, on a timer, with support — they are worth no fewer points than the project.
Shyness at the presentationThe whole crew stands together, the speaker keeps the sheet, and the audience listens with a task (catch one item of clothing).
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
