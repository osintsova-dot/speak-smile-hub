Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name materials and say what things are made of: “What's a (sweater) made of? — It's made of (wool). / What are (boots) made of? — They're made of (leather).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «What's it made of?» (CD3 13); consolidate made of noticed in the story (L3) — now taught properly; discover the rule — It's made of (one thing) · They're made of (plural things); recycle machines & used for from L1–L2; a real guessing communication game (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on made of + material (not just the material); model each material with a touch/feel gesture; keep the game moving — short turns, everyone guesses.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 materials (wool · cotton · leather · plastic · gold · silver);
  • ask & answer “What's it/are they made of?”;
  • choose It's (singular) vs They're (plural) correctly.

Structure
What's a sweater made of? — It's made of wool.  ·  What are boots made of? — They're made of leather.  ·  Earrings are made of silver.  ·  A bottle is made of plastic.
Form
It's made of + material (one object) · They're made of + material (plural). Table: A sweater / bracelet / T-shirt is made of · Boots / earrings / bottles are made of.
Pron. watch
wool /wʊl/ (short vowel); leather /ˈleðə/ (not “lee-ther”); plastic /ˈplæstɪk/; jewellery: gold /ɡəʊld/ · silver /ˈsɪlvə/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“a sweater — It's or They're made of?” → It's (one).
“boots — It's or They're made of?” → They're (two boots).

Assumptions: made of was met in the story (L3); the 6 materials are new but very touchable (bring real objects if possible). The switch to It's/They're made of + choosing is/are is the key challenge. Readers can read short material words. (Book teaches 6: wool · cotton · leather · plastic · gold · silver — КТП listed a different set: metal · glass · wood · rubber.)

of dropped (“made wool”)Board frame keeps made OF bright; back-chain: wool → of wool → made of wool.
Materials mispronounced (leather/wool)Touch-and-say: feel a woolly jumper, a leather shoe; mirror the mouth for /ð/ in leather.

Song lyrics too fastFirst run listen & touch the material; second run join in on “It's made of…”.

27–37′
Guess & make true sentences
desks, pairs → 2 teams

Model first. Show an object card, hide it: It's made of wool. Is it a sweater? — Yes, it is!

▶ Talk Partners (act. 4). Pairs make true sentences: A says It's made of wool. B guesses Is it a sweater? — Yes, it is / No, it isn't. Try again! Swap. Both write one sentence.

▶ Noughts & Crosses — materials (2 teams). 3×3 grid of object pictures. To claim a cell, the team ASKS — What are the earrings made of? — the other team answers They're made of silver! Both sides speak every turn. Rotate the asker.

✔ Assess: circulate — is it “made of” + is/are correct? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: object cards have the material printed. ⭐ Extension: add: My … is made of … and it's very ….
