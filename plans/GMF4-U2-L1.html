Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 adjectives of character (in opposite pairs) and describe people: “What's he/she like? — He's/She's (kind and friendly). / Are you (lazy)? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing the vocabulary song & chant (CD1 28); brainstorm words to describe family (act. 1, Learning to learn); notice the un- opposites (tidy↔untidy, kind↔unkind); preview the past — “What were you like when you were young?” (bridge to L2); early literacy — read the adjectives and solve the AB crossword.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — the Quiz-Quiz-Trade is high-energy, plan the freezes. Insist on the full description (He's / She's + adjective), not one word.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 character adjectives and their opposites;
  • ask & answer “What's he/she like?” and “Are you…?”;
  • read the adjectives and match them to descriptions.

Structure
What's your (aunt) like? — She's confident and kind.  ·  Are you lazy? — No, I'm not. I'm active!  ·  preview: What were you like when you were young?
Form
be + adjective of character: I'm / you're / he's / she's + (kind). Yes/No: Are you…? — Yes, I am / No, I'm not. un- = opposite (tidy→untidy · friendly→unfriendly · kind→unkind).
Pron. watch
confident /ˈkɒnfɪdənt/ (3 syll.); quiet /ˈkwaɪət/ (2 syll., ≠ quite); un- is unstressed — unTIdy, unFRIENDly.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“tidy — books on the shelf, clothes away. Opposite? Add un-!” → untidy.
“She's confident — happy to speak and try? or scared and quiet?” → happy to try = confident (≠ shy).

Assumptions: Starter & Unit 1 routines are back; pupils know some feeling/appearance words from GMF3 (revised in the Starter). The 12 character adjectives are largely NEW; description with be is known — the opposites and precise meanings are today's stretch. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures.

Presentation · 12 adjectives
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD1 28). Each card: What's he like? He's… tidy! Confident! Active! Choral ×3 + one clear gesture (shy = hide face, noisy = hands to mouth, kind = heart).

Opposites with un-. Board: two columns — hold the positive card, class flips it to the opposite: tidy → untidy! friendly → unfriendly! kind → unkind! Chorus the un- pairs.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if this is a GOOD-friend word… kind! — flash cards fast, everyone reacts every time (trick them with un- words!).

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + word. ⭐ Extension: full sentence — He's confident and he's kind.

Character trade
mingle

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Each child holds an adjective card. Music → mingle; off → pair: A shows the card, What's this? Say the opposite too! B says the word + its un-/pair opposite, then swap, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges — everyone speaks many times.

Whole-class check: Hands up if your last word had UN- at the front!
