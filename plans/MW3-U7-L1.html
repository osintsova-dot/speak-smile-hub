Week 01.03–07.03 · Lesson 1✨ NEW · cinema · museum · shopping centre · swimming pool · toy shop · zoo~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 17–18 review (/aʊ/ ou · /ɔɪ/ oi · ue er ar · p.46–51) · Genki: Where Are You Going?(56)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name six places in town — cinema · museum · shopping centre · swimming pool · toy shop · zoo — and match each to its colour on the Big Wheel: “Where's the museum? — It's on blue!”
  • Subsidiary. Clap the syllables of each place (pre-reading rhythm); play Go to the zoo! & Teddy Dancing (35); listen & stick the town stickers (PB p.45); play Listen & Memory; Phonics Hero — review the /aʊ/ ou & /ɔɪ/ oi (+ ue er ar) spellings, blend & speed-read p.46–51.
  • Personal. Tiny teacher-talk, big TPR — Speaky mimes a trip round town, children copy. Genki recipe: disco first, phonics early, input through curiosity, energy up in the games then down for the table. Every place gets its own big gesture and its own colour on the wheel.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name six places in town;
  • say a place for a colour — “It's on blue — museum!”;
  • clap the syllables of a place (zoo · toy-shop · su-per-mar-ket).

Structure
Where's the (museum)? — It's on (blue).  ·  Go to the (zoo)!  ·  It's a (cinema).
Form
Where's the + place → It's on + colour. Places named as fixed chunks (shopping centre, swimming pool = two-word places). No new tense — just naming places I know.
Pron. watch
clap the syllables: zoo (1), toy-shop / mu-se-um / ci-ne-ma; keep two-word places joined: shopping-centre, swimming-pool.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 (our reading workbook) — a spiral review: the /aʊ/ sound written ou (out, house, town, cloud) and /ɔɪ/ oi (coin, oil, soil), plus ue · er · ar. Show the Jolly actions, blend words with a meaning picture, then speed-read p.46–51. town & house tie right into our unit! Reading review, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Zoo — do we see animals or buy shoes?” → animals!
“Swimming pool — morning or swimming?” → swimming!

Assumptions: Colours (blue · green · orange · pink · red · yellow) & greetings are secure from Units 1–6 and re-used here to pin places to the wheel. The six town places are new but very picturable/mimeable and many children know them from real life. They already know all the phonic sounds; today they REVIEW the /aʊ/ ou & /ɔɪ/ oi spellings.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lessons 17–18 — sounds ou (/aʊ/) · oi (/ɔɪ/)  +  ue · er · ar. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds into a word: ou-t → out, h-ou-se → house, t-ow-n → town, c-oi-n → coin — big arm-sweep + a meaning picture (town & house are our topic!). ▶ Blend & jump — Speaky says an ou / oi word slowly; children blend it with the actions and jump on the whole word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.46–51 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read house · out together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.46–51 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · six places in town + wheel
carpet → Big Wheel mat

Guess the place! Slowly slide a place flashcard out of an envelope — Where are we? Guess! …the ZOO! Reveal + do the BIG mime together (watch a film, swim, look at dinosaurs…), whole class echoes. Do all six (cinema · museum · shopping centre · swimming pool · toy shop · zoo).

Clap the syllables. Clap the syllables of each place — zoo (one clap), toy-shop (two), ci-ne-ma / mu-se-um (three). Children guess the place from the claps.

Places on the wheel. Lay the six flashcards on the wheel — one per colour. Point to each pod: Where's the museum? It's on blue! Where's the zoo? It's on red!

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the interactive Big Wheel on screen with the six town-place pods (orange = toy shop · blue = museum · pink = shopping centre · yellow = swimming pool · green = cinema · red = zoo) — children point & answer It's on blue — museum!. The wheel comes from the coursebook app; our songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: mime & copy the puppet. ⭐ Extension: say the place for a colour — It's on green — cinema!

Game · Go to the zoo! + Teddy Dancing (35) ⚡
whole room, standing

▶ Go to the zoo! Stick the six place flashcards on different walls. Go to the zoo! …Go to the swimming pool! two children (then more) walk to that place, touch it and say it. Fast rounds — everyone moves.

▶ Teddy Dancing Genki Teddy Dancing: place mini-cards spread at the back. Music plays, children dance; music stops — everyone grabs the nearest card, mimes the place and shouts it. Repeat fast.

32–38′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Where Do You Live?» aloud — Speaky stands on a big MAP with houses, trees, a river and a mountain — the perfect calm read to open our town unit, straight after the dancing game.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Where do you live? I live here!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each spread — children point to the places on Speaky's map and echo the key line Where do you live?; on the last page find a place like ours (a house, a park) together.
