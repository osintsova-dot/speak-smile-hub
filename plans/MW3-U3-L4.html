🎡 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Unit 3 “The park” · Lesson 4 — Story Song «Fly the kite»
Pre-primary / early years (~5–6 y.o., pre-readers) · 60 min · Genki recipe · Disco warm-up → phonics → curiosity input → Genki games → song → craft · TPR + puppet · Phonics Hero 2.0 (reading: recognise → blend → speed-read) · carpet + tables
Week 09.11–15.11 · Lesson 4🎵 Song focus · «Fly the kite» · order the objects 1–4~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 18 (ue · er · ar · p.49–51) · Genki: Numbers(08)·What's The Weather Like?(10)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act out the story song «Fly the kite» (CD1 T42) and put the park objects in order 1–4, saying “There's the (pond)! There are the (trees)!” as each one comes.
  • Subsidiary. Mime the story with an imaginary kite; sing «Fly the kite» with actions; trace numbers 1–4 & match to the picture sequence (PB p.56); sing Genki «Numbers» (08) & «What's The Weather Like?» (10); read «What's the weather?»; Phonics Hero — sounds ue · er · ar, speed-read p.49–51.
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions, then words. Repeat several times without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (loud / quiet / fast / slow-mo). TB tip: pairs check each other's number-order.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing «Fly the kite» with actions;
  • put the park objects in order 1–4;
  • say “There's the…! There are the…!” for each object.

Assumptions: Song language met in L2 and the story in L3 — today deepens both. Numbers 1–6 are secure from Units 1–2; ordering 1–4 is the new step. Pre-readers order by pictures & numbers, not text.

Only a few singGive everyone an action job; sing the actions even without the words.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Grab your kite… hold the string… look UP! Ready to fly it? Mime holding a kite string, look up, then dance.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

Story flash. mime yesterday's story fast: windy sway, fly kite, stuck!, cheer — class calls It's windy! The kite's in the tree!

▶ Park sentences. say what's in the park with There's a pond! / There are flowers! to warm the frames.

 story cards · park flashcards
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 18 (again) — sounds ue · er · ar. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: ar → park, er → water, ue → glue — arm-sweep + a picture. Park link: p-ar-k · wat-er! ▶ Capital / sound hop — Speaky calls a sound; children jump to its card and blend a word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.49–51 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read want · because together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.49–51 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Play with an imaginary kite + «Fly the kite» in stages
carpet, standing

Imaginary kite. Mime holding a kite string, looking up at the sky — children copy. Use the song language: It's very windy! There's the pond! Help us, please!

Sing & move. Play «Fly the kite» (CD1 T42). Round 1 — join the ACTIONS only (fly kite, point to pond/trees). Round 2 — add words. Round 3 — a silly voice.

💪 Support: do the actions, hum along. ⭐ Extension: lead an action for the class.

 «Fly the kite» song (CD1 T42) · Speaky
25–31′
Game · Order the objects 1–4
carpet

▶ Song line-up. Lay the song's object cards (pond · trees · grass · kite) mixed, face-up. Play the song; What's first? The pond! together decide the order 1→4; then hand 4 children a card each to stand in the line while the class claps & says each: There's the pond! There are the trees!

🎮 Pairs check (TB tip): partners look at each other's PB numbers 1–4 and check they match the song order — thumbs-up when they agree.

✔ Assess: do they order by the numbers & pictures? Prompt with the song if stuck.

 song object cards ×4 · «Fly the kite» (CD1 T42)
31–37′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «What's the weather?» aloud — Speaky checks the sky — sunny, rainy, windy. A calm read that lands the story's WINDY day (the wind flies the kite!) right after the song.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line What's the weather? It's windy!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each page — children do the weather action (wiggle fingers for rain, sway for wind, big circle for sun) and shout the weather; land hard on It's windy! to tie back to the kite.

 «What's the weather?» (Speak & Smile Genki story-book · weather.pdf) · Speaky
37–45′
Song — perform + Genki «Numbers» + «Weather»
carpet, standing

Act to the song. Play «Fly the kite» again; children act it right through (fly kite, point to each object in order, cheer). Everyone moves the whole time.

Count & weather. Sing Genki «Numbers 1 To 12» (08) — count the objects 1→4; then «What's The Weather Like?» (10) — a quick windy reprise.

 «Fly the kite» (CD1 T42) · Genki «Numbers» (08) · «Weather» (10)
45–54′
Table time · trace & order (PB p.56) 🧘
tables

Sing & point. Books open; play the song once more — children point to each object as it comes and join in softly.

Trace & match. children trace the numbers 1–4 and match each number to the right picture to show the song's order — say There's the pond! There are the trees! as they go.

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Show your partner your favourite «Fly the kite» action. — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can sing «Fly the kite»! I can order 1–4! colour one star.

Beautiful singing, stars! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
