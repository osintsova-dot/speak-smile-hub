Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to follow & enjoy the story «Hurry up, Mummy!» (CD1 T20) and hand over items politely: “Here's your (hat)!” — with the story words T-shirt · shorts · shoes · socks · hat · water.
  • Subsidiary. Predict from the 4 pictures; listen (CD1 T20) with the story cards; play the dynamic Help Mummy! game; act tiny bits; circle/colour the items (PB p.4); Phonics Hero — sounds b j v w x y z qu, blend + speed-read p.17–25.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, surprise. Don't test comprehension; celebrate joining in. Give every child a card to hand over so all are involved.
By the end, pupils can…
  • point to what happens in each story picture;
  • name the story items (hat · water · shoes…);
  • hand something over — “Here's your hat!”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Mummy is late — does she hurry or sleep?” → hurry!
“I give you a hat — do I say Here's your hat or Goodbye?” → Here's your hat!

Assumptions: Family, greetings, numbers & colours are active from L1–L2; reading continues in the Phonics Hero workbook. The story-time routine (listen · point · join in) was set up in L1–L2. Item words are new but very picturable.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Story audio a bit fastPlay once for fun, then again pausing on each picture; point as you go.
Children lose the threadUse the big story cards frame by frame; ask a picture question after each.
Everyone wants to hand a card at onceGive one item each; call the item — only that child hands it over.
Just says the item, not the phrasePuppet models Here's your… WATER!; child echoes with the giving gesture.
Wriggling during listeningGive a job: wave when you hear a hat! — active listening.

“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! It's morning… everyone is sleepy… and Mummy is LATE! Uh-oh! Yawn & stretch, then dance awake.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

▶ Hello mingle. greet round the circle Hello! How are you? — I'm fine, thank you! — swap feelings (sleepy · happy).

Ask the wheel. quick Big Wheel: Where's number four? — It's on yellow! links yesterday's question before the story.

 Big Wheel mat · Speaky
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lessons 7–9 — sounds b · j · v · w · x · y · z · qu. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: b-u-s → bus, j-a-m → jam, z-i-p → zip — arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Blend relay — Speaky shows a word; teams blend it and race to touch its picture.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.17–25 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read he · she · we together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.17–25 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–26′
Curiosity input · Story «Hurry up, Mummy!»
carpet

Predict. Show the 4 story cards — Who is late? What do Mimi & Dylan give Mummy? Take 2–3 guesses (Russian OK).

Listen 1 — for fun. Play the story (CD1 T20) with the cards. Pause on each picture: What's the matter? — I'm sleepy! Here's your T-shirt! Children point & join the words they can.

Listen 2 — spot the item. Wave when you hear WATER! children clap/wave when they hear it — active listening.

 story cards ×4 · «Hurry up, Mummy!» (CD1 T20)
26–37′
Game · Steal the Bacon (19) → Help Mummy! ⚡
carpet / floor space

▶ Help Mummy! Genki Steal the Bacon, re-skinned: two teams line up opposite; item flashcards (T-shirt · shorts · shoes · socks · hat · water) in the middle. 🦝 Speaky (Mummy) calls Oh no, I'm late! Where are my shoes? — one runner from each team races to grab it and gives it: Here's your shoes!

Every item called → everyone runs & gives at least once. Big thank-yous: Thank you! Hurry, hurry!

💪 Support: run & give, copy Here's your… ⭐ Extension: add the colour: Here's your RED hat!

 item flashcards ×6 · Speaky (Mummy)
37–45′
Perform · act the story + Genki «How Are You?»
carpet, standing

Act it out. Play the story again; children mime: rub eyes (sleepy), run on the spot (late), hand over items, cheer at the end. Pick 2 to be Mimi & Dylan; the class narrates the item words.

Feelings song. Sing Genki «How Are You?» (04) — the story's feelings (I'm sleepy / I'm fine) with big faces & actions.

 story cards · item cards · Genki «How Are You?» (04)
45–53′
Table time · Pupil's Book p.4 🧘
tables

Story questions. Point to each picture; ask simple questions: Is it morning? — Yes! Is Mummy late? — Yes! What does Mimi give Mummy? children answer yes/no or point.

Circle & colour. children circle the items Mimi & Dylan give Mummy, then colour them (watch: NOT the apple!).

 Pupil's Book p.4 · crayons
53–58′
Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Give your partner a pretend hat — “Here's your hat!” — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I know the story! I can say “Here's your hat!” colour one star.

Wonderful helpers today! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.

 “I can…” badges · crayons · Goodbye song (CD1 T3) · Speaky

door

Line up at the door with 🦝 Speaky. At the door, 🦝 Speaky is late: Where's my water? hand over the pretend item and say Here's your water! Answered / shown → high-five out: Bye-bye! See you next time!
