📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. An honest check on the unit: vocabulary (clothes & accessories + describing clothes), Present continuous (all forms), Present simple vs continuous, time expressions, listening, a short piece of writing — with the official Macmillan test.
  • Subsidiary. Test strategy BEFORE it starts (time management, the order of the tasks); feedback straight after; reflection on the growth from the P1 gate until today; a first look at the Midterm.
  • Personal. The tone of the pair: the test is proof of progress, not a verdict. Higher goes to the “ours” track, Standard to the “new” one; inside the group I never advertise who has which. Feedback happens the same day, while it is hot.
By the end, students can…
  • manage test time across sections
  • apply the unit language under test conditions
  • check answers against the key and explain own errors
  • name their own growth since Pair 1

Likely problemWhat you do
Panic in part of the classThe strategy before the test + a reminder: the gap list is closed, the growth has been measured since P1 (the first gate — 8 words, today — a whole text).
The fast ones finish and get boredA quiet bonus task on the back: describe what everyone in the room is doing right now (5 lines).
Feedback turns into marks read out loudMarks stay private; feedback goes by type of error, with no names.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
