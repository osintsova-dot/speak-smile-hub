🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The test is quiet and fair — no games DURING it. The rematch first lowers anxiety with DATA (“you've already beaten your September self”), the revenge quiz after is the emotional payoff: errors become ammunition, not shame.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“The rematch. Same three gates, same rules. September-you is waiting.” “Beat yourself in 2 of 3 — growth badge, plus five!” “That was the revision. The test asks the SAME formats on bigger paper. You've already beaten it once today.”

WHAT WE DO: Inside-Outside Circle as a review: quiet music + “readiness 1–5”; a look at the wall of nations; the mindset “rematch first, then the test”

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Quiet music before entry. At the door: One word — how do you feel?
  2. I build Kagan Inside-Outside Circle: two circles facing each other, a question → both answer → Outside circle, rotate two steps! — a new partner, a new question; 3 rotations, everyone speaks.
  3. I point at the poster wall: Look at your nations. People who FOUND countries don't fear a test.
  4. I ask: Show test-readiness 1–5. Whatever you showed — first comes the REMATCH, and it will show you the truth: you're ready.
📺 On screen: quiet music · the poster wall
👦 Students do: enter calmly, get in the mood through pride in the project. Expected: readiness 3–5; the anxious ones breathe out at the words “rematch first”.

Revenge quiz: turning errors into points
🏁 REVENGE QUIZ · beat your own test
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: I reword errors from the box into questions on the fly; teams write FULL corrections; hit 80% and the test is beaten

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I announce: REVENGE. The test beat some of you. Now WE beat the test. Errors on the board belong to the whole class!
  2. I frame it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYONE writes at the same time, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift them together — I see everyone's answer, no hiding.
  3. I pull slips, reword on the fly; teams write FULL corrections on mini-boards within 15 sec: Not ‘B’ — write: She isn't Dutch, she's Danish!
  4. Rules: streak ×2 after 3 in a row · an intercept on a miss.
  5. I count: the class needs to clear 80% of the box → THE TEST IS OFFICIALLY BEATEN!
📺 On screen: box · mini-boards · a “X out of Y cleared” counter on the board · leaderboard
👦 Students do: finish off their own errors as teams with full corrections. Expected: loud, exciting, catharsis at 80%. Typical error: a single letter instead of a correction — doesn't count, a full move is required.

Unit 1 test — specification (DRAFT until the TRC pack: Progress tests standard/higher in the Teacher's Resource Centre, TB pp. 5, 30)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 8 items: country → nationality adjective, spelling counts (China → Chinese; the Netherlands → Dutch). B) 6 items: opposite adjectives (crowded → empty; safe → dangerous).
Part 2 · Grammar be (18 pts)A) 8 gaps aff/neg with contractions (My parents ___ from Moscow. It ___ (not) cold today.). B) 6 items: order the words to make questions + write a true short answer (from / you / Are / Russia ?). C) 4 items: correct the mistake (I is 11. → I'm 11.).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A short description of a country (script in the plan; teacher-read twice if no track): 4 true/false + 4 notes to complete (population / capital / weather / sport) — mirrors SB p.17 ex.6–7.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)6 sentences about you (name, age, country, nationality, your city + one adjective). Marked for: be forms correct (4) · capital letters (3) · task complete (3) — mirrors keypal criteria SB p.21.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.21 — every format was met in class during the unit. As soon as the TRC pack is downloaded, this is replaced by the official Progress test (🐣 standard / 🚀 higher).

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
