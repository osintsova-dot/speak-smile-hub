How to use this plan (RU)

🔧 Это ШАБЛОН-конструктор на 2 финальных резервных урока (71–72) — ПОСЛЕ Final Test (69) и Speaking Exam (70). Экзамены сданы, давление снято: это празднично-повторительные уроки. Ты латаешь пробелы в Units 5–9 и красиво закрываешь год.

Шаг 1. Быстрая диагностика (этап 3–12′) по Units 5–9 — либо возьми реальные слабые места из Final Test (у тебя уже есть проверенные работы!). Шаг 2. 3 станции под западающие темы из «Меню». Шаг 3. Команда-квиз с наградой. Шаг 4. Рефлексия + итоги года.

End-of-year опция (по желанию, из КТП): Jar of Awesomeness — в течение обоих уроков дети (и ты) кидают в банку записки «this year I can… / my best moment was…», в конце достаёте и читаете вслух. Тёплое закрытие года, работает на самооценку и английскую речь о себе.

Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end of the two reserve lessons, learners will have re-consolidated the shakiest language from Units 5–9 (teacher-selected, ideally from Final-Test evidence) — comparatives, be going to, was/were, Past Simple, telling the time — and celebrated a year of English.
  • Subsidiary. Retrieval practice, peer-teaching via Kagan, positive self-talk about the whole year (Jar of Awesomeness), reading recognition across genres.
  • Personal. Exams are DONE — radiate «we made it» joy. Diagnose to the gap, keep it playful, and make sure every child hears something they're proud of before summer.
By the end, pupils can…
  • re-produce target words & one key structure from each revised unit;
  • peer-teach a partner and talk about the year in English;
  • name one thing they're proud of learning this year.

🎯 Revision stations (3 × 8′)
3 tables + carpet

Rotation (Cooperative learning). 3 stations from the Station menu under your chosen topics — one Lexis, one Grammar, one Reading. 3 small teams, rotate every 8′ on Freeze! Move on!

Station A — Lexis (movement). ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade with the weak unit's word cards — all up, pairs quiz, swap, new partner. Kagan: StandUp-HandUp-PairUp.

Station B — Grammar (talk). ▶ RallyRobin survey — take turns asking the unit question (Are you going to…? / Did you watch…? / What time did you…?) & noting answers. Kagan: RallyRobin.

Station C — Reading (calm, seated). re-read a genre text from the unit (article / letter / script / review / email) → True-False or match. Kagan: RallyCoach.

💪 support: picture-word cards at each station · ⭐ extension: “write one more sentence” card.  🏺 Jar of Awesomeness sits on carpet all lesson — finishers drop in a note.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — tick who now produces each target structure unaided.

38–52′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 2 teams

Consolidation quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles mixing revised units: picture→word · spelling · make the target sentence (comparative / past / time) · True/False · a “whole-year” bonus tile. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/says on their mini-whiteboard → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a 🎳 bowling or 🏀 basketball shot (paper ball into box / on-screen animation). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Offline setup: card grid on whiteboard + real paper-ball shot into a box/basket — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on “Show me!”)   Finale: count scores in English; both teams Give me five!
