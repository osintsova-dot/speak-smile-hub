Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Have you got any juice — why any, not some?” → question. “I've got some juice?” → affirmative.
“Two breads?” → No — bread is uncountable: some bread.
“Notes = full sentences?” → Never — words, letters, symbols (the subskill).

Assumptions: some/any met in GMF4 orally; the real ceiling is written accuracy + the C/UC logic behind it. Shopping-centre set partly new (chemist's, stationery shop, food stall). Note-taking is a NEW skill — model it. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
