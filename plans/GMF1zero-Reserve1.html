How to use this plan (RU)

🔧 Это ШАБЛОН-конструктор на 1 резервный урок (72) в конце года. Ты не «проходишь» новую тему — ты чинишь пробелы из Units 1–9 (по итогам Final Test / Speaking) И празднуешь целый год. Каркас: диагностика → 3 станции под западающие темы → команда-квиз с наградой → праздник чтения. По усмотрению педагога (КТП урок 72: «педагог выбирает темы»).

Шаг 1. Прогони быструю диагностику (этап 3–12′) — увидишь, что подзабыли. Шаг 2. Из «Меню станций» выбери 3 под западающие темы. Шаг 3. Команда-квиз (боулинг/футбол) собирает всё вместе. Шаг 4. Праздник чтения — дети читают любимые истории тетради (Phonics Hero) — гордый финал первого года «с нуля».

Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have re-consolidated the shakiest language from Units 1–9 (teacher-selected) — naming, one target structure per topic, and reading recognition — and shown it in a low-stakes Team Quiz with reward game + a proud Reading Party.
  • Subsidiary. Retrieval practice, peer-teaching via Kagan, honest self-check; celebrate a whole first year from zero (reading, speaking, singing).
  • Personal. This is repair + celebration, not new load: keep it light and joyful. Diagnose first, teach to the gap. Every child works at every station — no watching.
By the end, pupils can…
  • re-produce the target words & one key structure from each revised topic;
  • peer-teach a partner what they remember;
  • read a favourite story aloud and feel proud of a whole year of English.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«Is this a test?» → no — it's a memory party at the end of the year; mistakes just show what to play more.
«In the quiz, who answers?» → EVERYONE, on their own board / with their own card.

Assumptions: Units 1–9 all taught & tested; Final Test & Speaking done. Pupils know Quiz-Quiz-Trade, RallyRobin, Showdown, the traffic-light self-check and the Phonics Hero stories. Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
You don't know what to reviseLet the diagnosis + Final-Test errors decide: the 2–3 topics with most red hands become your stations. Don't revise everything.
End-of-year over-excitementKeep pace tight, stations timed (visible timer), one warning system; channel energy into movement stations, not chaos.
Mixed pace at stationsEach station has a 💪 core task + ⭐ extension card — fast finishers keep going, no one waits.
Weaker pupils hide in the teamShowdown / own card: every child answers every question; rotate who runs to the board.
«We did this already, boring»Frame as a game/challenge + reward animation; new format (Quiz-Quiz-Trade, mime), not the same worksheet.
Classroom management — signals
«Class? — Yes!» — you: «Class!» → pupils stop & echo «Yes!»
«WiFi» 🖐 — say «WiFi» + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
«Give me five» — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — «Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.»
«Freeze!» — statue, mid-movement reset.

🔍 Quick diagnosis
carpet

Fast gap-finder (all together, ~1′ per topic). Flash 3–4 signal cards per topic; whole-class chorus + one target sentence each:

U1 Where's my pencil? — It's here! · U2/U3 Is it a robot? — Yes, it is! / I've got a nose. · U4/U5 I like apples. / Birds can fly. · U6/U7 Where's Beth? — behind the tree. / There's a zoo. · U8/U9 He's riding a horse. / I brush my teeth every day.

▶ Traffic-light vote (eyes closed): after each topic Green — easy! Yellow — a bit tricky. Red — help me! — count reds. ✔ Decide live (with your Final-Test notes): the 3 topics with most yellow/red become today's stations. Today we play with THESE three!

14–36′
🎯 Revision stations (3 × 7′)
3 tables + carpet

Rotation (Cooperative learning). 3 stations built from the Station menu under your chosen topics — typically one Lexis, one Grammar, one Reading. Split class into 3 small teams; rotate every 7′ on Freeze! Move on!

Station A — Lexis (movement). e.g. ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade with the weak topic's word cards — every child up, pairs quiz, swap cards, new partner. Kagan: StandUp-HandUp-PairUp.

Station B — Grammar (talk). e.g. ▶ RallyRobin / mime survey — take turns with the topic structure (Is it a…? / Can you…? / I … every day). Kagan: RallyRobin.

Station C — Reading (calm, seated). word–picture match / sentence strips → point & read. Kagan: RallyCoach (coach the partner).

💪 support: picture–word cards at each station · ⭐ extension: «say one more sentence» challenge card.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — tick who now produces the target structure unaided.

37–50′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 3 teams

Consolidation quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles mixing the revised topics: picture→word · Is it a…? · make the target sentence · True/False · a mime tile. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/draws or holds up their card → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a 🎳 bowling or ⚽ football shot (paper ball into box / on-screen animation). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Ready-made engines: routines-quiz (U9) or review3-quiz (mixed Units 7–9) — the team answers chorally, one child throws; the score counts itself. Offline: card grid on whiteboard + real paper-ball shot into a box — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on «Show me!»)   Finale: count scores in English; both teams Give me five!

 quiz grid (board / projector) · mini-whiteboards or own cards · box + paper ball · 🎮 routines-quiz · review3-quiz
50–56′
🦸 Reading Party
carpet, pairs

Phonics Hero — праздник чтения (тетрадь). 🦝 Speaky: Remember September? You couldn't read at all. Now — read me your FAVOURITE story! Each child opens their favourite Phonics Hero story from the тетрадь; RallyRobin: pairs read a favourite to each other, one frame each, with actor voices. A few volunteers read one page to the class — big applause. A proud, joyful end to a whole first year from zero.

▶ Timed Pair Share: Tell your partner: your favourite thing you learnt this year. (20 sec each).
