Week 14.12–20.12 · Lesson 30💛 Values thank you · Content meerkats · Culture Five little monkeys~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 23 (Magic E review · p.64–65) · Genki: How Many?(30)·Numbers(08) · game: Koala Game(8)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to say «thank you» kindly (Values — the Lion & the Mouse, CD2 T13), know a fun fact about meerkats (Content — like Mimi's family!), and sing & count with «Five little monkeys» (Culture, CD2 T14) — animals around the world.
  • Subsidiary. Values — «I'm hungry / I can help you! / Thank you» + Genki Koala Game (8, please/thank you); Content — meerkats live together, dig & keep watch; Culture — count 1–5 monkeys + Genki «How Many?» (30) / «Numbers» (08); Phonics Hero — Magic E review, speed-read p.64–65.
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values through DOING (say thank you for real in class — Teacher's tip). Every child says thank you, moves like a meerkat, and shows a monkey number.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say «thank you» kindly when someone helps me;
  • say one thing meerkats do (live together / keep watch);
  • count 1–5 monkeys and sing «Five little monkeys».

“How many little monkeys in the tree?” → five!

Assumptions: Animals & descriptions are secure from L1–L5; reading is at Magic E review. Children know social routines. Content & culture stay concrete, visual & active. Values ties to the class thank-you routine.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Thank you” said flat / forgottenModel it live all lesson — say thank you yourself, add a bow; gentle reminder if forgotten (Teacher's tip).
Story (lion/mouse) too longUse the four pictures frame by frame; ask a picture question after each.
Content = just listeningEveryone MOVES like a meerkat: dig, then stand tall to keep watch on a signal.
Counting muddledShow monkeys on fingers 1→5; take one away each verse (5→1).
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Someone helps you… what do you say? THANK YOU! Big smile, little bow! Practise happy thank-you bows, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 23 · Magic E review (again) — sounds Magic E: all four — read faster. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-read the magic-e words — cake · bike · home · flute — a bit faster; blend the sticky ones. ▶ Speed-read race

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.64–65 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read right · goes together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Values · saying thank you (Lion & Mouse)
carpet

Little Book of Values. Use the puppet: Harry & Zee tell the Lion & the Mouse story (CD2 T13). Four pictures: I'm hungry! — I'm little. — I can help you! The lion doesn't eat the mouse; the mouse frees the lion — both say Thank you!

Who helps? Who's hungry? the lion, Who's little? the mouse — everyone says a big kind thank-you.

💪 Support: say Thank you! with a bow. ⭐ Extension: add I can help you! when offering help.

25–31′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Thank You, Speaky!» aloud — Speaky is helped by his friends all day and remembers the magic words — the perfect calm read for our saying-thank-you value, right on theme with the Lion & the Mouse.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Thank you, Speaky!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each spread — children spot the kind action and chorus Thank you! with a little bow; link it back — both the lion AND the mouse say thank you.

31–37′
Values game · Koala Game (8) ⚡
carpet, circle

▶ Koala Game Genki Koala Game: children sit in a circle. Pass a cuddly toy; you can only take it if you say Please! and only give it with Thank you! Everyone practises the magic words in a fast, happy round.

Add an animal each pass: Please — a lion! Thank you! — please & thank you meet the wild animals.
