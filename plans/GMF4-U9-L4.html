Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 holiday activities and ask & answer about experiences: “Have you ever (eaten sushi)? — Yes, I have. / Has he ever (seen a whale)? — No, he hasn't.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Have you ever?» (CD4 21); consolidate present perfect from L2 with new verbs; discover the irregular past participles — eat→eaten, take→taken, see→seen, buy→bought (regular: play→played, visit→visited); a real class survey (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on the past participle (not the base verb — “eaten”, not “eat”); model each activity with a big TPR action; keep the survey moving — short turns, everyone asks.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 holiday activities (eat sushi · take a photo · see a whale · play cricket · buy a souvenir · visit a monument);
  • ask & answer “Have/Has … ever + past participle …?”;
  • use the past participles eaten · taken · seen · bought · played · visited.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Have you ever ___ sushi? — eat or eaten?” → eaten (past participle!).
“see → the past participle is…?” → seen (not ‘saw’ — that's the past simple).

Assumptions: Present perfect (have/has + been) is active from L2–L3; the 6 activities are new but very picturable. The switch to new past participles (especially irregular eaten/taken/seen/bought) is the key challenge. Readers can read short activity phrases.

27–37′
Class survey · Have you ever…?
desks → mingle

Model first. Show the survey grid (6 activities, Me / friend). Model publicly: Have you ever eaten sushi? — Yes, I have! / No, I haven't.

▶ Find Someone Who (mingle survey, AB p.93 ex.3 style). Each child fills their OWN column first, then mingles: Have you ever seen a whale? — record friends' answers with a tick/cross. Find one “Yes” for each activity if you can. 6–8 exchanges — everyone asks & answers.

▶ Report back (Numbered Heads). Call a number: Has your partner ever played cricket? → Yes, he has. / No, she hasn't. third-person practice.

✔ Assess: circulate — is it have/has + the right participle? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: survey grid has the phrases printed with icons. ⭐ Extension: add a follow-up: Where did you eat it?
