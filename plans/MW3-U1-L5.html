🎡 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Starter block · Lesson 5 — Grammar · Have you got…? / Has Sam got…?
Pre-primary / early years (~6–7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · Grammar recipe (model/dialogue → controlled drill → freer → production) · revising Mimi's Wheel 2 · Unit 1 “Classroom” · TPR + puppet + Phonics Hero 2.0 · carpet + tables
Starter block · Lesson 5 of 4–8 (School review)✨ Focus · Have you got your…? / Has Sam got…?~8 pupils · teams of 3–4 + pairsPH ур.11 (big reading · стр.28–29 · you/your) · Genki: May I borrow your…? (2nd run)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to ask & answer about having school things: “Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.”, and to talk about a third person: “Has Sam got (the paints)? — Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.”
  • Subsidiary. Re-activate school-things vocabulary from Mimi's Wheel 2 Unit 1 (paints, paper, crayons, glue, pencil, pencil case, school bag, scissors) in context; sing Genki «May I borrow your…?» (2nd run) as the language song; Phonics Hero ур.11 — big reading, стр.28–29, tricky words you · your.
  • Personal. Model each question with the puppet many times before children produce it; back-chain the long question so no child freezes; hide every drill inside a game so everyone is asking, not waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask & answer “Have you got your (glue)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.”;
  • ask & answer about Sam — “Has Sam got the (paints)? — Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.”;
  • read the tricky words you · your as whole words.

Structure
Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.  ·  Has Sam got (the paints)? — Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.
Form
Have + you + got + your + noun? → Yes, I have / No, I haven't. 3rd person: Has + Sam + got…? → Yes, he has / No, he hasn't. (Sam = character.)
Pron. watch
rising question Have you got…? ↗; keep haven't / hasn't clear (headshake gesture); linked your /jə/; final /z/ in paints, crayons, scissors.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 ур.11 — big reading, стр.28–29 (finger-under, read the row fast, celebrate speed). Tricky words you · your read as whole words → tie-in: Have you got YOUR bag?
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I look in your bag and the glue is there. So — Yes, I …?” → Yes, I have.
“Has Sam got the paints — am I asking about you or about Sam?” → about Sam → he has / he hasn't.
“No paints in Sam's bag — Yes, he has, or No, he hasn't?” → No, he hasn't!

Assumptions: school-things words are known from Mimi's Wheel 2 Unit 1 (this Starter block = revision) and are re-checked in the warm recall; have got was met last year receptively — producing the question + short answer is today's stretch; reading continues from earlier Starter lessons. Teams: max 4 (8=2×4 · 7=3+4 · 6=3+3). No Mimi 2 coursebook in the base → run from flashcards + real objects.

carpet → board

① Warm the sounds. Quick review of known sounds with Jolly actions — teacher voices, children gesture (we don't drill bare sounds).

Tricky words. Read you · your as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out). Tie-in on the board: Have you got YOUR bag? — today's question, now in print!

19–27′
Grammar · I Do → We Do
carpet

I Do (model + think-aloud). 🦝 Speaky has a bag. Teacher: Have you got your glue, Speaky? Speaky looks inside — glue there! → Yes, I have! (thumbs up). Then: Have you got your scissors? — empty → No, I haven't! (headshake gesture). Say the thinking aloud: “I look inside — it's there → Yes, I have; it's NOT there → No, I haven't.”

Meet Sam (3rd person). Show the Sam-card — 🦝 Speaky: This is Sam! (Sam is a meerkat friend from Mimi's Wheel) — with his bag: Has SAM got the paints? Point to Sam. Paints there → Yes, he has. · empty → No, he hasn't. Contrast side by side: I have (point to self) vs he has (point to Sam).

Chant. Do the «Have you got your school bag?» chant with actions (pat-pat rhythm), whole class.

We Do (controlled, choral). Point to a card, everyone asks together: Have you got your pencil? → you answer for the class first, then teams echo. CCQ: Asking about Sam — I have, or he has? (→ he has).

💪 Support: answer with just Yes / No + gesture. ⭐ Extension: add a reason — No, I haven't. It's at home!

 Speaky + bag · Sam-card + mini-bag · school-things cards · board
27–34′
Game · Ask & Grab ⚡
carpet, teams

▶ Ask & Grab (Спроси-и-хватай) — controlled drill hidden in a race. Teams of 3–4 sit round a set of 8 school-things cards, face-up. Teacher (or a team caller) asks: Have you got the crayons? — the first hand to grab the crayons must say the full answer to keep it: Yes, I have! I've got the crayons! Wrong grab / no sentence → card goes back.

Rotate the asker each round so everyone produces the question. One hand only; Freeze! to reset if it heats up. Most cards = happy team cheer.

💪 Support: a picture-cue card of the question. ⭐ Extension: the asker uses your — Have you got YOUR glue?

 2–3 sets of school-things cards · Speaky
34–41′
Game · Tic-Tac-Toe grammar ⚡
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Крестики-нолики (Оксана-format) — a 3×3 grid = 9 A4 cards of school things on the carpet (each card = one cell). Two teams: ✕ and ⭕ counters on top.

To take a cell, a team must ask and answer a full sentence about that card: Have you got your paints? — Yes, I have! A bare word or silence = no cell, other team's turn. Three in a row wins.

Numbered Heads inside each team: team whispers together, then the called number says the sentence — so everyone thinks, nobody hijacks. Mix in a Sam-card cell → team must switch to Has Sam got…? — Yes, he has!

 9 A4 school-things cards · ✕/⭕ counters · 1 Sam-card · Speaky
41–48′
Game · Has Sam got…? Detective ⚡
carpet

▶ Sam's Secret Bag — freer, 3rd-person production. Secretly put 3–4 cards inside Sam's bag; the rest stay outside. Teams take turns asking: Has Sam got the scissors? 🦝 Speaky peeks & answers Yes, he has! / No, he hasn't! — a correct guess is celebrated; keep asking until the bag is worked out.

Round 2: a child becomes “Sam” and answers for himself — the class asks Have you got the glue? → child: Yes, I have! (now both persons in one game).

💪 Support: point to the card while asking. ⭐ Extension: guess two things — Has Sam got the paints AND the paper?

 Sam's bag + school-things cards · Speaky
48–54′
Production · Ask your friend 🧘
carpet → pairs

Real things, real questions. Each child holds their own pencil case / a picture card. ▶ RallyRobin (pairs): take turns — A: Have you got your glue? B: Yes, I have! / No, I haven't. then swap. Music on = find a new partner, music off = ask again (Mix-Pair).

Fast pause & share: point to two children — Has she got her scissors? class answers Yes, she has! (stretch to she).
