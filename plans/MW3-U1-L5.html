Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to greet a friend and reply in a short exchange: “Hello! How are you? — I'm fine, thank you. Goodbye!” — a life skill: saying hello, thank you and goodbye kindly.
  • Subsidiary. Role-play meeting friends with the puppet; sing Genki «How Are You?» (04) & «What's Your Name?» (03); add feeling faces; Phonics Hero — big reading (all 26 sounds), speed-read p.28–29; run the Monster Game (5).
  • Personal. First speaking-focus lesson — never force a shy child; non-verbal (wave, thumbs) counts. Model with the puppet first, then let them try. Keep exchanges ~20 seconds, then swap.
By the end, pupils can…
  • start a greeting — “Hello! How are you?”;
  • reply politely — “I'm fine, thank you.”;
  • say goodbye kindly — “Goodbye! See you!”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“My friend says hello — do I turn away or say hello back?” → say hello back!
“Someone gives you a pencil — what do you say?” → thank you!

Assumptions: Greetings met in L1–L2; children can produce parts of the exchange. Today builds the whole polite routine as a life skill. Reading has reached whole words & first sentences (Phonics Hero p.28–29).

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello, hello! Wave to a friend! Now blow them a kiss hello! Warm, silly greetings, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 11 · big reading — sounds review all 26 sounds — first read. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read short CVC words & the first little sentences on the page — blend any sticky one together first. ▶ Speed-read race — beat Speaky's clap-clock reading the row of words.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.28–29 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read you · your together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · the polite greeting
carpet

Watch the puppet. Speaky peeks shyly: Ooh — a new friend! How do we say hello nicely? With the puppet, act meeting a friend: Hello! — Hello! — How are you? — I'm fine, thank you! — Goodbye! Point to the 3 steps on your fingers.

Feeling faces. try different feelings: sleepy voice, happy voice, giant voice — children copy the puppet.

26–31′
Game · Stand up if…
carpet, standing

▶ Stand & greet. Stand up if you're HAPPY today! children who feel that way stand, wave, and say Hello! I'm happy! Everyone reacts every round.

Mix feelings fast (happy · sleepy · fine) — lots of standing, sitting, waving.

 feeling-face cards · Speaky
31–37′
Game · Monster Game (5) ⚡
floor space

▶ Monster Game Genki Monster Game: children on the “safe” side want to cross to the other side. Speaky is the friendly monster in the middle. To pass, a child must greet it: Hello! How are you? — I'm fine, thank you! If they do the full exchange, they run across; freeze on the safe side.

The best greeting game — everyone crosses several times, greeting each run.

💪 Support: wave + say Hello! to cross. ⭐ Extension: add Goodbye! See you! as you run.

 open floor · Speaky (monster)
37–42′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Good Morning, Speaky» aloud — Speaky wakes up and greets everyone through the day — the perfect calm read for our greetings life-skills lesson, straight after the running Monster Game.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Good morning! Hello! How are you?. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Show each page — children wave and greet back Good morning! Hello!; add the feeling on the reply page (I'm fine, thank you!) with a happy face.

42–48′
Song — Genki «How Are You?» + «What's Your Name?»
carpet, mingle

Meet & greet. Play Genki «How Are You?» (04): children mingle; on the pause they meet the nearest friend and do the full exchange Hello! How are you? — I'm fine, thank you! Goodbye!

Names too. Play Genki «What's Your Name?» (03) — add names to the meeting: Hello! What's your name? — I'm… New partner each pause + a handshake.

✔ Assess: who does all 3 steps? who needs the puppet nearby? Note it — celebrate every hello.

Give it kindly. in pairs, give the card: Hello! This is for you! — Thank you! — a calm, kind finish that practises the words.
