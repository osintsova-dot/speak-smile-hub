Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to value caring for our world (nature · animals · little bugs · family · friends), understand the life cycle of a butterfly (egg → caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly), and enjoy the culture story «The ant and the grasshopper» — and earn their Phonics Hero Diploma.
  • Subsidiary. Values — sing «I care for our world» (CD4 T26) & say what they care for; Content — mime the butterfly life cycle & number the stages (CD4 T27); Culture — act out «The ant and the grasshopper» (CD4 T28, work vs play); Phonics Hero finale — each child reads their line & earns the Diploma (p.91).
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values & culture through DOING (mime the life cycle, act the ant & grasshopper) not talking-at. The Phonics Diploma is a big proud moment — make it feel special. Every child cares-for-something, mimes the life cycle, and reads their diploma line.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say what I care for — “I care for little bugs!”;
  • mime & order the butterfly life cycle (egg → caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly);
  • act the ant & the grasshopper — work & play.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do we care for little bugs, or squash them?” → care for them!
“A caterpillar goes into a chrysalis and comes out as a…?” → a beautiful butterfly!

Assumptions: All the bug language is secure (L1–L5); reading is at its diploma celebration. Children handle three short blocks well if each is active & bridged by a song. Being curious / caring and the life cycle build on the whole unit. The ant-and-grasshopper drama recycles work/play, happy/sad, hungry.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Care for” too abstractMake it doable: mime stroking an animal, watering a flower, gently watching a bug — praise it live as caring!
Life cycle confusingDo it as a whole-body mime: tiny egg (curl) → hungry caterpillar (wriggle & grow) → still chrysalis (freeze) → butterfly (fly).
Culture block = just listeningEveryone is an ant (busy, carrying) or a grasshopper (playing) & acts the seasons — deep/light voices.
Diploma rushed / one child at a time = waitingAll read together first, then a quick carousel of one line each; everyone claps every reader.
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

17–25′
Values · caring for our world + song
carpet, standing

Talk & do. Do you care for our world? What do you care for? Elicit what children care for (nature · animals · bugs · family · friends). Make the point: we care for people, animals AND things.

Values song. Sing «I care for our world» (CD4 T26) — mime a tree, stroke a dog, hug yourself, flutter like a butterfly. Sing 2×.

 «I care for our world» (CD4 T26) · Speaky
25–33′
Content · butterfly life cycle mime ⚡
carpet, standing

Mime the life cycle. Show the four stages (egg on a leaf → caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly). Then mime it with your whole body: You're a tiny egg on a leaf… now a hungry caterpillar, eat and grow… now a chrysalis, stay VERY still… now — a beautiful butterfly! Fly!

Order it. children number the stages 1–4 in the air, then reprise the mime faster.

💪 Support: do the life-cycle mime. ⭐ Extension: name each stage — egg · caterpillar · chrysalis · butterfly!

 life-cycle picture cards ×4 · Speaky
33–43′
Culture · «The ant and the grasshopper» + act
carpet

Culture story. Play «The ant and the grasshopper» (CD4 T28) with the story cards. Pause: in summer the Ant works & the Grasshopper plays; in winter the Ant has food & is happy, the Grasshopper is hungry & sad.

▶ Act it out two groups — Ants (busy, carrying food, I'm busy!) and Grasshoppers (playing, I want to play!). Act the seasons: sunny summer → cold winter.

Think. Who's happy in winter? Who's sad? Was the Ant right? — a gentle talk about being ready & caring.

 story cards · «The ant and the grasshopper» (CD4 T28) · Speaky
43–53′
Table time · Values p.96 + Content p.97 🧘
tables

Colour what I care for. Play the values song (CD4 T26); children colour the frames of the pictures that show things they care for (PB p.96), then draw & say I care for my family. I care for little bugs.

Number the life cycle. children listen (CD4 T27) & number the stages of the butterfly life cycle 1–4, then draw & colour them (PB p.97).
