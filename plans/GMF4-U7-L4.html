Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name transport and make future predictions: “Robots will drive. / Submarines won't fly. / Trams will have wings in the future.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «In the future» (CD3 32); consolidate will / won't for predictions (built on L2–L3); sort transport by category (have wheels · fly · go to the moon · travel under the sea); a real prediction communication game (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on will / won't + base verb (not “wills fly”); model each transport word with a big TPR action; keep the game moving — short turns, everyone predicts.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 transport words (submarine · helicopter · hot-air balloon · motorbike · tram · underground);
  • make predictions with will & won't;
  • sort transport by what it does (wheels · fly · sea · moon).

Structure
Robots will drive.  ·  Submarines won't fly.  ·  Trams will have wings in the future.  ·  Motorbikes will travel under the sea.
Form
will + base verb (fly · drive · travel · have) for a positive prediction · won't (will not) + base verb for a negative one. Same form for all subjects: Cars will fly / Children won't go to school.
Pron. watch
submarine /ˈsʌbməriːn/; helicopter /ˈhelɪkɒptə/ (4 syll.); tram /træm/; underground /ˈʌndəɡraʊnd/; won't /wəʊnt/ (long /əʊ/).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Submarines won't ___ — fly or flies?” → fly (base verb after will/won't!).
“A submarine — does it fly or travel under the sea?” → travel under the sea.

Assumptions: Will there be…? & will/won't statements are active (L2–L3); the 6 transport words are new but very picturable. The switch to will/won't + base verb for predictions is the key stretch. Readers can read short transport words. (Book teaches 6 transport words — note КТП listed a different set: ferry/taxi/scooter/lorry/van.)

Listen, point and say (act. 1, CD3 31). Cards: Submarine! Helicopter! Hot-air balloon! Motorbike! Tram! Underground! Choral ×3 + a big TPR action each. Speed rounds — you say, they DO.

💪 Support: action carries the meaning. ⭐ Extension: read the transport word without the picture.

27–37′
Prediction communication game
desks, pairs → 2 teams

Model first. Show a transport + an action card. Ask 2 pupils publicly: Will trams have wings in the future? — Yes, they will! / No, they won't.

▶ Talk Partners (act. 4). Pairs match transport to future actions & make predictions: Motorbikes will travel under the sea. Helicopters won't go to the moon. Both write 4 predictions.

▶ Listen & match (team relay). You call a prediction — teams race to hold up the matching transport card & say the full sentence. Point per correct.

✔ Assess: circulate — is it “will/won't” + base verb? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: transport cards have the word printed. ⭐ Extension: add: but people won't drive them.
