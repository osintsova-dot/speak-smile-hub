👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

WB Unit 5 (pp.36–43) · notebook · points to the leaderboard next pair
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can voice a 60-sec «character trailer» about what their avatar CAN and CAN'T do, using 8+ ability collocations correctly (catch a ball · fly a plane · speak a language).
  • Subsidiary. Gate exposes the real ceiling — verb+noun collocations, not the verbs themselves; 22 abilities fixed at speed; Video skills (smartphone gadget crafts) watched with maker-tasks; can/can't used in context (no rules today — the rules pair is P3).
  • Personal. First lesson after the holidays: not “let's review can” but “let's launch the mission”. Acknowledge their GMF4 base out loud for 30 seconds, then raise the bar straight to collocations and connected speech. A brand-new unit leaderboard — a clean start for everyone.
By the end, students can…
  • say 8+ ability collocations without the wrong noun (catch a BALL, not catch a language)
  • ask and answer Can you …? questions with real follow-ups
  • react to a 'how to' video like a maker, not a viewer
  • deliver a 60-sec character trailer to the bar

Structure
Can you play a musical instrument? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't. · My character can climb mountains but he can't swim. · How fast can you run 100 metres?
Form
verb + its noun (collocation!): catch a ball · fly a plane · spell a word · speak a language · talk TO your friend · tell stories TO someone · play football / play the guitar.
Pron. watch
climb — silent b · talk /tɔːk/ — silent l · run /rʌn/ · can in a statement is weak /kən/ (TB pp.70, 74).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Speak or talk — Russian?” → SPEAK a language, TALK to a person.
“Catch a language?” → No! catch a BALL — the verb lives with its own word.
“In the trailer, is ‘he can run’ enough?” → No: run 100 metres in ten seconds — details give richness.

Assumptions: Post-GMF4: can/can't is already in speech, verbs of action are familiar, numbers are automatic. Real gaps: collocations (speak/talk, catch/carry), longer answers, accuracy in questions with can. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (question word order with can / comparative spelling / possessive pronouns), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
