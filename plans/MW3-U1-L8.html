Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a “My favourite number” Big Wheel pod — choosing a number 1–6, colouring it, and saying: “My favourite number is (three). It's (red)!” in a mini gallery.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle numbers · colours · greetings through a creative product; sing Genki «Numbers» (08) & «Colours» (52); take turns presenting; Phonics Hero — Part-1 finish + diploma, speed-read p.30–31; happy end-of-unit gallery.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the presenting sentence first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (one sentence each) so no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • choose & colour a favourite number pod;
  • present it — “My favourite number is three. It's red!”;
  • say “Well done!” to a friend's work.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“How many pods will you make — one favourite, or all six?” → one favourite!
“Your friend shows their work — what do we say?” → Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 1 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can count 1–6, name colours, and greet; reading has reached the Part-1 finish. They know classroom making routines.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Can't decide on a numberOffer a quick eeny-meeny or “point to your favourite pod” to choose.
Making takes over the lessonSimple pod outline ready; time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; a wave + showing the pod is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone claps & says Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentPresent in a fast carousel, one sentence each; or in small groups at once.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 12 · Part-1 finish + diploma — sounds all sounds — proud reading. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read your favourite words from the page — proud, fluent reading before the diploma! ▶ Diploma read — read a word to Speaky to earn your Part-1 Phonics Hero stamp.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.30–31 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read review Red tricky words together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).
