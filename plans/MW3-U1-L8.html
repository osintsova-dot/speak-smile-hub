🦝 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Starter block “Back to school!” · Lesson 8 — Review
Pre-A1 young learners (~6–7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · Review & consolidation (games-first) · carpet + desks
Starter · Lesson 8 of 8🏆 REVIEW + reward quiz~10 pupils · 2–3 teams🦸 Phonics: speed-reading review (JP Book 1 sounds)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated the “Classroom” topic (from Mimi's Wheel 2, Unit 1): name 8 classroom objects and ask & answer about possession — “Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't” · “Has Sam got…?”
  • Subsidiary. Interleave known language — colours + numbers 1–10 with objects (“How many red crayons?”); phonics review (recognise → blend → segment learned sounds, speed reading); listen for detail (songs); build confidence & “I can…” self-assessment before Unit 1.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every game; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; make everyone answer at once (Showdown / whiteboards / fingers) — no “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 8 classroom objects + their colours;
  • ask & answer “Have you got your…? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't”;
  • answer “Has Sam got…?” and count objects 1–10;
  • respond to “Go to the…” / “Point to the…” (school places & room parts);
  • read known sounds fast & blend a short word.
Target language
Lexis

Structure
Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.  ·  Have you got a (pencil)? — I've got (paper).  ·  Has Sam got (scissors)? — Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.  ·  How many (crayons)? — (Five).  ·  Go to the (playground). / Point to the (ceiling).
Form
Have + you + got + your + noun? → short answer with have / haven't. Connected “Have you” /həvjə/, “haven't” /ˈhævnt/.
Pron. watch
/z/ ending in crayons · scissors · paints (plural, not sung as /s/); /uː/ in glue; stress on got in the question.
Phonics
Speed-reading review of learned Jolly Phonics Book 1 sounds (s·a·t·i·p·n·c·k·e·h·r·m·d·g·o·u·l·f…): recognise → blend a short word (bag · pen · red) → segment. All-response cards, no new sound (Review lesson).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is the school bag YOURS?” → yes → “Have you got YOUR school bag?”
“I have it — do I say have or haven't?” → have.
“Sam is a boy meerkat — he has or she has?” → he has.

Assumptions: objects, colours & numbers 1–10 were met over Starter L4–L7 (Mimi's Wheel 2, Unit 1 “Classroom”) → this is consolidation, not new input. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → rely on pictures & audio, not printed text. Characters: Mimi, Dylan, Sam are meerkats (сурикаты), not puppies. Teams: max 4 per team; 10 = 3+3+4 · 12 = 3×4 · 8 = 2×4.

Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… boards down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

 Disco Warm Up mp3 · Speaky · carpet
3–9′
Retrieval warm-up ⚡
carpet, standing

Fast retrieval (memory science: warm the brain before you use it). Numbers & colours — everyone answers at once. Flash number cards 1–10: class shows that many fingers + says Five! Then flash a colour: all touch something that colour + say It's red!

▶ Go to the…! Point to the room's 4 corners = 4 school places (classroom · dining room · library · playground picture-signs). Call Go to the playground! — everyone runs to that corner. 4–5 fast calls, everyone moves every time.

▶ RallyRobin (in pairs): Take turns — say a colour, a number, a school thing. 30 sec, then swap. Signal: Class? — Yes!

 number cards 1–10 · colour cards · 4 place-signs (corners) · ▶ RallyRobin
9–11′
🎵 Bridge
carpet

Now — our borrowing song! Sing Genki «May I borrow your…?»; hold up real objects (glue, scissors, crayons) as they come → straight into the classroom-things review.

 Genki «May I borrow your…?» mp3 · real objects
11–20′
Phonics — speed-reading review ⚡
board → carpet

Speed reading (Review — no new sound). Letter/sound strip of learned Jolly Phonics Book 1 sounds — Read with me — fast! Whole class, choral, there and back. Twice, a little faster each time.

Blend it. Show 3–4 short words tied to today's topic — bag · pen · red · cut: Sound it out… b-a-g… bag! Class blends together, then segments back (say the word → tap each sound on fingers).

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: flash sound cards — if you can hear it in bag → stand tall; if not → crouch small. 8–10 cards, fast — every child moves every card.

 sound strip · CVC word cards (bag/pen/red/cut) · board
20–22′
🎵 Bridge
carpet

Get your paint hands ready — the paint song! Sing Genki «The paint song»; wave imaginary brushes, call the colours → into the objects + colours memo.

 Genki «The paint song» mp3
22–29′
▶ Memo — objects & colours
carpet, teams

Set up. Each team spreads a memo set face-down: picture cards of the 8 objects (some in different colours). Turn two cards. Say what you see!

▶ Memory (memo): a child flips two cards and must name them in a sentence to keep a pair: It's a red pencil. It's blue glue. Just a word = no pair, next player's turn. Match → keep it; most pairs wins.

ICQ: Do I say “pencil” or “It's a red pencil”? (→ full sentence!) Rotate turns clockwise so everyone plays.

 2–3 memo sets (objects × colours) · Speaky
29–37′
▶ “Have you got…?” relay ⚡
carpet → back wall

Model first (I do). 🦝 Speaky to a child: Have you got your school bag? Child: Yes, I have! / No, I haven't! Choral back-chain: …school bag? → got your school bag? → Have you got your school bag?

▶ Card-grab relay (teams): object cards spread on the far mat. You call Have you got a pencil? — the first runner from each team races, grabs the pencil card, runs back and says I've got a pencil! Correct card + full answer = 1 point. Teams cheer; rotate runners each round (everyone runs).

Add possession twist: hold Sam (meerkat puppet) with 2–3 cards — Has Sam got glue? Class answers together: Yes, he has! / No, he hasn't!

Reset if loud: Freeze!

 object cards (far mat) · Sam meerkat puppet + 2–3 cards · team point cards
37–39′
🎵 TPR switcher
carpet, standing

Hands up — Wind the bobbin up! Sing «Wind the bobbin up» with the actions (a calm-down after the relay). Then ▶ Point to the…! — Point to the door / window / floor / ceiling! Class parts of the room, everyone points, fast then slow to settle.

 «Wind the bobbin up» mp3
39–43′
📓 Match It 🧘
desks

Interactive notebook — Match It (oral, no MW3 cut-out sheet in the catalogue → teacher-made strip). Give each child a strip: 8 mini-pictures on the left, 8 words on the right (jumbled). Draw a line — picture to word. Say it as you match!

Check with a neighbour, then whole-class: point & chorus each pair. Fast finishers colour the pictures in the right colours.

Countdown. Finishing in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… pencils down.

 “Match It” strip (1 per pupil) · pencils / crayons
43–55′
🏆 ▶ Review Quiz Showdown + Penalty
carpet, teams

Flagship reward quiz (Review only). Kagan Showdown — everyone answers, then reveal. Teams sit together, each child with a mini-whiteboard/paper + marker. You ask; everyone draws/writes their answer face-down; on Showdown! all flip up together. Team agrees the answer.

Interleaved questions (mix all the block's topics so they must choose which to use — not blocked): ① object: Draw the scissors. ② colour+object: What colour is Mimi's pencil case? ③ number+object: How many crayons? (show a card) ④ grammar: Sam has NO glue — answer: “Has Sam got glue?” (No, he hasn't) ⑤ Have you got your school bag today? Yes/No! ⑥ place: You read books here — which place? (library) ⑦ room part: Point to the ceiling!

▶ Reward — Penalty kick: each correct team answer earns one soft-ball kick at the goal (or a bowling roll). Goal = ⚽ on the team scoreboard. Loud cheer — Goal! Well done, team!

Formative read (Wiliam): the Showdown reveal shows you at a glance who still mixes have/haven't or he has → re-model once, quickly, before closing.

 mini-whiteboards/paper + markers · question cards (mixed) · soft ball + toy goal / mini bowling · ⚽ scoreboard
55–58′
