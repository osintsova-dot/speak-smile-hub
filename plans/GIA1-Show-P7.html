📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, every learner has hosted a stand (welcome → game → praise) and visited all other stands asking questions in English — the language of every unit of the year run through live.
  • Subsidiary. The fair passport drives guests through EVERY stand (a stamp = played + asked a question); token voting by category; detailed feedback and points to the season leaderboard.
  • Personal. Celebration day #3: my role is timekeeper and «minister of atmosphere», not controller. Fair noise is working noise; the one-clap signal brings back silence in 3 seconds when needed.
By the end, students can…
  • host: welcome, explain, run, praise — in English
  • visit: play and ask questions at every stand
  • vote with reasons
  • give feedback with the axes

Target language
Lexis
Welcome to our stand!Try our quiz! · Have a go!Guess…!You win a point!I like the part about…My question is…Can you tell us more about…?stamp, please! · Here's your stamp!I vote for… because…
Structure
Host: Welcome! The rule is… Well done — here's your stamp! Guest: Can I try? · My question is… · I vote for the Unit 5 stand because the game was smart.
Form
a stamp is given ONLY for: played + 1 question in English; a vote = category + a because-reason.
Pron. watch
the fairground voice — loud, but not a shout; a guest's question — full (not «why?»).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«A stamp just for coming over?» → No: play + ask. No stamps for passive guests.
«Can I vote for my friends?» → For the stand, not the friendship — and with a because-reason.
«Can a host go off and play?» → During their own session — no: a stand with no host is dead. Your game is in the other session.

Assumptions: Stands are ready (P6 + homework), the 2-session layout is clear. If external guests turn up — registrars hand them passports and a mini guest-phrase cheat sheet.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
A crowd at one stand, empty at anotherThe passport requires ALL the stamps; the timekeeper announces «free stands»; I direct the flow personally.
Hosts switch to Russian with guest-friendsThe stamp rule: the question and the game are in English, otherwise no stamp; I go round and listen.
A stand's game breaks/runs out of propsExpress fix: simplify the rule down to a spoken version; my spare craft box in the corner.
Voting turns into team collusionTokens go into closed boxes + a because-reason out loud when handing it in — collusion fizzles out.
External guests are shyRegistrars hand them the guest cheat sheet (3 phrases) and walk them to the first stand personally.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
