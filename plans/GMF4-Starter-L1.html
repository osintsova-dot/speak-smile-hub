A2 young learners (~10 y.o., readers · continuing line) · 60 min · PPP + TPR + Talk Partners · carpet + desks
Week 03.09–06.09 · Lesson 1✨ FIRST LESSON of the year~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.4 · AB p.2
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to give information about other people — “His name's (Luke). He's (23). He's a (scientist). He likes (travelling).” — and ask the four 3rd-person questions: “What's his/her name? How old is he/she? What does he/she do? What does he/she like doing?”
  • Subsidiary. Meet the Level-4 characters again (Lily, Josh, Ravi & big brother Luke); re-establish routines & signals after the summer; sing «This is my best friend» (CD1 02); retrieve Level 3 vocabulary (sea animals · water sports · water-sports equipment · free-time activities · numbers · food · places) via the «Find and say» mind-map.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; re-train the signals patiently — this lesson sets the tone for the whole year. Give extra wait-time and picture support — Level-3 words are rusty after summer.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask & answer the four 3rd-person questions about a person;
  • say 4 facts about someone (name · age · job · likes);
  • retrieve Level 3 vocabulary in 7 categories.

Structure
What's his/her name? — His/Her name's (Luke).  ·  How old is he/she? — He's (23).  ·  What does he/she do? — He's a (scientist).  ·  What does he/she like doing? — He likes (travelling).
Form
Wh-word + is (name/age) OR does + he/she (job/likes)…? Answers: His/Her name's · He's + age/job · He likes + verb-ing. 3rd person -s: he likes, she does.
Pron. watch
scientist /ˈsaɪəntɪst/; contraction name's /neɪmz/; he's /hiːz/; -ing endings unstressed (tra-vel-ling).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“What does he do? — do I answer with his age or his JOB?” → his job (He's a scientist).
“He likes travelling — one person or many? Which word shows it?” → one person → likes.

Assumptions: Pupils finished GMF3 in May: Level 3 vocabulary (sea animals, water sports, food, places, numbers) is passive after the summer — plan extra recall time. 3rd-person questions existed receptively; production is the stretch. They read & write short sentences (readers). Carpet & team routines need re-training after the break.

19–29′
PB p.4 · Meet the people
carpet → desks

Open your books at page 4. Josh, Lily and Ravi are waiting for their big brother Luke. Listen & read, act out (act. 2, CD1 01). Point to each speech bubble: What's his name? — His name's Luke. How old is he? — He's 23. What does he do? — He's a scientist.

Form focus (board). Write the four Qs & answers; circle is (name/age) vs does (job/likes): How old IS he? What DOES he do? and the 3rd-person -s in likes.

ICQ: For “what does he do” — do I say his age or his JOB? (→ his job!)

29–37′
Talk about my family
carpet, pairs

Talk Partners (act. 4). Model with a strong pupil about YOUR relative, then pairs ask & answer about someone in their family: What's his name? How old is he? What does he do? What does he like doing? Swap on your clap.

Sing (act. 3, CD1 02). «This is my best friend» — listen & point to each friend, then sing verse for one character with a gesture.

💪 Support: answer with 2 facts (name + age). ⭐ Extension: add: He likes taking photos. with the -s.

37–46′
Person-card carousel
mingle

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Each child gets a person card (a made-up name · age · job · a “likes” picture). Music → mingle; music off → pair: A asks a 3rd-person question, B answers as the card — then swap, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges — everyone asks & answers many times, no spectators.

Find-someone twist. Find a card person who is a doctor! Find someone who likes swimming!

✔ Assess: stand in the flow — can they choose is/does correctly and keep the -s? Note names for tomorrow's recycling.
