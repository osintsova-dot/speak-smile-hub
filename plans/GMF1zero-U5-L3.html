Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow and understand the story “Animal magic” (Pupil's Book pp. 52–53, CD2 42) and act it out using key story language: “Can you see the cat? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.”, “Is it a cow? — Yes, it is. But cows can't fly!”, “This sheep can climb trees.”, “Bad luck, Toby!”.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — farm animals + “Can you see a…? / Is it a…?” (L1–L2) — woven into the story work (spot the animals, question the magic); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.27 (b) закрепление: oa·ow·o-e (sort → dictation → tricky they, eight → story «Коза и лодка»); value: everyone is good at something; preview action verbs + can/can't for Lesson 4 (this sheep can climb, cows can't fly).
  • Personal. Keep story listening active (gesture responses, prediction); manage drama noise with Freeze; every child acts in a group at the same time — no “2 on stage, 10 watching” until the final 1-min showcase.
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they understand the story (order the frames, answer with gestures);
  • say the key lines in role: “But cows can't fly!”, “This sheep can climb trees.”;
  • read, sort & write oa/ow/o-e words (goat, snow, home, road) and chorus the story «Коза и лодка»;
  • notice that every animal can do something special (value).
Target language
Lexis

Structure
Can you see the (cat)? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't. · Is it a (cow)? — Yes, it is. But cows can't fly! · This sheep can climb trees. · Dogs can't climb trees. Bad luck, Toby!
Form
can / can't + verb: cows can't fly · this sheep can climb (receptive here — meaning through gesture; full focus in L4).
Pron. watch
keep -n't in can't; /оу/ one sound in goat, snow; sentence stress: Cows CAN'T fly.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.27 (b) закрепление: oa /оу/ · ow · o-e — read & sort goat · boat · snow · home · road · nose · dictation goat, snow, home, road · tricky strip they, eight · story «Коза и лодка» (тетрадь стр.76) · sight words: they, eight.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Frame with the flying cow: “Is it a cow?” → “Yes, it is.” “Do cows really fly?” → “No! Cows can't fly — it's MAGIC!”
The sheep in the tree: “Can this sheep climb?” → “Yes! This sheep can climb trees.”
Toby tries to climb: “Can dogs climb trees?” → “No! Dogs can't climb. Bad luck, Toby!”

Assumptions: farm animals + Can you see a…? known from L1–L2; children know Beth, Jake, Molly & the magic bike from earlier units. Emergent readers → they FOLLOW the pictures while listening; the teacher points, children don't read the bubbles alone. fly/swim/climb + can/can't are met here in context and studied properly next lesson. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Speed reading. The full strip up to oa · ow · o-e: There and back — beat your time! Goat recall: /оу/ — goat! boat! snow!

Read & sort — тетрадь стр.76 №1–2 (all point first). Read each word together (goat · boat · snow · home · road · nose), everyone points to the right house (oa / ow / o-e) → write it in the column. Neighbour-check: Goat — oa? — Yes!

Dictation — стр.76 №3 (short, story day). 4 words once each: goat · snow · home · road → segment on fingers (/g/ /oa/ /t/ — goat!) → write → Show me! Tricky strip: they · eight — there and back.

📺 Story — listen & follow
carpet, books/screen

Before you read (PB p. 52). Big story picture on screen / books open. Pre-teach with gesture: magic (wand sparkle) · climb (climbing hands) · Shoo! (wave the cat away).

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — “How many animals?” ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 find-it-u5
Screen: the U5 “Find it!” engine on the big story picture. · How: Speaky asks, a child taps an animal; it lights up. · Pupils: whole class points in their book / calls it — A cow! A sheep! A cat! Toby! — then chorus the count. · Teacher: 🦝 How many animals? Point! — 4–5 things, quick. · Offline reserve: no projector → point in the open Pupil's Book (p. 52), pairs count 15 sec & chorus the number.

Predict (thinking) — before we listen. The magic bike is here! What will the animals do? Fly? Swim? Climb? Thumbs vote. Let's listen and check your guess!

Listen 1 — global (CD2 42). “Listening hands” behind ears. Teacher points frame by frame; children follow the pictures. Gist check: Is the cow flying? Show me! (thumbs) And Toby — is he happy at the end?

Detail check — True or False? (thumbs up / down, all react). Say a sentence, class shows a thumb: The cow can fly. (👍 — magic!) · The sheep can climb trees. (👍) · Dogs can climb trees. (👎 — Bad luck, Toby!) · It's a horse. (👎 — a cow!). Re-point the frame to fix any wrong thumb.

Listen 2 — active (all respond). Play again; children do the story gestures at the right moments: magic sparkle ✨, flying cow 🐄, climbing sheep 🐑, poor Toby 🐕. CCQs between frames (see box above). At the flying frame stop: Is it a cow? — Yes, it is! But cows can't…? — FLY!

43–51′
Act out — drama
carpet, trios

Roles. Trios: Beth (red badge) · Jake / Molly (green) · the magic animal + Toby (blue). Teacher narrates and models once with a strong trio.

All trios act at the same time. Narrate slowly, frame by frame; every trio plays it in their own spot (magic on the spot!): Can you see the cat? — Yes, I can! — Abracadabra! — Look! Is it a cow? — Yes, it is. But cows can't fly! — And this sheep — it can climb trees! — Toby wants to climb… — No, Toby! Dogs can't climb trees. Bad luck, Toby!

Create (thinking) — make it yours. Before round 2: Your turn — choose an animal and a MAGIC power! Each trio invents a line — a swimming horse, a flying frog: Abracadabra! This horse can swim! Amazing! (support: point to an animal card + gesture; extension: invent the whole exchange).

Swap roles, run it again (2 rounds). Volunteers: ONE trio shows 30 sec for the class — big applause. Value (PB p. 53): Every animal is good at something — and so are YOU!

Support: quieter children get Toby (short line Bad luck! + gesture) + a line card in hand. Extension: confident children play Beth / narrate and lead the exchange.
