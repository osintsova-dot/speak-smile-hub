Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask about the obligations of a job: “Do you have to (wear a uniform)? / Does he have to (go to university)?” and answer “Yes, I do / he does. / No, I don't / he doesn't.”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Josh & Lily's dialogue about being a vet / a carpenter (CD3 45); discover the rule — Do with I/you/we/they, Does with he/she + have to + verb; recycle the 12 jobs from L1; write questions & short answers about jobs (AB).
  • Personal. Drill the Do/Does contrast until it's automatic; keep have to + bare verb visible (no “to wear” after have to → “have to wear”); model the full short answer, recast one-word replies.
By the end, pupils can…
  • choose Do (I/you/we/they) vs Does (he/she) correctly;
  • ask “… have to …?” about a job and give a Yes/No short answer;
  • talk about what different jobs have to do.

Structure
Do you have to wear a uniform? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  ·  Does she have to go to university? — Yes, she does.  ·  Do nurses have to work at night? — Yes, they do.
Form
Do + I/you/we/they + have to + verb? · Does + he/she + have to + verb? Short answers: Yes, I do / he does · No, I don't / she doesn't. (have to = obligation, not choice.)
Pron. watch
have to /ˈhæftə/ (unvoiced “haf-tuh”, not “hav too”); does /dʌz/; weak do you /djə/ at speed; uniform /ˈjuːnɪfɔːm/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Does she ___ go to university? — have to or has to?” → have to (Does already shows 3rd person).
“You have to wear a uniform — is it your choice or a rule?” → a rule — you must.

Assumptions: 12 jobs are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up). have to met before receptively; forming the question with Do/Does + choosing the right auxiliary is today's stretch. Pupils can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar table.

Job interview info-gap
desks, pairs

Model first. Show two half-fact-files (A knows a firefighter's rules, B knows a vet's). Model publicly: Does a firefighter have to wear a uniform? — Yes, he does.

▶ Talk Partners info-gap (act. 4-style). Pairs ask & answer to complete the missing rules — Do they have to work at night? Does she have to go to university? Both tick Yes/No. 5–6 exchanges.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — who chooses Do/Does correctly + keeps have to? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: question stems printed on the half-sheet. ⭐ Extension: add a reason — Yes, because it's dangerous.
