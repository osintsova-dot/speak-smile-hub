Week 18.01–24.01 · Занятие 37✨ VOCAB+GRAM · fruit~12 pupils · groups of 4 + circlePB p.44 · AB p.41🦸 Phonics Hero ур.25a · /и:/ ee·ea·e-e·y
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 fruits — mango, grape, peach, cherry, lemon, coconut (Pupil's Book p. 44) — and ask & answer “Do you like (grapes)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”, running a fruit survey.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — foods (L1), “Do you like…? — Yes, I do / No, I don't” (L2), Genki fruit — woven into the survey (“Do you like cherries? What about you?”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.25 (a) первое знакомство: /и:/ spelled ee · ea · e-e · y (see, tree, sea, tea, these, happy, тетрадь стр.69); a fruit-market game.
  • Personal. Keep plural fruit + the short answer right (“Do you like grapes? — Yes, I do”); give an ICQ before every game; every task keeps ALL children responding (own survey grid / show-me / all groups play at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 6 fruits;
  • ask & answer “Do you like (grapes)? — Yes, I do / No, I don't”;
  • survey friends & report a favourite fruit;
  • read & blend /и:/ words (see, tree, sea, tea, these, happy).
Target language
Lexis
mangograpepeachcherrylemoncoconut (recycled: chicken, pizza, rice, salad, fish, pasta, cake, ice cream)
Structure
Do you like (grapes)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't. · I like / I don't like (peaches). · Talk Partners: Do you like cherries? — Yes, I do. What about you?
Form
Do + you + like + noun (plural) + ? → short answers Yes, I do / No, I don't (not just yes/no). Fruit go plural: grapes, peaches, cherries.
Pron. watch
rising ↗ on “Do you like…?”; plural endings — /s/ in grapes vs an extra beat /ɪz/ in peaches · cherries; coconut = /ˈkəʊkənʌt/ (3 beats).
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.25 (a) первое знакомство: /и:/ = ee (see, tree) · ea (sea, tea) · e-e (these) · y (happy), тетрадь стр.69 · sight words: Повт. Green set.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
One grape vs a bunch: “Do you like grape or grapes?” → grapes (fruit go plural).
Sour lemon face 😖 vs sweet mango 😋: “Do you like lemons? — No, I don't!”
Phonics: “see and sea and happy — same sound?” → yes, /и:/! four spellings, one sound.

Assumptions: foods + “Do you like…?” solid from L1–L2; Genki fruit met earlier. Phonics: /эй/ consolidated last lesson; today the /и:/ family opens. Emergent readers → picture cards & survey grids, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Singular fruit: “Do you like grape?”Show a bunch; add the /s/ with a hiss; drill grapes · cherries · peaches as a plural chant.
Bare “yes / no” answerDrill the pair — nod Yes, I do, shake No, I don't; Speaky refuses a bare “yes”.
Four /и:/ spellings confuse (ee/ea/e-e/y)“Four spellings, one sound /и:/ — a big smile!”; sort by picture; happy-face gesture for y.
Survey drifts / children wanderFixed “ask 4 friends” target on a mini grid; music start/stop; RallyRobin roles.
Memory game: one child hogs the cardsStrict turn circle in the group of 4; “flip two, everyone says the sentence together”.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Cognitive load: only the 6 fruit words are truly NEW — “Do you like…? — Yes, I do / No, I don't” came last lesson, so the fruit vocab + plurals carry the challenge (scaffold: plural chant, survey grid, sentence frame). The phonics is FIRST MEETING — meet /и:/ gently, no dictation today. Buffers to drop first if short: Memory round 2, the 3rd survey partner. Better every child asks one full Do you like cherries? well than six stages half-done. Offline reserve: every 🎮 has a paper twin (flashcards / cards).

32–38′
▶ Fruit memory
desks, groups of 4

▶ Fruit Memory (= our Игротека «Мемори»; all groups play simultaneously). Each group of 4: 12 cards face-down — 6 fruit pairs.

Turn order round the group: flip two cards, name BOTH aloud — Grapes… grapes — a pair! I like grapes! Match found → keep it (whole group repeats the sentence chorally). No match → face-down again. ICQ: Do you just flip, or SAY the fruit? (→ say it!)

Most pairs wins a 🦝 sticker.

💪 Support: start with only 8 cards (4 pairs) and let the child say the fruit only. ⭐ Extension: round 2 — add Do you like…? — Yes, I do! each flip.

🎮 Digital option (projector): food-memo — S&S memory pairs on screen, 1–4 teams: a rotating child flips two cards, the whole class names every fruit aloud. Swap it in for the whole-class warm-round.
