Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name Mimi's family (Mummy · Daddy · Mimi · Dylan) and the Big Wheel, count 1–6, and match numbers to colours: “Where's number (one)? — It's on (red).”
  • Subsidiary. Greet with “Hello, how are you? — I'm fine, thank you”; sing the Big Wheel song (CD1 T17) + Genki «Numbers 1 To 12» (08) with actions; Phonics Hero — review sounds s a t i p n c k e, blend CVC + speed-read p.3–9; stick number stickers & circle a favourite number (PB p.3).
  • Personal. Tiny teacher-talk, big TPR — let Speaky do the talking. Genki recipe: disco first, phonics early, input through curiosity, energy up in the games then down for the table. Accept non-verbal answers this early — smiles first, words next.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name Mimi's family and the Big Wheel;
  • count from one to six in English;
  • say a colour for a number — “It's on red.”

Structure
Hello, how are you? — I'm fine, thank you.  ·  Where's number (one)? — It's on (red).
Form
Where's = Where is. Answer: It's on + colour. Numbers 1–6; colours as single words (no article).
Pron. watch
colour words clear & short; count crisply one–two–three; the /ð/ in the Big Wheel.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 (our reading workbook) — review sounds s a t i p n c k e with the Jolly gesture, blend CVC (s-a-t → sat) with a meaning picture, then speed-read p.3–9. Children already know the 26 sounds — this is learning to READ (GMF1-style).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Show me number three — how many fingers?” → three fingers up.
“Red — is it a number or a colour?” → a colour!

Assumptions: First lesson of the year. Children may know a few colours/numbers passively; the family words & the Big Wheel are new. They already know all 26 phonic sounds — reading starts now. Pre-readers of text — everything lands through pictures, song, movement & the puppet.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Wriggling / can't sit long on the carpetKeep every stage 5–8 min; use Freeze! and the volume-arm; a movement stage always follows a sitting one.
Too shy to speakThe puppet asks, not you; non-verbal (point / wave / show fingers) is a full answer at this stage.
Colours & numbers get mixed upAlways pair them on the Big Wheel — point to the pod, chorus number three… it's on orange.
Answers in RussianAccept warmly, recast in English once with the puppet — don't correct, just model.
Sticker frenzyStickers only at the table, one at a time, on your countdown: 5…4…3…
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lessons 1–3 — sounds s · a · t · i · p · n · c · k · e. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds into a word: s-a-t → sat, p-i-n → pin, c-a-t → cat — big arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Blend & jump — Speaky says a CVC word slowly; children blend it with the actions and jump on the whole word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.3–9 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read the · a · I · is together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.3–9 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · family + wheel
carpet → Big Wheel mat

Guess who! Slowly slide a family flashcard out of an envelope — Who's this? Guess! Children guess; reveal + BIG wave: Hello, Mimi! Do all four (Mummy · Daddy · Mimi · Dylan) + the Big Wheel, whole class echoes.

Numbers 1–6 + colours. Point to each coloured pod — big gesture per colour, then count the pods together: What colour? Red! What number? One!… one, two, three, four, five, six!

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the interactive Big Wheel (numbers 1–6 on coloured pods) on screen — children point & answer It's on red!. The spinning wheel itself comes from the coursebook app; our songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: point & copy the puppet. ⭐ Extension: say the full It's on red.

25–30′
Game · Island Hopping (23)
carpet

▶ Island Hopping Genki Island Hopping: each child gets a little pile of family/colour mini-cards (their “island”). 🦝 Speaky calls Show me… Mimi! Show me… red! — children who have it hold it UP and say it; everyone else copies.

Calm, seated start — all cards up every round, nobody waits. Speed up the calls.

 family & colour mini-cards (a set per child)
30–36′
Game · Dinosaur Danger! (12) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Dinosaur Danger! Genki Dinosaur Danger!: children on the “safe” side. 🦝 Speaky holds up a pod card: children make the sentence It's on red! — then when Speaky roars Dinosaur!, everyone runs to the other safe side. Freeze on landing.

Number-then-colour calls link the two: Number three… it's on orange! Slow-mo + Freeze keep it safe.

✔ Assess: who says It's on + colour, who only runs? Note it — no pressure, just for next time.

 Big Wheel pod cards · Speaky
36–42′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Speaky's Family» aloud — our mascot's own family (Mummy · Daddy · brothers · sister · Grandma · Grandpa). A warm, calm read after the running game — bonds with Speaky and recycles today's family words.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Hello, Mummy! Hello, Daddy!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Point to each family member — children wave and say Hello, (Mummy)!; count the meerkats on the last page (1→6) to fold in today's numbers.

Song — Big Wheel + Genki Numbers
carpet, standing

Sing & move. Play the Big Wheel song (CD1 T17). Teach the actions: big circle arms (Big Wheel), 1–2–3 fingers, point to flashcards then to yourself, wave hello, thumbs-up on I'm fine, thank you! Sing it 3× — normal, super-quiet, giant voice.

Count & jump. Play Genki «Numbers 1 To 12» (08) — jump & count, showing fingers 1→6. A pure counting boost on today's numbers.
