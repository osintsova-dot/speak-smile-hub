📋 Справка для подготовки (читать ДО урока, не в классе)
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can ask and answer present continuous Yes/No and Wh- questions accurately and get real information at an information desk using the unit Key phrases.
  • Subsidiary. Порядок слов (be ПЕРЕД подлежащим), краткие ответы без сокращения в +; Wh- вопросы; Grammar round-up (кумулятивный U1–6); Key phrases + Real-world grammar «We're staying near there»; интонация и ударение в Wh-вопросах.
  • Personal. Их слабое место не понимание, а ПОРЯДОК СЛОВ на письме и «Yes, he's» вместо «Yes, he is». Держать соревновательную рамку допроса — вопросы должны быть нужны им, чтобы выиграть.
By the end, students can…
  • build Yes/No questions with be before the subject
  • give correct short answers (Yes, he is / No, they aren't)
  • ask Wh- questions with correct word order and stress
  • ask for information about a place: times, price, how to get there

Structure
Are you having a good time? — Yes, we are. · Is she wearing glasses? — No, she isn't. · What are you doing? · Where are you sitting? · I'd like some information about this exhibition.
Form
Wh- questions: question word + be + subject + verb-ing? → What is she doing? · Where are you going? · Краткие ответы: Yes, he IS (без сокращения!) / No, he ISN'T (с сокращением).
Pron. watch
ABCD TB p.92: в Wh-вопросах интонация ПАДАЮЩАЯ, ударение на информационном слове: What TIME is it open? · How MUCH does it cost? · HOW can I get there?
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“You are wearing? — вопрос?” → No: ARE YOU wearing? (be перед подлежащим).
“Yes, he's — можно?” → No! В утвердительном коротком ответе полная форма: Yes, he is.
“What she is doing?” → No — What IS SHE doing? (be сразу после вопросительного слова).
“We're staying near there — они там живут?” → No, временно: приехали на несколько дней.

Assumptions: Вопросы с be для них не новость (U1), но present continuous questions письменно + короткие ответы — дыра; Key phrases информационного окна — новый функциональный блок для обеих линий. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (-ing spelling / missing ‘be’ / question word order), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
