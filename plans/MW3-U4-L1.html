Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name six wild animals (elephant · parrot · lion · hippo · crocodile · monkey), ask & answer «What's that? — It's a (lion)!», and match each animal to its colour on the Big Wheel: «Where's the (elephant)? — It's on (red).»
  • Subsidiary. Play Memory with the animals on the wheel (CD2 T1–3); sing Genki «Do You Like Animals?» (13) & «I Like Animals» (15) with actions; Phonics Hero — Magic E i-e, blend & speed-read p.58–60; stick the wild-animal stickers & circle the animals you like (PB p.21).
  • Personal. Tiny teacher-talk, big TPR — let Speaky do the talking. Use the feely bag of toy animals (Teacher's tip) so children identify by touch first. Genki recipe: disco first, phonics early, input through curiosity. Accept non-verbal answers — smiles first, words next.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name six wild animals (elephant · parrot · lion · hippo · crocodile · monkey);
  • ask & answer «What's that? — It's a (monkey)!»;
  • say which colour an animal is on — «It's on red.»

Form
What's that? Answer: It's a/an + animal. Colour link: It's on + colour. Six animals; colours as single words.
Pron. watch
clear animal words; an elephant (a/an); crisp colour words; the /ð/ in the hippo.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 (our reading workbook) — Magic E: i-e: the silent e makes the i say its name (bike · kite · five). Recognise the pattern, blend CVCe, then speed-read p.58–60. Children already know the 26 sounds — this is reading.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I hold up a lion — do you say It's a lion or hello lion?” → It's a lion!
“The elephant is on RED — is red a colour or an animal?” → a colour!

Assumptions: First lesson of the unit. Children know colours & the Big Wheel «It's on…» frame from Unit 1 (checked in the warm review). The six animals are new but very picturable. Magic E reading continues from earlier units. Pre-readers of text — everything lands through pictures, song, movement & the puppet.

Wriggling / can't sit long on the carpetKeep every stage 5–8 min; use Freeze! and the volume-arm; a movement stage always follows a sitting one.
Too shy to say the animalThe puppet asks, not you; non-verbal (point / mime the animal) is a full answer at this stage.
a / an muddle (an elephant)Don't drill the rule — model warmly: an elephant, an with the puppet; children just echo.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Look — a big wheel full of WILD animals! Grrr! Hold up the Big Wheel mat with the animal flashcards.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

Colour chorus. point to a Big Wheel pod → whole class chorus the colour: It's on red! It's on blue! Recycles Unit 1's «It's on…» before we add the animals.

▶ Colour flash. flash a colour → children who wear that colour wave. A quick, all-in colour warm before the wild animals land on the wheel.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 21 · Magic E — sounds Magic E: i-e (long i). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds, then add the magic e: b-i-ke → bike, k-i-te → kite, f-i-ve → five — the e makes the i say its name. ▶ Magic-E blend & jump

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.58–60 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read after · cover together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.58–60 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · feely bag + Big Wheel
carpet → Big Wheel mat

Guess by touch! Slowly pull a toy animal out of the feely bag — What's that? Guess! Children feel/guess, reveal + name it BIG: It's a lion! Grrr! Do all six (elephant · parrot · lion · hippo · crocodile · monkey), whole class echoes.

Animals + colours. Place each animal on its colour pod, then chorus the link: Where's the elephant? — It's on red! Where's the monkey? — It's on orange!

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): open the Navio App (coursebook app) on screen — the vocabulary game, the songs, and the «The surprise» story video. Children point & answer It's an elephant!. The app plays the audio/video; our Genki songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: point & copy the puppet. ⭐ Extension: say the full It's a lion. It's on yellow.

25–30′
Game · Island Hopping (23)
carpet

▶ Island Hopping Genki Island Hopping: each child gets a little pile of animal mini-cards (their “island”). 🦝 Speaky calls Show me… a lion! Show me… a hippo! — children who have it hold it UP and say it; everyone else copies.

Calm, seated start — all cards up every round, nobody waits. Speed up the calls.

 animal mini-cards (a set per child) · Speaky
30–36′
Game · Dinosaur Danger! (12) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Dinosaur Danger! Genki Dinosaur Danger! (Genki's own «I like animals» game): children on the “safe” side. 🦝 Speaky holds up an animal card: children make the sentence It's a crocodile! — then when Speaky roars Dinosaur!, everyone runs to the other safe side. Freeze on landing.

Link animal + colour in the call: It's a parrot… it's on blue! Slow-mo + Freeze keep it safe.

✔ Assess: who says It's a + animal, who only runs? Note it — no pressure, just for next time.

 animal flashcards · Speaky
36–42′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Speaky's Noisy Farm» aloud — Speaky meets one noisy animal after another and asks what each one is — a calm, cuddly read after the running game that recycles What's that? It's a… and animal SOUNDS, warming up for our wild-animal party.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line What's that? — It's a…!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Point to each animal — children guess What's that?, name it and make its sound; wild or farm, it's all It's a…! practice with our best animal faces.
