WHAT WE DO: 60 seconds: as many TRUE sentences about their own family as they can with 've got/'s got/haven't/hasn't got; the clean ones are counted

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I say: Write as many TRUE sentences about your family as you can. Only contracted have got: 've got, 's got, haven't got, hasn't got. Sixty seconds — GO!
  2. From zero: a rule card: apostrophe in place? he/she → HAS? We count ONLY the clean ones, tally in the margin.
  3. The turn: Six clean? Three? You've SPOKEN have got for years — writing it fast and clean is the licence. Rematch at the end of the lesson.

The apostrophe case: possessive 's
🔥 Challenge · apostrophe crime scene
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: possessive 's as a case: the rule ex.4 in 2 min → duel ex.5 (mum's/grandparents'/cousins') → the Will Smith text ex.6 on the 5-second rule

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. The rule ex.4 as a sprint: Match the rules — two minutes. Singular owner → 's. Plural in -s → just the apostrophe AFTER.
  2. A sorting ritual: every 's on the board meets the question: Owner or verb? My dad's car — owner! He's got — verb!
  3. Duel ex.5 on mini-boards: mums → mum's · grandparents → grandparents' · dads → dad's · cousins → cousins' · parents → parents' · Lucys → Lucy's. 10 sec per item.
  4. Ex.6 Will Smith on the 5-sec rule with justification: sons' — WHY? Two sons! (TB key: 's · 've · sons' · 's · hasn't · daughter's · Will's · hasn't).
📺 On screen: the SB p.28 · mini-boards · board: 2 rules + the “owner or verb?” question (stays up)
👦 Students do: they fix apostrophes at speed and justify each one. Expected: “cousins-apostrophe — TWO cousins!”. Typical error: childrens' — “children is already plural WITHOUT -s → children's” (TB note).

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can file flawless have got + possessive 's sentences at speed (Act 1, 45′) and crack the ‘impossible twins’ listening case using 13 body/face words (Act 2, 45′).
  • Subsidiary. Gate exposes the written have-got ceiling; both tables closed in a sprint; apostrophe crime scene ('s/s'); Brain teaser race; body vocab + hair rule (length–type–colour); subskill using what you know; 60-sec case report to the bar.
  • Personal. Grammar is NEVER the announced topic — the case is. The KTP holds the boundary: lesson A (grammar) = exactly 0–45′, lesson B (lexis+listening) = 45–90′. Collect live errors silently for the clinic.
By the end, students can…
  • write 8+ clean have-got sentences in 60 sec
  • place 's/s' correctly and explain the difference
  • name 13 body/face words at duel speed
  • report the twin case fluently for 60 sec

Structure

Form
've got / 's got, haven't/hasn't got; 's = owner (singular), s' = owners (plural ending in -s); hair order: length–type–colour (long, curly, brown hair).
Pron. watch
half /hɑːf/ · moustache /məˈstɑːʃ/ · beard /bɪəd/ · eye /aɪ/; unstressed have/has in questions.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“You've SAID ‘I've got’ for four years. Can you write eight clean lines in 60 seconds?” → gate answers.
“my brothers' room — how many brothers?” → two or more; apostrophe AFTER the s.
“Curly brown long hair — OK?” → No: length–type–colour. Long, curly, brown.

Assumptions: have got aff/neg 100% familiar orally (GMF4). Real ceilings: apostrophes in writing ('s got vs possessive 's!), s' plural rule, body-vocab set as a system. Listening at natural speed = genuine challenge. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
