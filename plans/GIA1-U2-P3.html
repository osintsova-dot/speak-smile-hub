ЧТО ДЕЛАЕМ: possessive 's как дело: правило ex.4 за 2 мин → дуэль ex.5 (mum's/grandparents'/cousins') → Will Smith текст ex.6 по правилу 5 сек

👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Правило ex.4 спринтом: Match the rules — two minutes. Singular owner → 's. Plural in -s → just the apostrophe AFTER.
  2. Сорт-ритуал: каждое 's на доске встречает вопрос: Owner or verb? My dad's car — owner! He's got — verb!
  3. Дуэль ex.5 на мини-досках: mums → mum's · grandparents → grandparents' · dads → dad's · cousins → cousins' · parents → parents' · Lucys → Lucy's. 10 сек на пункт.
  4. Ex.6 Will Smith по 5-сек правилу с обоснованием: sons' — WHY? Two sons! (ключ TB: 's · 've · sons' · 's · hasn't · daughter's · Will's · hasn't).

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“You've SAID ‘I've got’ for four years. Can you write eight clean lines in 60 seconds?” → gate answers.
“my brothers' room — how many brothers?” → two or more; apostrophe AFTER the s.
“Curly brown long hair — OK?” → No: length–type–colour. Long, curly, brown.

Assumptions: have got aff/neg 100% familiar orally (GMF4). Real ceilings: apostrophes in writing ('s got vs possessive 's!), s' plural rule, body-vocab set as a system. Listening at natural speed = genuine challenge. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.
