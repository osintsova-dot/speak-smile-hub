WHAT WE DO: ex.4 adjectives in context + the game “opposites with your body” (TB extra): we show and name

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

WHAT WE DO: stations per the syllabus: 4 corners × 4–5 min with rotation — spelling · categories · collocations · time; peer help

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Station 1 “Spelling desk”: unit photo cards — lay out the letter tiles/write the word; pairs inside the team check each other.
  2. Station 2 “Category sort”: a stack of the year's words — sort them into the 9 categories (envelopes).
  3. Station 3 “Verb match”: cards do/play/have/take/go + nouns — make the pairs and SAY THEM OUT LOUD.
  4. Station 4 “Clock corner”: the clock cards from U4 — What time is it? — It's half past two. in pairs.
  5. Rotate on the signal every 4–5 min; each station has a self-check sheet (the key underneath, turned over).
  6. Help = the first letter or a gesture, not the ready-made word.
📺 On screen: 4 stations: cards/envelopes/letter tiles/clocks · self-check sheets · timer signal
👦 Students do: they move round the stations in teams, drilling 4 skills with peer checking — everyone moves and talks. Expected: clock corner is the hardest — that's where I help most. Typical error: they argue over the sort — the self-check sheet settles it.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can name 2–3 words for each of the 9 unit categories and use the verb groups do/play/have/take/go with support (anchor columns) in short true sentences about themselves.
  • Subsidiary. A personal checklist of categories “I know it / revise” is set up; photos A–I + the secret word; adjective pairs, ex.4; questions, ex.6, in pairs WITH SUPPORT; station games with peer help (per the syllabus).
  • Personal. The unit's main message for the from-zero line: “in September it was ‘hello’, now it's nine topics”. Don't rush the pace: EVERYONE's success matters more than records. The checklist is their personal map, not a ranking.
By the end, students can…
  • name the 9 photo items
  • give 2–3 words per unit category (with book support where needed)
  • match do/play/have/take/go to their nouns using the anchor columns
  • ask 3 ex.6 questions to a partner from the frame

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Play sport?” → No — DO sport (play — with games: play basketball).
“Category shops — do we write apple?” → No: apple = food; shops = baker's, sports shop.
“Is a checklist ‘revise’ mark a bad thing?” → No! It's your plan. Knowing your own ‘revise’ spots is a superpower.

Assumptions: The year was covered on the from-zero line: the unit sets are familiar, but recalled slowly and with support. That's normal: the book and the Quick review columns are legal helpers the whole double lesson. The secret to success is small wins every 10 minutes.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“I forgot everything” — panicThe very first exercise (photos A–I) gives 9 wins in a row; we call the checklist a ‘map’, not a ‘test’.
Categories are empty without the bookI let them peek at the unit (ex.3 says exactly that: look at the unit) — that's strategy, not cheating.
The strong ones in the group rush itStation rule: EVERYONE gives a word in turn; help = a hint of the first letter, not the whole word.
play/do/have get mixed upThe anchor columns stay on the board till the end of the lesson; we answer while pointing at the column.
They get tired of ‘revision’Every 8–10 minutes — a format change: photos → a standing game → pairs → stations. We never sit for more than 10 minutes.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
