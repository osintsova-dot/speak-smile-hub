🦝 Give Me Five! 1 · Unit 1 “Ready for school!” · Lesson 4 — Vocabulary & Grammar
Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · Vocab + Grammar (PPP + TPR + games) · carpet + desks
Week 21.09–27.09 · Lesson 7✨ NEW~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.12 · Part 1 review + diploma
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 classroom-furniture words and give & follow tidy-up instructions using “Put the (book) on / in / under the (shelf / bin / desk).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle Unit-1 classroom objects + “Where's my…? — It's here!” by placing them on the new furniture; phonics — Part 1 sounds review (recognise → blend) + diploma; listen for detail (song, tick); pre-literacy sight words by · one; fine motor (cut & place notebook cards, colour badge).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; drill the 3 preposition gestures once, then hide all practice inside games; run a stirrer→settler rhythm.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name shelf · cupboard · bin · desk · board · chair;
  • give & follow “Put the … on / in / under the …”;
  • ask & answer “Where's the …? — It's on the …”;
  • blend Part 1 CVC words at speed.
Target language
Lexis (new)

Structure
Put the (book) on the (shelf). / in the (bin). / under the (desk).  ·  Where's the (book)? — It's on the (shelf).
Form
Imperative: Put + the + noun + on/in/under + the + noun. Prepositions of place (3 fixed gestures).
Pron. watch
/ʃ/ in shelf; cupboard = /ˈkʌbəd/ (silent p, NOT “cup-board”); final /d/ in board; stress the preposition + object.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.12 — итоговый Части 1 + диплом (тетрадь стр.30–31), (a) первое знакомство: speed-review of all Part 1 sounds (s·a·t·i·p·n·c·k·e·h·r·m·d·g·o·u·l·f·b·j·v·w·x·y·z·qu) → blend CVC fast; sight words: by · one.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is the book on the shelf or in the bin?” → checks preposition meaning.
“Do I want you to look or to put?” → put (imperative = do it now).

Assumptions: classroom objects (pencil, book, bag, crayon, paper…) & “Where's my…? — It's here!” are known from L1–L3; numbers/colours from Starter. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → rely on pictures & audio, not printed text. Carpet routines known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuse shelf / cupboardPresent as a contrasting pair — one gesture each (shelf = flat hand high; cupboard = open two “doors”).
Mix up on / in / underDrill the 3 fixed gestures first (on = flat hand on top · in = fist into cupped hand · under = hand dives below), then use them in every game.
“Put the…” said too fastBack-chain: …on the shelf → the book on the shelf → Put the book on the shelf.
cupboard as “cup-board”Short exaggerated choral drill of /ˈkʌbəd/ (silent p).
Relay over-excitementFreeze! + one runner at a time rule; a settler after every stirrer.
Teams argue over cardsAssign the 4 roles inside each team; rotate every turn.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

22–30′
Presentation · Vocabulary
carpet → teams

Presentation — furniture (PB p.12, ex.1). Hold up each A4 card: What's this? If unknown, model: It's a shelf. Shelf. Choral drill ×3, then 2–3 children individually. Words: shelf · cupboard · bin · desk · board · chair.

Contrast the tricky pair (hold both): This is a shelf. This is a cupboard. One gesture each. Fix cup-board? No! /ˈkʌbəd/.

▶ Memo (мемори) — teams of 4 on the carpet, each team its own set of 12 mini-cards (6 furniture ×2). All cards face-down. In turn each child flips 2 and names them — It's a desk… it's a chair. No! A match → keep it & say It's a desk! again. Everyone plays every round (own set = no waiting).

ICQ: Do you turn ONE card or TWO? (→ two).

 6 A4 flashcards · 3 memo sets (12 mini-cards each) · ▶ Memo
30–37′
Song “Let's tidy up” + TPR
carpet, standing

Teach the 3 gestures first. On! (flat hand on top) · In! (fist into cupped hand) · Under! (hand dives below). Drill the 3, fast, mixed.

Listen & move. Play the coursebook song “Let's tidy up” (PB p.12, ex.2 · CD1 17): Put the book ON the shelf… Put the paper IN the bin… Put the chair UNDER the desk… Children do the gesture on each preposition. Play again: Now let's SING and TIDY! — mime placing real objects.

Optional Genki drill tie-in: Genki “Where Is Mr. Monkey?” — extra in/on/under practice by ear.

 “Let's tidy up” CD1 17 (coursebook) · real objects · opt. Genki “Where Is Mr. Monkey?” mp3
37–43′
▶ Basketball · shoot & say
carpet, 2–3 teams

Controlled → freer, active. Put the real class bin (basket) at the front — nice tie-in: This is the bin! Each child holds a scene-card (object + place: book+shelf · paper+bin · chair+desk · crayon+cupboard…). Teams line up.

▶ Basketball: to take a shot the child shows the card & says the whole sentence first — Put the book on the shelf! — THEN throws the soft ball at the bin. Right sentence = the shot counts; a basket = bonus point. Retrieval practice: the card cues the structure, so every shot = one full sentence recalled (not just a word).

Everyone plays every turn. While the shooter speaks, the whole class does the matching gesture (on/in/under) and the waiting team are judges — thumbs up 👍 / down 👎. Fast rotation so every child shoots. Model first (I do): Watch me — Put the paper… in the bin! …score!

ICQ: Do you throw first or SAY first? (→ say first).

 class bin / basket · soft ball · scene-cards (1 per child) · Speaky · ▶ Basketball
43–50′
📓 Notebook · My classroom pocket
desks

📓 Interactive notebook — MY CLASSROOM POCKET (тетрадь): calm settler at desks. Children cut out the object cards along the dotted line and glue the pocket base into the notebook; the places are labelled SHELF · BOARD · CUPBOARD · DESK · BIN · CHAIR. Model one; pre-cut a set for slower cutters.

Speaky says — place it! 🦝 gives a command, each child places the matching card in the right spot at their own desk (all working at once — no waiting): Put the book on the shelf! · Put the paper in the bin! · Put the pencil under the desk! · Put the crayon under the chair! · Put the notebook in the cupboard! · Put the school bag in front of the desk! Check with a neighbour — same place?

I CAN + TEAMWORK strip. Colour: I can say classroom objects · follow instructions · tidy up my classroom + I listened carefully · I helped my team today. Countdown: Finishing in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… pencils down.

50–56′
▶ Tidy-up Relay
carpet / open floor

Freer, fast, active. Set 3 furniture “stations” on the floor (a shelf, a bin, a desk — A4 cards or real). Each team lines up with a stack of object cards.

▶ Relay: 🦝 calls Put the book ON the shelf! The first runner grabs the book card, runs, places it on the shelf, shouts The book is on the shelf! runs back, tags the next child. New instruction each turn (mix on/in/under). First team to finish the stack & sit down wins.

ICQ: Do you run when I say GO or now? (→ on GO). Reset if loud: Freeze!
