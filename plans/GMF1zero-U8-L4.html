Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 5 outdoor activities — sailing a boat, riding a horse, playing the guitar, playing volleyball, singing songs (+ swimming) (Pupil's Book p. 86) — and ask & answer in the present continuous: “What's he/she doing? — He's / She's (riding a horse).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — clothes + “I'm wearing…” (L1–L2), the camp story activities (L3), can/can't — woven into describing what campers are doing; phonics — Phonics Hero ур.30: /ё:/ er·ir·ur (three spellings, one sound: her, girl, surf; тетрадь стр.83–85); listen & number (PB p. 86).
  • Personal. Keep the -ing on every verb (“swimming”, not “swim”); model he's / she's contrasted; every task keeps ALL children responding (own mime / thumbs / all groups play at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 5 outdoor activities + swimming;
  • ask & answer “What's he/she doing? — He's/She's (sailing a boat).”;
  • keep the -ing ending on the verb;
  • read /ё:/ words spelt er·ir·ur (her, girl, surf).
Target language
Lexis
sailing a boatriding a horseplaying the guitarplaying volleyballsinging songs (recycled: swimming — “He's swimming”; clothes — T-shirt, shorts, sun hat, swimsuit…)
Structure
What's he/she doing? — He's / She's (riding a horse). · Talk Partners: What's Dan doing? — He's swimming. · combine: She's sailing a boat. She's having fun!
Form
He's / She's = He is / She is + verb-ing (present continuous — action happening now). he for a boy, she for a girl.
Pron. watch
keep the -ing /ɪŋ/ (nasal, no hard g); contraction “He's” /hiːz/, “She's” /ʃiːz/; “guitar” = /ɡɪˈtɑː/.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.30: /ё:/ er·ir·ur — three spellings, ONE sound (her, water · girl, bird · surf, turn) — read, sort, write (тетрадь стр.83–85). No rolled r — one long «ё:»!
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Point to a boy card: What's HE doing? → “He's riding a horse!” Girl card: What's SHE doing? → “She's swimming!”
“swim or swimming?” → swimming — action NOW, keep the -ing!

Assumptions: clothes + “I'm wearing…” from L1–L2; the camp activities felt in the L3 story (She's riding a horse); he/she from Genki pronouns block. /ё:/ spellings met in Часть 4; ур.30 today. Emergent readers → picture grids & mime, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”

Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = interactive (projector) 💪 support · ⭐ extension […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Cognitive load: the 5 activities + present continuous He's/She's …ing are the real challenge — clothes & the camp scene are recycled. Scaffold the frame with the “-ing tail” gesture & he/she picture cues; drill it well, keep everything else light. Phonics ур.30 is a brisk read + sort. Buffers to drop first: the 2nd memory round, the 3rd mingle swap. Better every child says one full He's riding a horse well than six stages half-done.
