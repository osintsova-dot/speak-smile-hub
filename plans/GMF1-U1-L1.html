Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 10 classroom objects in context and use them in What's this? — It's a (pencil). and Where's my (pencil)? — It's here! — all practised inside games (slow-reveal, team relay, team memory), not drills.
  • Subsidiary. Retrieve last-year language (greetings / colours / numbers) and re-use it on today's objects; phonics (recognise CAPITAL ↔ small A–Z) through movement games; pre-literacy & fine motor (short writing bursts, stickers).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every game; hide the drilling inside play; run a stirrer→settler rhythm — no long sitting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name & point to the 10 classroom objects;
  • ask & answer “What's this? — It's a…”;
  • ask & answer “Where's my…? — It's here!”;
  • match a CAPITAL letter to its small partner.

Structure
What's this? — It's a (…)  ·  Where's my (object)? — It's here!
Form
What + 's (is) + this? → It + 's + a + noun.  Where + 's (is) + my + noun? → It + 's + here. Connected “Where's” /weəz/.
Pron. watch
/ʃ/ in sharpener; two-word chunks pencil case / school bag (stress on first word); final /k/ in book.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.10: CAPITAL letters A–Z — «звучит так же, как маленькая, просто нарядная»: for names (Sam!) and the start of a sentence; match CAPITAL ↔ small (тетрадь стр.26–27) · sight words: me, be, my.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is it a pen or a pencil?” → hold both → chorus the right one.
“Is the pencil near or far?” → near → “It's here.”
“Do I want to know where it is?” → yes → “Where's my…?”

Assumptions: greetings, numbers 1–10, colours & the Genki song «May I borrow your…?» are known from last year (продолжающие line). Pupils are non-/emergent readers → rely on pictures & audio, not printed text. Carpet routines are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuse the pen / pencil / pencil case clusterPresent as a contrasting set inside the slow-reveal game: hold two at once, one clear gesture each.
Relay turns into a running stampedeOne runner at a time, clear lane, ball is “the baton”; seated team must chant the answer or the point doesn't count; Freeze! resets.
Team memory game — kids grab & argue over the cardsAssign the roles, rotate every turn. A card is kept only after a full sentence (It's a… / It's here!) — a bare word or silence = card goes back, other team's go.
Two-word chunk pencil case / school bag squashedBack-chain: case → pencil case; clap the two beats.
Over-excitement on the carpetFollow every stirrer with a settler (song / memory game) + settle signal.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset (key for relays).

Phonics Hero — Capitals A–Z ⚡/🧘
board → desks

Speed reading. Hub «⚡ Speed reading» / letter strip: the small letters they know — Read with me — fast! (whole class, choral, there and back).

Meet the CAPITALS (тетрадь стр.26). Show a and A: Same sound! The capital is just… fancy! «Заглавная звучит так же — просто нарядная»: for names — Sam! Pat! [Masha]! — and the start of a sentence. Air-draw a few: small → BIG.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: flash letter cards — CAPITAL → stand up tall; small → crouch small. 8–10 cards, fast — every child moves every time.

▶ Fly swatters — capital ↔ small (teams): capital & small cards spread on the board ledge / floor; one child per team with a swatter. You call Big A! / small a! — SWAT the right one, first team wins the card. Fast rotation — new pair of children every 2 calls, everyone gets a turn. (Seated teams call the letter + sound out loud.)

Short write — тетрадь стр.26–27 (desks, 3–4 min burst). №1 trace the capitals (name letter + sound as you trace) · №2 write A · B · C + SAT — top-down! · стр.27 join the pairs (G–g, R–r, D–d, Q–q, E–e, B–b). Colour the star «Я молодец!»

Sight words. Flash me · be · my: chorus, air-write. Tie-in: Where's MY pencil? — today's question, now in print!

22–29′
Presentation = ▶ Slow reveal 🧘
carpet

Present through play — dual-coded (picture + word + gesture). ▶ Slow reveal / «Угадай по кусочку»: cover each A4 card, open it edge by edge. What's this? Kids guess — It's a… crayon! The intrigue does the drilling.

As each card is revealed, pin one fixed gesture to it (rubber = rubbing; “open book” hands for book; typing for computer). Quick chorus ×2 with the gesture — no long “repeat after me”, keep it snappy.

Contrast the tricky cluster (hold two at once): Is it a pen or a pencil? … This is a pen. This is a pencil. This is a pencil case. Chorus the right one.

Vocab-tiering: known words (pen, book) fly past fast; new/tricky (sharpener, pencil case, computer) get an extra reveal + gesture.

 A4 flashcards (10 objects) + mask sheet · Speaky · ▶ Slow reveal
29–37′
▶ Relay card race ⚡
teams, open space

Active Genki-style speed game — the drilling is the race. ▶ Эстафета / Relay (Игротека). 3 teams in columns; a chair with the object cards at the far end.

ICQ first: Do you run and grab, then ASK or ANSWER? (→ run · grab a card · ask your team What's this? · team answers It's a [book]!) Card only counts if the phrase is said — grabbing alone = no point.

Ready… steady… GO! Runner 1 races, grabs a card, brings it back, asks What's this?, the team chorus answers It's a […]! → card kept → next runner. Most cards in 3–4 min wins. Seated runners cheer & answer — everyone talks every round.

If it gets wild: Freeze! → reset the lane → GO! again.

 3 sets of object cards · 3 chairs · ▶ Relay (Игротека)
37–44′
▶ Memory · Where's my…? 🧘
carpet, teams/pairs

Settler right after the relay — model first (board). Tap 🦝 Speaky — he asks Where's my [crayon]? class points → It's here! (2–3 times, model the phrase + the “near = here” gesture).

The drilling IS the recall (retrieval practice). ▶ Memory / Мемо (Игротека) — team pairs. Split each team into pairs, or run team-vs-team on one set. Each pair/team lays 4–5 object cards face-up: show → name (It's a pen.) for each, then flip all face-down.

All down → Child 1 asks Where's my pen? → the partner turns one over from memory. Match → It's here! and keep the card; no match → put it back, other side's go. Card counts only with the full sentence. Swap roles. Most cards wins.

Everyone plays at once on their own set — no one waits. Reset if it gets loud: Freeze!

 4–5 flashcards per pair/team · Speaky (board model) · ▶ Memory / Мемо

📓 Interactive notebook — «I'm Ready for School!» flaps + My Song Badge

Дети вклеивают ОСНОВУ (Лист 2) в тетрадь по всему периметру, затем приклеивают только внешние края КЛАПАНОВ (Лист 1) сверху — под каждым клапаном прячется предмет (pen · pencil · crayon · rubber · sharpener · book · school bag · ruler). To save time: flaps are pre-cut / pre-scored — kids only glue the base & fold the flaps. ICQ: Do you cut, or just glue and fold?

Sing & touch (TPR — dual-coded: picture + word + song + action). Play the song again — on each line a child opens the flap and TOUCHES the named object: Touch the pen! … Touch the crayon! … Touch the school bag! Everyone touches on their own notebook at the same time — no waiting.

My Song Badge ✩ — colour it: I can sing the song · I know the school words · I am ready for school! (leads straight into the reflection).

Now — find the ten stickers. Listen and stick! PB p.8 ex.2 (CD1 12). Model one; check with a neighbour. Countdown: Finishing in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… pencils down.
