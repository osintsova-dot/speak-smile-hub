🦝 Give Me Five! 1 · Unit 1 “Ready for school!” · Lesson 1 — Vocabulary
Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · PPP + TPR + Phonics · carpet + desks
Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 4✨ NEW~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4PB p. 8 · AB p. 6🦸 Phonics Hero ур.10 · Заглавные A–Z
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name ten classroom objects and use them in context — “What's this? — It's a (pencil).” / “Where's my (pencil)? — It's here!” — through the Unit 1 song I'm ready for school! (Pupil's Book p. 8).
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — Starter greetings, colours & numbers 1–10 and the Starter L3 chunk “Where's my…? — It's here.” — woven straight onto today's words (“It's a red crayon!” · “How many pencils? — Five!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.10: заглавные A–Z (recognise CAPITAL ↔ small, write A·B·C, тетрадь стр.26–27); listen for detail (listen & point, listen & stick, PB p. 8 ex. 1–2); pre-literacy & fine motor (stickers, cut-and-glue notebook page); sight words me · be · my.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm with songs and signals; every task keeps ALL children responding (own card / point & say / all pairs at once) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the ten classroom objects: pen · pencil · crayon · rubber · ruler · sharpener · book · school bag · pencil case · computer;
  • ask & answer “What's this? — It's a (ruler).” and “Where's my (pencil)? — It's here!”;
  • sing I'm ready for school! and touch the right object as they sing;
  • tell a CAPITAL from its small partner (same sound!) and write A · B · C.
Target language
Lexis
penpencilcrayonrubberrulersharpenerbookschool bagpencil casecomputer (bonus, from the Genki song: glue · scissors · tape)
Structure
What's this? — It's a (pencil).  ·  Where's my (pencil)? — It's here!  ·  I'm ready for school! (chunk for the song: May I borrow your (glue)? — Of course!)
Form
It's = It is; a + noun (a pen, a ruler) → an only with the odd one out. Where + 's (is) + my + noun? → It + 's + here. Connected “Where's” /weəz/.
Pron. watch
final /g/ in bag; /ʃ/ in sharpener; crayon = 2 syllables /ˈkreɪ.ɒn/, not “ka-ra-yon”; rubber ≠ ruler (the /b/ vs /l/); stress on the object word.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.10: заглавные буквы A–Z — «звучит так же, как маленькая, просто нарядная»: capitals for names (Sam!) and the start of a sentence; match CAPITAL ↔ small (тетрадь стр.26–27) · sight words: me, be, my.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Hold up a pencil: “Is it a pen or a pencil?” → pencil → “It's a pencil.”
“Is the pencil near or far?” → near → “It's here.” (“Do I want to know where it is?” → yes → so I ask.)
Phonics: show a and A — “Two letters — the same sound or a new sound?” → the same. “Which one do we use for Sam?” → the BIG one.

Assumptions: numbers 1–10, colours, shapes & greetings are known from the Starter (revision, not teaching); the classroom objects and the chunk “Where's my…? — It's here.” were first met in Starter L3 (“The storm”) → today they are named properly and drilled; the 26 single sounds are known, capitals are new. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → they rely on pictures & audio, we do not read the song text. Carpet routines are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuse rubber / rulerPresent as a contrasting pair with one clear gesture each (rub · measure); drill them back-to-back, then mixed.
One-word answers: “Pencil.” instead of “It's a pencil.”No point until the full answer: model with a big pointing gesture, back-chain …pencil → a pencil → It's a pencil.
Final /g/ in bag dropped; crayon stretched to three syllablesShort exaggerated choral drill; clap the syllables — cray·on ✋✋.
“Capital = a different letter / a different sound”Hold the pair side by side: Same sound! Just fancy! — same gesture for both; sort the cards into pairs, not into two new sets.
Cutting & gluing the notebook page eats the lessonPre-cut Sheet 1 for the slowest cutters; glue the base FIRST, all together, on your countdown; late finishers glue at the end of the lesson.
Over-excitement on the carpet / teams argue over cardsSettle signal + a settler after every stirrer; assign the 4 roles inside each team and rotate them.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive 💪 support · ⭐ extension […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. This is the first lesson of the unit: ten new words + a new song + a cut-and-glue page + the phonics block. That is a full plate for seven-year-olds, so pick, don't cram. The core = presentation of the ten words (23–31′) + the song with the notebook flaps (31–41′) + one round of the memory game (46–53′). Buffers to drop first if you are short: the Flashcards flash round (42–46′), the second memory round, Timed Pair Share, the тетрадь стр.27 “соедини пары” (send it home). If cutting runs long: children glue only the BASE sheet in the lesson and finish the flaps at home — the song still works with the picture side. Better ten words said well by every child than twelve half-learnt.

Phonics Hero — Capitals A–Z
board → desks

Speed reading (warm the eyes up). The small letters they already know, on the strip / Hub: Read with me — fast! Whole class, choral, there and back. 60 seconds, no more.

Meet the CAPITALS — тетрадь стр.26. Show a and A side by side: Same sound! The capital is just… fancy! «Заглавная звучит так же — просто нарядная». Where do we need the fancy one? — for names: Sam! Pat! [Masha]! — and for the start of a sentence. Air-draw three: small → BIG.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down (all move every time). Flash letter cards — CAPITAL → stand up tall; small → crouch down small. 8–10 cards, fast. Then swap: you say the SOUND, they show the size with their body.

🎮 Phonics Hub (проектор): speakandsmile.ru/gmf1/phonics/ — Station 2 «Заглавные — matching (строчная ↔ ЗАГЛАВНАЯ)» ✅ ГОТОВ
Screen: a capital appears with a line of small letters underneath. Mechanic: tap the small partner → it links up and plays the sound. Pupils: BEFORE the tap the whole class calls the letter and its sound and points to their own hand-written pair; a rotating child comes up and taps. Teacher: hold the tap until you hear everybody. 🧠 Predict: Which small letter is his partner? Point first! Offline fallback: capital/small card pairs on the board ledge, children point from their seats.

Write — тетрадь стр.26–27 (desks, settler). №1 обведи заглавные — name the letter + sound as you trace · №2 write A · B · C and the word SAT: Start at the top — capitals always go top to bottom! Then стр.27 №2 «Соедини пары» (G–g, R–r, D–d, Q–q, E–e, B–b) — everyone works, neighbour-check after: G? — Yes! Colour the star «Я молодец!»

Sight words. Flash me · be · my: chorus, air-write. Tie-in: Where's MY pencil? — today's question, now in print!

🎮 Reading-game option: read-race — ⚔ battle mode, level «CVC · первые звуки»: a word pops up, everyone reads it aloud, the fastest correct team races ahead. Swap it in for the speed-reading strip when the screen is free.

💪 Support: a card with the pair a A on the desk as a model; trace only the first three capitals. ⭐ Extension: write your OWN name with a capital first letter; find two capitals in the classroom (on the board, on a book).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 тетрадь стр.26–27 (1 per pupil) · Phonics Hub (board) · capital/small letter cards · pencils · 🎮 read-race

Presentation · Vocabulary
carpet → teams

Presentation — out of Speaky's bag (the guess pays off). Pull each thing out slowly, half-hidden: What's this? If nobody knows, model: It's a pencil. Pencil. Choral drill ×3, then 2–3 children individually. Cover all ten: pen · pencil · crayon · rubber · ruler · sharpener · book · school bag · pencil case · computer.

Contrast the tricky pair (hold both up): This is a rubber. This is a ruler. Rub · measure — one clear gesture each, then mix them: Rubber? Ruler? Show me the gesture!

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): What's this? Spinner — school deck ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 whats-this-spinner-u1
Screen: a wheel of the ten school things next to Speaky. Mechanic: tap to spin → it lands on one object → tap it to zoom in + play the word. Pupils: BEFORE the reveal the whole class asks together What's this? and everyone holds up their own guess-card; on reveal, chorus It's a (ruler)! Teacher: spin, hold the reveal until every child has pointed, then tap. 🧠 Predict: Where will it stop? Point and guess! Offline fallback: the A4 flashcards face-down, pull one at random.

▶ Fan-N-Pick (teams of 4, mini-cards — all four are busy). ① fans the cards · ② picks & asks What's this? · ③ answers It's a […]. · ④ praises Well done! Rotate the roles after every turn so each child does each job.

ICQ: Do you say “What's this?” or “Where's my…?” (→ What's this?)

💪 Support: a team-mate may whisper the first sound (/r/…) instead of the word. ⭐ Extension: add the colour and a number — It's a blue pencil case. Two blue pencils!

⭐ Swap (rotation): if Fan-N-Pick was used last lesson, run «Угадай по кусочку» (slow reveal) instead — cover a flashcard and open it edge by edge; every child whispers the guess to a partner first, then the class calls it out: It's a… crayon!

📓 Interactive notebook: «Lesson 1 — I'm ready for school!» (flaps + “Touch the…” + My Song Badge)
What it is: a two-sheet A5 flap page — Лист 2 «ОСНОВА / BASE» is glued into the notebook, Лист 1 «КЛАПАНЫ / FLAPS ONLY» is cut out and laid on top, so every school thing hides under its own little door: pen · pencil · crayon · rubber · sharpener · book · school bag · ruler. Teacher: build it in three steps on your countdown — ① glue the BASE into the notebook all round the edge; ② cut Sheet 1 along the outline; ③ glue only the outer edge of each flap so it still opens. Then play the song and call the commands: Touch the pen! Touch the crayon! Touch the school bag! Pupils: open the flap, touch the picture and say the word — It's a pen! — then sing along, opening a new flap in each verse. Finish: colour the My Song Badge ✩ — I can sing the song · I know the school words · I am ready for school! (this badge is their reflection badge at 53′). Time: 6 min inside this stage. 🖨 Prep: print Drive «Lesson 1» — both sheets (Лист 1 «клапаны» + Лист 2 «основа»), A5, 1 set per pupil · scissors + glue stick per child · coloured pencils. Pre-cut Sheet 1 for the slowest cutters. ⭐ Richer option (same lesson, more time): Drive «L1» — a three-panel fold-out (210×148 mm: left flap 52 · centre 106, glued in · right flap 52; fold on the dotted lines, do not cut them) with the whole song text — children open it and follow the lines with a finger while singing; print Drive «Рюкзак» on top of it as the outer backpack cover, so the closed page looks like a school bag. Note: Drive «Lesson1» is the SAME flap page in a different cover version (“Open me!”) — print one version or the other, never both.

🎮 Song option (projector): u1-song-tidy («Let's tidy up») — the song pictures come up in a jumble; the whole class calls each object aloud — A pen! A pencil! — and a rotating child drags it into place. Use it as the “listen again” round when the screen is free.

💪 Support: give a pre-cut Sheet 1 and glue the base for them — they still cut nothing and sing everything. ⭐ Extension: write the first letter of each word under its flap (p, c, r…) with a CAPITAL — today's phonics, straight into the page.

46–53′
▶ Ask & Find · Where's my…?
carpet, in pairs
🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — Where's my…? ✅ ГОТОВ (the MODEL before they play in pairs) · 🎮 find-it-u1
Screen: a drawn classroom with the school things tucked around it. Mechanic: tap Speaky → 🦝 asks Where's my [crayon]?; children scan and point from the carpet; tap the object → it glows and It's here! pops up. Pupils: everyone points at the screen from their place and answers chorally. Teacher: 2–3 rounds only — this is the model, then they play for real.

▶ Memory “Where's my…?” — in pairs, on the carpet (every pair plays at once). Each pair has 4–5 object cards. Child 1 shows a card, names it — It's a pen. — and places it face-down. Repeat for all 4–5 (show → name → place).

When all are down, Child 1 asks: Where's my pen? Child 2 tries — from memory, first try — to turn over the right card. Correct → It's here! and keeps it. Then swap roles. (Most cards wins.) Rule: find it → SAY it — no card counts without It's here!

🧠 Create: last round — children swap one picture card for a real thing of their own (a real rubber, a hair-clip, a toy) and ask about it: Where's my [clip]? — It's here! Their object, their question.

Reset if it gets loud: Freeze!

🎮 Digital option (projector): class-memo — S&S memory pairs on screen, 1–4 teams: a rotating child flips two cards, the whole class names every picture aloud — It's a pencil! Swap it in for one round when the screen is free.

💪 Support: start with 3 cards, not 5; leave one card face-up as an anchor. ⭐ Extension: add the colour to the question — Where's my red crayon?

⭐ Swap (rotation): if Memory was played in the Starter block, run «Спроси и хватай» (Karuta grab) instead — the 4–5 cards face-UP between the pair; you call Where's my ruler? and both children race to grab it and shout It's here! Same language, more movement.
