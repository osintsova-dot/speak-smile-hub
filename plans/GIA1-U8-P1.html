ЧТО ДЕЛАЕМ: финал: репортаж 60 сек — 3 факта из досье + 1 отрицание + 1 мнение с осью; сначала все в тройках, затем 3–4 в эфир

👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Правила продукта на слайд: 3 facts (with a number or a year) + 1 sentence with ‘didn't’ + 1 opinion with an axis + 60 seconds.
  2. 1 мин подготовки ключевыми словами (не текстом!). ICQ: Full script? — No. Keywords only.
  3. Сначала ВСЕ в тройках: спикер — судья — таймер, ротация 3×60 сек. Вовлечён каждый.
  4. Затем 3–4 добровольца «в эфир»: Sixty seconds — GO! Судьи по рубрике; один судья обосновывает вслух.
  5. Очки в лидерборд живьём; gold run = +5.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I go to school by foot?” → No — ON foot. Единственный предлог-исключение.
“A coach and a bus — the same?” → A coach takes people from one city to ANOTHER; a bus — по городу.
“In the report, is ‘Scooters are cool’ a fact?” → No, opinion. Bar: 3 FACTS + 1 opinion.

Assumptions: Post-GMF4: past simple affirmative и was/were (U7) в активе, plane/car/bus знают с началки. Реальные дыры: coach/lorry/tram/underground/travelcard, предлоги by/on foot, длинная связная речь на факте. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (didn't + infinitive / question word order / superlative spelling), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
