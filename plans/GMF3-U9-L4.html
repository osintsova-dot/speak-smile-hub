Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to tell the time (quarter past · quarter to · five past · twenty to…) and ask & answer about the past: “What time did she (go to bed)? — She (went to bed) at (twenty past nine).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Tick-tock» (CD4 21); consolidate the irregular past from L2 inside real times; discover the rule — What time did…? is answered in the past simple; a real listening & speaking game (CD4 24 · Talk Partners draw the times).
  • Personal. Use a big demo clock — move the hands for every phrase; anchor past (right side, 1–30) vs to (left side, back to o'clock); keep the survey moving — max 30 sec per partner.
By the end, pupils can…
  • tell the time with past / to / quarter / half;
  • ask & answer “What time did you (go to bed)? — I went at…”;
  • listen for a time and draw it on a clock.

Target language
Lexis
o'clock · five past · ten past · quarter past · twenty past · twenty-five past · half past · twenty-five to · twenty to · quarter to · ten to · five to
Structure
What time is it? — It's (a quarter past three).  ·  What time did you (go to bed)? — I (went to bed) at (half past nine).
Form
past = minutes 1–30 after the hour (right side of the clock); to = minutes back to the next hour (left side). Answer with irregular/regular past: What time did you go? → I went at…
Pron. watch
quarter /ˈkwɔːtə/ (silent-ish r); “past” vs “to” contrast; link five to four vs five past four (Listening tip!).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Quarter past three or quarter to three — which is EARLIER?” → quarter past (3:15 before 3:45… to is later!).
“What time DID you go? — is the answer go or went?” → went (past!).

Assumptions: Irregular past active from L2–L3; pupils met o'clock / half past earlier. NEW: quarter past/to, five/ten/twenty past & to, and joining time to What time did…?. Readers can read clock labels.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
past / to confusedBig demo clock split down the middle: RIGHT = past (going up), LEFT = to (coming back); move the hands for every phrase.
“half to” inventedFix the fixed phrases: only half PAST; drill half past as one chunk.
five to / five past misheardListening tip poster; exaggerate to ↘ vs past; pupils show ‘to’=hand left, ‘past’=hand right.
What time did… answered in presentBoard: question has did → answer is PAST; recast with the ‘stretch it’ gesture.
Survey / draw-the-clock chaosModel ONE clock publicly; ICQ: ask, listen, DRAW, then swap; 30-sec cap.

Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.

Presentation · telling the time
carpet → board

Present (act. 1, CD4 20). Big demo clock: It's five past three. It's quarter past three. It's half past three. It's quarter to four. Choral ×3; move the hands. Split the clock: RIGHT = past, LEFT = to.

Sing «Tick-tock» (act. 2, CD4 21). PB p.96. First run listen & point; second sing while you move the clock hands to each time.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if this is a ‘past’ time… quarter past six! …sit for ‘to’ times — everyone reacts every card.

💪 Support: read the time off the labelled clock. ⭐ Extension: say the time with no label.

Grammar box · What time did…?
carpet → board

Grammar box (act. 3, CD4 22). Board: What time did + you + go to bed / have lunch / watch TV… + yesterday? Grammar clue: What time did…? → answer in the PAST! Model: What time did you go to bed? — I went to bed at half past nine.

Talk Partners (act. 4). Quick pair round: What time did you get up? — I got up at seven o'clock. Full past answers.

ICQ: “What time did you have lunch?” — I have or I had? (→ I had!)

27–37′
Time survey · Quiz-Quiz-Trade
mingle → desks

Model first. Ask 2 pupils publicly: What time did you go to bed yesterday? — I went to bed at nine o'clock.

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Each child holds a card (go to bed · have dinner · get up · do homework) + a Me/Friend grid. Music → mingle; off → pair: A What time did you have dinner? B answers with a real time; draw/write it, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges.

Harvest. Who went to bed the latest? Who got up the earliest?

✔ Assess: circulate — is the answer in the past (went/had, not go/have)? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: card shows the time in words. ⭐ Extension: follow-up: Why so late? — Because…

 prompt cards · Me/Friend grids · music · ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade
37–52′
Listen & draw the clocks
desks, pairs

Listen & spot (PB p.97, act. 2, CD4 24). Six clocks (arrived / rode the ghost train / ate candyfloss / rode the roller coaster / watched fireworks / went home). Listen — find FOUR differences! Listening tip: five to seven vs five past seven.

▶ Talk Partners — draw the times (act. 3). Info-gap: A What time did Freddy arrive at the festival? — He arrived at ten past six. B draws it on the blank clock; swap roles. Both draw all times.

Showdown check. Mini-whiteboards: you say a time, all draw it, Show me! Self-check against the demo clock. 4 rounds.

💪 Support: times given in words on a helper strip. ⭐ Extension: reader gives the time only — partner works out past/to.
