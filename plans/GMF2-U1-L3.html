Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to read, listen to and understand the story “The very busy week” and to talk about a weekly sports routine in the 3rd person singular — “Molly plays tennis on Monday. / She goes swimming on Friday.”
  • Subsidiary. Reading comprehension cycle (predict → listen for gist → read the text → sequence → retell); recycle sports lexis + days of the week; notice the 3rd-person -s (plays / goes); act out story lines with expression; values — organise your things.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; make the reading feel like thinking, not form-filling.
By the end, pupils can…
  • follow the story text with picture support;
  • say “She plays / goes (sport) on (day)” with the -s;
  • sequence the 8 story frames & retell in order;
  • act out short story lines.

Structure
Molly plays tennis on Monday. · She goes swimming on Friday. · I play baseball on Saturday. · You can't play basketball in a swimsuit.
Form
3rd person singular Present Simple: verb + -s (play→plays, go→goes). play + ball sport · go + -ing sport. on + day.
Pron. watch
voiced /z/ ending in plays, goes (not /s/); linking “on Monday”; stress on the sport + day.
Reading focus
Story lesson (no new phonics — ai/ay comes in L5). Read for meaning: recognise sports & day words in print; notice the little -s on the verb as you read.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Does Molly play the same sport every day?” → No — a different sport.
“It's Monday. Does she play tennis or basketball?” → tennis.
“We say play tennis but go ___?” → swimming / rollerblading.

Assumptions: sports lexis & “I play / I go” are known from Unit 1 L1–L2; days of the week are known from Level 1; pupils are early readers — they can decode short sentences with picture support and a first listening. Carpet + team routines are known. Teams: max 4; 12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4.

① Before you read · Predict
carpet, PB p.10–11

Picture walk (predicting). Books open to the pictures only — no reading yet. Title: “The very busy week.” Look — who is this? This is Molly. What sports are in the story? Take a few guesses; point to the pictures.

Teacher think-aloud (model the strategy). In picture 4 I can see a basket and a ball — I think Molly plays basketball here. And she's wearing a swimsuit… oh! Maybe that's her problem! — show them that reading = predicting & checking, not just decoding.

④ Sequence by ear

Order it by ear (books CLOSED). Each team gets a set of the 8 jumbled story-frame cards spread on the carpet. Play CD1 17 again — as they listen, teams lay the frames in order. ICQ: Books open or closed? (closed!) Do you read, or listen? (listen!)

34–40′
⑥ Read the text · -s noticing
carpet → board

Now we read it (readers!). Go frame by frame in order. Play/read a frame, class does echo reading (you read → they read the same line, following in the book), miming the sport. Snappy — 1–2 lines per frame.

Notice the -s. At each “Molly plays / goes…” line, tap the printed -s: Molly plays tennis. She goes swimming. Choral drill with the /z/ buzz. Sort on the board: play + ball · go + -ing.

Comprehension — quick hinge check (fingers). Molly plays basketball on…? Show fingers: 1 = Monday · 2 = Wednesday · 3 = Friday. (Answer: 2 — Wednesday.) 80%+ correct → move on; if split → re-read frame 4 together.

Values (30 sec). Why does Molly bring the wrong thing? Because her room is a mess! Remember to organise your things. (PB p.11 Values.)

40–46′
⑦ Phrases · Retell race
board · teams

▶ Busy Week Relay (active, all run). Board shows the day headers Monday–Saturday. Teams line up. First runner grabs a sport card, runs, sticks it under the right day, and says the full sentence — Molly plays football on Tuesday. Then next runner. The team chants each sentence with their runner; wrong sport → the card comes back.

First team to build Molly's whole week (correct sport under every day + all six sentences said) wins. ICQ: Do you just stick it, or say the sentence too? (→ say it!)

 6 sport cards ×3 sets · day headers on board · sticky tack
46–50′
⑦ Ask & Grab
carpet floor · teams

▶ Ask & Grab (story facts). Lay the 6 sport cards face-up on the floor. Teams sit round; one runner per team (rotate). The team asks you a yes/no story question — Does Molly play tennis on Monday? You answer No. (new question) or, when you choose the moment, Yes! → runners grab that sport card; fastest hand keeps it.

Only full questions count: Does she go swimming on Friday? Does she play baseball on Saturday? The rest of the team must chorus the question with the runner. Most cards wins. Reset with Freeze! if it gets loud.

50–55′
⑦ Act it out · drama
carpet → “stage”

Whole-class first (everyone acts). Replay 2–3 key frames; the whole class mimes and says the lines together — “Come on, Beth. Run!” · “Oh no! This is my swimsuit!” · “You can't go swimming today, Molly.”

▶ Act out — teams take a frame, choose roles (Molly / friend / narrator), rehearse 1 min, then perform. While one team acts, the audience has a job: Which day is this? — they guess the day. Applause + a Spiky sticker for expression.
