Week 01.02–07.02 · Lesson 8🎨 Project · My daily routines · concertina book~8 pupils · make + presentPH 28 (/juː/ ue ew u-e · p.77–78) · songs: This Is My Day(111)·«What do you do every day?»(T19)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a “My daily routines” concertina book — drawing their day in order and saying: “I get up. Then I have breakfast. I brush my teeth!” in a mini gallery.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle all ten routines & What do you do every day? through a creative product; sing Genki «This Is My Day» (111) & the language song; take turns presenting; Phonics Hero — /juː/ ue ew u-e (consolidation), speed-read p.77–78; a happy end-of-unit gallery.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the presenting sentences first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (2–3 routines each) so no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • draw my daily routines in order;
  • present my day — “I get up. Then I have breakfast!”;
  • say “Well done!” to a friend's book.

“Your friend shows their book — what do we say?” → Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 5 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can name the ten routines, say their day, and answer Do you…?; reading is on /juː/. They know the making & presenting routines from earlier projects.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Can't decide the orderModel with the Big Wheel / flashcards laid out in day-order first; copy that order.
Making takes over the lessonA ready folded concertina strip (4–5 panels); time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; showing the book + one routine is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone claps & says Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentPresent in a fast carousel / small groups at once — 2–3 routines each.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 28 · long /juː/ (again) — sounds ue · ew · u-e  (all say /juː/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read your favourite words from the page — proud, fluent (blue · new · cube)! ▶ Star read — read a /juː/ word to Speaky to earn your reading star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.77–78 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read always · made together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Make · My daily routines book 🧘

Make it. Children draw their routines in order on the concertina panels (get up → … → bedtime) and decorate. Write/trace the routine words if they can.

Rehearse as they work. go round: Tell me — first you… then you…? help each child rehearse their day quietly at the table.
