Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have reviewed all of Unit 3 — park things, There's a…/There are…, Is there…?/Are there…?, the story «Mimi's kite» — and shown it in the flagship Park Team Game with Genki «Banana Tree» (17) as the reward.
  • Subsidiary. Circle the park words you know & listen-and-answer (CD1 T45/46); every child answers (all show at once, then teams); “I can…” self-assessment faces; recycle the park song & Genki «Where Is It?» (82); Phonics Hero — Magic E a-e review reading, speed-read p.55–57; a joyful, both-teams-win finish.
  • Personal. Team game = EVERY child answers (show me your card / stand up), not just the fastest. Keep the reward short & happy — it's the cherry. Both teams cheer at the end; no sore losers.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name park things & say what there is (There's a… / There are…);
  • ask & answer “Is there…? / Are there…?”;
  • show what I can do with a happy/sad face (self-check).

Likely problemWhat you do
Excitement → noise spiralRules up front: answer on Show me!; volume-arm; Freeze resets.
Quieter children hide in a teamEveryone shows a card / stands to answer — team point = most hands up correct.
Reward game dragsOne banana per point, quick; big cheer, move on.
Sore losers / tearsBoth teams get the “Well done!” cheer + a sticker — we beat the game together.

11–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 20 · Magic E a-e — sounds a-e: cake · gate · name · game. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read the a-e row — cake · gate · name · game · plane and all the Magic-E words you can now read! ▶ Reading relay — each child reads one word to earn a park star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.55–57 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read many · any together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

24–47′
🏆 PARK TEAM GAME + Genki «Banana Tree» (17)
carpet, 2 teams

Flagship game. Spin the Big Wheel (app or a pointer on the mat) — it lands on a pod = a challenge card: name the park thing · say There's a…/There are… · answer Is there…?/Are there…? · what's the weather in the story? · where's the kite? Card shown → Show me! EVERY child answers (show the card / do the action / say it) → most-correct team gets a point.

Reward: Genki «Banana Tree» (perfect for a park!): draw two trees on the board; for each team point, add a banana (or a leaf/flower) to your team's tree — the tree with the most bananas wins the round. Rotate who draws the banana every time.

🎮 Offline setup: Big Wheel mat + pod challenge cards on the carpet; two paper trees on the board + banana/leaf cut-outs (blu-tack) — one banana per point, quick. (Или мягкий мяч в обруч — basketball-вариант.)

ICQ: When do you answer? (→ on Show me! only)   Finale. count the bananas in English; both teams: Well done! Give me five!

✔ Assess: across the game — who names park things · uses There's a/There are · asks & answers Is/Are there · knows the story? A picture of the whole unit.

 Big Wheel mat / app · pod challenge cards · two paper trees + banana cut-outs (or soft ball + hoop)
