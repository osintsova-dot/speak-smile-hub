🎡 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Starter block · Lesson 4 — School things & “Have you got your…?”
Pre-primary / early years (~5–6 y.o., pre-readers) · 60 min · Genki recipe · Disco warm-up → phonics → curiosity input → Genki games → song → craft · TPR + puppet · Phonics Hero 2.0 (reading: recognise → blend → speed-read) · carpet + tables · Starter-block review woven from Mimi's Wheel 2 Unit 1 «Classroom» (coursebook Starter has 3 lessons; L4–L8 recycle school things before the October School unit)
Week 14.09–20.09 · Starter L4✨ NEW · school things · Have you got…?~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 10 (capitals A–Z · p.26–27) · Genki: May I Borrow Your…? (80) · games: Ask & Grab · Memory · Boat Race (46)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name 6 classroom things (crayons · glue · pencil · pencil case · school bag · scissors) and ask & answer about having them: “Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle greetings · colours · numbers from Starter L1–3, woven into the school things (a red pencil · a blue school bag · How many crayons?); sing Genki «May I Borrow Your…?» (80) to model polite asking; Phonics Hero — capital letters A–Z, speed-read p.26–27; a calm draw-and-say table finish.
  • Personal. Games-first — keep teacher-talk tiny and let Speaky ask; drill the short answer with a thumb (👍 Yes, I have / 👎 No, I haven't) so every child answers non-verbally too. Stirrer→settler rhythm; a movement stage always follows a sitting one.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 6 classroom things (with a colour);
  • ask & answer “Have you got your…? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.”;
  • sing & act the “May I Borrow Your…?” song.

Structure
Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.  ·  May I borrow your (glue)?  ·  What's this? — It's a (pencil).
Form
Have you got + your + noun? Short answers: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. Colour before noun: a red pencil. scissors & crayons = always plural.
Pron. watch
voiced /v/ in have; /ʃ/ in scissors; the /h/–/v/ chunk have you said fast & friendly.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — capital letters A–Z: match each small sound to its BIG capital, air-write capitals, then speed-read p.26–27. Tricky words: me · be · my.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I've got NO glue in my bag — Yes, I have or No, I haven't?” → No, I haven't! (thumb down 👎)
“Have you got = do you have?” → yes, the same!

Assumptions: This is a review/bridge lesson — the coursebook Starter has only 3 lessons, so Starter L4–L8 recycle Mimi's Wheel 2 Unit 1 «Classroom» (school things + Have you got…?) to warm up before the October School unit. The Mimi 2 coursebook is not in class — we teach from Mimi 2 flashcards + real objects. Children know colours/numbers/greetings from Starter L1–3; the school things & Have you got…? are the new focus. Pre-readers of text → pictures, song, movement & the puppet. Mimi & Dylan are meerkats.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuse pencil / pencil case / crayonsPresent the pencil-things together as a family; one clear gesture each (write · zip open · scribble rainbow).
“Have you got” said too fast / blurredBack-chain: …your bag → got your bag → Have you got your bag?
Short answer dropped (just “yes”)Pair every answer with a thumb: 👍 Yes, I have. / 👎 No, I haven't. — chorus it.
Over-excitement in the relayUse Freeze! + the volume-arm; follow the relay with the calm draw stage.
Cards / objects grabbedOne card per child; hold it up, place on your countdown; roles inside each team.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Peek inside a real school bag: Look! What's in my school bag today? Let's see! Pull out one thing → big gasp. Sets the topic.

carpet

 Mimi 2 flashcards (school things) · colour/number recall · Speaky
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 10 · capital letters — sounds capitals A–Z (match small → BIG). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Match each small sound to its BIG capital: a → A, s → S, t → T — air-write the capital letter. ▶ Capital hop — Speaky shows a small letter; children air-write its capital and jump to the matching capital card.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.26–27 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read me · be · my together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out) — tie-in: Have you got MY glue?

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.26–27 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–24′
Curiosity input · school things + Have you got…?
carpet

Meet the things. Pull each item slowly from the school bag — What's this? Guess! Reveal + name: It's a pencil. Pencil! Choral ×3, then 2–3 children. Do all six (crayons · glue · pencil · pencil case · school bag · scissors) with a gesture each; contrast the tricky family (hold both): This is a pencil. This is a pencil case.

Model the question. 🦝 Speaky pats his (empty) bag: Oh no! Have you got your glue? Teacher shows glue → Yes, I have! (👍). Then hide it → Have you got your glue? — No, I haven't! (👎). Chorus both with the thumbs. Back-chain …your bag → got your bag → Have you got your bag?

ICQ: Thumb up — is it “Yes, I have” or “No, I haven't”? (→ Yes, I have!)

💪 Support: answer with just the thumb. ⭐ Extension: add a colour — Yes, I have. It's a red pencil case.

 real school things (glue, scissors, crayons, pencil, pencil case, bag) · Mimi 2 flashcards · Speaky
24–30′
Game · Ask & Grab (спроси-и-хватай)
carpet, seated teams

▶ Ask & Grab. Teams of 3–4 sit round a set of school-thing mini-cards spread face-up in the middle. 🦝 Speaky (or teacher) calls Have you got your… scissors? — the fastest hand grabs the scissors card, holds it UP and answers Yes, I have! Everyone else copies the sentence + thumb. Keep the card = 1 point.

Every round all children reach → nobody just watches. Weave the old language: Have you got your RED pencil? Speed up the calls.

✔ Assess: who grabs the right picture & says the short answer, who only grabs? Note it — no pressure, just for next time.

 school-thing mini-cards (a set per team) · Speaky
30–32′
🎵 Bridge · Genki «May I Borrow Your…?»
carpet → standing

Now — a new song! May I borrow your…? Sing Genki «May I Borrow Your…?» (80); hold up real objects (glue, scissors, crayons) and act the polite ask-and-lend on the beat. It models the exact chunk we're playing with — your + a school thing.

 Genki «May I Borrow Your…?» mp3 (80) · real objects
32–38′
Game · Memory / memo (memo pairs)
carpet, teams

▶ Memory · Have you got…? In pairs on the carpet, each pair has 4–5 school-thing cards. Child 1 shows a card, names it — It's a pencil. — and places it face-down. Repeat for all (show → name → place).

When all are down, Child 1 asks Have you got your pencil? Child 2 turns over one card from memory, first try — right card → Yes, I have! and keeps it; wrong → No, I haven't! Then swap roles. (Most cards wins.)

🧠 Retrieval, not recognition: the cards are face-down so children reconstruct where each thing is from memory — that first-try recall is what makes the words stick.

Reset if it gets loud: Freeze!

 4–5 school-thing cards per pair · Speaky
38–44′
Game · Boat Race (46) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Boat Race. Genki Boat Race: teams in lines, school-thing mini-cards spread in front. 🦝 Speaky calls Have you got your scissors? — the front child touches the right card, answers Yes, I have!, runs to the back; next child goes. First team through wins. Colour version for extension: Have you got your blue crayons?

Freeze finish. End with a quick Freeze! and a big cheer before we sit down calmly.

 school-thing mini-cards ×sets · Speaky
44–51′
Table time · My school bag 🧘
tables

Draw & colour. On a plain sheet each child draws their own school bag and 3 things inside it — colour them in (a red pencil case, blue crayons…). A calm, focused finish after the relay.

Say it. Go round: Have you got your (crayons)? — Yes, I have! It's a green pencil. Children point to their drawing and answer. Neighbours ask each other one question too.

💪 Support: draw & point, answer with a thumb. ⭐ Extension: ask a friend Have you got your…?

 plain paper · crayons · pencils · school-thing cards (models)
51–56′
Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Ask your partner: Have you got your pencil? — they answer with the thumb + words (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can say my school things! I can say Yes, I have and No, I haven't! colour one star.

Wonderful work, stars! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
