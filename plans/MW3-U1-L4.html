Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 4🎵 Song focus · Big Wheel · order the story~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 10 (capitals A–Z · p.26–27) · Genki: Colours(52) · games: Boat Race(46)·3-2-1 Jump!(34)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing the Big Wheel song (CD1 T17) with full actions and put the story «Hurry up, Mummy!» in order, re-telling it through movement — recycling family · numbers · colours · story items.
  • Subsidiary. Order the 4 story cards; act the song like Mimi & Dylan; sing Genki «Colours» (52) for the wheel colours; Phonics Hero — capital letters A–Z, speed-read p.26–27; trace/colour on the book page (PB).
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions, then words. Repeat the song several times without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (loud/quiet/fast). Every child gets a card in the ordering game.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing the Big Wheel song with actions;
  • put the 4 story cards in the right order;
  • re-tell the story with movement & key words.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Which happens FIRST — Mummy sleepy, or Mummy runs happy?” → sleepy first!
“The song — do we sit still or move?” → move!

Assumptions: Song met in L1–L2 and story in L3 — today deepens both. The Hello unit uses the Big Wheel song as its story-song (no separate story song in this unit). Reading now moves to capital letters. Pre-readers order by pictures, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by repetition of the songChange the mode each time: whisper it, giant-voice it, super-fast, slow-motion.
Story order muddledOrder together first with the big cards on the floor, then children try in groups.
Only a few singGive everyone an action job; sing the actions even without the words.
Cards get grabbedOne card per child; hold it up, place it in the line on your countdown.
Energy too high after songFreeze + a slow breathing verse before the table.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 10 · capital letters — sounds capitals A–Z (match small → BIG). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Match each small sound to its BIG capital: a → A, s → S, t → T — air-write the capital letter. ▶ Capital hop — Speaky shows a small letter; children air-write its capital and jump to the matching capital card.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.26–27 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read me · be · my together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · Big Wheel song in stages
carpet, standing

Sing & move. Play the Big Wheel song (CD1 T17). Round 1 — join the ACTIONS only (big circle, count fingers, wave, thumbs-up). Round 2 — add the words you can. Round 3 — a silly voice.

Point to the Big Wheel mat pods as the numbers/colours come up — sing the sequence together.

💪 Support: do the actions, hum along. ⭐ Extension: lead an action for the class.

 Big Wheel song (CD1 T17) · Big Wheel mat
26–31′
Game · Order the story
carpet

▶ Story line-up. Lay the 4 story cards face-up, mixed. What happens first? Mummy is sleepy… then? Together decide first → last and line them up; then hand 4 children a card each to stand in order while the class claps the sequence (sleepy → shoes → water → happy!).

✔ Assess: do they order by the pictures & item clues? Prompt with a story question if stuck.

31–37′
Game · Boat Race (46) + 3-2-1 Jump! (34) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Boat Race Genki Boat Race: teams in lines, a set of number/colour mini-cards spread in front. 🦝 Speaky calls Number two! It's on blue! — the front child touches the right card, says it, runs to the back; next child goes. First team through wins.

Jump! finish with a quick round of 3-2-1 Jump! — everyone holds a pod card in a circle and jumps on their colour when called.

 number/colour mini-cards · Speaky
37–42′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Count with Speaky» aloud — Speaky counts his balloons 1→6 — a calm read after the relay that recycles today's numbers & colours on our Big Wheel song day.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line One, two, three… red, blue, green!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each page — children hold up fingers for the number and shout the colour of the balloons; match them back to the Big Wheel pods (1 red · 2 blue · 3 orange…).
