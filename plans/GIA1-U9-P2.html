WHAT WE DO: the gate: 9 mini-tasks — one per point of the year — in writing in 4 min; self-check, a square map

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Slide, 9 points: 1 they ___ (be) twins · 2 he ___ (have) got a bike · 3 Is there ___ milk? · 4 she ___ (watch) TV · 5 I ___ swim (can, neg) · 6 Look! It ___ (rain) · 7 go → past? · 8 they ___ (not go) yesterday · 9 big → superlative?
  2. One item per grammar boss. Four minutes, in writing. This is your personal map of the wall.
  3. The key on the slide (are · has · any · watches · can't · is raining · went · didn't go · the biggest); score in the margin out of 9.
  4. The reveal: Which square did YOU lose? That's your target on the wall today.
📺 On screen: gate slide 9 points + key · timer (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: solve the 9 mini-points, find personal gaps. Expected: 6–7 out of 9; failures on 7–9. Typical error: «goed» — I don't fix it, it's a target for the wall.

WHAT WE DO: Kagan Numbered Heads Together: squares 4–6 (present simple · can/comparatives · present continuous) — the crew checks together, the called number answers

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Launching the structure: Number off one-two-three! Solve the square, then HEADS TOGETHER — make sure EVERY head can explain it. I call a number — that person answers for the crew.
  2. A 3-min time-box for a «square + ex» pair; after each — Number two, stand up! (a new number every time — everyone must be ready).
  3. Ex.4 check with transformations aloud (Do your cousins live…? · Does Ömer have…? · Azra studies).
  4. Ex.5 traps: heavier — NOT more heavy · yours — no apostrophe · hotter THAN.
  5. Ex.6 present continuous: Spelling alert — having drops E, sitting doubles T, chatting doubles T!
  6. Every clean square = +1; a reason is required.

🎮 Noughts & crosses: the wall match
two teams

WHAT WE DO: TB extra as a match + Kagan Showdown on every move: teams O/X claim the wall's cells; tasks are variations of the squares; every move — a new speaker

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ▶ Noughts & crosses (TB p.124): the class = teams O and X.
  2. A move = Showdown: the team names a cell → 60 sec EVERYONE solves a variation of that square's task in secret on mini-boards (I change the numbers/names from the ex.) → Showdown — boards UP! → the team's speaker answers WITH A REASON.
  3. Clean = a mark in the cell; a mistake = the opponents can finish it off and take it.
  4. The speaker rotates every move — everyone speaks; mini-boards keep everyone in the game.
  5. Match until a line or 15 min; +3 to the winners, +1 for beautiful reasons.
📺 On screen: XO grid · mini-boards · my task-variation cards for the 9 squares
👦 Students do: the whole class solves every move in writing, speakers duel. Expected: tactics like «take 5 — that's can, we're strong there». Typical error: arguing about the reason — the arbiter is the Quick review column.

🏁 PRODUCT · «Catch my mistake» show
trios

WHAT WE DO: the finale: trios prepare 3 sentences with a TRICKY mistake from the year (one from a red zone) + 1 clean one; the class catches them

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Task: Your crew writes FOUR sentences: three with ONE hidden classic mistake each (from the year's grammar), one totally clean. Make them TRICKY. Five minutes.
  2. Model: He don't like Mondays (don't→doesn't) · Turkey is more big than England (bigger) · My parent's car is blue — clean or not? Depends on parents!
  3. Show: the trio reads out — the class on mini-boards: FIX or OK; the trio gets +1 for each classmate caught «in the trap», the catchers +1 for a correct fix with a reason.
  4. Gold run = all 4 sentences make grammatical sense and the trap is real.
📺 On screen: trio slips · mini-boards
👦 Students do: construct traps — deeper than solving someone else's; the whole class catches at once. Expected: traps about -s and didn't. Typical error: an accidental mistake in the «clean» sentence — we sort it out kindly, it counts for the class too.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can solve tasks on all 9 grammar points of the year (be → have got → some/any → present simple → can/comparatives → present continuous → past simple → superlatives) with reasons: «it's C because…».
  • Subsidiary. The wall mechanic mastered (white rule-square → colour ex.); noughts & crosses from the TB extra; «catch the mistake» on the year's classic traps; written accuracy under a 5-sec limit.
  • Personal. Anti-whinge: «you SPEAK in every tense — but can you solve a square clean in a minute?» Points only with a reason: reasons ARE exam-craft.
By the end, students can…
  • answer the white-square rule questions from memory
  • solve the 9 colour exercises fast and clean
  • explain WHY an answer is correct (grammar reasons)
  • fix the year's classic mistakes on sight

Target language
Lexis
grammar terms: affirmative · negative · question · short answer · countable/uncountable · comparative · superlative · past form
Structure
It's ‘hasn't got’ because Josh is he. · We use ‘any’ in questions. · What are you doing at the moment? · They didn't go to the cinema last night. · The most dangerous way to travel is…
Form
the wall's route: white square N (the rule) → colour ex.N (practice); the year's 9 points — see the GRAMMAR_WALL reference in the content layer.
Pron. watch
doesn't /ˈdʌznt/ · didn't /ˈdɪdnt/ · than /ðən/ unstressed · comparative -er runs together: heavier /ˈheviə/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«Some or any in a question?» → any (except request sentences).
«He don't like — why not?» → he/she/it → doesn't.
«More hotter?» → No — hotter. A short adjective = -er, no more.

Assumptions: All 9 points were introduced during the year; spoken fluency is there, written accuracy and REASONS for answers are the growth zone. Explain the wall mechanic once, clearly (TB General instructions). Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (mixed-year items), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our revision zone before the exam formats”.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The wall mechanic isn't clearOne demo on square 1 with the whole class: white square → check → colour ex.1. Then on their own.
«Grammar again, ugh»This isn't grammar — it's noughts and crosses for season points. The 1–9 grid, teams, cell-choice tactics.
The strong one solves for the teamThe rotation rule: a new «answer speaker» for every square; no speaker, no point counted.
An answer with no reasonThe year's evidence rule: «C!» doesn't count — «C because has got goes with he». The gesture «because…?»
Stuck on past simple (ex.7–8)That's normal — U7–8 are the freshest and rawest. A double time-box is built in + a support of irregular verbs on the board.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
