Pron. watch

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Some или any в вопросе?” → any (кроме предложений-просьб).
“He don't like — почему нет?” → he/she/it → doesn't.
“More hotter?” → No — hotter. Короткое прилагательное = -er, без more.

Assumptions: Все 9 точек введены в течение года; беглость устная есть, письменная точность и ПРИЧИНЫ ответов — зона роста. Механику стены объяснить один раз чётко (TB General instructions). Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (mixed-year items), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our revision zone before the exam formats”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
