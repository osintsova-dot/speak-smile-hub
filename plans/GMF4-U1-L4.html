Give Me Five! 4 · Unit 1 “Let's eat!” · Lesson 4 — Vocabulary & Grammar
A1+ young learners (~10–11 y.o., confident readers) · 60 min · PPP + TPR + Kagan · desks + open floor
Week 21.09–27.09 · Lesson 7 (Unit 1 · L4)✨ NEW~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🥣 Cooking verbs + need to
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 cooking verbs and ask & answer about requirements using “What do/does (he) need to do? — We need to (chop) the carrots. / She needs to (pour) the water.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle Unit 1 ingredients + a lot of / some / a few and How many/much…? (L1–L2, woven into cooking); listen for sequence in the song; use time sequencers First → Finally; write a mini-recipe (interactive notebook).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; hand grammar over fast (I Do → We Do → You Do).
By the end, pupils can…
  • say & mime the 6 cooking verbs (stir, mix, chop, heat, pour, add);
  • ask & answer “What do/does … need to do? — (We/She) need(s) to …”;
  • order recipe steps with First · Next · Then · After that · Finally.

Structure
What do (I/you/we/they) need to do? · What does (he/she) need to do? — We need to chop the carrots. / She needs to pour the water.
Form
What + do (I/you/we/they) / does (he/she) + need to + base verb? → subject + need(s) to + base verb. Base verb after need to (chop, not chops). Weak to /tə/.
Pron. watch
/tʃ/ in chop; consonant blend /st/ in stir; need to → “need-tə”; keep base verb after does … need to.
Sequencers
First · Next · Then · After that · Finally (from the song & recipe).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“He needs to stir — do I say need or needs?” → needs (he/she).
“What does he need to do? — after does, is it need or needs?” → need (base). Trap!
“Is chop now, or the whole action?” → base verb (the action).

Assumptions: ingredients & a lot of / some / a few are known from L1–L3; present-simple do/does questions are known from Level 3 & Starter; pupils read confidently → text & grammar table support learning. Talk Partners routine is set. Teams: max 4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

▶ Memo — teams
floor, teams

▶ Memory (Memo) · in teams of 4. Each team gets 12 face-down cards = 6 matching pairs: a verb-picture card ↔ a sentence card (We need to stir. · We need to chop. …). 27–36′
Grammar focus · need to
board → desks

I Do (model). Build the question on the board from the PB p.12 Act.3 table: What do we need to do? — We need to chop the carrots. Then switch subject: What does he need to do? — He needs to stir the soup.

Highlight form + trap. Colour-code pronouns: do = I/you/we/they · does = he/she (Grammar clue). Big arrow to the trap: does he need — NOT needs! CCQs: He needs or need? · After “does”, need or needs?

We Do (guided). Substitution drill — you give a subject + verb card, class forms the Q&A chorus, then 2–3 individuals: she / pour → What does she need to do? — She needs to pour the water.

You Do (RallyRobin). PB p.12 Act.4 Talk Partners: A points at a verb card & asks, B answers, then swap: What do we need to do? — We need to add the sugar.

 PB p.12 Act.3–4 · board · subject/verb cards
36–43′
▶ Basketball challenge
open floor, teams

▶ Basketball · sentence-then-shoot. Two team lines, one bin/hoop, one soft ball. Flip a card (a subject + a verb, e.g. he / heat). Before shooting, the player must say a correct full sentence: What does he need to do? — He needs to heat the soup! Correct sentence → take the shot; goal = 2 points, correct sentence alone = 1 point.

Wrong grammar (e.g. does he needs) → no shot, next team's turn. Everyone in line prepares their sentence, so all are “on” every round.

ICQ: Shoot first, or say the sentence first? (→ sentence first!) Reset with Freeze! if it gets loud.

48–55′
▶ Recipe relay — production
open floor, teams

▶ Recipe relay. Each team has 5 jumbled step-cards from the soup (get a pan · chop the carrots · mix the beans · add the water · heat it up). One runner at a time races to the board, pins the next step in order and calls it with a sequencer + full sentence: First, we need to get a pan! · Next, we need to chop the carrots! Then the next runner goes.

First team with all 5 in the right order and read aloud correctly wins. Extra challenge round: change the chef — retell in he: He needs to chop the carrots.

ICQ: One runner, or all at once? (→ one at a time!)
