Get Involved! A2 · Unit 1 “School life” · Pair 7 — Project & Unit Review
A2 teens (~12–14 y.o., readers) · 90 min · Project + Revision cycle (THINK → PLAN → CREATE → PRESENT + Quick review) · desks / stations
October · Lessons 21–22★ PROJECT + REVIEW~12 pupils · pairs & teams of 3–4SB pp.22–23 · Quick review p.21 · Planner p.118
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated Unit 1 language (daily routines · school subjects · present simple · object pronouns) by designing, writing up and presenting a timetable for their ideal school and justifying their choices.
  • Subsidiary. Develop the critical-thinking sub-skill “making logical decisions”; practise negotiation Key phrases; retrieval & interleaving of the whole unit; peer review; written accuracy (present simple + capital letters).
  • Personal. Keep TTT low, hand the floor to pupils; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm with a genuine brain-break at ~45′; make praise specific, not generic.
By the end, pupils can…
  • talk about the importance of different school subjects;
  • make & justify logical decisions using Key phrases;
  • use present simple & object pronouns accurately across a whole unit;
  • present a project and give useful peer feedback.
Target language — recycled & consolidated (review pair)
Lexis

Structure
Present simple aff/neg · Yes/No + short answers · object pronouns (me, you, him, her, it, us, them). Project frames: “Our school day starts at… and finishes at… because…” · “Students learn (music) because we think…”
Form
he/she/it + verb-s/es/ies · doesn't/don't + base verb · Do/Does…? · object pronoun goes after the verb: “I finish it at seven.”
Pron. watch
3rd-person -s: /s/ starts · /z/ goes · /ɪz/ teaches, studies; weak “do you” /djə/; stress in Key phrases “What about…?”
Key phrases
Wait a moment. · Slow down. · What about…? · Yes, but don't forget… · I'd like to learn… · I'd prefer… · I think / don't think that's a good thing. · Me too. · Really?
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“She go or she goes to school?” → goes. “And ‘I’?” → I go (no -s).
“Is him a subject or an object?” → object → comes after the verb.
“For a logical decision — decide first, or list advantages/disadvantages first?” → list first.

Assumptions: daily routines, school subjects, present simple & object pronouns were all taught across Unit 1 P1–P6 (this pair recycles & applies them). Pupils are confident readers and can write short sentences. Pair/team routines are established. Teams: 3–4 max (12 = 3×4 · 10 = 3+3+4 · 9 = 3×3 · 8 = 2×4 · 7 = 3+4).

Unit 1 brain dump. Close your books. You've got 90 seconds — write everything you remember from Unit 1: daily routines, school subjects, one present-simple sentence about you. Silent free recall (low-stakes, retrieval practice).

Interleaved review
whole room, standing

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (Kagan) — each pupil gets one review card (Q on front, A on back), mixing all four strands: vocab (“the opposite of ‘start school’?”), present simple (“he / go → ?”), object pronouns (“I meet my friends and play with ___.”), spelling rule (“study → he ___”).

Stand up, hand up, pair up! Quiz your partner → they quiz you → coach if needed → trade cards → new partner. 4–5 swaps. Everyone answers every round — no spectators.

ICQ: Do you keep your card or trade it? (→ trade). Snapshot check of 2–3 tricky cards on the board.

Super skill — “Making logical decisions” (I do). Think aloud with the Tips: Hmm, more art or more maths? I won't decide fast — let me list the advantages and disadvantages first… so, I think… Model one negotiation using the Key phrases.

We do — Ex 5. Pairs practise saying the Key phrases (Wait a moment · Slow down · What about…? · I'd prefer… · Me too) with ▶ RallyCoach — partner A proposes, partner B reacts with a Key phrase, swap.

Project STEP 4 · PRESENT
pair → pair rotation

Ex 9 — present. Read the How to… tips on p.118 (notes not sentences · one fact each · look up). Then each pair joins another pair: Tell them about your ideal school timetable — take turns, one lesson each. Rotate so every pair presents to two others.

Ex 10 — Peer review. Listeners have a task: Choose the timetable you think is most interesting and useful for all students — and say why. Report one winner + reason to the class.

ICQ: While you listen — are you silent, or do you have a job? (→ choose the best + reason).

★ Quick review challenge
teams of 3–4

Now — the Unit 1 Champions Challenge! Flagship team quiz (this is the review pair). Use ▶ Showdown (Kagan): read a question → all write on mini-whiteboards → Showdown! → boards up together. Interleaved rounds off Quick review p.21: R1 vocab, R2 present simple (aff/neg/Yes-No), R3 object pronouns, R4 “fix the sentence”.

Every pupil writes every round (no hitch-hikers). Team point for a correct board; running score on the board. Winning team gets the reward (star stickers / homework pass 🌟).

Keep it fast and fair — recycle any error straight into the next round as a CCQ.

📓 Interactive notebook · Match It
desks

📓 Interactive notebook — Unit 1 Translation: MATCH IT! + TRANSLATE IT! (2-up A4, 1 sheet per 2 pupils).

  • MATCH IT! (in class): glue in the sheet — 12 Russian sentences → English A–L (daily routine · present simple in all three forms · school subjects · object pronouns · checking phrases). Join the pairs in pencil — first alone, then check with a partner.
  • TRANSLATE IT! (start now → finish for HW): glue the second sheet on the next page; begin writing the English from memory, no looking back. Self-check against the matched sheet; every mistake rewritten as a full sentence; score out of 12 in the margin — the unit's exit line.

Monitor: spot-check 2–3 pairs' matches; put one recurring error on the board and drill it chorally.
