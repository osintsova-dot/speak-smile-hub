Gateway to the World B2 · Unit 1 “Make the grade” · Lesson 6 — Review (Check it, pp. 16–17)
B2 teens (~15–16, EGE track) · 90 min · Consolidation & retrieval before the unit test · CELTA staging
Week 28.09–04.10 · Lesson 9 of the year✦ REVIEW~10–12 students · pairs / teams of 3–4Next lesson = Unit 1 Test (Standard / Higher)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated and self-tested the whole of Unit 1 — school life / higher education lexis, do & make collocations, and the three grammar areas (present habits · present perfect simple vs continuous · gerunds & infinitives) — through retrieval and interleaved practice, and will know exactly what to revise for the test.
  • Subsidiary. Systematise the Grammar reference “in their own words”; interweave lexis + grammar in mixed practice; rehearse the exam-format tasks (word transformation, gap-fill, definitions, informal email); diagnose and fix the two costliest unit errors.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; make every retrieval task low-stakes; ensure every student writes/answers on every item (Showdown); give clear ICQs and a self-check band after each test-yourself block.
By the end, students can…
  • retrieve Unit 1 lexis and do/make collocations from memory;
  • state and apply the three grammar rules and their signal words;
  • complete the p.17 “Test yourself” (40 pts) and self-mark it;
  • plan an informal email and name the exam-day test format.

Grammar
① present habits (present simple / continuous + always, constantly / will) · ② present perfect simple vs continuous (result vs process; for/since) · ③ gerunds & infinitives (after prepositions, verbs of liking, go+activity / to explain purpose, after adjectives, too/enough).
Exam link
Use of English (word transformation, gap-fill) · informal email (120–150 w.) · vocab in definitions — mirrors EGE lexico-grammar + personal letter.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I’ve read that book” vs “I’ve been reading that book” — which one is finished? (→ the first: result). Which stresses the process/duration? (→ the second).
“I enjoy studying” vs “I want to study” — after enjoy: gerund or infinitive? (→ gerund). After want? (→ infinitive).
“…… a decision” — do or make? (→ make). Why? (→ create/produce something new → make).

Assumptions: all Unit 1 language has been met across Lessons 1–5; students can self-mark against a key; they are motivated by the imminent test and can choose Standard / Higher. Teams: 3–4 per team; pairs for Quiz-Quiz-Trade & RallyCoach.

0–9′
Lead-in · retrieval brain-dump
desks → pairs

Frame the lesson. Books closed. This is our last lesson before the Unit 1 test — today we pull everything back out of your memory and find the gaps.

Free recall (books closed). 90 sec, individually, on scrap paper: Write every Unit 1 word and grammar rule you can remember — school, university, do/make, tenses. Go. Retrieval > re-reading — struggling to recall is what fixes it.

▶ RallyRobin (pairs): take turns adding one item each until they dry up; then combine lists. Whole-class board map: harvest into four columns — school life · higher education · do/make · grammar. ICQ: Books open or closed for this part? (→ closed).

 scrap paper · board (4 columns) · timer
9–24′
Vocabulary consolidation
carpet space / standing

Check & fill. Open SB p.16 “Check it” — Vocabulary. In pairs, tick what was on their brain-dump, highlight what they missed (school life · higher education · do/make). 3 min.

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (Kagan, standing mingle): each student holds a card — one side an English definition or a do/make gap (“…… a decision”), the other the answer. Stand up, pair up. A quizzes B, B quizzes A, they coach if needed, praise, trade cards, find a new partner. Interleave the deck — mix higher-ed nouns with do/make items so students switch categories every turn. 6–7 min, everyone speaking every round.

📓 Interactive notebook — Review Matching (Match It). Hand out the two Review Matching sheets (8 + 8): students draw lines Russian ↔ English for the whole-unit chunks. Pairs check each other. This is the recognition layer before the harder recall tasks.

 SB p.16 · Quiz-Quiz-Trade card deck (interleaved) · 📓 Review Matching 1–2
24–39′
Grammar — systematise it
desks / teams of 3–4

Rules in their own words. Split the three areas across teams: ① present habits · ② present perfect simple vs continuous · ③ gerunds & infinitives. Using SB p.16 Grammar reference, each team writes a 2-line “rule + signal words + one example” summary for its area, not copied verbatim — reformulating is the point.

▶ Numbered Heads Together: teams teach the class their area; you fire a check question at a random number: Number 3 — result or process: “I’ve painted my room”? (→ result/simple).

📓 Interactive notebook — Grammar cards. Students complete the DECISION PATH + SIGNAL WORDS + EXAM TRAPS lines on their unit grammar cards (e.g. for/since; preposition → -ing; want/decide → to). Keep it tight — 4 min.

 SB p.16 Grammar reference · 📓 Grammar cards · team mini-whiteboards
39–45′
▶ Vocabulary Showdown
teams, whiteboards

▶ Showdown (Kagan — everyone writes every item). You call a prompt; all students write silently on personal whiteboards; on Show me! boards go up together. Prompts interleave the whole unit, fast: a synonym for “grade”… “make” or “do” + progress?… a first-year student?… the word for a university teacher?… a scholarship pays for…? Team point for a correct board. Retrieval, not competition-for-its-own-sake: the misses tell you what to reteach in the next block.

 personal whiteboards + pens · prompt list · team score
45–48′
🧘 Brain-break
standing

Reset (~mid-point). 2-minute stretch + a quick ▶ “Stand up if…”: Stand up if you’ve studied abroad… if you do coursework you enjoy… if you’ve been learning English for more than five years. (Sneaks present perfect + unit lexis into the break.) Sit, water, refocus.

48–66′
Test yourself — Grammar (timed, self-check)
desks, exam mode

Exam rehearsal. This is exactly the test format — silent, timed, then you mark your own. SB p.17 Grammar test: ① present simple/continuous & habits (6) · ② present perfect simple vs continuous — choose the alternative (7) · ③ gerunds & infinitives — key-word transformation, 2–5 words (7). 10–11 min, individually.

Self-mark + traffic light. Project the key; students mark, note their score, and flag 🟢/🟡/🔴 by area. CCQ on the tricky one: “I’ve been finishing my exam” — right or wrong? (→ wrong; a one-off completed action → present perfect simple “I’ve finished”).

📓 Interactive notebook — Review Translate (Translate It), part 1. Students write the full English translation of the first Translate sheet (whole-unit chunks & structures), then run the SELF-CHECK band: whole chunks · verb form · tense. Any 🔴 items go straight onto their “revise tonight” list.

 SB p.17 Grammar test · answer key (board) · 📓 Review Translate 1
66–76′
Test yourself — Vocabulary + fix errors
desks → pairs

Vocabulary test. SB p.17 Vocabulary test: ① school life gap-fill (6) · ② higher education — write simple definitions (7) · ③ do & make sort (7). 7 min; ▶ RallyCoach pairs to check — one explains, one records, then swap.

The two most expensive mistakes. From what you saw on the Showdown + self-marks, put the two costliest unit errors on the board (e.g. do/make mix-ups; for vs since; gerund after a preposition). Students correct three wrong sentences on mini-whiteboards. Show me! — instant formative read on whether it’s fixed.

 SB p.17 Vocabulary test · whiteboards · ▶ RallyCoach
76–85′
Speaking & writing recall
pairs / desks

Speaking mini (interleave the productive skills). ▶ Timed Pair Share — 45 sec each: Would you rather study abroad or at home? Give a reason and an example. Push them to use unit lexis + present perfect (“I’ve always wanted to…”).

Writing recall — informal email. Books closed: pairs rebuild the 3-paragraph plan for the informal email (greeting + intro → advice → ending) and list 4 informal chunks (Hey…, By the way…, Anyway…, Write back soon). Cross-check against SB p.15 model. This previews tonight’s exam-format homework.

📓 Interactive notebook — Review Translate (Translate It), part 2. If time, students start the second Translate sheet in class and finish it for homework (SELF-CHECK band).

 SB p.15 model email · 📓 Review Translate 2 · ▶ Timed Pair Share
85–90′
Test brief & wrap-up
whole class

Announce the exam format. Next lesson is the Unit 1 test — 100 points. Parts & order: listening 12 → vocab 20 → grammar 25 → Use of English 8 → reading 15 → writing 10 (informal email) → speaking 10 (in pairs, filmed). Offer the choice: Standard or Higher — decide with me now.

Exit reflection. Each student says one 🟢 (“I’m confident with…”) and one 🔴 (“Tonight I’ll revise…”). Short Q&A on anything still shaky. You’ve got this — revise your red items, do the email, and I’ll see you for the test.
