Form

Pron. watch

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“What are you? — правильный вопрос о работе?” → No: What do you do?
“20.07.1969 — читаем как ‘twenty oh seven’?” → No: the twentieth of July, nineteen sixty-nine.
“Laika IS a street dog?” → No — WAS: это прошлое, и это наш следующий урок.

Assumptions: Post-GMF4: месяцы и числа знают, часть профессий тоже; was/were слышали. Реальные дыры: порядковые в написании (fifth/ninth/twelfth), произнесение годов, длинное связное высказывание с фактами. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (irregular forms / -ed spelling / was–were agreement), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
