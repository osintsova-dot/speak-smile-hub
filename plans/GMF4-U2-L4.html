Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 life events and say when they happened: “I learnt to walk when I was one. / She started nursery school when she was three. / They went on a plane when they were five.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «I was a very special person» (CD1 36); consolidate the past simple noticed in the story (L3) — now drilled; discover regular (started · moved) vs irregular (learnt · wrote · went · got) past forms; a real timeline communication game (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on the past verb + when + was/were + age pattern; model each life event with a big TPR action; keep the game moving — short turns, everyone acts. Watch was (I/he/she) vs were (they).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 past life events (learn to walk · start nursery school · move house · get a pet · go on a plane · write a story);
  • say them in the past: I learnt to walk / I wrote a story;
  • add “when I was (age)” with the right was/were.

Structure
I learnt to walk when I was one.  ·  She started nursery school when she was three.  ·  They went on a plane when they were five.
Form
past simple: regular (start→started, move→moved) · irregular (learn→learnt, get→got, go→went, write→wrote) + when + was (I/he/she) / were (they).
Pron. watch
learnt /lɜːnt/; wrote /rəʊt/ (silent w); went /went/; the -ed in started /ˈstɑːtɪd/ (extra syllable), moved /muːvd/ (no extra syllable).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“go in the past — go or went?” → went (irregular!).
“They ___ on a plane when they ___ five. — was or were?” → went … were (they!).

Assumptions: Past simple was met in the story (L3); the 6 life events are new but very picturable/personal. The switch to irregular past forms + choosing was/were is the key challenge. Readers can read short verb words. (Book teaches these 6 events — note КТП listed a different set: ride a bike / lost a tooth / made a friend.)

27–37′
Timeline info-gap
desks, pairs

Model first. Show two half-timelines (A knows ages 1 & 5, B knows ages 3 & 8). Model publicly: When did he learn to walk? — He learnt to walk when he was one.

▶ Talk Partners info-gap (act. 4). Pairs ask & answer to complete the missing life events on a character's timeline — When did she start nursery school? / When did they go on a plane? Both write. 5–6 exchanges.

▶ Listen & match (team relay). You call a life event — teams race to hold up the matching card & say the full past sentence. Point per correct.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — who uses the past verb + was/were correctly? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: cards have the past verb printed. ⭐ Extension: add an age they choose: He wrote a story when he was seven.
