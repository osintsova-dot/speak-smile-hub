WHAT WE DO: Inside-Outside Circle on repeat: a calm entry; closing the unit question in one phrase; a clear plan: rehearsal → test → revenge

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Entry: One sentence: what's the best way to travel — and why? 3–4 answers (+1 each). I compare with their own answers from pair 1.
  2. I build Kagan Inside-Outside Circle: two circles facing each other, a question → both answer → Outside circle, rotate two steps! — new partner, new question; 3 rotations, everyone speaks.
  3. Plan on the board: REHEARSAL 8′ → STRATEGY 4′ → TEST 36′ → REVENGE → PODIUM.
  4. No competition until the papers are in. After that — everything is a game again.
📺 On screen: board: plan for the day · WDYT slide (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they answer the unit question more like grown-ups than at the start. Expected: “the underground is the fastest because there's no traffic” — the unit's language works.

Last rehearsal: four targets
Last rehearsal · three targets
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: an inoculation 10 minutes before the test: three forms where points get lost — didn't + infinitive, question word order, superlative spelling

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Target 1 on mini-boards: In 1910, children ___ (not go) to school by bus. → didn't go. The “ate it” gesture, in chorus.
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYONE writes at the same time, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift together — I see everyone's answer, no hiding.
  3. Target 2: build a question (you / did / how / to school / come / today ?) → How did you come to school today?
  4. Target 3: big · good · bad · expensive · slow → the biggest · the best · the worst · the most expensive · the slowest.
  5. Target 4 (fast): Before I had breakfast, I had a shower — what happened first? → the shower.
  6. These four are worth about half the test. You just did all four.
📺 On screen: mini-boards · four-targets slide (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they hammer out the four targets in a volley — everyone at once. Expected: confidence instead of panic. Typical error: if “didn't went” is still alive — I repeat it in chorus three times.

🏁 REVENGE QUIZ · win back your points

WHAT WE DO: an express revenge review: hot spots noticed while walking around come back as questions in the same formats

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. While collecting the papers — I noticed the hot spots; I put up 8 revenge questions: negative · Wh-question order · superlative spelling · collocation · before/after · word-from-definition.
  2. ▶ Revenge on mini-boards: Boards up! The test took your points — take them BACK. Two points each.
  3. After each question — 15 sec of explanation from the winning crew (explained = another +1).
  4. Wrap-up: Everything you just fixed, you fixed BEFORE you saw the mark. That's how you get better.

Final duel: the best way to travel
Verdict duel · What's the best way to travel?
standing · pairs

WHAT WE DO: the unit's oral final: a duel of opinions with a mandatory superlative and a reason — the unit's language in free speech

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Duel rules: Thirty seconds each. You must use one superlative and one reason with 'because'. Your partner must disagree — politely.
  2. Model: The underground is the fastest way to travel in a big city, because there's no traffic. — I'm not sure. It's the least comfortable, and…
  3. 3 rotations of 60 sec — everyone talks at the same time; then 2 pairs go “on air”.
  4. Class vote: So — what IS the best way to travel? Vote with your feet. I record the class verdict on the unit poster.
📺 On screen: board: duel model + disagreement phrases (I'm not sure · That's true, but…)
👦 Students do: they argue in pairs with rotation, then vote with their feet. Expected: a lively finale, the unit's language speaks for itself. Typical error: an opinion with no because — the partner demands a reason.

Unit 8 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 8 items: types of transport from definitions/pictures (It takes people to and from hospital → ambulance; It's the system of trains under a city → the underground) — mirrors SB p.97 ex.3. B) 6 items: collocations — ___ a ticket (buy), cross ___ (the road), get on/___ a bus (off), go on ___ (foot), ___ a bike (ride), wait in a ___ (queue) — mirrors SB p.101 ex.3.
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 6 gaps: past simple negative, didn't + INFINITIVE (the “didn't went” trap: In 1910, children ___ (not go) to school by bus). B) 6 items: order the words → a Did-question and a Wh-question + a true short answer (you / did / TV / watch / yesterday ?) — mirrors SB p.100 ex.5. C) 6 items: superlatives with spelling (big → the biggest; happy → the happiest; expensive → the most expensive; good → the best; bad → the worst; slow → the slowest — do NOT double -w).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A radio item about school transport week (in the style of SB p.101 audio 56; the teacher reads it twice if there’s no track): 2 pts — choose the best summary (a/b/c) + 6 gaps with information — mirrors SB p.101 ex.6–7.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A story: “My best or worst journey” (60+ words — a shortened format of the SB p.104 competition). Marked for: past simple forms incl. negatives (4) · before/after show the order of events, comma in place (3) · task complete + the story is interesting, paragraphs (3) — mirrors SB p.105 Skills boost.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
