Opening · check-in + rewind

WHAT WE DO: opening; rewind P1: “living clocks” with hands + 3 routine gestures; bridge

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. At the door: One word — your morning: good or bad?
  2. Rewind: Hands are clock hands! Show: half past seven… quarter to four… 3 rounds; 3 routine gestures — the class chorus the phrase.
  3. Bridge: You get up at seven, poor you! Today — kids who get up at FIVE and go to bed after MIDNIGHT. Why?!
📺 On screen: anchor clock on the board (rebuild it)
👦 Students do: they show the time with their hands and the phrases with gestures — everybody moves from minute one. Expected: warmed up in 3 min.

Hook: the K-pop quiz
Hook · K-pop quiz
desks · pairs

WHAT WE DO: ex.1 K-pop quiz in pairs + ex.2 checking against the Did you know? box

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Ex.1: Four questions about K-pop — pairs, two minutes! Where is it from? What type of music?
  2. I collect their guesses (without confirming); ex.2: Sixty seconds — check in the ‘Did you know?’ box on p.51!
  3. Key: South Korea · mix of styles · dance AND sing + famous videos · their own answers.
  4. +1 for correct guesses; a 30-sec culture note: Gangnam Style — top of the charts in 30+ countries (TB p.60).
📺 On screen: SB pp.50–51 · a slide with 4 questions (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they guess in pairs and check against the box. Expected: half the class knows BTS — the energy takes care of itself. Typical error: they mix up Korea/Japan — the box fixes it.

Input: 6 words before reading
Discover · Word work first (glossary)
board

WHAT WE DO: 6 words from the text BEFORE reading, with pictures and gestures: fan · star · lucky · breaks · concerts · become

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I introduce them one by one with a picture/gesture: fan — a person who LOVES a band · star — a famous person · lucky — happy chance! · breaks — free time between lessons · concerts · become — to start to be.
  2. Chorus ×2 on each; a mini-check: Free time between lessons? — Breaks! A Manchester City lover? — A fan!
  3. The words stay on the board in a column — the hunt glossary.
📺 On screen: 6 word cards with pictures · glossary board
👦 Students do: they repeat the words with gestures and answer the mini-check. Expected: fan/star are known, breaks/become are new. Typical error: break = to break something — gesture “the gap between lessons”.

18–26′
Reading technique + first pass
Discover 2 · subskill + quick read
desks

WHAT WE DO: the reading for detail subskill step by step + ex.3 the first pass: easy or difficult?

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Subskill on the board as a diagram: THE GAP → what TYPE of word? (a time? an action?) → look NEARBY in the text.
  2. We train it on one example together: ex.4 line 1 — “5:00 am ___, have breakfast” → the gap = an action → we look for: get up!
  3. Ex.3: Ninety seconds, look through the article: is it EASY or DIFFICULT to become a K-pop singer? Just find the answer.
  4. Check: It's difficult — and why (they practise a lot).
📺 On screen: SB pp.50–51 · the subskill diagram on the board
👦 Students do: they master the technique on a shared example, take a first short pass into the text. Expected: everybody finds “difficult!”. Typical error: they read straight through — point at the diagram: the gap first!

26–36′
Reading in chunks: the academy schedule
Read · schedule chunk by chunk
desks · teams

WHAT WE DO: ex.4 the academy schedule: the text in 4 chunks, filling in our own gaps after each one (audio 30 in the background)

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Chunk 1 (paragraphs 1–2): we read it (audio 30 or me aloud), teams fill in the gaps of this chunk. Checking straight away.
  2. Chunk 2 (finish school → dinner), chunk 3 (after dinner → languages), chunk 4 (lessons finish → start again) — the same way.
  3. The key is built on the board: get up · 8:00 am · 5:00 pm · have lessons · 10:00 pm · go to bed.
  4. Reinforcement (TB): first find all the TIMES in the text, then the actions.
📺 On screen: SB pp.50–51 · audio 30 · the schedule key is built on the board (Backup: no track — I read the script aloud at listening speed.)
👦 Students do: they read in chunks with an instant check — success after every chunk. Expected: all 6 gaps by the end. Typical error: they mix up 5:00 am and 5:00 pm — a morning/evening gesture.

45–52′
Warm-up: a memory chain of routines
Play · Memory game routines
standing · teams

WHAT WE DO: Timed RoundRobin: a bridge warmer: a memory game with routine phrases (TB Warmer) — movement after reading

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ▶ Memory game (Activities bank TB p.7): My day: I get up. — the next one repeats and adds: I get up, I have breakfast…
  2. This is Kagan Timed RoundRobin: strictly round the circle against the timer, one turn — one person, no skipping: Clockwise, one each — keep it moving, no skips!
  3. Two team-chains run in parallel (everybody is busy); if you drop the chain, the team starts again, the length is scored on the board.
  4. Final: the longest chain +3.
📺 On screen: board: chain-length score
👦 Students do: they build a growing chain of routines from memory — moving and laughing. Expected: chains of 6–8 phrases. Typical error: the order of the phrases — that’s legal, what matters is remembering the form.

Critical thinking: we vote and argue from frames
Critical thinking · vote & frames
board · standing

WHAT WE DO: critical thinking through choice: a “hard/OK?” vote + advantages/disadvantages frames (2 each)

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. A foot vote: wall A = Academy life is TOO hard · wall B = It's OK — it's their dream. Everybody stands by a wall.
  2. 3 votes from each wall — from frames: They learn (to dance / languages). · They haven't got time for (friends / homework).
  3. Apply (ex.2) briefly: A teen swimmer — same? They practise every day too!
  4. Pairs: 1 advantage + 1 disadvantage in writing, in the notebook (same frames).
📺 On screen: frames on the board · two wall signs
👦 Students do: they vote with their body and defend their wall from the frames — many speak, everybody writes. Expected: a 50/50 split — perfect. Typical error: an argument in Russian — recast + repeat in English.

70–84′
Writing: one day at the academy
Build · «One day at the academy»
desks

WHAT WE DO: mini-product: 4 sentences as an academy student, from a support (TB extra) + reading to a partner + 2 to the class

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Support: I get up at ___ . I start school at ___ . I don't ___ . I go to bed ___ . + bank: five o'clock · eight o'clock · relax · after midnight.
  2. You are an academy student for five minutes. Write YOUR day — four sentences. 7 min, I walk round and help.
  3. Reading to a partner “like an interview for a journalist” (TB extra activity): the partner reacts Wow! / Really? / Poor you!
  4. 2 volunteers — to the class (+2).
📺 On screen: board: support + word bank · notebooks
👦 Students do: they write a portion in someone else's voice from the frames and read it with reactions. Expected: 4 clean sentences from everybody; “poor you!” enters everyday use. Typical error: I don't relax → I don't relaxing — frame: a dictionary verb after don't.
💪 Support: the first words of every sentence already in the support. ⭐ For the fast ones: +2 sentences with Word work (breaks, become).		notebooks

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can read for detail with support (complete the academy schedule) and say 3 facts about the text with times: They get up at five o'clock. They don't relax…
  • Subsidiary. Subskill “reading for detail” introduced step by step (first the gap → the word type → the hunt); Word work glossary BEFORE reading; critical thinking through voting and choice; mini-product “One day at the academy” from a support.
  • Personal. The unit’s first long read for beginners: we cut the text into chunks with a check after each one. Success = “I understood a real article”, not speed. Voting keeps everybody in the game.
By the end, students can…
  • use the 6 Word-work items with pictures
  • complete a schedule reading chunk by chunk
  • answer T/F with «where does it say?» support
  • write 4 sentences as an academy student (frame)

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Reading for detail — do we read absolutely everything?” → No: first we look at WHAT we’re searching for, then we search for IT.
“Break — to break something?” → Here it’s a noun: the gap between lessons.
“They don't relax — they relax?” → No, they do NOT relax.

Assumptions: Time and routines are active after P1; K-pop is culturally familiar as a topic (plus motivation); a long text — for the first time in the unit, hence chunks+glossary+supports.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The text scares them with its lengthNever “read the article” whole: 4 chunks, a quick check after each; success builds up.
Unfamiliar words stop themWord work glossary with pictures BEFORE reading; rule: underline an unfamiliar word and keep reading.
They answer T/F at randomSupport “where does it say?” — we only answer with a finger on the line; the teacher accepts “I didn’t find it” as an honest answer too.
Critical thinking goes silentNot “discuss it” — voting with stickers + frames “They learn… / They haven’t got time for…”.
The strong ones get bored on the chunksFast finishers (TB): their own sentences with Word work; an envelope “find one more time in the text”.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
