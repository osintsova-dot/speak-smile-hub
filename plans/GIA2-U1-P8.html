Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. To fairly and calmly assess what each learner can now do with Unit 1 language across all skills: daily-routine & school-subject lexis, Present simple (aff/neg, Yes/No & Wh- questions), object pronouns, time prepositions — reading, listening, writing and speaking.
  • Subsidiary. Build test-literacy through a model-first walkthrough (learners understand every task type before starting); protect construct validity (we measure English, not puzzle-solving); support wellbeing so nerves don't hide real ability.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk minimal once the test starts; project a visible timer; give a clear finished-early protocol; assess speaking 1:1 in parallel without disturbing the room.
By the end, pupils will have…
  • completed the Unit 1 Standard test (Vocabulary, Grammar, Use of English, Listening, Reading, Writing) — /90 on paper;
  • done the paired Speaking task 1:1 with the teacher — /10;
  • named one thing they feel confident about and one to review (private reflection, sets up next-lesson error log).

Objective sections by the Standard answer key (teacher, not shown to pupils). Writing & Speaking by the Macmillan band descriptors (0–10 each). Keep marking consistent for reliability.

This is summative but supportive — a fair snapshot, not a trap. Layout: desks spaced, own paper only, phones away. Speaking is assessed 1:1 at a side table while the class works on paper.

Test-room management — signals
Visible timer — on the board; update the section name + minutes left.
Silent hand-up — a question? raise a hand, teacher comes over (no calling out).
“Eyes on your own paper” — quiet reminder, no discussion of answers.
Time calls — “5 minutes left… 1 minute… pens down, please.”
Listening protocol — “Pens down, listen. Now write.” Play 1 = listen; play 2 = answer.
Finished? — turn the paper over, re-read, read silently. Don't disturb others.

0–6′
Settle & frame
desks, exam layout

Calm entry. Desks already spaced. Greet quietly: Come in, sit down, phones in your bags. Today is our Unit 1 test — and you're ready for it.

Self-efficacy framing (mastery mindset). Everything on this paper is language we've practised together for six weeks — your daily routine, school subjects, the Present simple. This is your chance to show what you can already do. Keep it warm, low-stakes: Do your best, stay calm — there are no trick questions.

Hand out the paper face-down. Write your name at the top. Don't turn it over yet. Show the timer and the section plan on the board.

6–15′
Model-first walkthrough
board

Walk through each section with YOUR OWN examples (never the test items) so every task type is clear — this protects fairness: we test English, not puzzle-reading.

Vocabulary. Word-in-the-box: “I ______ my bag before school. → pack.” · Choose the word: “We do / go shopping at the weekend → go.” · Text gap: “I return books at the ______ → library.”

Grammar. Present simple form: “She ______ (get up) at seven → gets up.” · Time prepositions: “at night · in the morning · on Monday.” · Correct the mistake / adverb place: “He is always late. → not: He always is late.” · Questions: “______ you ______ (like) music? → Do … like.” · Wh- word: “______ do you go to school? — By bus. → How.”

Use of English / Reading: Read the whole text first, then choose. Underline key words. · Listening: You'll hear it twice — listen first, write on the second play. · Writing: greeting → who you are → school & routine → free time → sign-off; use contractions (I'm, don't); count your words (30–45).

Quick ICQs (see box): How many times will you hear the audio? How many words in the email? Then: Turn your paper over — you may begin. Good luck.

Speaking assessed 1:1 in parallel. S&S oral-pair format: partners ask & answer to each other while you score with the band descriptors (/10). Add a quick EN↔RU vocabulary check: say 3 Unit-1 words in Russian, pupil gives the English — a fast, fair speaking-of-vocabulary snapshot. ~2–3 min per pair; rotate quietly so the room stays calm.

Monitor discreetly, timer visible. Time call at 40′: Five minutes for this part — then we listen together.

Quiet posture reset — allowed in a test. Pens down. Stand, roll your shoulders, take a slow breath, sit. Well done — halfway. A calm 90-sec change of state before the Listening; keeps focus without breaking exam quiet.

Listening
whole class, silent

Set up. Ex. 10 and 11 — a radio show. First, read the questions. Then listen once with pens down. You'll hear it twice. Give 40 s to read ex. 10 (match people 1–6 to a–f) and ex. 11 (six True/False).

Play 1 — pens down, listen. Pause ~30 s. Now answer what you can. Play 2 — learners complete and check. Listening /12.

Written test — Part 3
Writing
silent, desks

Ring-fenced writing time. Ex. 15 — you are David. Write an informal email to your new friend Sara: your name, age, home town and family · your school life · your daily routine · your free-time activities. 30–45 words.

Recall the model shape from the walkthrough: greeting → who you are → school & routine → free time → sign-off; contractions; exclamation marks for warmth. Writing /10 (band descriptors).

Time calls: Five minutes — check your word count. … One minute — finish your sentence. … Pens down, thank you.

Full feedback and your marked paper come next lesson — we'll fix the tricky bits together. Have a good day!
