Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 10 parts of the body and count them in context — “What's this? — It's my (nose).” / “How many (arms)? — Two arms!” — through the circus song This is me (PB p. 28).
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — Genki numbers 1–12 & Unit 2 size adjectives (big/small/long/short) — and fold it onto the body (“Ten fingers! Two big feet! One small nose!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.20 (b) закрепление: Magic E a-e /eɪ/ (cane, tape, cake, name, made, snake: read → gap-fill → dictation → tricky strip → story «Торт и змея»); listen for detail (point & stick to the song); pre-literacy & fine motor (stickers, draw-a-monster). Light preview of L2 I've got….
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; every task keeps ALL children responding (touch own body / show-me / stand-sit) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • point to & name the 10 body parts;
  • count parts: “Two feet, ten toes, one nose”;
  • ask & answer “What's this? — It's my (nose)”;
  • read & write a-e words (gap-fill + dictation: cake, name, made, snake) and read the story «Торт и змея».

Structure
What's this? — It's my (nose).  ·  How many (arms)? — Two (arms)!  ·  This is my body. Count with me. (preview → L2: I've got two arms.)
Form
my + noun; number + plural noun (arm → arms, toe → toes). Count aloud: one head, two arms, ten fingers.
Pron. watch
voiced final /z/ on plurals (eyes, ears, legs, toes); /θ/ at end of mouth; long /iː/ in feet; stress = number + noun.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.20 (b) закрепление: Magic E a-e — silent e makes a say its name /eɪ/ (can→cane, tap→tape) · gap-fill c_k_, n_m_, m_d_, sn_k_ · dictation cake, name, made, snake · tricky strip they, their · story «Торт и змея» (тетрадь стр.56–57) · sight words: made, does.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“How many noses have you got?” → one. “How many fingers?” → ten.
Phonics: “cake — is the e loud or silent?” → silent → but it makes a say its name /eɪ/! (magic wand ✨)

Assumptions: numbers 1–12, colours & size adjectives (big/small/long/short) known from the Genki block (занятия 1–12) & Unit 2 → recycled, not taught. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → rely on pictures, TPR & audio, not printed text. Phonics: Magic E a-e met last lesson (ур.20a) → today consolidates. Carpet routines & team roles are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuse feet / fingers or eyes / earsPresent as contrasting pairs with one clear gesture each; point to your own body.
Plural -s dropped (“two arm”)Exaggerate final /z/ in the choral count; hold up fingers as you say the number.
Magic E read aloud: “ca-ke” with a spoken e“The e is a ghost — silent, but it waves a wand”: cross out the e with a finger, say /keɪk/.
Over-excitement in Simon Says (touching bodies)Use Freeze! + follow a stirrer with a settler; sit for the next task.
Teams grab the flashcards / stickersAssign the 4 roles inside each team; hands on knees until it's your turn.

10–22′
Phonics Hero — Magic E a-e (b)
board → desks

Speed reading — the big strip. Board / Hub: vowel names chant (a /эй/ · e /и:/ · i /ай/ · o /оу/ · u /ю:/) + digraph strip. There and back — beat your time! Magic-wand recall: 🦝 Speaky waves the wand — can ✨→ cane · tap ✨→ tape!

Gap-fill — тетрадь стр.56 №2 «Допиши слово» (all vote first). Picture up (cake · name · snake · gate) — say the word together, then every child shows the magic-e gesture (palm 🖐 = long) → all write the missing letters into the gap: c_k_ · n_m_ · sn_k_ · g_t_. RallyCoach: partner checks before the next word.

Dictation — стр.56 №3 (everyone writes). Now YOU write. Listen: cake… word once → say the sounds (/c/ /a/ /k/ + magic e) → write → Show me! → reveal on the board, self-check. Words: cake, name, made, snake.

Tricky strip — стр.56 №4. they · their — read for speed, there and back. + today's sight pair made · does — flash, chorus, air-write: 🦝 Speaky: Speaky MADE a big cake!

Story — стр.57 «Торт и змея» (settler). Chorus the 5 frames, finger under the words: A cake. A snake! The snake makes a face. The cake is safe! ⭐ Star task: find & circle every cake. Colour the star «Я молодец!» (стр.56).

🎮 Reading-game option: read-race — ⚔ battle mode, level «Magic E»: a word pops up, everyone reads it aloud, the fastest correct team races ahead. Swap it in for the speed-reading strip when the screen is free.
💪 Support: in the gap-fill give the a-e card to copy; in dictation write c_k_. ⭐ Extension: a 5th dictation word by ear (gate); in the story find snake too.

✔ Assess: the dictation lines are your live check of ур.20 — note who forgets the silent e; re-drill with the wand at the next speed reading.

24–33′
Presentation · body parts
carpet → mingle

Presentation — 10 parts of the body. Hold up each A4 card AND touch your own body: What's this? — It's my head. Head. Choral drill ×3, then 2–3 children individually. Cover all 10: head · mouth · nose · eyes · ears · arms · legs · feet · toes · fingers. Contrast the tricky pairs (touch each): These are my eyes. These are my ears. · These are my feet. These are my fingers.

▶ Teacher Says (Simon Says) — all move at once. Teacher says touch your nose!… Touch your ears! (no “teacher says” = don't move). Everyone touches their OWN body; funny speed-ups. Reset with Freeze!

🎮 Interactive (projector): What's this? — body spinner (колесо body). Screen: a wheel of the 10 body parts next to Speaky; a rotating child taps to spin. Mechanics: Predict before the reveal — Where will it stop? Point on your own body!; the wheel lands → whole class chants. Language: What's this? — It's my nose! Teacher: every child points to that part on their body = all engaged; tap-to-spin rotates each round. Status ✅ ready (reuse What's this? spinner, body deck). Offline reserve: flip the A4 flashcards face-down, pull one at random. · 🎮 whats-this-spinner-u3

ICQ: Do we point and say “It's my nose” or “Where's my nose?” (→ It's my nose.)
