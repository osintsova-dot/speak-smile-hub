Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 8 foods and say what they like — “I like (chicken). I love (ice cream). I don't like (fish).” (Pupil's Book p. 40), through the food song.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — Genki vegetables & “Do you like…?”, Unit 2 favourites — folded into the food topic (“What's your favourite food?”, “Do you like carrots?”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.23 (b) закрепление: Magic E повторение (mixed cake, kite, home, cube: read → sort → dictation → story «Я знаю Magic E!»); listen for detail (point & stick to the song); fine motor (stickers). Light preview of L2 Do you like…? — Yes, I do.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; every task keeps ALL children responding (own card / thumbs like/don't-like / stand-sit) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 8 foods;
  • say “I like… / I love… / I don't like…”;
  • show a like/don't-like face for each food;
  • read & sort Magic E words (cake, kite, home, cube) and read «Я знаю Magic E!».

Structure
I like (chicken).  ·  I love (ice cream).  ·  I don't like (fish).  ·  It's delicious! (preview → L2: Do you like…? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.)
Form
like / love / don't like + food noun (no article: “I like fish”). don't = do not. Stress the feeling word — “I LOVE ice cream!”
Pron. watch
don't /dəʊnt/ — say the whole word, not “I no like”; love /lʌv/ vs like /laɪk/; sandwiches = two beats /ˈsænwɪdʒɪz/; final /ʃ/ in fish.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.23 (b) закрепление: Magic E повторение — mixed cake, kite, home, cube (a-e·i-e·o-e·u-e) · sort by vowel · dictation cake, kite, home, cube · story «Я знаю Magic E!» (тетрадь стр.64–65) · sight words: love, another.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Yummy face 😋 vs yuck face 🤢: “I LOVE it — big yummy!” vs “I DON'T like it — yuck!”
Phonics: “cake — which vowel says its name?” → a /эй/! and “home?” → o /оу/!

Assumptions: Genki vegetables & “Do you like…?” + Unit 2 favourites are known → recycled, not taught. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → pictures, TPR & audio, not printed text. Phonics: all Magic E met earlier; today a mixed consolidation round + reading. Carpet routines & team roles known. First lesson after the winter holidays — warm re-bond. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“I no like” instead of “I don't like”Drill don't with a cross-arms 🙅 gesture; back-chain …like fish → don't like fish → I don't like fish.
Confuse pasta / pizza or soup / saladPresent as contrasting pairs, one clear gesture each (twirl pasta / triangle pizza; spoon soup / toss salad).
Magic E read as two sounds againCross out the ghost e; quick wand recall — cake, home!
Over-excitement (yum/yuck shouting)Freeze! + follow a stirrer with a settler; sit for the next task.
Teams grab the flashcards / stickersAssign the 4 roles inside each team; hands on knees until it's your turn.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Phonics Hero — Magic E повт. (b)
board → desks

Speed reading — the big strip. Vowel names chant + Magic E (a-e · i-e · o-e · u-e). There and back — beat your time! Wand recall: cake… kite… home… cube!

Sort — тетрадь стр.64 №2 (all vote first). Words up (cake · kite · home · cube · name · five) — say each together, then every child shows the vowel (a/i/o/u) → sort into the four columns. RallyCoach: partner checks before the next word.

Dictation — стр.64 №3 (everyone writes). Now YOU write. Listen: cake… word once → sounds + magic e → write → Show me! → reveal & self-check. Words: cake, kite, home, cube.

Story — стр.65 «Я знаю Magic E!» (settler). Chorus the frames, finger under the words: I can read cake… kite… home… cube! I know Magic E! ⭐ Star task: circle every Magic E word. Colour the star «Я молодец!» (стр.64).

Sight words. Flash love · another: chorus, air-write — 🦝 Speaky: I LOVE pizza! Can I have ANOTHER one? (today's topic words!)

🎮 Reading-game option: read-race — ⚔ battle mode, level «Magic E»: a word pops up, everyone reads it aloud, the fastest correct team races ahead. Swap it in for the speed-reading strip when the screen is free.
💪 Support: in the sort give a vowel card to copy; in dictation write c_k_. ⭐ Extension: a 5th dictation word by ear (bike); read the story solo.

✔ Assess: the dictation lines check that Magic E is secure before the /эй/·/и:/·/ай/ spellings coming this unit; note who still drops the silent e.
