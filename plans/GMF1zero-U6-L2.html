Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask where a person is and say where he or she is, choosing he / she correctly: “Where's (Beth)? — She's on the swing.” / “Where's (Jake)? — He's on the roundabout.” (Pupil's Book p. 61 dialogue & Grammar box), and act out the “lost sister / lost brother” dialogue.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — L1 park objects + “What's this?”, U1 prepositions in/on/under, Genki «Where is Mr Monkey?» — woven into today's question (“Where's Beth? — She's ON the slide!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.30 (a) первое знакомство: sound /ё:/ in three houses er · ir · ur (meet → blend → trace girl, turn, тетрадь стр.83); listen for detail (PB p. 61 ex. 2 “Listen and circle”); tricky review — Red set (I · the · he · she · we · me · be · you · your).
  • Personal. Insist on the FULL question (“Where's Beth?”, not just a point); model he / she equally; every task keeps ALL children responding (own cards / thumbs / all pairs at once) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Where's (Beth)?” about a person in the park;
  • answer with the right pronoun + place: “She's on the swing. / He's on the slide.” and act out the “lost family” dialogue;
  • blend & read the first er/ir/ur words (her, sister, bird, girl, turn, purple) and trace the letters.
Target language
Lexis

Structure
Where's (Beth)? — She's on the swing.  ·  Where's (Jake)? — He's on the roundabout.  ·  I'm lost!  ·  Look! Come on. Let's play, too.  ·  preview (L4): They're (behind / next to) the (tree).
Form
Where + 's (is) + name? → He's (boy) / She's (girl) + on the + place. Rising tone on the question ↗, falling on the answer ↘.
Pron. watch
he / she chosen by the person, not by luck; contracted He's /hiːz/, She's /ʃiːz/; /ё:/ one sound in girl, her (no rolled r).
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.30 (a) первое знакомство: sound /ё:/ — er (her, sister) · ir (bird, girl) · ur (turn, purple) — trace girl · turn (тетрадь стр.83) · tricky review: Red set — I · the · he · she · we · me · be · you · your.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Point at a girl on the swing: “Beth is a girl — he or she?” → “She's on the swing!”
Point at a boy: “Jake is a boy — he or she?” → “He's on the slide!”
Phonics: “girl — how many sounds in ir?” → ONE /ё:/ — girl, bird (no rolled r).

Assumptions: the playground objects & the song It's fun in the park met in L1; “Where's my…? — It's here” & on from Unit 1; Genki «Where is Mr Monkey?» gives the He's on… feel; the /ау/·/ой/ houses consolidated last lesson → today Part opens with er·ir·ur. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → picture grids, models & audio, not printed text. Carpet & pair-work signals known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
He/She mixed upBoy card = He gesture (point to a boy), girl card = She; drill in pairs before the question game.
Just pointing, no questionRule “ask before you point”: back-chain …Beth? → Where's Beth?
Drop the place: “She's swing”Model the whole chunk with the ON gesture: She's ON the swing.
er/ir/ur read as two letters (rolled r)“Two letters — ONE sound /ё:/”: two fingers into a fist; no rolled r; the thinking hum errr…
Tic-tac-toe pairs squabble over squaresModel with Speaky first; rule “ask → partner answers → THEN mark”; RallyCoach roles.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game […] = fill-in slot 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Full plate — pick, don't cram. The core = dialogue model (24–33′) + one production game + Listen & circle. Buffers to drop first: the 3rd hide-and-guess round, tic-tac-toe grid 2, one Mix-Pair-Share swap. Cognitive load: only the he/she + place chunk is truly new — the park objects, prepositions & “Where's…?” feel are recycled, so scaffold he/she (boy/girl gesture) and keep everything else light. Offline reserve: every 🎮 has a paper twin (park poster / cards / dice).

Speed reading — тетрадь стр.83 №1. The long strip + ou·ow·oi·oy: There and back — beat your time! Vary the voice: Boys… girls… whisper… LOUD!

Meet er · ir · ur — стр.83 №2. One sound /ё:/, three houses: 🏠 er — her! sister! (thinking hum “errr…”) · 🏠 ir — bird! girl! · 🏠 ur — turn! purple! All copy each + gesture ×3. Check: hold up ir — Two letters… how many sounds? → every child shows ONE finger (no rolled r!).

Blend — Hub Station 3 (all together). /g/ /ir/ /l/… girl! /b/ /ir/ /d/… bird! /t/ /ur/ /n/… turn! Every child slides a “blend finger”.

Trace — стр.83 №3 (desks, settle). Trace by the arrows: er · ir · ur · girl · turn — whisper each. (The sort, dictation & story «Мальчик и море» of ур.30 come next lesson — today is first meeting only.)

Tricky review — Red set. Speed strip: I · the · he · she · we · me · be · you · your — there and back — HE and SHE — our question words today!

▶ Where's the paper person?
carpet, 3 teams

▶ Hide & Ask (Mido №16 idea, hide-and-guess version). A big park poster on the board; 🦝 Speaky secretly “hides” a paper Beth or Jake ON one of the objects (card in his pocket). On the board: a little path with 5 steps to the ice-cream 🍦.

Two teams take turns. Kagan — Numbered Heads: team huddles 10 sec, agrees on a guess, then the called number asks: Where's Beth? Is she on the swing? Before Speaky answers, the WHOLE class votes thumbs up/down. Speaky: No! She's not on the swing! → move one step; Yes! She's on the swing! → found — the team scores & everyone jumps up “Hooray!” and sits back.

3 quick rounds, new hiding spot each time. Reset if loud: Freeze!

◆ Support: let the team point to the object card as they ask. ▲ Extension: the guesser uses the full frame each time — Is she on the roundabout?

▶ Park tic-tac-toe

▶ Where's-she Tic-Tac-Toe (Mido №118, pairs re-skin — every pair plays at once). Each pair: one 3×3 grid — a boy or a girl figure on each park object + pencils (X / O).

Model on the board first. To take a square, A points to a figure & asks Where's she? B answers She's on the seesaw! → correct chunk → mark O & swap. Wrong pronoun/place → no mark, turn passes. Three in a row wins; flip the sheet — grid 2, change who starts.
