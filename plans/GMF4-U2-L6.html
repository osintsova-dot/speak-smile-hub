Week 26.10–01.11 · Lesson 16🏆 Flagship Team Quiz + reward~10 pupils · 2 teamsActivity Book p.24 (Review)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated Unit 2 — 12 character adjectives, 6 past life events, was/were, and “when I was (age)” — and shown it in the flagship Team Quiz with reward game.
  • Subsidiary. AB Review «Ready, Steady, Go!» (p.24): name character adjectives & opposites, complete was/were sentences, order a life timeline (learnt/started/moved/went), say who's famous in Hawaii; spelling check; self-assessment — readiness for the L7 test.
  • Personal. Quiz = every child answers (Showdown boards), not captains only; keep the reward game short and joyful — it's the cherry, not the cake. Both teams celebrate at the end.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name unit words from pictures AND spell key ones;
  • use was/were + past life events correctly;
  • self-assess honestly with the traffic light.

Structure
What's he/she like? — He's/She's…  ·  What were you like when you were young? — I was… / I wasn't…  ·  I learnt/started/moved/went… when I was (age).
Form
be + adjective; was (I/he/she) vs were (you/we/they) + wasn't/weren't; past simple regular vs irregular + when + age. Error harvest from the week feeds the quiz items.
Pron. watch
confident, quiet, learnt, wrote — the week's watch-points, recycled in quiz items.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is the quiz a test?” → no — it's training with fun; mistakes are how we learn.
“Who answers in the quiz — the captain?” → EVERYONE writes/answers.

Assumptions: All unit language introduced; today is consolidation before the test. Pupils know Showdown mechanics from earlier lessons and the number-code from the Starter.

 mini-whiteboards
24–49′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 2 teams

Flagship quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles: picture→adjective, adjective→opposite, was/were?, past form (go→?), “when I was…” sentence, order-the-timeline, famous-Hawaiian. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/says on their board → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a bowling / penalty shot (paper ball into a box). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Offline setup: card grid on the whiteboard + a real paper-ball penalty into a box/basket — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on “Show me!”)

Finale. Count scores together in English; both teams: Give me five!
