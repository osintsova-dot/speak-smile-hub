ЧТО ДЕЛАЕМ: презентации троек + Peer review по 3 вопросам ex.8; судейство по осям TB p.82

👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Каждая тройка — 2 минуты (все трое говорят). Судейские оси (TB p.82): Layout (крупный рисунок + подписи-стрелки к деталям) · Content (мало текста на слайде, подробности — голосом) · Language (заметки, не полные предложения; can для способностей + comparatives для сравнения) · Visuals (понятно без объяснений) — TB p.82.

  4. Языковой счёт ведёт учитель: галочка за каждую мишень (can / comparative / possessive / problem-solution).
  5. 🚀-бонус superlative award: Comparatives compare two. To crown ONE you need the superlative — not in this unit, but you're getting it: the MOST useful robot, the FASTEST robot. Вручаю 3 награды формулами со слайда.

Спецификация теста Unit 5 (ЧЕРНОВИК до официального пака Macmillan A1+)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: ability collocations — ___ a ball (catch), ___ a plane (fly), ___ a language (speak), ___ a bike (repair), ___ numbers (add), ___ stories (tell). B) 4 items: label the tech nouns from a picture (touchpad · charger · screen · speaker — ловушки keyboard/touchpad). C) 4 items: adjective opposites (cheap ↔ ___ · heavy ↔ ___ · difficult ↔ ___ · fast ↔ ___).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 6 gaps: can / can't affirmative & negative (Robots ___ get tired. · He ___ sing, but he ___ play the piano.). B) 4 items: order the words → a Can-question and a Wh-question + a true short answer (you / fly / can / a plane ?). C) 5 items: comparative forms across all five rules (big → ___ · easy → ___ · expensive → ___ · good → ___ · light → ___) + than-ловушка. D) 3 items: possessive pronouns (Is this your tablet? — No, it isn't ___ .).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A short talk about two gadgets (в духе SB p.65 ex.6–7; читает учитель дважды, если нет трека): 4 items — complete a table «You can …» с галочками + 4 items — true/false about price and size. Зеркалит Subskill: completing a table.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)An advert (30+ слов) for an object you want to sell. Marked for: can/can't + what you can do with it (3) · at least two comparatives with than (3) · adjectives + price/condition (2) · spelling of unit words (2) — зеркалит SB p.69 ex.8 Skills boost и Subskill: Spelling.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.69 — every format was met in class during the unit. Линии: 🚀 higher (Wh-вопросы с can письменно, все пять правил comparative, больше spelling-ловушек) · 🐣 standard (Yes/No-вопросы, comparative по правилам 1, 3 и 4, ловушки только знакомые).

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
