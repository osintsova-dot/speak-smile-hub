Revenge quiz: turning errors into points
🏁 REVENGE QUIZ · beat your own test
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: I rephrase the errors from the box into questions on the fly; crews write FULL corrections; hit 80% — the test is beaten

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I announce: REVENGE. The test beat some of you. Now WE beat the test. Errors on the board belong to the whole class!
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: everyone writes AT THE SAME TIME, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift together — I see everyone's answer, nobody can hide.
  3. I draw slips, rephrase them; crews write FULL corrections on mini-boards in 15 sec: Not ‘B’ — write: My brothers' room, TWO brothers!
  4. Rules: streak ×2 after 3 in a row · an interception on a miss.
  5. The counter “X of Y beaten”: 80% of the box → THE TEST IS OFFICIALLY BEATEN!
📺 On screen: the box · mini-boards · a counter on the board · leaderboard
👦 Students do: in crews they finish off their own errors with full corrections. Expected: loud, exciting, catharsis at 80%. Typical error: a single letter instead of a correction — doesn't count.

Unit 2 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ TRC pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 8 items: family definitions → word, spelling counts (My father's sister is my ___ → aunt; My sister's daughter is my ___ → niece). B) 6 items: body/face/hair — label the picture + opposites (long/short hair, curly/straight, fair/dark).
Part 2 · Grammar have got + 's (18 pts)A) 8 gaps aff/neg with contractions (My cousin ___ (+) blue eyes. We ___ (−) a car.). B) 6 items: order the words to make Have/Has…got? questions + write a true short answer (got / any / you / Have / cousins ?). C) 4 items: apostrophe surgery — put the apostrophe in the right place and say its job (my brothers room → brother's/brothers' · Lucys cat · Hes got glasses).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A short description of a family/two siblings (script in the test pack; teacher-read twice if no track): 4 true/false + 4 notes to complete (hair / eyes / family members / age) — mirrors SB p.29 ex.6–8.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A short email (6 sentences): ask someone to meet a friend + describe the friend. Marked for: have got forms correct (4) · apostrophes both jobs correct (3) · task complete: problem + description (3) — mirrors the CHECK list SB p.33 ex.7–8.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.33 — every format was met in class during the unit.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
