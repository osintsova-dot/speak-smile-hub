Week 08.03–14.03 · Lesson 3✨ Story · A day out · I want to go to the (museum). Let's go to the (…)!~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 19–20 Magic E (a-e i-e o-e u-e · p.53–57) · game: Steal the Bacon(19) → Day-out dash
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to follow & enjoy the story «A day out» and understand “I want to go to the (toy shop). Let's go to the (cinema)!” — Daddy takes Mimi (who loves dinosaurs) & Dylan (who loves space) on a day out, ending with the film Dinosaurs in Space.
  • Subsidiary. Predict from the pictures; listen with the story cards; play I want to go to…! & the dynamic Day-out dash (19); act tiny bits; answer story questions & circle the places they go (PB p.47); Phonics Hero — review the Magic E spellings a-e i-e o-e u-e, blend & speed-read p.53–57.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big warm Daddy voice, pauses, the fun of Mimi wanting dinosaurs and Dylan wanting space. Don't test every word; celebrate joining in. Give every child a place card so all are involved.
By the end, pupils can…
  • follow the story & point to what happens in each picture;
  • understand “I want to go to the (toy shop). Let's go!”;
  • name the places Mimi, Dylan & Daddy go to.

Pron. watch

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — review the Magic E family: silent e makes the vowel say its name — a-e i-e o-e u-e (cake, bike, home, cube). Jolly technique → blend → speed-read p.53–57.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Mimi loves dinosaurs — what does Dylan love?” → space!
“Daddy's surprise at the cinema — what is the film?” → Dinosaurs in Space!

Assumptions: All ten places & Do you want to go to the…? are active from L1–L2; reading continues in the Phonics Hero workbook (now Magic E). The story-time routine (listen · point · join in) is well established. I want to go to… / Let's go to… are new but very supported by mime.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Story audio a bit fastPlay once for fun, then again pausing on each picture; point & mime as you go.
Children lose the threadUse the six big story cards frame by frame; ask a yes/no picture question after each.
Everyone wants to run at onceGive one place card each; only the child with the called place runs.
Just says the place, not the phrasePuppet models I want to go to… the TOY SHOP!; child echoes with the mime.
Wriggling during listeningGive a job: wave when you hear a place! — active listening.

“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Mimi loves dinosaurs… Dylan loves space… and today Daddy takes them on a DAY OUT! Where will they go?! Dino stomp, then rocket-arms up.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lessons 19–20 · Magic E — sounds a-e · i-e · o-e · u-e  (silent e = long vowel). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: c-a-ke → cake, b-i-ke → bike, h-o-me → home, c-u-be → cube — the silent e gives a magic tap + arm-sweep + a picture. ▶ Magic-E relay — Speaky shows a word; teams add the silent e, blend it and race to touch its picture.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.53–57 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read come · some together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · Story «A day out»
carpet

Predict. Show the six story cards — Daddy takes Mimi & Dylan on a day out. Where do they want to go? Take 2–3 guesses (Russian OK).

Listen 1 — for fun. Play the story with the cards. Pause on each picture — enjoy Mimi & Dylan choosing places: I want to go to the toy shop! …the bookshop! Let's go to the cinema! Children point & join the places they can.

Listen 2 — spot the place. Wave when you hear TOY SHOP! children wave when they hear it — active listening.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): Navio App — play the town-places vocabulary game and watch the A day out story video on screen; children tap the place they hear.

 story cards ×6 · story audio
26–36′
Game · I want to go to…! + Day-out dash (19) ⚡
carpet / floor space

▶ I want to go to…! Say I want to go to… ? and mime a place; children name it. Fast, several rounds — everyone answers.

▶ Day-out dash Genki Steal the Bacon, re-skinned: two teams line up; place flashcards in the middle. 🦝 Speaky (Daddy) calls Let's go to… the museum! — one runner from each team races to grab it, mimes it and says the place. Every place called → everyone runs at least once.

💪 Support: run, grab & mime the place. ⭐ Extension: say the whole line Let's go to the museum!

 place flashcards ×10 · Speaky (Daddy)
36–42′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Where Do You Live?» aloud — Speaky explores his map of town — houses, a park, a river — a gentle places read, calm after the running game and perfect for our day-out story.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line I want to go here! Let's go!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Point to each place on Speaky's map — children guess Let's go to the…! and mime it; cheer at the end.

42–48′
Perform · act the story «A day out»
carpet, standing

Act it out. Play the story again; children mime through it — choosing places, walking to the toy shop, the bookshop, and the BIG surprise film at the end. Pick 2 to be Mimi & Dylan; the class mimes each place.

Everyone in. each time someone says Let's go to the…!, the whole class mimes the place loudly.
