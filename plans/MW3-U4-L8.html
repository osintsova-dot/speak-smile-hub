Week 21.12–27.12 · Lesson 32🎨 Project · My favourite wild animal poster~8 pupils · make + presentPH 24 (/эй/ ai·ay·a-e · p.67–68) · Genki: What's Your Favorite…?(55)·I Like Animals(15)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a «My favourite animal» poster — choosing a wild animal, drawing & colouring it, and saying: «My favourite animal is a (lion). It's (yellow). It's got (big teeth)!» in a mini gallery.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle all ten animals + «It's a… / It's got…» through a creative product; sing Genki «What's Your Favorite…?» (55) & «I Like Animals» (15); take turns presenting; Phonics Hero — /эй/ ai·ay·a-e, proud speed-read p.67–68; happy end-of-unit gallery.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the presenting sentence first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (one sentence each) so no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • choose & draw a favourite wild animal;
  • present it — «My favourite animal is a lion. It's got big teeth!»;
  • say «Well done!» to a friend's poster.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“How many animals for your poster — one favourite, or all ten?” → one favourite!
“Your friend shows their poster — what do we say?” → Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 4 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can name & describe the ten animals; reading is at the /эй/ spellings. They know classroom making routines.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Can't decide on an animalOffer “point to your favourite animal card” or a quick eeny-meeny to choose.
Making takes over the lessonSimple poster frame ready; time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; a wave + showing the poster is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone claps & says Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentPresent in a fast carousel, one sentence each; or in small groups at once.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 24 · /эй/ ai·ay·a-e (again) — sounds ai · ay · a-e — snake · cake · rain · play. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read your favourite /эй/ words — proud & fluent: snake · cake · rain · day · play. ▶ Speed-read stars

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.67–68 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read review Red tricky words together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).
