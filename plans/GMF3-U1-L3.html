Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, sequence, read and act out the story «The new school» and talk about the school day: “When do we have PE? / I'm not very good at Music. / Josh is brilliant at PE!”
  • Subsidiary. Reading strategies — predict from pictures → listen for gist → sequence by ear → read to confirm (Duke & Pearson); recycle L1–L2 Wh-questions & school places; Values — helping new pupils at school; seed “good at + subject” for L4 (noticed today, drilled in L4).
  • Personal. Hold books CLOSED until the sequence is built from the ear — reading is the late step, not the first. Let the story breathe: big voices, pauses, prediction. Give every child a story role and a turn to run.
By the end, pupils can…
  • put the 8 story pictures in order from the audio (books closed);
  • read their role's lines and act out a frame;
  • correct false statements: “No, it doesn't. School begins at nine o'clock.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Josh is brilliant at PE — is he GOOD or VERY, very good?” → very, very good!
“The swimming pool — is it IN the school or NEXT TO the school?” → next to it.

Assumptions: Wh-questions and the 10 school places are active from L1–L2; the alphabet code (A=4, step +4) was learnt in the Starter (recycled in the pre-reading). Act-out routine was modelled in the Starter. Readers follow text with a finger — but only after the sequence is built by ear. Teams: 4 max; 10 = 3+3+4.

9–15′
① Look & predict · ② describe
carpet · pictures only

Books to the pictures — NO reading yet. Open to pp.10–11 but Cover the speech bubbles with your hand. Just look at the pictures. (Or show the 8 frames on the projector.)

Teacher questions → pupils describe/predict (dialogic). Frame by frame: What can you see? Where are the children? Which lesson is this? How does Josh feel here? Pupils answer freely. Use dialogic moves — revoice (So you think Josh is worried — why?) and press (What makes you say that?). Russian words OK, recast into English.

💪 Support: point to the child's face / the object to prompt a word. ⭐ Extension: What do you think happens at the end?

First listening (CD1 16, books CLOSED). Set ONE gist task before you press play: Listen — how many lessons do the children have today? And… does Josh find the bracelet?

20–30′
④ Sequence by ear ⑤ glue · comprehension
carpet → desks

Books stay closed — order the story from the ear. Each team gets the 8 story pictures shuffled. Play CD1 16 again: Listen and lay the pictures in order. Books closed — use your ears! Teams race to lay the 8 frames left→right.

▶ Sequence race (teams). First team with the correct order calls Ready!; you check against the audio, not the book. Replay any tricky frame.

✔ Assess: the laid-out order is your check — which teams followed the story by ear? Note who needs the second listening with pauses.

💪 Support: pre-number the backs of 2 anchor cards (1 and 8). ⭐ Extension: Say what happens in each picture: First… Then… After the break…

30–39′
⑥ Read the story · Read & correct
desks → carpet

NOW open the book and read (books open pp.10–11, Listen 2 with pauses). The sequence is built — reading confirms it. Finger under the text; echo / chunked reading, frame by frame. Model one think-aloud: The first lesson is Arts and Crafts… so Josh says “I'm not very good at Arts and Crafts.” — he's worried! Raise a “good AT” card each time it sounds. Class echoes the key lines: When do we have PE? — We have PE after the break!

After you read · Read and correct (act. 3, Talk Partners). Statements on the board: 1 School begins at eight o'clock. 2 The first lesson is Music. 3 Josh can play the drums. 4 The swimming pool is in the school. 5 Josh can't swim. Pairs correct the false ones FULLY: No, it doesn't. School begins at nine o'clock. Every pair speaks at least one correction.

💪 Support: point to the frame that holds the answer. ⭐ Extension: add a second true fact about that character.

⑦ Act out
carpet, teams of 4

Model a frame. You + 3 pupils play the swimming-pool frame with big drama: Wow! This swimming pool is amazing! Kick your legs, everyone!

Teams of 4 (Lily · Josh · Ravi · Teacher/Coach). Rehearse 2 frames with the book as a script. Rotate roles once so a quieter child gets a short line.

47–55′
▶ Ask & Grab + Story dash
carpet / floor, teams

▶ Спроси-и-хватай · Ask & Grab. 8 A4 cards on the floor: bracelet · drums · basketball · swimming pool · lighthouse · Arts and Crafts · Music · PE. Each team sends ONE runner (rotate every round so everyone runs). The team asks YOU a yes/no story question — you answer No! (new question) or Yes! → runners grab the matching card, fastest keeps it. Example: Is the swimming pool IN the school? — No! … Is it NEXT TO the school? — Yes! → grab swimming pool. You choose the “yes” moment (tease them). Recycles Is / Can / Does…? question forms on story facts.

▶ Story dash relay (эстафета). Teams line up. A rotating caller holds a prompt: Who finds the bracelet? / When do we have PE? / What is Josh brilliant at? The team RallyRobins the answer (all whisper it first), then the front runner races to the board, taps the picture/word, and says the FULL sentence: Josh finds the bracelet! / We have PE after the break! Correct = a point; rotate the runner every question — everyone runs and everyone speaks.

🎮 Projector option: run the dash on the board — a rotating child taps, the WHOLE class chants the sentence.

ICQ: Do you run first or say the sentence first? (→ whisper the sentence, then run & say it out loud)
