Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, read and act out the story «The new school» and talk about the school day: “When do we have PE? / I'm not very good at Music. / Josh is brilliant at PE.”
  • Subsidiary. Break Luke's code again («GOOD LUCK at your new school!») — recycles the Starter code; Values — helping new pupils at school; story skills: predict → listen → read along → Read & correct → act out; prepare the “good at + subject” idea for L4.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, prediction; “good at” is NOTICED today, drilled properly in L4 — don't over-correct. Give every child a story role.
By the end, pupils can…
  • retell 2–3 things that happen on the first day;
  • read their role's lines from the book;
  • correct false statements: “No, school begins at nine o'clock.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Josh is brilliant at PE — is he GOOD or BAD at PE?” → very, very good!
“The children are new — is it their FIRST day or their LAST day?” → first day.

Assumptions: Wh-questions and 12 places are active from L1–L2; the alphabet code was learnt in the Starter (recycled in the pre-reading). First full-unit story of the year — act-out routine was modelled in the Starter. Readers follow the text with a finger.

Code pre-reading too hardSolve the first two letters together (28=G, 60=O); give the A=4 key card — it's a warm hook, not a test.
“good at” + wrong prepositionAnchor: good AT with a thumbs-up gesture; notice-and-mark when it sounds in the audio — no lecture.

Read & correct · comprehension
carpet → desks

Read and correct (act. 3, Talk Partners). Statements: School begins at eight o'clock… true or false? Pairs correct the false ones fully: No, it doesn't. School begins at nine o'clock. 5 statements — every pair speaks.

Who am I? (Showdown). Mini-whiteboards: you read a clue — I can't play the drums. Who am I? — all write the name (Josh), Show me! 4 rounds.

✔ Assess: the boards are your check — who follows the story? The rest sit with you in the board game.

💪 Support: point to the right frame in the book. ⭐ Extension: add a second true fact about that character.

28–40′
Act out
carpet, groups of 4

Model a frame. You + 3 pupils play the swimming-pool frame with big drama: Wow! This swimming pool is amazing! Kick your legs, everyone!

Groups of 4 (Lily · Josh · Ravi · Mr Green/teacher). Rehearse 2 frames with books as scripts. Rotate roles once.

Showtime. Each group performs ONE frame (30–40 sec — fast carousel, no long waits). Audience task: shout the lesson (Arts and Crafts / Music / PE) at the end.

💪 Support: shortest lines / the “everyone in the pool” chorus for the shyest child. ⭐ Extension: add an extra line from the frame.
