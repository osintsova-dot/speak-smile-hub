Week 07.12–13.12 · Lesson 28🎵 Song focus · «The party surprise» · I'm a (tiger)! Grrr!~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 22 (Magic E o-e·u-e · p.61–63) · Genki: Animal Voices(18) · games: 3-2-1 Jump!(34)·Ball & Music(16)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act out the story song «The party surprise» (CD2 T8) — «I'm a tiger! Grrr! I'm a snake! Hiss!» — becoming each party animal with its sound, and answer «What's that?» about the animals.
  • Subsidiary. Re-tell the story waving on each animal; mime-guess-say the party animals; match animals & characters and circle a favourite (PB p.24); sing with Genki «Animal Voices» (18); Phonics Hero — Magic E o-e · u-e, speed-read p.61–63.
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions & sounds, then words. Repeat without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (loud/quiet/slow-mo). If they over-excite, mime the animals VERY slowly to calm down (Teacher's tip).
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing «The party surprise» with the animal actions;
  • say «I'm a (lion)!» + the animal sound;
  • match each animal to its party character.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“In the song, do we SIT still or MOVE like the animals?” → move!
“I'm a tiger — am I the tiger, or is the tiger over there?” → I AM the tiger!

Assumptions: The song met in L2–L3 and the story in L3 — today deepens both. Children can name & sound the animals. Magic E reading continues. Pre-readers sing by ear + pictures, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by repetition of the songChange the mode each time: whisper it, giant-voice it, slow-motion, super-fast.
Over-excited mimingSlow the actions right down (Teacher's tip) — calming — then speed up again.
Only a few singGive everyone an animal + sound job; the sound counts even without the words.
Cards get grabbed in the gameOne animal each; mime for the class; class guesses, then all say I'm a…
Energy too high before the tableFreeze + a slow breathing verse before sitting down.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Open your eyes… for the party surprise! I'm a tiger! Grrr! Who are YOU? Everyone strikes an animal pose.

▶ Animal flash. quick What's that? round with the animal cards to warm the words.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 22 · Magic E (again) — sounds Magic E: o-e · u-e — rose · flute · June. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Blend with the magic e: b-o-ne → bone, t-u-be → tube, c-u-te → cute — sweep + a picture. ▶ Magic-E hop

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.61–63 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read always · upon together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–25′
Curiosity input · the song in stages
carpet, standing

Sing & move. Re-tell the story; children wave every time they hear a wild animal. Then play «The party surprise» (CD2 T8): Round 1 — join the SOUNDS & actions; Round 2 — add the words; Round 3 — hands round eyes on Open your eyes!

Ask Which animal do you want to be? Children respond, then become that animal.

💪 Support: do the actions & sounds. ⭐ Extension: sing the whole line: I'm a snake! Hiss!

 «The party surprise» (CD2 T8) · story pictures
25–31′
Game · 3-2-1 Jump! (34)
carpet, standing

▶ 3-2-1 Jump! Genki 3-2-1 Jump!: everyone in a circle with an animal card. Speaky calls 3… 2… 1… tigers! — children with that animal say I'm a tiger! Grrr! and JUMP into the middle, then back.

All cards up, everyone watching for their animal — fast, no waiting.

 animal cards (one per child) · Speaky
31–37′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Speaky's Pets» aloud — Speaky shows his pets one by one and asks what each one is — a gentle read between the jumping and the party song that recycles It's a… / What's that? with even more animals.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line What's that? — It's a…!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each spread — children ask What's that?, name the pet and make its sound; a calm animal cuddle before we party.

▶ Ball & Music (16) A quick Ball & Music with Genki «Animal Voices» (18): pass a soft toy; when it stops, that child says I'm a…! and the class copies its sound.
