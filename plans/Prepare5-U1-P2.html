Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to write a short text (~80 words) — a magazine-competition entry about the last cool thing they bought — using shopping lexis, determiners and a range of tenses, in the format of B1 Preliminary Writing Part 2 / ЕГЭ personal writing.
  • Subsidiary. Consolidate the “any” family (any / anyone / anything / anywhere / any more / any better / any good) — meaning by sentence type (positive · negative · question); recycle spread-1 shopping vocabulary & determiners; reading for gist and for language (model-text deconstruction).
  • Personal. Keep TTT low; run a clear I-Do → We-Do → You-Do on the model text; give an ICQ before every task; monitor the writing and feed error correction back at the end, not during composing.
By the end, learners can…
  • use any correctly across positive / negative / question sentences;
  • identify the 5 parts of a short competition text in a model;
  • plan and write ~80 words that answer all the questions, add extra detail and mix tenses;
  • peer-check a text against a 4-point checklist.

Writing skill
A short text ~80 words: PLAN = what · where · why · what friends think · extra (price / discount / problem) → answer all Qs · add extra info · use a range of tenses.
Useful chunks
Last month, I bought… · I chose it because… · There was a promotion, so I got a 10% discount. · My friends think… · I’ve already…
Pron. watch
weak /ˈeni/ in connected speech; stress in ANYthing / ANYwhere; contrast any (any old one) vs ANY (emphatic).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I haven’t got any money.” — some, or zero? → zero (negative = same as no).
“Any colour will do.” — one special colour, or it doesn’t matter? → doesn’t matter (positive).
“Is there anywhere to eat?” — statement or question? → question.

Assumptions: shopping lexis & determiners (some/much/many/no…) were met on spread 1 (Lesson 4) — this lesson recycles them. Learners are confident readers/writers of connected text and know the basic B1 exam layout. Grouping: pairs for coaching, teams of 4 for the game; adjust if numbers differ.

Guided discovery (SB p.12, Vocabulary any, ex 1). Learners read the three examples and complete the rules with negative / positive / question: (a) with a positive verb, any = it doesn’t matter which; (b) with a negative verb, any = no; (c) use any in a question. Elicit, don’t tell.

CCQ check. Fire the three CCQs (see card). Colour-code on the board: Positive = it doesn’t matter · Negative = zero · Question = is there even one?

ANY — ONE WORD, MANY USES: the whole family (anyone/anybody · anything · anywhere · any + noun · not…any more/any longer · any better · any good · hardly any) with a definition + example each, plus the decision strip POSITIVE / NEGATIVE / QUESTION. Learners mark the three sentence types in three colours and write four sentences of their own — one per meaning.

Controlled practice (SB p.12, ex 3). Choose the right form — anything / any good · any more / any better · anyone · anywhere. ▶ RallyCoach in pairs: A does & thinks aloud, B coaches & praises, then swap. ICQ: Check the verb first, the meaning second.

Compose (~80 words). Write your own short text for the competition. Answer all five points, use at least three shopping words and two tenses. About 80 words. Timer visible.

Monitor silently. Do not correct while composing — note recurring errors (especially any, tense, determiners) for the feedback board. Nudge under-writers with the frame strip; challenge fast finishers to add a rhetorical question or a “problem/result” line.
