👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Возвращаю 2 домика: Дом 1: пропала буква (he's = he is · don't) · Дом 2: хозяин (Ben's train).
  2. Охота ex.4: Find EVERY apostrophe in the email. Which ONE shows the owner? (ключ TB: Ben's parents; ловушка: «Ben's 13» = Ben IS!).
  3. Ex.5 цепочкой устно: контракция → полная форма (I hope you are well · I have got · Ben is 13 · He is not very tall · He has got…).
  4. CCQ: Ben's 13 — owner? — NO! Ben IS. House one!
📺 Показываем: доска: 2 домика (остаются) · учебник p.32
👦 Дети: расселяют каждый апостроф по домикам. Ожидаемо: ловушка «Ben's 13» ловит и учит. Типичная ошибка: «Ben's parents = Ben is parents?!» — смех + домик 2.

📋 Справка для подготовки (читать ДО урока, не в классе)
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can write a 5–7 sentence favour email by steps with correct apostrophes (both jobs) and have got — and peer-check it by 4 boxes.
  • Subsidiary. Joshua model → 5-move skeleton; subskill: apostrophes (possession vs contraction); contraction blitz; writing cycle THINK→PREPARE→WRITE→CHECK in timed portions; «market» vote; Quick review lightning = the map of the test.
  • Personal. Writing pair — never 20 minutes of silence: portions with timers, market with chips. The two jobs of the apostrophe get their own visual (2 домика from P3 return!). Step tracker ritual continues.
By the end, students can…
  • reproduce the 5-move email skeleton
  • tell apostrophe jobs apart (owner / verb)
  • write a favour email in portions
  • peer-check by 4 concrete boxes

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Ben's 13 — owner or missing letter?” → missing letter: Ben IS 13.
“Do we write the email in one go?” → No — two portions with a timer.
“In peer review do we say ‘плохо’?” → No — check the 4 boxes and help fix.

Assumptions: have got (all forms) + possessive 's live after P3–P4; the favour situation is real for kids (meet someone, feed a cat, bring keys). Apostrophe's two jobs met separately — today unified.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
