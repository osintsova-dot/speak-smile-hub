🦝 Give Me Five! 1 (с нуля) · Unit 5 “Fun on the farm” · Занятие 41 / L2 — Grammar: Can you see a…? Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия с нуля) · 60 min · Dialogue → controlled drill → freer → production · carpet + desks
Week 01.02–07.02 · Занятие 41✨ GRAMMAR~12 pupils · pairs + 3 teams🦸 Phonics Hero ур.27a · /оу/ oa·ow·o-e
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask if someone can see a farm animal and answer yes / no: “Can you see a (cow)? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.” (Pupil's Book p. 51 dialogue & Grammar box), and act out the “find Toby the dog” dialogue.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — L1 farm animals + “What's this? — It's a…”, Unit 2 “Is it a…?”, colours & numbers — woven into today's finding game (“Can you see a brown cow? — Yes, I can!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.27 (a) первое знакомство: sound /оу/ in three houses oa · ow · o-e (meet → blend → trace goat, snow, тетрадь стр.75); listen for detail (PB p. 51 ex. 2 “Listen and circle”); tricky words does, their.
  • Personal. Insist on the FULL question (“Can you see a cow?”, not “Cow?”); model both Yes- and No-answers equally; every task keeps ALL children responding (own cards / thumbs / all pairs at once) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Can you see a (goat)?” about the farm scene;
  • answer “Yes, I can. / No, I can't.” honestly and act out the “find Toby” dialogue;
  • blend & read the first oa/ow/o-e words (goat, boat, snow, window, home, nose) and trace the letters.
Target language
Lexis

Structure
Can you see a (cow)? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.  ·  Can you see Toby? — No, I can't.  ·  The cat is here. But where's Toby?  ·  preview (L4): Cows can't fly. / Ducks can swim.
Form
Can + you + see + a + noun? → short answers Yes, I can. / No, I can't (= cannot). Rising tone on the question ↗, falling on the answer ↘.
Pron. watch
keep -n't in can't /kɑːnt/; weak “Can you” /kən jə/, strong can in the answer; don't let the question shrink to one word.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.27 (a) первое знакомство: sound /оу/ — oa (goat, boat) · ow at the end (snow, window) · o-e (home, nose) — trace goat · snow (тетрадь стр.75) · sight words: does, their.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Point at the busy farm: “Is the horse THERE? Can you find it?” → yes → “So: Yes, I can!”
“Can you see a shark on the farm?” → no → “No, I can't!” (head-shake).
Phonics: “goat — how many sounds in oa?” → ONE /оу/ — goat, boat.

Assumptions: the 10 farm animals + “What's this? — It's a…” met in L1; “Can you see a…?” heard in the L1 song On the farm; “Is it a…?” from Unit 2; single sounds, digraphs & Magic E known; /ай/ (ур.26) consolidated last lesson → today Part 4 opens with /оу/ oa·ow·o-e. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → picture grids & audio, not printed text. Carpet & pair-work signals known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
One-word guesses: “Cow?” instead of “Can you see a cow?”Speaky won't answer until the FULL question; back-chain …a cow? → see a cow? → Can you see a cow?
Yes-bias: “Yes, I can” for everythingDrill No-answers with a big head-shake first; ask about animals that are NOT on the page (shark, lion).
Drop -n't: “No, I can”Exaggerated choral drill No, I can't with the head-shake gesture every time.
Digraph oa read as two letters /о-а/“Two letters — ONE sound /оу/”: merge two fingers into a fist; the goat says goat!
Tic-tac-toe pairs squabble over squaresModel with Speaky first; rule “ask → partner answers → THEN mark”; RallyCoach roles — partner is the checker.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game […] = fill-in slot 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Full plate, so pick, don't cram. The core = dialogue model (24–33′) + one production game + Listen & circle. Buffers to drop first if short: the 3rd secret-animal round, the tic-tac-toe grid 2, one Mix-Pair-Share swap. Better one full question (“Can you see a cow?”) said well by every child than three stages half-done. Cognitive load: only the Yes/No short answers are new — the animals, colours & “What's this?” are recycled, so scaffold the question (back-chain, hands-behind-back rule) and keep everything else light. Offline reserve: every 🎮 has a paper twin (flashcards / farm poster / dice) — no board needed.

Speed reading — тетрадь стр.75 №1. The strip so far: single sounds + digraphs + Magic E + ie·igh·y·i-e. There and back — beat your time! Vary the voice: Boys… girls… whisper… LOUD!

Meet oa · ow · o-e — стр.75 №2. One sound /оу/, three houses: 🏠 oa — goat! boat! · 🏠 ow at the end — snow! window! · 🏠 o-e — home! nose! (magic e makes o say its name). All copy each + gesture ×3. Check: hold up oa — Two letters… how many sounds? → every child shows ONE finger.

Blend — Hub Station 3 / стр.75 (all together). Sound windows open one by one: /g/ /oa/ /t/… goat! /h/ /o/… nose? no — /n/ /o/ /se/… nose! Then boat · snow — picture for meaning; every child slides a “blend finger” in the air.

Trace — стр.75 №3 (desks, settle). Trace by the arrows, start at the yellow dot: oa · ow · o-e · goat · snow — whisper each as you write. (The sort, dictation & story of ур.27 come next lesson — today is first meeting only.)

Sight words. Flash does · their: chorus ×3, air-write — Can you see THEIR dog? (today's finding words).

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — the farm scene ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 find-it-u5
Screen: a busy farm scene. Mechanic: the WHOLE class asks together Can you see a goat? → a child taps → it glows if it's there → Yes, I can! Ask for an animal that's NOT there → No, I can't! Pupils: every child points at their own animal card while asking = no watchers. Offline: the paper farm poster + pointing.

Act out (ALL pairs at once). Pairs on the carpet, each with a small farm picture. A asks: Can you see a [horse]? B scans & answers: Yes, I can! / No, I can't. Swap pictures, change roles. ICQ: Do you say “cow” or “Can you see a cow”? (→ the full question!)

◆ Support: weaker child is the asker with a strip of animal pictures to read. ▲ Extension: add a colour to the question — Can you see a white sheep?

33–40′
▶ Guess the secret animal
carpet, 3 teams

▶ Speaky's Secret Animal (Mido №16 “Hungman”, guessing rescue version). 8 animal pictures in a row on the board; 🦝 Speaky secretly picks ONE (card in his pocket). On the board: a fence with 5 planks — a little Toby climbing home 🐕.

Two teams take turns. Kagan — Numbered Heads: team huddles 10 sec, agrees on a guess, then the called number asks for the team: Can you see a sheep? Before Speaky answers, the WHOLE class votes thumbs up/down — what will he say? Speaky: No, I can't! → cross that animal out + add one plank to the fence — everyone jumps up “MOO!” and sits back (keeps all moving between guesses). Yes, I can! → Toby is found, the guessing team scores.

3 quick rounds, new secret each time; wrong guesses shrink the choice, so the logic is visible. Reset if loud: Freeze!

◆ Support: let the team point to their chosen animal card as they ask. ▲ Extension: the guesser adds a colour clue — Can you see a black horse?

⭐ Swap (rotation): if you ran a board-guessing game last lesson, swap in ▶ Fly-swatters — 8 animal cards on the board; call Can you see a duck? and two players race to swat it, while everyone else holds up their own matching mini-card at the same time.

45–53′
▶ Farm tic-tac-toe
desks, pairs

▶ Farm Tic-Tac-Toe (Mido №118, pairs re-skin — every pair plays at once, nobody waits). Each pair: one 3×3 grid of animal pictures + pencils (X / O).

Model on the board first. To take a square you must ASK for it: A covers a picture with a hand, B asks Can you see a cow? A peeks: Yes, I can! → B marks O and they swap. Named wrong → No, I can't! → no mark, turn passes. Three in a row wins; then flip the sheet — grid 2, change who starts.

Kagan — RallyCoach: partner = checker; praise the full question: Good question! Teacher monitors, feeds fast finishers grid 2.

◆ Support: weaker child is the checker first (just peeks & says Yes, I can / No, I can't), then swap to asking. ▲ Extension: add a colour to win the square — Can you see a green frog?
