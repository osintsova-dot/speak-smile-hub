Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have reviewed all of Unit 7 — ten places in town, Do you want to go to the…?, I want to go to the (park), the story «A day out» & the museum — and shown it in the flagship Town Team Game with Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41) as the reward.
  • Subsidiary. “I can…” self-assessment with happy/sad faces; every child answers (all show at once, then teams); recycle the language song (CD T19) & Genki «Where Are You Going?» (56); listen, point & answer (PB p.50); Phonics Hero — review the /iː/ spellings ee ea e-e y, speed-read p.69–71; a joyful, both-teams-win finish.
  • Personal. Team game = EVERY child answers (mime it / show your card / stand up), not just the fastest. Keep the reward short & happy — it's the cherry. Both teams cheer at the end; no sore losers.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all ten places & say where I want to go;
  • answer “Do you want to go to the…? — Yes, please. I want to go to the park.”;
  • show what I can do with a happy/sad face (self-check).

11–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lesson 25 · /iː/ — sounds ee · ea · e-e · y  (all say /iː/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds: s-ee → see, ea-t → eat, th-e-se → these, happ-y — big arm-sweep + a picture. ▶ Reading relay — each child reads one /iː/ word to earn a review star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.69–71 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read see · we together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

24–47′
🏆 TOWN TEAM GAME + Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41)
carpet, 2 teams

Flagship game. Spin the Big Wheel (app or pointer) — it lands on a pod = a challenge: name the place on that colour · mime it · invite Do you want to go to the…? · answer Yes, please / No, thanks · say I want to go to the park · what happens in «A day out»? Challenge shown → Show me! EVERY child mimes/says it → most-correct team gets a point.

Reward: Genki «World Cup Soccer!»: draw a pitch on the board with a 2-D ball; for each team point, one player kicks the ball one space toward the goal — GOAL wins the round. Rotate the kicker every time.

🎮 Offline setup: Big Wheel mat + place challenge cards on the carpet; a board pitch + cut-out ball (magnet/blu-tack) for the reward kick — one kick per point, quick. (Или мягкий мяч в обруч — basketball-вариант.)

ICQ: When do you answer? (→ on Show me! only)   Finale. both teams: Well done! Give me five!

✔ Assess: across the game — who names places · invites · answers politely · says I want to go to… · recalls the story? A picture of the whole unit.
