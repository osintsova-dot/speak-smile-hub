Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, read and act out the story «Great teamwork!» and retell past events: “We carried some water. / We all jumped together. / It was fantastic!”
  • Subsidiary. Break Luke's code («DOCUMENTARY») — recycles the alphabet code; Values — working together / teamwork; story skills: predict → listen → read along → Ask & answer → act out; notice the Past-Simple -ed verbs (carried, jumped, climbed) that L4 will drill.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, prediction; the -ed verbs are NOTICED today, drilled properly in L4 — don't over-correct. Give every child a role; use the red/blue teams from the story.
By the end, pupils can…
  • retell 2–3 things the teams do on the show;
  • read their role's lines from the book;
  • answer comprehension questions: “It's called Teamwork.”

Listen 1 — gist (CD3 49, books closed). Listen: how many games do the teams play? Check on fingers (three).

Listen 2 — read along (books open pp.84–85). Finger under the text. Pause per frame: What's the first game? — Carry water through rings! Who wins the first game? — The red team! Raise a “-ed” card each time a past verb sounds (carried, jumped, climbed).

Values (act. 4). Who wants to win alone? Do YOU prefer working alone or in a team? Why is teamwork good? 3–4 hands, Russian OK, recast key words.

Act out the game show
carpet, groups of 4

Model a frame. You + 3 pupils play the water-race frame with big drama: The teams work together to carry water through some rings!

Groups of 4 (Ravi · Josh · Lily · Presenter). Rehearse 2 frames with books as scripts — the red/blue team energy. Rotate roles once.

Showtime. Each group performs ONE frame (30–40 sec — fast carousel, no long waits). Audience task: shout the game (water / flag / jump) at the end.

💪 Support: shortest line / the “One, two, three, jump!” chorus for the shyest child. ⭐ Extension: add an extra line from the frame.

40–52′
“Teamwork” challenge relay
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Re-run the show (cooperative relay). Two teams do 3 mini-challenges like the story — but the “prize” is LANGUAGE: at each station a card asks What did you do? — We carried the water / We climbed the slide / We jumped together! Only a full past sentence wins the point. Whole team says it together.

🎮 Projector option: show the 3 game frames on the board; a rotating child names the game as the WHOLE class says the past sentence.

ICQ: Do you win by running fast or by saying the sentence? (→ say the sentence together!)

Both teams: Great teamwork! — celebrate together, the story's value.
