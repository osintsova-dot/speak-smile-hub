Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. Assess Unit 8 language — clothes, outdoor activities, “What are you wearing? — I'm wearing…”, “What's he/she doing? — He's/She's …ing” (present continuous) — with the GMF1 Unit 8 Standard Level Test (Listening · Reading · Writing · Read & write · Speaking), in a calm, confidence-first atmosphere.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics Hero — free reading day: re-read favourite Часть-1 stories at speed (тетрадь стр.7–29), calm and celebratory on test day; exam micro-skills: work alone, listen twice, check your page.
  • Personal. Model-first: show every task type before the paper is out; no competitive games today; keep voice low and pace unhurried; no child ever sits with nothing to do (quiet poster task during Speaking turns).
By the end, pupils can…
  • complete all written parts of the Unit 8 test independently;
  • answer the Speaking questions one-to-one (What's this? · What are you wearing? · What's he/she doing?);
  • write clothes words from initial letters (s___ = shorts, T-___ = T-shirt);
  • leave smiling — the test is a “show what I know” day, not a scary day.

Structure

Form
Test answers: tick · number · match · write from initial letters · full frames He's/She's …ing.
Pron. watch
Speaking part: accept clear meaning over perfect accent; gently remodel, don't correct mid-test.
Phonics
Phonics Hero: free reading — favourite Часть-1 stories re-read for speed (тетрадь стр.7–29). A celebration of “I can read!”, not new work.
Concept checks (ICQs) · Assumptions
Before the paper: Do we work alone or with a friend? → alone! Is it OK to not know one? → yes — skip it, come back!
Listening: How many times do we listen? → two times — first just listen, then write.

Assumptions: all task types rehearsed in L5 (review + AB p. 87) and via the unit trainer at home; children write by copying / initial letters; anxiety possible for some → breathing ritual + model-first + Speaky cheering, not judging. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Differentiation
  • 💪 Support (needs more). Seat these children within arm's reach; read every instruction aloud again right beside them, point to the worked example, keep the word box visible, and give extra time — hold the next listening item until their pencil moves. For ex. 4/5 let them copy the board model and swap the clothes/activity word. Praise effort, not speed.
  • ⭐ Extension (fast finishers). After the poster: a bonus “At the camp” mini-poster (draw + label a camper with “She's wearing… She's …ing”). Never “more test”, always create + apply.
Self-evaluation (thinking)

At the very end each child evaluates their own effort with a traffic-light card — 🟢 easy · 🟡 okay · 🔴 tricky:

Show me your light. What was EASY today? What was a bit TRICKY? 2–3 children name one thing each. No right/wrong — a friendly check-in, not a score.

Calm, non-competitive buffer for the very fast: draw one camper for the class camp picture-book and label what they're wearing / doing. No race, no losers.

23–43′
UNIT 8 TEST — written parts
desks

Read every rubric aloud. They are emergent readers — before EACH exercise, read the instruction to the whole class and point to the example: Exercise 1 — listen and tick. Like this. Re-read privately beside any child who needs it.

Listening (paced together). Ex. 1 Listen and tick ✓ (clothes, 6 items — Track 19) · ex. 2 Listen and number (outdoor activities, 6 pictures — Track 20) — play each track TWICE: first listen, pencils down; second time tick / number. Watch the room, pause between items.

Reading → Writing → Reading & Writing (own pace). Ex. 3 Read and match the “…ing” sentences to the pictures (singing songs · riding a horse…) · ex. 4 Look and write the clothes from the initial letters (I'm wearing s___ and a T-___) · ex. 5 Look and read. Write the activity + clothes sentences (He's/She's …ing; She's wearing a ___). Circulate silently; encourage with a nod, never with answers: Take your time. You're doing great.

Countdown at the end: Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down. Collect written pages (keep the Speaking picture page).
