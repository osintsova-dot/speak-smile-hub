Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия с нуля) · 60 min · Story: listen → follow → act out (drama) · carpet + desks
Week 18.01–24.01 · Занятие 36✨ STORY~12 pupils · trios for dramaPB p.42–43 · AB p.40🦸 Phonics Hero ур.24b · /эй/ ai·ay·a-e
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow and understand the story “I'm hungry” (Pupil's Book pp. 42–43, CD1) and act it out using key story language: “I'm hungry!”, “I like pizza / ice cream / cake!”, “Ring the bell!”, “Here you are!”, “Yummy! Thank you!”.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — foods + “I like / Do you like…?” (L1–L2) — woven into the story work (what does everyone like at the restaurant?); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.24 (b) закрепление: /эй/ ai·ay·a-e (read strip → dictation → tricky (Повт. Yellow) → the /эй/ reading passage; the story's food TRAY = /эй/!); values: saying please / thank you at the table.
  • Personal. Keep story listening active (gesture responses, prediction); manage drama noise with Freeze; every child acts in a trio at the same time — no “2 on stage, 10 watching” until the final 30-sec showcase.
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they understand the story (order the frames, answer with gestures);
  • say the key lines in role: “I'm hungry!”, “I like pizza!”, “Here you are!”;
  • read, match & write /эй/ words (rain, train, play, day) and chorus the /эй/ passage;
  • say “please” & “thank you” at the table (values).

Restaurant frame: “What does Molly like?” → “Pizza!” / “What does Jake like?” → “Ice cream!”
Last frame: “The food comes! What do they say?” → “Yummy! Thank you!”

Assumptions: foods + I like / Do you like…? known from L1–L2; children know the Magic Bike characters (Molly, Jake, Beth). Emergent readers → they FOLLOW the pictures while listening; the teacher points, children don't read the bubbles alone. Speed reading. The full strip + the three /эй/ houses: There and back — beat your time! Wave recall: Hey! /эй/ — rain, play, cake! Story tie-in: The waiter brings a food TRAY — /эй/!

24–34′
📺 Story — listen & follow
carpet, books/screen

Before you read (PB p. 42). Big story picture on screen / books open. Pre-teach with gesture: hungry (rub tummy) · restaurant (table & menu) · waiter — ring the bell! (carry a tray).

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — “What food can you see?” ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 find-it-u4
Screen: the U4 “Find it!” engine on the big restaurant picture. · How: Speaky asks, a child taps a food; it lights up. · Pupils: whole class points in their book / calls it — Ice cream! Cake! An orange! Grapes! · Teacher: 🦝 Where's the ice cream? Point! — 4–5 things, quick. · Offline reserve: point in the open Pupil's Book (p. 42), pairs find & chorus.

Predict (thinking) — before we listen. The food is not real yet… but a MAGIC bell rings! What will happen? Will the food be real? Thumbs vote. Let's listen and check!

Listen 1 — global (CD1). “Listening hands” behind ears. Teacher points frame by frame; children follow the pictures. Gist check: Are they hungry at the start? Show me! (thumbs) And at the end? Was your guess right?

Detail check — True or False? (thumbs up / down, all react). Say a sentence, class shows a thumb: Molly likes pizza. (👍) · Jake likes fish. (👎 — ice cream!) · A magic bell rings. (👍) · The food is yummy. (👍). Re-point the frame to fix any wrong thumb.

Listen 2 — active (all respond). Play again; children do the story gestures at the right moments: hungry 🍽, ring the bell 🔔, yummy 😋, cheer Thank you! CCQs between frames (see box above).

▶ Build the story
desks, pairs

▶ Story Builders (picture re-skin for non-readers — all pairs work at once). Each pair gets the story frame-cards, shuffled.

Order it. Put the story in order — one card each, take turns! (RallyRobin with cards.) Countdown 2 min.

Check with the audio. Replay CD1: pairs touch each card as they hear it — self-check, fix the order. Then choral retell, teacher holds up big frames one by one, class says the line: I'm hungry! · I like pizza! · Ring the bell! · Here you are! · Yummy! Thank you!

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Hungry Story-Sequence 1–5 ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 u4-story-hungry
Screen: the 5 key frames appear shuffled, each with an empty number badge 1–5. · How: tap two frames to swap (or drag); a “Check” button lights correct frames green with a cheer. · Pupils: the class calls the order — Number one — I'm hungry! — and gesture each frame. · Teacher: take votes (Numbered Heads), tap as they decide, then replay CD1 to confirm. · Offline reserve: big frame cards on the board (blu-tack), children come up in a fast rotation.

Support: hand that pair only the 3 clearest frames & number the first. Extension: after ordering, say one whole line per frame from memory before the audio check.

43–51′
Act out — drama
carpet, trios

Roles. Trios: Molly (red badge, likes pizza) · Jake (green badge, likes ice cream) · Waiter + bell (blue badge, rings the bell & brings food). Teacher narrates and models once with a strong trio.

All trios act at the same time. Narrate slowly, frame by frame; every trio plays it at their pretend table (on the spot!): I'm hungry! — Me too! — What do you like? — I like pizza! — I like ice cream! — Ring the bell! — Here you are! — Yummy! Thank you!

Create (thinking) — make it yours. Before round 2: What do YOU order at the magic restaurant? You choose! Each trio swaps in their own food — I like cake! — Here you are! — Yummy! (support: point to a food card; extension: invent the whole exchange).

Swap roles, run it again (2 rounds). Volunteers: ONE trio shows 30 sec for the class — big applause. Values (PB p. 43): At the table we say “please” and “thank you”!

Support: quieter children get the waiter (short line + bell) with a line card in hand. Extension: confident children lead the ordering.
