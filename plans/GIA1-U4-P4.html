WB Unit 4 (pp.28–35) · notebook · points to the leaderboard next pair
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can build Do/Does- and Wh-questions with clean word order, answer with object pronouns (I love THEM) and negotiate weekend plans with Key phrases + a counter-offer (Sorry, I'm busy… What about Sunday?).
  • Subsidiary. Grammar = exactly 45′: gate (word order) → tables → likes+pronouns grab → the CUMULATIVE Round-up Teqball (U1–4 — a Mid-year radar!) → product-A interview show. Lesson B: video model → Key phrases drills → PERFORM with rotation and peer review.
  • Personal. The continuers speak in questions, but WRITE them with a crooked word order. The bar: written inversion + a polite refusal with a reason + a counter-offer — that's already negotiation, not a scripted dialogue.
By the end, students can…
  • order Do/Does and Wh-questions in writing
  • answer likes with object pronouns (love HIM/THEM)
  • survive a cumulative U1–4 round-up
  • negotiate plans: refuse politely + counter-offer

Target language
Lexis
Why don't we (meet)?Are you free on (Saturday)?What about (Sunday / 11:30)?Is (Sunday / 10:00) OK?(Sunday)'s good. / OK, fine. / Yes, great.Sorry, I'm busy. (+ a reason!)
Structure
Do you do sport? — Yes, I do. · What time does she get up? · I love them! · Are you free on Saturday? — Sorry, I'm busy. What about Sunday? · Why don't we meet at 11:30?
Form
Do/Does + subject + infinitive; Wh-word + the same order; short answers do/does/don't/doesn't; object pronouns: me·you·him·her·it·us·them; on + a day, at + a time (ABCD TB p.65).
Pron. watch
do you = /dʒʊ/ · busy /ˈbɪzɪ/ · question intonation ↑, a counter-offer — a friendly tone (the video model).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Does she plays?” → No — after does, the infinitive.
“I love Bruno Mars → I love… he?” → HIM (object pronoun).
“Sorry, I'm busy — is that the end of the conversation?” → No: a reason + a counter-offer = polite and grown-up.

Assumptions: Questions are there orally since GMF; written word order and object pronouns as a system are gaps. The Round-up Teqball is cumulative (be·some/any·has got·there are) — direct Mid-year reconnaissance. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (time-in-words / -s spelling / question word order), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
