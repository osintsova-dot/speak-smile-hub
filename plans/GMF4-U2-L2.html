Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to talk about the past with was/were: “What were you like when you were young? — I was confident. / She wasn't tidy. / Were you shy? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Josh & Grandma's dialogue (CD1 31); discover the rule — was (I/he/she) vs were (you/we/they); negatives wasn't/weren't; recycle the 12 adjectives from L1; play a true-or-false game & write past questions (AB).
  • Personal. Drill was↔were until automatic; keep the negatives (wasn't/weren't) visible; model the full answer, accept short answers and recast. Watch when I was young as a fixed chunk.
By the end, pupils can…
  • choose was (I/he/she) vs were (you/we/they) correctly;
  • make negatives with wasn't / weren't;
  • ask & answer “Were you…?” about when they were young.

Structure
What were you like when you were young? — I was confident.  ·  She wasn't tidy.  ·  Were you shy? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
Form
was + I / he / she · were + you / we / they. Neg: wasn't / weren't. Q: Was I/he/she…? · Were you/we/they…? Time chunk: when I was young.
Pron. watch
was /wəz/ weak → /wɒz/ stressed in answers; weren't /wɜːnt/; wasn't /ˈwɒznt/; linking were you /wɜːjə/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“She ___ tidy. — was or were?” → was (she!).
“___ you noisy? — Was or Were?” → Were (you!).

Assumptions: 12 adjectives are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up). Past of be met before receptively; choosing was/were correctly + negatives is the new focus. Pupils can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar table.

19–27′
True or false · was/were
desks, 2 teams

▶ True-or-false game (act. 4, Showdown). You (then a team) say a past sentence about a class hero / cartoon: When Elsa was young, she was shy. Every child shows a T/F card on Show me! Then a pupil corrects a false one: No — she wasn't shy, she was confident! — everyone reacts every round.

🎮 Projector/board option: two big TRUE/FALSE zones on the whiteboard; a rotating child stands in the right zone as the WHOLE class calls it.

ICQ: To correct a false one — wasn't or weren't for “she”? (→ wasn't!)

27–37′
Find someone who… was
mingle

Model first. Grid of past facts. Model publicly: Were you shy when you were young? — Yes, I was! Write my name.

▶ Find Someone Who (mingle). Each child has a grid: …was noisy · …wasn't tidy · …was active… Ask Were you (noisy) when you were young? Full answer, write a name, new partner. 5–6 people.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — who asks Were…? and answers Yes,I was / No,I wasn't unaided? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: question stems printed on the grid. ⭐ Extension: add a follow-up: What were you like at nursery school?
