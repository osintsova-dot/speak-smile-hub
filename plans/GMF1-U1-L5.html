Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · Phonics (recognise→blend→segment) + Culture + Literacy product · carpet + desks
Week 28.09–04.10 · Lesson 8✨ Sound /r/~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.12 · итоговый + диплом Части 1Line: продолжающие
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to hear, recognise, blend & segment the sound /r/ at the start of words (red, rabbit, ruler, run, rug, ring, rat) and say the tongue twister A red rabbit on a red ruler.
  • Subsidiary — Culture. Learn about a first day / a British primary school (assembly, sit on the floor, listen to the head teacher, sing songs, clap hands) and compare with their own school (What do you do at school?).
  • Subsidiary — Literacy. Read a classroom-rules poster (poem) and make a mini class-rules poster (draw + say one rule). Review Unit 1 objects + prepositions in/on/under woven into the /r/ theme.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; hide the phonics drill inside movement games.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say if a word starts with /r/ or not;
  • blend & segment CVC /r/ words (r-a-t → rat);
  • name 3 things children do at a British school;
  • say & act out 2–3 class rules and colour one for a poster.

Pron. watch
English /r/ ≠ Russian «р» — do not roll/trill it! Round the lips, tongue back & up, no tap: /r/…red. Contrast start sounds /r/ vs /p/, /r/ vs /h/.
Phonics Hero
ур.12 — итоговый + диплом Части 1 (тетрадь стр.30–31) — (b) закрепление. Sight/tricky word review: red.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Does rabbit start with /r/?” → yes. “Does pen start with /r/?” → no, /p/.
“In rat — how many sounds? r-a-t.” → three. Blend: r-a-t → rat.
“Do we run in the classroom?” → no — we walk. “Hand up to speak?” → yes.

Assumptions: Unit 1 classroom objects (pencil, book, bag, ruler, rubber, crayon, pencil case, sharpener, scissors) + prepositions in/on/under and Where's my…? — It's here / in / on / under are known from L1–L4. Single letter-sounds s·a·t·i·p·n… reviewed in Starter (Phonics Hero). Emergent readers → rely on pictures, audio, gesture, not printed text. Carpet routines known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Pupils roll /r/ like Russian «р»Model puppy-growl /r/ (Jolly action, no tap); mirror lips; “soft, no engine!”.
Segmenting rat as one blobRobot-talk: tap 3 fingers r · a · t, then blend fast. Model twice.
Culture text too long to “read”They don't read — you read/play audio; they match to pictures & do the action (TPR).
Over-excitement at the flyswatter boardFreeze signal + only the two “swatters” move; whole class segments on fingers first, then swat. Fast rotation.
Child swats/guesses with just a word, not the sentenceRule: a point counts ONLY with the full sentence (Put your hand up to speak.); team may whisper-help (RallyCoach).
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Phonics /r/ — RECOGNISE
board / carpet

Meet /r/. Jolly action: Be a puppy — hold your rag and shake it: rrrr! (soft, no rolling). Say it 3× — big, small, whisper. Watch: English /r/ is not the Russian «р» — no engine, no tap.

Toby's tongue twister (PB p.13 · CD1 20). Play once: r – r – r. A red rabbit on a red ruler. Do it with actions (rabbit ears + slide along an imaginary ruler). Chant slow → fast. Point the red tricky word.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down (dual-coding: each word = picture + gesture): flash picture cards — starts with /r/ (rug · ring · rabbit · rat · read · run) → jump up + hands as ears; NOT /r/ (pen · hat · book) → crouch down small. 10 cards, fast — every child moves every card. This is retrieval, disguised as a game.

ICQ: /r/ word — do we jump or crouch? (→ jump!)

17–24′
Phonics /r/ — BLEND & SEGMENT
carpet → board, teams

Robot talk (segment → blend). Say words like a robot, tapping 3 fingers: r-a-t… rat! r-u-n… run! r-u-g… rug! Children echo, tap, then blend fast. How many sounds? Three! A few children segment a new /r/ word alone.

▶ Мухобойки / Flyswatter (/r/ blend & swat) — stick the picture cards (rug · ring · rabbit · rat · read · run + non-/r/: pen · hat · book) on the board. Two teams; one “swatter” from each comes up with a flyswatter. You sound it out robot-style r-a-t… → the WHOLE class segments on fingers together (everyone active), then the two swatters race to swat the right card and say the full word rat! — point only if they say the word. Rotate swatters every turn (Numbered Heads: Number 2s, come up!); seated pupils are judges — thumbs up / down. Freeze! to reset. This is retrieval, disguised as a game.

Settle: And… WiFi. Sit down.

 picture cards on board (r & non-r) · 2 flyswatters · ▶ Мухобойки / Flyswatter

▶ Guess by the piece — say the rule
carpet, teams

▶ Угадай-по-кусочку / Slow reveal (rules & culture) — cover a class-rule / culture picture with a card and reveal it a little at a time. Teams watch and guess — but a point counts only with the FULL sentence for that picture: Put your hand up to speak. / We walk in the classroom. / We sit on the floor. / We sing songs. / We listen to the head teacher. Slower reveal = more points. Teams whisper-confer first (RallyCoach), then answer.

Keep ALL engaged: every child does the action for the picture the moment they think they know it (finger to lips / hand up / sit on floor) — you scan the room before anyone speaks (retrieval + check-for-understanding); answer given by rotating Numbered Heads (Number 3s!).
