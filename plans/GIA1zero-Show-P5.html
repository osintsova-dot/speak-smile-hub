🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The 🐣 editors are editing for the FIRST TIME — I lead step by step from the screen, one action at a time. Premiere feedback is strictly warm: ticks and sympathies, no “best film” ranking — only nomination categories where everyone wins.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Step 1 — ORDER the scenes… Step 2 — CUT the empty start… Step 3 — TITLE!” “Our film is called… Enjoy!”

WHAT WE DO: editing step by step behind me (order → trim → title); I walk round and rescue; the team works in parallel on the poster and announcement

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I lead step by step: a step from the screen → teams repeat it → hands up “done” → next step.
  2. I walk round and rescue anyone stuck; I ask the fast ones to help their neighbours (helping = a smiley).
  3. Posters are drawn in parallel: a title + 1 picture + the team's names.
  4. Export/save — together, the last step in chorus.
📺 On screen: phones · poster sheets
👦 Students do: they edit for the first time in their lives — and it works, because it's step by step; parallel roles keep everyone busy. Expected: 20 minutes is enough for a one-minute film. Typical error: they delete a scene — I teach “undo”, calmly.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, every team has a finished 1–1.5 min video (scenes in order + a title card) and it premiered under a warm peer review of 2–3 ticks and a sympathy vote.
  • Subsidiary. Minimal editing with my support (scene order + trimming + 1 title); a phrase check by ear; the premiere = a celebration of courage, a nomination for every team.
  • Personal. The 🐣 editors are editing for the FIRST TIME — I lead step by step from the screen, one action at a time. Premiere feedback is strictly warm: ticks and sympathies, no “best film” ranking — only nomination categories where everyone wins.
By the end, students can…
  • order and trim scenes with guided steps
  • make one English title
  • give warm feedback with 2–3 ticks
  • vote with a simple reason

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is our film worse than the others?” → The words “worse/better” don't work today: different categories, different sympathies, every team gets a nomination.
“Is editing hard?” → Three actions: order, trim, title. I show each one from the screen — you repeat it.
“Is watching yourself embarrassing?” → The first 10 seconds feel odd for everyone (adults too!). Then — pride.

Assumptions: The material is filmed (P4); editing happens on 1 team phone, I mirror the steps from a projector on mine. Premiere via phone/projector — backup: showing it row by row.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The editor gets lost in the appI lead one action at a time from the screen: “has everyone found the scissors? cut.” The pace of the slowest is fine — the film is short.
The team gets bored during editingIn parallel: the rest prepare the premiere announcement (Our film is called… Enjoy!) and rehearse it + draw a “poster” for the film.
The file goes missingBackup: a live premiere — the team shows the guide live around the classroom; I announce it as a special genre, “live cinema”.
Laughing AT someone's shotThe premiere rule: we laugh TOGETHER (at the funny part), not AT (the person); if broken — a silent viewer for the rest of the screening.
Voting hurts feelingsA sympathy vote by category (funny/clear/kind) — every team collects its own; nominations — for everyone.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
