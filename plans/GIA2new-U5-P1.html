🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): For newcomers the mission is simple and “about me”: learn to name food in English and talk about your own favourite dish — what it is, what is in it, when you eat it. That gives a safe product (I talk about myself, from a frame, with the sheet in my hand) and at the same time feeds in the 24 words of the set. The Mon recipe video is not a task but a model: you can see how a whole story is built out of a handful of words.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Look at this. Eighteen Oreo cookies, some sugar, some cream cheese — and that's a cheesecake. A two-minute video, a million views. Today two things: we learn to name every food in English — and each of you tells us about YOUR favourite dish, six lines: what it is, what's in it, when you eat it. At the end you speak with your notes in your hand. This is not a test. Let's start with MY dish…” (and I show the model straight away)
In simpler words, if the class needs support: we watch a clip about a cheesecake made of biscuits, learn to name food in English and build a story about our own favourite dish — six lines: what it is, what is in it, when you eat it. At the end we speak with the sheet in hand.

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. MODEL: I speak first and show with my hand which way the turn will travel: I'm Oksana and my favourite food is pizza. The frame stays on the board for the whole circle.
  2. Then Kagan RoundRobin: the circle goes one way, everyone says one phrase and passes the turn to the neighbour on the left; no interrupting and no prompting, you may say pass and come back at the end.
  3. The circle: I'm … and my favourite food is … (one word is allowed).
  4. A reminder of the format: crews, roles (captain · writer · timekeeper · speaker), score, the product of the pair.
  5. Unit 4 blitz: What's the weather like today? Is it colder than yesterday?
  6. The bridge: Weather — done. Now: food. And a big question for the whole unit — what makes a town a good place to live in?

Video skills · a recipe step by step
screen

WHAT WE DO: the Mon video with the task set BEFORE watching; afterwards — the ingredients on mini-whiteboards

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. The task before: Two recipes. Write every ingredient you HEAR. Numbers count too.
  2. We watch with pauses; at each stop the crews discuss (Russian is allowed → then in English).
  3. MODEL: we write the first ingredient together — I say it, point at “18 Oreo cookies” on the board and explain that the number counts too.
  4. Then Kagan Showdown: the crew finishes the list on its mini-whiteboard and holds it face down; on the call Showdown! everyone lifts at once — nobody copies off the neighbours, and I can see immediately who heard what.
  5. Mini-whiteboards (TB key ex.9): Oreo cheesecake — 18 Oreo cookies, 80 g of sugar, 200 g cream cheese, 300 ml of cream; mango lemonade — 6 lemons, 1 mango, 300 ml of water, 2 tbsp of sugar.
  6. The formula of the genre goes on the board: You need … · Cut … · Mix … · Add … · And that's it!
📺 On screen: Video skills SB p.61 (backup — I read a chunk of the video script off the board: “You need 18 Oreo cookies. Cut half of them into bigger pieces… Then, you need some sugar and some cream cheese. Mix these two ingredients together…”) · mini-whiteboards
👦 Students do: they watch with a task and write it down. Expect: catching grams by ear is hard — they ask for a second play, and that is fine

WHAT WE DO: the product: 6 lines about your own dish + one “how it is made” step; the speaker holds the sheet

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

  3. You can hold your notes. Six lines + one step. The crew stands together.
  4. The audience listens with a task: Thumbs up when you hear a food YOU like!

WB Unit 5 (pp. 36–43) · bring the 6 lines to P2
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can name 24 food and drink words, sort them into seven food groups and describe a favourite dish in 6 lines (name + ingredients + when you eat it).
  • Subsidiary. The seven-group table (ex.2 and ex.4) with a prompt sheet; Fun food facts ex.3 from a model; Video skills (“Picnic snack ideas”) with the task set before watching; ex.7 three ingredients for a cake and a pizza.
  • Personal. I never call on anyone without a draft. Every speaking task comes AFTER written fixing. The word bank stays on the screen all lesson.
By the end, students can…
  • say and write 24 food and drink words
  • sort food into seven groups
  • list the ingredients of a dish
  • describe my favourite dish in 6 sentences

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“a milk or some milk?” → some milk: milk is not counted.
“herbs” — do we hear the h? → in British English yes: /hɜː(r)bz/.
“yoghurt” — how is it spelt? → y-o-g-h-u-r-t, with an h inside.

Assumptions: SB pp.60–61, KTP pair 38. Some of the words are familiar (cheese, chicken, milk, chocolate); new ones — noodles, yoghurt, herbs and spices, sauce, melon, peas, mushroom, oil.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Afraid to speak in front of strangersThe rule of the pair: first we write, then we read it to a partner, and we come to the board with the sheet in our hands.
24 words at once will not fitI feed them in seven portions, following the groups of the table, with a gesture for each group; recognition first (point at it), naming second.
They ignore the word bank and go quietThe word bank stays on the screen all lesson; a “look up” gesture instead of supplying the word.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
