Give Me Five! 3 · Unit 1 “Time for school” · Lesson 7 — Unit Test
A1 young learners (~9–10 y.o., confident readers) · 60 min · Summative test (Listening · Reading · Writing · Speaking) · desks, quiet
Week 05.10–11.10 · Lesson 10📝 Unit 1 TestStandard level · /80~12 pupils · individual work
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. To assess how well each pupil can use Unit 1 language — places in a school, school subjects, and the structures Where/When do you…? · I'm (brilliant) at… · Are you good at…? — across listening, reading, writing and speaking.
  • Subsidiary. Build test-taking confidence (model-first, low anxiety); train self-checking and self-assessment; give every pupil a calm mastery experience.
  • Personal. Keep it calm and fair: model each task before pupils do it, keep instructions short, circulate quietly, never rush, never rank aloud.
By the end, pupils have shown they can…
  • recognise & write places in a school + school subjects;
  • ask & answer Where/When do you…? and say what they are good at;
  • read a timetable / school map and speak about it.

Structures
(Where / When) do you (play with your friends / do PE)? — I (play) in the (playground).  ·  I'm (brilliant / very good / good / not very good) at (History).  ·  Are you good at (Maths)? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Test parts
Listening ex 1–2 (/20) · Reading ex 3–4 (/20) · Writing ex 5–6 (/20) · Speaking oral pair-matching (/20). Total /80, Standard level.
Skills
Listen for detail · read a timetable & a map · write short Q&A · say and read words aloud (Eng ↔ Rus pairs).
Fairness, access & assumptions
We test the language, not instruction-reading: model every task type first with one example (validity — we are not measuring who understood the rubric).
Give the task instruction in English, then a 3-word Russian gloss if needed, so no child is stuck on what to do.
Audio played twice; consistent /10 mark scheme per exercise → reliability. Marks are not announced in class.

Assumptions: Unit 1 was taught L1–L6 and reviewed in L6; pupils are confident readers & can write short answers; carpet/desk routines known. No new language today. Groups: individual work; the oral part runs 1:1 in a quiet corner while the rest work.

Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
Quiet hand 🖐 — need help? raise a hand, wait — the teacher comes to you (no calling out).
Volume = zero — test voice is silent; whisper only to the teacher.
“Pencils down” — eyes up, hands still between exercises.
Countdown — “Two minutes left… one minute… pencils down.”
Green card — finished & checked → put the green card on the desk.

Calm start. Greet quietly, seat pupils spaced out. Good morning! Today is our Unit 1 test — no worries, you know all of this.

Easy win (self-efficacy). One quick warm recall, everyone answers together, nothing written: hold up 3 picture cards — What's this room? … Yes! Library. Then Tell me one subject you're good at. Nods and smiles all round: See? You already know it. Just show me your best today.

4–10′
Model the test (model-first)
board

Walk through every task type with ONE board example each so nobody is stuck on what to do (we test the English, not the instructions):

① Listen & tick/cross — show a room picture, model a ✓ and an ✗. ② Listen & write — one word in the table. ③ Read the timetable — When do we have Maths? — At nine o'clock. ④ True / false from the map. ⑤ Look & write — I'm good at English. ⑥ Write the question — Where do you…?

Read every question twice. If you don't understand the task, raise your hand — I'll come to you. Ready? Names on your paper.

📝 Listening · ex 1–2
desks, silent

Ex 1 — Listen & tick (✓) or cross (✗), /10. Six school-place pictures. Track 04. Listen and tick or cross. I'll play it twice. Play once straight through, pause, play again. Pupils mark ✓/✗.

Ex 2 — Listen & write, /10. Table of subjects (PE · Geography · …) with very good / good / not very good / brilliant. Track 05. Listen and write the words in the table. Play twice, short pause between speakers.

Between exercises: Pencils down, eyes up. Keep the room silent; circulate slowly.

24–38′
📝 Reading · ex 3–4
desks, silent

Ex 3 — Look & write, /10. Read the timetable, complete 5 When…? / Where…? answers (e.g. Where do we have Drama? — In the school hall.). Re-point the worked example on the board once, then hands off.

Ex 4 — Look, read & write true/false, /10. School map + 6 sentences (There's a football pitch at our school.). Model the given true example, then independent.

Access. If a child freezes, quietly re-read the instruction (not the answer) and give a 3-word Russian gloss. Take your time.

24–52′
🗣 Speaking · oral pair-matching (parallel, 1:1)
quiet corner

Runs alongside the written work. While the class does Reading & Writing, call pupils one at a time (or in twos) to a side table; the rest work / self-check quietly.

Task (/20). Lay out Unit 1 word cards: English words + Russian translations (and a few picture ↔ word). The child orally makes the pairs — reads the English aloud and matches it to its Russian / picture: library — библиотека · canteen — столовая · I'm good at Maths. As a reader, the child reads each pair aloud; you check pronunciation + meaning.

Warm and short (~1–1.5 min each). Well done — thank you. Marks recorded on your sheet, never said aloud. Send the child back to finish the paper.

Ex 5 — Look & write, /10. Key (✗/✓/✓✓/★) → complete Mary/John/Beth sentences: I'm not very good at Maths, but I'm good at English. Point to the board model, then independent.

Ex 6 — Look & write, /10. Six pictures → write the Where…? question + answer: Where do you play with your friends? — I play with my friends in the playground.

Early finishers: Check your answers — read every question again. Green card on the desk when checked.
