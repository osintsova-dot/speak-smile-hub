Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to say which instruments they like playing and ask & answer: “Do you like playing the guitar? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” — a life skill: sharing my own likes.
  • Subsidiary. Watch Auntie Vicky, Alex & Lucy's video (CD4 T10) & match who likes what; play Guess the flashcard & Sticky Fingers (30) mingling; sing Genki «What Are You Doing?» (58); circle & talk about the instruments I like (PB p.83); Phonics Hero — tricky c · g · ph + air ear are, speed-read p.86–90.
  • Personal. A real speaking focus — never force a shy child; a nod at the flashcard counts. Model with the puppet first. Keep each exchange ~20 seconds, then swap partner.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say which instruments I like playing;
  • ask “Do you like playing the guitar?”;
  • answer “Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — the tricky ones: c & g can be soft (city, giant), and ph says /f/ (phone, photo); plus air · ear · are (hair, bear, care). Blend & speed-read p.86–90.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I ask Do you like playing the piano? — do you say YES or NO honestly?” → whatever YOU think!
“Auntie Vicky, Alex, Lucy — do they all like the same instrument?” → no, different ones!

Assumptions: All instruments & Do you like playing…? Yes, I do met in L1–L4; today is the proper speaking & mingling focus, mirrored by Auntie Vicky's video. Reading reaches the trickier c/g/ph + air/ear/are spellings.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Forgets the short answerPuppet always models Yes, I do! (nod) / No, I don't! (shake) — children copy the gesture.
Shy to speak to a peerAsk the puppet first, then a friend; pointing to the flashcard is a full answer.
Only a few speakEveryone holds an instrument card & answers chorally, then mingles — nobody just watches.
Same partner every timeMusic on = walk, music off = new partner (Mix-Pair); raise a hand to find a new friend.
Over-excited minglingSmall space, walking feet, Freeze on the tambourine.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 31 · tricky c·g·ph + air ear are — sounds soft c·g · ph=/f/ · air · ear · are. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Blend: c-i-ty → city, g-iant → giant, ph-one → phone, h-air → hair, b-ear → bear, c-are → care — sweep + a picture. ▶ Word-swat — Speaky says a word; children point / swat it on the page fast.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.86–90 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read because · animal together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Watch & match. Watch Auntie Vicky, Alex & Lucy's video (CD4 T10) — who likes playing what? Ask What does Lucy like playing? children guess, then check & match.

25–32′
Game · Guess the flashcard ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Guess the flashcard Give each child an instrument card secretly. One at a time (fast) they mime it; the others guess Do you like playing the drum? If right, the mimer says Yes, I do — the drum! Everyone mimes.
