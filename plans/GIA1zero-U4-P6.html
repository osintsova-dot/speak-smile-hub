WHAT WE DO: Quiz-Quiz-Trade to revise: opening; closing the unit question; the plan for the day: poster → test → a calm review

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Opening: One word: whose day is interesting — a singer? a footballer?
  2. Kagan Quiz-Quiz-Trade with printed unit cards (handout GIA1-QQT: a question on top, the answer on the back after folding; no printout — the kids write their own cards in 2 min).
  3. Teams: Stand up, hand up, pair up! Quiz your partner — praise — TRADE cards — find a new partner! 3–4 exchanges, everybody asks at the same time.
  4. WDYT closing: Why organise your time? Answer with what you KNOW now! 2–3 answers from the frame (because…).
  5. The day's plan on the board: POSTER → TEST → CHECK. The test is the same exercises we did all week. No surprises — promise.
📺 On screen: board: WDYT + the day's plan
👦 Students do: they answer the unit question more confidently than in P1. Expected: “because K-pop students don't relax!”

Unit 4 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: write the time in words (7:45 → quarter to eight; 12:00 → midday). B) 8 items: routine/free-time collocations — have ___ (lunch), ___ the guitar (play), go ___ (home — trap: no to), do ___ (sport).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 8 gaps: present simple aff/neg with 3rd person spelling (watch → watches; study → studies; not have → doesn't have). B) 6 items: order the words → a Do/Does- and a Wh-question + a true short answer (up / does / time / she / what / get ?). C) 4 items: object pronoun replacement / love-like-hate (Do you like Bruno Mars? → I love ___ .).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A description of someone's day (a script in the style of SB p.52 ex.7; teacher-read twice if no track): 4 true/false + 4 times to fill in — mirrors SB p.50 ex.4.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A note/message (25+ words) to a friend with because + a reply. Marked for: present simple forms (4) · because position + subject-verb (3) · message format Hi…/See you! + task complete (3) — mirrors SB p.56 Skills boost.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.57 — every format was met in class during the unit. The two lines: 🚀 higher (+ Wh-questions in writing, more spelling traps) · 🐣 standard.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
