Спецификация теста Unit 4 (ЧЕРНОВИК до официального пака Macmillan A1+)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: write the time in words (7:45 → quarter to eight; 12:00 → midday). B) 8 items: routine/free-time collocations — have ___ (lunch), ___ the guitar (play), go ___ (home — ловушка: без to), do ___ (sport).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 8 gaps: present simple aff/neg со спеллингом 3-го лица (watch → watches; study → studies; not have → doesn't have). B) 6 items: order the words → Do/Does- и Wh-вопрос + true short answer (up / does / time / she / what / get ?). C) 4 items: object pronoun replacement / love-like-hate (Do you like Bruno Mars? → I love ___ .).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)Описание чьего-то дня (скрипт в духе SB p.52 ex.7; читает учитель дважды, если нет трека): 4 true/false + 4 времени вписать — зеркалит SB p.50 ex.4.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A note/message (25+ слов) другу с because + reply. Marked for: present simple forms (4) · because position + subject-verb (3) · message format Hi…/See you! + task complete (3) — зеркалит SB p.56 Skills boost.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
