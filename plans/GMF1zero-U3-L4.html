Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to describe people's hair & eyes — long / short / blonde / brown / black / red hair; green / brown / blue eyes (Pupil's Book p. 32) — and ask & answer “He's / She's got (green eyes) and (short hair).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — body parts (L1), I've got (L2), the circus clown (L3), Genki colours — woven into describing people (“The clown has got a big red nose and green hair!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.22 (a) первое знакомство: Magic E o-e / u-e (nose, bone, home; cube, mule, cute, flute — the clown's NOSE has an o-e!, тетрадь стр.61–62); a describing / “Guess who?” game.
  • Personal. Keep He's / She's got (not “he have”); give an ICQ before every game; every task keeps ALL children responding (own describe-card / show-me / all groups play at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name hair length/colour & eye colour;
  • describe a person: “She's got long blonde hair and blue eyes.”;
  • ask & answer “Who is it? — He's got…” (Guess who?);
  • read & write o-e / u-e words (nose, home, cube, flute).
Target language
Lexis
long hairshort hairblonde hairbrown hairblack hairred hairgreen eyesbrown eyesblue eyes (recycled: head · nose · ears · big/small)
Structure
He's got (green eyes). · She's got (long hair). · She's got (brown eyes) and (short hair). · Talk Partners: My friend has got black hair.
Form
He / She + 's got (= has got) + adjective + noun. Chunk: has got for description. Join two facts with and.
Pron. watch
blonde = /blɒnd/ (one beat, silent “e” — NOT “blond-ee”); hair /heə/ (not “her”); She's got = /ʃiːz gɒt/ (’s = has → /z/); eyes /aɪz/.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.22 (a) первое знакомство: Magic E o-e / u-e — the magic e makes o say /оу/ (nose, bone, home) and u say /ю:/ (cube, mule, cute, flute), тетрадь стр.61–62 · sight words: also, of.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Two faces: “Which is long hair? Which is short hair?” (point).
Speaky the boy: “Do I say He's got or He have got?” → He's got! (’s = has)
Phonics: “nose — the magic e makes o say…?” → its name /оу/! — home, bone!

Assumptions: body parts + I've got solid from L1–L3; the clown (with a red nose & hair) already met in the story → today hair & eyes get named & drilled. Colours from the Genki block. Emergent readers → picture cards & memory, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“He have got” / “she have got”Chant with a clap: he's got / she's got; the ’s hand-flick; drill boy/girl faces side by side.
hair confused with her (from ur.18)Contrast openly: hair = on your head (touch it); her = the girl. One gesture each.
Magic E o-e/u-e read as two sounds: nose = /но-се/Cross out the ghost e; “o says /оу/, u says /ю:/” — nose, cube!
“Guess who?” — one child grabs all guessesNumbered Heads: the team agrees, YOU call the number; everyone points on their own card while guessing.
Memory game: one child hogs the cardsStrict turn circle in the group of 4; “flip two, everyone says the sentence together”.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Cognitive load: only the hair/eye words + the chunk “He's / She's got green eyes and short hair” are truly NEW — body parts, colours & I've got are all recycling, so let the description chunk carry the challenge (scaffold: boy/girl gesture, clap-chant, sentence frame). The phonics is FIRST MEETING — meet o-e/u-e gently, no dictation today. Buffers to drop first if short: the 2nd Memory round, the 3rd Guess-who round, the exit-ticket line-up (fold into the door goodbye). Better every child says one full She's got long blonde hair and blue eyes. well than six stages half-done. Offline reserve: every 🎮 has a paper twin (flashcards / cards).

Presentation · hair & eyes
carpet

Present with faces (PB p. 32 ex. 1, CD1 53). Big face cards on the board: She's got long blonde hair. He's got short black hair. She's got green eyes. He's got blue eyes. Point & say with the CD; contrast pairs: long — short · blonde — black.

▶ Point & say (all react). Hold up a face — whole class describes together: She's got long brown hair and blue eyes! Speed up; then trick them — swap to a boy: HE'S got…?

Order drill. Clap-chant: She's · got · LONG · hair! He's · got · GREEN · eyes! — join two facts with and. CCQ (see box).

💪 Support: keep the face + word up; child says just the colour, teacher adds the noun. ⭐ Extension: full sentence with and — She's got long blonde hair AND blue eyes!
🎮 Интерактив (проектор): “Guess who?” face wheel · 🎮 whats-this-spinner-u3 Screen: a wheel of face cards (different hair & eyes); arrow spins and lands on one. Mechanics: tap Spin → arrow stops → whole class describes the face, then a named child; land twice to compare a pair. Language: He's got short black hair and brown eyes! 32–38′
▶ Faces memory
desks, groups of 4

▶ Faces Memory (= our Игротека «Мемори»; all groups play simultaneously). Each group of 4: 12 cards face-down — 6 face pairs (identical faces to match: long blonde hair, short black hair, red hair + blue eyes…).

Turn order round the group: flip two cards, describe BOTH aloud — She's got long blonde hair… she's got long blonde hair — a pair! Match found → keep it (whole group repeats the sentence chorally). No match → face-down again. ICQ: Do you just point, or SAY the face? (→ say it!)

Most pairs wins a 🦝 sticker.

💪 Support: start with only 8 cards (4 pairs) and let the child say the hair only (teacher adds eyes). ⭐ Extension: round 2 — say the full sentence with and each flip.

🎮 Digital option (projector): body-memo — S&S memory pairs on screen, 1–4 teams: a rotating child flips two cards, the whole class describes every face aloud. Swap it in for the whole-class warm-round.

39–48′
▶ Guess who?
carpet → teams

▶ Guess who? (describing game, teams of 4 — all guess). A grid of 8–10 face cards on the board (each child also has the same small grid). 🦝 Speaky secretly picks ONE.

Kagan — Numbered Heads: team huddles 10 sec, agrees on a description guess, then the called number asks for the team: Has she got long hair? Before Speaky answers, the whole class votes thumbs. Speaky: Yes, she has! / No, she hasn't! → cross faces out on the board (and everyone crosses on their own grid) until the right person: She's got long blonde hair and green eyes — that's her!

3 quick rounds; wrong guesses shrink the choice, so the logic is visible. Reset if loud: Freeze!

💪 Support: let the team point to a face as they ask, so the question is anchored. ⭐ Extension: ask two facts at once — Has she got long hair and blue eyes?
🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — “Which friend?” · 🎮 find-it-u3 Screen: a board of faces; a spoken description names one. Mechanics: say the description → children point on their OWN grid FIRST → tap the face → it lights up to confirm; every child answers, not just the tapper. Language: Find her — she's got short red hair and green eyes! ✅ ГОТОВ — U3 Find it! (face deck). 📴 Offline: printed face grids, teacher describes.

Finish: 2–3 volunteers show 🦝 Speaky their friend: My friend has got red hair and green eyes!
