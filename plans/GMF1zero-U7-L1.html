Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 10 places in town and identify them using “What's this? — It's a (zoo).”, “There's a (cinema).” and “Let's go to the (café).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — Unit 6 park & “Where's the…? / It's next to the…”, Genki numbers & colours — and fold it into the new town topic (“There's a park. It's green.”, “Where's the zoo? — Next to the café!”, “How many shops?”); phonics — Phonics Hero: повторение Части 2, диграфы ур.16–18 (ch·sh·th · ou·oi · ue·er·ar): speed strip → gap-fill → dictation → tricky strip → re-read a Часть-2 story (тетрадь стр.43–51); listen for detail (order the town chant); pre-literacy & fine motor (word–picture, stickers). (Phonics = revision, not the day's main aim — keep it brisk.)
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; every task keeps ALL children responding (own card / show-me / stand-sit) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 10 town places;
  • ask & answer “What's this? — It's a…” and say “There's a (zoo). Let's go to the (café)!”;
  • read/write revision digraph words (ch·sh·th·ou·oi·ue·er·ar: shop, fish, chin, out, coin, car) and re-read a Часть-2 story.

Form
There's = There is (one place). “It's a” /ˈɪtsə/. “Let's go to the” — one chunk, to + the. Article a with singular places.
Pron. watch
sh /ʃ/ = one quiet sound (finger to lips) in shop, swimming; ch /tʃ/ in cinema? no — /s/! model clearly; “café” = /ˈkæfeɪ/ (two beats); “restaurant” = /ˈrestrɒnt/.

10–22′
Phonics Hero — Часть 2 digraphs (revision)
board → desks

Speed reading — the big strip. Board / Hub: ai · oa · ie · ee · or · ng · oo · ch · sh · th · ou · oi · ue · er · ar. There and back — beat your time! Recall the calls: finger to lips sh! (тихо!) · hands round the mouth ou! (ау!) · surprised sailor oi! (ой!) — all ×3.

Gap-fill — тетрадь стр.43–45 «Допиши диграф» (all vote first). Picture up (shop · fish · coin · car) — say the word together, then every child calls the missing digraph → all write it into the gap: sh__p · f__sh · c__n · c__r. RallyCoach: partner checks before the next word.

Dictation — стр.46–47 (everyone writes). Now YOU write. Listen: shop… word once → segment on fingers (/sh/ /o/ /p/) → write → Show me! → reveal on the board, self-check. Words: shop, fish, out, car — our SHOPping town words!

Tricky strip. Flash the sight-word strip from Часть 2 (your · she · other · were) — read for speed, there and back.

Story re-read — стр.48–51 «Королева и туча» / «Звезда над фермой» (settler). Chorus the frames, finger under the words. ⭐ Star task: find & circle every sh. Colour the star «Я молодец!».

🎮 Reading-game option: read-race — ⚔ battle mode, level «Диграфы»: a word pops up, everyone reads it aloud, the fastest correct team races ahead. Swap it in for the speed-reading strip when the screen is free.

💪 Support: in the gap-fill give the digraph card to copy; in dictation write sh_p. ⭐ Extension: a 5th dictation word by ear (coin); in the story find ch and th too.

✔ Assess: the dictation lines are your live check — note who writes a digraph as two letters/sounds; re-drill at the next speed reading. This is revision: keep it brisk, ≈10 min.

44–53′
Guess the place
carpet → teams
🎮 Interactive (projector): Find it! — the town scene. Screen: a busy town scene full of places. Tap Speaky — 🦝 hides one behind a cloud. Mechanics: whole class guesses, points on screen; reveal on correct. Language: Is it a zoo? — No! … Is it a café? — Yes! Let's go to the café! (2–3 rounds — models the question + place for all). Teacher: every child points at their own place card while guessing = no watchers. Status ✅ ready. Offline reserve: real cloud cut-out + place card on the board. · 🎮 find-it-u7

▶ Mystery Bag — What's this? — in teams of 4 (all guess). A feely bag holds place cards / small models. Child 1 secretly draws one and mimes it; all three others guess — quick fire round the group, each with a place card in front of them to point at: Is it a swimming pool? Mimer confirms — Yes! It's a swimming pool! / No, try again! The correct guesser mimes next, so the bag keeps moving and no one sits idle.

Reset if it gets loud: Freeze!

✔ Assess: stand behind one team at a time — can each child produce a place name and the frame It's a… unprompted? That's the lesson's success check.

🎮 Digital option (projector): town-memo — S&S memory pairs on screen, 1–4 teams: a rotating child flips two cards, the whole class names every picture aloud — It's a zoo! Swap it in for one round when the screen is free.

💪 Support: keep a picture strip of the places visible to point to while guessing. ⭐ Extension: add a destination line — Let's go to the zoo! after guessing. 		feely bag + place cards per team · Find it! (board, model) · Speaky · 🎮 town-memo · 🎮 find-it-u7
