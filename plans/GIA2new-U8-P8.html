Unit 8 “Summer fun” test — specification (OUR DRAFT)

⚠️ Important — check this before you print anything. The files tests/A2_U8_Test_Standard.pdf and A2_U8_Test_Higher.pdf belong to a different Macmillan course, not to Get Involved! A2. They test personality adjectives, social problems (crime · poverty · unemployment), comparative and superlative adjectives, the articles a/an/the and a text about homeless people — not one topic from “Summer fun”. Do not hand them out. There is no official Macmillan test for Unit 8 in the folder — replace this draft as soon as you download it from the Teacher's Resource Centre. The specification below is ours: a genuine Macmillan unit-test format, filled with Get Involved! A2 Unit 8 content (Quick review SB p.105 + “Look what you know! 8”, WB pp.66–67).
For reference: of everything in tests/, only A2_U1_Test_* belongs to our course (and even that mixes in vocabulary from neighbouring units). A2 Progress Test 1, A2 Mid-Year Test, A2 End-of-Year Test and A2_U8_Test_* are from other courses.

PartContents & marking
Vocabulary 1 · 7 markstypes of holiday and places to stay: label seven photos + six «where did I stay» riddles (format of SB p.96 ex.1 and ex.3, WB p. 60 tasks 1 and 3)
Vocabulary 2 · 7 marksfacilities and holiday activities: seven icons + seven phrases with go on / go to / go for (format of SB p.97 ex.6, SB p.101 ex.3, WB p. 62 tasks 1 and 3)
Vocabulary 3 · 6 marksa holiday description text with six gaps (format of SB p.96 ex.7 and WB p. 60 task 5) — vocabulary in context, not as a list
Grammar 1 · 7 markswill for predictions: aff/neg + questions and short answers (format of SB p.100 ex.2 and ex.5, WB p. 61 tasks 1 and 3)
Grammar 2 · 6 marksgoing to: affirmative, negative, question + short answer (format of SB p.102 ex.2 and ex.5, WB p. 63 tasks 1 and 3)
Grammar 3 · 6 markspresent continuous for arrangements: a diary → six sentences (format of SB p.102 ex.8, WB p. 63 task 5)
Grammar 4 · 6 markscorrect the mistake: will / going to / present continuous mixed up (format of Grammar round-up SB p.102 ex.9)
Use of English · 8 marksa connected text about the future of travel with eight gaps and an A/B/C choice (format of SB p.100 ex.7 and WB Round-up p. 67)
Listening · 12 markstwo tasks on one recording: 6 — how the speaker feels (format of track 50), 6 — details of the plans: who, when, where, what they decided (format of track 51)
Reading · 15 marksa short travel text: 6 — fact or opinion with the signal underlined (format of SB p.98 ex.6 and WB p. 64 task 3), 6 — true/false with evidence, 3 — Word work from context
Writing · 10 marksan invitation exchange of 6–8 messages: an invitation + a refusal with a reason + a second invitation + agreement + where and when (format of SB p.104 ex.8). Criteria: content 4 · going to and present continuous 3 · phrases for inviting and replying 2 · apostrophes 1
Speaking · 10 marksa pair arranges something to do together from their own diaries: at least five Key phrases, one failed attempt, and an ending — where and at what time (format of SB p.103 ex.5)

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
