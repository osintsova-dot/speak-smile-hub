📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. The unit check: vocabulary (food and drink + places in a town), countable/uncountable + a/an/some/any, there is/are, Is there…?/Are there…?, How much/How many, listening, a short description of a place.
  • Subsidiary. The “easy first” strategy; a practice run before the test (to take the fear out of the format); feedback on the same day; the unit celebration.
  • Personal. ⚠️ There is NO official Macmillan pack for Unit 5 in the tests folder (it only has U1 and U8, and Progress Test 1 / Mid-Year / End-of-Year belong to a different course) — the test is built to the specification from Quick review SB p.69 and WB p. 43. The tone is gentle, the marks are private, and the only comparison is with yourself (the P2–P5 mini-tests).
By the end, students can…
  • start with easy items and mark hard ones
  • apply the unit language in test format
  • check against the key and sort own slips
  • see their own growth across the unit

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Fear of “a real test”A practice run of 3 items BEFORE the test — the format is familiar, and the fear drops.
The slower ones run out of timeThe volume is calculated with room to spare; the listening has pauses; the writing follows the familiar P6 plan.
Emptiness after the testThe unit ceremony: an award for everyone, the wall of achievements, the Unit 6 teaser.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
