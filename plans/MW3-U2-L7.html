Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have reviewed all of Unit 2 — school activities, «Where's…? — It's on…», «What do you do at school?» and «Do you want to…? — I want to jump!» — and shown it in the flagship School Team Game with Genki «Number Golf» (10) as the reward.
  • Subsidiary. “I can…” self-assessment with happy/sad faces (PB p.10, CD1 T32); every child answers (all show at once, then teams); recycle Genki «Colours» (52) & «What Do You Want To Do?» (59); Phonics Hero — ch · sh · th, speed-read p.43–45; a joyful, both-teams-win finish.
  • Personal. Team game = EVERY child answers (show me your card / do the action), not just the fastest. Keep the reward short & happy. Both teams cheer at the end; no sore losers.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name school activities & do the actions;
  • answer «What do you do?» & «Do you want to…?»;
  • show what I can do with a happy/sad face (self-check).

11–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 16 — sounds ch · sh · th (letter-teams). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Blend the team: ch-i-p → chip, sh-o-p → shop, th-i-n → thin — sweep the two letters + a picture. ▶ Reading relay — each child reads one word to earn a star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.43–45 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read said · here together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

24–47′
🏆 SCHOOL TEAM GAME + Genki «Number Golf» (10)
carpet, 2 teams

Flagship game. Spin the Big Wheel (app on screen or a pointer on the mat) — it lands on a pod = a challenge card: do & name the activity · what's on this colour · What do you do at school? · Do you want to (draw)? · who's new in the story? (Katy!). Card shown → Show me! EVERY child answers (show the card / do the action / say it) → most-correct team gets a point.

Reward: Genki «Number Golf»: draw a golf flag with a ‘hole’ and two tees on the board; for each team point, a player ‘putts’ the ball one space toward the hole — in the HOLE wins the round. Rotate the putter every time.

🎮 Offline setup: Big Wheel mat + pod challenge cards on the carpet; a board golf-hole + cut-out ball (magnet/blu-tack) for the putt — one putt per point, quick. (Или мягкий мяч в стакан-лунку — putting-вариант.)

ICQ: When do you answer? (→ on Show me! only)   Finale. count the points in English; both teams: Well done! Give me five!

✔ Assess: across the game — who names activities · answers What do you do? · Do you want to…? · the story? A picture of the whole unit.
