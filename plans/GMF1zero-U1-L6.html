Pre-A1 young learners (~6–7 y.o., beginners / emergent readers) · 60 min · Unit 1 Test (calm, model-first, supportive) · carpet + desks
Week 09.11–15.11 · Lesson 21 (year)📋 Assessment day~12 pupils · individual work at desks🦸 Phonics Hero ур.17 · gentle first look ou·oi
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. To assess, in a calm low-stress way, what pupils learned in Unit 1: name classroom objects & furniture, say where things are (in / on / under, “Where's my…? — It's here.”) and recognise classroom instructions — via the GMF1 Unit 1 Standard-Level Test (Listening · Reading · Writing · Speaking).
  • Subsidiary. Light retrieval of unit language to prime success before testing; a gentle first look at digraphs ou · oi (recognise only); build test-taking confidence & self-checking.
  • Personal. Keep it calm — no races, no scores compared aloud. Model every task with one shared example (“let's try one together first”) so we test the language, not the format. Attribute success to effort: “You knew that because you practised.”
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they can name Unit 1 objects & furniture;
  • show they understand in / on / under and “Where's my…?”;
  • orally match Unit 1 words — English ↔ Russian / picture ↔ word (speaking check);
  • self-check with a calm thumbs / “I can” badge.

Task-check questions (ICQs) · Assumptions
“Do you circle one picture or two?” → one.
“In / on / under — is the bin under the desk?” → yes (from the picture).
“Is this a race?” → no! → “We take our time.”

Assumptions: the whole of Unit 1 (objects, furniture, in/on/under, classroom instructions) was taught across the compressed L1–L4 and reviewed in L5; pupils are beginners / emergent readers → Reading task (Ex 3) is picture-supported memorised chunks, teacher reads each line aloud once as an accommodation. Pupils know carpet ↔ desk routines. This is a low-stakes class test (max 20 in BigBen) — data to inform teaching, not to rank children.

37–48′
Speaking — oral matching (pairs) + calm colouring
desks · quiet corner

Set-up — everyone busy, calm rotation. While the class colours / self-checks quietly, call pupils one at a time (or in tiny groups of 2–3) to the quiet corner. On the table: the Unit 1 word cards next to a picture card (or an English word next to its Russian). The child's job — say the matching pair out loud: Match them for me — which picture goes with this word?

Model first (always one together). Let's do one together. Look — “pencil”… and here's the pencil. “Pencil” — карандаш! Then hand over: point to a card, child names the match — Now you. This one? … Yes — “ruler” — линейка! Do 4–5 pairs each (picture ↔ word, or English ↔ Russian), warm and unhurried; if stuck, give the picture as a hint — no red flags, just note the mark privately.

🦝 to each child: Fantastic! You practised — and it shows. (attribution to effort & strategy, not “clever”). Collect papers gently as each child finishes; no scores said aloud.
