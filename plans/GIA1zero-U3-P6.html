WHAT WE DO: Showdown on the rewind: a calm check-in; 3 radar points (from Quick review) on mini-boards; the day's plan, big

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. At the door, calmly: One word: how do you feel?
  2. I frame it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYBODY writes at the same time, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift them all together — I see everyone's answer, nobody can sit it out.
  3. Mini-boards, 3 radar flashes: a word from a picture · a some/any gap · fix Yes, there's.
  4. The day's plan: PROJECT (our ideal meal) → TEST (page 45 on paper) → PRIDE LINE. Two parts today. Both — yours to win. Calm and kind.
📺 On screen: board: the day's plan · mini-boards
👦 Students do: they fix 3 points, see the plan — anxiety drops. Expected: “is the test long?” — “30 minutes, like the quick review”.

Step 1: five branches of the mind map

WHAT WE DO: RallyRobin: 5 branches into the notebook; ex.2 idea sorting with support; +1 for your own idea on a branch

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Branches on the board with icons: Reason 🎉 · What to eat 🍕 · Where 📍 · When 🕐 · Who 👥. They copy them.
  2. I run the pair as Kagan RallyRobin: we alternate one answer at a time — Ping-pong it: A — one, B — one, back to A. No monologues, no lists!
  3. Ex.2 sorting in pairs with support (we do the first 2 together): TB key — Reason: cousin's visit·birthday·no reason / What: chocolate·fish and salad·pizza / Where: Italian restaurant·vegetarian café·home / When: after school·Friday evening·Sunday lunchtime / Who: family·grandparents·classmates.
  4. Ex.3: ONE own idea per branch — two minutes.
📺 On screen: the SB p.46 · board: 5 branches with icons
👦 Students do: they build the skeleton, sort with support, add their own ideas. Expected: the icons hold the structure. Typical error: an idea on the wrong branch — their partner catches it.

18–25′
Step 2: the plan in pairs (collaboration phrases)
PLAN · pairs + collaboration
desks · pairs

WHAT WE DO: pairs agree on THEIR OWN ideal meal using collaboration phrases; the 10-second rule; a plan in words

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Collaboration Key phrases on the board + a choral drill (p.46): What do you think about…? — OK, I agree. / That's a good idea. / No, but what about…?
  2. The 10-second rule: haven't decided with a frame — a coin decides (I show the coin, we laugh, it works).
  3. Pairs fill in a plan table (5 branches × word notes): 5 min.
  4. I walk round, listen for the frames, +1 for a nice “No, but what about…?”.
📺 On screen: board: Key phrases · the coin · plan tables
👦 Students do: they agree using frames, and at a deadlock — the coin; write the plan in words. Expected: the coin resolves disputes instantly. Typical error: one person decides alone — “Ask the frame! What does your partner think?”

Strategy briefing

WHAT WE DO: test strategy: 4 parts; we solve the first item of each part TOGETHER as an example; the rule “stuck — skip it”

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners have planned and presented an ideal meal as a mind map (Super skill: Collaboration) and completed the compact Unit 3 test (standard variant) calmly, with a personal pride line at the end.
  • Subsidiary. The unit's WDYT? closes with everybody's answer; collaboration Key phrases with support; Final reflection with smileys; a test on the Quick review spec (standard, a draft until the Macmillan pack); a gentle close with no competition — the “pride line”.
  • Personal. Hold the project in timers, but without race-panic; the switch into the test — with a silence ritual. For beginners the test is an event: remind them “this is page 45 on paper”, the error clinic will be calm and on the next pair. No games with a score during the test.

  • agree/disagree with collaboration frames

Unit 3 test — specification, COMPACT, ~30 min (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (10 pts)A) 6 items: food & drink from definitions (You can make this from oranges → juice), spelling counts — silent letters watched (biscuit, sandwich). B) 4 items: where do you buy it? (bread → baker's; medicine → chemist's).
Part 2 · Grammar (14 pts)A) 4 gaps a/an/some (I've got ___ apple and ___ water). B) 4 gaps some/any/a lot of (We haven't got ___ tomatoes.). C) 6 items there is/are: 2 gaps aff/neg + 2 make a question from words + 2 correct the mistake (There're two cafés. → There are. · Yes, there's. → Yes, there is.) — mirrors TB Common errors.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A short description of a shopping centre (script in the plan; teacher-read twice if no track): 4 true/false + 4 notes to complete (types of shops / places to eat / other things) — mirrors SB p.41 ex.6.
Part 4 · Writing (8 pts)5–6 sentences: food in your fridge / your ideal lunchbox. Must use: some/any/a lot of (3) · there is/are (2) · one and + one but (2) · task complete (1) — mirrors blog CHECK list SB p.45.

Total 40 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.45 — every format was met in class during the unit. The test shares the pair with the project, so it is compact: 4 parts, no padding.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
