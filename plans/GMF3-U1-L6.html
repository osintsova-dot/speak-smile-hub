Give Me Five! 3 · Unit 1 “Time for school” · Lesson 6 — Review
A1 young learners (~9–10 y.o., readers) · 60 min · Review & consolidation (retrieval + interleaving) · carpet + team tables
Week 28.09–04.10 · Lesson 9🔁 REVIEW~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🏆 Flagship quiz — reward day
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated all Unit 1 language — places in a school + school subjects — and can ask & answer with the unit's questions: “Where do you…?”, “Are you good at…?”, “What's your favourite subject?”
  • Subsidiary. Retrieval practice (recall from memory, not re-teaching); interleave vocabulary + structures + capital-letter rule + phonics /dʒ/; cooperative fluency; end-of-unit self-assessment before the test.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; make every response public (whiteboards / cards up) so no one hides; run the reward quiz on effort, not just speed.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name school places & subjects from memory;
  • write the question for a given answer (When/Where/What… do you…?);
  • say what they are good / brilliant at + their favourite subject;
  • spell words that need a CAPITAL letter (days, subjects, names).

Structure
Where/When/What do you (play / eat lunch)? — I (play) in the (playground).  ·  Are you good at (Maths)? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.  ·  I'm (brilliant / good / not very good) at (History).  ·  What's your favourite subject? — It's (Art).
Form
Wh- + do + you + verb? → answer drops “do”. be good at + noun. Adverbs of degree: not very good → good → brilliant at.
Pron. watch
word stress: comPUter room, GeOgraphy; connected “Where do you” /ˈweə dʒə/; /dʒ/ in Geography, Drama-teacher's gym.
Phonics · spelling
revisit consonant /dʒ/ j (giraffes jumping judo gym); CAPITAL letters: days, school subjects, names (English, Monday, Josh) — small: lunch, school.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I / you / we / they — do we use do or does?” → do. (he/she → does)
“brilliant at — a little good or very good?” → very good.
“english — small or BIG letter?” → BIG: subjects & days always take a capital.

Assumptions: all Unit 1 language met over Lessons 1–5 (places L1–2, subjects + good at L4, phonics /dʒ/ + capitals L5) — today is retrieval, not first teaching. Pupils read & write short sentences. Групповая математика: команда — максимум 4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Retrieval warmer

Brain dump (30 sec). In your team — how many school places AND subjects can you say? Go! Team scribe ticks them off fingers — pure recall, books closed.

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (Kagan): each pupil holds one unit card (place or subject). Stand up, hand up, pair up! A shows the picture: What's this? Where is it? → B answers in a full sentence (It's the science lab. We do Science there.), then swap, praise, trade cards, new partner. 3–4 rounds — every child talks every round.

Why now: low-stakes recall from memory (retrieval practice — Roediger & Butler) primes the whole unit before the written review.

22–32′
▶ Team Memo
team tables

▶ Memory / Memo (teams of 4): each set = 12 cards making 6 pairs — a picture card + its word/sentence card (place ↔ “I eat lunch in the canteen”; subject ↔ “I'm good at Science”). Cards face-down in a grid.

In turn a player flips two. Rule (drill hidden inside): you keep the pair only if you say the full sentence — This is the library. I read books in the library. Bare word = flip back, next player. Numbered Heads order keeps turns fair; whole team watches & helps (English only).

Most pairs = team point on the scoreboard. Reset noise: Freeze — Class? — Yes!

 3 Memo sets (12 cards each: picture + sentence) · team scoreboard
32–42′
▶ Question Relay
open floor, board

▶ Card-Grab Relay ⚡ (interleaving vocab + grammar). Two answer-cards taped at the far wall per round (e.g. in the playground / at the weekend). Teams line up.

Read a question stem — Where do you play with your friends? — first runner races, grabs the matching answer card, runs back and the team chants the full exchange: Where do you play? — I play in the playground! Correct grab + full sentence = point. Rotate every runner; mix stems: Where / When / What / Are you good at…?

Keep it fair & loud-safe: only the current runner moves — Freeze! resets.

 answer cards (places · days · subjects) · tape · question-stem list
42–55′
🏆 Flagship quiz — Penalty Shoot-Out
teams + goal zone

Set up. Draw a goal on the board; each team has a mini-whiteboard. Answer right — you earn a penalty kick. Score the goal — win the trophy!

▶ Showdown quiz (Kagan): read a mixed question — interleave all Unit 1 language: ① Name the place: you do experiments here. (science lab) ② Make the question: “… I have PE on Wednesday.” (When do you have PE?) ③ Answer: “Are you brilliant at Maths?” ④ Fix the capital: “i love english.” ⑤ Which word has the /dʒ/ sound: garden, Geography, garage?

Every teammate writes privately, you call Showdown! → all boards up together, team agrees. Correct → a chosen player takes a penalty kick (soft ball / paper ball at the goal) for a bonus point. All-response check — no one spectates (Wiliam, formative).

Tally goals; the winning team gets the reward 🏆 (champion badge / stickers). Keep it warm — celebrate every team's best answer.
