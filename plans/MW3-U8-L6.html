Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to value practising (Keep practising! You can do it!), clap different rhythms — fast · slow · loud · quiet — and enjoy the culture song «I am the music man» with new instruments (violin · trombone).
  • Subsidiary. Values — the story «Keep practising!» (CD4 T11) with Harry & Zoe; Content — rhythm (clap fast/slow/loud/quiet); Culture — «I am the music man» (CD4 T13) + What can you play? I play the violin; play Chocolate Monster (32) & Freeze!; Phonics Hero — free reading of decodable stories, Part 1 (p.7–29).
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values & culture through DOING (act the practising, mime the music man) not talking-at. Every child claps a rhythm & plays a new instrument.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say “Keep practising! You can do it!”;
  • clap a rhythm fast · slow · loud · quiet;
  • sing «I am the music man» & play the violin / trombone.

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 · free reading (Part 1, p.7–29) — children now READ whole little decodable stories; they know every sound. A reading celebration, not new sounds.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Harry isn't good at the recorder yet — does he stop, or keep practising?” → keep practising!
“A LOUD rhythm or a quiet rhythm — which is soft like a whisper?” → quiet!

Assumptions: All instruments & What are you doing? / Do you like…? secure from L1–L5. Three short blocks work well if each is active & bridged by a song. big/loud/quiet feelings & simple new instruments (violin, trombone) are easy to mime. Reading is now free reading of stories.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Practise” too abstractAct it: Harry plays the recorder badly → practises → gets better. Cheer Keep practising! You can do it! live.
Rhythms sound the sameOne clear contrast at a time: LOUD (big claps) vs quiet (tiny claps); fast vs slow — copy Speaky.
New instruments hard to sayMime + sound them: violin (bow arm), trombone (slide arm), big bass drum (big boom).
Culture block = just listeningEveryone mimes the music man & plays a new instrument on their verse.
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning! Are you good at the drum? Not yet? Keep practising — you can do it! Play, play, PLAY! Practise an air-instrument, cheer, freeze.

Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Read together. Phonics Hero 2.0 · free reading (Part 1) — children know all 26 sounds AND the long-vowel spellings now, so today they READ whole little decodable stories, not single rows. 🦝 Speaky opens a page.

② Story reading. a different child reads each line; the group blends the tricky word and helps — ▶ Reading relay celebrate every sentence read from zero (GMF1-style).

③ Pages. open Phonics Hero 2.0 p.7–29 — each child reads a line/box to earn a reading star. Start with the first little story together, then children take a line each.

Tricky words: read little · story together as whole words.

16–24′
Values · keep practising! + story
carpet, standing

Talk & act. Play the video/story «Keep practising!» (CD4 T11) with cards — Harry practises the recorder, Zoe the piano. Ask What is Harry doing? What is Zoe doing? (Practising for the show!) They're nervous… then the show is great!

▶ Act & cheer. Act it: play an instrument badly → practise → cheer each other Keep practising! You can do it! Well done! Make the point: practise & you can do it!

 story cards · «Keep practising!» (CD4 T11) · Speaky
24–31′
Content · clap the rhythm ⚡
carpet

Clap the rhythm. Rhythm is the pattern of sounds in music! Clap a simple rhythm (two slow claps + three quick), children copy. Then clap it loud… quiet… fast… slow! children say which. Everyone claps every round.

▶ Fast / slow / loud / quiet! Call a word — Loud! children clap the rhythm that way. Ask Do you like the fast rhythm?

💪 Support: copy the rhythm claps. ⭐ Extension: say the rhythm word — It's loud!

 Speaky · rhythm cards (fast/slow/loud/quiet)
31–40′
Culture · «I am the music man» + new instruments
carpet, standing

Culture song. Play «I am the music man» (CD4 T13) with actions. Meet the new instruments — What can you play? — I play the violin! …the trombone! mime & sound each (violin = bow arm, trombone = slide arm, big bass drum = big boom, piano).

▶ Chocolate Monster + Freeze! Genki Chocolate Monster re-skin: point at a music-man card & a child says What can you play? — I play the trombone! the monster-puppet 'plays' along. Then Freeze! statue holding your instrument.
