🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The main thing is to take the fear of performing out of day one: show the “My world” frame, let them hold the notes, promise rehearsals. The bar is courage and growth, not “best of all”.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Four events: my talk → our video → our fair → SHOWCASE DAY with parents.” “Notes in hands — YES. Rehearsals — many. Alone — never.” “Five lines. You know EVERY word already.”

WHAT WE DO: we go through the talk frame line by line: chorus → pairs; everyone chooses THEIR OWN 5 lines and fills in their own words

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. The frame line by line, chorus with gestures: Hello! My name's… (hand to chest) · I like… (heart) · I can… (bicep) · I've got… (hug)…
  2. Everyone chooses 5+ lines and fills their own words into the template (name, hobby, pet…).
  3. Pairs: read your 2 favourite lines to your partner. Partner: Nice!
  4. 💪 Support: a substitution word bank (hobbies/animals/food) on a card for weaker learners.
📺 On screen: frame templates (🐣 asset) · the substitution bank
👦 Students do: they put together their first talk draft from ready-made lines — the season's product starts on day 1. Expected: “can I have 8 lines?” — yes! Typical error: they write a hobby in Russian — we find the word together.

WHAT WE DO: Kagan · Quiz-Quiz-Trade: reviewing the year's weak spots as a game — cards with EASY questions on core structures

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ▶ Kagan · Quiz-Quiz-Trade: everyone has a card (a question + an answer hint underneath). Ask — answer — TRADE — new partner!
  2. The deck is core language only: Do you like…? · Can you…? · Have you got…? · Is there a… in your room? · What's your favourite…?
  3. The hint under the card is a safety net: forgot it — peeked — said it.
  4. 5–6 trades; the children bring me the hard questions themselves — I pin them onto the “let's review” corner of the board.
📺 On screen: a QQT deck of easy questions (asset) · the “let's review” corner
👦 Students do: they ask, answer and swap all at once — a review of the year without desks or stress. Expected: 90% of answers confident. Typical error: a bare yes/no — the hint underneath pulls out a full answer.

Teams and roles (at their level)
Teams & roles at their level
desks · teams

WHAT WE DO: teams of 3–4 (I put them together myself), roles at their level with a mandatory mini speaking part; a team promise-contract

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I announce the line-ups (prepared at their level). Roles: Same roles, matched to ability: the strongest — speaker, the quietest — editor/camera operator; EVERYONE has a speaking part (even if it’s 2 sentences using the prompts).
  2. A role card for everyone: a picture + 1 sentence about the job (I'm the editor. My job is the video.) — read out loud in the team.
  3. The team's promise-contract, 2 lines: We help each other. We try in English. Signatures.
  4. Check: everyone names their role to the class, in chorus by team.
📺 On screen: role cards (picture+phrase) · contract forms
👦 Students do: they get roles they can manage and say them out loud — everyone has a place on the team from day one. Expected: the quiet ones are glad of “unscary” roles — and they already have 1 phrase.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners know the 4 season events, have a team + role at their level and a personal phrase bank (6 phrases with notes) for their own talk.
  • Subsidiary. The season roadmap is accepted without fear (“everyone performs — everyone with notes”); a review of the year's core language through games (Kagan QQT with easy questions); roles are given out at each learner's level — everyone has a speaking part.
  • Personal. The main thing is to take the fear of performing out of day one: show the “My world” frame, let them hold the notes, promise rehearsals. The bar is courage and growth, not “best of all”.
By the end, students can…
  • name the four season events
  • say their role and its job with support
  • collect 6 phrases they CAN say
  • say 2 lines from the «My world» frame already today

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Will only the strong ones perform?” → No: EVERYONE. But everyone has notes in hand and rehearsals before.
“Are notes cheating?” → No: notes are a safety net. Actors rehearse with a script too.
“Will parents come to watch?” → Yes, right at the end — and they'll see how much you've grown. We'll get ready together.

Assumptions: The year has been covered at Foundation pace, the Unit 9 review is closed (pairs 56–60). Anxiety runs higher than with the 🚀 line — half the lesson works on “I can do this”: frames, demos, easy review games.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
