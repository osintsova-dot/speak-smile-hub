Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to describe what is in a town with “There's a (zoo).” and “There are (two sweet shops).”, and ask & answer quantities “How many (cafés) are there? — There are two.” (Pupil's Book p. 73).
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — the 10 places (L1), numbers 1–10 & colours from Genki — woven into the town survey (“There are two green cafés!”); phonics — Phonics Hero: повторение Части 3, Magic E ур.19–21 (silent e makes the vowel say its name: a-e, i-e — cake, name, bike, kite, тетрадь стр.53–60); listen for detail (PB p. 73 “Listen and count”); singular vs plural a shop → two shops.
  • Personal. Insist on the FULL frame (“There are two cafés”, not “two cafés”); model both There's / There are equally; every task keeps ALL children responding (own count / fingers / all pairs at once) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say “There's a (zoo). There are (two) sweet shops.” about a town scene;
  • ask & answer “How many (cafés) are there? — There are two.”;
  • read Magic-E words (cake, name, bike, kite) and hear the long vowel say its name.

Structure
There's a (zoo).  ·  There are (two) sweet shops.  ·  How many (cafés) are there? — There are two.  ·  Welcome to (Newtown)!  ·  Great! I love (animals). Fantastic!
Form
There's a + one place · There are + number + places (plural -s). Question: How many + places + are there? Answer: There are + number.
Pron. watch
plural /s/ & /z/ endings (shops /s/, zoos /z/); keep the linking “There's a” /ðeəzə/; don't drop “are there”.
Phonics
Phonics Hero: повторение Части 3 — Magic E ур.19–21: silent e = the vowel says its NAME (a-e /эй/ cake, name · i-e /ай/ bike, kite) — sort short✊/long🖐 · read & write cake, bike (тетрадь стр.53–60).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Point to one café: “How many? — One → There's a café.” Point to two: “Two → There are two cafés.” (hear the -s!)
Phonics: “bik or bike — which one is our toy?” → bike — the magic e wakes the long /ай/.

Assumptions: the 10 places & “What's this? / There's a…” met in L1; numbers 1–10 & colours from the Genki block. Magic E (a-e, i-e) was first studied in Часть 3 — today it's revision. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → picture-count grids & audio, not printed text. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“There's” used for many: “There's two cafés”One-finger vs many-fingers gesture; rule “one = There's a, many = There are”; redrill instantly.
Plural -s dropped: “two café”Exaggerate the ending — caféSSS; snake-hiss the /s/, clap on the -s.
Question shrinks: “How many cafés?”Back-chain: …are there? → cafés are there? → How many cafés are there?
Miscount the scenePoint & count together aloud first; ICQ “count with your finger — no shouting”.
Magic-E read as short vowel (kit for kite)Wand gesture: silent e “wakes” the vowel to say its name; palm 🖐 on the long one.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging

⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Cognitive load: only There's a / There are / How many…? and the plural -s are truly NEW — the 10 places, numbers & colours are all recycling, so let the frame carry the challenge (scaffold: one-finger/many-fingers gesture, plural-hiss). Phonics is REVISION (Magic E) — keep it a brisk speed-read + sort. Buffers to drop first if short: the 2nd survey round, the 3rd tic-tac-toe grid. Better every child says one full There are two cafés well than six stages half-done.
