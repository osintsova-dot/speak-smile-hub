Week 23.11–29.11 · Lesson 8🎨 Project · My dream park~8 pupils · make + presentPH 20 (Magic E a-e · p.55–57) · song «There's a pond in the park» (CD1 T39)·Colours(52)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a “My dream park” picture — drawing the park things they choose and describing it: “In my park, there's a (pond). There are (trees)!” in a mini gallery.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle all park words & the There's a…/There are… frames through a creative product; sing «There's a pond in the park» (CD1 T39) & Genki «Colours» (52); take turns presenting; Phonics Hero — Magic E a-e (consolidation), speed-read p.55–57; happy end-of-unit gallery.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the describing sentence first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (one or two sentences each) so no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • choose & draw the park things I want;
  • describe my park — “There's a (pond). There are (trees)!”;
  • say “Well done!” to a friend's park.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do I draw someone else's park or MY dream park?” → my dream park!
“Your friend shows their park — what do we say?” → Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 3 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can name park things, use both frames & ask questions; reading is at Magic E a-e. They know classroom making routines.

Anticipated problems & solutions

Can't decide what to drawOffer a quick menu: point to the park cards — pick three things you love.
Making takes over the lessonSimple park frame ready; time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; pointing + one sentence is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone claps & says Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentPresent in a fast carousel or in small groups at once; one or two sentences each.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello, park designers! Today we make YOUR dream park — anything you like! Show a finished model park, then dance.

9–16′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 20 · Magic E a-e — sounds a-e: proud reading. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read your favourite Magic-E words from the page — proud, fluent reading: cake · gate · name · lake · game! ▶ Star read — read a word to Speaky to earn your park star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.55–57 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read could · should together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Make · My dream park 🧘
tables

Make it. Children draw their dream park — choose the things they love (pond · trees · flowers · swings · slide · roundabout…) and colour it.

Rehearse as they work. go round: Tell me — in my park, there's a…? there are…? help each child rehearse the sentence quietly at the table.

💪 Support: draw & say There's a pond! with the puppet. ⭐ Extension: add a colour & a question: Is there a slide? Yes!

Gallery · present & celebrate

▶ Show & say. In a fast carousel, each child holds up their park: In my park, there's a pond. There are trees! The audience claps and says Well done!

Pairs → circle. pairs first (describe your park to your partner), then a few to the whole circle — keeps waiting short, everyone speaks.

✔ Assess: who describes with There's a/There are? who needs the puppet? — a lovely end-of-unit picture of progress.
