Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“She's a good person also — правильно?” → No: She's ALSO a good person.
“She also had problems или She had also problems?” → She ALSO HAD problems (перед обычным глаголом).
“В каком абзаце past simple?” → Во втором — семья и ранняя жизнь.

Assumptions: После P2–P3 обе формы прошедшего в активе; also как слово знают, но ставят в конец предложения (интерференция с «тоже»). Проектная работа в тройках знакома с U1–U6, роли и таймеры принимают. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (irregular forms / -ed spelling / was–were agreement), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
