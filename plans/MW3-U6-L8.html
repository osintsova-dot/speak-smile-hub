Week 01.03–07.03 · Lesson 8🎨 Project · My favourite meal · paper-plate menu~8 pupils · make + presentPH Part 2 review (les.15–16 · p.39–45) · songs: «What do you have for breakfast?»(T5)·Do You Like Food?(12)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a “My favourite meal” paper-plate menu — drawing their food and saying: “This is my favourite meal. I have pizza and salad for dinner!” in a mini café gallery.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle all ten foods & What do you have for…? / I have… through a creative product; sing Genki «Do You Like Food?» (12) & the language song; take turns presenting like a café; Phonics Hero — Part 2 review, digraphs from lessons 15–16 (ng·oo · ch·sh·th), speed-read our story pages p.39–45; a happy end-of-unit gallery.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the presenting sentences first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (my meal + 2 foods each) so no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • draw my favourite meal on a plate;
  • present it — “I have pizza and salad for dinner!”;
  • say “Well done!” to a friend's meal.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do we draw ALL the food we know, or our favourite meal?” → my favourite meal!
“Your friend shows their plate — what do we say?” → Yummy! Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 6 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can name the ten foods, say what they have for a meal, and order politely; reading loops through the last Part-2 digraph stories. They know the making & presenting routines from earlier projects.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Can't decide a mealModel with the Big Wheel / flashcards first: pick a meal (breakfast/lunch/dinner) then 2–3 foods.
Making takes over the lessonA ready paper plate + food picture-stickers to add; time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; showing the plate + one food is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone says Yummy! Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentPresent as a fast café carousel / small groups at once — my meal + 2 foods each.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning, chefs! Today we make our OWN favourite meal on a plate — what's YOUR favourite food? Show a finished model plate, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero · Part 2 review (lessons 15–16) — sounds ng · oo  ·  ch · sh · th. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-blend: r-i-ng → ring, m-oo-n → moon, ch-i-p → chip, sh-i-p → ship, th-i-n → thin — proud, fluent! ▶ Star read — read a digraph word from our story page to Speaky to earn your reading star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.39–45 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read review Yellow tricky words together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.39–45 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–22′
Model the product + sentences
carpet

Watch & copy. Show how to make the paper-plate meal — draw / stick 2–3 foods for a favourite meal. Model presenting with the puppet: This is my favourite meal. I have pizza and salad for dinner! Everyone says it together once.

22–38′
Make · My favourite meal plate 🧘
tables

Make it. Children draw / stick their favourite foods on a paper plate and decorate. Write/trace the food words if they can.

Rehearse as they work. go round: Tell me — what's your favourite meal? What do you have? help each child rehearse their meal quietly at the table.

💪 Support: draw & name the foods with the puppet. ⭐ Extension: join them: I have pizza AND salad for dinner.

42–52′
Café gallery · present & celebrate
carpet, circle

▶ Show & say. In a fast café carousel, each child holds up their plate: This is my favourite meal. I have pizza and salad for dinner! The audience claps & says Yummy! Well done!

Pairs → circle. pairs first (say it to your partner), then a few to the whole circle — keeps waiting short, everyone speaks.

✔ Assess: who says their meal with I have… and… for…? who needs the puppet? — a lovely end-of-unit picture of progress.

What amazing chefs — I'm so proud of you! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
