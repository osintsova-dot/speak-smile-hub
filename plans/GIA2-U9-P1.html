🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why (for the teacher): The first of the last two lessons of the course. This lesson has one trap and it is very predictable: the unit is called «Look what you know!», it contains not a single new word, and the class decides on the way in that it is «just revision» — meaning they can switch off. So the topic «we are revising the whole year» is never announced at all. What is announced is an audit: the year is over, every agent has an arsenal, and today we check what in it still works and what has gone rusty. The twenty questions round the edge of the spread on pp.110–111 are the perfect gate: they are short, they cover ALL the grammar of the course, and they show the holes immediately. The word «grammar» is not spoken once in this lesson — the word is «systems». The second purpose of the lesson is to give you a map of the class before the FINAL block: who needs pulling where in the last lessons.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Final audit. Nine months. Eight missions. Everything you built is on this desk — and today we find out what still works. Twenty checks around the edge of the page: eight minutes, no book, no neighbour. Then four colour zones: red, green, blue, yellow. Clear a zone — your crew takes the points. Leave a hole — it goes on your card, and your card is the only revision you'll need for the final. One rule today: ‘I knew that in October’ is not an answer. Show me now. Page 110. Timer on.”
The gist, if you need to restate it: the final audit: the year is over, we check what of everything you have built actually works. 20 quick checks round the edge of the spread, then four colour zones. Anything you don't close goes onto your personal map of the year — and that will be your only revision for the final test.

WHAT WE DO: 8 minutes: the 20 questions round the edge of the spread pp.110–111 — no looking inside the page, no neighbour

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Pages 110 and 111. Look at the EDGE of the page only — the numbered questions. Twenty of them. Eight minutes. Write the answers in your notebook. No talking, no looking inside the page.
  2. Give one time warning each at minute 4 and at minute 7.
  3. They count their own score and write it in the margin: Your number out of twenty. Nobody else sees it.
  4. Close it with no feedback: Keep that number. We come back to it at the end.
📺 On screen: SB pp.110–111 (the margins only) · 8-minute timer · mini-whiteboards (Backup: write «8:00» on the board and count down aloud from ten seconds)
👦 Students do: students write in silence, 20 short answers. Error: they look inside the page for a prompt — close the gate with a gesture, not with your voice

WHAT WE DO: p.108 ex.1–4: nine pictures → the letter puzzle → the odd word out with each verb, as a race

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ex.1 against the clock: Nine pictures, nine words. One word per picture. Three minutes. Crew record on the board. Give the TB hint only if they get stuck: B and E are places. F is a job.

WHAT WE DO: p.109 ex.1–6: the three subskill tips of the year → where the texts come from → prediction → track 56 → True/False → repairing the false ones

🚀 Grammar gauntlet · four colours
crews · stations

WHAT WE DO: pp.110–111: four colour zones of 4 minutes each — red, green, blue, yellow; a zone is closed only when EVERY sheet is filled in

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. The order is exactly as in the book and in the Teacher's Book: first the edge questions of that colour (they were already in the gate), then the exercises of the central section in the same colour.
  2. 🔴 Red zone (4 min): ex.1 possessive and object pronouns · ex.2 present simple and adverbs of frequency · ex.3 questions and answers in present simple.
  3. 🟢 Green zone (4 min): ex.1 present simple vs present continuous in a dialogue · ex.2 comparatives and superlatives · ex.3 school rules with can/can't/must/mustn't.
  4. 🔵 Blue zone (4 min): ex.1 find and repair the error (a/an, some, any, there is/are) · ex.2 build How much / How many questions · ex.3 a past simple dialogue with was/were and there was/were.
  5. 🟡 Yellow zone (4 min): ex.1 rewrite in the right past simple form · ex.2 reconstruct the questions from the answers · ex.3 choose the future form.
  6. The check after every zone is Kagan Numbered Heads Together, 60 seconds, and crew points straight away. Zone closed only when EVERY sheet in the crew is filled in. One empty sheet — zone not closed.
  7. Everyone keeps a personal gap list right there in the margin: Write down what YOU missed. Not what your crew missed.
📺 On screen: SB pp.110–111 · timer · four coloured markers per crew (Backup: the four zones = the four corners of the room, each zone's task on an A4 sheet on the desk, a coloured circle instead of the screen)
👦 Students do: students work through four zones in a row and keep a personal gap list. Expected: red and yellow go faster (present simple and past simple are well drilled), blue fails most often — much/many and any in negatives. Error: in green ex.1 they put present simple where continuous is needed («What do you do at the moment?») — they are not reading the time marker, so point at the words at the moment instead of speaking

PRODUCT · map of the year
solo → crews

WHAT WE DO: the product of the lesson: a personal map of the year on one A4 sheet — 5 gaps + 1 strength, presented to the crew in 40 seconds

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. One sheet. Two sections. Section one: FIVE lines — things that are not automatic yet. Exactly five, not more. Section two: ONE line — the thing you can do now that you couldn't do in September.
  2. Be strict about the format: ‘Grammar’ is not a line. ‘Tenses’ is not a line. Write the actual thing: ‘much / many’, ‘there was / there were’.
  3. Kagan Timed RoundRobin, 40 seconds each: everyone names their one strength and their most painful gap to the crew. The crew does not comment, the crew listens.
  4. Back to the gate: Your number out of twenty from minute eight. Which four of those twenty are on your card now? Circle them.
  5. Crew points: for cards filled in by all four, for zones cleared, for gold runs.
📺 On screen: an A4 sheet each (the card template) · the leaderboard poster (Backup: they draw the card in their exercise book in 30 seconds from the model on the board — two sections, 5 lines and 1 line)
👦 Students do: students write their card and name a gap aloud in the crew. Expected: every card is different — and that is the point of the lesson. Error: they write «revise everything» — send it back to be rewritten: «everything» cannot be learned, «much/many» can

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«She's playing tennis» — which tense is that and when do we take it? → present continuous: happening now (and also for future arrangements).
«There is three museums» — what is wrong? → there ARE: the noun is plural.
«He didn't went home» — why not? → after didn't comes the base form: didn't go.
«I'm going to buy» and «I'll buy» — what is the difference? → going to = a plan, already decided; will = a decision or a prediction made right now.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
«This is revision, we know all of this» — the most predictable reaction to a unit like thisDon't argue and don't persuade: from minute 8 the gate of 20 checks, no book, no neighbour. The score is private. They usually close 11–14 out of 20 — after that the argument ends by itself.
End of the year: motivation has dropped, «can't we just be on holiday»The bet is not on «we have to revise» but on competition and on the season finale: a crew race through the zones, the leaderboard, and an honest promise — everything you close today you will not have to cram before the final test. Plus physically short blocks: nothing longer than 16 minutes.
They race through the zones — the strong ones carry it and the weak ones copyKagan Numbered Heads Together when each zone is checked: the number I call answers, not the one who is fastest. Plus the rule: a zone is closed only when EVERY student in the crew has their own sheet filled in.
The map of the year turns into a box-ticking exerciseThe format of the map is strict and short: exactly 5 lines of «not automatic yet» + one line of «what I definitely can do». Long lists are banned: a long list = nothing gets learned.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
