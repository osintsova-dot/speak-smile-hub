🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Language aim
Form and choose the present and past simple passive and the modal passive (can be done, must not be used, might be given), and say WHY a sentence is passive: the doer is unknown, unimportant, or less important than the thing.
What is genuinely hard
Two separate difficulties, and they need separate treatment. The first is mechanical: Russian marks the passive on the verb itself, so the auxiliary BE simply vanishes — «this photo changed by computer». The second is a choice: Russian prefers the active with an unnamed subject (мне сказали), English prefers the passive (I was told), and no rule in the book teaches the preference. Then the modal adds a third part to hold: modal + BE + third form.
Typical error to expect
«This photo changed by computer», «the film watched by everyone» — the passive needs the verb BE, and Russian does not: Russian marks the passive on the verb itself, so the auxiliary simply disappears. It is the single commonest passive error in the Russian paper.
«was took», «were showed», «is broke» — the passive takes the THIRD form. Half the marks in the passive section of the block test are irregular participles, not the rule.
«must used», «can be use», «should cleaned» — a modal in the passive needs both parts: modal + BE + past participle, and the modal is followed by a bare infinitive. Write must be used on the board and leave it there.
Why the lesson is built this way
The rule is not presented — it is extracted from a text the class read yesterday and has already understood, so all the attention is free for the form. The Teacher's Book warmer does the concept in four questions on the board (whose image? who changed it? does it matter who? then underline the verb), and that order is kept because it makes the CHOICE the first thing learned and the form the second. The modal passive gets its own board line and its own colour because it is a three-part structure and the middle part is the one that disappears. The productive stage is a public notice — the one genre in English that is written almost entirely in modal passives — so the grammar is not practised, it is needed.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Yesterday's article had one sentence in it forty times and nobody noticed: photos ARE CHANGED, it WAS USED, the adverts COULDN'T BE SHOWN. Today: the grammar of everything written about you rather than by you.”

WHAT WE DO: ex.1–2: name the two tenses and complete the five-gap rule

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ex.1, in the book: underline the passive forms in the airbrushing example and name the tense of each. Answer: are changed = present simple passive · was used = past simple passive.
  2. ex.2, three minutes, individually: the rule with five gaps (be · present · past · subject · by). Then compare in pairs before the class check.
  3. Check aloud: the rule, five gaps: a be · b present · c past · d subject · e by. Write the completed rule on the board in the students' own words, not the book's.
  4. One extra minute on the agent, because it is a marks issue: I was given lots of presents BY my friends. Then delete by my friends and ask whether the sentence still works (it does). Rule of thumb on the board: keep the agent only if it is news.
  5. Say the two-beat routine once and use it for the rest of the lesson: every passive is clapped in two beats — was TAKEN.

WHAT WE DO: ex.3: the three-part structure named, coloured and drilled

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ex.3, in the book: three examples from yesterday's article — what can be done on a computer · the results that could be achieved · the adverts couldn't be shown. Elicit the pattern into the gap: modal + be + past participle.
  2. Colour the three parts on the board differently and say the rule that costs marks: after a modal, be is a BARE infinitive — never to be.
  3. Thirty-second drill, whole class, three times each with the two-beat clap: must be booked · can't be identified · may be allowed.
  4. Pairs, two minutes: turn three active sentences from the board into modal passives — You must book tickets. · We can edit the photo. · They might cancel the trip. Check on the board in writing.
  5. Name the trap before it happens: If you write 'must used', you have written nothing. The middle word is the mark.

WHAT WE DO: ex.4: the productive form drill, checked in writing because spelling is the mark

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Irregular participle lists go on the desks that need them NOW — before the exercise, not after the failure.
  2. ex.4 individually, five minutes, clock visible: six sentences, each needing a modal passive.

  5. Two minutes of the reverse question, and it is the deepest part of the lesson: Which of these six sentences would be worse in the active? Why?

Structure
Photos are changed by using computer software. · In the beginning, it was used to tidy up small problems. · It's amazing what can be done on a computer. · Mobile phones must not be used during performances.
Form
be + past participle, and be carries the tense: is/are + pp (present simple) · was/were + pp (past simple) · modal + BE + pp, always a bare infinitive after the modal · by + agent only when the agent matters.
Pron. watch
the eight social-media verbs are easy to say and hard to stress in a sentence: the class will put the accent on the pronoun (I TAGGED her) where English puts it on the new information · the phrases with in run together and the vowel of in reduces to a schwa: /ɪnfækt/, /ɪnəˈvɑːns/, /ɪnpəˈtɪkjələ/ — say them as one word or they sound like a list · in the passive, was and were are weak forms: /wəzˈteɪkən/, /wəˈʃəʊn/, which is why students do not hear the passive in the listening
Concept checks (CCQs) & ICQs · Assumptions
«My bag was taken.» Do we know who took it? → no. So why passive? → because the person is unknown, and the bag is what matters.
«This game can be play online» — right or wrong? → wrong: can be PLAYED. The modal takes be, and be takes the third form.
«I was given a present by my friends.» Can we delete by my friends? → yes, and the sentence still works. So when do we keep it? → when the agent is news.

Classroom management — signals
“Eyes up in 3-2-1” — a calm countdown → on “1” every pair of eyes is on you. Never talk over the class.
Visible timer — timer running = we work; timer stops = pens down. The timer is the boss, not you.
“Boards up!” — a mini-whiteboard round: everyone answers at the same moment — nobody hides, nobody shouts out first.
Silent mode — palm down = we work alone and in silence: listening or writing. Questions afterwards, not during.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, the class claps twice and freezes — an instant reset in the middle of a game.
English-first — an idea may be started in Russian, but the POINT is only given for the English version: reformulate and repeat it.

🇷🇺 Where this lands in the EGE (Russian state exam, grades 10–11) — teacher's note, never taken into class

The same skill is tested by: Tasks 19–25 (grammar in a text) — the passive is a named line of the codifier and appears in almost every paper.

The Russian paper gives the verb in capitals and expects the candidate to decide, unprompted, that the sentence is passive: «The camera IS CONSIDERED a miraculous invention», «British people WERE SHOWN a fake film». Two marks go missing in the same two places every year — the wrong participle (was showed) and the missing form of be after a modal (must used instead of must be used). Both are drilled today, on the board, in writing.

Who needs this: not every student in the group will sit the EGE. Those who will get the trainer link in the homework as an optional extra. The rest are told nothing — the lesson works as language teaching without it.
