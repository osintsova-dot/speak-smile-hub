🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The first project of the unit for new students follows the coursebook steps strictly (THINK→PLAN→CREATE→PRESENT), with a note-taking frame and active-listening phrases on cards. The unit review is built in as stations, framed as «the check before you go on air» — test preparation without the word «test». The presentation is the whole crew at the board, the speaker holding a card.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Today we tell the world about our home. A visitor from another country wants to know: when should I come, and what should I bring? Your pair makes a short video — four seasons, real numbers, real advice, just like the girl from Finland. One studio rule: while your partner speaks, you listen and then repeat it back. After that, four quick checks — you'll see, you know all of it — and then we go on air.”
In simpler words, if the class needs support: We tell the world about our region: when to come and what to bring. Each pair makes a short video about the four seasons, like the girl from Finland. Studio rule: listen to your partner and say it back. Then four checks — and we go on air.

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

WHAT WE DO: ex.3, active-listening phrases on cards → ex.4, notes on the three remaining seasons

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

WHAT WE DO: 4-minute stations with support: weather · one word/two words · than and the …est · modals (SB p.57)

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Four checks. Four minutes each. The posters on the wall help you!
  2. At every station there is a prompt card and the poster from its own pair (NOUN/ADJECTIVE, the ONE/TWO baskets, TWO→than / THREE+→the …est, the three colours of the modals).
  3. The stations: a weather-word blitz (track 28) · the compound baskets (track 29) · the comparison forms · choose the modal and build the question.
  4. Each station finishes with Kagan Numbered Heads Together: Number ones — answer for your team!
  5. A personal «superpower list»: whatever did not work, I write down for myself.
📺 On screen: SB p.57 Quick review · the station cards · the class posters · timer
👦 Students do: they revise the unit with support. Expected: they recognise their own materials and cope faster than they expect to
📓 Interactive notebook — Get_Involved_A2_Unit4_Translation_Match_It_2up_A4 + Get_Involved_A2_Unit4_Translation_Translate_It_2up_A4
two 2-up sheets (one A4 = 2 pupils each): MATCH IT! — 12 Russian sentences to English A–L (weather by season, comparatives and superlatives, can/must/mustn't, packing, suggestions and agreement), then TRANSLATE IT! — the same 12 from memory
  • Glue MATCH IT! in and join the pairs in pencil — first alone, then check with a partner.
  • Glue TRANSLATE IT! on the next page and write the English without looking back.
  • Self-check against the matched sheet; every mistake is rewritten as a full sentence.
  • Write your score out of 12 in the margin — that is the unit's exit line.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can make notes about four seasons and present them in a short video with a frame, and revise the whole unit at four stations.
  • Subsidiary. The model video about Finland (ex.1) and the analysis of the Model project (ex.2); Super skills communication with phrase cards (ex.3); ex.4, the notes; ex.5–7, sharing out the parts, rehearsal, filming; ex.8, peer review; Quick review p.57 as stations with support; the unit's WDYT question.
  • Personal. The project follows the coursebook steps, I do not cut it. At the stations there are prompt cards and the posters from the previous pairs. I call the gap list «the superpower list»; the word «test» comes only at the very end of the pair. If there is nothing to film with, we present live to another pair (TB backup).
By the end, students can…
  • make notes (not sentences) about four seasons
  • use active-listening phrases with a partner
  • present one season from a note card
  • pass four revision stations

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Are there full sentences in the Model project? → no, only notes.
Does active listening mean keeping quiet? → no: nodding and saying it back, «So you think…».
Are the stations a test? → no, they are the check before we go on air.

Assumptions: SB pp.57–59, KTP pair 36. The first full project of the unit for this line; success = they went through all the steps, not «the best video».

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The discussion slides into RussianPhrase cards on the table; the rule: every turn to your partner starts with a phrase from the card.
They do not understand what to write in the notesA five-line frame per season (MONTHS · TEMPERATURE · WEATHER · ACTIVITIES · BRING) + a sample filled in by me for winter.
The stations get squeezed out by the projectThe stations sit firmly in the middle of the pair, on a timer, with the posters and the cards — they earn as many points as the project does.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
