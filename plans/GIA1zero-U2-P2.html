👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Вывожу утверждение 1 (Bill and Barb have got two daughters).

ЧТО ДЕЛАЕМ: плюсы/минусы большой семьи из текста (Understand) → маленькой из головы (Apply) → голосование «идеальный размер» (Evaluate)

👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Understand: Two GOOD things + two HARD things about a big family — FROM the text! Three minutes. (помощь: never lonely · homework help / share a room · two bathrooms · noisy).
  2. Apply: Now a SMALL family — your ideas, the frame helps: You've got your own room. It's quiet.
  3. Evaluate голосованием: The ideal number of people in a family? Write YOUR number — boards up! Defend in one frame-sentence.
  4. Рамки на доске: A big family is ___ because ___ . / In a small family you've got ___ .
📺 Показываем: доска: таблица «good/hard» + рамки · мини-доски
👦 Дети: вытаскивают аргументы из текста, добавляют свои, голосуют числом с защитой. Ожидаемо: числа 3–6, споры по рамкам. Типичная ошибка: аргумент по-русски — не попадает в таблицу (правило объявлено).

WB Unit 2 (стр. 12–19) · фото доски на телефон — можно
📋 Справка для подготовки (читать ДО урока, не в классе)
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can find facts in a TV-guide article and report them with simple have got sentences: “They've got a bus! The children haven't got their own bedroom.”
  • Subsidiary. Reading subskill: predicting what a text is about (bets before reading); Word work — 6 text words; online comments (Eli·Clara·Toby); Critical thinking Understand→Apply→Evaluate with heavy scaffolds.
  • Personal. Never read the page aloud frontally — bets and hunts do the reading. Timer visible in every hunt. Praise fact-finding speed, not translation. Seed haven't got corrections for Pair 3.
By the end, students can…
  • predict text content from photos and check
  • find a named fact within a time limit
  • match 6 new words to definitions
  • say one advantage and one disadvantage of a big family

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Predicting = guessing after reading?” → No — BEFORE. Photos and titles speak first.
“Lonely = alone?” → No: alone = один, lonely = грустно одному.
“Share a room = у каждого своя комната?” → No — one room TOGETHER.

Assumptions: 17 family words fresh from P1 (rewind retrieves them). Reading a long authentic page is scary — success = find facts, not understand every word; say it out loud. have got frames from P1 keep working receptively.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
