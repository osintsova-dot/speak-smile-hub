Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer Wh-questions in the present: “Where/When/What do you (study Maths)? — I (read books) in the (library).”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Ravi & Josh's dialogue about school (CD1 14); discover the rule — we use do in the question, NOT in the answer (Grammar clue); recycle the 12 places from L1; write questions and answers about our school (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Drill the do-question vs no-do-answer contrast until it's visible; keep the interview stage strictly timed; model the full answer, accept short answers and recast.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Where/When/What do you…?” correctly;
  • answer WITHOUT do: “I study Maths in the classroom.”;
  • complete a school timetable by asking & answering.

Form
Wh-word + do + you + verb…? Answer: I + verb (no do): When do you study Music? → I study Music on Friday.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Where DO you play…? — is do in the question or in the answer?” → in the question only.
“What do you do — how many dos? Why two?” → two: one for the question, one is the verb “do”.

Assumptions: 12 places are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up). Wh-words met before, but the do-support pattern is the new focus. Pupils can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar table.

19–27′
Tic-Tac-Toe · Wh-questions
desks, 2 teams

▶ Noughts & Crosses — school. 3×3 grid of place pictures. To claim a cell, the team ASKS the other team a Wh-question about it — Where do you read books? — and the other team answers in chorus WITHOUT do: We read books in the library! Both sides speak every turn. Rotate the asker.

🎮 Projector/board option: draw the 3×3 grid on the whiteboard; a rotating child taps the winning cell as the WHOLE team says the question aloud.

ICQ: Is “do” in the question or the answer? (→ the question!)

A5 glue-in grammar map — QUESTION BUILDER (WH-word + do + you + base verb) with WHAT/WHEN/WHERE keys and a “DO has two jobs” panel
  • Pupils glue the map on the page facing their L1 school directory, so both open together.
  • Point at the QUESTION BUILDER; the class reads it as one chunk: WHERE — DO — YOU — PLAY?
  • Everyone traces do in the question box in colour, then crosses it out in the answer box — the rule becomes a picture.
  • The map stays open during Tic-Tac-Toe: a team may look, but the question must come out with no gaps.
  • Struggling team — read them the “DO has two jobs” panel before their turn instead of giving the answer.
When: ~4 min at the start of the 19–27′ “Tic-Tac-Toe · Wh-questions” stage (desks, 2 teams) — glue first, then play with the map openPrep: 1 × A5 per pupil. Glue sticks + one coloured pencil each (for tracing do).

Timetable info-gap
desks, pairs

Model first. Show two half-timetables (A has Monday, B has Tuesday). Model publicly: When do we have Music? — We have Music on Monday. Where? — In the music room.

▶ Talk Partners info-gap (act. 4-style). Pairs ask & answer to complete the missing cells — When do we have PE? Where do we have PE? Both write. 5–6 exchanges.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — who keeps the do out of the answer? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: question stems printed on the half-sheet. ⭐ Extension: add: What do you do in PE? — We play basketball.
