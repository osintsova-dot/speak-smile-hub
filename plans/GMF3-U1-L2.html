Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer present-simple Wh- questions about school using “Where / When / What + do + you + (verb)?” — and to answer without do (“I play football on the pitch.”).
  • Subsidiary. Recycle Lesson 1 places-in-a-school lexis inside the new structure; read & act out a model dialogue; write question–answer pairs; speak in a Talk-Partners survey.
  • Personal. Model on the board first (I do → we do → you do); give an ICQ before every task; keep a stirrer→settler rhythm with signals; make the “do-rule” a picture, not a lecture.
By the end, pupils can…
  • build a correct Wh- question with do (word order right);
  • choose Where / When / What for place / time / thing;
  • answer a Wh- question without do, using a school place.

Structure
Where / When / What + do + you + (verb)? — “Where do you play football?” · “When do you have PE?” · “What do you do in the library?” → answer: “I read books in the library.”
Form
WH-word + do + you + base verb? → Answer: I + base verb + place / time. do lives in the question, not in the answer.
Pron. watch
connected speech: “What do you” /ˈwɒdʒə/, “Where do you” /ˈweədʒə/ — one smooth chunk; falling intonation on Wh- questions.
Note (phonics)
No new phonics today — the consonant sound j comes on Lesson 5 (L5 Phon+Cult+Lit). This lesson = grammar only.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“In Where do you play? — is do in the question?” → yes.
“In the answer I play football — do we say do?” → no.
“Where…? asks about a place or a time?” → place. “When…?” → time. “What…?” → thing / action.

Assumptions: pupils know the present-simple affirmative (“I play…”), know the 10–12 school places from Lesson 1, and can read short sentences. Teams: max 4 per team — 12 = 3×4 · 10 = 3+3+4 · 9 = 3×3 · 8 = 2×4 · 7 = 3+4.

Presentation · model (I do)
board → desks

Model dialogue — Pupil's Book p.9, ex 1 (CD1 14). Books open, page 9. Listen and read. Play Ravi & Josh at the gym. Then Listen and repeat. In pairs act it out (swap roles). Draw out the questions: What do you do in here? · When do you have PE? · Where do you play football?

Grammar box — ex 3 (CD1 15). Think-aloud. Build one question on the board strip by strip: Where … do … you … play football? Say your thinking aloud: First the Wh-word, then do, then you, then the verb. CCQ: Is “do” in the question? (yes) → answer it: I play football on the pitch — do we say “do” here? (no). Cross out the ghost do in the answer. Colour-key: Where=place · When=time · What=thing.

Drill the chunk. Back-chain the tricky connected form: …do you / What do you / What do you do? Chorus ×3, then 2–3 pupils solo.

Tic-Tac-Toe · Wh-questions (we do)
carpet, 2 teams

📓 Interactive notebook — glue in GMF3_Unit1_Lesson2_WH_Questions_A5 (QUESTION BUILDER + “DO has two jobs” panel). Glue it facing the L1 school-directory so both open together. Class reads the builder as one chunk: WHERE — DO — YOU — PLAY? Everyone colour-traces “do” in the question box, then crosses it out in the answer box — the rule becomes a picture. Map stays open during the game. ~4 min.

▶ Tic-Tac-Toe (Оксана's format). Nine A4 cards = a 3×3 grid on the carpet (each card = a place or a Wh-word prompt). Two teams, ✕ and ⭕ tokens laid on top. To claim a cell a player must say a full correct Wh- question using that card — e.g. Where do you play basketball? A single word or silence = no cell, other team's turn. First to three in a row wins. Everyone in the team whispers the question together before the speaker says it (all rehearse).

ICQ: Do we say one word, or the whole question? (→ whole question!)

27–35′
Quiz-Quiz-Trade (we do)
mingle, standing

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (Kagan — everyone active at once). Each pupil holds a card: a Wh-word + a place/verb picture (e.g. When · PE / Where · football / What · library). Stand up, hand up, pair up!

Partner A quizzes: When do you have PE? Partner B answers I have PE on Monday. — then B quizzes A. Coach, don't tell: if stuck, the card-holder points at the Wh-key and gives a starter, not the whole answer. Praise: Nice question! → trade cards → new partner. 3–4 rounds.

Settle signal to stop: WiFi 🖐.

 Wh-question mingle cards (1 per pupil) · music · ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade
35–43′
Boat Race relay (freer)
carpet → wall, teams

▶ Boat Race (Genki — team relay for speed). Two teams in lines, a paper “boat” each. On the wall a row of answer cards (“I read books in the library.” “I play football on the pitch.”). Runner 1 grabs an answer, says the question that fits it — What do you do in the library? — correct → the boat moves one step forward; then runs back and tags Runner 2.

Wrong or missing do → the boat stays; try again next turn (peer help allowed). First boat to the finish line wins. Fast, everyone runs, grammar hides inside the race.

Reset if loud: Freeze!

 2 paper boats · answer cards on wall · finish line · ▶ Boat Race
43–53′
My favourite place (you do)
desks, then pairs

Production — write, then speak (Talk Partners). Think of your favourite place in our school. Pupils write three Q&A pairs in their notebooks with the sentence frames on the board (support for weaker pupils):

My favourite place is the [computer room]. · — What do you do there? — I [play games and study]. · — When do you go there? — I go there on [Tuesday].

Then ▶ Talk Partners: swap partners and interview each other with real Wh- questions; listener repeats one fact back (So you play games in the computer room!). Monitor for the do-rule; note 2–3 nice questions to share.
