What this document is (RU)

📝 Это ПРОТОКОЛ проведения итогового письменного контроля за весь год (Units 1–9), а не поурочный план новой темы. Сам тест — издательский, из папки курса: GMF_L3_End_of_Year_Standard_Level_Test (+ Answer Key). Содержимое теста здесь НЕ воспроизводится — только методика проведения: тайминг, рассадка, ICQ, тайм-менеджмент, что делать с быстро закончившими.

Формат-опора: итоговый тест построен по логике Cambridge YLE A1 Movers practice (Reading & Writing + Listening) — знакомый детям тип заданий (picture-word, gap, tick, match). Части: лексика · грамматика · чтение · аудирование. Уровень «Standard» (есть и Higher — для сильной группы, на твоё усмотрение). Проверь заранее: распечатай по числу детей, подготовь CD/аудио-трек для Listening, сверься с Answer Key.

Pre-flight checklist (before pupils arrive)
  • Print the End-of-Year Standard Level Test — one per child + 1–2 spare.
  • Audio ready & cued: Listening track, speaker tested, volume checked from the back row.
  • Seating: desks apart, solo, folder screens between neighbours («exam style, like big kids»).
  • On each desk: a sharp pencil + spare, an eraser, and a face-down quiet menu for early finishers.
  • Board: write today's 4 parts as icons (📝 words · ⚙ grammar · 👀 reading · 🎧 listening) + a simple visual timer.
  • Answer Key to hand (for your post-lesson marking, not shown to pupils).
  • Class list for ticking who finished / needs support.
  • Tone plan: you are calm and warm — no competitive games today, help with procedure, never answers.
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. Pupils show what they know across Units 1–9 on the Final Test (vocabulary · grammar · reading · listening) in a calm, supported, model-first format — no surprises.
  • Subsidiary. Test-taking micro-skills at year's end: read the example first, skip & return, check your name, use the full time, quiet early-finisher behaviour.
  • Personal. Radiate calm & pride in the year. Manage procedure, not answers. Note common errors → feed the Reserve-2 revision lessons (71–72).
By the end, pupils can…
  • follow test instructions independently across four parts;
  • complete a year-end YLE-style paper calmly;
  • use “skip and come back” and self-check before handing in.

ICQ before the test (check understanding)
“If a task is hard — stop, or skip and come back?” → skip and come back.
“Is it OK to look at a friend's paper?” → no — I want to see what YOU know; that's how I help you.
“Finished early — what do you do?” → check my paper, then the quiet menu, silently.
Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Year-end / «big test» nervesNormalise: «it's the same as our books, just quiet — and it's everything you already know»; breathing in-2-3-out-2-3 before starting.
Listening part — a child loses the placeHold up your copy, point-follow the item numbers; play each item twice; pause between items.
Early finishers disturb othersFace-down quiet menu on desks before the test (word-search · reading corner); «no walking about» rule set up front.
A child freezes completelyKneel beside, whisper the example through, do the first item together, then step away — procedure help only.
Copy-glancingFolder screens between desks; positive frame («exam style»); calm reminder, not scolding.
Runs out of timeVisible timer + calm countdowns at 20/10/5′; «skip & return» taught up front so easy marks aren't lost.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 👋 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — administration timeline (60 min)
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) ▶ = game 🎮 = interactive (projector) 💪 support · ⭐ extension
TimeStage / whereProcedureMaterials
0–3′
Settle & reassure
door → desks

Greet each child at the door: Hello! Come in! To solo desks. Hello! Today is our show-what-you-know day for the WHOLE year. It's easy — it's everything we've practised. I'm proud of you already.

Everyone stands, one deep breath together: Breathe in… 2, 3… and out… 2, 3. Sit down. Big smile.

 carpet-free room · solo desks ready
3–8′
Confidence loop + how-to
desks

Low-key warm-up (no games). Flash 6 of the easiest cross-unit cards — quick whole-class chorus, just to prime confidence.

Test how-to (show a blank paper). Name here! Read the example first! Hard task? Skip it — come back later. Finished? Check your paper, then the quiet menu. Silent, please.

ICQ: What do you do if it's hard? (→ skip & return)   Can you look at a friend's paper? (→ no)

 6 easy flashcards · blank test copy to demo
8–20′
Parts ➊ Vocabulary + ➋ Grammar
solo desks

Hand out papers face-down. Turn over… now. Write your name. Read Part 1 example aloud, do the example together, then: Off you go. Silent work.

➊ Vocabulary (picture → word, cross-unit) · ➋ Grammar (choose/complete the correct structure). ~6′ guide per part; circulate quietly, help procedure only. Calm reminder at the halfway point.

 GMF_L3 End-of-Year Test · pencils · folder screens
20–30′
Part ➌ Reading
solo desks

➌ Reading (read & match / tick / gap — YLE-style). Read the example aloud, check with an ICQ: Do you read the whole text first, then answer? (→ yes). Take your time. Skip and return if you're stuck.

Micro-check: scan the room — anyone stuck on the example gets a whispered walk-through of the example ONLY.

 GMF_L3 End-of-Year Test
30–42′
Part ➍ Listening
solo desks

➍ Listening (listen & tick / number / match). Pencils down, eyes on your paper, listen first. Play each item twice, pause between items; hold your copy up and point-follow the numbers so no one loses the place. ICQ before play: How many times will you hear it? (→ two).

After the track: Finish this part. Two more minutes.

 Listening CD/track · speaker · teacher's copy to point-follow
42–48′
Check & collect
solo desks

Countdown & self-check. Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1. Pencils down. Now check: is your name on it? Any empty boxes? Fill your best guess.

Collect papers yourself, desk by desk, thanking each child: Thank you. Well done. ✔ Early finishers worked on the quiet menu (word-search · reading corner) — no walking about.

 quiet menu sheets · collection tray
48–56′
Brain unwind (non-competitive)
carpet

Calm cooperative round. Move to carpet. ▶ Class chain (class vs itself): build the longest cross-unit sentence chain — This year I can name sea animals… I can tell the time… I can talk about the past… Stretch + breathe. Praise the WORK: You worked so hard — a whole year in one test. I'm proud of you.

56–60′
Reflection & close
carpet → door

▶ Timed Pair Share: Tell your partner: one part that was easy, one that was tricky. (20 sec each). «I can…» tick: Colour your progress card — I finished the Final Test!

I'm proud of you! Let's sing Goodbye. Thank you for the lesson! Sing Goodbye. High-five line at the door — each child hears one personal praise word.

 «I can…» progress cards · Goodbye song mp3 · reward stickers

Homework

В день итогового теста — без письменного ДЗ. По желанию — поиграть в любимые игры года в приложении (5–7 min). Впереди — Speaking Exam (урок 70), не волнуйся: это разговор о том, что ты уже умеешь.

 app (optional) · no written HW
After the lesson — marking & feedback

Mark with the Answer Key (GMF_L3 End-of-Year Standard Level Test Answer Key). Record per-part scores on the class list. Harvest the 2–3 most common errors across the group → these become the diagnosis-backed stations in Reserve 2 (Lessons 71–72). Report to parents in the school's usual format: praise effort + name one strength and one next step per child.

Не показывай детям ключ / не разбирай ответы на уроке — только эмоциональная поддержка.
