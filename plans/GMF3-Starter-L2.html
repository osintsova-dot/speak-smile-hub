Week 07.09–13.09 · Lesson 2✨ NEW · Do you like …-ing? + love/like/don't like~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.5 · AB p.3
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer about likes: “Do you like (dancing)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” and express the like-scale: “I love / I like / I don't like (swimming).”
  • Subsidiary. Name 8 free-time activities; listen for detail — Josh likes… / Lily loves… / Ravi doesn't like… (CD1 04); sing verse 2 of «Welcome to the lighthouse!» with mime (CD1 05); recycle personal-information questions from L1.
  • Personal. Drill the short answers until automatic — full “Yes, I do”, never bare “yes”. Model the love/like/don't-like scale with a thumb (up / sideways / down); accept one-word answers from slower pupils and recast.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 8 free-time activities;
  • ask & answer “Do you like …-ing? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”;
  • say what they love / like / don't like doing.

Structure
Do you like (dancing)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  ·  I love / I like / I don't like (swimming).
Form
Do + you + like + verb-ing? Short answers with do/don't — never “Yes, I like”. The like-scale: love 👍👍 > like 👍 > don't like 👎.
Pron. watch
-ing endings unstressed (dan-cing, cy-cling); don't with a clean /əʊ/; rising intonation on the question ↗.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do you like tennis? — can I answer just ‘yes’?” → better: Yes, I do!
“love or like — which is stronger?” → love!

Assumptions: Personal-info Qs are warming up (L1). Free-time activities met in GMF2 — some new as -ing forms. Do you…? — Yes, I do existed receptively; production is the new challenge. Pupils can read the speech bubbles & short activity words.

Presentation · 8 activities

Listen & repeat, mime (act. 2, CD1 05). Hold up each card: Dancing! Singing! Cycling! Choral ×3 + one action each; 2–3 solo.

The like-scale. Board: three columns 👍👍 love / 👍 like / 👎 don't like. Listen again — Josh LIKES… Lily LOVES… Ravi DOESN'T LIKE… Sort the activities from CD1 04 into the columns; class calls the column each time.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if you LIKE… swimming! — flash cards fast, everyone reacts every time.

💪 Support: picture card carries the meaning. ⭐ Extension: read the activity word without the picture.

29–38′
Mime & guess
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Slow-motion mime. One player mimes an activity IN SLOW MOTION; their team asks together: Do you like cycling? Mimer answers truthfully: Yes, I do! / No, I don't. Point per correct guess; rotate the mimer every round — everyone mimes once.

Whole team asks together — no solo shouting: One voice, one team!
