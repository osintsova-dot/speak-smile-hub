WHAT WE DO: everyone performs 40–90 sec from notes, showing their best work; I'm right there as a prompter; blocks of 5 performers

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I announce in blocks of 5; the speaker comes up with their notes and portfolio.
  2. I stand half a step away: a freeze — I whisper the first word of the line; a full stop — we read it together, the finish in their own voice.
  3. After each one — applause + parents' cameras.
  4. I put the fragile ones in the middle of the blocks; the strong ones open and close the act.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Will parents hear my mistakes?” → Parents will hear their child SPEAKING ENGLISH. That's the only thing they'll remember.
“Isn't it embarrassing to hold notes?” → TV presenters read from an autocue every day. Notes are professional.
“What about the certificates?” → A separate celebration at the last meeting. Today — a show.

Assumptions: Invitations handed over; the plan and roles rehearsed at P8. Force majeure “few guests” → the audience = a neighbouring group/admin staff; we film everything for all the parents.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
A child freezes in front of their parentsI'm right there: I whisper the first letter/word of the line; if it's really stuck, we read the line TOGETHER, the finish in their own voice. The success counts, full applause.
Parents coo out loud during a talkAn MC line in the opening: voices down during talks, phones up for photos — parents fall into line instantly.
Timing creeps (this line speaks more slowly)Talks in blocks of 5; the fair is a flexible zone: it can be trimmed with no loss; a timekeeper holds a “5 min” card.
Technology failsA film held on a phone is a premiere too; a live guide as a backup genre.
A guest-parent speaks RussianThat's fine: the child answers in English from the card + gestures; parents get a mini-dictionary in the programme, Well done!/Try!
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
