📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. An honest check on the unit: vocabulary (jobs + adjectives to describe people), the past simple of irregular verbs (affirmative and negative), Did…? / Wh- did…? questions + short answers, ago and time expressions, listening, a short biography.
  • Subsidiary. Going through the task types BEFORE the test (they have met every type already); strategy and timing; feedback straight afterwards; reflecting on the growth from P1 to today.
  • Personal. ⚠️ There is NO official Macmillan pack for Unit 7 in the tests folder (the only one that is ours is Unit 1; “A2_U8_Test_*”, Progress Test 1, Mid-Year and End-of-Year belong to another course) — I work from the specification below, built from Quick review SB p.93 and WB p. 59. The tone of these two lessons: a test is a photograph, not a verdict. The posters come off the wall only for the duration of the paper.
By the end, students can…
  • recognise every task type before the test starts
  • manage test time across sections
  • check answers against the key without panic
  • name their own growth since Pair 1

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Panic before the testThe first 15 minutes are a warm run through the task types from “Look what you know! 7” and off the posters. There will be nothing new in the test, and that is said out loud.
The fast ones finish and get boredA quiet bonus task on the back: three past simple questions about any hero of the unit + an answer to each.
Tears over marksMarks privately, on the back of the paper. In class only the nominations and the growth since P1 are said out loud.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
