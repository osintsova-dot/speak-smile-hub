Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have reviewed all of Unit 4 — the ten wild animals, «What's that? / It's a… / It's got…» and the story «The surprise» — and shown it in the flagship Wild Animals Team Game with Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41) as the reward.
  • Subsidiary. «I can…» self-assessment with happy/sad faces (PB p.26); every child answers (all show at once, then teams); recycle the animals with a Photo safari & a Guessing game; sing Genki «I Like Animals» (15); Phonics Hero — /эй/ ai·ay·a-e, speed-read p.67–68; a joyful both-teams-win finish.
  • Personal. Team game = EVERY child answers (show me your card / stand up), not just the fastest. Keep the reward short & happy — it's the cherry. Both teams cheer at the end; no sore losers.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the ten wild animals from a picture or description;
  • answer «What's that? — It's a… It's got…»;
  • show what I can do with a happy/sad face (self-check).

Warm review · last time
carpet

Fast rounds, all together. ① flash all ten animals — chorus It's a…! + mime; ② describe → guess It's got long legs! — a giraffe!; ③ Photo safari — animals on the walls, Find the crocodile! It's got big teeth! children “photograph” the right one.

11–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 24 · /эй/ ai·ay·a-e — sounds the /эй/ sound: ai · ay · a-e. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Three spellings, one sound: r-ai-n → rain, d-ay → day, sn-a-ke → snake, c-a-ke → cake — read them all! ▶ Reading relay

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.67–68 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read also · of together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.67–68 · sound-action cards · Speaky

Warm-up review circuit + Guessing game
carpet

Describe & name. Genki Guessing game in pairs: one child describes an animal It's got big ears!, the partner names it It's an elephant! — swap. A playful, no-writing check where everyone talks.

Sing & move. a quick recycle of Genki «I Like Animals» (15) — mime & jump to raise the energy for the game.

 animal cards · Genki «I Like Animals» (15) · Speaky
24–47′
🏆 WILD ANIMALS TEAM GAME + Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41)
carpet, 2 teams

Flagship game. Spin the Big Wheel (Navio App on screen or a pointer on the mat) — it lands on an animal = a challenge card: name it · describe it (It's got…) · make its sound · what's it at the party (I'm a…) · what colour is it on the wheel? Card shown → Show me! EVERY child answers (show the card / do the action / say it) → most-correct team gets a point.

Reward: Genki «World Cup Soccer!»: draw a pitch on the board with a 2-D ball in the middle; for each team point, one player kicks the ball one space toward the goal — GOAL wins the round. Rotate the kicker every time.

🎮 Offline setup: Big Wheel mat + animal challenge cards on the carpet; a board pitch + cut-out ball (magnet/blu-tack) for the reward kick — one kick per point, quick. (Или мягкий мяч в обруч — basketball-вариант.)

ICQ: When do you answer? (→ on Show me! only)   Finale. count the points in English; both teams: Well done! Give me five!

✔ Assess: across the game — who names · describes · sounds · self-checks the animals? A picture of the whole unit.

 Big Wheel mat / Navio App · animal challenge cards · board pitch + cut-out ball (or soft ball + hoop)
