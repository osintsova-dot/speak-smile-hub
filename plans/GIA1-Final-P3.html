WHAT WE DO: Kagan · Inside-Outside Circle: an interview carousel on the year's question cards; I pull students one at a time into a “checkpoint corner” for a 1–2 min personal chat

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ▶ Kagan · Inside-Outside Circle: Two circles, face a partner. Inside — pick a question card, interview! Full answers plus one detail. Swap roles. Outside — rotate two!
  2. Cards — the year's questions (Key phrases U1–8): interviews don't repeat, partners change every ~2 min.
  3. In parallel — my checkpoint: I pull them one at a time into the corner, 1–2 min: 3 questions from the cards + 1 free one. I listen for SPEECH, tick boxes, praise specifically.
  4. The carousel runs without me — the rule: the circle never stops.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is the speaking check a second test?” → No: a conversation with me about your year. I don't count errors — I count sentences.
“Does everyone get a certificate?” → Everyone who's been through the year. The nomination is your own — for your strong side.
“Will the test score be said out loud?” → No: a slip handed over privately. Publicly — only the nominations.

Assumptions: The test is marked, slips with the score + 2 personal comments are ready; certificates are signed, nominations are thought through for EVERYONE (by their strong side of the year); the year's favourite games — a vote, prepared in advance.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Tears/hurt feelings over the test scoreThe score stays private + always 2 pluses alongside it (“here's what grew”); the public layer of the day is only nominations and the party.
The queue for the speaking check gets boredThe Inside-Outside Circle carousel works WITHOUT me: I pull one student at a time into the corner, the class runs its own interviews.
The party tips into chaosGames are chosen by vote from MY list (3 tried-and-tested year favourites); timing runs 8–10 min each, the one-clap signal works at the party too.
“I'll forget it all over the summer”The summer challenge is light and playful (the “English survival kit” checklist); the letter to self includes the line Don't worry, you'll remember.
Parents ask for results at the doorReady-made mini-summaries: scores + growth + a summer plan — I give them to parents as a slip/message after the lesson, not at the door.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
