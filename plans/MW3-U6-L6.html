Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to be willing to try new food (like Harry & Zoe with peas, carrots & tomatoes), understand where food comes from, and enjoy the traditional culture song «Mix a pancake» — miming mix · toss · catch · cook.
  • Subsidiary. Values — the Little Book of Values story «Harry & Zoe» (CD3 T13) + Do you want (peas)? Yes, please / No, thank you · I like / I don't like…; Content — food grows (garden → plate); Culture — «Mix a pancake» (CD3 T14) + mix · toss · catch · cook + order the pancake steps 1–5; Phonics Hero — tricky c·g·ph + air ear are (consolidation), speed-read p.86–90.
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values & culture through DOING (try a pretend pea, mime making a pancake) not talking-at. Cook safely — remind children to only cook with an adult. Every child tries a new food-mime and tosses a pretend pancake.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say I'll try new food — “Yes, please, I'll try it!”;
  • say food grows before it's on my plate;
  • mime & order making a pancake: mix · toss · catch · cook.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Harry doesn't like green food — should he try it or say «yuck»?” → try it!
“Before carrots are on my plate, where do they grow?” → in the garden / in the ground!

Assumptions: All food is secure from L1–L5; reading is on the tricky letters & /eə/. Children handle three short blocks well if each is active & bridged by a song. Do you want…? / I like… are recycled here for the values story about trying new food.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
«Try new food» too abstractMake it doable: a pretend tasting — brave face, tiny bite mime, Mmm, I like it! — praise it live.
«Yuck!» refusals take overModel kind words: No, thank you instead of «yuck»; cheer anyone who tries.
Cooking = safety worrySay it up front: we only cook with a grown-up! — everything is a mime, no real stove.
Pancake steps out of orderDo the steps as a chant with numbers 1–5: mix (1) · pour (2) · toss (3) · catch (4) · eat (5).
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning! Today we're brave — we TRY new food! And we make a pancake — mix… toss… catch… yum! Mime a pancake toss, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 31 · tricky c·g·ph + /eə/ (again) — sounds c · g · ph  ·  air · ear · are. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-read: city · gem · phone · hair · care · bear — blend the sticky ones together. ▶ Word-swat — Speaky says a word; children point / swat it on the page fast.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.86–90 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read cover · love together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Values · willingness to try food + story
carpet

Try new food. Play the Little Book of Values story «Harry & Zoe» (CD3 T13) with the pictures. Harry doesn't like green food… but he tries the peas, carrots & tomatoes! Ask Does Harry like green food? Does he try the peas? Is it delicious?

Brave tasting. a brave tasting circle — pass a pretend new food, each child takes a tiny bite-mime: Do you want peas? — Yes, please, I'll try it! Mmm — I like it!

💪 Support: do the brave bite-mime. ⭐ Extension: say I'll try it! I like it!

25–31′
Content · food grows! + Speaky's Garden ⚡
carpet

From garden to plate. Where does food come from before the plate? Carrots grow in the ground! Peas grow on a plant! Mime planting a seed, watering, growing tall, picking a carrot.

Food around the world. Speaky travels — food around the world: some children eat rice for breakfast, some have soup, some pancakes. 2 joyful examples.

 seed/plant picture cards · Speaky
31–37′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Speaky's Garden» aloud — Speaky grows carrots, tomatoes, peas & potatoes in his garden — a gentle vegetable read that lands perfectly on «try new food» and «food grows», calm after the moving content block.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Carrots, tomatoes, peas! I grow food. I try it!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each spread — children mime planting & picking each vegetable and echo I grow food. I try it!; on the last page do a brave tasting of Speaky's harvest.

 «Speaky's Garden» (Speak & Smile Genki story-book · chveg.pdf) · Speaky
37–45′
Culture · «Mix a pancake» + make a pancake
carpet, standing

Culture song. Play the traditional song «Mix a pancake» (CD3 T14). Mime it together: Mix a pancake, stir a pancake — toss it, catch it, EAT it! — mix (1) · pour (2) · toss (3) · catch (4) · eat (5)!

▶ Mix a pancake! Draw a circle in the air — Can you guess the food? It's a pancake! Let's make a pancake! Then in a chant, put the five pancake steps in order 1–5, everyone miming.

Cook safely. remind kindly: We only cook with a grown-up! — cook safely, always with a grown-up.
