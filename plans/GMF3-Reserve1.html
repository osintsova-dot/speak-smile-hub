How to use this plan (RU)

🔧 Это ШАБЛОН-конструктор на 2 резервных урока (32–33) перед каникулами. Ты не «проходишь» новую тему — ты чинишь пробелы в Units 1–4. Каркас один и тот же для обоих уроков, меняется только наполнение станций: на уроке 32 бери 2 самые слабые темы, на уроке 33 — следующие 2 (или углуби те же).

Шаг 1. Прогони быструю диагностику (этап 3–12′) — увидишь, что дети реально подзабыли. Шаг 2. Из «Меню станций» ниже выбери 3 станции под западающие темы. Шаг 3. Команда-квиз с наградой (боулинг/баскетбол) собирает всё вместе. Шаг 4. Рефлексия «что вспомнили».

Festival-опция (по желанию, из КТП): если хочешь праздничный вайб перед каникулами — вплети мини-блок Chinese New Year (красный/золотой цвет, число года, «Happy New Year!») в станцию Culture ИЛИ в награду квиза. Genki-песни сезона: Let's Decorate the Christmas Tree, Let's Build a Snowman, Happy New Year! — как бридж/заряд.

Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end of the two reserve lessons, learners will have re-consolidated the shakiest language from Units 1–4 (teacher-selected) — naming, one target structure per unit, and reading recognition — and shown it in a low-stakes Team Quiz with reward game.
  • Subsidiary. Retrieval practice (say it before you're taught it again), peer-teaching via Kagan, honest self-check — so pupils return from the holidays ready for Unit 5.
  • Personal. This is repair, not new load: keep it light and celebratory (pre-holiday). Diagnose first, teach to the gap, not to the whole book. Every child works at every station — no watching.
By the end, pupils can…
  • re-produce the target words & one key structure from each revised unit;
  • peer-teach a partner what they remember;
  • self-assess which topic still needs work before Unit 5.

Structure

Form
3rd-person -s (washes, prefers) · must + bare infinitive · quantifiers with uncountables. Error harvest from diagnosis feeds the stations & quiz items.
Pron. watch
Whatever the diagnosis surfaces — e.g. prefers /z/, washes /ɪz/, headache /k/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is this a test?” → no — it's a memory workout before the holidays; mistakes just show what to practise.
“In the quiz, who answers?” → EVERYONE, on their own board.

Assumptions: Units 1–4 all taught & tested (Lessons 4–31). Pupils know Quiz-Quiz-Trade, RallyRobin, Showdown and the traffic-light self-check from the year.

14–38′
🎯 Revision stations (3 × 8′)
3 tables + carpet

Rotation (Cooperative learning). 3 stations built from the Station menu above under your chosen topics — typically one Lexis, one Grammar, one Reading station. Split class into 3 small teams; rotate every 8′ on Freeze! Move on!

Station A — Lexis (movement). e.g. ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade with the weak unit's word cards — every child up, pairs quiz, swap cards, new partner. Kagan: StandUp-HandUp-PairUp.

Station B — Grammar (talk). e.g. ▶ RallyRobin / Talk Partners survey — take turns asking the unit question (Are you good at…? / How often do you…?) & noting answers. Kagan: RallyRobin.

Station C — Reading (calm, seated). re-read a story chunk / sentence strips from the unit → True-False or match. Kagan: RallyCoach (coach the partner).

💪 support: picture-word cards at each station · ⭐ extension: “write one more sentence” challenge card.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — tick who now produces the target structure unaided; note who still needs it in the quiz & after holidays.

 3 station kits (word cards · survey grid · sentence strips / story) · timer · class list
38–54′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 2 teams

Consolidation quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles mixing the revised units: picture→word · spelling · make the target sentence · True/False · a code-break tile. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/says on their mini-whiteboard → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a 🎳 bowling or 🏀 basketball shot (paper ball into box / on-screen animation, like the site quizzes). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Offline setup: card grid on whiteboard + real paper-ball shot into a box/basket — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on “Show me!”)   Finale: count scores in English; both teams Give me five!
