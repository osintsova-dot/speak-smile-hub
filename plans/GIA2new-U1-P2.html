🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): A long text frightens newcomers, so the way in has three steps: predicting from the pictures (safe — you cannot be wrong), skimming with one question, scanning in pairs with a line of evidence. The product is modest and reachable: 4 sentences about online school, from a frame.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Look at the pictures. A boy with a laptop. No school building anywhere. This school has no classrooms — it lives on the Internet. Guess three things about a day in this school. Then we read — and check YOUR guesses. At the end: four sentences — is online school good or bad for you?”
In simpler words, if the class needs support: A school with no building, it lives on the Internet: we guess three things about it from the pictures, then read and check our guesses; at the end — 4 sentences, «online school suits me / does not suit me».

Reading part by part
crews

WHAT WE DO: the text in 3 parts through Jigsaw: each crew prepares 2 facts from its part and teaches the class → we assemble the text together → we check the sticky notes

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I show how it is done on the opening paragraph: I read it aloud and name my two facts — that is the model answer.
  2. Then Kagan Jigsaw: Your crew owns ONE part. Find two facts. You will teach the class. Only they read their own part — which means the class cannot assemble the text without their report.
  3. Assembly: the crews report in order, and I build the timeline of the day on the board.
  4. Checking the sticky notes: Which guesses came TRUE? — we circle the ones that matched.
📺 On screen: SB pp.12–13 (the text in 3 parts) · timeline on the board
👦 Students do: they own a part and listen to the others. Expect: the assembly gives the whole picture without the fear of length

Checking understanding · True/False with evidence
pairs

WHAT WE DO: ex.5 in pairs through RallyCoach with the «finger on the line» support; we correct the false ones with the frame No — they…

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I go through the first statement aloud myself: I read it, run my finger along the line, say the answer — that is what it sounds like.
  2. Then Kagan RallyCoach: the first student answers aloud and keeps a finger on the line of evidence, the second is the coach: checks the line and praises (Yes! Good line!), and after each item they swap. True or false — finger on the proof line! No line, no answer.
  3. We check the key one by one: 1 F · 2 T · 3 T · 4 F · 5 T · 6 F — the false ones we correct chorally, using the frame.
  4. ex.6 (finish the sentences) — together on the board, the students dictate.
📺 On screen: SB p.13 ex.5–6
👦 Students do: they hunt for the lines with a finger. Expect: slow, but on their own

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can read a real article with support (predict → skim → scan), answer True/False with the line that proves it, and write 4 opinion sentences by frame.
  • Subsidiary. Subskill: prediction from the headline and the photos; Word work (5 words) with pictures; an oral comparison with our own school, from a frame.
  • Personal. I cut the text into 3 parts — one part per crew, then we put it together. The whole thing at once is frightening. Vocabulary BEFORE the reading, not during it.
By the end, students can…
  • predict article content from pictures
  • find an answer and point at the proof line
  • match 5 new words to definitions
  • write 4 sentences about online school with a frame

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«log on» — do you connect to the network or leave it? → you connect, you start working.
Your prediction was wrong — is that bad? → no! A prediction exists precisely so that you can check it.
grades — is it the class or the marks? → the marks (5, 4, A, B…).

Assumptions: SB pp.12–13, KTP pair 6. They read more slowly than «ours» — the text goes part by part, and the tasks come with frames.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The length of the text scares themThe text is cut into 3 parts, one per crew; each crew owns its own part and tells the others about it.
New words stall the readingWord work BEFORE the text, with pictures; a mini-glossary in the margin.
«I don't know what to think» at the product stageAn opinion frame: I think… because… + a bank of reasons on the slide.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
