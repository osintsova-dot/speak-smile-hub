Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 free-time activities and ask & answer with How often…?: “How often does (your mum) (watch DVDs)? — She (watches DVDs) (twice a week).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Free time» (CD1 37); consolidate the he/she -s from L2 in a new set of verbs; discover the frequency phrases every day · twice a week · once a week · never (Grammar clue); a real free-time survey about the family (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on the -s in the answer (watches, does); model the frequency phrases with finger-counts (once = 1, twice = 2); keep the survey moving — max 30 sec per partner.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 free-time activities;
  • ask “How often does …?” with does;
  • answer with a frequency phrase: “She watches DVDs twice a week.”

Form
How often + does + subject + verb…? Answer: subject + verb + -s + frequency phrase: She watches TV twice a week. / My mum never watches TV.
Pron. watch
How often /ˈhaʊ ˈɒfn/ (silent t in often OK); watches /ɪz/; twice /twaɪs/; puzzle /ˈpʌzl/; comics /ˈkɒmɪks/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“How often does she watch DVDs? — do I use do or does?” → does (he/she!).
“twice a week — how many times?” → two times.

Assumptions: The he/she -s and does-support are fresh from L2; chores are active. The 6 free-time activities are largely NEW; the switch to How often…? + frequency phrases is today's stretch. Readers can read short activity phrases.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
do instead of does (“How often do she…?”)Rule card: he/she/it → does; contrast-drill do (I/you) vs does (he/she) side by side.
-s dropped in the answerMagnet -s jumps onto the verb; beat drill: she — watch — ES.
Frequency phrases muddled (once/twice)Finger-count: once = 1 finger, twice = 2; sort activities onto a “how often” line.
Activities mispronounced (puzzles/comics)Clap the syllables; one action per activity (do puzzles = fit a piece, read comics = open a comic).
Survey chaosMusic on = mingle, off = pair; 30-sec cap; ICQ before starting.

27–37′
Free-time survey
mingle → desks

Model first. Ask 2 pupils publicly: How often do you play computer games? — Twice a week!

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade survey. Each child holds an activity card + a Me/Friend grid. Music → mingle; off → pair: A How often do you watch DVDs? B answers truthfully Every day / Never. Tick the grid, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges.

Harvest. Who plays board games every day? Find someone who reads comics as often as you!

✔ Assess: circulate — is it “does” + verb + -s in the he/she answers? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: card shows the full question text. ⭐ Extension: report a friend: She watches DVDs every day.

📓 Interactive notebook — GMF3_Unit2_Lesson4_How_Often_A5
A5 glue-in note — 6 free-time activities (watch DVDs · play computer games · read comics · draw pictures · play board games · do puzzles) + BUILD THE QUESTION strip (HOW OFTEN + DOES + person + base verb) + a frequency scale never → once a week → twice a week → three times a week → every day
  • Pupils glue the note in and mark their own place on the frequency scale for each of the 6 activities.
  • Class reads the BUILD THE QUESTION strip as one chunk: HOW OFTEN — DOES — YOUR SISTER — DO PUZZLES?
  • Teams build questions and answers off the note; a point only if the action verb stayed in the base form after does.
  • Everyone circles the -s on DOES and crosses out any -s they were tempted to add to the verb.
