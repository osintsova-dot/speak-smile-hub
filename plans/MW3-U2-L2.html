Week 05.10–11.10 · Lesson 10✨ +dance · jump · run · think · What do you do at school?~8 pupils · circle + pairsPH 13 (ai oa ie · p.33–35) · Genki: Superhero I Can(05) · games: Chocolate Monster(32)·Steal the Bacon(19)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to ask & answer about school: “What do you do at school? — I (draw) at school”, using ten activities (adding dance · jump · run · think).
  • Subsidiary. Sing the coursebook song «What do you do at school?» (CD1 T25) + Genki «I'm A Superhero! I Can…» (05) for the action verbs; ask-and-say with the puppet; Phonics Hero — ai · oa · ie (consolidation), speed-read p.33–35; listen & find the new activities (PB p.6).
  • Personal. Model the question with the puppet many times before children ask it. Keep ask-and-say short & paired — no long waiting. Praise every attempt at the full answer.
By the end, pupils can…
  • do & name four new activities (dance · jump · run · think);
  • ask “What do you do at school?”;
  • answer “I (draw) at school.”

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello, school stars! At school I draw… I count… and now — I JUMP! Can you jump? Jump, run, dance, freeze.

carpet

▶ Activity flash. flash the six L1 activity cards — chorus + do the action; quick wheel: Where's play? — It's on green!

Do & say. point to a card → class does the action & names it — warms the activities before we add four new ones.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 13 (consolidation) — sounds ai · oa · ie (letter-teams). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build faster: t-ai-l → tail, s-oa-p → soap, t-ie → tie — sweep the team + a picture. ▶ Team relay — Speaky shows a word; teams blend it and race to touch its picture.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.33–35 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read little · down together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.33–35 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–26′
Curiosity input · four new activities + the question
carpet

Which are new? Lay all ten activity cards on the floor. Ooh — a NEW one! Watch… I RUN! Run, run, run! Reveal the four new ones with a HUGE action: dance (wiggle) · jump (jump) · run (jog) · think (finger to head). Whole class copies.

Ask the puppet. Give the puppet a card: What do you do at school? — I dance at school! Model the answer, then children ask the puppet in chorus; swap cards fast.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): play the unit videos, songs & the Big Wheel game on screen — the story «A new friend», the Katy Kangaroo song and Go in and out the window come from the coursebook app (Navio); our Genki songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: do the action + say the verb. ⭐ Extension: say the whole I (run) at school.

26–31′
Game · Chocolate Monster (32)
carpet

▶ Chocolate Monster Genki Chocolate Monster: activity cards on the board. A child picks one in their head and the class asks together What do you do at school? — the child does the action, everyone guesses the verb. Everyone asks every round.

Calm & seated; the whole class chants the question — lots of talking, no waiting.

 activity flashcards on board · Speaky
31–37′
Game · Steal the Bacon (19) → Grab the activity! ⚡
floor space

▶ Grab the activity! Genki Steal the Bacon, re-skinned: two teams line up opposite; activity cards in the middle. The class asks What do you do at school? — 🦝 Speaky calls a verb; one runner from each team races to grab it and does the action: I jump at school!

Every verb called → everyone runs & answers at least once. Slow-mo + Freeze keep it safe.

💪 Support: run & do the action, copy I (jump). ⭐ Extension: add at school: I jump at school!

45–54′
Table time · Listen & find (PB p.6) 🧘

Listen & find. Play CD1 T24 — children listen and put a finger on the four NEW activities as they hear them (run · dance · jump · think).

Point, match, answer. Play the song again; children point to the activities, then match each to Alex or Lucy; answer What do you do at school?

In pairs: A asks, B answers & does the action, then swap.
