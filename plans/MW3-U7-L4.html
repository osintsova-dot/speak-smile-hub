Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act the story song «Dinosaurs in space» with full actions, and say what they want to do: “I want to play with dinosaur toys. I want to learn about space.”
  • Subsidiary. Re-tell the story & order the story cards; play Dino says! (Simon Says) & Ball & Music (16); sing Genki «What Do You Want To Do?» (59); match Mimi & Dylan to what they want to do & draw something they want to learn about (PB p.48); Phonics Hero — Magic E (a-e i-e o-e u-e) consolidation, speed-read p.58–63.
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions (dino arms, rocket arms), then words. Repeat several times without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (whisper, super-fast, giant voice). After the big acting, use the calm breathing reset before the table.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing «Dinosaurs in space» with the dino & rocket actions;
  • say “I want to play with dinosaur toys!”;
  • say “I want to learn about (space / animals).”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I want to LEARN about dinosaurs — do I read & find out, or run away?” → read & find out!
“Dino toys — do I play with them or eat them?” → play with them!

Assumptions: Story met in L3 — today deepens it through the song. Children know the ten places & the Simon-Says format from earlier units. Reading continues on Magic E. Pre-readers follow the song by pictures & actions, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by repetition of the songChange the mode each time: whisper, super-fast, slow-motion, giant voice.
Only a few singGive everyone an action job (dino arms / rocket arms); sing the actions even without the words.
Dino says — same few winMistake = help, not out; keep everyone standing and re-joining so all mime.
Over-excited after actingUse the breathing reset — breathe in… breathe out… four times before the table.
Says the toy, not the sentencePuppet models I want to play with… DINOSAUR TOYS!; child copies with the mime.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Dinosaurs… in SPACE! Stomp like a dinosaur… now blast off like a rocket! What do you want to do today? Dino stomp + rocket blast-off.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lessons 21–22 · Magic E — sounds a-e · i-e · o-e · u-e  (silent e = long vowel). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: n-o-se → nose, t-u-be → tube, k-i-te → kite, ca-ke → cake — silent-e tap + sweep + a picture. ▶ Speed-read race — beat Speaky's clap-clock reading the Magic-E row.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.58–63 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read have · give together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

carpet

Game · Dino says! (Simon Says) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Dino says! Say Dino says… learn about space! children do the dino stomp. Say just Play with dino toys (no “Dino says”) — they stand still, hands on heads. Mistake = a friendly wobble, keep going. Practise all the wants.

31–37′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Where Do You Live?» aloud — A calm places read to settle after Dino says — Speaky's map of town, a gentle recall of where we can go and what we want to do.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Where do you want to go? I want to go here!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Children point to Speaky's map and say I want to go to the…! — a quiet recall of the day's language.

44–54′
Table time · Pupil's Book p.48 🧘
tables

Listen, point & sing. Play the song; children point to the pictures & sing along (karaoke version when confident).

Match & say. children match Mimi & Dylan to what they want to do, then point & say Mimi wants to learn about dinosaurs. Dylan wants to learn about space.

Draw & say. children draw one thing THEY want to learn about, then tell a partner I want to learn about… dogs!

Calm down. a slow breathing reset — Breathe in… breathe out… four times — before we finish.
