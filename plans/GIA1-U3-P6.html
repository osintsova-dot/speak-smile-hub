Step 4: presentations against the bar
🏁 PRESENT + peer review
stage · pairs×pairs

WHAT WE DO: Timed Pair Share: pairs present to pairs (60 sec), peer review against the book's 2 questions; the 2 best maps — for the class

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Pairs×pairs format (TB): Present your map in sixty seconds — expand the words into sentences. Listeners, two questions: ① Are the food and place right for the people? ② Would YOU like to go? Why? (ex.8)
  2. The format is Kagan Timed Pair Share: A speaks for their time on the timer, B is the active listener (counts pauses on fingers, catches the best phrase), then Switch roles! The listener's response starts with My favourite bit was…
  3. Round 1 — one pair to the other, round 2 — the other way round. Listeners answer the questions.
  4. 2 maps chosen by applause — for the class (⭐ TB Challenge), the full rubric applies.
  5. Points to the leaderboard; a language checkpoint: heard there is/are + a quantifier in the presentation = +1.
📺 On screen: stopwatch · peer review questions on the board · leaderboard
👦 Students do: they present to each other, unpacking words into sentences, answer 2 questions, the best go up in front of the class. Expected: everyone talks at once in the rounds — everyone's engaged. Typical error: reading the words off the map without sentences — the gesture “expand!”
🏆 The bar for the product (the judges' rubric, every pair): Fluency — pauses no longer than 3 sec (1–3) · Accuracy — clean forms (1–3) · Richness — quantifiers, connectors, bonus structures (1–3). 9/9 = a “gold run”, +5 to the leaderboard.		stopwatch · rubric cards

Unit 3 test — specification, COMPACT, ~30 min (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (10 pts)A) 6 items: food & drink from definitions (You can make this from oranges → juice), spelling counts — silent letters watched (biscuit, sandwich). B) 4 items: where do you buy it? (bread → baker's; medicine → chemist's).
Part 2 · Grammar (14 pts)A) 4 gaps a/an/some (I've got ___ apple and ___ water). B) 4 gaps some/any/a lot of (We haven't got ___ tomatoes.). C) 6 items there is/are: 2 gaps aff/neg + 2 make a question from words + 2 correct the mistake (There're two cafés. → There are. · Yes, there's. → Yes, there is.) — mirrors TB Common errors.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A short description of a shopping centre (script in the plan; teacher-read twice if no track): 4 true/false + 4 notes to complete (types of shops / places to eat / other things) — mirrors SB p.41 ex.6.
Part 4 · Writing (8 pts)5–6 sentences: food in your fridge / your ideal lunchbox. Must use: some/any/a lot of (3) · there is/are (2) · one and + one but (2) · task complete (1) — mirrors blog CHECK list SB p.45.

Total 40 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.45 — every format was met in class during the unit. The test shares the pair with the project, so it is compact: 4 parts, no padding.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
