Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer about clothes — “What are you wearing? — I'm wearing (shorts) and a (T-shirt).” (Pupil's Book p. 83) — and act out a short camp dialogue with weather: “Is it cold? — No, it isn't. It's hot today.”, “Hurry up! — I'm coming.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — the 10 clothes (L1), colours (Genki), plural items — woven into the wearing frame (“I'm wearing a blue T-shirt and red shorts!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.28: /ю:/ ue·ew·u-e (three spellings, one sound: blue, new, cube; тетрадь стр.77–78); listen for detail (PB p. 83 “Listen and match”).
  • Personal. Insist on the FULL frame (“I'm wearing a T-shirt”, not “T-shirt”); model both the question & answer equally; every task keeps ALL children responding (own clothes / thumbs / all pairs at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask & answer “What are you wearing? — I'm wearing (shorts) and a (T-shirt).”;
  • use plural items correctly (shorts / trousers / trainers, no “a”);
  • say “Is it hot/cold? — It's hot today.” in a camp dialogue;
  • read /ю:/ words spelt ue·ew·u-e (blue, new, cube).

Structure
What are you wearing? — I'm wearing (shorts) and a (T-shirt).  ·  Is it cold? — No, it isn't. It's hot today.  ·  Hurry up! — I'm coming.
Form
I'm wearing = I am wearing. and joins two items: a T-shirt and shorts. Plural items take no “a”.
Pron. watch
contraction “I'm” /aɪm/ (not “I am”); linking “wearing a” /ˈweərɪŋə/; keep the -s on plural items.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.28: /ю:/ ue·ew·u-e — three spellings, ONE sound (blue, glue · new, few · cube, tune) — read, sort, write (тетрадь стр.77–78). BLUE is a colour for our clothes!
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Point to a child: What are you wearing? → “I'm wearing a T-shirt and shorts!” (two items with and).
Hot picture: Is it cold? → “No, it isn't. It's hot today!” (fanning gesture).

Assumptions: the 10 clothes & “What's this? / I'm wearing…” met in L1; colours from Genki; plural items introduced in L1. /ю:/ spellings met in Часть 4; ур.28 today. Emergent readers → picture grids & audio, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
One-word answers: “T-shirt” instead of the frameBack-chain: …a T-shirt → wearing a T-shirt → I'm wearing a T-shirt.
“a shorts / a trousers”Two-legs gesture: “these are TWO — no ‘a’”; drill plural items.
Forgets to join with andLink two clothes cards with a chain-link gesture: a T-shirt AND shorts.
Digraph read as two letters: ue = /u-e/“Two letters — ONE sound”: /ю:/; palm 🖐 for the long name; blue, new.
Dialogue too fast / mumbled weatherModel with Speaky first; big fanning (hot) / shivering (cold) gestures; drill the exchange slowly.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = interactive (projector) 💪 support · ⭐ extension […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Cognitive load: only the frame I'm wearing … and … + plural items are truly NEW — the 10 clothes & colours are recycled, so let the frame carry the challenge (scaffold: back-chain, chain-link gesture). Phonics ур.28 is a brisk read + sort. Buffers to drop first if short: the 2nd tic-tac-toe grid, the 3rd mingle swap. Better every child says one full I'm wearing a T-shirt and shorts well than six stages half-done.
