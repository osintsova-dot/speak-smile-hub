Rehearsal: four targets
Rehearsal · four targets
desks · teams

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: a booster shot right before the test: four forms that will definitely come up — we drill them in chorus and on mini-boards

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Target 1: In 1910, children ___ (not go) to school by bus. → didn't go. The “ate it” gesture, chorus ×2.
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYBODY writes at the same time, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift them all together — I see everyone's answer, nobody can sit it out.
  3. Target 2: build a question from the strip-diagram (did / you / come / how / today ?) → How did you come today?
  4. Target 3 from the traffic light: big → the biggest 🟢 · good → the best ⭐ · expensive → the most expensive 🔴 · slow → the slowest 🟢.
  5. Target 4: Before I had breakfast, I had a shower — what happened first? → the shower (we look at the timeline).
  6. These four are half the test. You just did all four — together.
📺 On screen: mini-boards · the board: the anchor, the strip-diagrams, the traffic light, the timeline — all up
👦 Students do: they drill the four targets in one volley with the supports on the board. Expected: anxiety drops, hands get more confident. Typical error: “didn't went” — chorus three times, calmly.

Test strategy
Strategy · how to write the test
desks

WHAT WE DO: strategy from examples + an opening ritual: seating, phones, papers face down

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. One example from each part (the specification at the bottom of the plan): Part 1 — the transport words. Part 2 — didn't + infinitive, Did-questions, superlatives. Part 3 — read the three summaries FIRST. Part 4 — a short story with before or after.
  2. Rule: Stuck? Leave it, go on, come back. An empty gap is zero; a guess can be right.
  3. Ritual: seating, phones in the box, papers face down. Questions about the task — yes. Questions about English — after the test.
📺 On screen: example slides for the parts · papers face down (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they listen to the strategy, recognise all the formats. Expected: “this is just like the notebook” — yes, literally.

WHAT WE DO: the audio part by a familiar protocol: first we read the options, then two listens

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

53–63′
Reviewing together: fixing typical errors
Review together · fixing typical errors
board · whole class

WHAT WE DO: a calm review: I write typical errors on the board (anonymously) and the class fixes them, everybody in their notebook

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. While collecting the papers — I noticed 5 typical spots. I write them on the board with NO names.
  2. We fix them one by one, as a whole class: ‘She didn't went’ — what's wrong? Right: didn't eats the past. She didn't GO. Everybody writes the corrected version down for themselves.
  3. I make sure to praise what most people got right: Almost everybody got the transport words. Look how far you came from Pair 1.
  4. Everybody writes themselves two lines: “my two mistakes that I'm fixing”.
📺 On screen: the board: 5 typical errors · notebooks
👦 Students do: they fix the errors together, write down the correction and their own two lines. Expected: calm, no shame — the errors are “ours”, not somebody's. Typical error: they get upset — I switch to “look what you can already do”.

73–83′
Unit finale: the best way to travel
Verdict · What's the best way to travel?
circle · pairs

WHAT WE DO: Timed Pair Share: an oral finale of the unit from a frame: your own opinion with a superlative and a reason; a class vote

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. A frame on the board: I think the ___ is the best way to travel in our city, because it's the ___ est / the most ___ .
  2. Format — Kagan Timed Pair Share: A talks for their time on the timer, B is an active listener (counts pauses on their fingers, catches the best phrase), then Switch roles! The listener's response starts with My favourite bit was…
  3. Pairs, 3 rotations of 60 sec — everybody talks at the same time, the partner listens and nods.
  4. Then a circle: everybody says ONE sentence from the frame (the quiet ones can hold the support in their hand).
  5. A vote with your feet: Bus? Car? Underground? Walking? Go to your corner! We count and write the class verdict on the unit poster.
📺 On screen: the board: the opinion frame · the corners of the room labelled
👦 Students do: they speak from the frame in pairs and in a circle, then vote with their feet. Expected: everybody has their say — the frame guarantees it. Typical error: they skip because — a finger on the frame.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners have written the Unit 8 test (standard) — transport lexis, past simple negatives and questions, superlatives, a short story — and have gone through their own errors calmly, naming two things to fix.
  • Subsidiary. A rehearsal of four targets before the test; a strategy from examples; the audio part by a familiar protocol; a calm review at the board (not a competition); a soft cumulative rewind of U5–U8 as a game; the unit's WDYT? closes from a frame; setting the mood for Unit 9.
  • Personal. The test pair for 🐣 is about safety: a familiar format, a rehearsal right before, a review with no comparing children to each other. We do NOT use competitive games today — only cooperative ones.

Unit 8 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 8 items: types of transport from definitions/pictures (It takes people to and from hospital → ambulance; It's the system of trains under a city → the underground) — mirrors SB p.97 ex.3. B) 6 items: collocations — ___ a ticket (buy), cross ___ (the road), get on/___ a bus (off), go on ___ (foot), ___ a bike (ride), wait in a ___ (queue) — mirrors SB p.101 ex.3.
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 6 gaps: past simple negative, didn't + INFINITIVE (the “didn't went” trap: In 1910, children ___ (not go) to school by bus). B) 6 items: order the words → a Did-question and a Wh-question + a true short answer (you / did / TV / watch / yesterday ?) — mirrors SB p.100 ex.5. C) 6 items: superlatives with spelling (big → the biggest; happy → the happiest; expensive → the most expensive; good → the best; bad → the worst; slow → the slowest — do NOT double -w).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A radio item about school transport week (in the style of SB p.101 audio 56; the teacher reads it twice if there’s no track): 2 pts — choose the best summary (a/b/c) + 6 gaps with information — mirrors SB p.101 ex.6–7.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A story: “My best or worst journey” (60+ words — a shortened format of the SB p.104 competition). Marked for: past simple forms incl. negatives (4) · before/after show the order of events, comma in place (3) · task complete + the story is interesting, paragraphs (3) — mirrors SB p.105 Skills boost.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
