Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to say what they like and ask & answer about food: “I like / I love / I don't like (pizza).” and “Do you like (soup)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” (Pupil's Book p. 41 dialogue), running a food survey.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — L1 foods + like/don't like, Genki vegetables — woven into today's question game (“Do you like carrots? Yes, I do!”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.24 (a) первое знакомство: /эй/ spelled ai · ay · a-e (rain, train, play, day, cake: meet → blend → «домики /эй/», тетрадь стр.67); listen for detail (PB p. 41 ex. 2).
  • Personal. Insist on the FULL question & the short answer (“Do you like soup?” → “Yes, I do”, not just “yes”); model both answers equally; every task keeps ALL children responding (own cards / thumbs / all pairs at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • say “I like / I love / I don't like (food)”;
  • ask “Do you like (pizza)?” and answer “Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”;
  • survey friends & report a favourite;
  • blend & read the first /эй/ words (rain, train, play, day, cake).

Structure
I like / I love / I don't like (pizza).  ·  Do you like (soup)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  ·  What about you?
Form
Do + you + like + noun? → short answers Yes, I do. / No, I don't (not “Yes, I like”). Rising tone ↗ on the question, falling ↘ on the answer.
Pron. watch
keep do / don't in the answer; don't shrink the question to “like soup?”; link “Do you” /djʊ/.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.24 (a) первое знакомство: /эй/ = ai (rain, train) · ay (play, day) · a-e (cake) — «домики /эй/» (тетрадь стр.67) · sight words: Повт. Red — the Red tricky-word set (the · a · I · is · go · no · she · we).

board → desks

Speed reading — тетрадь стр.67 №1. Vowel names + Magic E strip: There and back, beat your time!

Meet /эй/ — стр.67 №2 «Домики». 🦝 Speaky: One sound — /эй/ — has THREE houses! ai — rain · train! · ay — play · day! · a-e — cake! (we know this one). All say each + a wave gesture Hey! /эй/ ×3. Check: hold up train — Which house — ai, ay or a-e? → children point to the house.

Blend — Hub Station 3 / стр.67 №3 (all together). Sound windows: /r/ /ai/ /n/… rain! /p/ /l/ /ay/… play! Then train, day, cake — picture for meaning; every child slides a “blend finger”.

Sort into houses — стр.67 №4 (desks, settle). Put each word in its house: rain · train → ai; play · day → ay; cake → a-e. Whisper each as you write. (Dictation & the /эй/ reading strip come next lesson — today is first meeting.)

Sight words — Повт. Red. Fast re-read of the Red tricky-word strip (the · a · I · is · go · no · she · we): there and back, beat the clock.
