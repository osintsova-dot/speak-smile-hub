  1. До урока развешиваю 4 увеличенных текста по углам класса.

Workbook U1 · notebook
📋 Справка для подготовки (читать ДО урока, не в классе)
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can find facts in four short informational texts and report them with simple be sentences: “Amsterdam is the capital. 25% of the country is under the sea!”
  • Subsidiary. Reading subskill: identifying different types of text; Word work — 6 text words; first Critical thinking ritual (Evaluate: order 8 ‘happiness’ words) — with heavy scaffolds.
  • Personal. Never read the page aloud frontally — the quest does the reading. Timer visible in every hunt. Praise fact-finding speed, not translation.
By the end, students can…
  • say what type a text is by its look
  • find a named fact in a text within a time limit
  • match 6 new words to definitions
  • rank ideas and say “№1 is family” with a reason word

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A fact file — full sentences or words and numbers?” → words and numbers.
“Diet — only when you want to be thin?” → No, diet = the food people eat.
“Currency — money or a shop?” → the money a country uses.

Assumptions: 13 nationalities from P1 are fresh (opening rewind retrieves them). Reading long texts in English is NEW — success = find facts, not understand every word. Say this out loud to lower anxiety.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Panic at a wall of English textRule on slide: “You do NOT need every word. You need THE fact.” Model one hunt yourself, thinking aloud.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
