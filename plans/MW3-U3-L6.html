Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to look after the park (put rubbish in the bin), say how a plant grows (seed → roots → stem → flower), and enjoy a traditional story set in the woods — “Little Red Riding Hood” (CD1 T48).
  • Subsidiary. Values — looking after the environment + song «Put your rubbish in the bin» (CD1 T47, PB p.59); Content — plants (seed · roots · stem · flower) + Genki «How Many?» (30); Culture — the traditional story «Little Red Riding Hood» (CD1 T48, PB p.60); Phonics Hero — Magic E (consolidation), speed-read p.53–54.
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values & content through DOING (pick up litter, grow like a seed) not talking-at. Every child does the plant mime & the litter action.
By the end, pupils can…
  • put your rubbish in the bin (look after nature);
  • show how a plant grows — seed → roots → stem → flower;
  • join in a traditional story set in the woods.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Looking after nature” too abstractMake it doable: a pretend-litter pick-up round the carpet into a real bin — praise it live.
Plant sequence forgottenGrow it with bodies: curl up (seed) → wiggle down (roots) → stand tall (stem) → open arms (flower).
Culture block = just listeningEveryone joins the story — walk the path, knock-knock, big-bad-wolf actions.
Reading too hard for someBlend/read together; success = joining in, not solo reading.
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello, nature helpers! Today — keep the park clean, grow a plant, and a story in the woods! Grow-from-a-seed stretch, then dance.

carpet

 park cards · clean/messy park picture · Speaky
9–16′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 19 · Magic E (again) — sounds silent e: short → long vowel. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-read the Magic-E words — a bit faster today: kite · bike · cake · rose · nose; blend the sticky ones together. ▶ Word-swat — Speaky says a word; children point / swat it on the page fast.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.53–54 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read more · before together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

16–24′
Values · put your rubbish in the bin + song (CD1 T47)
carpet

Clean the park! Scatter a few pretend-litter cards on the carpet: Oh no — rubbish in the park! children pick one up and Put your rubbish in the bin! put it in the bin — you narrate & praise: Wow, you helped! The park is clean!

Values song. Sing «Put your rubbish in the bin» (CD1 T47) with the bin action — where do YOU put rubbish? in the park / classroom / playground.

💪 Support: do the bin action. ⭐ Extension: say I put my rubbish in the bin!

 pretend-litter cards · a bin · «Put your rubbish in the bin» (CD1 T47)
24–32′
Content · how a plant grows + Genki «How Many?» ⚡
carpet, standing

Grow a plant! Grow a plant with the whole body: A tiny seed… roots… a stem… and a FLOWER! Curl up tiny (seed) → wiggle fingers down (roots) → stand tall (stem) → open arms wide (flower)! Do it 2–3× faster.

Count the flowers. Sing Genki «How Many?» (30) — count the flowers in the park: How many flowers? — There are flowers!

✔ Assess: who can name seed → roots → stem → flower in order? Celebrate, don't test.

 plant-grow picture cards (seed · roots · stem · flower) · Genki «How Many?» (30)
32–41′
Culture · «Little Red Riding Hood» (CD1 T48)
carpet

A woodland story. Show the story pictures — a walk through the woods with trees, flowers, a path… and a wolf! What's in the wood? Trees? Flowers? A path? Who's this — a WOLF?! (TB tip: ask what the characters are called in Russian.) Take 2 guesses.

▶ Act the story. Play the story (CD1 T48). Children join with actions: walk the path, pick flowers, knock-knock, big-bad-wolf paws; point & say There are trees! There are flowers!

 «Little Red Riding Hood» story cards · «Little Red Riding Hood» (CD1 T48) · Speaky
41–53′
Table time · look after nature page 🧘
tables

Draw & colour. Children draw a clean, happy park (trees, flowers, grass, a bin) and colour it.

Show a friend. to a partner: Look! There are trees! There are flowers! Put your rubbish in the bin! — nature words in a full sentence.

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘

▶ Turn & tell: Do one thing to look after nature and tell your partner. — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can look after the park! I know how a plant grows! colour one star.

You are all such good nature helpers! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
