Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. To assess what learners can do with Unit 1 language: sports & ball actions vocabulary and the structures I play / I go · Do you…? — Yes, I do / No, I don't · He/She plays / throws — across listening, reading and writing.
  • Subsidiary. A short oral pairing check (each child matches English ↔ Russian / picture ↔ word aloud, 1:1); calm test-taking behaviour; self-checking one's own paper before handing in.
  • Personal. Keep the room calm and low-stakes; model one item of every section before pupils start; never announce marks; reassure — “you know all these words, we played them all week.”
By the end, the teacher can…
  • see each child recognise & write Unit 1 sports words;
  • see each child use I play / I go · Do you…? in writing;
  • hear each child match & say Unit 1 pairs (EN↔RU / pic↔word).

Test parts
① Listen & number ② Listen & match ③ Read & match ④ Write (Play/Go) ⑤ Read & write (Do you…?) + Speaking (oral pairs).
Instruction checks (ICQs) · Assumptions
Ex 1 — “Do you write a word or a number in the circle?” → a number.
Ex 4 — “Where does swimming go — Play or Go?” → Go.
Ex 5 — “A tick ✓ in the picture — do you write Yes, I do or No, I don't?” → Yes, I do.

Assumptions: pupils met all of this across L1–L6 (song, chant, stickers, story, review) — the test holds no new language. GMF2 are early readers → they can read short test items, but the teacher reads every rubric aloud + models one item so reading load never hides sports knowledge. Validity note: keep the task measuring sports language, not test-taking or handwriting — model first, read rubrics, mark meaning not neatness. Groups: individual work; oral pairs done 1:1 or in 2s while the rest self-check quietly.

Classroom management — signals (test day)
“Test voice” 🤫 — whisper only; point to ear = “quiet, please.”
“WiFi” 🖐 — raise a spread hand → pupils stop & look for the next instruction.
Volume down — lower your arm slowly = settle back to silence.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · pencil · paper · quiet.
Countdown — “Two more minutes on this page… pencils ready.”
Hand up 🙋 — a question? Raise your hand, I'll come to you (no calling out).

0–5′
Calm settle & recall
desks

Settle. Greet quietly, pupils sit at spaced desks. 🦝 Speaky gives a calm thumbs-up: Today we show what we know. Take a big breath… you know all these sports!

Warm recall (no pressure, choral). Flash 5–6 sports cards: What sport is this? Class answers together: It's football! I play football. I go swimming. Then 2–3 action mimes: Throw… catch… kick. This wakes the words up — it is not graded.

 sports flashcards · Speaky · calm music (low)
5–10′
Test walkthrough · model-first
board → desks

Hand out the test. Write your name at the top. Don't start yet — let's look together.

Model ONE item of each section on the board (self-efficacy: they see it is easy): Ex 1 — Listen… football is number 1. We write 1 in the circle. Ex 4 — Football goes under “Play”. Swimming goes under “Go”. Ex 5 — Do you play basketball? — Yes, I do.

Rules, kindly. Your own paper. Hand up if you need me. Whisper voice. Take your time. ICQ: Can we help our neighbour? (→ No.) Can I put my hand up? (→ Yes!)

📝 Listening
desks

Ex 1 · Listen and number (Track 04). Look at the six sports. Listen and write the number in the circle. Play twice: first to listen, second to check. Sports pictured: rollerblading · football (example = 1) · skateboarding · hockey · baseball · swimming.

Ex 2 · Listen and match (Track 05). Now — days of the week and sports. Listen and draw a line, like Monday → swimming. Play twice. Pictures: volleyball · basketball · skateboarding · cycling · tennis · swimming.

Between plays: Pencils down, just listen the first time. Now check the second time. Calm countdown into the next page.

📝 Reading & Writing
desks

Ex 5 · Read and write (Do you…?). Look at the picture. Answer: Yes, I do — or — No, I don't. Model 1: Do you play basketball? — Yes, I do. Six items (basketball · hockey · skateboarding · cycling · tennis · hockey).

When you finish, check your own paper — read every answer once more. Early finishers get the calm sports colouring picture (test scene, bottom of p.3). Then move quietly into the oral pairs.

 test p.3 · sports colouring sheet
44–56′
📝 Speaking · oral pairs
quiet corner + desks

Set the room. The class self-checks quietly / colours; you call pupils to the quiet corner one at a time (or in twos). Whisper voice everywhere.

The task — child makes the PAIRS aloud. Spread a small set of Unit 1 pair cards: English ↔ Russian (football ↔ футбол, swimming ↔ плавание, throw ↔ бросать…) and/or picture ↔ word. Let's do one together: football… футбол. Now you — match and say. The child slides each pair together and says it: Basketball — баскетбол. I play basketball. GMF2 read the pair aloud too.

Warm feedback, no scores. Nod, smile, quiet Well done — thank you! Note the tick sheet privately; never say a number out loud. If a child hesitates, offer the first sound / do one more together (mastery, not failure).
