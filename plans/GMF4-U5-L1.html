A2 young learners (~10 y.o., readers · continuing line) · 60 min · PPP + TPR + Talk Partners · carpet + desks
Week 11.01–17.01 · Lesson 34✨ NEW · 12 free-time activities~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.50 · AB p.48
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 free-time activities and suggest / ask about them: “What shall we do today? — Let's go bowling! / Let's have a barbecue!” · “Do you go bowling? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «What shall we do today?» (CD2 34) & the vocabulary chant (CD2 33); brainstorm free-time activities and say what you did last weekend (past-simple recycle); notice the verb + activity collocations (GO · HAVE · PLAY · RIDE · MAKE); early literacy — find activity words in a word-search and sort them by verb (AB).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — the mime game is high-energy, plan the freezes. Insist on the RIGHT verb (go / have / play / ride / make) — that's today's real challenge, not the nouns.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 free-time activities;
  • use the right verb: go / have / play / ride / make + activity;
  • suggest with “Let's…” and ask “Do you…? — Yes, I do / No, I don't.”

Structure
What shall we do today? — Let's go bowling! · Let's have a barbecue!  ·  Do you go bowling? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  ·  preview (L2): If it's sunny, we can…
Form
GO + -ing / place (sailing, bowling, hiking, windsurfing, horse riding, shopping) · HAVE + a/an (a picnic, a barbecue, an ice lolly) · PLAY (table tennis) · RIDE a (mountain bike) · MAKE a (cake). Suggest: Let's + base verb.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“bowling — go bowling or play bowling?” → GO bowling.
“a picnic — go, play or have a picnic?” → HAVE a picnic.

Assumptions: Pupils know some activities from GMF3 free-time/sports; the 12 collocations are largely NEW and the verb choice (go/have/play/ride/make) is the stretch. Conditional-with-can comes in L2. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures. Past simple (“What did you do…?”) is active from Unit 3.

10–20′
Presentation · 12 activities
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD2 33). Each card: What's this? Go bowling! Have a picnic! Ride a mountain bike! Choral ×3 + one gesture (sailing = row, bowling = roll, barbecue = flip).

Sort by verb. Board: five columns — GO · HAVE · PLAY · RIDE · MAKE. Hold each card — class calls the verb, then says the whole thing: Let's go bowling! / Let's have a barbecue!

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if this is a GO activity… go hiking! — flash cards fast, everyone reacts every time (trick them with HAVE / RIDE).

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + activity. ⭐ Extension: full suggestion — Let's ride a mountain bike in the park!

28–36′
Mime game
carpet, pairs

▶ Mime game (act. 4, Talk Partners). A mimes an activity; B guesses with a question: Do you go bowling? Do you play table tennis? A answers Yes, I do. / No, I don't. until guessed. Swap. Everyone mimes and asks.

ICQ: Do you SAY the activity, or mime it? (→ mime it — partner asks!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair — is the verb right in the question (go / have / play)?

 Pupil's Book p.50 · activity cards
36–44′
Activity trade
mingle

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Each child holds an activity card. Music → mingle; off → pair: A shows the card, Do you (go bowling)? B answers truthfully Yes, I do / No, I don't, then swap, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges — everyone speaks many times.

Whole-class check: Hands up if your card was a GO activity!

 activity cards · music · ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade
44–52′
Sort & write
desks

Speak. ▶ RallyRobin: Take turns — Let's go… / Let's have… / Let's play… — activities you like.

Write (notebooks / AB p.48 format). Board frame from the columns: I like… / Let's go ___. Let's have ___. Let's play ___. Each child writes 3 suggestions with the right verb. Fast finishers add because it's fun.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from the verb columns. ⭐ Extension: add a reason: Let's have a picnic because it's sunny!
