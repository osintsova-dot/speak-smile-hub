Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have closed their own gaps in Units 5–9 — the 2–3 topics YOU choose from the diagnostic — through targeted stations and shown them in a team review game with a reward shot.
  • Subsidiary. Learner autonomy: pupils name what they find easy / tricky (traffic light), then work at the right station. Cooperative-learning micro-skills (RallyRobin, Sage & Scribe, Quiz-Quiz-Trade). End-of-year victory lap — celebrate how far they’ve come; exams are done.
  • Personal. This is a constructor, not a script — run the diagnostic first, then load the stations with the topics that actually wobbled. Every child works at every moment; the reward game is the cherry, not the cake. Both slots (lessons 71–72) use this template with different chosen topics.
By the end, pupils can…
  • use their 2–3 revised Units 5–9 topics more accurately;
  • self-diagnose — say which topic is easy / tricky;
  • ask & answer the revised structures in a team game.

Constructor — build the lesson in 3 steps
  • 1 · Before class. Skim the Unit 5–9 tests + Final Test / Speaking Exam notes → pre-mark weak topics.
  • 2 · In class. Run the 9-min diagnostic → confirm the 2–3 weakest → load stations A/B/C with exactly those.
  • 3 · Reward. Pick bowling 🎳 or basketball 🏀 for the team-review shot. It’s a victory lap — keep it joyful.
🎮 Offline setup: 3 desk-clusters = 3 stations, sign on each. Reward game needs only a box/basket + a paper ball. Everything else = flashcards + mini-whiteboards you already own.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is this a test?” → no — it’s free training to fix tricky bits; mistakes are welcome.
“Who works at a station — one person?” → EVERYONE, all the time.

Assumptions: Units 5–9 taught earlier this year; pupils know Showdown, station rotation and the traffic-light self-check. Teacher has skimmed the recent unit tests / L6 self-assessments to pre-select likely weak spots.

How the 3 stations run (all-active)
  • A · Vocabulary. Quiz-Quiz-Trade cards / picture→word mini-whiteboards — each child holds a card, all quiz in pairs.
  • B · Grammar. Sage & Scribe (Rally Coach): one coaches, one writes the target structure, then swap — both busy.
  • C · Reading. Read-and-match / true-false strips from the revised units — each child has own strip set.
Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Diagnostic shows too many gapsPick only the 2–3 weakest; the rest wait for next year. Depth over coverage.
Fast group finishes a station earlyExtension card on each station (⭐ write your own example / make one for a friend).
Rotation chaos on “Freeze!”Number the teams; teams move clockwise only; you count 5…4…3 to the next station.
Weaker pupils hide in the team gameShowdown: every child writes on their own board; team point = majority correct.
Reward game drags / sore losersOne shot per point, 20 sec; both teams get the “Well done!” cheer — “we beat the gaps together”.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🙋 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Diagnostic gap-finder
carpet

Rapid all-respond sweep (Units 5–9). Fire ~10 quick items across the units (flash a card / say a prompt) — every child answers on a mini-whiteboard: Show me! One per unit for each skill: a word, a structure gap, a picture. You are not scoring — you are spotting.

Confidence thermometer. Read each topic name; Easy? Green. Tricky? Red. Eyes on your own hand. Tally on the board.

✔ Decide now: circle the 2–3 weakest topics (most reds + most Showdown errors). Those load the stations & the game. Announce them: Today we fix … and …!

12–40′
Target-practice STATIONS
desks · 3 teams

3 stations × ~9′, rotate on Freeze (Cooperative learning). Load each with your chosen topics. A Vocabulary (Quiz-Quiz-Trade cards) · B Grammar (Sage & Scribe on the target structure) · C Reading (read-and-match strips). Model each in 20 sec before starting.

ICQ: At a station, who is working? (→ everyone). Rotate: Freeze! … Teams, move clockwise. 5…4…3…2…1 — go! Movement between stations = the brain-break.

🎮 Support / extension: 💪 stuck pupils get a word/structure prompt strip; ⭐ fast finishers write their own example for a friend to solve.
