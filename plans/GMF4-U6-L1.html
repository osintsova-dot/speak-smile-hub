Week 01.02–07.02 · Lesson 41✨ NEW · 12 machines~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.60 · AB p.58
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 machines in the house and say where they are & what we use them for: “There's a (cooker) in the kitchen. We use it for (cooking food).” · “Have you got a (dishwasher)? — Yes, I have.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Our house» (CD3 06) & the vocabulary chant (CD3 05); brainstorm the machines at home (Learning to learn); recycle rooms of the house + prepositions of place (on · next to · between · under · in front of); ask about frequency — “How often do you use it?” (every day / once a week); early literacy — read machine words & place them by room (AB).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — the memory game is high-energy, plan the freezes. Insist on the full frame (There's a … in the …. We use it for …-ing) and on every day / once a week for frequency.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 house machines;
  • say “There's a … in the …. We use it for …-ing.”;
  • ask “Have you got …? Where is it? How often do you use it?” and answer truthfully.

Structure
There's a (fridge) in the kitchen. We use it for (keeping food cool).  ·  Have you got a (dishwasher)? — Yes, I have.  ·  Where is it? — It's in the kitchen.  ·  How often do you use it? — I use it every day.
Form
There's a + machine + in the + room. We use it for + verb-ing (cooking / drying / washing). Question: Have you got a …? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't. Frequency: every day · once/twice a week · never.
Pron. watch

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“fridge — where do we keep it? What do we use it for?” → in the kitchen · keeping food cool.
“We use it for … — cook or cooking?” → cooking (verb + -ing after for).

Assumptions: Pupils know rooms of the house, prepositions of place & there is/are from earlier levels (recycled in the warm-up); some machines are known (fridge, TV) but the 12-item set is largely NEW. We use it for + -ing is receptive — production is today's stretch. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures.

10–20′
Presentation · 12 machines
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD3 05). Each card: What's this? It's a… fridge! toaster! hairdryer! Choral ×3 + one gesture (hairdryer = dry hair, toaster = pop up, hoover = push & hum).

Where + what for. Board: three rooms — kitchen (fridge, cooker, dishwasher, blender, microwave, toaster) · bedroom (hairdryer) · living room (DVD player, tablet, printer). Hold each card — class says: There's a fridge in the kitchen. We use it for keeping food cool.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if this machine is in the KITCHEN… blender! — flash cards fast, everyone reacts every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + word. ⭐ Extension: full frame — There's a blender in the kitchen. We use it for making cakes.

Song · Our house
desks

We use it for… Read the song lines: There's a cooker in the kitchen. We use it for cooking food. There's a dishwasher in the kitchen. We use it for washing dirty plates. Choral — plants the memory game. Freeze between verses.

28–36′
Have you got…? · memory game
carpet → desks, pairs

▶ Talk Partners (act. 4). Pairs study the PB p.60 house picture 30 sec, then close books. A asks: Have you got a dishwasher? Where is it? How often do you use it? B answers Yes, I have. It's in the kitchen. I use it every day. Open to check. Swap. Point per full answer.

ICQ: Do you answer with ONE word or a full sentence? (→ full sentence!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair — is it the full frame + a real frequency word?

36–44′
Machine trade
mingle

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Each child holds a machine card. Music → mingle; off → pair: A shows the card, What's this? What do we use it for? B answers It's a hoover. We use it for cleaning the floor. then swap, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges — everyone speaks many times.

Whole-class check: Hands up if your machine is in the KITCHEN!

Write (notebooks / AB p.58 format). Board frame: There's a ___ in the ___. We use it for ___-ing. Each child writes 3 sentences, one machine per room. Fast finishers add a frequency word.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from 3 word cards. ⭐ Extension: add frequency: I use it every day.
