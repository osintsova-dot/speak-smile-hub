🦝 Give Me Five! 1 · Unit 1 “Ready for school!” · Lesson 2 — Grammar
Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., emergent readers) · 60 min · Model → controlled drill → freer → production + TPR + Phonics · carpet + desks
Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 5✨ Grammar focusLine: продолжающие~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.11 · Big reading стр.28–29
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer where a classroom object is — accurately producing Where's my (pencil)? — It's here. and extending the answer to a place: It's in / on / under the (bag).
  • Subsidiary. Recycle Unit 1 / Starter language (classroom objects + colours + greetings) inside the new question; phonics ур.11 — big reading (recognise → blend → read short sentences); pre-literacy tricky words you · your; fine motor (Activity Book follow-and-draw).
  • Personal. Model first (I do → we do → you do); give an ICQ before every game; bury the drill inside games (tic-tac-toe, relay), never “repeat after me”; run stirrer → settler with songs and signals.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask Where's my…? and answer It's here. in a full sentence;
  • say where a thing is with in / on / under: It's under the bag.;
  • blend & read short school-things sentences; read tricky words you · your.

Structure
Core (PB p.9): Where's my (pencil)? — It's here.  ·  Stretch (KTP · Review p.16): It's (in / on / under) the (bag).
Form
Where + 's (is) + my + noun? → It + 's + here / preposition + the + noun. Connected Where's /weəz/. Contraction Where's = Where is, It's = It is.
Pron. watch
squeeze Where's to one beat /weəz/ (not “where — is”); final /g/ in bag; stress on where + object.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.11: big reading (тетрадь стр.28–29) — first meeting. Speed-read known sounds → blend into short words & a sentence (I sat, a pin, Sam is in). Point & read left→right. Sight/tricky words: you · your — tie-in Where's YOUR pencil?
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do I know where my pencil is?” → no → I ask: Where's my pencil?
Put a pencil inside the bag: “Is it in, on or under?” → in → It's in the bag.
Put it on top: “In or on?” → on → It's on the bag.

Assumptions: classroom objects, colours & greetings are known (Starter + Unit 1 Lesson 1). Where's my…? — It's here. was met in L1 — today we focus on the form & push to production. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → lean on real objects, pictures & audio. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

20–28′
Presentation — model & form
carpet

I do — model the dialogue (PB p.9, CD1·13). Act out with Speaky: hunt through a bag — 🦝 Oh no! Where's my pencil? You find it: It's here! — 🦝 And where's my book? — It's here, too. Look! Thank you! Play the audio once; pupils just watch/listen.

Form focus. Show the frame on the board (with pictures): Where's my ___? — It's ___. Squeeze it: Where's = Where is. Back-chain: pencil → my pencil → Where's my pencil? Choral drill ×3, then 3–4 children solo.

Push to a place (new: in / on / under). Hold a real bag + pencil. Put it IN: Where's my pencil? — It's IN the bag. ON: It's ON the bag. UNDER: It's UNDER the bag. Big TPR gesture for each. CCQ: hide it under — In or under? (→ under).

We do. You ask Where's my [ruler]?, place it somewhere, class answers together It's on the desk! ×4.

📓 Interactive notebook: «Lesson 2 — Ask and Find + Spinner» (spinner + classroom find-scene + I CAN strip; alt: object cards)
What it is: an A5 page with a paperclip spinner of school things (crayons · book · pencil case · pencil · rubber · school bag · sharpener · ruler) over a classroom find-scene, plus a BE KIND reminder to ask politely. Pupils: ① ASK AND FIND — ask Where's my ___? and find the object in the scene; ② SPIN! — flick the paperclip; ③ ASK AND ANSWER — whatever it lands on, ask about it, partner points and answers It's here! Swap and go again; then colour the I CAN… ask: Where's my…? · answer: It's here! strip. Teacher: spin one yourself and model the polite ask first; insist on the full question + It's here! (not one word). Alt (Where card page): take a card, ask Where's my ___?, find the object on the desk, put it back and say It's here! — pair work. Time: 6–7 min inside this stage, timing unchanged (this is the “you do” of the model). 🖨 Prep: print Drive «Lesson 2 / Grammar» (spinner) or «Where» (cards) A5, 1 per pupil · paperclip + pencil per pair · glue · coloured pencils.

28–36′
Controlled drill · Tic-Tac-Toe
carpet → teams

▶ Крестики-нолики (Tic-Tac-Toe) 🧘⚡ — 2 teams (✕ & ⭕). Lay 9 A4 object cards in a 3×3 grid on the carpet. To win a cell, a team must say a full sentence about that card — no sentence, no cell.

Team ✕ points to a cell: their asker says Where's my [crayon]? and a second child answers with a place: It's on the shelf! / It's in the bag! Correct → drop your ✕ token on the card. Then team ⭕.

Everyone plays (Numbered Heads): the whole team whispers the sentence together first; you call a number → that child says it aloud. Three in a row wins. ICQ: One word or a whole sentence to win a card? (→ whole sentence).

Reset if loud: Freeze! Replay with a fresh grid if time.

 9 A4 object cards · ✕/⭕ tokens · carpet · ▶ Tic-Tac-Toe
36–44′
Freer · card-grab relay
carpet, teams in lines

▶ Boat Race — «Where's my…?» relay ⚡ (Genki). Split into 2–3 teams, each in a line. Spread a set of object mini-cards on the floor in front of each line.

🦝 Speaky (or you) calls Where's my [rubber]? → the first runner from each team races to grab the right card, holds it up and shouts It's here! First correct grabber keeps the card & goes to the back; next runner ready.

All stay in the language: the seated team-mates chant the answer It's here! together while their runner grabs. Safety ICQ: Do we push or do we run? (→ run, one runner!). Most cards wins.

 2–3 sets of object mini-cards · Speaky · open carpet · ▶ Boat Race (Genki)
44–52′
Production · Hide & ask (pairs)
carpet → desks, in pairs

Model (board). ▶ Hide & Find: hide a pencil under a real bag while a child covers their eyes. They ask Where's my pencil? → class helps It's under the bag! (2× to model).

▶ RallyCoach, in pairs 🧘. Each pair has 4–5 objects + a bag/box. Child A closes eyes; Child B hides one object in / on / under the bag. A asks Where's my [ruler]?, opens eyes, guesses: It's on the bag! B praises & confirms Yes! Well done! — then swap roles. Both children ask AND answer every round.

Monitor: nudge full sentences (It's IN the bag, not just “bag”). Reset with Freeze! if needed.

 4–5 objects + bag/box per pair · ▶ Hide & Find / RallyCoach
