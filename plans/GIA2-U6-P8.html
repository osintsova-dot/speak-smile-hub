📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. An honest check on the unit: vocabulary (animals and describing + taking-action collocations), past simple of be (was/were, there was/were, questions), past simple of regular verbs (affirmative/negative + spelling), reading, listening, a short piece of writing.
  • Subsidiary. Test strategy BEFORE the start (time management, the order of the tasks); feedback straight afterwards; a reflection on the growth from the P1 gate to today.
  • Personal. ⚠️ There is NO official Macmillan pack for Unit 6 in the tests folder (only U1 and U8; Progress Test 1, Mid-Year and End-of-Year in that folder belong to a different course, Close-up A2) — work from the specification below, built from Quick review SB p.81 and WB pp. 50–51. The tone of the lesson: the test is proof of growth, not a verdict. The feedback happens the same day.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Which task do you start with? → the one that gives you more points for less time.
You are stuck on a question — what do you do? → mark it, move on, come back.
Forgotten whether it is was or were? → look at the subject: one — was, many and you/we/they — were.

Assumptions: The test is built by the teacher from the TEST_SPEC (Quick review SB p.81 + WB pp. 50–51 + the task formats of the unit's lessons). Syllabus pair 53. The students have met every format in class.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
