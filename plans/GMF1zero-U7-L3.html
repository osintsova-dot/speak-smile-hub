Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия с нуля) · 60 min · Story: listen → follow → act out · carpet + desks
Week 22.03–28.03 · Занятие 54📖 STORY~12 pupils · trios + pairsPupil's Book pp.74–75 · AB p.72🦸 Phonics Hero: повт. Части 3 (Magic E ур.22–23)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow and understand the story “The treasure hunt” (Pupil's Book pp. 74–75, CD3 29) and act it out using key story language: “It's a treasure map. Let's look for the treasure!”, “There's a (big, old tree) next to the (swimming pool).”, “There are (two yellow rocks) in front of the (zoo).”, “How many steps are there? — There are 20.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — town places, There's a / There are / How many (L1–L2), Unit 6 prepositions next to / in front of — woven into following the map; phonics — Phonics Hero: повторение Части 3, Magic E ур.22–23 (o-e /оу/ bone, nose · u-e /ю:/ cube, tune — the treasure dog's BONE = o-e!) + dictation (тетрадь стр.61–65); values: helping & teamwork on the hunt.
  • Personal. Keep story listening active (gesture responses, prediction); manage drama noise with Freeze; every child acts in a trio at the same time — no “2 on stage, 10 watching” until the final 1-min showcase.
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they understand the story (order the frames, answer with gestures);
  • say the key lines in role: “Let's look for the treasure!”, “There's a tree next to the pool!”, “How many steps? — Twenty!”;
  • read & write o-e/u-e words (bone, nose, cube, tune) from dictation;
  • say “Let's help!” and work as a team (values).

Structure
Can we explore your town? — Yes, of course. · It's a treasure map. Let's look for the treasure! · There's a (big, old tree) next to the (swimming pool). · There are (two yellow rocks) in front of the (zoo). · How many steps are there? — There are 20.
Form
There's a / There are + preposition phrase (next to / in front of the…). Receptive — meaning through the map & gesture; full production in the act-out.
Pron. watch
“treasure” = /ˈtreʒə/ (soft zh, not “tre-a-sure”); count 11–20 clearly; stress the place: next to the SWIMMING pool.
Phonics
Phonics Hero: повторение Части 3, Magic E ур.22–23: o-e /оу/ (bone, nose, home) · u-e /ю:/ (cube, tune) — read & match · dictation bone, nose, cube, home (тетрадь стр.61–65). Toby's BONE is the treasure — o-e!
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Point to the map: “Where's the tree? Next to the pool or the zoo?” → “Next to the swimming pool!” (point).
“How many steps are there?” → count on fingers → “Twenty!”
Last frame: “What did they find?” → “The treasure! And Toby's bone!”

Assumptions: places + There's a / There are + numbers 1–20 known from L1–L2; prepositions next to / in front of from Unit 6. Emergent readers → they FOLLOW the pictures & the map while listening; the teacher points. Magic E o-e/u-e studied in Часть 3 — today revision. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
New words block the story (treasure, rocks, steps)Pre-teach the 3 words before listening with picture + gesture (shiny gold, rock-fists, step-step-step up).
Children call answers over the audio“Listening hands” rule — hands cupped behind ears while the CD plays; talk only when it stops.
Prepositions confused (next to / in front of)Body gestures: shoulder-to-shoulder = next to; hand-in-front = in front of; re-point on the map.
Drama turns to chaos (running to “find treasure”)The hunt is ON THE SPOT / around a taped map; roles fixed by badges; “Freeze!” resets.
Frame cards ordered by one child in the pairRule “one card each turn, take turns” (RallyRobin with cards); praise pairs where both hands work.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset (essential in drama).

24–34′
📺 Story — listen & follow
carpet, books/screen

Before you read (PB pp. 74–75). Big story picture on screen / books open. Pre-teach with gesture: treasure (shiny gold) · rocks (rock-fists) · steps (step-step-step) · bone (dog chewing).

🎮 Interactive (projector): Find it! — “Follow the map.” Screen: the treasure-map scene. How: Speaky asks, a child taps the next clue; it lights up. Pupils: whole class points in their book / calls it — The tree! Next to the pool! The yellow rocks! Teacher: 🦝 Where next? Point! — 4–5 clues, quick. Offline reserve: point in the open Pupil's Book (pp. 74–75). · 🎮 find-it-u7

Predict (thinking) — before we listen. Where is the treasure? Next to the tree? Up the steps? Or… in front of the zoo? Thumbs vote. Let's listen and check your guess!

Listen 1 — global (CD3 29). “Listening hands” behind ears. Teacher points frame by frame; children follow. Gist check: Do they find the treasure? Show me! (thumbs) Was your guess right?

Detail check — True or False? (thumbs up / down, all react). There's a tree next to the swimming pool. (👍) · There are two rocks in front of the café. (👎 — the zoo!) · There are 20 steps. (👍) · Toby finds a bone. (👍). Re-point the frame to fix any wrong thumb.

Listen 2 — active (all respond). Play again; children do the story gestures at the right moments: point next-to 🌳, in-front-of, count the steps 1–20, dig 🕳, cheer Treasure! CCQs between frames (see box above).

▶ Build the story
desks, pairs

▶ Story Builders (picture re-skin for non-readers — all pairs work at once). Each pair gets 6–8 mini frame-cards of the treasure hunt, shuffled.

Order it. Put the story in order — one card each, take turns! (RallyRobin with cards.) Countdown 2 min.

Check with the audio. Replay CD3 29: pairs touch each card as they hear it — self-check, fix the order. Then choral retell, teacher holds up big frames, class says the line: It's a treasure map! · There's a tree next to the pool! · There are two rocks in front of the zoo! · Twenty steps! · Dig! · Treasure! · Toby's bone!

43–51′
Act out — drama
carpet, trios

Roles. Trios: Map reader (red badge, holds the map) · Explorer (green) · Toby the dog + Magic Bike bell (blue). Teacher narrates and models once with a strong trio.

All trios act at the same time. Narrate slowly, frame by frame; every trio plays it in their own spot around a taped map: Can we explore your town? — Yes, of course! · It's a treasure map. Let's look for the treasure! · There's a tree next to the pool! · There are two rocks in front of the zoo! · How many steps? — Twenty! · Dig, dig, dig! · Treasure! And Toby's bone! Hooray!

Create (thinking) — make it yours. Before round 2: Hide YOUR treasure! Where is it? Next to what? In front of what? Each trio adds a line: There's a café. The treasure is next to the café! (support: point to a place card; extension: invent the whole clue).

Swap roles, run it again (2 rounds). Volunteers: ONE trio shows 30 sec for the class — big applause. Values: A hunt is better together — what do we say to our team? → Let's help! Well done, team!

💪 Support: quieter children get Toby + the bell (short line + gesture); a line card in hand. ⭐ Extension: confident children read the map / narrate and lead the clues. 		role badges ×3 colours · taped map · bell · place cards · PB p. 75 (values)
