Week 01.02–07.02 · Занятие 40✨ NEW · 10 farm animals~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4Pupil's Book p.50 · AB p.48🦸 Phonics Hero ур.26b · /ай/ ie·igh·y·i-e
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 10 farm animals and ask & answer using “Can you see a (cow)? — Yes, I can!” and “What's this? — It's a (sheep).” in the farm context.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — Genki «Do you like animals?» & «Animal Noises» (moo · baa · quack), numbers 1–12 & colours, and “Is it a…? — Yes, it is / No, it isn't” (Unit 2) — folded onto the new farm animals (“It's a brown cow”, “How many ducks?”, “Is it a goat?”); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.26 (b) закрепление: sound /ай/ in its houses ie · igh · y · i-e (word grid → sort → dictation → story «Ночь и свет»); tricky words always, made; pre-literacy & fine motor (sticker chant).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm with songs & signals; every task keeps ALL children responding (own card / show-me / stand-sit) — never “2 play, 10 watch”; catch & recast the long /iː/ in sheep (not “ship”).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 10 farm animals (cat · cow · sheep · goat · horse · duck · frog · bird · mouse · chicken);
  • ask & answer “Can you see a…? — Yes, I can!”;
  • read & sort /ай/ words by their houses (igh · y · i-e) and write pie, night, my, sky from dictation.

Structure
Can you see a (cow)? — Yes, I can!  ·  What's this? — It's a (…).  ·  recycle: The (duck) is (yellow). Is it a (goat)? — Yes, it is.
Form
Can + you + see + a + noun? → Yes, I + can. Weak “Can you” /kən jə/; strong can in the answer. “one sheep, two sheep” (no plural -s).
Pron. watch
long /iː/ sheep (not “ship”); /aʊ/ in cow; /tʃ/ in chicken; /ай/ = ONE sound in night, sky, my.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.26 (b) закрепление: sound /ай/ — houses ie (pie, tie) · igh (light, night — g·h silent!) · y at the end (my, sky, fly) · i-e (kite, time) — word grid, sort, dictation pie, night, my, sky, story «Ночь и свет» (тетрадь стр.72–74). Tricky words: always · made.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is the cow there, in the picture? Can I find it?” → yes → “Yes, I can!”
“night, sky, my — same sound /ай/? → yes! Same letters? → NO — different houses!”

Assumptions: numbers 1–12, colours, likes, «Do you like animals?» & «Animal Noises» are known from the Genki block (занятия 1–12); “Is it a…? — Yes, it is / No, it isn't” from Unit 2; school things & «Where's my…?» from Unit 1; food & “I like…” from Unit 4. Phonics: Часть 1 закончена с дипломом «I can read!», диграфы & Magic E пройдены; /ай/ met last lesson (ур.26a) → today consolidates. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → rely on pictures & audio, not printed text. Carpet & team routines are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
sheep heard/said as “ship”Exaggerate long /iː/ with a smile-mouth gesture; drill sheep–seat–meat slowly together.
Plural “sheeps / two sheep-s”Hold up 2 fingers: one sheep, two sheep — same word.
Confuse frog ↔ dog (Toby the pet is on the page)One clear gesture for frog (jump), name Toby separately as “a dog, our friend”.
igh read as three letters /i-g-h/“Three letters, ONE sound /ай/ — g and h are SILENT (sleeping!)”; slide three fingers into one fist.
Over-excited animal noises don't stopFreeze! + volume-control arm; follow every stirrer with a settler.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

10–22′
Phonics Hero — /ай/ ie·igh·y·i-e (b)
board → desks

Recall the houses. One sound /ай/ — FOUR houses! 🏠 ie — pie, tie · 🏠 igh — light, night (g·h sleeping — silent!) · 🏠 hvostik y — my, sky, fly! · 🏠 i-e — kite, time. Quick chorus of each house. ▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if you hear /ай/… sky!… cat!… night! — everyone reacts every time.

Word grid — тетрадь стр.73 №1 «Читаем слова» (all read together). Picture beside each word: pie · tie · light · night · high · sky · fly · kite. Kagan — RallyRobin: pairs take turns down the grid.

Sort — стр.73 №2 «Раздели слова» (all vote first). For each word, every child points to the right house card (igh / y / i-e) → check → write it in the column. Do 6 in class, the rest go home. RallyCoach: partner checks before the next.

Dictation — стр.73 №3 (everyone writes). Now YOU write. Listen: pie… word once → segment on fingers → all write → Show me! → reveal & self-check. Words: pie · night · my · sky. Then слова-молнии (стр.73 №4): speed strip, there and back; + tricky words always · made — These are tricky — we just KNOW them!

Story — стр.74 «Ночь и свет» (settler). Chorus the 5 frames, finger under the words: It is night. The moon is bright. A bat flies high… ⭐ Star task: find & circle every night. Colour the star «Я молодец!» (стр.73).

🎮 Reading-game option: read-race — ⚔ battle mode: a word pops up, everyone reads it aloud, the fastest correct team races ahead. Swap it in for the speed-reading strip when the screen is free.

💪 Support: pre-write the house letters on their line; they copy the word under. ⭐ Extension: add one more /ай/ word by ear (tie · time).

✔ Assess: the “Show me!” pages are your live check of ур.26 — note who mixes the igh/y houses; recast on the spot.

carpet, in pairs
🎮 Interactive (projector): “Find it!” — farm scene. Tap Speaky — 🦝 asks Can you see a (goat)? the class scans the farm on screen, points & answers Yes, I can! (2–3 rounds, to model the phrase for all — every child points at their own card). · 🎮 find-it-u5

▶ Memory · Can you see a…? — in pairs, on the carpet. Each pair has 5 animal cards. Child 1 shows a card, names it — It's a cow. — and places it face-down. Repeat for all 5 (show → name → place).

Then Child 1 asks: Can you see a cow? Child 2 — from memory, first try — turns the right card. Correct → Yes, I can! and keeps it. Then swap roles. (Most cards wins.) Reset if loud: Freeze!

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair at a time — can they both produce the question AND the answer unprompted? That's the lesson's success check.
