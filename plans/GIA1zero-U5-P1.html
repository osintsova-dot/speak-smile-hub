WHAT WE DO: Timed Pair Share on repeat: check-in 2′ · rewind 3′ (Unit 4: Present simple + free time — in the new topic) · bridge 1′

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Check-in (2′). At the door: Hi! One word — how was your holiday? Fingers for mood, 1 to 5.
  2. Format — Kagan Timed Pair Share: A talks for their time on the timer, B is the active listener (counts pauses on their fingers, catches the best phrase), then Switch roles! The listener's response starts with My favourite bit was…
  3. Rewind U4 (3′). Frame on the board: In my free time I … . I don't … . Pairs 90 sec: Tell your partner three things you do in your free time. I collect 3 answers out loud (+1 to the team).
  4. Bridge (1′). You said what you DO. Today: what you CAN do. Play the guitar? Repair a bike? Let's find out.
📺 On screen: board: U4 frame “In my free time I…” · take down the U4 scoreboard → put up a clean U5 scoreboard
👦 Students do: tell a partner about their free time from the frame. Expected: a warm, calm start. Typical error: «I play piano» without the — I gently supply the, no stopping.

WHAT WE DO: introducing vocabulary through the symbols of ex.3 with gestures and chorus; three speak/talk/tell cards on the board

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I open p.61: 14 symbols from a video game. Each one — picture + word + gesture: catch (catching a ball) · climb (climbing) · fly (wings) · repair (turning a screwdriver) · wash (rubbing my face)…
  2. Chorus ×2 for each word, then a “freeze-frame”: I show the gesture — the class says the word.
  3. Ex.3 in pairs, 2 minutes. Reinforcement (TB p.70): they may ask What does … mean? Key: 1 catch · 2 talk · 3 send · 4 fly · 5 spell · 6 carry · 7 add · 8 wash · 9 jump · 10 clean · 11 climb · 12 repair · 13 tell stories · 14 speak.
  4. I put up three colour cards: SPEAK + a language · TALK to + a person · TELL + stories — they stay up all lesson.
  5. Pronunciation: clim(b) — no B! tal(k) — no L! Chorus ×3.
📺 On screen: SB p.61 ex.3 · three speak/talk/tell cards (stay up) · gestures
👦 Students do: repeat the words in chorus with gestures, standing — everyone moves. Expected: the gestures glue the words in for good. Typical error: climb with a [b] sound — the “finger to lips” gesture.

Try it · Can you …? frame drill

WHAT WE DO: the first output into speech by a frame: Can you…? — Yes, I can / No, I can't; ▶ Mix-Pair-Share around the class

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Frame on the board, BIG: Can you ___ ? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't. I model it with 3 students.
  2. Choral drill ×4: Can you cook? Can you dance? Can you fly a plane? Can you speak French?
  3. ▶ Mix-Pair-Share: everyone has a card with a picture of an action. Walk! Find a partner. Ask about YOUR card, answer their card. Swap cards. New partner! 4 minutes.
  4. They count how many people answered Yes, I can — 2 results out loud.
  5. Checkpoint 45′: You can ask and answer. After the break — a girl who MAKES gadgets from rubbish.
📺 On screen: action-picture cards (one per student) · frame on the board
👦 Students do: walk round the class, ask and answer, swap cards — everyone talks at the same time. Expected: 5–7 partners each. Typical error: «Yes, I can cook» — I point at the frame, a short answer is enough.

WHAT WE DO: Timed RoundRobin: introducing the negative by a model (no theory): I can't … ; choral drill + a “true about me” circle

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Frame next to the first one: I can ___ . / I can't ___ . I model it myself: I can cook, but I can't repair a bike.
  2. This is Kagan Timed RoundRobin: strictly round the circle against the timer, one turn — one person, no skipping: Clockwise, one each — keep it moving, no skips!
  3. Chorus ×3 on can't — we drill the long sound: ca:n't — long and strong. (ABCD TB p.74)
  4. Circle: each person — one sentence I can … , but I can't … by the frame; the class echoes can't.
  5. No rules yet: The full rules come next lesson — today we just say it.
📺 On screen: board: two frames, big · can't highlighted in red
👦 Students do: build a sentence about themselves round the circle — everyone speaks. Expected: the frame carries everyone, including the weaker ones. Typical error: «I can't to swim» — the echo model, no explanation.

WHAT WE DO: a moving consolidation: abilities bingo + a gesture relay; everyone moves

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ▶ Ability Bingo: everyone has a 3×3 grid, they fill in 9 actions from the cards. I call out: catch a ball… speak a language… — they cross it off. First row = Bingo! 2 rounds.
  2. ▶ Gesture relay: teams in columns. I show the first person a gesture — they say the phrase, run to the back; next one. Whoever completes the circle fastest.
  3. ▶ A quick round of Charades (Activities bank TB p.6): one person mimes — the team guesses the phrase with can (You can play the guitar!). 4 rounds of 30 sec.
  4. Points to the teams on the scoreboard.
📺 On screen: bingo grids (to print) · action cards · scoreboard
👦 Students do: play bingo and the relay — everyone moves for 10 minutes. Expected: the vocabulary becomes automatic, the class is warmed up. Typical error: they write in Russian — «English only, or no bingo».

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can name 14+ ability verbs with the right noun (catch a ball · fly a plane · repair a bike) and ask and answer Can you …? by a frame.
  • Subsidiary. The unit question is opened with a vote; collocations are introduced with gestures; Video skills (home-made gadgets) is watched with visual tasks; can/can't — model ONLY, no theory (the system comes in Pair 3).
  • Personal. First lesson after the holidays: a soft start, plenty of movement, supports on the board right to the end. Don't rush can — today it lives in a frame, not a rule.
By the end, students can…
  • say 14 abilities with the correct noun after the verb
  • ask a partner Can you …? and answer Yes, I can / No, I can't
  • understand a short 'how to' video with visual tasks
  • write 4–5 sentences about what they can and can't do

Structure
Can you cook? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't. · I can play the guitar. · I can't fly a plane.
Form
verb + its own word: catch a ball · fly a plane · speak a language · talk TO a friend · tell stories · play football / play the guitar. can + verb (for now, just a frame).
Pron. watch
clim(b) — the b is silent · tal(k) — the l is silent · can in a statement sounds short, /kən/ (TB pp.70, 74).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“speak or talk — about a language?” → SPEAK a language.
“catch a language?” → no: catch a BALL.
“Can you swim? — Yes, I can swim yes?” → that's enough: Yes, I can.

Assumptions: After U1–U4: be, have got, there is/are, Present simple are all active. They've met can in songs/phrases, but never studied it as a system. Tech words (laptop, tablet) they know from everyday Russian.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
