Give Me Five! 2 · Unit 1 “Move your body” · Lesson 1 — Vocabulary
A1 young learners (~8–9 y.o., early readers) · 60 min · PPP + TPR + games · carpet + desks
Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 4✨ NEW~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4Line: продолжающие🎯 10 sports · I play / I go
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 10 sports and say which they do using “I play (football)” / “I go (swimming)”, noticing play + ball games vs go + …-ing.
  • Subsidiary. Recognise the question “Do you play any sports?” receptively in song & chant (drilled in L2); early-reader literacy — match printed sport words to pictures; review action verbs / “favourite subject — PE!” (interleaved lead-in); listen for detail (order the sticker chant).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every game; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; hide the drill inside games so every child speaks.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 10 sports;
  • say & write “I play (football)” / “I go (swimming)”;
  • sort a sport into the play or go group;
  • read the 10 sport words with picture support.
Target language

Structure
I play (football / tennis / basketball). · I go (swimming / cycling / skateboarding / rollerblading). · Receptive: Do you play any sports? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Form
play + ball/team game (football, tennis…) · go + activity ending in -ing (swimming, cycling…). I + play/go + sport.
Pron. watch
long words by syllable: bas-ket-ball · roll-er-bla-ding · skate-boar-ding; stress on first syllable. Clear final -ing /ɪŋ/.
Phonics / literacy
GMF2 phonics ai/ay belongs to L5 — not imported early. Today = light word-recognition only: read the 10 sport labels with picture support; syllable-clap the long words.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I play football — is there a ball?” → yes → play (ball / team game).
“I go swimming — does the word end in -ing?” → yes → go + …-ing.
“Football or swimming — which one uses go?” → swimming.

Assumptions: Starter unit done (subjects, “What's your favourite subject?”, “What's his/her name?”); some sports known from Level 1. Early readers → words supported by pictures & audio, not text alone. Carpet & team routines known. Teams: max 4 per team; other sizes → 12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4.

Presentation · Vocabulary
carpet → teams ⚡🧘

Present the 10 sports. Hold up each A4 flashcard: What's this sport? Model + drill ×3, then 2–3 children. As you go, sort onto two board columns: I PLAY (football, basketball, tennis, baseball, volleyball, hockey) · I GO (swimming, cycling, skateboarding, rollerblading).

CCQ the split. Football — play or go? Swimming — play or go? Gesture: bounce a ball = play, move body = go. Syllable-clap the long words: roll-er-bla-ding!

▶ Slow reveal / Guess it! (teams). Cover a flashcard; slide it out bit by bit. First team to call the full sentence wins the point: It's basketball — I play basketball! (word alone = no point, next team's turn). Do all 10, fast. Every child on a team whispers the guess to their partner first (all engaged).

ICQ: Do you say just “football” or “I play football”? (→ the full sentence!)

28–36′
▶ Memory (teams)
carpet, teams ⚡

Back to the carpet — team game! ▶ Memory / Pelmanism: each team gets a set of picture–word pairs (sport picture ↔ printed sport word — good for early readers). Cards face-down in a grid.

On your turn, a child flips two cards and must say the sentence to claim a pair: It's tennis — I play tennis! / It's cycling — I go cycling! Match + correct sentence → keep the pair & go again. No match → face-down, next player.

Roles rotate (flipper · sentence-checker). Most pairs wins. Reset if loud: Freeze!

▶ Card-grab relay
open space ⚡⚡

Line up in your teams — let's race! ▶ Sports relay (эстафета): sport flashcards spread on the far wall/floor. Call a sport (or 🦝 calls it); the front runner of each team races, grabs the right card, races back and shouts the sentence: I go rollerblading! Correct sentence = point for the team, card goes to the back of the line.

Run 8–10 rounds so everyone runs. Play Genki #48 “What Sports Do You Play?” under the relay for pace. Manage with Freeze! between rounds.
