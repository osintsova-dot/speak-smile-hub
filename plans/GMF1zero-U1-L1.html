🦝 Give Me Five! 1 (с нуля) · Unit 1 “Ready for school!” · Занятие 16 / L1 — Vocabulary
Pre-A1 young learners (~6–7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия с нуля) · 60 min · PPP + TPR + Phonics + games · carpet + desks
Week 26.10–01.11 · Занятие 16✨ NEW VOCAB~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.14b · ee·or
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 10 classroom objects in context (frame It's a (…)) and begin to ask & answer where a thing is — Where's my (pencil)? — It's here! (Pupil's Book p. 8, song I'm ready for school!).
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — Genki greetings / colours / numbers (занятия 1–12) — woven into the topic (It's a red crayon! · How many pencils?); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.14 (b): digraphs ee /iː/ · or /ɔː/ (recognise → blend → segment, тетрадь стр.36–38); listen & sing for detail; pre-literacy & fine motor (stickers, notebook flaps, badge).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; every task keeps ALL children responding (own card / point & say / all teams at once) — never “2 play, 10 watch”.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 10 classroom objects (It's a book / glue / scissors…);
  • begin to ask & answer Where's my…? — It's here!;
  • sort & write ee/or words from dictation and read the story «Пчёлы и пирог».
Target language

Structure
What's this? — It's a (…).  ·  Where's my (pencil)? — It's here!  ·  preview: Put the (book) in / on / under the (bag).
Form
It + 's (is) + a + noun.   Where + 's (is) + my + noun? → It + 's + here. Connected “Where's” /weəz/, one word.
Pron. watch
final /g/ in bag; /ʃ/ in sharpener; scissors /ˈsɪzəz/ (silent c, /z/); keep It's in the answer, not bare “Here”.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.14 (b) закрепление: ee /iː/ (bee, tree, feet, green) · or /ɔː/ (fork, corn, storm) — the donkey call ee-or!; sort · dictation · story «Пчёлы и пирог» (тетрадь стр.36–38) · tricky strip here · there · by · sight words: who, which.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Peek a card out of Speaky's bag: “Can you see all of it?” → no → “So guess — What's this?”
Hide a pencil behind your back: “Can you see it?” → no → “So I ask: Where's my pencil?” → find it → It's here!
Phonics: “ee — two letters. How many sounds?” → ONE → /iː/ (a long smile: bee!).

Assumptions: greetings, colours, numbers & classroom commands are known from the Genki block (занятия 1–12, первый год) + Starter; the 26 single sounds & first digraphs (ai·oa·ie·ee·or) are met. Pupils are non-/emergent readers → rely on pictures & audio, not printed text. Carpet routines known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuse rubber / rulerPresent as a contrasting pair with one clear gesture each (rub-rub vs. measure).
Name one word, drop the frame (“book!” not “It's a book!”)Posted sentence frame + hand-sweep gesture; no point / no card until the whole frame It's a book! is said.
Final /g/ in bag dropped; scissors hard to sayShort exaggerated choral drill; break sci-ssors in two, snip fingers.
Relay: waiting team runs wild / only the runner talksRule “the whole team says the sentence for the point”; a Freeze line + one runner at a time.
Memory: child flips a match but stays silentRule “flip → SAY it”; no pair is kept until It's a (…)! is said by the whole team.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game […] = fill-in slot 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. This is a busy, PLAYFUL lesson (3 games + song + notebook). Keep it moving, pick don't cram. The core = phonics (own aim) + Slow-Reveal presentation + one big game (Relay) + song & notebook. Buffers to drop first if you're short: the 2nd Memory round, the prepositions preview, Timed Pair Share. Better ten words said with joy by every child than a rushed pile of stages.

10–22′
Phonics Hero — ee·or (b)
board → desks

Speed reading (тетрадь стр.36 №1). Strip ai · oa · ie · ee · or: There and back — fast! Donkey recall: How does the donkey cry? — ee-or! (a long smile for ee, a big open mouth for or).

“ee or or?” — whole-class show-me (all react). Every child holds a smile card 🙂 (ee) + a fork card 🍴 (or). Say words — tree · fork · feet · corn · green · storm · bee · sport… → Show me! — all raise the right card. 8–10 words, fast.

Sort — стр.37 (all write). Word up (tree · corn · feet · fork · green · storm) → read together → everyone writes it in the right column. Kagan RallyCoach: partner checks, praises.

Dictation — стр.37 (everyone writes). Word once → segment on fingers (/b/ /ee/ — bee!) → write → Show me! → reveal on the board. Words: bee, tree, fork, corn.

Tricky strip + sight words. here · there · by — read for speed, there and back. Tie-in: Where's my pencil? — It's HERE! — today's answer, now in print. + sight pair who · which: flash, chorus, air-write.

Story — стр.38 «Пчёлы и пирог» (settler). Chorus the frames, finger under the words: A green tree. Bees buzz in the tree. I see a pie!… ⭐ find & circle every tree. Colour the star «Я молодец!».

💪 Support: dictation with b__ started; sort with the digraph highlighted. ⭐ Extension: 5th word by ear (green). 🎮 Phonics Hub (speakandsmile.ru/gmf1/phonics/) — набор ee·or, ✅ готов; offline: тетрадь + board.

🎮 Reading-game option: read-race — ⚔ battle mode, level «Диграфы»: a word pops up, everyone reads it aloud, the fastest correct team races ahead. Swap it in for the speed-reading strip when the screen is free.

24–31′
▶ Slow Reveal · Presentation
carpet → guessing

Present the 10 school things — угадай-по-кусочку (dual coding: picture + word + gesture). All 10 object cards are in 🦝 Speaky's bag. Pull each card out very slowly (or slide it out from behind a cover card), showing a tiny bit at a time.

▶ Slow Reveal / «Что в рюкзаке?» Before it's fully out, the whole class guesses, pointing: Is it a ruler? Is it a book? Full reveal → confirm with the frame: Yes! It's a book! Choral drill ×3 + the gesture; then 2–3 children solo. Do all 10.

Contrast the tricky pair (hold both up). This is a rubber. (rub-rub) This is a ruler. (measure) — class copies each gesture.

ICQ: Do we shout one word, or the whole sentence? (→ the whole sentence — It's a book!)

🧠 Predict: hold the reveal — What comes next? Guess! — children point before it's out. ▲ Extension: add colour — It's a blue book!

31–39′
▶ Relay · Pack the bag!
whole room ⚡

▶ Эстафета «Pack Speaky's bag!» (Игротека — эстафета / card-grab relay — the whole class runs, nobody sits out). Object cards spread in a big pile across the room. Teams line up in relay files.

How it runs. Teacher / 🦝 Speaky calls: Bring me the glue! The first runner from each team races to the pile, grabs the right card, runs back — and the WHOLE team shouts the frame together for the point: It's the glue! Here you are! Card correct + full sentence said → 1 point, card goes in Speaky's bag. Then the next runner. Rotate so every child runs.

Keep ALL engaged. Waiting teammates chant the target word for their runner; no point unless the whole team says the sentence. Reset with Freeze! if it gets loud; one runner at a time.

◆ Support: call the word AND show its card for slower runners. ▲ Extension: call two — Bring me the scissors and the ruler! · or add colour — the red crayon!

48–54′
▶ Memory · teams
carpet, teams

▶ Memory / Мемо (Игротека — memory pairs · retrieval practice — every team plays at once). Each team of 4 has a grid of object-picture pairs face-down on the carpet.

How it runs. A child flips two cards; the whole team names each aloud with the frame — It's a ruler!… It's a book! A match → the team keeps it (only if the sentence was said); no match → flip back, next child. Rotate the flipper each turn so everyone plays.

Kagan — Numbered Heads energy: before a flipper turns a card, the team whispers a guess together. Most pairs = the team cheer. Reset if loud: Freeze! Run once; a 2nd round only if time (buffer).

🧠 “Where's my…?” twist: stronger teams play with the day's question — the flipper asks Where's my glue? and turns a card to check → It's here!

🎮 Digital option (projector): class-memo — S&S memory pairs on screen, 1–4 teams: a rotating child flips two cards, the whole class names every picture aloud — It's a pencil! Swap it in for one round when the screen is free.
