Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to follow & enjoy the story «A new friend» (CD1 T27) — Katy Kangaroo is new at school — and to ask & refuse politely: “Do you want to (draw)? — No, I want to jump!”
  • Subsidiary. Predict from the 6 story pictures; listen (CD1 T27) with the story cards; wave when they hear a school activity (TB tip); play Sticky Fingers; act tiny bits; circle who's new & stick the story sticker (PB p.7); Phonics Hero — ee · or, blend + speed-read p.36–38.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, surprise. Don't test comprehension; celebrate joining in. This is the seed of the ‘being kind’ value (L6) — notice Mimi being kind.
By the end, pupils can…
  • point to what happens in each story picture;
  • ask “Do you want to (draw)?”;
  • refuse & say what you want — “No, I want to jump!”

Assumptions: The ten activities & What do you do at school? are active from L1–L2; reading continues in the workbook. The story-time routine (listen · point · join in) is set from Unit 1. Do you want to…? is understood receptively; producing it is today's stretch.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Boing! A NEW friend is coming to school — and she can JUMP! Who is it? Let's find out! Jump like a kangaroo, then freeze.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 14 — sounds ee · or (letter-teams). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Blend the team: f-ee-t → feet, s-ee → see, f-or-k → fork — sweep the two letters + a picture. ▶ Team hop — children hop to the letter-team Speaky calls, then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.36–38 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read what · when together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · Story «A new friend»
carpet

Predict. Show the 6 story cards — Who is new at school? What does the new friend want to do? Take 2–3 guesses (Russian OK).

Listen 1 — for fun. Play the story (CD1 T27) with the cards. Pause on each picture: Do you want to draw, Katy? — No! I want to jump! Children point & join the words they can.

Listen 2 — spot the activity. Wave when you hear DRAW… PAINT… JUMP! wave your arms every time you hear a school activity — active listening.

26–32′
Game · Sticky Fingers (30) → Do you want to…?
carpet, circle

▶ Sticky Fingers Genki Sticky Fingers: children each hold one of your outstretched fingers. 🦝 Speaky asks a child Do you want to draw? — that child answers No, I want to jump! (does the action) and Speaky ‘releases’ the finger.

Everyone waits holding a finger, everyone gets a turn — calm but each child speaks & moves.

💪 Support: answer Yes / No + the action. ⭐ Extension: add I want to (jump)!

 Speaky puppet · activity cards
32–40′
Perform · act the story ⚡
carpet, standing

Act it out. Play the story again; children mime: draw/colour/paint (Mimi), sulk (Katy: No! I don't like school), then all JUMP with Katy, and cheer — friends! Pick 2 to be Mimi & Katy; the class narrates the activities.

Spot the kindness. Notice the kindness: Is Mimi kind to Katy? (Yes!) — a first taste of L6's ‘being kind’ value.

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Ask your partner “Do you want to draw?” — refuse and jump! — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I know the story! I can say “I want to jump!” colour one star.

Wonderful kind friends today! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
