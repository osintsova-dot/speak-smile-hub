Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to read & say the /iː/ sound spelt y and ey (happy · very · monkey · chimney · key), talk about famous Hawaiian people, and read & plan a biography / autobiography — three mini-blocks, one “people & the past” thread.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics: notice that y and ey can both spell /iː/ (recognise → say → sort, next to ee & ea); culture — famous Hawaiian people (Obama · Kamehameha · Onizuka · Bruno Mars) + Channel 21 “heroes” video, 21C: researching information & planning a presentation; literacy — text type a biography / autobiography (chronological order, inverted commas), plan & write your own.
  • Personal. Three blocks — keep transitions crisp (chant/action bridges); the spelling “cross the river” is play; cap each block hard so the autobiography actually gets planned.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the /iː/ words and sort y / ey spellings;
  • name 2–3 famous Hawaiian people and say why they're famous;
  • find the parts of a biography and plan an autobiography with a partner.

Structure
How do you spell chimney? — C-H-I-M-N-E-Y.  ·  Barack Obama was born in Hawaii.  ·  biography: He was born in… When he was young… Then… Finally…
Form
/iː/ = y (happy, very, noisy, lazy, lorry) AND ey (monkey, donkey, key, chimney) — (also ee, ea). Biography layout: born → childhood → achievements, chronological, past simple, inverted commas for speech.
Pron. watch
/iː/ clean in happy/key/chimney; Hawaii /həˈwaɪiː/; Onizuka /ˌɒnɪˈzuːkə/; biography /baɪˈɒɡrəfi/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“happy and key — same /iː/ sound? Same letters?” → same sound, different spelling (y vs ey)!
“A biography — the events in ANY order or in ORDER?” → in order — born first, then… Finally.

Assumptions: Character adjectives, was/were, past activities & when-I-was are active (L1–L4). Sound–letter awareness from GMF3 phonics is enough to enjoy the y / ey noticing (по учебнику). Pupils can read the short biography with support.

Block 1 · Phonics /iː/ (y · ey)
carpet → desks

Speak and spell (act. 1, PB p.23 · CD1 39-area). Listen & say: happy · very · noisy · monkey · donkey · key · lorry · chimney. Speed ladder: slow → fast → whisper. Pairs test each other ×3.

Sort the /iː/ spellings. Hoops/columns: y (happy, very, lazy, lorry) · ey (monkey, donkey, key, chimney) · (spares: ee — bee · ea — sea). Hold up word cards — every child points/steps to the right hoop BEFORE you place each card.

Notice the rule. Happy and key — same sound /iː/, different letters! y at the end and ey both say /iː/. (notice, don't lecture — and beware: fly says /aɪ/!).

💪 Support: say the word, teacher shows the hoop. ⭐ Extension: add one more /iː/ word to a hoop (party, money).

✔ Assess: who still reads word-final y as /aɪ/? Note them — extra RallyRead next unit.

Block 3 · Literacy · a biography
carpet → desks, pairs

Read a biography (PB p.24, act. 1–3, CD1 40). «Roald Dahl»: Where was he born? What was he good at? What was his first children's book? Read/listen; find the order — born → childhood → books.

▶ Biography scavenger hunt. Race in pairs to find on PB p.24: When was he born? What did he hide in the garden? What did he love? First hands up read the answer aloud — everyone scans.

Writing tip (PB p.25 · Writing). A biography is in ORDER: born first, then childhood, then… Use inverted commas for what people said! Chorus the sequencers (First · Then · After that · Finally) with finger-counting.

▶ Plan an autobiography (Cooperative learning, AB p.22 format). Pairs make notes about THEMSELVES: Born · When I was young · Things I learnt · Now. 2 pairs share one line aloud.

💪 Support: fill the plan template with word/verb support. ⭐ Extension: write the full autobiography with all sequencers + one inverted-comma quote.
