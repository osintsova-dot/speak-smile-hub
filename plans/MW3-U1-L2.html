Week 07.09–13.09 · Lesson 2✨ Focus · Where's number (one)? — It's on (red)~8 pupils · circle + pairsPH 4–6 (h r m d g o u l f · p.10–16) · Genki: Where Is It?(82)·How Are You?(04)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to ask & answer about the Big Wheel: “Where's number (three)? — It's on (orange)”, and to greet: “Hello, how are you? — I'm fine, thank you.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing the Big Wheel song + Genki «Where Is It?» (82) as the language songs; ask-and-say in pairs with the puppet; show the Big Wheel via the coursebook app; Phonics Hero — sounds h r m d g o u l f, blend + speed-read p.10–16; listen and find numbers/colours (PB p.3).
  • Personal. Model the question with the puppet many times before children ask it. Keep the ask-and-say short and paired — no long waiting. Praise every attempt at the full answer.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Where's number (two)?”;
  • answer “It's on (blue)”;
  • greet and reply — “I'm fine, thank you.”

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — review sounds h r m d g o u l f (Jolly gesture) → blend CVC (d-o-g → dog) with a meaning picture → speed-read p.10–16. Reading from zero, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I ask Where's number two? — do you tell me a number or a colour?” → a colour!
“How are you? — sad face or happy answer?” → I'm fine, thank you! (happy)

Assumptions: Family, numbers 1–6 and colours are fresh from L1 (checked in the warm review). The question Where's number…? is understood receptively; producing it is today's stretch. Reading continues from L1. Support with the mat & the coursebook-app wheel.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! How are you today? Show me a happy face… a sleepy face! Faces round the circle.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

▶ Family & count. flash the family cards — chorus + wave Hello, Mimi!; count the Big Wheel pods 1→6 with fingers.

Colour chorus. point to a pod → whole class chorus the colour: It's on blue! It's on yellow! Warms the L1 numbers+colours before we add the question.

 family & number flashcards · Big Wheel mat
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lessons 4–6 — sounds h · r · m · d · g · o · u · l · f. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: h-e-n → hen, d-o-g → dog, m-u-d → mud — sweep the sounds together + a picture. ▶ Sound hop — children hop to the sound-card Speaky calls, do the action, then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.10–16 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read go · no · so together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.10–16 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–26′
Curiosity input · the question
carpet

Guess & reveal. Cover a pod with your hand: Ooh… where's number three? What colour? Lift it slowly — children guess the colour, then chorus the answer It's on orange! Big shrug-gesture for Where's…?, big colour-gesture for It's on….

Choral first. point to a pod → whole class asks-and-answers together: Where's number two? — It's on blue! Model 4–5 numbers with the puppet.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the interactive Big Wheel (numbers 1–6 on coloured pods) on screen — children point & answer It's on red!. The spinning wheel itself comes from the coursebook app; our songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

26–31′
Game · Mingle (4)
carpet, standing

▶ Mingle Genki Mingle: everyone walks with a number card. Speaky calls Make groups of THREE! — children find friends and make groups of that number, then hold up their cards and answer It's on orange!

A calm-ish moving game on numbers; regroup fast so nobody waits.

 number cards 1–6 · Speaky
31–37′
Game · Cockroach Game (14) ⚡
carpet

▶ Cockroach Game Genki Cockroach Game on «How are you?»: draw an evolution ladder on the board (egg → chick → bird…). Two children do Hello! How are you? — I'm fine, thank you!; the winner climbs one step. Everyone chants the exchange each round — lots of energy, everyone reacting.

💪 Support: copy the greeting + thumbs-up. ⭐ Extension: answer with a feeling: I'm sleepy / happy!

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Ask your partner one Big Wheel question — full answer! — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can ask “Where's number…?” I can say “I'm fine!” colour one star.

Super asking, everyone! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
