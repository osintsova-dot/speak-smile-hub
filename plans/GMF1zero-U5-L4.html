Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия с нуля) · 60 min · New vocab + structure together (PPP + TPR) · carpet + desks

Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 action verbs — fly, swim, run, walk, climb, jump (Pupil's Book p. 54) — and say what animals can & can't do with can / can't: “Birds can fly. · Cows can't climb. · Horses can swim. · Ducks can't fly.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — farm animals (L1), “Can you see a…?” (L2), the story Animal magic where cows can't fly and this sheep can climb trees (L3), Genki «I'm a superhero!» (I can jump/run/swim/fly) — woven into today's target: the animals they know now DO things. Phonics — Phonics Hero ур.28 (a) первое знакомство: sound /ю:/ in three houses ue · ew · u-e (blue, new, cube; trace blue, new); sing the coursebook song Cats can't fly (CD2 44); value: respect what every animal can do.
  • Personal. Keep the strong can vs contracted can't clear; give an ICQ before every game; every task keeps ALL children responding (own bingo card / show-me gestures / all groups play at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name & mime the 6 actions (fly · swim · run · walk · climb · jump);
  • say what an animal can & can't do: “Birds can fly. Cows can't climb.”;
  • make a simple animal riddle: “I can swim. I can't climb. What am I?”;
  • trace & read the first ue/ew/u-e words (blue, glue, new, cube, cute).
Target language
Lexis
flyswimrunwalkclimbjump (recycled: cow, horse, sheep, duck, chicken, goat, dog, cat, frog, bird · colours)
Structure
(Birds) can fly. · (Cows) can't climb. · (Horses) can swim. · (Ducks) can't fly. · Riddle: I can (swim). I can't (fly). What am I? · Talk Partners: Cats can fly. — No! · Cats can swim. — Yes!
Form
subject + can / can't + verb (no -s, no “to”): Birds can fly (NOT “flies / to fly”). Strong can /kæn/ in affirmative, can't /kɑːnt/ stressed in negative.
Pron. watch
keep the clear -n't in can't; “favourite-style” quick can when positive; /ю:/ one sound in blue, new.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.28 (a) первое знакомство: sound /ю:/ — three houses ue (blue, glue) · ew (new, chew) · u-e (cube, cute); trace blue · new (тетрадь стр.77) · sight words: of, also.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Bird card: “Can a bird fly?” (flap!) → “Yes! Birds can fly.” Cow card: “Can a cow fly?” → “No! Cows can't fly.”
“Birds can fly or flies?” → fly! (no -s after can).
“blue — how many sounds in ue?” → ONE /ю:/ — blue, glue.

Assumptions: farm animals + Can you see a…? solid from L1–L3; fly/swim/climb already FELT in the story (Animal magic) — today they get named & drilled with the full set; Genki «I'm a superhero!» gives the “I can + verb” pattern. Emergent readers → picture bingo & memory, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Birds can flies / can to fly”Clap-chant: Birds (clap) can (clap) FLY! — one verb, no -s, no “to”; fix instantly, redrill.
Drop -n't: “Cows can fly” (means the opposite!)Big head-shake + arms-cross on can't; exaggerate Cows can't FLY.
Confuse walk ↔ run / climb ↔ jumpOne clear TPR gesture each; contrast pairs slowly (walk vs run, climb vs jump).
Dictation panic (new /ю:/ houses)Sound-cards warm-up first (show-me), only trace + read today — no full dictation (first meeting).
Memory game: one child hogs the cardsStrict turn circle in the group of 4; “flip two, everyone says the sentence together”.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Full plate — pick, don't cram. Cognitive load: only the 6 verbs + the chunk “Birds can fly / Cows can't climb” are truly NEW — the animals, colours & fly/swim/climb (from the story) are recycling, so let the verbs + can/can't carry the challenge (gestures, clap-chant, head-shake on can't). The phonics is FIRST MEETING — trace & read only, no dictation; if behind, cut to a fast show-me. Buffers to drop first if short: Memory round 2 (adding colour), the 2nd Bingo round, the exit-ticket line-up. Better every child says one clear Birds can fly. Cows can't climb. than six stages half-done. Offline reserve: every 🎮 has a paper twin.

Presentation · Actions + can/can't
carpet

Present the 6 verbs (PB p. 54 ex. 1, CD2 43). One clear TPR gesture each: fly (arm wings) · swim (paddle) · run (fast arms) · walk (slow march) · climb (climbing hands) · jump (bounce). Listen, mime & say with the CD. Choral drill ×3, then 2–3 children solo.

Add the animal (can / can't). Hold a bird card + flap: Birds CAN fly! (👍) Hold a cow card + arms-cross: Cows CAN'T fly! (👎) Model 4–5: horses can swim · ducks can't climb · frogs can jump · fish can't walk. Class shows 👍/👎 then says the whole sentence.

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): “What's this?” spinner — can it? · 🎮 whats-this-spinner-u5 Screen: a wheel of animals; arrow spins & lands on one; a verb icon appears (fly / swim / climb…). Mechanics: Predict first — Can it fly? Show me 👍/👎! — everyone votes, then the class says the full sentence; a named child confirms. Language: Birds can fly! · Cows can't fly! ✅ ГОТОВ — reuse the animal spinner (add verb icons). 📴 Offline: flip an animal card + a verb card together, class says the sentence. 💪 Support: keep both gesture + picture up; child says just the verb, teacher frames the animal. ⭐ Extension: add a second clause — Ducks can swim but they can't climb.

▶ Can/Can't Memory
desks, groups of 4

▶ Can/Can't Memory (card game; Mido №45 “Concentration” = our Игротека «Мемори»; all groups play simultaneously). Each group of 4: 12 cards face-down — 6 animal cards + 6 verb cards.

Turn order round the group: flip an animal + a verb, make the sentence together — A bird… fly… Birds CAN fly! — a match! or A cow… fly… Cows CAN'T fly — no match! A true “can” pair is kept; a “can't” pair goes back. ICQ: Do you keep “Cows can fly”? (→ No — that's not true!)

Most true pairs wins a 🦝 sticker.
