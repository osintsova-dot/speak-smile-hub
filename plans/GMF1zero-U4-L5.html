Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated all Unit 4 language — the 8 foods + 6 fruits, “I like / I love / I don't like…”, “Do you like…? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” — and can use it confidently in the review tasks and the quiz.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics Hero ур.25 (b) закрепление — /и:/ ee·ea·e-e·y: match + dictation + story «Счастливый щенок» (тетрадь стр.70–71); sight words Повт. Brown; self-assessment (My progress traffic light, AB p. 45); listening for detail (PB p. 48).
  • Personal. Run the flagship quiz by PIES: every child writes/shows every answer (Showdown), the responder is picked by Numbered Heads — no “captains answer everything”; keep the reward moment short so language time wins.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all the foods & fruits without support;
  • say “I like / I love / I don't like…”;
  • ask & answer “Do you like…? — Yes, I do / No, I don't”;
  • read /и:/ words (see, tree, sea, tea, happy);
  • self-assess with the traffic light — ready for the Unit 4 test.

33–51′
🏆 FLAGSHIP QUIZ with reward
📺 teams · projector

Set-up (2 min). 3 teams of 4, numbered heads 1–4, one mini-whiteboard EACH. Quiz grid on screen — categories: Food 🍕 · Fruit 🍒 · Do you like? ❓ · Sounds 🔤. Rules + ICQ: Who writes? — Everybody! When do you show? — On “Showdown!”

Round flow (Kagan Showdown + Numbered Heads — all answer every question). Question up (e.g. food card: What's this? · a food + face: Do you like fish? · sound: Write the /и:/ word — t-r-ee! · read a /и:/ word). Every child writes/draws → Showdown! → all boards up → call a number: Number 3! — that child answers aloud in a full sentence.

Predict (thinking). On a half-hidden food, before revealing: Look — just a slice! Is it pizza or cake? Write your guess! Then reveal — Were you right?

Reward shot. Correct = the answering child takes the shot: animated bowling / football on screen — knocked pins = team points, class counts in English: One, two, three… five! (No projector → soft-toy pins + ball.) Rotate teams; over ~10–12 questions every child throws at least once.

Support ▸ a food / word bank stays visible so weaker writers copy, not freeze.   Extension ▸ when a strong pupil is “Number”, require the full answer — No, I don't. I don't like lemons.
🔨 Flagship interactive · projector · BUILD · “Unit 4 Team Quiz + Reward”  🔨 BUILD
Screen 1 — Grid: 4×3 board of numbered tiles under the 4 category headers (Food 🍕 / Fruit 🍒 / Do you like? ❓ / Sounds 🔤). Tap a tile → question. Used tiles grey out.
Screen 2 — Answer: “Show your boards!” + a 5-second timer; ✔ / ✗ to score; a full-answer model appears for choral echo.
Screen 3 — Reward: bowling — child “rolls”, pins fall (or a football penalty reskin). One shot = +points.
Screen 4 — Scoreboard: 3 team bars, huge numbers from the back row, + a “Teamwork ⭐” toggle.
Teacher: host only — YOU tap tiles & call the number so no captain dominates. Offline reserve: printed grid + soft-toy pins + ball. Flagship quiz runs only on Review day.

Finish with totals + teamwork star: Give me five, champions!
