Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to read & say the /k/ sound spelt ck and k (black monkey kayaks quickly on the lake), talk about extreme sports in New Zealand, and read & plan an informal letter — three mini-blocks, one “sports adventure” thread.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics: notice that ck and k spell the same /k/ sound (recognise → say → sort); culture — extreme sports in New Zealand (rafting · zorbing · sand surfing · bungee jumping) + Channel 21 video, 21C: staying safe when doing sport; literacy — text type a letter (address · date · Dear… · First/Next/Finally · Love…), plan & write one.
  • Personal. Three blocks — keep transitions crisp (chant/drum bridges); the monkey tongue-twister is play; cap each block hard so the letter plan actually gets written.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the monkey /k/ tongue-twister and sort ck / k words;
  • name 2–3 New Zealand extreme sports and the safety gear;
  • find the parts of a letter and plan one with a partner.

Structure
The (black) (monkey) (kayaks) (quickly) on the (lake).  ·  You can go (rafting) in New Zealand. You must wear a (helmet).  ·  letter: Dear Grandma, … First, I'm going to… Love, …
Form
/k/ = ck (black, quickly, hockey) AND k (monkey, kayak, lake). Letter: address top-right, date, Dear …, body with First · Next · Finally, Love, (name).
Pron. watch
/k/ clean in black/monkey/kayak; quickly /ˈkwɪkli/; contrast ck (black) vs k (lake) — same sound; zorbing /ˈzɔːbɪŋ/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“black and lake — same last SOUND? Same last LETTERS?” → same sound /k/, different spelling (ck vs k)!
“A letter to Grandma — where do we write the address and the date?” → at the top.

Assumptions: Equipment, going to and adverbs are active (L1–L4). Sound–letter awareness from earlier phonics is enough to enjoy the ck / k noticing (по учебнику). Pupils can read the short letter texts with support.

Block 1 · Phonics /k/
carpet → desks

Listen, read and say (act. 1, CD3 16). PB p.65: “ck” says “k”. The black monkey kayaks quickly on the lake! Listen, then the speed ladder: slow → fast → whisper. Pairs test each other ×3.

Sort ck / k. Two hoops/columns: ck (black, quickly, hockey, neck, back) · k (monkey, kayak, lake, shark, snake, kite). Hold up word cards — every child points/steps to the right hoop BEFORE you place each card.

Notice the rule. black, quickly — the /k/ is written “ck”. monkey, lake — it's just “k”. Same sound, different spelling! (notice, don't lecture).

💪 Support: say the word, teacher shows the hoop. ⭐ Extension: add one more /k/ word to a hoop (e.g. rocket, kangaroo).

✔ Assess: who still reads ck as two sounds? Note them — extra RallyRead next unit.

Block 3 · Literacy · a letter
carpet → desks, pairs

Before/After you read (act. 1–2, CD3 18, p.66). Robert's letter to Grandma & Grandad: Which activities is Robert going to do? — volleyball, kayaking (life jacket + helmet), mountain biking. Reading tip: what things do people write in letters?

▶ Letter scavenger hunt. Race in pairs to find on the letter: Where's the address? Where's the date? How does he begin? How does he end? First hands up read it aloud — everyone scans.

Investigate a text (PB p.67, CD3 19). Laura's letter: Which activity is Laura NOT going to do? (rock climbing — it's scary). Writing tip: Use First, Next and Finally to order your activities!

▶ Plan a letter (Cooperative learning, AB p.64 format). Pairs choose a camp (sports / adventure) and make notes: What type of camp? · When? · Where? · What THREE activities? 2 pairs share one plan aloud.

💪 Support: fill the plan template with word support. ⭐ Extension: write the full letter with First / Next / Finally + Love, (name).
