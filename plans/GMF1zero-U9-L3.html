Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, understand and act out the story “The wobbly tooth” (Pupil's Book pp. 94–95, CD4 13) using key story language: “Are you ready for breakfast? — Yes, I am. I have breakfast every morning.”, “What's the matter? — I've got a wobbly tooth!”, “Let's play volleyball!”, “Can you see my tooth? — No, it isn't in the grass.”, “Magic Bike, you're incredible!”.
  • Subsidiary. Retrieve real prior learning across the whole course, woven into the story: prepositions in / on / under (U1 · U6), body parts tooth · teeth · hands · feet (U3), food & hungry (U4), volleyball (U8) + present-simple daily routines from this unit (I have breakfast every morning). Folded L5: the English sound /θ/ th (tongue twister Three thin tigers…, story words tooth · teeth) + culture — the Tooth Fairy (box under the pillow, comes at night, leaves a coin). Phonics — Phonics Hero свободное чтение: истории Части 4 (тетрадь стр.66–90, «почти настоящие книги»). Values: look after your teeth — brush before bed.
  • Personal. Keep story listening active (gestures, prediction); manage drama noise with Freeze; every child acts in a small group at the same time — no “2 on stage, 10 watching” until the final 30-sec showcase. Give this last story a warm, celebratory send-off.
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they understand the story (number the frames 1–5, answer True/False with thumbs);
  • say key lines in role: “I've got a wobbly tooth!”, “Can you see my tooth? — No, it isn't in the grass.”, “Magic Bike, you're incredible!”;
  • say the /θ/ tongue twister with a clear English th and read a Part-4 story aloud with a partner;
  • name 2–3 things about the Tooth Fairy (box · pillow · night · coin) and ask “Have you got a wobbly tooth? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't” (Values: brush your teeth before bed).

Form
Present simple routine: I have + meal + every morning. · Negative It isn't + in / on / under + the + place. · Have got: I've got / Have you got …? (receptive, recycled from U3). · th + word (initial & final: thin · bath).
Pron. watch
English /θ/ — tongue tip gently between the teeth, soft air out (tooth · teeth · three · thin · Thursday) — not Russian «с/ф/т». Weak in the /ɪn ðə/; stress: It isn't in the GRASS.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
New lines block the story (wobbly tooth · you're incredible)Pre-teach 3 things before listening with picture + gesture (wobbly tooth = wobble a finger, magic bike = sparkle fingers, incredible = thumbs-up “Wow!”).
th said as /t/ or /f/ (“toof / toot”)“Tongue peeps out” mirror trick — tongue between teeth, gentle blow: th–th–three; show-me the tongue; keep it light.
Children call answers over the audio“Listening hands” rule — hands cupped behind ears while the CD plays; talk only when it stops.
A child is upset (“no Tooth Fairy at my home”)Frame it as In Britain… then And in Russia? — warmly celebrate their own custom (мышка). No “right” answer.
Drama turns to chaos (real volleyball)Movements are ON THE SPOT (volleyball = mime a bounce in place); roles fixed by colour badges; “Freeze!” resets.

“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.

“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.

Review → into the story
carpet, circle

Retrieve the WHOLE course, woven into today. Fast “My day” mime chain — you call, ALL do the routine action (from L1–L2): Wake up! Wash your face! Brush your TEETH! Have breakfast! Go to school! Snap the beat.

Show-me — everyone moves (U1 / U6 prepositions). Speaky “loses” his tooth in a little box: Where's my tooth? Is it IN the box? ON the box? UNDER the box? — three gestures: in (hand inside fist) · on (flat on top) · under (below). Call fast, all react.

Body + food quick-fire (U3 · U4). Point to your teeth! Your hands! Your feet! then Are you hungry? What do you have for breakfast? — thumbs / one-word answers.

23–33′
📺 Story — listen & follow
carpet, books/screen

Before you read (PB p. 94). Big story picture on screen / books open. Pairs point & name what they see 15 sec, then chorus. Pre-teach with gesture: wobbly tooth (wobble finger) · magic bike (sparkle fingers) · volleyball (mime a bounce) · incredible (thumbs-up “Wow!”).

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — “What's in Jake's day?” ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 find-it-u9
Screen: the U9 “Find it!” engine on the big story picture. · Pupils: whole class points to their book / calls it — Breakfast! The garden! The volleyball! The magic bike! · Teacher: 🦝 Where's the magic bike? Point! — 4–5 things, quick (find the hidden bike — hook).

Predict (thinking) — before we listen. Jake's tooth is wobbly… I think it will fall out! Who will find it? The dog? Mum? Or… the MAGIC BIKE? Thumbs vote (dog / mum / bike). Let's listen and check your guess!

Listen 1 — global (CD4 13). “Listening hands” behind ears. Teacher points frame by frame; children follow the pictures. Gist check: Does the tooth fall out? Show me! (thumbs) Who finds it — was your guess right?

Detail check — True or False? (thumbs up / down, all react). Jake has got a wobbly tooth. (👍) · They play football in the garden. (👎 — volleyball!) · The tooth is in the grass. (👎 — It isn't in the grass!) · The Magic Bike finds the tooth. (👍) · The tooth is under the bed. (👎 — under his pillow!). Re-point the frame to fix any wrong thumb.

Listen 2 — active (all respond). Play again; children do the story gestures at the right moments: stretch (frame 1) · wobble finger (frame 2) · bounce volleyball (frames 3–4) · hands searching the grass (frame 5) · sparkle fingers magic bike (frames 7–8) · big thumbs-up You're incredible! Stop at frame 5: Is the tooth in the grass? — No!

41–49′
Act out — drama
carpet, groups

Roles. Groups of 4–5: Jake (yellow badge, wobbly tooth) · Dad/Mum (blue) · 2 friends (red + green, volleyball & search) · Magic Bike + dog (purple, sparkle & “Woof!”). Model once with a strong group.

All groups act at the same time. Narrate slowly, frame by frame; every group plays it in their own spot (volleyball ON THE SPOT!): Are you ready for breakfast? — Yes, I have breakfast every morning! · What's the matter? — I've got a wobbly tooth! · Let's play volleyball! · Jake, where's your tooth? — No, it isn't in the grass. · Look — the magic bike! It's under your pillow! — Magic Bike, you're incredible!

Swap roles, run it again — keep it to 2 rounds (Freeze! the moment energy tips). Volunteers: ONE group shows 30 sec — big applause for the Magic Bike's last ride of the course. Values (PB p. 95 ex. 2): Look after your teeth! Brush your ___ before you go to bed. → teeth!

Support: quieter children get the “friend / search” role (short lines + gesture). Extension: confident children play Jake / the Magic Bike and lead.
