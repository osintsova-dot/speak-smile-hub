Pre-primary / early years (~5–6 y.o., pre-readers) · 60 min · Genki recipe · Disco warm-up → phonics → curiosity input → Genki games → song → craft · TPR + puppet · Phonics Hero 2.0 (reading: recognise → blend → speed-read) · carpet + tables
Week 12.10–18.10 · Lesson 12🎵 Song · Katy Kangaroo · No, I want to jump!~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 14 (ee or · p.36–38) · Genki: What Do You Want To Do?(59) · game: What's Left?(40)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act the story song «Katy Kangaroo» (CD1 T28) with actions, and say what they want to do: “No, I don't. I want to jump!” — re-telling «A new friend» through movement.
  • Subsidiary. Order the 6 story cards; learn the song in stages (TB tip); pretend to be Katy Kangaroo; sing Genki «What Do You Want To Do?» (59); Phonics Hero — ee · or (consolidation), speed-read p.36–38; trace ✓/✗ & colour Katy (PB p.8).
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions, then key words (TB tip). Repeat the song several times by changing HOW (loud/quiet/fast). Every child gets a card in the ordering game.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing & act the Katy Kangaroo song;
  • put the 6 story cards in order;
  • say “No, I don't. I want to jump!”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Katy — does she want to draw, or jump?” → jump!
“The song — do we sit still or move?” → move!

Assumptions: Story met in L3 and Do you want to…? understood — today deepens both through song. Reading is at digraphs ee·or. Pre-readers order the story by pictures, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by song repetitionChange the mode each time: whisper it, giant-voice it, super-fast, slow-motion (TB tip).
Story order muddledOrder together first with the big cards on the floor, then children try.
Only a few singGive everyone an action job — sing the actions even without the words.
Cards get grabbedOne card per child; hold it up, place it in the line on your countdown.
Energy too high after songFreeze + a slow breathing verse before the table.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Boing, boing! Ready to be Katy Kangaroo? Knees bent, arms in… and JUMP! Kangaroo bounces, then freeze.

Warm review · last time
carpet

▶ Story flash. re-tell «A new friend» fast: draw (Mimi), sulk (Katy), all JUMP, cheer — Actions. wave arms on each school activity.

Ask & refuse. ask round the circle Do you want to draw? — No, I want to jump!

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 14 (consolidation) — sounds ee · or (letter-teams). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-read faster: b-ee → bee, c-or-n → corn, s-ee-d → seed — sweep the team + a picture. ▶ Word-swat — Speaky says a word; children point / swat it on the page fast.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.36–38 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read why · where together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.36–38 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–26′
Curiosity input · Katy Kangaroo song in stages
carpet, standing

Sing & move. Play the Katy Kangaroo song (CD1 T28). Round 1 — join the ACTIONS only (draw, colour, paint, then JUMP). Round 2 — add the key words. Round 3 — a silly voice (TB tip: learn a song in stages).

Be Katy Kangaroo. Be Katy: knees bent, arms in. Do you want to draw? Do you want to paint? children answer No, I don't. I want to jump! and jump on the spot.

 Katy Kangaroo song (CD1 T28) · coursebook app
26–31′
Game · What's Left? (40)
carpet

▶ What's Left? Genki What's Left?: every child holds the six activity mini-cards. 🦝 Speaky calls an activity to ‘put down’; children find it, name it & turn it over. At the end everyone holds up their last card & shouts it — all together!

Calm & seated; every child handles every card — a gentle listening check, nobody waits.

 activity mini-cards ×6 per child · Speaky
31–37′
Game · Order the story ⚡
carpet

▶ Story line-up. Lay the 6 story cards face-up, mixed. What happens first? Katy is new… then? Together decide first → last; then hand 6 children a card each to stand in order while the class claps the sequence (new girl → doesn't like school → all jump → friends!).

✔ Assess: do they order by the pictures? Prompt with a story question if stuck.

 story cards ×6
37–42′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «How are you, Speaky?» aloud — Speaky feels happy, sad, sleepy… — it mirrors Katy's journey (sad at first → happy at the end). A calm read on our story-song day.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line I'm happy! I'm sad.. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Show each spread — children make the feeling face & echo it; ask Is Katy happy or sad now? to tie it back to the Katy Kangaroo story.

 «How are you, Speaky?» (Speak & Smile Genki story-book · feelings.pdf) · Speaky

Genki reprise. Sing Genki «What Do You Want To Do?» (59) — the grammar match, with big want to actions.
