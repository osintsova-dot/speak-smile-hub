Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 kinds of transport — bus, boat, motorbike, train, helicopter, lorry (Pupil's Book p. 76) — and ask & answer quantities with numbers 11–20: “How many (buses) are there? — There are (fifteen).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — There's a / There are / How many (L2), town places (L1), the story hunt (L3) — woven into a transport count; phonics — Phonics Hero ур.24: /эй/ ai·ay·a-e (three spellings, one sound: train, play, cake — TRAIN = our transport word!, тетрадь стр.67–68); numbers 11–20 in context.
  • Personal. Keep the plural -s clear (“five buses”); give an ICQ before every game; every task keeps ALL children responding (own count card / show-me fingers / all groups play at once).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 6 transport words;
  • count 11–20 and ask/answer “How many buses are there? — There are fifteen.”;
  • read /эй/ words spelt ai·ay·a-e (train, play, cake).

Structure

Form
How many + nouns + are there? → There are + number (+ nouns). Numbers 11–20: teen-endings (thirteen, fourteen…).
Pron. watch
teen vs -ty stress not needed yet, but say the teen clearly; “helicopter” = 4 beats he-li-COP-ter; “lorry” = /ˈlɒri/.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.24: /эй/ ai·ay·a-e — three spellings, ONE sound (train, rain · play, day · cake, name) — read, sort by spelling, write (тетрадь стр.67–68). TRAIN is today's transport word!
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Show a helicopter: “Bus or helicopter?” → helicopter (propeller gesture).
Count 15 buses together: “How many? — Fifteen! There are fifteen buses.”
Phonics: “train — ai · play — ay · cake — a-e. Same sound? → Yes! /эй/!”

Assumptions: There's a / There are / How many secured in L2; numbers 1–10 from Genki, 11–20 met & now consolidated; town places from L1. /эй/ spellings first taught in Часть 4 — ур.24 today (revision-forward). Emergent readers → picture count grids, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).
