  1. Вход: Your mood as ONE emoji on the board — up!
  2. Оформляю как Kagan Showdown: пишут ВСЕ одновременно, доски лицом вниз, по команде No peeking… Boards up! поднимают разом — вижу ответ каждого, отсидеться нельзя.

  4. Чант красного ряда: he-she-it — HAS got it!
  5. Мостик: You can SAY things about people. But can you FIND OUT? I'm thinking of a person in this class… and today you build the question machine.

👦 Дети: рисуют эмодзи, флипают, чинят апостроф. Ожидаемо: чисто после P3. Типичная ошибка: hasn't→haven't у she — красный ряд чинит.

ЧТО ДЕЛАЕМ: дети-слова: «Have» физически перебегает вперёд — рождается вопрос; хором с интонацией ↗

👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Раздаю карточки-слова пятёрке: you / have / got / a / pet ? — строю утверждение-порядок.
  2. Хлопок: The QUESTION word runs to the front! — «Have» перебегает: Have you got a pet?
  3. Класс читает хором с ↗. Прогоняю 4 набора (has your uncle got a beard? · have they got blue eyes?…), карточки у новых детей.
  4. Жест a/any: один палец = a/an (одна вещь), растопыренные = any (много): Have you got ANY cousins? Has she got A dog?
📺 Показываем: 4–5 наборов карточек-слов · пространство у доски
👦 Дети: физически строят вопросы, «Have» бегает, класс читает с ↗. Ожидаемо: смех + телесная инверсия. Типичная ошибка: бежит не тот («got») — класс кричит «Have! Have!».

  • Main aim. By the end, learners can ask have got questions with short answers (Act 1, 45′) and act a ‘talking about photos’ dialogue with Key phrases (Act 2, 45′).

  • give polite short answers
  • use a/an vs any correctly in questions
  • act a photo dialogue with 3 phrase groups

Target language
Lexis

Structure
Have you got any cousins? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. · Has she got curly hair? — No, she hasn't. · Who's this? That's my auntie. That's a great photo!
Form
Have/Has jumps before the subject, got stays; short answers with have/has (never «Yes, I've»); a/an + singular, any + plural.
Pron. watch
questions rise ↗; rhythm: Yes-I-HAVE! No-she-HASn't!; opinion phrases with enthusiasm.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Have you got a sister? — can I answer just ‘Yes’?” → polite English wants Yes, I have.
“Yes, I've. — OK?” → ✗! Short affirmative answers never contract.
“Have you got ANY brother?” → ✗ — any goes with PLURAL: any brothers?

Assumptions: have got statements + possessive 's systematic after P3; questions heard receptively in Seven Families (P1!). New: inversion mechanics, etiquette, a/an vs any, opinion phrases.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
No inversion (“You have got a pet?”)Human-questions game: the child holding «Have» physically RUNS to the front — body remembers.
«Yes, I've» slipRule card ✓/✗ on board; buzzer game — class softly buzzes, speaker self-corrects.
any/a confusionGesture: one finger = a/an, spread fingers = any; drill 4 items on boards before writing.
Shyness at PERFORMPhotos may be drawn/invented; all pairs perform simultaneously first, volunteers go public; applause protocol.
No videosRWS: живая сценка по скрипту TB pp.138–139 с сильным учеником, задачи те же.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
