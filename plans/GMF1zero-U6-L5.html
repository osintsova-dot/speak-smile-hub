Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated all Unit 6 language — the playground objects (slide, swing, roundabout, seesaw, climbing frame) + nature words (grass, flower, tree, bush, rock), “Where's (Beth)? — He's / She's (on the swing)”, “Where are (Molly and Jake)? — They're (behind / in front of / next to / between / near) the (tree)” and the prepositions of place — and can use it confidently in the review tasks and the quiz.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics Hero ур.31 (b) закрепление — the /эа/ houses air·ear·are + sort c·g·ph, dictation (тетрадь стр.87–90) + the three big Part-2 stories; sight words review — Brown set; self-assessment (My progress traffic light, AB p. 65); listening for detail (PB p. 68 ex. 1).
  • Personal. Run the flagship quiz by PIES: every child writes/shows every answer (Showdown), the responder is picked by Numbered Heads — no “captains answer everything”; keep the reward moment short so language time wins.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all the park objects and answer “What's this?” without support;
  • ask & answer “Where's…?” with the right pronoun (He's/She's);
  • ask & answer “Where are they?” with the prepositions (behind · next to · between…);
  • read the /эа/ houses & the tricky c·g·ph words;
  • self-assess with the traffic light — ready for the Unit 6 test.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
One child: Where's Beth? → She's on the swing. Two children: Where are they? → They're behind the tree. — one vs TWO fingers.
Quiz rule ICQ: Who writes the answer — one child or EVERYBODY? → everybody, on their own board!

Assumptions: all Unit 6 language met in L1–L4; the tricky letters c·g·ph & the /эа/ houses started in L4 (ур.31a) — today we sort & dictate & read the big stories. Teams of 4 with numbered members (1–4) are routine. The flagship quiz with reward runs only on Review day. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

33–51′
🏆 FLAGSHIP QUIZ with reward
📺 teams · projector

Set-up (2 min). 3 teams of 4, numbered heads 1–4, one mini-whiteboard EACH. Quiz grid on screen — categories: Park 🛝 · Where's…? 👧 · Where are they? 👥 · Sounds 🔤. Rules + ICQ: Who writes? — Everybody! When do you show? — On “Showdown!”

Round flow (Kagan Showdown + Numbered Heads — all answer every question). Question up (e.g. picture: What's this? · doll: Where's Beth? · two dolls: Where are they? · sound: Write the house — chair! · read a /эа/ word). Every child writes/draws → Showdown! → all boards up → call a number: Number 3! — that child answers aloud in a full sentence.

Predict (thinking). On a half-hidden scene: Look — just a tree! Are they behind it or next to it? Write your guess! Then reveal — Were you right?

Reward shot. Correct = the answering child takes the shot: animated bowling / football on screen — knocked pins = team points, class counts: One, two, three… five! (No projector → soft-toy pins + ball.) Rotate teams; over ~10–12 questions every child throws at least once.

Support ▸ a park / preposition bank stays visible so weaker writers copy, not freeze; accept one correct word.   Extension ▸ when a strong pupil is “Number”, require the full sentence — They're between the two rocks.
🔨 Flagship interactive · projector · BUILD · “Unit 6 Team Quiz + Reward”  🔨 BUILD
Screen 1 — Grid: 4×3 board of numbered tiles under the 4 category headers. Tap a tile → the question (picture / doll / two-dolls scene / audio / /эа/ word). Used tiles grey out.
Screen 2 — Answer: big “Show your boards!” + a 5-sec timer; tap ✔ / ✗ to score; a full-sentence model appears for choral echo.
Screen 3 — Reward: bowling — child “rolls”, pins fall (or football). One shot = +points.
Screen 4 — Scoreboard: 3 team bars, huge numbers, + a “Teamwork ⭐” toggle.
Teacher: host only — YOU tap tiles & call the number so no captain dominates. Self-contained HTML slide (S&S purple/honey). Offline reserve: printed grid + soft-toy pins + ball. Flagship quiz runs only on Review day.

Finish with totals + teamwork star: Give me five, champions!
