Prepare 3 · Unit 1 “It's a challenge!” · Page 2 (SB pp. 12–13) — Personal details & Talking about yourself
A2 teens (~12–14 y.o.) · 90 min · Skills: Vocabulary + Reading + Listening + Speaking · seats + pair & team stations
Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 5A2 Key · Speaking Part 1~8–12 pupils · pairs / teams of 3–4Brain-break ≈ min 43
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to give and ask for personal details (name, age, address, contacts, language) and answer A2 Key Speaking Part 1 questions about themselves in full-sentence chunks, spelling names and emails aloud.
  • Subsidiary. Reading for gist & detail (school award web page); listening for specific information (contact details); revise/say the alphabet and spell in context; recycle Unit 1 personality language (P1) inside a self-introduction.
  • Personal. Keep TTT low; ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; delay error correction to a feedback slot; ensure every learner speaks in each pair stage (no “2 speak, 10 watch”).
By the end, learners can…
  • ask & answer 7 personal-detail questions (What's your surname? — My surname is…);
  • spell a name and an email aloud (How do you spell that? · @ = “at”, . = “dot”);
  • listen for names, numbers and addresses and note them down;
  • give an extended answer to Speaking Part 1 questions (2+ details, not one word).

Structure
What's your (first name / surname / email address)? · How old are you? · Where do you live? · What do you speak at home? · What's your phone number? · How do you spell that? — My … is …
Form
What's = What is + your + noun? → answer with the stem My … is … / I'm … (years old). Spelling: letter names A–Z · @ = “at” · . = “dot” · “double L”.
Pron. watch
connected What's your…? /wɒtʃɔː/; letter-name vowel groups (/eɪ/ A H J K · /iː/ B C D E G · /e/ F L M N S X Z · /aɪ/ I Y); read email as “at / dot”, phone digit by digit + “double”.
Skills focus
Reading: web page (Duke of Edinburgh's Award), gist → detail (SB p.12). Listening: friends choosing activities + Finn's contact details (SB p.13). Speaking: A2 Key Part 1 — personal & free-time questions (SB p.13 → EXAM p.133).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is your surname your first name or your family name?” → family name.
“In an email, do we say ‘at’ or ‘a’ for @?” → at. And “.” ? → dot.
“First language — a school subject, or the language you learnt first / speak at home?” → learnt first / speak at home.

Assumptions: learners know the alphabet letter names (revised & sharpened here), read short texts, and from P1 can already describe personality & use present simple/continuous to talk about themselves — today applies that to a full self-introduction. Grouping: pairs for info-gap & exam rehearsal; teams of 3–4 for Quiz-Quiz-Trade (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Mix up first name / surname / first languageColour-code the three on the My Profile sheet; drill “family name = surname”.
Forget letter names or @/dot when spellingAlphabet drill (SB ex 3–4) + spelling relay; model at / dot / double.
Say phone/email too fast; no clarificationPre-teach & require How do you spell that? / Can you repeat that? in the info-gap.
One-word exam answersRequire the stem My … is … + one extra detail; use the tiered frames.
Unknown words in the web page (award, volunteering)Gist task first; guess from context; do not pre-teach every word.
Classroom management — signals
“Eyes here — 3, 2, 1.” — countdown to full attention & silence.
Timer on the board — every pair/team task gets a visible countdown.
“Show me” — thumbs up / to the side / down = got it / not sure / lost.
“English only” 🖐 — raised hand = switch back to English.
Volume control — raise/lower arm = louder/quieter.
“Pens down, look up” — reset before instructions.

🔄 Brain-break · spelling stir
whole class, up

▶ Take Off – Touch Down (spelling): you call a letter — stand if it's in the /eɪ/ family (A H J K), sit if not. 8–10 fast rounds — every learner moves every time.

Then ▶ Back-spell relay (2 teams): you say a word (name / email word), teams race to write it letter-by-letter on the board saying the letter names aloud — at and dot count. Fastest correct spelling scores.

64–78′
🎯 Speaking · A2 Key Part 1
pairs → new pairs

Frame the exam task. SB p.13 “Prepare for the exam · Speaking Part 1”. In Part 1 the examiner asks about you. Answer in a full sentence and add one more detail.

Tiered answer frames (from academic-language-sentence-frame-generator — put on the board, learners pick their level):

Emerging: My name is … . I'm … years old. I live in … .
Developing: My surname is … — that's … (spell). In my free time I … , and I usually … .
Secure: Well, my first language is … , but I'm also learning … . At the weekend I often … because … .

▶ SB ex 1 — pairs interview each other & complete the exam form (First name · Surname · Age · Address · Email · Phone), spelling surnames aloud. ▶ SB ex 2 — ask & answer the School & Free time questions (homework, favourite subject, free time, who with, last weekend).

▶ Numbered Heads to nominate: call a number → that learner reports one fact about their partner — keeps everyone accountable.
