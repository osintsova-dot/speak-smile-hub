Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name four MORE places in town — supermarket · restaurant · bookshop · park — and invite & answer: “Do you want to go to the zoo? — Yes, please. / No, thanks.”
  • Subsidiary. Learn quiet ‘inside’ voices (bookshop/museum/cinema) vs louder ‘outside’ voices (park/swimming pool); sing the language song «Do you want to go to the zoo?» (CD T19) with actions; play Do you want to go to the…? (bag game) & What's Left? (40); Phonics Hero — a digraph dictation review, speed-read p.46–51.
  • Personal. Model Do you want to go to the zoo? many times with the puppet before children say it. Keep speaking short & choral first, then paired — no long waiting. Praise every place named and every polite Yes, please / No, thanks.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name four new places (supermarket · restaurant · bookshop · park);
  • invite a friend — “Do you want to go to the zoo?”;
  • answer politely “Yes, please. / No, thanks.”

Structure

Form
Invitation chunk Do you want to go to the + place? → polite Yes, please. / No, thanks. A follow-up: Let's go today!
Pron. watch
rising Do you want to go to the zoo? ↗; polite, warm Yes, please. / No, thanks.; keep supermarket / restaurant as smooth chunks.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — a digraph dictation review (Part 2, lessons 13–18): Speaky says a word, children point to / trace the right digraph (ch sh th · ai ee oa · ou oi · ar er), then speed-read p.46–51. Reading review, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“In a bookshop — quiet inside voice or shouting?” → quiet inside voice!
“I say Do you want to go to the park? — do I tell you, or ask you?” → ask me — I answer yes or no!

Assumptions: The six places from L1 are fresh (checked in the warm review) and re-used. The four new places are new; the invitation Do you want to go to the…? is understood receptively — producing it politely is today's stretch. Reading continues from L1 in the Phonics Hero workbook.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Confuses supermarket / shopping centreAnchor: supermarket = food & shopping trolley; shopping centre = LOTS of shops together; mime a trolley vs walking round many shops.
Says only the place, not the invitePuppet models the frame Do you want to go to the… PARK?; child echoes with the mime.
Forgets please / thanksAlways model the polite words with a nod (Yes, please!) / a wave (No, thanks!) — children copy the gesture.
Same partner every timeMusic on = walk, music off = new partner (Mix-Pair).
Waiting = wrigglingEveryone holds a place card and answers chorally, then pairs — nobody just watches.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero digraph dictation · Part 2 (ур.13–18) — sounds ch sh th · ai ee oa · ou oi · ar er. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Speaky says a word (ship, rain, boat, coin, star) — children point to / trace the digraph they hear, then blend the whole word with a picture. ▶ Digraph hop — hop to the digraph Speaky calls (sh / ai / oi), then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.46–51 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read saw · put together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Curiosity input · four new places + inside/outside voices
carpet

Guess the new ones! Slide out the four new cards one by one: Where else can we go in town? Guess! Reveal + mime + echo (supermarket · restaurant · bookshop · park).

Inside / outside voices. Where do we use a quiet inside voice? (bookshop · museum · cinema) Where can we use a louder outside voice? (park · swimming pool) — whisper vs cheer for each place card.

Choral first. Point to the flashcards and model the invite, then ask: Do you want to go to the park? — Yes, please! Let's go today! children answer chorally with you.
