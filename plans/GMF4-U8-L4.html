Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name places of work and define jobs & places with relative clauses: “A dentist is a person who works in a clinic. / A garage is a place where a mechanic works.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «In my street» (CD4 02); consolidate the “works in / works at” idea noticed in the story (L3) — now taught properly; discover the rule — who for people, where for places; match each of the 12 jobs to its workplace; a true/false relative-clause game (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on the full relative clause (not just “dentist → clinic”); model each place with a picture & the matching job; keep the game moving — short turns, everyone speaks.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 places of work (clinic · salon · garage · factory · workshop · office);
  • make sentences with who (people) and where (places);
  • match jobs and workplaces and check them true/false.

Form
who (people) + verb: A dentist is a person who works in… · where (places) + subject + verb: A garage is a place where a mechanic works.
Pron. watch
salon /ˈsælɒn/; garage /ˈɡærɑːʒ/ or /ˈɡærɪdʒ/; factory /ˈfæktri/ (2 syll.); clinic /ˈklɪnɪk/; the relative who /huː/ vs where /weə/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A dentist is a person ___ works in a clinic — who or where?” → who (a dentist is a person).
“A clinic is a place ___ a dentist works — who or where?” → where (a clinic is a place).

Assumptions: “works in/at” was met in the story (L3); the 6 places are new but very picturable and pair 1-to-1 with known jobs. The switch to who / where relative clauses is the key challenge. Readers can read short place words. (Book teaches 6 places: clinic · salon · garage · factory · workshop · office — note КТП listed a different set.)

27–37′
Job–place match game
desks, pairs → 2 teams

Model first. Show a jumbled set of job cards + place cards. Ask 2 pupils publicly: Where does a carpenter work? — A carpenter is a person who works in a workshop.

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (jobs ↔ places). Each child holds a job OR a place card; mingle → pair → make the who/where sentence, then TRADE. 5–6 exchanges, new partner every time.

▶ Listen & match (team relay). You call a job — teams race to hold up the matching place card & say the full sentence. Point per correct.

✔ Assess: circulate — is it who/where correct + full relative clause? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: cards have the word printed + an icon. ⭐ Extension: add: A dentist looks after your teeth.

 job + place cards · ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade
37–46′
True or false? · who / where
carpet, 2 teams

▶ True or false game (act. 4, Talk Partners → teams). You (then a pupil) say a sentence — A dentist is a person who works in a salon. — teams huddle 5 sec, then Show me! thumbs up/down. If FALSE, they must say the correct sentence: A dentist is a person who works in a clinic! Point per correct fix.

ICQ: If it's false, do you just say “false” — or fix it? (→ fix it with the full sentence!)
