📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. An honest check on the unit: vocabulary (seasons & weather + compound nouns), comparative and superlative, modal verbs of obligation / permission / prohibition, listening, a short set of instructions.
  • Subsidiary. The test strategy BEFORE it starts (time management, the order of the tasks); feedback straight afterwards; reflection on the growth from the P1 gate to today.
  • Personal. ⚠️ There is NO official Macmillan pack for Unit 4 in the tests folder (only U1 and U8; 'A2 Progress Test', 'Mid-Year' and 'End-of-Year' belong to another course, Close-up A2 — do not use them). Unit 4 test — specification (DRAFT: the official Macmillan A2 U4 pack is not in the tests folder yet)
PartContents & marking
Vocabulary 1seasons & weather: sort 12 words into three baskets + build the adjective from the noun (format of WB p.28 ex.1–2 and p.34 Vocabulary review ex.1) — 10
Vocabulary 2compound nouns: build 8 compound nouns from halves and put them into sentences (format of WB p.30 ex.1 and p.34 ex.2) — 10
Grammar 1comparative & superlative: a table of forms with the spelling + 6 sentences (format of WB p.29 ex.1–2 and p.35 Grammar review ex.1) — 10
Grammar 2modal verbs: choose can / can't / must / mustn't from a picture sign + build a question and a short answer (format of WB p.31 ex.1, 4 and p.35 ex.3) — 10
Listeninga briefing / the rules of a place (format of track 27): order of the pictures + 4 details — 5
Writingmini instructions of 4–5 lines, 'what to take and how to pack it', with too/also, one comparative form and one modal (the P6 scheme) — 5

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
