Week 14.09–20.09 · Lesson 4✨ NEW · 12 places in a school~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.8 · AB p.6
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 places in a school and say what our school has: “There's a (gym) in our school. / There isn't a (music room).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «My school is cool» (CD1 12) & the vocabulary chant (CD1 11); brainstorm places (Learning to learn); preview the Wh-question “Where do you (play the drums)? — In the (music room)”; early literacy — read the place words and find them in a word-search (AB).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — place-mime is high-energy, plan the freezes. Insist on the article: a gym, a library.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 places in a school;
  • say “There's a … / There isn't a …” about our school;
  • read the place words and match word ↔ picture.

Structure
There's a (gym) in our school. / There isn't a (music room).  ·  preview: Where do you (play the drums)? — In the (music room).
Form
There's = There is + a + place. Negative: There isn't a + place. Watch the article a before every place.
Pron. watch
corridor /ˈkɒrɪdɔː/; gym /dʒɪm/ (soft g!); vegetable /ˈvedʒtəbl/ (3 syllables); silent letters — none, but stress on the first part of compounds (FOOT-ball pitch).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“There's a gym — does our school HAVE a gym or NOT?” → yes, it has one.
“music room — one word or two words for the place?” → two words, one place.

Assumptions: Starter routines are back; pupils know some school things from GMF2 and classroom. The 12 places are largely NEW; There's / There isn't is known receptively — production is today's stretch. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures.

Anticipated problems & solutions

Long compound words (basketball court)Chunk & clap: BAS-ket-ball · COURT; icon on every card.

10–20′
Presentation · 12 places
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD1 11). Each card: What's this? It's… the gym! Choral ×3 + one gesture (gym = lift weights, library = open book, canteen = eat). Contrast pair held together: GYM — GARDEN!

There's / There isn't (act. 4). Board frame: There's a gym in our school. There isn't a swimming pool. Hold each card — class says True/False for OUR school, then the full sentence.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if our school has this place… library! — flash cards fast, everyone moves every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + word. ⭐ Extension: full sentence — There's a big library in our school.

28–36′
Word ↔ picture · Memory
carpet, pairs

▶ Memory · There's a … — in pairs. Each pair: 6 picture cards + 6 WORD cards (readers!) face-down in two rows. Turn one of each; a match is claimed only with a sentence: There's a canteen! Wrong/no article → cards go back. Most pairs wins.

ICQ: Can you keep the cards if you forget the “a”? (→ no — There's A canteen!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair at a time — is the article automatic yet?

 6+6 cards per pair
36–44′
Mime & guess
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Slow-motion place mime. One player mimes what you DO in a place (reading = library); their team guesses the place together: It's the library! Mimer confirms. Point per correct guess; rotate the mimer every round — everyone mimes once.

Whole team must guess together — no solo shouting: One voice, one team!

Our school · speak & write
desks

Speak. ▶ RallyRobin: Take turns: There's a… / There isn't a… — about OUR school.

Write (notebooks / AB p.6 format). Two frames from the board: There's a ___. There isn't a ___. Each child writes 3 true sentences about our school, copying place words. Fast finishers add and.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from 3 word cards. ⭐ Extension: add an adjective: There's a BIG playground.
