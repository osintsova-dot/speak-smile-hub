Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 places in a school (match picture ↔ printed word) and describe their own school using “There's a (gym) / There isn't a (music room) in our school.”
  • Subsidiary. Revise Starter language (alphabet, numbers, “How do you spell…?”) woven into the new topic; spelling & word-recognition of the new lexis; listen for detail (vocabulary chant + song “My school is cool”); pre-writing (School Directory notebook insert).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; hide the drilling inside games so every child is producing, not watching.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name & read the 12 school places;
  • say “There's a… / There isn't a…” about their school;
  • ask & answer “Where do you (play the drums)? — In the (music room).”

Structure
There's a (gym) in our school. · There isn't a (music room).  ·  Where do you (play the drums)? — I (play the drums) in the (music room).
Form
There + 's (is) / isn't + a + place. Contrast affirmative ↔ negative. Connected “There's” /ðeəz/.
Pron. watch
/dʒ/ in gym; stress canteen; corridor /ˈkɒrɪdɔː/; vegetable /ˈvedʒtəbl/ (3 syll.).
Phonics / spelling
Unit's phonics (consonant sound j) is taught at L5 — not today. Today's early-literacy slot = spelling of the place-words (word search + “How do you spell…?” from Starter).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“There's a gym.” → Do we have one? → yes. “There isn't a music room.” → Do we have one? → no.
“Is library a place or a subject?” → a place.

Assumptions: alphabet, numbers 1–100 & “How do you spell…?” are known from the Starter (revision). Pupils are readers → can match & read printed words. Talk Partners & team routines are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4; делим по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Slow reveal (guess-by-a-piece). ▶ Slow Reveal — cover each A4 photo; uncover it slice by slice: What's this place? First correct guess wins the reveal. Model any unknown: It's the school hall. School hall. Choral drill ×3, then 2–3 pupils individually.

22–30′
▶ Memory · teams
teams, on the floor

Controlled practice — picture ↔ printed word (retrieval + dual coding). Each team of 4 gets a set of 24 cards: 12 photos + 12 matching printed words, all face-down in a grid.

▶ Memory (Pelmanism): in turn a player flips two cards. A pair counts only if the player reads the word AND says a full sentence: It's the library. Word-only / silence = flip back, next player. Match → keep the pair, go again. Roles rotate (dealer · reader · checker · scorer).

ICQ: One word — or a whole sentence? (→ a whole sentence!) Most pairs wins. Reset noise with Freeze!

 3 team sets (12 photo + 12 word cards) · ▶ Memory
30–38′
Song + 📓 Notebook
desks

To your tables — let's sing about our school! Play “My school is cool” (CD1 12); pupils point to each place as it's sung, then join the chorus: This is my school. My school is cool!

📓 Interactive notebook — “School Directory” (A5). Hand out GMF3_Unit1_Lesson1_My_School_Is_Cool_A5: pupils cut & glue it in, then, as the song replays, match each action to its place (play the drums → music room; hang your coat → corridor; throw a ball → basketball court), write the place on the line and circle in / on. Quick sticker step: find the Unit 1 stickers, listen and stick the places.

38–45′
▶ Match & Build relay
board, running

Active practice — every child moves. Two/three team lines. Photos scattered on one table; printed-word cards stuck along the board.

▶ Card-grab relay: on Go! the first runner grabs a photo, races to the board, sticks it under the correct word, and shouts the sentence — There's a gym in our school! — then tags the next runner. Wrong match / no sentence = the next teammate fixes it. Fastest correct board wins.

Settle with Freeze! and a group read-back of the finished board.

Round 2 — swap to Where do you (play football)? — I (play football) on the football pitch. Monitor, feed pronunciation of corridor / vegetable garden. Quick round-up: 2–3 pairs share to the class.
