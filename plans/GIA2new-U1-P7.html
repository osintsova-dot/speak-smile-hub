🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The newcomers' first project follows the ready-made steps of the coursebook (THINK→PLAN→CREATE→PRESENT), with a graphic organiser and the discussion phrases on cards. The unit review is built in as stations — «the inspection a school passes before it opens» — test preparation without the word «test». For the presentation the whole crew stands at the board, the speaker holds notes.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Your crew opens a school. The timetable is yours: which subjects? what times? Use the phrases: I'd like to learn… Wait a moment… Then — inspection! Every new school passes four checks before it opens. Pass them, present your school — and the class chooses the one they'd love to study in.”
In simpler words, if the class needs support: The crew opens a school: its own timetable, its own times; before it opens there are four inspections (the review stations); then the presentation, and the class picks its dream school.

WHAT WE DO: 5-minute stations with support: routines + subjects · Present simple · questions + pronouns · capitals + spelling

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Four inspections. Five minutes each. Station cards help you!
  2. Station 1 is Kagan Quiz-Quiz-Trade. First I act out the move with one student in front of the class: I asked from the card → he answered → I praised him → we swapped cards → we moved on to the next person. The answer is on the back of the card, so asking is not frightening.
  3. Every station carries the prompt card from its own pair (a table, a frame, a checklist).
  4. A personal «superpower list»: whatever did not work, I write down for myself.
📺 On screen: station cards · timer
👦 Students do: they revise the unit with support. Expect: the scaffolds on the walls work — they recognise their own materials

WB Unit 1 · the test is next pair (announced gently)
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can design and present an ideal-school timetable following the project steps, and revise the whole unit at four stations.
  • Subsidiary. Super skill: pros and cons before deciding (simplified: 2 pluses per subject); the project phrases from cards; Quick review as stations, with support.
  • Personal. The project follows the coursebook steps, I do not cut them; the stations carry the prompt cards from previous pairs. I call the gap list «the superpower list» — the word test is not mentioned until the end of the pair.
By the end, students can…
  • choose subjects with reasons (two pluses each)
  • use project phrases in the crew
  • present a timetable with the crew (speaker with notes)
  • pass four revision stations

Do we choose the subject first or the reasons? → 2 pluses first, then the choice.
Can the speaker read from a sheet? → yes, in our class the sheet is a friend.
Are the stations a test? → no, they are the inspection a school passes before it opens.

Assumptions: SB pp.21–23, KTP pair 11. The first full project of the line; success = you went through all the steps, not «the best pitch».

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The discussion slides into RussianThe phrase cards are on the table; the rule: a decision is only made with a phrase from a card.
The stations get squeezed out by the projectThe stations sit firmly in the middle of the pair, on a timer, with support — they earn no fewer points than the project.
Shyness at the presentationThe whole crew stands together, the speaker holds notes, and the audience listens with a task (catch your favourite subject).
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
