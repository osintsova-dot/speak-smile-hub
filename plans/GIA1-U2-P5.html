🎯 GATE · apostrophe crime scene
desks · solo

WHAT WE DO: 2 minutes: they rewrite a text with 8 apostrophe “crimes”; each fix is marked: owner or verb

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I put up the text in lower case: bens train is at 6:35. hes 13 and hes got glasses. my brothers room is small. lucys cat is funny. dont be late!
  2. I say: Rewrite. Find all EIGHT apostrophe crimes — and for each one say its JOB: owner or verb. Two minutes!
  3. Key + breakdown: Ben's (owner) · he's = he is (verb) · he's got (verb) · brother's/brothers' (owner!) · Lucy's (owner) · don't (verb).
  4. The turn: One sign, two jobs. Today's product is judged on THIS.
📺 On screen: the crime-text slide → key · an “owner / verb” table on the board (stays up) (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they rewrite at speed, mark the job of every sign. Expected: 6–8 of 8; brothers' sparks debate — excellent. Typical error: hes → “he's” with no understanding — the “owner or verb?” question out loud.

13–24′
Sprint: dissecting the model email
⚡ Base sprint · dissect the model
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: Joshua's email at speed: ex.1 choose (who/why) → ex.2 paragraph order = the 5-move skeleton → ex.3 fix the facts on mini-boards

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Sprint ex.1: Read Joshua's email — 90 seconds. What is it: plans, HELP, or a new friend? (key: b — asks to help).
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: everyone writes AT THE SAME TIME, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift together — I see everyone's answer, nobody can hide.
  3. Ex.2 → the skeleton on the board: ① hello → ② favour + problem → ③ describe Ben → ④ thanks → ⑤ sign (TB key: c·e·b·a·d).
  4. Ex.3 on mini-boards: all 5 sentences are FALSE — we fix them with detail: Ben isn't from London — he's from MANCHESTER! The train is at 6:35!
  5. I log it: Details win favours: the exact train, the exact time, the glasses.
📺 On screen: the SB p.32 · board: the 5-move skeleton (stays up) · mini-boards
👦 Students do: they dissect the model at speed, fix the facts with two moves. Expected: double speed. Typical error: move ② skipped in the retell — “what must granddad DO?”

24–33′
Apostrophe surgery (the two jobs of the sign)
🔥 Challenge · apostrophe surgery
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: Timed RoundRobin: ex.4–5 (find every apostrophe in the email + full forms) → a blitz p.33 ex.6: 10 contractions on mini-boards, 10 sec each

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Ex.4: Hunt EVERY apostrophe in the email. Which ONE shows possession? (TB key: Ben's parents; the trick: “Ben's 13” = Ben IS!).
  2. This is Kagan Timed RoundRobin: strictly round the circle against the timer, one turn — one person, no skipping: Clockwise, one each — keep it moving, no skips!
  3. Ex.5 orally in a chain: contraction → full form.
  4. Blitz ex.6 (p.33): 10 rounds on mini-boards, 10 sec each: She's 14 · Don't talk · I've got two cousins · They aren't English · He hasn't got brown eyes · It's Monday · I'm not her sister · He isn't a teacher · You aren't in this class · We're from Istanbul.
  5. Rounds 6–10 — STANDING (a movement break in a writing pair): everyone stands up, a clean board = you sit down; the last one standing gets a choral rescue check.
  6. Scored in streaks: 3 clean in a row = ×2.
📺 On screen: the SB pp.32–33 · mini-boards · timer
👦 Students do: they hunt for the signs, explain each one's job, blitz the contractions. Expected: “Ben's 13 — that's IS!” — the pair's insight. Typical error: “their's” out of nowhere — “their never carries an apostrophe.”
✔ Assess: 2 live blitz errors — into the CHECK checklist.		SB pp.32–33 · mini-boards

WHAT WE DO: a letter in two timed chunks: moves ①–② (5 min) → moves ③–⑤ (6 min) + a bonus line; criteria on the board

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. WRITE chunk 1 (5 min): Moves one and two: hello + how are you + the favour + your problem. Exact time and place!
  2. WRITE chunk 2 (6 min): Moves three-four-five: describe the person (have got, hair rule!), thanks, sign — and smuggle your bonus line in.

WB Unit 2 (pp. 12–19) · points to the leaderboard next pair
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can write a 6–8 sentence favour email with flawless apostrophes (both jobs) — and win «would you do the favour?» votes from real readers.
  • Subsidiary. Gate = apostrophe crime scene; the Joshua model dissected into a 5-move skeleton; contraction blitz ×10; favour psychology (3 moves that make people say yes); writing cycle in timed portions; peer review as judging; Quick review quiz = the map of the test.
  • Personal. Writing pair — but never 20 minutes of silence: portions with timers, market with chips, votes. The apostrophe carries BOTH jobs today (possession + contraction) — it's the unit's accuracy boss fight.
By the end, students can…
  • fix 8 apostrophe crimes at speed and name the job of each
  • reproduce the 5-move email skeleton
  • write a favour email that wins votes
  • map their weak spot via the Quick review quiz

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Ben's 13 — whose 13?” → Nobody's! It's Ben IS — job 2.
“An email without exact time and place — does granddad say yes?” → No: details make favours happen.
“Is ‘URGENTLY MEET BEN’ a good favour email?” → No — polite opening + reason + thanks.

Assumptions: have got + questions live (P3–P4); Joshua model is genuinely useful (real-life favour). Apostrophes met twice (P3 possession, contraction since U1) — now unified as ONE sign with two jobs. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
