Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“He can to sing — what's wrong?” → после can нет to.
“She cans dance?” → у can никогда нет -s, ни у can, ни у глагола после него.
“Yes, I can — сильный или слабый can?” → сильный /kæn/: в коротком ответе он ударный.

Assumptions: Post-GMF4: can в речи есть, но письменная точность вопросов и отсутствие to — не автоматизированы. Tech nouns частично знакомы из жизни (laptop, screen), touchpad/charger — новые. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (question word order with can / comparative spelling / possessive pronouns), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
