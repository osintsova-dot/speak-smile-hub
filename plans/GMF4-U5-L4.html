Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 musical instruments and talk about preferences: “Would you rather play (the drums) or sing? — I'd rather play (the drums) than sing.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «I'd rather sing» (CD2 40); consolidate I'd rather noticed in the story (L3) — now drilled properly; discover the rule — I'd = I would; I'd rather (do X) than (do Y); a real preferences communication game (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on the frame I'd rather … than … (not just the instrument); model each instrument with a big TPR mime; keep the game moving — short turns, everyone plays.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 musical instruments (drums · xylophone · keyboard · cymbals · tambourine · electric guitar);
  • ask & answer “Would you rather … or …?”;
  • state a preference: “I'd rather play the drums than sing.”

Structure

Form
I'd = I would. I'd rather + verb (play the drums) than + verb (sing). Question: Would you rather (play the keyboard) or (sing)?
Pron. watch
I'd rather /aɪd ˈrɑːðə/ (soft /ð/); cymbals /ˈsɪmbəlz/ (starts /s/); xylophone /ˈzaɪləfəʊn/ (starts /z/); tambourine /ˌtæmbəˈriːn/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I'd rather — what two words is I'd?” → I + would.
“I'd rather play the drums ___ sing — or or than?” → THAN (I'd rather X than Y).

Assumptions: “I'd rather” was met in the story (L3); the 6 instruments are new but very picturable and act-out-able. The frame I'd rather … than … and the question Would you rather…? are the key challenge. Readers can read short instrument words. (Book teaches 6 instruments — note КТП listed a different set: guitar/violin/piano/trumpet/flute/recorder.)

27–37′
Would you rather…? · game
desks, pairs → 2 teams

Model first. Show two instrument cards + a “sing” card. Ask 2 pupils publicly: Would you rather play the cymbals or sing? — I'd rather play the cymbals than sing.

▶ Talk Partners (act. 4). Pairs ask & answer with instrument/sing cards: Would you rather play the tambourine or the keyboard? Both give full preferences. 5–6 exchanges, trade cards.

▶ Preference line-up (team). Call two options — drums or electric guitar? — pupils move to the side that matches THEIR choice, then say I'd rather play the electric guitar than the drums. Everyone commits & speaks.

✔ Assess: circulate — is it “I'd rather … than …”, full frame? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: option cards have the word printed. ⭐ Extension: add a reason: …because it's loud and fun!

 instrument + sing cards · ▶ Talk Partners
37–46′
I'd rather… build
desks, 2 teams

Make true sentences (Showdown). Mini-whiteboards: you show two option cards (e.g. keyboard + sing) → all write their true preference I'd rather play the keyboard than sing. — Show me! Reveal together, self-check the frame. 5 rounds.

ICQ: Do we say “I'd rather to play”? (→ no — I'd rather PLAY, no “to”!)
