Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated Unit 3 — 12 health problems, Has/Does questions, healthy habits + must/mustn't — shown it in the flagship Team Quiz with reward game, and launched My project 1 «The zoo challenge» (There's / There are).
  • Subsidiary. AB Review «Ready, Steady, Go!» (p.34): name problems & habits, must/mustn't advice, break Luke's code (→ INDIA, a bridge to Unit 4); spelling check; self-assessment. Project launch (PB pp.38–39): investigate a zoo & use “There's a (crocodile). It's got (a pool). There are (some giraffes).”
  • Personal. Quiz = every child answers (Showdown boards), not captains only; keep the reward game short & joyful — it's the cherry, not the cake. Keep the project launch a light, exciting brief (full design is homework / reserve).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name unit words from pictures AND spell key ones;
  • ask & answer Has/Does + give must/mustn't advice;
  • start the zoo project with There's / There are; self-assess honestly.

22–42′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 2 teams

Flagship quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles: picture→problem, problem→spelling, Has/Does question, matched short answer, healthy/unhealthy sort, must/mustn't advice, break-the-code. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/says on their board → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a bowling / penalty shot (paper ball into a box). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Offline setup: card grid on the whiteboard + a real paper-ball penalty into a box/basket — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on “Show me!”)

Finale. Count scores together in English; both teams: Give me five!

 quiz grid (board) · mini-whiteboards · box + paper ball
42–49′
🦁 My project 1 launch · The zoo challenge
carpet → desks, groups

Brief (PB pp.38–39). New challenge — design a zoo and present it! Is there a zoo in your town? What can you see and do there? Quick brainstorm.

Model There's / There are. Board pair with animal cards: There's a crocodile in the zoo. It's got a pool. There are some giraffes. They've got some trees. Class echoes.

▶ Group investigate (act. 2). Groups of 3–4 (▶ RallyRobin turns): name a zoo, list animals (choose ~8) & one thing each animal needs — We can have some elephants. The crocodile needs a pool. Jot on the notes sheet.

ICQ: There's a giraffe or There are some giraffes — for many? (→ There are some!)

💪 Support: choose animals from a picture bank. ⭐ Extension: add a service for visitors: There's a picnic area.
