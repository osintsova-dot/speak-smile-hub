Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name school subjects and ask & answer with to be: “Are you good at (Maths)? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.” and rate ability: “I'm (brilliant / very good / good / not very good) at (History).”
  • Subsidiary. Retrieval of L1–L3 language (school places · “good at” from the story) woven straight into the new subjects; sing «I'm brilliant at PE!» (CD1 19); discover the rule — we use to be in the question AND the answer (Grammar clue); a real ability survey (AB follow-up). Subjects & structure are drilled inside three games — Memo, Relay, Basketball — not by “repeat after me”.
  • Personal. Insist on the am / 'm not short answers (never bare “yes/no”); model the ability ladder with height gestures (brilliant = tallest); keep every game moving with a clear ICQ + countdown; make sure a point is only won when the full sentence comes out.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 new school subjects (+ recycle 4 known);
  • ask & answer “Are you good at…? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.”;
  • rate ability: “I'm very good at Geography.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Are you good at Maths? — do I answer with do or am?” → am (to be!).
“brilliant or good — which is stronger?” → brilliant!

Assumptions: “good at” was met in the story (L3); Maths/English/Music/Science known from before. ICT/Geography/History/Arts and Crafts/Drama likely new. The switch from L2's do-questions to L4's to be-questions is the key challenge. Readers can read short subject words. Teams: max 4–5 per team; at other sizes split 2 teams of 3–5 for Relay/Basketball, pairs for the survey.

Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”

10–18′
Presentation · subjects + ladder
carpet → board

I Do (act. 1, CD1 18). Icon cards one by one: It's ICT. It's Geography. It's History. Choral ×3 + one action each (History = crown, Geography = spin a globe, Drama = bow). Recycle the 4 known: Maths, English, Music, Science.

We Do — ability ladder. Board ladder brilliant → very good → good → not very good. Think aloud: I'm brilliant at PE… but I'm not very good at Art. Sort a few subjects onto YOUR ladder; the class calls the rung with height gestures (brilliant = reach high).

▶ Take Off–Touch Down (stir): Stand up tall if you're GOOD at… Maths! — flash subjects fast, everyone reacts every time.

💪 Support: icon carries the meaning. ⭐ Extension: read the subject word with the icon covered.

 subject icon + word cards · board ladder · CD1 18
18–26′
▶ Memo · subjects
desks, teams

Controlled practice — vocabulary, in teams. ▶ Memo (Concentration). Each team of 4–5 gets a face-down grid: 6 subject icon cards + 6 subject word cards (reading!). In turn a player flips two and reads both aloud — Geography… PE.

Icon + matching word = a pair — but you only keep it by saying a full sentence off the ladder: It's History. I'm good at History! No sentence → cards go back. Most pairs wins.

Everyone busy (PIES): the whole team whispers/chorus-reads every card as it flips, so all 5 rehearse the words each turn — not just the player. Rotate the flipper clockwise.

ICQ: Do you keep the pair with silence, or with a sentence? (→ a sentence!)

💪 Support: 4 pairs instead of 6. ⭐ Extension: claim with a full very good / not very good at sentence.

 Memo sets: 6 icon + 6 word cards per team · ▶ Memo / Concentration
26–34′
Song + grammar box
carpet → board

Sing (act. 2, CD1 19). PB p.12. First run listen & point; second run sing with the ladder gestures (a quick stir after Memo).

Grammar box (act. 3, CD1 20). Board: Are you good at…? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. Grammar clue: We use the verb “to be” in the question AND the answer! Circle Are / am / 'm not. Put L2's do-box right next to it and contrast.

ICQ: “Are you good at ICT?” — Yes, I do or Yes, I am? (→ Yes, I am!)

34–42′
▶ Grab & Spell Relay
board, 2 teams

You Do — active vocab + spelling race (mirrors AB p.9 ex.1 “Order and write the school subjects”). Two teams line up facing the board; a set of scrambled subject letters / icons at each side.

▶ Relay: you call a subject — Geography! The front runner races up, orders/writes the word, runs back, tags the next. Seated team-mates are the “coaches” — they chant the sentence together while their runner writes: We're good at Geography! A correct, correctly-spelled word = a point.

Everyone runs — 6 subjects = every child gets a turn; rotate the line. Reset noise with Freeze!

💪 Support: copy from an icon-word card. ⭐ Extension: add the ability word — write brilliant at Geography.

Listen & match / build
desks

Build true sentences. Board frame from the ladder: I'm (brilliant / very good / good / not very good) at ___. Each pupil builds 2–3 true sentences aloud to a partner first, then the notebook goes in.

📓 Interactive notebook — GMF3_Unit1_Lesson4_My_School_Superpowers_A5
A5 glue-in note — POWER PATH ability ladder (not very good at → good at → very good at → brilliant at) with 6 subject icons + a SING THE QUESTION panel (Are you good at…? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.)
  • Pupils glue the note in and colour their own rung on the POWER PATH for each of the 6 subjects.
  • Read the ladder bottom-to-top with the class, one hand climbing: not very good — good — very good — BRILLIANT!
  • Pairs build sentences straight off the note: She's brilliant at PE. He's not very good at Geography. — self-check the sentence has at.
  • Everyone circles at on every rung in colour — that is the word that gets dropped.
When: ~5 min inside the 42–50′ “Listen & match / build” stage (desks)Prep: 1 × A5 per pupil. Glue sticks + coloured pencils (the ladder is coloured in, not just read).

✔ Assess: circulate — is it “good AT” (not “good in”), and does the ability word match the truth? Recast, note names for the survey/HW.

50–55′
▶ Subject Basketball
front, 2 teams

Freer production + reward throw. A soft ball + a basket by the front wall. ▶ Basketball: pull a subject card and ask a player Are you good at Drama? — a correct full answer Yes, I am! / No, I'm not. (or a ladder sentence I'm very good at Maths!) earns a throw: the ball must bounce once off the wall into the basket. In = a point for the team.

Everyone speaks: before each throw the WHOLE opposing team must chorus the answer with the thrower — no silent watching. Rotate so every child throws once.

Rule: no at / bare “yes/no” = no throw, next player. Keep it fast; two attempts each.

💪 Support: answer to a yes/no card. ⭐ Extension: add …because it's fun!
