Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, read and act out the story «Sci-Fi City» and talk about the future: “Will there be robots in the future? — Yes, there will.”
  • Subsidiary. Value — using your imagination (Josh dreams of Sci-Fi City); break Ravi's book-title code (→ ROBOT WORLD); recycle Will there be…? from L2; story skills — predict → listen → read along → Read & answer → act out; bridge to the transport of the future in L4.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, prediction; give every child a story role; keep the code a warm hook, not a test — solve the first letters together.
By the end, pupils can…
  • retell 2–3 things that happen in the story;
  • read their role's lines from the book;
  • answer comprehension questions in full: “Josh travels to the future.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Where does the spaceship take Josh — the past or the future?” → the future — Sci-Fi City!
“Is it real, or is Josh using his imagination?” → his imagination (a dream/adventure).

Assumptions: Will there be…? is active from L2; the alphabet-number code was learnt in the Starter (recycled in the code activity). Readers follow the text with a finger. The act-out routine is familiar from earlier units.

Read & answer · comprehension
carpet → desks

Read and answer (act. 3, Talk Partners). Questions: What is Josh reading? Where does the spaceship take him? What does he see in Sci-Fi City? Who does he tell in the morning? Pairs answer in full: Josh travels to the future. Every pair speaks.

Who said it? (Showdown). Mini-whiteboards: you read a line — “Will there be robots in the future?” Who says it? — all write the name, Show me! 4 rounds.

✔ Assess: the boards are your check — who follows the story? The rest sit with you in the board game.

💪 Support: point to the right frame in the book. ⭐ Extension: add a second true fact about that moment.

28–40′
Act out
carpet, groups of 4

Model a frame. You + 3 pupils play the spaceship-arrival frame with big drama: Wow! A spaceship! Get in, Josh! We're going to the future!

Groups of 4 (Josh · Lily · Ravi · Alien). Rehearse 2 frames with books as scripts. Rotate roles once.

Showtime. Each group performs ONE frame (30–40 sec — fast carousel, no long waits). Audience task: shout where the spaceship goes (“To the future!”) at the end.

💪 Support: shortest lines / the “Wow!” reaction for the shyest child. ⭐ Extension: add an extra line from the frame.

Use the code (act. on p.74). Ravi's science-fiction book: It's called… Decode with the A=153 key: 715-637-177-637-783 871-637-715-495-231 → ROBOT WORLD!

▶ Code-breaker teams (RallyCoach). Each group decodes a short future word (robot · city · alien) with the key; partner A reads a number, B finds the letter, then swap. Everyone reads the word aloud.

🎮 Projector option: show the number code on screen; a rotating child calls the next number, the WHOLE class calls the letter.

ICQ: Number 715 — which letter? (→ R!)
