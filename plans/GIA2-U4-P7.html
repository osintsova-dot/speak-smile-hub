🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The WDYT project from the book is turned into the launch of a channel: the crew films video advice for tourists about their own region and defends it in front of the class. The super skill of the unit (communication — active listening) works as a condition of handing it in: in the pair you may not interrupt and you have to ask again, otherwise the notes come out one-sided. The Quick review is built into the middle as stations, as 'the pre-broadcast check': revision for the test without the word 'test'.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“The channel goes live today. Your crew makes a video that tells a foreign visitor when to come to our region and what to bring. Four seasons, real numbers, real advice — like the girl from Finland, only better, because it's OUR place. One rule from the studio: while your partner speaks, you don't interrupt — you listen and then repeat it back. Notes only, no scripts. Cameras in forty minutes.”
In simpler words, if the class needs support: We are launching a channel: the crew films video advice for tourists about our region — four seasons, real numbers, what to bring. The studio rule: don't interrupt your partner, ask again and say it back in your own words. Notes, not a script.

WHAT WE DO: ex.3 the Key phrases of active listening → ex.4 notes on the four seasons of their own region

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ex.3: we practise the phrases in pairs: You go first. · OK, let me check I understand. · So you think… · Can you explain about…? · Sorry, can you say that bit again? · What do you mean about…?
  2. The studio rule (TB tip): show that you are listening — a nod, eye contact, saying it back in your own words. I check the word interrupt.
  3. ex.4: Your region. Four seasons. Notes, NOT sentences. Weather · what people do · what to bring.
  4. Kagan Timed RoundRobin inside the crew: 40 seconds per person, everyone talks about their own season, the others listen and ask again — only then do we write.
  5. I count the use of the phrases: every phrase I hear = a point for the crew.
📺 On screen: SB p.58 Super skills · notes form · timer
👦 Students do: they make notes while listening to each other. Expect: the phrase 'So you think…' catches on faster than the others

WHAT WE DO: 4 stations of 4 minutes each: weather · compound nouns · comparison · modals (SB p.57)

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Before you go on air, the channel is checked. Four stations, four minutes each.
  2. The stations: a blitz on the 27 weather words (track 28) · the ONE WORD / TWO WORDS baskets (track 29) · the forms with the Spelling rules (than vs the …est) · the three columns of modals + a question with a short answer.
  3. Each station finishes through Kagan Numbered Heads Together: Number twos — answer for the whole crew! — that way nobody sits it out.
  4. A personal gap list: Write down what YOU missed — that's your test prep list, and it's short.

👦 Students do: they work through the stations and log their holes honestly. Expect: the gap lists differ — that is the point

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, crews can plan and record a video giving weather advice about their own region (four seasons: weather · activities · what to bring) using notes only; the whole unit is revised for the P8 test.
  • Subsidiary. The model video about Finland (ex.1) and taking the Model project apart (ex.2); Super skills communication — active listening with the Key phrases (ex.3); ex.4 notes on the four seasons; ex.5–7 sharing out the lines, rehearsing, recording; ex.8 peer review; Quick review p.57 in stations; the WDYT question of the unit.
  • Personal. The project is the weaker students' moment of glory: roles matched to their strength (a season · the numbers · 'what to bring' · the camera). I never call the Quick review preparation for the test — it is 'the pre-broadcast check'. If there is nothing to film with, the crew performs live in front of another pair (the TB backup).

  • use active-listening phrases with a partner

Pron. watch

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Are there full sentences in the Model project? → no, notes only; the sentences are born on air.
Is active listening the same as keeping quiet? → no: nod, ask again, say it back in your own words ('So you think…').
Is the Quick review a test? → no, it is the pre-broadcast check: find your own holes today.

Assumptions: SB pp.57–59, KTP pair 36. All the material of the unit has been covered; what is new is notes instead of a text and working to camera.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The project eats the revisionA firm frame: the revision stations take 18 minutes IN THE MIDDLE of the pair, before the recording — they are 'the pre-broadcast check'.
They read off the page on cameraThe studio rule: only a card with 5 words in your hands. The ex.6 rehearsal is compulsory — no rehearsal, no camera.
Every crew takes the same regionI split the regions between the crews in advance (TB technique): north · south · the coast · the mountains · our own town.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
