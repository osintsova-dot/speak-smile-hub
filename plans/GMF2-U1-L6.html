Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated Unit 1: name 10 sports + 6 ball actions, and use I play / I go, Do you…? — Yes, I do / No, I don't and 3rd-person He plays / She catches the ball with accuracy, through interleaved retrieval.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle phonics ai/ay (play, day, rain, snail); pre-literacy read & write (Match It, spelling ladder, AB Review); listening for detail (quiz); self-assessment (traffic light + “I can…”).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every game; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; make every child accountable on every question (Showdown, mini-boards).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 10 sports + 6 ball actions;
  • ask & answer Do you play/go…?;
  • make 3rd-person sentences He plays / She catches the ball;
  • spell key words & sort ai/ay.

Structure
I play (tennis). / I go (swimming). · Do you play/go…? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't. · He/She plays / goes · He throws / She catches the ball · Molly plays tennis on Monday.
Form
3rd person sing.: verb + -s (play→plays), + -es after ch/ss/o (catch→catches, go→goes). play = ball sports · go = -ing sports.
Pron. watch
3rd-person /s/ vs /z/ vs /ɪz/ (kicks /s/, throws /z/, catches /ɪz/); linking Do you /djə/.
Phonics
ai/ay (Unit 1 L5): ai in the middle (rain, snail), ay at the end (play, day). Quick recap sort.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“He play or He plays football?” → he plays (add -s for he/she).
“Swimming — do we say play or go?” → go swimming. “Tennis?” → play tennis.
“play or ay? Where does ay go — start or end?” → end.

Assumptions: Lessons 1–5 of Unit 1 are done (sports, actions, Do you…?, He plays, ai/ay, Sports Day poem). Pupils are early readers → short printed sentences OK with picture support. Carpet + team routines known; mini-whiteboards available. Групи: команда — максимум 4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Retrieval carousel ⚡
carpet, standing

Retrieval — bring back the whole unit. Give each child a picture card (sports + actions mixed). ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (Kagan): music on → Stand up, hand up, pair up!

Partner A holds card up: Do you play tennis? / What's he doing? — He kicks the ball. Partner B answers, A praises Nice! Swap quizzer → trade cards → new partner. 3–4 rounds.

Interleaving: cards deliberately mix ball sports (play), -ing sports (go) and actions (he/she + -s) so pupils switch structures each turn — not blocked. Signal: Class? — Yes!

13–22′
▶ Memo · teams ⚡
carpet → teams

▶ Memory (memo), teams of 4. Each team has a face-down grid of paired cards (picture ↔ picture, sports + actions). In turn (rotate the 4 roles), a child flips 2 cards.

Rule (say it as full sentence to keep the pair): a match is only won when the child says a whole target sentence — I play basketball. / She catches the ball. Just a word = no pair, next player's turn.

ICQ: One word or a whole sentence to keep the cards? (→ whole sentence). Most pairs wins. Every child gets equal turns.

31–39′
▶ Card-grab relay ⚡
carpet, teams in lines

▶ Card-grab relay (active listening → production). Spread all picture cards on the floor/board ledge. Two teams in lines. 🦝 Speaky calls a clue: She catches the ball! / He goes cycling! / a phonics word rain… play!

The front runner from each team races, grabs the matching card, holds it up and says the full sentence. Correct + fastest = point; wrong sentence = card back, no point. Runner goes to the back, next child up — everyone runs.

Manage: Freeze! only the runner moves. Rotate so all get ~2 turns.

 picture card pile · Speaky · 2 team lines

Spelling sprint 🧘
desks
📓 Interactive notebook · «Прописи — spelling ladder» · 4 min.

Glue-in ladder (Lesson 1 sports + Lesson 4 actions): write the word, losing one letter each line, down to one letter — hockey · kick · bounce, sentences He kicks the ball. / She hits the ball. Quick ai/ay tie-in: circle ay in play and ai in rain on the board.

✂ Prep: print «Прописи» A5 (both sheets), 1 per pupil · pencils. Colour the “I can spell the words” strip at the end.

 «Прописи» ladder A5 · pencils · board (ai/ay)
43–55′
▶ FLAGSHIP QUIZ + reward ⚡
carpet / goal zone

▶ Penalty Shootout Quiz (⭐ flagship review game — reward only on L6). Two teams. Show a question on the board; run answers with Kagan Showdown so every child is accountable.

Showdown: each child writes the answer on a mini-whiteboard → on Showdown! all reveal together → team confers → captain gives the team answer.

Interleaved question bank (mix each turn): ① picture → What sport? — swimming · ② gap He ___ the ball (catch) → catches · ③ Do you play hockey? → Yes, I do. · ④ Molly ___ tennis on Monday (play) → plays · ⑤ phonics play / rain — ai or ay?

Correct answer = one penalty kick: a player kicks/throws the soft ball into the goal (or beanbag into the hoop). GOAL! = a point. Winning team gets Speaky's 🏅 medal sticker.

ICQ: Do we shout, or write and wait for “Showdown”? (→ write & wait).

 board questions · mini-whiteboards + pens · soft ball · goal/hoop · 🏅 stickers
