What this document is (RU)

📝 Это ПРОТОКОЛ проведения итогового письменного контроля за весь год (Units 1–9), а не поурочный план новой темы. Сам тест — издательский, из папки курса: GMF1 End-of-Year / Final Standard Level Test (+ Answer Key). Содержимое теста здесь НЕ воспроизводится — только методика проведения: тайминг, рассадка, ICQ, тайм-менеджмент, что делать с быстро закончившими. Дети — ранние pre-readers ~7 лет: учитель читает каждую инструкцию вслух, показывает пример, аудио включает дважды.

Формат-опора: тест построен по логике Cambridge YLE Starters — знакомые детям типы заданий (picture–word, tick, number, match, circle). Части: лексика · грамматика · чтение · аудирование (+ Speaking — на занятии 71). Уровень «Standard». Проверь заранее: распечатай по числу детей, подготовь CD/аудио-трек для Listening, сверься с Answer Key.

Pre-flight checklist (before pupils arrive)
  • Print the Final Standard Level Test — one per child + 1–2 spare.
  • Audio ready & cued: Listening track, speaker tested, volume checked from the back row.
  • Seating: desks apart, solo, folder screens between neighbours («exam style, like big kids»).
  • On each desk: a sharp pencil + spare, an eraser, and a face-down quiet menu for early finishers («My favourite unit» draw-and-label).
  • Board: today's 4 parts as icons (📝 words · ⚙️ grammar · 👀 reading · 🎧 listening) + a simple visual timer.
  • Answer Key to hand (for your post-lesson marking, not shown to pupils).
  • Class list for ticking who finished / needs support.
  • Tone plan: you are calm and warm — no competitive games today, help with procedure, never answers. 🦝 Speaky wears a medal and cheers.
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. Pupils show what they know across Units 1–9 on the Final Test (vocabulary · grammar · reading · listening) in a calm, supported, model-first format — no surprises.
  • Subsidiary. Test-taking micro-skills at year's end: read/look at the example first, skip & return, check your name, use the full time, quiet early-finisher behaviour.
  • Personal. Radiate calm & pride in the year. Manage procedure, not answers. Note common errors → feed the Reserve revision lesson (72).
By the end, pupils can…
  • follow test instructions (read aloud + example) across four parts;
  • complete a year-end Starters-style paper calmly;
  • use «skip and come back» and self-check before handing in.

What the test covers (assessed, not taught today)
Lexis
Units 1–9: school things · toys · body/circus · food & fruit · farm animals · playground/park · town & transport · clothes · daily routines & meals
Grammar
Where's my…? · Is it a…? — Yes, it is / No, it isn't · have got · like / don't like · can / can't · There's a… / There are… · present continuous (He's …ing) · I (…) every day · I have (…) in the (morning).
Parts
❶ Vocabulary   ❷ Grammar   ❸ Reading   ❹ Listening — YLE Starters-style task types (picture–word · tick · number · match · circle).
Level
Standard (default) · Higher available for a strong group.
ICQ before the test (check understanding)
«If a task is hard — stop, or skip and come back?» → skip and come back.
«Is it OK to look at a friend's paper?» → no — I want to see what YOU know; that's how I help you.
«Finished early — what do you do?» → check my paper, then the quiet menu, silently.
Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Year-end / «big test» nervesNormalise: «it's the same as our books, just quiet — and it's everything you already know»; breathing (smell the flower — blow the candle) before starting. Speaky wears a medal, cheers.
Can't read a rubric (emergent readers)Read EVERY instruction aloud to the class + point to the example; mime it; re-read privately beside a child who needs it.
Listening — a child loses the placeHold up your copy, point-follow the item numbers; play each item twice; pause between items.
Early finishers disturb othersFace-down quiet menu on desks before the test («My favourite unit» draw & label); «no walking about» rule set up front.
A child freezes completelyKneel beside, whisper the example through, do the first item together, then step away — procedure help only.
Copy-glancingFolder screens between desks; positive frame («exam style»); calm reminder, not scolding.
Classroom management — signals
«Class? — Yes!» — you: «Class!» → pupils stop & echo «Yes!»
«WiFi» 🖐 — say «WiFi» + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — arm low all lesson = whisper day.
«Give me five» — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — «Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.»

Procedure — administration timeline (60 min)
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = structure (no competitive games today) 💪 support · ⭐ extension
TimeStage / whereProcedureMaterials
0–3′
Settle & reassure
door → desks

Greet each child at the door: Hello! Come in! Big smile! To solo desks. 🦝 Speaky wears a medal: Today is our show-what-you-know day for the WHOLE year. It's easy — it's everything we've practised. I'm proud of you already.

Everyone stands, breathing: Smell the flower… blow the candle… ×3. Sit down. Big smile.

 solo desks ready · Speaky with medal
3–12′
Confidence loop + how-to + 🦸 Диплом
desks

Low-key warm-up (no games). Flash 6–8 of the easiest cross-unit cards (a ball · a dog · a car · a bed…) — quick whole-class chorus, just to prime confidence: It's a ball! I can jump! Then a calm final read of the year's sound strip.

🦸 Phonics Hero — Диплом Фоникс-героя (тетрадь стр.91). Celebrate: You are READERS now — a whole book of stories! Colour your Phonics Hero diploma. (Quiet, joyful — hand out the diploma page; children colour their name in.)

Test how-to (show a blank paper). Name here! Look at the example first! Hard task? Skip it — come back later. Finished? Check your paper, then the quiet menu. Silent, please. ICQ: Hard task — what do you do? (→ skip & return) Can you look at a friend's paper? (→ no)

 6–8 easy flashcards · blank test copy to demo · Phonics Hero diploma (стр.91) + crayons
12–24′
Parts ❶ Vocabulary + ❷ Grammar
solo desks

Hand out papers face-down. Turn over… now. Write your name. Read Part 1 rubric aloud, do the example together, then: Off you go. Silent work.

❶ Vocabulary (picture → word, cross-unit: toys · food · animals · clothes…) · ❷ Grammar (circle/complete the right structure: Is it a…? · There's a… · I …every day). ~6′ guide per part; read each rubric aloud + point to the example; circulate quietly, help procedure only. Calm reminder at the halfway point.

 GMF1 Final Test · pencils · folder screens
24–34′
Part ❸ Reading
solo desks

❸ Reading (read & match / tick / circle — Starters-style). Read the rubric + example aloud, mime it, check with an ICQ: Do you look at the picture, then read? (→ yes). Take your time. Skip and return if you're stuck.

Micro-check: scan the room — anyone stuck on the example gets a whispered walk-through of the example ONLY.

 GMF1 Final Test
34–44′
Part ❹ Listening
solo desks

❹ Listening (listen & tick / number / match). Pencils down, eyes on your paper, listen first. Play each item twice, pause between items; hold your copy up and point-follow the numbers so no one loses the place. ICQ before play: How many times will you hear it? (→ two).

After the track: Finish this part. Two more minutes.

 Listening CD/track · speaker · teacher's copy to point-follow
44–50′
Check & collect
solo desks

Countdown & self-check. Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1. Pencils down. Now check: is your name on it? Any empty boxes? Draw your best guess.

Collect papers yourself, desk by desk, thanking each child: Thank you. Well done. ✔ Early finishers worked on the quiet menu («My favourite unit» draw & label) — no walking about.

 quiet menu sheets · collection tray
50–56′
Brain unwind (non-competitive)
carpet

Calm cooperative round. Move to carpet. ▶ Class chain (class vs itself): build the longest «This year I can…» chain — This year I can name toys… I can count… I can say my day… I can read stories! Stretch + breathe. Praise the WORK: You worked so hard — a whole year in one test. I'm proud of you.

 —
56–60′
Reflection & close
carpet → door

▶ Timed Pair Share: Tell your partner: one part that was easy, one that was tricky. (20 sec each). «I can…» badge: Colour your badge — I finished the Final Test!

I'm proud of you! Let's sing Goodbye. Thank you for the lesson! Sing Goodbye. High-five line at the door — each child hears one personal praise word. Tomorrow — our English CHAT (Speaking). It's easy — I love hearing you talk!

 «I can…» badges · Goodbye song mp3 · reward stickers 🦝

 app (optional) · no written HW
After the lesson — marking & feedback

Mark with the Answer Key (GMF1 Final Standard Level Test Answer Key). Record per-part scores on the class list. Harvest the 2–3 most common errors across the group → these become the diagnosis-backed stations in the Reserve lesson (72). Report to parents in the school's usual format: praise effort + name one strength and one next step per child.

Не показывай детям красную ручку / не разбирай ошибки на уроке — только тёплая эмоциональная поддержка. Первый год с нуля закончен — это большой праздник.
