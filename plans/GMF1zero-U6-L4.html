Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 5 prepositions of place — behind · near · in front of · next to · between (Pupil's Book p. 64) — and ask & answer where two people/animals are: “Where are (Jake and Molly)? — They're (behind the tree).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — playground objects (L1), “Where's (Beth)? — She's on the swing.” (L2), the story Hide and seek where the children were behind the tree / near the rocks (L3), Genki «Where is the spider / Mr Monkey?» — woven into today's target: singular He's/She's… grows into plural They're…; phonics — Phonics Hero ур.31 (a) первое знакомство: the tricky letters c · g · ph (soft c/g, ph=/f/) and the /эа/ houses air · ear · are (trace); sing the coursebook song Hide and seek (CD3 8); value: play fair & take turns.
  • Personal. Keep Where are… / They're… (plural = TWO) apart from Where's… / He's-She's… (one); give an ICQ before every game; every task keeps ALL children responding.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the 5 prepositions with gestures (behind · near · in front of · next to · between);
  • ask & answer “Where are they? — They're (next to the bush).”;
  • choose singular vs plural: “Where's Beth? — She's…” vs “Where are they? — They're…”;
  • meet the tricky letters c·g·ph and the /эа/ houses (trace).
Target language
Lexis
behindnearin front ofnext tobetween (anchor nouns: tree · grass · flowers · bush · rocks; recycled: slide, swing, roundabout…)
Structure
Where are (Jake and Molly)? — They're (behind the tree). · Where are they? — They're (next to the bush). · Talk Partners: Where are Anna and David? — They're in front of the desk.
Form
Where are + plural? → They're (= they are) + preposition + the + place. Contrast: Where 's (is)… → He's/She's (one).
Pron. watch
contracted They're /ðeə/; keep the whole preposition phrase (in front of, next to); soft c /s/ (face), soft g /dʒ/ (giant), ph /f/ (phone).
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.31 (a) первое знакомство: tricky c (face, ice) · g (giant, cage) · ph (phone, dolphin) + /эа/ houses air (chair) · ear (bear) · are (hare) — trace (тетрадь стр.86) · tricky review: Green set — go · no · so · my · have · like · some · come · one.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
One child on the swing: “Where's she or Where are they?” → Where's… She's on… (one!)
Two children behind a tree: “One or TWO?” → two → “Where are they? — They're behind the tree!”
Phonics: “phone — does ph say /p/ or /f/?” → /f/! — phone, dolphin.

Assumptions: playground objects + Where's…? solid from L1–L3; the prepositions were FELT in the story (Hide and seek); Genki «Where is Mr Monkey?» seeded the pattern; the /ё:/ houses done last lesson. Emergent readers → picture cards & memory, minimal print. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Singular/plural mixed: “Where are Beth?”ONE finger = Where's… He's/She's; TWO fingers = Where are… They're; always show fingers.
in front of / next to mangledOne clear gesture each; chant the whole chunk slowly; use a teddy + a box to demo.
between confused with next toBetween = TWO things, one on each side (hands like a sandwich); next to = one side.
Drop They're: “behind the tree” onlyModel the whole chunk; the /ðeə/ contraction with the TWO-finger cue.
Memory game: one child hogs the cardsStrict turn circle in the group of 4; “flip two, everyone says the sentence together”.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
Procedure — staging
highlighted = exact words to say (teacher / class) 🦝 = Speaky lines ▶ = game 🎮 = on-screen (projector) interactive […] = fill-in slot
⏱ Pacing — honest 60 min. Full plate — pick, don't cram. Cognitive load: only the 5 prepositions + the plural chunk “Where are they? — They're…” are truly NEW — the objects, behind/near (from the story) & the Where's-feel are recycling, so let the prepositions + plural carry the challenge (gestures, teddy demo, TWO-finger cue). The phonics is FIRST MEETING — trace & read only, no dictation. Buffers to drop first if short: Memory round 2, the 2nd Bingo round, the exit-ticket line-up. Better every child says one clear They're behind the tree. than six stages half-done. Offline reserve: every 🎮 has a paper twin.

▶ Where-are-they Memory
desks, groups of 4

▶ Preposition Memory (card game; Mido №45 “Concentration” = our Игротека «Мемори»; all groups play simultaneously). Each group of 4: 12 cards face-down — 6 preposition-scene pairs (two dogs behind a tree ×2, two cats next to a bush ×2…).

Turn order round the group: flip two cards, say the sentence together — They're behind the tree… they're behind the tree — a match! A matching pair is kept (the whole group repeats the sentence); not a match → face-down. ICQ: Do you say “behind” or the WHOLE sentence? (→ the whole sentence!)

Most pairs wins a 🦝 sticker.
