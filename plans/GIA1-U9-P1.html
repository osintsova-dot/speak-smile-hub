WHAT WE DO: TB extra as a battle match: teams A/B trade category words like shots; with a ball; sets by category

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ▶ Vocabulary tennis (TB p.122): the class in 2 lines, a soft ball.
  2. Category: JOBS. Team A serves — one word, throw. Team B returns — a DIFFERENT word. Repeat or freeze — point to the other team!
  3. Sets: jobs → food → clothes → technology → transport; the losing team of the set serves next.
  4. Reinforcement: teams may whisper prompts to their player BEFORE the throw.
  5. Set scores on the board; the match = +3 to the winners, +1 for beautiful (non-standard) words.

🏁 PRODUCT · category masters
desks · trios

WHAT WE DO: the finale: trios build a «master map» — 5 categories × 4 words × 1 showcase sentence; a 60-second defence

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Task: Your crew's MASTER MAP: choose FIVE categories, four words each, plus ONE showcase sentence per category — a sentence that uses two of the words. Eight minutes.
  2. Model: Transport: tram, lorry, underground, scooter — ‘The underground is faster than the tram.’
  3. Defence: the trio's speaker — 60 sec, neighbouring judges use the rubric; speakers rotate by category so everyone speaks.
  4. Gold run = a map with no spelling errors + 5 clean sentences.
📺 On screen: A4 sheets for maps · judges' cards · timer
👦 Students do: build the map and defend it in their roles — everyone writes and speaks. Expected: the sentences pull in the year's grammar (comparatives, past). Typical error: a sentence made of one category word — the bar: TWO words in the sentence.
🏆 The bar for the product (the judges' rubric, every pair): Fluency — pauses no longer than 3 sec (1–3) · Accuracy — forms are clean across all the year's tenses (1–3) · Exam-craft — used a tactic from the Exam tip aloud/in writing (1–3). 9/9 = «gold run», +5 to the season leaderboard. 📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can retrieve and use vocabulary from all 8 unit sets at speed: name 3+ items per category, verb+noun collocations (do/play/have/take/go) clean, and win category duels with full sentences.
  • Subsidiary. Season diagnosis: a personal map of «strong/leaky» categories; adjective pairs + opposites; ex.6 questions in pairs with long answers; spelling under pressure (Stop the bus, secret word).
  • Personal. The tone of the finale: «look how MUCH you know — now let's run it like athletes before a match». We're not teaching anything new — we're raising retrieval speed. The unit leaderboard is the year's final season.
By the end, students can…
  • name the 9 photo items and spell 6+ of them
  • give 3 words for each of the 9 unit categories
  • use do/play/have/take/go collocations without slips
  • ask & answer the ex.6 questions in full sentences

Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (mixed-year items), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our revision zone before the exam formats”.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
«It's all old stuff, boring»Never «we're revising» — always «season finale: show your speed». Everything on the timer, everything for points.
The strong ones dominate the weak in racesRounds with rotating roles + the rule «EVERYONE gives a word in turn» (Vocabulary tennis).
Spelling is fine out loud, holes show up in writingRounds on mini-boards: boards up — everyone is visible; mistakes go into the personal map, not into shame.
Categories get mixed up (play/do)The anchor columns do/play/have/take/go stay on the board the whole lesson; point at the column instead of prompting.
Someone «dropped out» of a term's topicsThe personal category map 🟢/🟡/🔴 at the end of lesson A — a legal way to admit a gap and get a plan.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
