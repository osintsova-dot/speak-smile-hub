Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have reviewed all of Unit 1 — family, numbers 1–6, colours, greetings and the story «Hurry up, Mummy!» — and shown it in the flagship Big Wheel Team Game with Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41) as the reward.
  • Subsidiary. “I can…” self-assessment with happy/sad faces; every child answers (all show at once, then teams); recycle Genki «Numbers» (08) & «How Are You?» (04); Phonics Hero — Part-1 review reading (CCVC/CVCC), speed-read p.30–31; a joyful, both-teams-win finish.
  • Personal. Team game = EVERY child answers (show me your card / stand up), not just the fastest. Keep the reward short & happy — it's the cherry. Both teams cheer at the end; no sore losers.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name family, numbers 1–6 & colours from pictures;
  • greet & answer “Where's number…? It's on…”;
  • show what I can do with a happy/sad face (self-check).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Excitement → noise spiralRules up front: answer on Show me!; volume-arm; Freeze resets.
Quieter children hide in a teamEveryone shows a card / stands to answer — team point = most hands up correct.
Reward game dragsOne kick/throw per point, quick; big cheer, move on.
Sore losers / tearsBoth teams get the “Well done!” cheer + a sticker — we beat the game together.
Self-check all-happy (copying)Eyes closed when showing the happy/sad face — an honest signal to you.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

11–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 12 · Part-1 review — sounds all sounds — CCVC / CVCC reading. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read the Part-1 review row — stop · film · hand and all the words you can now read! ▶ Reading relay — each child reads one word to earn a Part-1 star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.30–31 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read by · one together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

carpet

Blend it! Speaky says a CVC/CCVC word slowly; children blend it with the actions and shout it — s-a-t… SAT! (a playful, no-writing check). Air-write the first letter with a big finger.

Count & jump. a quick recycle of Genki «Numbers» (08) — count & jump 1→6 to raise the energy for the game.

24–47′
🏆 BIG WHEEL TEAM GAME + Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41)
carpet, 2 teams

Flagship game. Spin the Big Wheel (app on screen or a pointer on the mat) — it lands on a pod = a challenge card: name the family member · count to a number · name a colour · greet the puppet · what does Mimi give Mummy? Card shown → Show me! EVERY child answers (show the card / do the action / say it) → most-correct team gets a point.

Reward: Genki «World Cup Soccer!»: draw a pitch on the board with a 2-D ball in the middle; for each team point, one player kicks the ball one space toward the goal — GOAL wins the round. Rotate the kicker every time.

🎮 Offline setup: Big Wheel mat + pod challenge cards on the carpet; a board pitch + cut-out ball (magnet/blu-tack) for the reward kick — one kick per point, quick. (Или мягкий мяч в обруч — basketball-вариант.)

ICQ: When do you answer? (→ on Show me! only)   Finale. count the points in English; both teams: Well done! Give me five!

✔ Assess: across the game — who names family · numbers · colours · greets · story? A picture of the whole unit.

 Big Wheel mat / app · pod challenge cards · board pitch + cut-out ball (or soft ball + hoop)
