Ресурсы урока🎮 Все игры курса
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be more fluent re-using the “school things” language from Mimi's Wheel 2 Unit 1 — asking to borrow (“May I borrow your…?”) and asking & answering “Have you got your…?” — with maximum speaking time for every child.
  • Subsidiary. Re-activate school-object lexis in context with colours and numbers 1–10 (“a red crayon”, “How many? — Five!”); phonics (итоговый повтор Части 1, tricky words by · one); listen & respond; fine-motor + fluency through games and songs.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; give a support sentence-frame first, then fade it; run a stirrer→settler rhythm so no two sitting stages in a row.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 8 school objects and add a colour/number;
  • ask & answer “May I borrow your…? — Here you are.”;
  • ask & answer “Have you got your…? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't.” and “Has (Mimi) got…?”
Target language
Lexis
pencilpencil caseschool bagcrayonsscissorsgluepaintspaper + revise colours: blue·green·orange·pink·red·yellow · numbers 1–10
Structure
May I borrow your (glue)? — Here you are. / Thank you.  ·  Have you got your (school bag)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.  ·  Has (Mimi) got her (crayons)? — Yes, she has.
Form
Have + you + got + your + noun? → Yes, I have / No, I haven't. 3rd person: Has she/he got…? Connected “Have you got” /həvjuːɡɒt/.
Pron. watch
/ʃ/ in scissors; final cluster in paints; weak “have you” → /həvjə/; contraction haven't.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.12: итоговый повтор Части 1 + диплом (тетрадь стр.30–31) — speed-read all sounds so far (s…qu) · tricky words: by · one. JP Book 2 встраивается: recognise → blend fast.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I want your glue for a minute. Do I keep it?” → no → “May I borrow your glue?”
“Your school bag is at home, not here. — Have you got it?” → no → “No, I haven't.”
“Mimi is a girl. Has Mimi got her crayons?” → “Yes, she has.”

Assumptions: school things, colours, numbers 1–10, greetings were met in Mimi's Wheel 2 Unit 1 (Starter = revision, not new). Pupils are non-readers → rely on pictures, real objects & audio. Mimi's Wheel characters (Mimi, Dylan, Mummy, Daddy) are meerkats 🦡 — use their picture cards for “Has (…) got…?”. Группы: команда — максимум 4; 12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Say “Give me your glue” (not polite)Model & drill May I borrow your…? — the puppet asks nicely first.
Mix up “Yes, I have / No, I haven't”Give a gesture pair: thumbs-up = have / hands-empty = haven't; back-chain the answer.
Drop the 3rd-person “Has (she) got…?”Show a meerkat card so the referent is visible; slow choral drill of she has / he has.
Over-excitement during relaysFollow every stirrer with a settler + Freeze!
A few children dominate speakingUse pair/team structures (Mix-Pair-Share, QQT) so ALL speak at once, not one-by-one.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

22–30′
Re-activate lexis · Memory 🧘
carpet, in pairs

Quick re-cap. Hold each A4 card: What's this? Class answers. Add colour/number: It's a red pencil. How many? Two pencils! Choral ×2.

▶ Memory · in pairs (all play at once). Each pair has 5 object cards. Child 1 shows & names each — It's a school bag. — then places them face-down. Child 2, from memory, turns one: correct → describe it It's a blue school bag! and keep it. Swap roles. (Most cards wins.)

ICQ: Do you turn one card or two? (→ one). Reset if loud: Freeze!

 A4 flashcards · 6 pair-sets of object cards · ▶ Memory
30–38′
Role-play · “May I borrow…?” ⚡
carpet, mingle

Model with the puppet. 🦝 Speaky: May I borrow your glue? You: Here you are. Speaky: Thank you! Back-chain the question, then choral drill.

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade: every child holds one object card. Stand up, find a partner! A: May I borrow your [scissors]? B (gives card): Here you are. A: Thank you! — then swap cards and find a new partner. Non-stop trading = everyone speaks constantly.

Support frame → fade. Frame on the board: May I borrow your ___? Remove it after 2 rounds so confident pairs go frame-free.

40–48′
Grammar activation · “Have you got…?” ⚡🧘
mingle → teams

Model. Show your bag: Have you got your pencil case? — Yes, I have! then empty hands: Have you got your paints? — No, I haven't. Gesture pair (thumbs-up / empty hands). Choral drill both answers.

▶ Survey mingle (Mix-Pair-Share) · “Uncle Dan” role (Mimi 2 p.11 format). Each child has a mini picture-checklist (4 objects) & colours the ones they've got. Ask 3 friends! A: Have you got your [glue]? B: Yes, I have! I've got glue. / No, I haven't. Tick a happy/sad face. Everyone asks & answers at once.

▶ Fan-N-Pick (teams of 4) · 3rd person. Meerkat cards (Mimi, Dylan) each hold objects. ① fans cards · ② picks & asks Has Mimi got her crayons? · ③ answers Yes, she has! / No, she hasn't. · ④ praises Well done! Rotate roles.

 mini picture-checklists · pencils · meerkat character cards + object cards · ▶ Mix-Pair-Share · Fan-N-Pick
48–54′
Speed finale · Card-grab relay ⚡
carpet, teams

▶ Card-grab relay (Boat Race style). Object cards spread on the floor between two teams. You call: Bring me… three red crayons! One runner per team grabs & says the full phrase Three red crayons! to score. Wrong/silent = no point, next runner. Fast rotation so every child runs.

Mix calls: objects + colour + number (recycles the whole lesson). Places twist (Mimi 2 p.13): tape 4 place signs round the room — classroom · dining room · library · playground; between grabs call Go to the playground! and teams run to the spot before the next card call. End with Freeze! Sit down, superstars.

 floor object cards (colours/numbers) · 4 place signs (classroom/dining room/library/playground) · 2–3 team baskets
