🦝 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Starter block “Back to school!” · Lesson 6 — Story & Story Song
Pre-A1 young learners (~6–7 y.o., non-readers) · 60 min · Story (picture & audio) + Story Song + Phonics · carpet + desks
Starter block · lessons 4–8 = revising “School” (Mimi's Wheel 2, Unit 1)📖 Story day~10–12 pupils · 3 teams of 3–4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.11 · большое чтение (b)
Ресурсы урока🎮 Все игры курса
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow the story “Fun with paint!” for gist & sequence and use its language to ask & answer about classroom art things: “Have you got (paper)? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't. I've got (red) paint.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle school / art vocab & colours (Mimi 2 revision) woven into the topic; listen for sequence (order the story by ear); phonics (blend & read short sentences — Phonics Hero ур.11); values — sharing & creating together; fine motor (cut & stick story frames, colour).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; give an ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm with songs & signals; use one hinge check before act-out.
By the end, pupils can…
  • retell the story in order from the picture frames;
  • ask & answer “Have you got…? — Yes, I have / No, I haven't. I've got (colour) paint.”;
  • blend & read short phonics sentences (tricky words: his, has, into).
Target language
Lexis
paintpapercrayon(s)scissorsgluepicture · colours redbluegreenyelloworangepink
Structure
Have you got (paper)? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.  ·  I've got (red) paint.
Form
Have + you + got + noun? → Yes, I have / No, I haven't. · I've got + (colour) + noun. Connected “Have you got” /həvjəˈɡɒt/.
Pron. watch
silent contraction I've /aɪv/; final /v/ in have; /ʃ/ in scissors; drop of /h/ in fast “haven't”.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.11: большое чтение — закрепление (b), тетрадь стр.28–29 — blend & read short sentences (there & back, fast). Tricky words: his · has · into — read on sight. Tie-in: I've got red paint.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I say Yes, I have — have I got the glue or not?” → yes (I've got it).
“The meerkats paint a picture together — is that kind or unkind?” → kind, we share.

Assumptions: school/art words & colours were met in Starter lessons 4–5 (Mimi 2 U1 revision) → today recycles & extends them, no cold start. Pupils are non-readers → rely on picture frames + audio, not printed text; “order the story” = order pictures, not lines. Carpet routines known. Персонажи Mimi's Wheel — сурикаты (meerkats) + учитель-кот. Группы: команда — максимум 4; 12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Story audio too fast to followPause after each frame; point to the matching picture; replay once.
“Have you got…?” said as “You got…?”Back-chain: …paper → got paper → Have you got paper?
Yes, I have / No, I haven't mixed upTwo gestures: thumbs-up = have · thumbs-down = haven't; drill fast.
Story frames get muddled on carpetNumber the backs 1–6; teams check against the numbers only AFTER ordering by ear.
Over-excitement in act-outGive roles first; use Freeze! as a reset; keep scenes short.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

 Disco Warm Up mp3 · Speaky · carpet
3–8′
Review — art things (woven in)
carpet, in teams

Recycle Starter (Mimi 2) art words + colours, already inside today's structure. Lay 6–7 object cards (paint · paper · crayons · scissors · glue · picture) face-up in the middle of each team.

▶ Ask & Grab (teams of 3–4): you call Have you got the glue? — every child scans, the first to slap the right card holds it up and says Yes, I have! I've got the glue. Wrong card → No, I haven't., put it back. Reshuffle & go fast — everyone reaches every round. Add a colour twist: Have you got red paint?

ICQ: Do you grab OR shout? (→ grab, then say the sentence).

 art-object cards (Flashcards Plus) ×3 sets · Speaky · ▶ Ask & Grab

🎵 Bridge
to the board

Come to the board — let's sing the sound song! Sing the ABC / phonics sounds song with finger actions → settle into the phonics block.

 Phonics sounds song mp3
10–22′
Phonics Hero — ур.11 большое чтение (b)
board → desks

Speed reading. Letter/sound strip they know — Read with me — fast! There and back! (whole class, choral, tap under each).

Blend & read short sentences (тетрадь стр.28). Model one: sound it out s-a-t → sat, then the sentence. My turn… your turn. Children blend on their fingers, then read the line together. Praise the sound-out, not the guess.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: flash a word — can you read it? read & correct → stand tall; still tricky → crouch small. 8–10 words, fast — every child moves every time.

Tricky words. Flash his · has · into: chorus, air-write, these ones we just KNOW — no sounding out! Tie-in to today: He has got red paint.

Write — тетрадь стр.28–29 (desks). Read & match / trace the sentence lines; tick the picture that matches. Colour the star «Я молодец!»

 Phonics Hero 2.0 тетрадь стр.28–29 (1 per pupil) · sound strip · word cards · pencils
22–24′
🎵 Bridge → into the story
carpet

Now — colours! Get ready to paint! Sing Genki «Colours» (52) — hold up colour cards; children shout the colour → straight into “Fun with paint!”.

 Genki «Colours» mp3 · colour cards
24–29′
Story — look & predict
carpet

① Look, no sound. Show the 6 story frames of Fun with paint! big on the board (no audio). Look! Who is in the story? → the meerkats + their teacher (a cat). Where are they? → at school, an art lesson.

② Teacher Qs, children describe / predict. Point & ask: What has she got? What colour is the paint? What are they making? Accept single words & short tries; recast up: child “red” → you Yes — she's got red paint! Hinge check (thumbs): Are the meerkats sharing… or fighting? thumbs-up = sharing / thumbs-down = fighting → if the class isn't sure, re-point to the frame where they pass the paint before playing the audio.

 MW2 Story Cards “Fun with paint!” (board) · Speaky
29–32′
Story — first listen
carpet

③ First listen (for gist). Now listen! Point to the picture you hear. Play the story audio straight through; you point to each frame in time. Quick check: Did they make a nice picture? — Yes!

 Mimi 2 story audio (iCloud mp3) · story frames
32–41′
Order the story by ear + notebook
carpet → desks

④ Team sequence — by ear. Give each team a set of the 6 story frames, shuffled, face-up on the carpet. ▶ Order-by-ear: play the audio again in chunks — after each part teams place the next frame in the line, left → right. What happens first? Put it here. Teams check the numbers on the backs only when the line is done.

⑤ 📓 Interactive notebook (desks). Hand out the mini story-frame strip (6 small pictures). Children cut & stick them in order into the notebook — a “story path”. ICQ: Glue first, or cut first? (→ cut, then order, then glue). 🦝 tip: Small dot of glue, superstars!

⑥ Listen again to check. Play once more — children run a finger along their story path frame by frame.

 6 story frames ×3 shuffled sets (numbered backs) · story-frame strip (1 per pupil) · scissors · glue · notebooks · audio
41–47′
Work the phrases + act out
carpet

⑦ Key lines — chorus & roles. Drill the two story lines with gestures: Have you got (blue) paint? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. I've got (green) paint. Boys ask, girls answer, then swap.

▶ Act it out (teams of 3–4): give roles — meerkats + teacher-cat. Hand out real props (paper, a brush, colour cards for “paint”). Teams re-play 2 frames: one meerkat asks Have you got red paint?, another answers & passes it Yes, I have! Here you are., all “paint” the picture. 2–3 teams perform; the rest are the audience clap. Reset with Freeze! if loud.

 paper · brush · colour cards (as “paint”) · role cards 🦝🐱
47–53′
Story Song + my picture
desks

Time to sing AND paint! Play Story Song «The paint song» (Mimi 2 U1, стр.9–10) — sing along with actions.

Draw & say. Each child draws their favourite place with crayons; as they colour, they say to a neighbour I've got (blue) here. I've got (green) here. Play the song again as a soft settler while they work.

Countdown. Finishing in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… crayons down.

 «The paint song» mp3 · crayons · notebooks
