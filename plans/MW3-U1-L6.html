Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to show being friendly (greet & help everyone), describe things with colours & shapes (the Big Wheel is big and round!), and say hello in different ways — a first taste of culture.
  • Subsidiary. Values — being friendly (greet · share · help); Content — big · small · round · flat + colours around us + Genki «Colours» (52); Culture — hello in different languages + Genki «Good Morning» (11); Phonics Hero — big reading (consolidation), speed-read p.28–29.
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values & culture through DOING (greet a friend, find a round thing) not talking-at. Every child finds/points at something.
By the end, pupils can…
  • greet & help a friend (being friendly);
  • say big / small / round + a colour about things;
  • say “hello” in two different ways.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A friend is sad — do we help or walk away?” → help!
“The Big Wheel — is it round or flat?” → round!

Assumptions: Greetings, colours & the Big Wheel are secure from L1–L5; reading is at whole words/sentences. Content & culture stay concrete & visual.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Being friendly” too abstractMake it doable: greet everyone, share a crayon, help pick up — praise it live.
Shapes confused (round/flat)Feel them: trace a circle in the air (round), press a flat hand (flat).
Culture block = just listeningEveryone tries the new hellos with actions (bow, wave, high-five).
Reading too hard for someBlend/read together; success = joining in, not solo reading.
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello, friends! Today — how to be a GOOD friend, and how the whole world says hello! Greet-everyone dance lap.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

▶ Friendly hello. greet the class the friendly way — hello, how are you, thank you, goodbye — from L5.

Colours. quick colour flash + What colour? — Red! Blue! to prime colours for today's content block.

 colour cards · Speaky
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 11 · big reading (again) — sounds review all 26 sounds — reading. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-read the CVC words & sentences — a bit faster today; blend the sticky ones together. ▶ Word-swat — Speaky says a word; children point / swat it on the page fast.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.28–29 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read his · has · into together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.28–29 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–24′
Values · being friendly
carpet

Talk & do. Remind the story: Mimi & Dylan HELP Mummy. Are they friendly? Do they help? (Yes!) How can WE be friendly?

Friendly in action. children practise: greet a friend, share a crayon, help pick something up — you narrate & praise: Wow, you helped! That's friendly!

💪 Support: do the friendly action. ⭐ Extension: say Here you are! when sharing.

 story cards · a few crayons to share · Speaky
24–33′
Content · shapes & colours + colour hunt ⚡
carpet → around room

Big & round. Hold up the Big Wheel: Is it big or small? Big! Is it round or flat? Round! What colour? Red! Trace a big round circle in the air together.

▶ Shape & colour hunt. Find something ROUND! Find something BLUE! children run to something in the room and name it: It's round! It's blue! Then sing Genki «Colours» (52) to celebrate.

✔ Assess: who can add a colour to a shape? — a big red ball. Celebrate, don't test.

 Big Wheel · classroom objects · Genki «Colours» (52)
33–41′
Culture · hello around the world + Ball & Music (16)
carpet

New hellos. Speaky travels the Big Wheel to new places: In English — Hello! Here — Bonjour! Here — Hola! Here — Ni hao! Children copy each with its action (wave · bow · high-five). Keep it joyful, 2–3 languages only.

▶ Ball & Music Genki Ball & Music: pass a ball while Genki «Good Morning» (11) plays; when it stops, the child holding it does a hello of their choice and everyone copies.

 Big Wheel mat · soft ball · Genki «Good Morning» (11) · Speaky
41–53′
Table time · friendly colours page 🧘
tables

Colour & draw. Children colour a big round Big Wheel and draw a friend next to it (a happy face).

Show a friend. to a partner: Hello! Look — it's big and round! It's red! — friendly words + colour + shape together.

 Big Wheel outline · crayons
53–58′
Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Do a friendly thing for your partner and say hello a new way. — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can be friendly! I can say hello around the world! colour one star.

You are all such good friends! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.

 “I can…” badges · crayons · Goodbye song (CD1 T3) · Speaky
58–60′
Exit ticket
door

Line up at the door with 🦝 Speaky. At the door, greet 🦝 Speaky in a NEW language (Bonjour / Hola) and name one round thing. Answered / shown → high-five out: Bye-bye! See you next time!
