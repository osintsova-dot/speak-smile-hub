Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 places in a city and say what you do there: “Where do you go? — I go to the (ice rink). — What do you do there? — I skate on the ice.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «I go to the city» (CD3 25) & the vocabulary chant (CD3 24); brainstorm places in a city (Learning to learn); early preview of Will there be…? (grammar comes properly in L2); literacy — read place words & solve the AB clue puzzle.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — the mingle game is high-energy, plan the freezes. Push the “What do you do there?” follow-up so places live in context, not as a bare list.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 places in a city;
  • ask & answer “Where do you go? — I go to the… / What do you do there?”;
  • read place words and match them to what you do there.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“You catch a plane here — which place?” → the airport.
“You watch a film here — cinema or market?” → the cinema.

Assumptions: Pupils know some town words from GMF3 (town category revised earlier). The 12 city places are largely NEW; go to + place is known. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures. This unit's grammar (Will there be…?) is only previewed today.

10–20′
Presentation · 12 city places
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD3 24). Each card: What's this? It's… the library! The airport! The ice rink! Choral ×3 + one gesture per card (library = open a book, airport = plane arms, ice rink = skate).

Where do you go? What do you do there? Board the question duo. Hold each card — class says: Where do you go? — I go to the football stadium. — What do you do there? — I watch a match!

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if you can WATCH A FILM here… the cinema! — flash cards fast, describe the place, everyone reacts every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + place word. ⭐ Extension: add the follow-up: I go to the market. I buy fruit.

28–36′
City memory game
carpet → desks, pairs

▶ Memory game (Talk Partners). Pairs study 8 place cards laid out for 30 sec, then cards flip face-down. A points at a spot: Where's the ice rink? B answers from memory It's here! I go there to skate. Flip to check. Swap. Point per correct memory.

ICQ: Do you look at the cards while your partner asks? (→ no — from memory!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair — is it a full place word + a “what you do there” idea?

 8 place cards per pair
36–44′
City trade
mingle

▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Each child holds a place card. Music → mingle; off → pair: A shows the card, Where do you go? What do you do there? B answers, then swap, TRADE cards, new partner. 5–6 exchanges — everyone speaks many times.

Whole-class check: Hands up if you had a place where you can BUY something! (shopping centre · market).

 place cards · music · ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade
44–52′
Clue puzzle · write
desks

Speak. ▶ RallyRobin: Take turns: I go to the… and I… — places you really go to.

Write (notebooks / AB p.70 format). Board frame: I go to the ___. I ___ there. Each child writes 3 true sentences + solves 2 place clues (You catch a plane here → the airport). Fast finishers add always / sometimes / never.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from 3 word cards. ⭐ Extension: add frequency: I sometimes go to the ice rink.
