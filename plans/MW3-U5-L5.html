Week 18.01–24.01 · Lesson 5🗣 Life skills · where I do things · Do you have lunch at school? Yes, I do~8 pupils · mingle + pairsPH 27 (/oʊ/ oa ow o-e · p.75–76) · Genki: This Is My Day(111) · game: Sticky Fingers(30)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to say WHERE they do routines — at home / at school — and ask & answer: “Do you have lunch at school? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” — a life skill: knowing my own daily routine.
  • Subsidiary. Sort routines home / school / both on a floor Venn (hoops); watch Lucy's video (CD2 T24); play Place the flashcard! & Sticky Fingers (30); sing Genki «This Is My Day» (111); draw & talk about my routines (PB p.33); Phonics Hero — /oʊ/ oa ow o-e, speed-read p.75–76.
  • Personal. First proper speaking focus of the unit — never force a shy child; a point at the hoop counts. Model with the puppet first. Keep each exchange ~20 seconds, then swap partner.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say where I do a routine — at home / at school;
  • ask “Do you have lunch at school?”;
  • answer “Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — the long /oʊ/ sound written oa · ow · o-e. Jolly /oa/ action → blend (b-oa-t → boat, s-n-ow → snow, h-o-me → home) → speed-read p.75–76.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do you have lunch at school — and where else might you?” → at home too — that's BOTH!
“Wash your hands — only at home?” → no, at home AND school!

Assumptions: All ten routines & What do you do every day? are secure from L1–L4. at home / at school and the Do you…? Yes, I do exchange are today's focus; children meet Lucy doing the same sorting in the video. Reading moves to /oʊ/.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“At home / at school” too abstractMake it physical: one side of the room = home, other = school; children WALK to their answer.
Forgets the short answerPuppet always models Yes, I do! (nod) / No, I don't! (shake) — children copy the gesture.
Some routines are both — confusionCelebrate BOTH: Yes! At home AND at school! — stand in the middle hoop.
Shy to speak to a peerGreet-and-ask the puppet first, then a friend; pointing to the hoop is a full answer.
Over-excited minglingSmall space, walking feet, Freeze on the tambourine.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning! Where do you do things — at HOME, or at SCHOOL? Point to home… point to school! Point-and-turn warm-up, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 27 · long /oʊ/ — sounds oa · ow · o-e  (all say /oʊ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds: b-oa-t → boat, s-n-ow → snow, h-o-me → home — arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Sound hop — children hop to the /oʊ/ spelling Speaky calls (oa / ow), then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.75–76 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read review Green tricky words together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–25′
Curiosity input · home / school + video
carpet → floor hoops

Sort my day. Two hoops crossing = a floor Venn: HOME · SCHOOL · BOTH. Hold up a routine card: Do you have breakfast at home or at school? …at home! Do you wash your hands? …at home AND school — both! Place it where children point.

Watch Lucy. Watch Lucy's video (CD2 T24) — Lucy sorts HER routines. Ask What does Lucy do at school? …at home? children guess, then check.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): Navio App — play the daily-routines vocabulary game and watch the Giant Joe story video on screen; children tap the routine they hear.
