📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can describe a place with there is/are in all forms with zero errors and run a polite café order scene with 6+ Key phrases, prices included.
  • Subsidiary. Gate exposes the there're/inversion ceiling (TB Common errors); tables + Lee Centre in a base sprint; error hunt + Hong Kong round-up; video model (Connor, $9.25); Skills boost THINK→PREPARE→PRACTISE→PERFORM compressed into the café opening; GMF4 bonus: extra-polite register.
  • Personal. Grammar is the café's “health code”, not the topic. The prices warm-up from the TB is quick and on-theme. The final scenes use rotation, so everyone speaks, not just a couple of brave ones.
By the end, students can…
  • write 6+ clean there is/are sentences fast
  • flip aff↔neg↔question in duels
  • order/serve food with Key phrases and real prices
  • perform a café scene to the bar

Structure
There's a cinema, and there are some cafés. · Is there a baker's? — Yes, there is (NOT Yes, there's!). · Can I have a veggie burger, please? · How much is that? — That's $9.25. · 🚀 bonus: Could I possibly have…? — Certainly, sir.
Form
the first noun decides is/are (There IS a bookshop and two shops — TB Common errors); There're — does NOT exist; the question = inversion Is/Are there…?
Pron. watch
the polite intonation rises: Can I have…?↗ · rising in Yes/No questions (TB) · $9.25 = nine twenty-five.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Yes, there's — OK?” → No: we do NOT contract the short affirmative — Yes, there is.
“There are a supermarket and two cafés?” → the first word is singular → There IS.
“An order without please — how much tip?” → Zero. Politeness = the café's currency.

Assumptions: there is/are met in GMF orally; ceilings: the there're contraction, inversion in the question, short answers. Ordering phrases are partly new (I'd like · Anything else?). Prices — a quick review of numbers. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
