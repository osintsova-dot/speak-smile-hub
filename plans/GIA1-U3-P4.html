ЧТО ДЕЛАЕМ: регистр выше учебника: Could I possibly…? / Certainly / Here you go / Would you recommend…? — строки для сцены

👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Поднимаю статус: Beginners say ‘Can I have’. Fine. A FIVE-STAR waiter hears: Could I possibly have…? — Certainly! Would you recommend the cheesecake? Not in this book.

Structure
There's a cinema, and there are some cafés. · Is there a baker's? — Yes, there is (NOT Yes, there's!). · Can I have a veggie burger, please? · How much is that? — That's $9.25. · 🚀 bonus: Could I possibly have…? — Certainly, sir.
Form
первое существительное решает is/are (There IS a bookshop and two shops — TB Common errors); There're — НЕ существует; вопрос = инверсия Is/Are there…?
Pron. watch
интонация вежливости вверх: Can I have…?↗ · rising в Yes/No вопросах (TB) · $9.25 = nine twenty-five.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Yes, there's — OK?” → No: краткий утвердительный НЕ сокращаем — Yes, there is.
“There are a supermarket and two cafés?” → первое слово singular → There IS.
“Заказ без please — сколько чаевых?” → Ноль. Politeness = валюта кафе.

Assumptions: there is/are met in GMF orally; ceilings: there're-контракция, инверсия в вопросе, краткие ответы. Ordering-фразы частично новые (I'd like · Anything else?). Цены — быстрый повтор чисел. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
