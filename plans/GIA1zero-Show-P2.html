Finishing the frame + anchors
Build · finishing the frame + grammar anchors

WHAT WE DO: filling in up to 5–8 lines; three anchors on the board (like+ing · can+verb · have got+a) — everyone fixes their own lines

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Anchors on the board with gestures: like + …ING (a wave) · can + verb (a bicep) · 've got + a/an (a hug).
  2. They finish the frame up to 5–8 lines with their own words; the substitution bank is on the desks.
  3. Self-check against the anchors: underline like/can/have got in your own lines — check with the gesture.
  4. I go round the weaker ones first; ⭐ for the fast ones — 2 bonus lines (My favourite unit… · In September I couldn't…).
📺 On screen: frame templates · the substitution bank · anchors on the board
👦 Students do: they finish the text and fix the grammar in THEIR OWN sentences — a review of the year in a personal project. Expected: I like swim — the wave gesture fixes it. Typical error: have got a pizza (uncountable food) — we discuss it with a laugh.

Rehearsals up the ladder (Timed Pair Share)
Practise · Timed Pair Share up the ladder
pairs

WHAT WE DO: Kagan · Timed Pair Share: rehearsals up the ladder — whisper to the wall → to a partner 60 sec → swap; tick feedback

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Rung 1 (2 min): everyone — whispers their whole talk to the wall/window (all at once, nobody can hear anyone else).
  2. ▶ Kagan · Timed Pair Share: Speaker — your talk, 60–90 sec, notes OK. Coach — LISTEN with two ticks: loud and clear? eye contact? Time up → 1 compliment → SWAP.
  3. 2 run-throughs × 2 roles; the coach after each: 1 compliment is mandatory.
  4. I walk round and listen to the quiet ones: their run-through must happen with me present (+a smiley for courage).
📺 On screen: tick cards · a timer
👦 Students do: they rehearse up the ladder from a whisper to a partner — 3 safe run-throughs for everyone. Expected: the “wall” rung takes the tension off in a fun, effective way. Typical error: the coach criticises — the card says: ticks and a compliment only.
💛 Friendly peer review (🐣, 2–3 checks): ✔ loud and clear · ✔ English sentences · ✔ eye contact (looking at us, not at the paper). Smiley scoring, points for courage and growth, not for “being the best”.		tick cards

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners have a complete 60–90 sec “My world” talk (5–8 lines from the frame) and have rehearsed it 3+ times with friendly peer review by ticks.
  • Subsidiary. A review of the needed grammar is WOVEN IN (like+ing/noun, can+infinitive, have got — fixed right inside the talk lines); a voice warm-up; a photo/picture built into the talk (“show and tell”).
  • Personal. More time on language than with the 🚀 line (KTP: “review of needed grammar”): every frame line is a mini-review of a structure from the year. Lots of short rehearsals: confidence = the number of safe run-throughs.
By the end, students can…
  • complete their frame with own words
  • fix like/can/have got in their own lines
  • perform the talk to a partner with notes
  • give friendly feedback with 2 ticks

Target language
Lexis
the lines of the “My world” frameThis is my… (photo) · Look!Loud and clear! · Eye contact!
Structure
The “My world in English” frame (8 support lines): Hello! My name's… · I'm … years old. · I like… · I can… · I've got… · My favourite … is… · This year I learned to… · Thank you! A minimum of 5 lines to choose from — EVERYONE speaks, notes in hand are allowed.
Form
like + noun/-ing (I like football / swimming) · can + infinitive WITHOUT to · I've got + a/an; the photo line: This is my dog Rex.
Pron. watch
volume “to the wall opposite” · My name's — linked /maɪˈneɪmz/ · favourite — two syllables.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I like swim — ok?” → No: I like swimmING or I CAN swim. Two different frame lines!
“Can I read from the paper?” → Peeking — yes, reading without ever looking up — no: eyes on the audience at least at the start of the line.
“Is 60 seconds a lot?” → 5–8 lines at a calm pace. Less than a minute of fear, more than a minute of glory.

Assumptions: Drafts from P1 (5 lines filled in), photos brought (those who forgot will draw a mini-sketch). The grammar in the lines (like/can/have got) was covered in U2–U5 — today we fix it in a personal context.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Lines with mistakes will stickGrammar station: before rehearsing, every line goes through my/a partner's check against 3 anchors (like+ing · can+verb · have got+a).
Afraid to perform even in a pairA ladder: whisper to the wall → to a partner → to a trio → (volunteers only) to the class. Every rung — a smiley.
Weaker learners don't fill in the frameA substitution bank + I go round the weaker ones for the first 5 minutes; minimum — 5 lines, 2 of which can be “like everyone's” (Hello/Thank you).
Feedback turns into criticismOnly 2 ticks (✔ loud and clear · ✔ eye contact) and 1 compliment. The words “bad/mistake” are banned in peer review.
Fast finishers are done and bored⭐ Upgrade: +2 lines (My favourite unit was… · In September I couldn't…) — a bridge to “My year” in pair 68.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
