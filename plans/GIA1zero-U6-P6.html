WHAT WE DO: Quiz-Quiz-Trade on repeat: entry; we bring back the unit question and check it against Pair 1's vote

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I take down the WDYT? poster from Pair 1: In Pair 1 you voted: is an upside-down house ART? Has anything changed?
  2. Kagan Quiz-Quiz-Trade with printed unit cards (handout GIA1-QQT: the question on top, the answer on the back after folding; no printouts — the students write their own cards in 2 min).
  3. Commands: Stand up, hand up, pair up! Quiz your partner — praise — TRADE cards — find a new partner! 3–4 swaps, EVERYONE quizzes at the same time.
  4. A quick revote by hands; 2 comments from the frame I think … is art because …
  5. Today you answer with your OWN exhibition.
📺 On screen: WDYT? poster from Pair 1 · answer frame
👦 Students do: revote and comment from the frame. Expected: opinions have shifted — it's nice for them to notice.

Step 3: rehearsing and recording
CREATE · Super skill Communication
pairs

WHAT WE DO: rules for clear speech + feedback cards; rehearsal describing two photos; a recording if there's the tech

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Super skills on the board: Super skills · COMMUNICATION (SB p.82): speak slowly and pronounce words clearly · speak loudly · make pauses so people have time to look at the photo.
  2. Feedback cards handed to the listener: That's very fast. · You're very quiet. · Try again. · Try to speak more loudly. · That's better. · I can't understand what you're saying..
  3. Rehearsal in pairs, 5 min: one describes, the other holds up a card (“That's very fast” / “You're very quiet”). Swap.
  4. We write descriptions of TWO photos from the frame (one each), 4 min, in keywords on a card — not as text.
  5. A 2-minute phone recording (or a live run-through).
📺 On screen: Key phrases cards (a set per pair) · phones · frame on the board
👦 Students do: rehearse with the cards — feedback becomes a game, not criticism. Expected: volume and pace really improve. Typical error: reading off the page — “6 words on the card, not text”.

Step 4: the exhibition + peer assessment
PRESENT · the exhibition + peer review
gallery

WHAT WE DO: a mini-gallery: pairs show their photo and read out the guide; peer review 3 checks; Final reflection with emojis

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Gallery format: pairs spread round the perimeter with their photo; half the class listens, then they swap — EVERYONE presents.
  2. Peer review (SB p.83 ex.10), 3 checks: 1 Do they say what people are doing? 2 Is the photo interesting? 3 Is the guide clear?
  3. I praise along the axes (TB p.95): Visuals (4 photos, varied and “telling”) · Content (description + who took it and why + why you like it) · Language (present continuous for actions, present simple for background, prepositions, clothes, colours) — TB p.95.
  4. Final reflection (SB p.83): three emojis (task · super skill · language); anyone with yellow/red — one phrase Next time I will …
📺 On screen: gallery round the perimeter · peer review cards · emojis
👦 Students do: everyone presents and everyone listens in two waves, gives checks. Expected: pride in “their own museum”. Typical error: a description with no actions — peer review question 1 catches it.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can present a short guide for four exhibition photos (who, where, what they're doing, what they're wearing) and complete the Unit 6 test in familiar formats.
  • Subsidiary. The graphic organiser gives a description skeleton; Super skill Communication (slowly · clearly · loudly · pauses) is drilled with feedback cards; WDYT? “What is art?” is closed; the test review is calm, through self-checking and a personal log.
  • Personal. The pair is packed: the project 42 minutes, the test 33, the review 15. We keep the project on a support (organiser + sentence frame) — otherwise they won't finish in time. The test is model-first, no competition; after the test, “what I can already do” is compulsory.
By the end, students can…
  • describe a photo in 3–4 sentences from a frame
  • speak slowly, clearly and loudly
  • give simple feedback with Key phrases
  • show unit language in a written test

Unit 6 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: rooms & furniture by definition/picture (You cook here → kitchen; You wash here → bathroom; It's above you → ceiling). B) 4 items: prepositions from a floor-plan picture (between · in front of · above · next to). C) 4 items: clothes & accessories + verb (You ___ an umbrella when it's raining → carry; You ___ trainers to do sport → wear).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 8 gaps: present continuous aff/neg with -ing spelling (sit → sitting; write → writing; not wear → aren't wearing). B) 6 items: order the words → Yes/No- and Wh- question + true short answer (you / are / what / wearing ?). C) 4 items: contrast with present simple (Every day I go… / Right now I ___ going…) — a trap on time expressions.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)Describe a photo/room (a script in the style of SB p.77 audio 43; the teacher reads it twice if there’s no track): 4 true/false + fill in 4 gaps — mirrors SB p.77 ex.6–7 (captions).
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A text message dialogue (6+ turns) with an invitation and a refusal. Marked for: present continuous forms (4) · so in the right position (3) · text-message format Hi… / See you! + task complete (3) — mirrors SB p.81 Skills boost.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.81 — every format was met in class during the unit. The two lines: 🚀 higher (+ Wh-questions in writing, more -ing spelling traps) · 🐣 standard.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
