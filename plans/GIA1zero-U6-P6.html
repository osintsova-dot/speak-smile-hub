Спецификация теста Unit 6 (ЧЕРНОВИК до официального пака Macmillan A1+)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: rooms & furniture по определению/картинке (You cook here → kitchen; You wash here → bathroom; It's above you → ceiling). B) 4 items: prepositions по картинке-плану (between · in front of · above · next to). C) 4 items: clothes & accessories + глагол (You ___ an umbrella when it's raining → carry; You ___ trainers to do sport → wear).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 8 gaps: present continuous aff/neg с -ing spelling (sit → sitting; write → writing; not wear → aren't wearing). B) 6 items: order the words → Yes/No- и Wh-вопрос + true short answer (you / are / what / wearing ?). C) 4 items: контраст с present simple (Every day I go… / Right now I ___ going…) — ловушка на time expressions.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)Описание фотографии/комнаты (скрипт в духе SB p.77 audio 43; читает учитель дважды, если нет трека): 4 true/false + 4 пропуска вписать — зеркалит SB p.77 ex.6–7 (captions).
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A text message dialogue (6+ реплик) с приглашением и отказом. Marked for: present continuous forms (4) · so в правильной позиции (3) · формат переписки Hi… / See you! + task complete (3) — зеркалит SB p.81 Skills boost.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
