Week 29.03–04.04 · Lesson 56✨ NEW · Did you watch…? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.83 · AB p.81
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer Past-Simple questions: “Did you watch (a cartoon) yesterday? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Ravi & Josh's guessing dialogue (CD3 47); discover the rule — Did + you + watch (no -ed in the question!) and the short answers did / didn't (Grammar clue); recycle the 12 programmes from L1; write questions & answers about their week (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Drill the short answer until it's automatic (never bare “yes/no”); keep the survey strictly timed; model the full question first, accept short answers and recast.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Did you watch…?” correctly (Did + you + base verb);
  • answer with “Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.”;
  • find out what a partner watched last week.

Structure
Did you watch (a sports programme) yesterday? — No, I didn't.  ·  Did you watch (a cartoon)? — Yes, I did.  ·  Did you watch (a film) last weekend?
Form
Did + subject + watch (base form — NO -ed!) + …? Short answers: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. Remember: did not = didn't.
Pron. watch
weak did you /dɪdʒə/ at speed; rising Wh-less yes/no intonation ↗ on the question; didn't /ˈdɪdnt/ — clean /d/ + /n/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Did you watch or Did you watched?” → watch — no -ed after did!
“Did you watch a cartoon? — I answer Yes, I do or Yes, I did?” → Yes, I did (past!).

Assumptions: 12 programmes are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up). Pupils met was/were past in Unit 7, so “the past” is familiar; the Did…? question-form is the new focus. Pupils can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar table.

19–27′
Tic-Tac-Toe · Did you…?
desks, 2 teams

▶ Noughts & Crosses — TV. 3×3 grid of programme pictures. To claim a cell, the team ASKS the other team: Did you watch a game show yesterday? — the other team answers truthfully in chorus: Yes, we did! / No, we didn't! Both sides speak every turn. Rotate the asker.

🎮 Projector/board option: draw the 3×3 grid on the whiteboard; a rotating child taps the winning cell as the WHOLE team says the question aloud.

ICQ: Do you say “watch” or “watched” after “Did”? (→ watch!)

27–37′
Find someone who… (info-gap)
mingle → desks

Model first. Ask 2 pupils publicly: Did you watch a film last weekend? — Yes, I did! / No, I didn't.

▶ Find Someone Who survey (act. 4-style). Each child holds a “Did you watch…?” grid (5 programmes + last night / last week). Music → mingle; off → pair: A asks, B answers Yes, I did / No, I didn't, A ticks the name. TRADE, new partner. 5–6 exchanges.

Harvest. Who watched a cartoon? Hands up! Who didn't watch TV at all?

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — is it “Yes, I did” (not “Yes, I do”)? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: grid shows the full question text. ⭐ Extension: add a follow-up: What did you watch? — A comedy programme.
