Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to read & say the /k/ sound (pink chameleon headache · king · stomach ache — “sometimes ch says k”), talk about sea animal rescue in Malta, and read & plan a quiz — three mini-blocks, one “fit & healthy” thread.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics: notice that k and ch can spell the same /k/ sound (recognise → say → sort); culture — sea animal rescue in Malta (turtles, divers, vets) + Channel 21 video, 21C: being fit & healthy; literacy — text type a quiz, question marks, plan & write a healthy-eating quiz.
  • Personal. Three blocks — keep transitions crisp (chant/drum bridges); the chameleon twister is play; cap each block hard so the literacy plan actually gets written.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the pink-chameleon twister and sort ch / k words;
  • name how divers & vets rescue turtles in Malta;
  • find the key features of a quiz and plan one with a partner.

Structure
The pink chameleon's got a headache and the king's got stomach ache.  ·  Divers help the turtles.  ·  quiz: How often do you eat fruit? Do you eat breakfast?
Form
/k/ = k (king, kite, pink) AND ch (chameleon, headache, stomach, school, chemist). Quiz: a question at the start of each item + a question mark ? at the end.
Pron. watch
/k/ clean in king / chameleon / stomach; contrast ch /k/ (school, ache) vs ch /tʃ/ (chips, lunch); Malta /ˈmɒltə/; turtle /ˈtɜːtl/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“king and ache — same first/last SOUND /k/? Same letters?” → same sound, different letters (k vs ch)!
“A quiz — one question or a long story? What goes at the end of a question?” → questions with a ? mark.

Assumptions: Health problems, habits and must/mustn't are active (L1–L4). Sound–letter awareness from earlier phonics is enough to enjoy the ch/k noticing (по учебнику). Pupils can read the short quiz items with support.

Block 1 · Phonics /k/
carpet → desks

Speak and spell (act. 1, CD2 09). PB p.33: Sometimes “ch” says “k”. The pink chameleon's got a headache and the king's got stomach ache! Listen, then the speed ladder: slow → fast → whisper. Pairs test each other ×3.

Sort k / ch-says-k. Two hoops/columns: k (king, kite, kiss, pink, karate) · ch-says-k (chameleon, headache, stomach, school, chemist). Hold up word cards — every child points/steps to the right hoop BEFORE you place each card.

Notice the rule. Look — headache, stomach, school, chameleon… here “ch” says /k/, not /tʃ/! (notice, don't lecture).

💪 Support: say the word, teacher shows the hoop. ⭐ Extension: add one more /k/ word to a hoop (e.g. Christmas, cook).

✔ Assess: who still reads ch as /tʃ/ everywhere? Note them — extra RallyRead next unit.

Block 3 · Literacy · a quiz
carpet → desks, pairs

Before/After you read (act. 1–2, CD2 11). PB p.34 «Are you fit and healthy?»: What must you do to be fit and healthy? What mustn't you do? Read/listen — do the quiz with a partner, count the points.

▶ Quiz scavenger hunt. Race in pairs to find on the quiz: Which answer is worth 3 points? How many questions are there? What's at the end of each question? First hands up read the answer aloud — everyone scans.

Investigate a text (PB p.35, CD2 12). «Do you have a healthy diet?» quiz: find the title · three answers a/b/c · the points. Writing tip: We write a QUESTION MARK at the end of a question — Do you eat breakfast?

▶ Plan a quiz (Cooperative learning, AB p.32 format). Pairs plan a healthy-eating quiz: list healthy and unhealthy food, then write 2–3 questions (How often do you…? Do you…?) with a/b/c answers. 2 pairs share one question aloud.

💪 Support: fill the quiz template with word support. ⭐ Extension: write the full quiz with a title + question marks.
