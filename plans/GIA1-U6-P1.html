Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A picture IN the wall — OK?” → No: ON the wall (on = поверхность).
“The dog is near TO the house?” → No — near the house, без to.
“In front the door или in front OF the door?” → OF обязательно, это «одно слово».
“Can I say ‘a furniture’?” → No — furniture uncountable: some furniture / a piece of furniture.

Assumptions: Post-GMF4: базовые комнаты и there is/are в активе, house-лексика частично знакома. Реальные дыры: точные предлоги, furniture-слова (ceiling, hall, shower), связная описательная речь. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (-ing spelling / missing ‘be’ / question word order), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
