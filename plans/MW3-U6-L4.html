Week 15.02–21.02 · Lesson 4🎵 Song focus · The toast song · Do you want (chicken)? Yes, please / No, thank you~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 30 (/ɜː/ er ir ur · p.83–85) · Genki: Eat! Drink! Dance!(57) · game: Mimi says!
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act the story song «The toast song» (CD3 T8) with full actions, and offer & accept/refuse food: “Do you want chicken? — Yes, please. / No, thank you. I want toast!”
  • Subsidiary. Re-tell the story; play Mimi says! (Simon Says) & Do you want pizza?; sing Genki «Eat! Drink! Dance!» (57); circle the smiley faces to show what Mimi wants & answer questions (PB p.40); Phonics Hero — /ɜː/ er ir ur (consolidation), speed-read p.83–85.
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions, then words. Repeat the song several times without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (yummy voice, whisper, super-fast). After the big acting, use the calm breathing reset before the table.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing «The toast song» with the food actions;
  • offer food — “Do you want chicken?”;
  • answer “Yes, please. / No, thank you.”

Form

Pron. watch

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — /ɜː/ er ir ur again, faster: blend (f-ur → fur, ch-ur-ch → church, th-ir-d → third) → speed-read p.83–85.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I offer you salad — you don't want it. Do you say «no» or «no, thank you»?” → No, thank you!
“Someone gives you toast you love — «yes» or «yes, please»?” → Yes, please!

Assumptions: Story met in L3 — today deepens it through the song. Children know the ten foods & the Simon-Says format from earlier units. Do you want…? Yes, please / No, thank you is today's focus. Reading continues on /ɜː/. Pre-readers follow the song by pictures & actions, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by repetition of the songChange the mode each time: yummy voice, whisper, super-fast, slow-motion.
Only a few singGive everyone an action job; sing the actions even without the words.
Mimi says — same few winMistake = help, not out; keep everyone standing and re-joining so all mime.
Drops «please / thank you»Puppet always models the polite word with a little bow; child copies bow + words.
Over-excited after actingUse the breathing reset — breathe in… breathe out… four times before the table.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning! Do you want toast? Yes, please! Do you want yuck-food? No, thank you! Show me your yummy face… your yuck face! Yummy/yuck faces, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 30 · /ɜː/ (again) — sounds er · ir · ur  (all say /ɜː/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: f-ur → fur, ch-ur-ch → church, th-ir-d → third, h-er → her — sweep + a picture. ▶ Speed-read race — beat Speaky's clap-clock reading the /ɜː/ row.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.83–85 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read every · mother together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

24–31′
Game · Mimi says! (Simon Says) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Mimi says! Say Mimi says… eat the chicken! children mime eating chicken. Say just Eat the chicken (no “Mimi says”) — they stand still, arms folded. Mistake = a friendly wobble, keep going. Practise offering every food.

31–37′
Game · Do you want pizza? (pass the plate)
carpet, seated circle

▶ Do you want pizza? Pass a food flashcard round the circle. Speaky asks a child Do you want pizza? that child answers Yes, please! or No, thank you! then asks the next child with a new card. Calm, seated, everyone gets a turn.

37–44′
Song — perform «The toast song» + Genki «Eat! Drink! Dance!» (57)
carpet, standing

Food boost. Sing Genki «Eat! Drink! Dance!» (57) — a joyful food-and-movement boost.

Table time · Pupil's Book p.40 🧘

Listen, point & sing. Play the song; children point to the food pictures & sing along (karaoke version when confident).

Circle & answer. children circle the smiley / sad face to show the food Mimi wants and doesn't want, then answer Do you want chicken? — Yes, please! Do you want fish? — No, thank you!

Calm down. a slow breathing reset — Breathe in… breathe out… four times — before we finish.
