Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to say what they want to see at the museum — dinosaurs · fish (life under the sea) · planets (space) — and ask & answer: “Do you want to see the fish? — Yes, please. / No, thank you. I want to learn about dinosaurs.” — a life skill: choosing what interests me.
  • Subsidiary. Sort museum things (dinosaurs / sea / space) on floor hoops; watch Auntie Vicky's video with Alex & Lucy; play Museum walk! & Sticky Fingers (30); sing Genki «Where Are You Going?» (56); match & colour the museum parts (PB p.49); Phonics Hero — Magic-E dictation, speed-read p.64–65.
  • Personal. First proper speaking focus of the unit — never force a shy child; a point at the hoop counts. Model with the puppet first. Keep each exchange ~20 seconds, then swap partner.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say what I want to see — dinosaurs / fish / planets;
  • ask “Do you want to see the fish?”;
  • answer “Yes, please. / No, thank you. I want to learn about dinosaurs.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions

“I say Do you want to see the fish? — happy yes, or a thing I'm not keen on?” → Yes, please!

Assumptions: All ten places & Do you want to go to the…? are secure from L1–L4; the museum (from the story) is familiar. Do you want to see…? I want to learn about… and museum things (dinosaurs / fish / planets) are today's focus; children meet Alex, Lucy & Auntie Vicky doing the same in the video. Reading is Magic-E dictation.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Museum ‘parts’ too abstractMake it physical: one hoop = dinosaurs, one = sea/fish, one = space/planets; children WALK to what they want to see.
Forgets the polite answerPuppet always models Yes, please! (nod) / No, thank you! (polite wave) — children copy the gesture.
Mixes see / learn aboutBoth are fine: I want to SEE the fish (point to eyes) · I want to LEARN about fish (tap head) — do the gesture.
Shy to speak to a peerAsk the puppet first, then a friend; pointing to the hoop is a full answer.
Over-excited minglingSmall space, walking feet, Freeze on the tambourine.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Today — a day at the MUSEUM! Dinosaurs… fish in the sea… planets in space! What do you want to see? Dino / fishy / rocket mimes, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lesson 23 · Magic-E dictation — sounds cap → cape · kit → kite · hop → hope. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Say a short word, then add the magic e: cap → cape, kit → kite, hop → hope — the vowel says its name. Sweep + a picture. ▶ Magic-swap — Speaky says a short word; children add the silent e in the air and read the new long word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.64–65 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read said · here together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.64–65 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · museum things + video
carpet → floor hoops

Sort the museum. Three hoops on the floor: DINOSAURS · SEA (fish) · SPACE (planets). Hold up a museum picture: Do you want to see the dinosaurs? …the fish? …the planets? Place it in the right hoop as children point.

Watch Alex & Lucy. Watch the video — Auntie Vicky, Alex & Lucy visit a museum. Ask What does Alex want to see? …Lucy? …Auntie Vicky? children guess, then check.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): Navio App — play the town-places vocabulary game and watch the A day out story video on screen; children tap the place they hear.

25–32′
Game · Museum walk! (dinosaurs ↔ sea ↔ space) ⚡
whole room

▶ Museum walk! One corner = DINOSAURS, one = SEA, one = SPACE. Hold a museum picture & ask Do you want to see the planets? — Yes, please! children walk to what they want to see & say it. Everyone moves every round.

💪 Support: walk & nod Yes! ⭐ Extension: say the full Yes, please — I want to see the planets!

32–39′
Game · Sticky Fingers (30)
carpet, seated circle

▶ Sticky Fingers Children each hold one of Speaky's outstretched fingers. Speaky asks one child Do you want to see the fish? — Yes, please! — that child answers, then the puppet tries to escape and everyone giggles. Calm, seated, everyone gets a turn quickly.

39–45′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Under the Sea!» aloud — Speaky dives down and meets the fish and the coral — a calm, wondrous read that matches the ‘life under the sea’ part of our museum, right before the table.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Look! A fish! Look under the sea!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Children point to each sea creature & echo Look! A fish!; wow together at the coral — a quiet recall of I want to see the fish.
