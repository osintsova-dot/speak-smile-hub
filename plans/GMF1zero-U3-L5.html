Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated all Unit 3 language — the 10 body parts, “What's this? — It's my…”, “I've got a (red nose)”, “He's / She's got (green eyes) and (short hair)” — and can use it confidently in the review tasks and the quiz.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics Hero ур.22 (b) закрепление — Magic E o-e·u-e: match + dictation + story «Милый мул» (тетрадь стр.62–63); sight words eight, every; self-assessment (My progress traffic light, AB p. 33); listening for detail (PB p. 36).
  • Personal. Run the flagship quiz by PIES: every child writes/shows every answer (Showdown), the responder is picked by Numbered Heads — no “captains answer everything”; keep the reward moment short so language time wins.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all 10 body parts and answer “What's this?” without support;
  • say “I've got a (big red nose)” and “He's / She's got…”;
  • describe a friend's hair & eyes;
  • read Magic E words (nose, home, cube, five, cake, kite);
  • self-assess with the traffic light — ready for the Unit 3 test.

Structure
What's this? — It's my (nose). · I've got a (red nose). · He's / She's got (green eyes) and (short hair).
Form
keep ’ve / ’s got; plural parts no “a” (big ears); join facts with and.
Pron. watch
keep the ’ve/’s: I've got, she's got; voiced /z/ on plurals (eyes, ears); blonde = one beat.

33–51′
🏆 FLAGSHIP QUIZ with reward
📺 teams · projector

Set-up (2 min). 3 teams of 4, numbered heads 1–4, one mini-whiteboard EACH. Quiz grid on screen — categories: Body 🧍 · I've got ❓ · He's/She's got 👧 · Sounds 🔤. Rules + ICQ: Who writes? — Everybody! When do you show? — On “Showdown!”

Round flow (Kagan Showdown + Numbered Heads — all answer every question). Question up (e.g. body card: What's this? · describe a face: She's got long hair and…? · sound: Write the magic-e word — n-o-s-e! · read a Magic E word). Every child writes/draws → Showdown! → all boards up → call a number: Number 3! — that child answers aloud in a full sentence.

Predict (thinking). On a half-hidden face, before revealing: Look — just the eyes! Long hair or short hair? Write your guess! Then reveal — Were you right?

Reward shot. Correct = the answering child takes the shot: animated bowling / football on screen — knocked pins = team points, class counts in English: One, two, three… five! (No projector → soft-toy pins + ball.) Rotate teams; over ~10–12 questions every child throws at least once.

Support ▸ a body / word bank stays visible so weaker writers copy, not freeze.   Extension ▸ when a strong pupil is “Number”, require the full sentence — She's got long blonde hair and blue eyes.
🔨 Flagship interactive · projector · BUILD · “Unit 3 Team Quiz + Reward”  🔨 BUILD
Screen 1 — Grid: 4×3 board of numbered tiles under the 4 category headers (Body 🧍 / I've got ❓ / He's-She's got 👧 / Sounds 🔤). Tap a tile → question. Used tiles grey out.
Screen 2 — Answer: “Show your boards!” + a 5-second timer; ✔ / ✗ to score; a full-sentence model appears for choral echo.
Screen 3 — Reward: bowling — child “rolls”, pins fall (or a football penalty reskin). One shot = +points.
Screen 4 — Scoreboard: 3 team bars, huge numbers from the back row, + a “Teamwork ⭐” toggle.
Teacher: host only — YOU tap tiles & call the number so no captain dominates. Offline reserve: printed grid + soft-toy pins + ball. Flagship quiz runs only on Review day.

Finish with totals + teamwork star: Give me five, champions!
