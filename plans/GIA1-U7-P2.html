  • Personal. Анти-нытьё: «was/were вы говорите — а порядок слов в Wh-вопросе и краткий ответ пишете чисто за 10 секунд?» Письменная точность = миссия дня.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Some gladiators … very popular — was или were?” → WERE (some gladiators = they).
“Was Doug the Pug the first dog in space? — Yes, he wasn't?” → No! Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.
“Where you were yesterday — правильно?” → No: Where WERE you yesterday?

Assumptions: Устно was/were звучит с GMF4, но письменно ломается согласование (there was/were, some gladiators) и порядок слов в вопросе. Feelings частично знакомы (tired, hungry), но -ed-прилагательные как система и pleased/worried/relaxed — новые. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (irregular forms / -ed spelling / was–were agreement), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
