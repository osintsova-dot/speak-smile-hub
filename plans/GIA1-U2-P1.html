👩‍🏫 Учитель — по шагам:
  1. Вывожу 8 определений: my father's sister · my brother's son · my sister's daughter · my uncle's children · my parents' parents (2 words) · my son's daughter · father and mother (1 word) · my aunt's son.
  2. Говорю: WRITE the word for each. You've known ‘mother–father’ for years — now prove NIECE and NEPHEW in writing. Three minutes! Таймер.
  3. Ключ: aunt · nephew · niece · cousins · grandparents · granddaughter · parents · cousin. Самопроверка, счёт на поля.
  4. Разворот: 8 of 8? …NIECE with -ie-, NEPHEW out of nowhere — THIS is our game: not ‘knowing family’, but fast-accurate-written. Rematch in Pair 6!
📺 Показываем: слайд: 8 определений → ключ (или столбиком на доске, ключ дописываю)
👦 Дети: соло пишут 8 слов на скорость, честно считают чистые (приватно). Ожидаемо: 4–6 из 8 — потолок вскрыт. Типичная ошибка: «neice/nefew» — не стыжу: «вот зона роста, реванш в P6».

  • Main aim. By the end, crews can pitch a family for a TV casting in 60 seconds without pauses, using 17 family words + 3 adjective pairs accurately (spelling included).

Pron. watch
/ə/ endings — mother, father, brother, sister, daughter (no ‘r’!); nephew /ˈnefjuː/; niece /niːs/; aunt /ɑːnt/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“You KNOW family words. Can you WRITE niece and daughter right in 10 seconds?” → that's the gate.
“Cousin — he or she?” → both, one word.
“In a casting pitch, is ‘my brother is nice’ enough?” → No — producers give facts: age, looks, one juicy detail.

Assumptions: Post-GMF4: basic family words met in GMF1–2, describing appearance in GMF4 U1. Real gaps: SPELLING (niece/nephew/granddaughter), the full 17-word system, sustained fluent speech. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (spelling/speed/writing), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
