WHAT WE DO: Showdown as a review: high-five; blitz-rewind of P5: the 3 letter-moves in chorus + one capitals “crime” + a pre-flight echo

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. At the door — a high-five + one word: project mood?
  2. I frame it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYONE writes at the same time, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift them together — I see everyone's answer, no hiding.
  3. Rewind of P5: The three message moves? — Start! Why! Ask to write! One “crime” on mini-boards: i'm from tokyo → Boards UP!
  4. Pre-flight echo: 3 flashes — nationality in writing · an opposite · a short answer. +1 for clean ones.
  5. Bridge: You've pitched, broadcast, interrogated, gone undercover and published. Final creative mission: FOUND YOUR OWN NATION.
📺 On screen: mini-boards · the crime line on the board
👦 Students do: give a high-five, answer in chorus, fix, warm up. Expected: warmed up within 6 minutes. Typical error: only half-fixing it — “TWO crimes!”

desks · trios

WHAT WE DO: trios choose 5 sections using ONLY Student's Book phrases; the country name — a minimum of 3 options put to a vote

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I hand out the Key phrases tent cards, announce the currency: Decisions happen ONLY in Key phrases. A Russian decision doesn't count!
  2. Task (ex.2–3, 5): choose 5 sections from the model's menu.
  3. Name auction: THREE name options minimum, then vote. First idea is a hostage, not a winner! (TB: it's easier to invent a name AFTER decisions about the country.)
  4. I pay out teamwork points for phrases heard.
📺 On screen: Key phrases tent cards · sections menu slide (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: haggle with phrases, vote for a name out of three. Expected: “No, that's not very original!” with relish. Typical error: a decision in Russian — we replay it in English within 20 sec, they laugh, it works.

Step 4: presentations, judges on 4 axes
🏁 PRODUCT · presentations on the 4 TB axes
stage

WHAT WE DO: Timed Pair Share: 3 min of rehearsal, then every trio member speaks their OWN 60 sec; judges score Layout/Content/Language/Visuals, 1–3

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. 3-min rehearsal: Everyone speaks SIXTY seconds. Keywords only. Toes to the audience! TB Challenge (p.30): in the video the presenters EXPANDED on what was written — do the same, don't read the poster.
  2. Format — Kagan Timed Pair Share: A speaks for their timed turn, B is the active listener (counts pauses on their fingers, catches the best phrase), then Switch roles! The listener's response starts with My favourite bit was…
  3. Presentations: a stopwatch on everyone; judging trios with the TB card: Layout · Content · Language · Visuals, 1–3 + peer questions ex.10 (how is it different from a real country? would you want to visit — why?).
  4. Judges defend one score out loud after every trio; a live bonus structure = +1.
  5. Points to the leaderboard between presentations.
📺 On screen: posters · stopwatch · TB judging cards for the 4 axes · peer cards
👦 Students do: hold their personal 60 seconds, judge across 4 professional axes. Expected: “Language 3 — negatives AND a perfect line!”. Typical error: reading off the poster — the Language axis suffers, a neighbour holds the poster.
🏆 The bar for the product (the judges' rubric, every pair): Fluency — pauses no longer than 3 sec (1–3) · Accuracy — clean forms (1–3) · Richness — adjectives, connectors, bonus structures (1–3). 9/9 = a “gold run”, +5 to the leaderboard.		posters · stopwatch · judge cards

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
