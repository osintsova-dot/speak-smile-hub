🎡 Mimi's Wheel 3 · Unit 6 “Meals” · Lesson 1 — Vocabulary · food on the Big Wheel
Pre-primary / early years (~5–6 y.o., pre-readers) · 60 min · Genki recipe · Disco warm-up → phonics → curiosity input → Genki games → song → craft · TPR + puppet · Phonics Hero 2.0 (reading: recognise → blend → speed-read) · carpet + tables
Week 01.02–07.02 · Lesson 1✨ NEW · toast · chicken · pancakes · salad · yogurt · rice~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 29 (/aʊ/ ou ow · /ɔɪ/ oi oy · p.79–82) · Genki: Do You Like Food?(12)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name six foods — toast · chicken · pancakes · salad · yogurt · rice — and match each to its colour on the Big Wheel: “Where's the toast? — It's on red!”
  • Subsidiary. Mime tasting each food (yum / yuck); play Say the colour!, Repeat if it's true! & Genki Island Hopping (23); sing Genki «Do You Like Food?» (12); Phonics Hero — read the /aʊ/ & /ɔɪ/ spellings ou ow · oi oy, blend & speed-read p.79–82; stick the food stickers on the wheel & play Memory (PB p.37).
  • Personal. Tiny teacher-talk, big TPR — Speaky serves each food off a pretend plate, children taste it (mmm / yuck). Genki recipe: disco first, phonics early, input through curiosity, energy up in the games then down for the table. Every food gets its own big mime and its own colour.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name and mime six foods;
  • say a food for a colour — “It's on red — toast!”;
  • join in Speaky's picnic story & song.

Structure
Where's the (toast)? — It's on (red).  ·  Do you like (yogurt)?  ·  I like (rice). Mmm!
Form
Where's the + food → It's on + colour. Food words as single naming chunks. No new tense — just naming what I eat.
Pron. watch
clear final sounds: toas-t, yoghur-t, chicken; two beats in pan-cakes, sa-lad; keep colour words short.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 (our reading workbook) — the /aʊ/ sound written ou · ow and the /ɔɪ/ sound written oi · oy. Show the Jolly actions, blend words (c-l-ou-d → cloud, c-ow → cow, c-oi-n → coin, t-oy → toy) with a meaning picture, then speed-read p.79–82. Reading from zero, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Yogurt — do I eat it with a spoon or with a fork?” → a spoon!
“Where's the rice — can you guess the colour?” → yellow!

Assumptions: Children come straight from Unit 5 (Daily routines) where they met have breakfast / lunch / dinner — re-used today to frame the food. Colours are secure and re-used to pin food to the wheel. The six foods are new but very picturable/mimeable. They already know all 26 phonic sounds; today they read the /aʊ/ & /ɔɪ/ spellings.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 29 · /aʊ/ & /ɔɪ/ — sounds ou · ow  (/aʊ/)  ·  oi · oy  (/ɔɪ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds into a word: c-l-ou-d → cloud, c-ow → cow, c-oi-n → coin, t-oy → toy — big arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Blend & jump — Speaky says an /aʊ/ or /ɔɪ/ word slowly; children blend it with the actions and jump on the whole word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.79–82 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read does · their together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–25′
Curiosity input · six foods + wheel
carpet → Big Wheel mat

Guess & taste! Slowly slide a food flashcard out of a lunchbox — What's in my lunchbox? Guess! …TOAST! Reveal + taste it together (mmm / yuck!), whole class echoes the word. Do all six (toast · chicken · pancakes · salad · yogurt · rice).

Food on the wheel. Lay the six flashcards on the wheel — one per colour. Point to each pod: Where's the toast? It's on red! Where's the chicken? It's on blue! The rice hides behind the ? — guess it!

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the interactive Big Wheel on screen with the six food pods (red = toast · blue = chicken · orange = pancakes · green = salad · pink = yogurt · yellow = rice) — children point & answer It's on red — toast!. The wheel comes from the coursebook app; our songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: taste & copy the puppet. ⭐ Extension: say the food for a colour — It's on green — salad!

25–32′
Game · Say the colour! + Island Hopping (23) ⚡
carpet, moving

▶ Say the colour! / Repeat if it's true! Name the food on the wheel — children shout its colour; then reverse (say the colour, they name the food). Fast, everyone chorus. Then I like toast! I like toast! / I don't like yogurt! — children repeat ONLY if it's true for them, or fold arms & stay silent.

▶ Island Hopping Genki Island Hopping: every child has their own pile of food mini-cards (their «islands»). Speaky calls a food — children jump to that card, hold it up & shout it. Everyone plays every round.

32–38′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Do you like food?» aloud — Speaky and his puppy have a picnic — toast, apples, cheese, sandwiches. The perfect calm read to open our meals unit, straight after the jumping game.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Do you like food? Yes! Mmm, yummy!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each spread — children taste-mime each food (crunch, slurp, mmm) and echo the line Do you like food? Yummy!; on the last page name every food in the picnic basket together.
