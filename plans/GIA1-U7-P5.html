🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The test is quiet and honest, no games DURING it. The revenge after is the emotional payoff: a mistake becomes ammunition, not a disgrace. Cover the verb wall for the duration of the test — it comes back for the revenge quiz.
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Don't open yet.” “Sweep one — every irregular verb: is that the real form? Sweep two — was or were: check the subject.” “ONE clean sentence beyond the book in Part 4 (a ‘didn't’ or a ‘Did…?’ from GMF4) = +1 bonus. Max one.”

Opening · a calm entry + mini-review
desks · calm

WHAT WE DO: quiet music and “readiness 1–5”; a silent 5-item mini-review; a bridge — “after the test — revenge”

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Quiet music before they come in. At the door: One word — how do you feel?
  2. Show test-readiness 1–5. Whatever you show — the Quick review round yesterday says you're ready.
  3. A silent review, 5 flash items on a draft, solo: 1 Laika ___ a street dog. 2 Gladiators ___ (−) boring. 3 study → ? 4 write → ? 5 12.03 in words. Key on screen, self-check.
  4. Bridge: That was a mini-test — passed. Same formats, bigger paper… and afterwards, REVENGE. Strategy first.
  5. I cover the verb wall with a sheet: The wall sleeps until the revenge quiz.
📺 On screen: quiet music · slide with 5 items → key (Backup: written on the board) · drafts · a sheet for the verb wall
👦 Students do: come in calmly, work silently, check themselves. Expected: 4–5 out of 5 — confidence instead of panic.

WHAT WE DO: one example from each part; a strategy card: the example first → skip and return → two sweeps (irregulars → was/were)

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

WHAT WE DO: 30 sec to study the task before the audio (subskill!); a news report twice: 3 opinion matches + 5 phrases to speakers

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

  3. I remind them: Unsure? Number and question mark. Fix it on the second listen.
  4. I wrap up: Pens down. Part 4 next.

Self-mark + ammo box
desks · pairs

WHAT WE DO: swap work, check against the key in coloured pens; every pair throws 2 of their most annoying errors into an anonymous “box”

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Swap with a neighbour, coloured pens, the key in parts on screen: Say the correct answer quietly as you tick — marking is learning round two.
  2. I collect Part 4 (it comes back with two stars and one wish).
  3. While they're checking: Each pair — your two UGLIEST errors on slips. Into the AMMO BOX. Anonymous.
  4. I take the sheet off the verb wall: The wall wakes up.
📺 On screen: key slides (Backup: read aloud in parts) · coloured pens · the “ammo box” · verb wall uncovered
👦 Students do: honestly check someone else's work, hand in their ammunition. Expected: the box is full in 3 minutes. Typical error: “we have no errors” — “hand in your two hardest spots”.

58–76′
🏁 REVENGE QUIZ · beat your own test
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: I turn the box's errors into questions on the fly; crews write FULL corrections; hit 80% — the test is beaten

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ⚡ Stand up for 60 seconds: shake your hands, three deep breaths — the test is over, the game begins.
  2. I frame it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYONE writes at once, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift together — I see everyone's answer, no hiding.
  3. I announce: REVENGE. The test beat some of you. Now WE beat the test. Errors on the board belong to the whole class.
  4. I pull a slip, rephrase it; crews write the FULL correction on mini-boards in 15 sec: Not ‘B’ — write: She TOOK a lot of photos.
  5. Rules: streak ×2 after three in a row · a steal on a miss · any verb not on the wall gets added to it immediately.
  6. A “X out of Y beaten” counter on the board: 80% of the box → THE TEST IS OFFICIALLY BEATEN!
📺 On screen: the box · mini-boards · counter on the board · verb wall · leaderboard
👦 Students do: finish off their own errors as teams with full corrections. Expected: loud, exciting, catharsis at 80%. Typical error: a letter instead of a correction — doesn't count.
ammo box · mini-boards

Unit 7 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: a job from its definition (I work in a hospital → doctor; I have ideas and make new things → inventor) — mirrors SB p.84 ex.2 and WB p.58 Vocabulary review. B) 4 items: dates in words (27.05 → the twenty-seventh of May; 01.10.2015 → the first of October, twenty fifteen). C) 4 items: feelings in context (I drink water when I'm ___ / Students are often ___ before a test) — SB p.89 ex.3.
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 6 gaps: was/were/wasn't/weren't in a mini-text about a celebrity of the past (format SB p.88 ex.3 “The Beatles”). B) 4 items: make a question with was/were from the given words + give a true short answer (half Wh-, half Yes/No) — SB p.88 ex.5–6. C) 4 items: past simple of regular verbs with spelling traps (study · stop · plan · play). D) 4 items: past simple of irregular verbs (go · take · write · meet) — list SB pp.126–127.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A news item in the style of SB p.89 ex.6–7 (three teens about a week without a phone; the teacher reads it twice if there’s no track): 3 items — match the speaker to the opinion (good / bad / mixed) + 5 items — match phrases a–e to speakers 1–3.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A description of a fictional character (35+ words, 3 paragraphs) — mirrors SB p.92 Skills boost. Marked for: past simple forms, regular and irregular (4) · position of also (2) · three-paragraph structure (2) · task complete + overall clarity (2).

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.93 — every format was met in class during the unit. The two lines: 🚀 higher (+ Wh-questions in writing, more spelling traps) · 🐣 standard.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
They rush and lose the “easy” marksStrategy card, point 3: two sweeps — exactly their traps; say it BEFORE handing out the papers.
Fast finishers get in the wayA quiet bonus sheet: 8 irregular verbs for speed + a mini date-in-words task.
The temptation to “improve” while self-markingColoured pens only; the objective parts are checked by a swap neighbour.
The revenge stings the error-makersAll items are anonymous and REPHRASED; rule: “an error on the board belongs to the whole class”.
Leaderboard losers feel hurtAlways two podiums: top score AND top growth (measured by the gate deltas from P1–P3).
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
