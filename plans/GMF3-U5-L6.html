Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated Unit 5 — 12 sea animals, describing adjectives, and comparatives (-er · -ier · more … than) — and shown it in the flagship Team Quiz with reward game.
  • Subsidiary. AB Review «Ready, Steady, Go!» (p.56): name sea animals, complete the comparative chart, compare a shark & a starfish, break Luke's code (→ NEW ZEALAND, a bridge to Unit 6 — extreme sports!); spelling check; self-assessment — readiness for the L7 test.
  • Personal. Quiz = every child answers (Showdown boards), not captains only; keep the reward game short and joyful — it's the cherry, not the cake. Both teams celebrate at the end.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name unit words from pictures AND spell key ones;
  • make all three comparative types (-er · -ier · more);
  • self-assess honestly with the traffic light.

Form

Pron. watch

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Is the quiz a test?” → no — it's training with fun; mistakes are how we learn.
“Who answers in the quiz — the captain?” → EVERYONE writes/answers.

Assumptions: All unit language introduced; today is consolidation before the test. Pupils know Showdown mechanics and the alphabet code from earlier lessons.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Quiz excitement → noise spiralFixed rules up front: answer only after “Show me!”; volume arm; one warning = minus point.
Weaker pupils hide in teamsShowdown: every child writes every answer on their own board; team point = majority correct.
Wrong comparative rule under pressureThree-rule strip stays on the board: -er · -ier · more; point to it, don't say the answer.
Reward game dragsOne roll/kick per win, 20 seconds, done; score big on the board.
Sore losersBoth teams get the «Well done!» cheer + stickers; “we beat the QUIZ together”.

24–46′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 2 teams

Flagship quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles: picture→animal, animal→spelling, make a comparison (-er / -ier / more), true/false comparison, describe-the-animal, break-the-code. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/says on their board → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a bowling / penalty shot (paper ball into a box). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Offline setup: card grid on the whiteboard + a real paper-ball penalty into a box/basket — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on “Show me!”)

Finale. Count scores together in English; both teams: Give me five!
