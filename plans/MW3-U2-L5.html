Week 12.10–18.10 · Lesson 13🗣 Life skills · saying what you want~8 pupils · mingle + pairsPH 15 (ng oo · p.39–42) · Genki: What Do You Want To Do?(59) · games: Stand up if…·Teddy Dancing(35)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to ask & answer about what they want to do: “Do you want to (paint)? — No, I want to (sing)!” — a life skill: saying kindly what you'd like.
  • Subsidiary. Role-play Auntie Vicky's rainy-day chat with the puppet (CD1 T30); sing Genki «What Do You Want To Do?» (59); play Stand up if you want to… & Teddy Dancing; Phonics Hero — ng · oo, speed-read p.39–42; circle what Alex & Lucy want, colour what YOU want (PB p.9).
  • Personal. First speaking-focus lesson of the unit — never force a shy child; non-verbal (point / do the action) counts (TB tip: give time to speak). Model with the puppet first; keep exchanges ~20 seconds, then swap.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Do you want to (paint)?”;
  • refuse kindly — “No, I don't.”;
  • say what you want — “I want to (sing)!”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“It's raining — can we play outside?” → no! We choose an INSIDE activity.
“I don't want to paint — do I shout or say it kindly?” → say it kindly!

Assumptions: Do you want to…? met in the story & song (L3–L4); children can produce parts. Today builds the whole polite ask-refuse-choose exchange as a life skill. Reading has reached digraphs ng·oo.

“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Oh no — it's RAINING! We can't play outside… so what do YOU want to do inside? Mime rain, then dance inside.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 15 — sounds ng · oo (letter-teams). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Blend the team: r-i-ng → ring, s-o-ng → song, m-oo-n → moon, b-oo-k → book — sweep + a picture. ▶ Team hop — hop to the letter-team Speaky calls, then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.39–42 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read who · which together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · Auntie Vicky's rainy day
carpet

Watch the puppet. Speaky is Auntie Vicky: It's raining! We can't play outside. Do you want to draw? Offer activities one by one; children refuse and choose: No, I don't. I want to sing!

Feeling voices. try it with feeling — sleepy voice, happy voice, giant voice; children copy the puppet.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): play the unit videos, songs & the Big Wheel game on screen — the story «A new friend», the Katy Kangaroo song and Go in and out the window come from the coursebook app (Navio); our Genki songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

26–31′
Game · Stand up if you want to… (TB)
carpet, standing

▶ Stand up if… Give activity cards to some children. Stand up if you want to DANCE! The child with that card stands, shows it & says I want to dance! Everyone with a want reacts every round.

Mix activities fast — lots of standing, sitting, showing cards.

 activity cards · Speaky
31–37′
Game · Teddy Dancing (35) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Teddy Dancing Genki Teddy Dancing for want to: activity cards on the floor. The class asks What do you want to do? — a child runs, grabs the card they want, does the action back to Speaky: I want to run!

Everyone asks together, then each child chooses & moves — every child speaks & moves.

💪 Support: grab a card & do the action. ⭐ Extension: say the full I want to (run)!

37–45′
Song — Genki «What Do You Want To Do?» (mingle)
carpet, mingle

Meet & ask. Play Genki «What Do You Want To Do?» (59): children mingle; on the pause they meet the nearest friend and do the exchange Do you want to paint? — No, I want to sing! New partner each pause.

✔ Assess: who asks AND chooses? who needs the puppet nearby? Note it — celebrate every attempt.
