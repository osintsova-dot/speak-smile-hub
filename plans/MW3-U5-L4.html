Week 18.01–24.01 · Lesson 4🎵 Song focus · Giant Joe's day · Do you wash your face? Yes, I do~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 26 (/aɪ/ ie igh y i-e · p.72–74) · Genki: Routines(110) · game: Giant Joe says! · Ball & Music(16)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to sing & act the story song «Giant Joe's day» (CD2 T22) with full actions, and answer about their day: “Do you wash your face? — Yes, I do.”
  • Subsidiary. Re-tell the story; play Giant Joe says! (Simon Says) & Ball & Music (16); sing Genki «Routines» (110); circle the routines Giant Joe does & answer questions (PB p.32); Phonics Hero — /aɪ/ ie igh y i-e (consolidation), speed-read p.72–74.
  • Personal. Sing in stages — join first with actions, then words. Repeat the song several times without it feeling repetitive by changing HOW (giant voice, whisper, super-fast). After the big acting, use the calm breathing reset before the table.
By the end, pupils can…
  • sing «Giant Joe's day» with the routine actions;
  • act out being Giant Joe;
  • answer “Do you wash your face? — Yes, I do.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Giant Joe — does he do his routines in the morning or never?” → every morning!
“I ask Do you brush your teeth? — happy yes or a routine you skip?” → Yes, I do!

Assumptions: Story met in L3 — today deepens it through the song. Children know the ten routines & the Simon-Says format from earlier units. Reading continues on /aɪ/. Pre-readers follow the song by pictures & actions, not text.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Bored by repetition of the songChange the mode each time: giant voice, whisper, super-fast, slow-motion.
Only a few singGive everyone an action job; sing the actions even without the words.
Giant Joe says — same few winMistake = help, not out; keep everyone standing and re-joining so all mime.
Over-excited after actingUse the breathing reset — breathe in… breathe out… four times before the table.
Yes/No answered in RussianPuppet models Yes, I do! with a big nod; child copies the nod + words.

“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Aye, ti, tee, to! I'm a giant — my name is Joe! Make yourself BIG… stomp… stretch tall! Giant march on the spot.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

▶ Story flash. re-tell «Giant Joe» fast: mime the routines; every time you say It's time to…, class mimes it big.

Wave & recall. wave your arms every time you hear a routine as we skim the story — active recall.

 story cards · Speaky
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 26 · long /aɪ/ (again) — sounds ie · igh · y · i-e  (all say /aɪ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: p-ie → pie, n-igh-t → night, f-l-y → fly, b-i-ke → bike — sweep + a picture. ▶ Speed-read race — beat Speaky's clap-clock reading the /aɪ/ row.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.72–74 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read review Yellow tricky words together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

24–31′
Game · Giant Joe says! (Simon Says) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Giant Joe says! Say Giant Joe says… have a shower! children mime having a shower. Say just Have a shower (no “Giant Joe says”) — they stand still, hands on heads. Mistake = a friendly wobble, keep going. Practise every routine.

 Speaky · open space
31–37′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Good Morning, Speaky» aloud — Speaky wakes and moves through his morning — greetings + morning routine, a calm read to settle after Giant Joe says.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Good morning! I get up. I wash my face.. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Children wave Good morning! and mime each morning routine with Speaky — a quiet recall of the day's language.

 «Good Morning, Speaky» (Speak & Smile Genki story-book · brhello.pdf) · Speaky
37–44′
Song — perform «Giant Joe's day» + Genki «Routines» (110)
carpet, standing

Sing & act. Play «Giant Joe's day» (CD2 T22): Round 1 — actions only (march, mime routines, rub tummy on yum, yum, yum); Round 2 — add the words; Round 3 — giant voice. Two volunteers are Giant Joe; everyone moves throughout.

Routine boost. Sing Genki «Routines» (110) — Little Red Riding Hood's day, a joyful routine boost.

44–54′
Table time · Pupil's Book p.32 🧘
tables

Listen, point & sing. Play the song; children point to the routine pictures & sing along (karaoke version when confident).

Circle & answer. children circle the routines Giant Joe does every day, then — as Giant Joe — answer Do you wash your hands? — Yes, I do! Do you have dinner? — Yes, I do!

Calm down. a slow breathing reset — Breathe in… breathe out… four times — before we finish.

54–58′
Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Ask your partner “Do you brush your teeth?” — Yes, I do! — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can sing «Giant Joe's day»! I can say Yes, I do! colour one star.

Beautiful singing, giant stars! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
