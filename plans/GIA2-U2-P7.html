🎯 Why this lesson looks like this — the design in one card
Why it is built this way (for the teacher): The WDYT project from the book is turned into a research company: the crew designs a survey, collects REAL data from the class and defends its summary. The unit's super skill (collaboration: planning and sharing out the work) works as a condition of delivery — a company without roles physically runs out of time. Quick review is sewn into the middle as stations, as «the company audit before publication»: revision for the test without the word «test».
How to announce it (say this, 30–40 seconds):
“Free Time Inc. opens today. Your crew is a research company and this class is your data. Six questions minimum — and all three types: yes-or-no, options, and open. Then you go out, collect real answers, and publish a summary the class will read. One rule from the boss: a company without roles doesn't finish. Decide who does what in the first two minutes. Clock's running.”
In simpler words, if the class needs support: We open a research company: your own survey of 6 questions of three types, collecting real answers from the class and publishing a summary. A company without roles runs out of time — share out the roles in two minutes.

Design · the company survey
crews

WHAT WE DO: STEP 1–2: going through the model survey (ex.2) → choosing a topic → 6 questions of their own, three types, round the circle on one form

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. ex.2 on the model survey SB p.35: Which question is yes-or-no? Which gives options? Which is open? (TB key: a — question 1 · b — 3 and 4 · c — 2, 5, 6; the words: a Who · b What · c How many · d Why).
  2. Topics to choose from (SB ex.4): creative hobbies · music · social media · socialising · sports.
  3. The six questions are written through Kagan RoundTable: one form per crew, going round the circle — each student writes their own question and passes it on. Six questions = at least one from each of them, nobody can sit it out.
  4. The table phrases (Super skills) as Kagan Talking Chips: everyone gets 3 chips, and a chip goes into the middle for every contribution — and only with a phrase from the card: We can discuss first and then write. · What do you think? · Can you write down our ideas? · We need to move to the next question. Out of chips — you listen; if you still have chips, the crew pulls you in.
  5. The check before they go out: Show me your three types. No types, no field work.
📺 On screen: SB pp.34–35 · phrase cards · Project planner p.118
👦 Students do: they design the survey and share out the roles. Expected: the third (open) question is the hardest of all

Field work · collecting the data
moving

WHAT WE DO: we reshuffle the groups the way the TB does: everyone surveys 4–5 people from other crews and notes the answers

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. New groups! One person from each company in every group. Ask YOUR questions, note the answers.
  2. In the new group we work through Kagan RoundRobin: round the circle each researcher asks their block of questions and the others answer in turn — your turn, then clockwise! That way everyone gets their data collected, not just the fastest one.
  3. Constraint: English only — a Russian answer doesn't go in the data.
  4. Back to the company: Back to your desks. Put your numbers together.
📺 On screen: survey forms · timer
👦 Students do: they move around and survey. Expected: noisy but on task; the data comes out genuine

WHAT WE DO: 4 stations of 4 min each: activities · do/go/play · adverbs and likes · Wh- questions (SB p.33)

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, crews can design a 6-question survey with three question types, collect real data and write a summary using the language of the unit; the whole unit is revised for the P8 test.
  • Subsidiary. Super skill collaboration (planning, sharing out the work, the project Key phrases); the model survey and the model summary (SB p.35); Quick review p.33 as stations; the unit's WDYT question gets its answer.
  • Personal. The project is the weaker students' moment of glory: roles matched to strengths (questions · collecting · counting · voice). I never call Quick review test preparation — it is «the audit before publication».
By the end, students can…
  • write survey questions of three different types
  • use project Key phrases to organise the crew
  • summarise results with 'Most students…', 'Only four students…'
  • pass all Quick-review stations of Unit 2

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
A survey of six identical Yes/No questions — will that do? → no: you need all three types.
Is a summary all the answers one after another? → no, only the main and the most interesting things (TB tip).
Is Quick review a test? → no, it is the audit before publication: find your own holes today.

Assumptions: SB pp.33–35, KTP pair 19. All the unit material has been covered; what is new is collecting real data and generalising from it.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
The project eats the revisionA firm frame: the revision stations take 18 minutes IN THE MIDDLE of the pair, before the summary is written — they are «the company audit».
A survey of six Yes/No questionsI check the three-types rule BEFORE they go into the field: fail the check and the survey does not go out.
Data collection turns into chaosI split them the way the TB does: one person from each crew into a new group — that way everyone has their own respondents and nobody surveys their own team.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
