Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to read & say the /dʒ/ sound (giraffes jumping judo gym — “sometimes g says j”), talk about after-school activities in Ireland, and read & plan a poster — three mini-blocks, one “school & free-time” thread.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics: notice that j and soft g spell the same /dʒ/ sound (recognise → say → sort); culture — after-school activities in Ireland (Springhill School) + Channel 21 video, 21C: trying new things; literacy — text type a poster, capital letters, plan & write a club poster.
  • Personal. Three blocks — keep transitions crisp (chant/drum bridges); the j tongue-twister is play; cap each block hard so the literacy plan actually gets written.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the giraffe /dʒ/ tongue-twister and sort j / soft-g words;
  • name 2–3 Irish after-school activities and compare with ours;
  • find the key facts on a poster and plan one with a partner.

Structure
The (giraffes) are (jumping) and doing (judo) in the (gym).  ·  I play (Gaelic football) every (Friday).  ·  poster: Join the (club). We meet on (Wednesday).
Form
/dʒ/ = j (juice, jump) AND soft g before e/i/y (giraffe, gym, orange). Poster: capital letters at the start of a sentence & for days/names (Wednesday, Mr Smith).
Pron. watch
/dʒ/ clean in giraffe/gym/judo; hurling /ˈhɜːlɪŋ/; Gaelic /ˈɡeɪlɪk/; contrast g /dʒ/ (gym) vs g /g/ (garden).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“gym and juice — same first SOUND? Same first LETTER?” → same sound /dʒ/, different letters!
“A poster — is it for READING quickly or for a long story?” → reading quickly; it tells you about a club.

Assumptions: Subjects, places and “good at” are active (L1–L4). Sound–letter awareness from GMF2 phonics is enough to enjoy the j / soft-g noticing (по учебнику). Pupils can read the short poster texts with support.

Block 1 · Phonics /dʒ/
carpet → desks

Speak and spell (act. 1, CD1 21). PB p.13: Sometimes “g” says “j”. The giraffes are jumping and doing judo in the gym! Listen, then the speed ladder: slow → fast → whisper. Pairs test each other ×3.

Sort j / soft-g. Two hoops/columns: j (juice, jump, jeans, judo) · g-says-j (giraffe, gym, orange, Geography). Hold up word cards — every child points/steps to the right hoop BEFORE you place each card.

Notice the rule. When does g say j? Look — gym, giraffe, orange… g before e, i, y! (notice, don't lecture).

💪 Support: say the word, teacher shows the hoop. ⭐ Extension: add one more /dʒ/ word to a hoop (e.g. jacket, page).

✔ Assess: who still reads soft g as /g/? Note them — extra RallyRead next unit.

Block 3 · Literacy · a poster
carpet → desks, pairs

Before you read (act. 1–3, CD1 23). PB p.14 «Roller Hockey Heroes»: What can you do at this club? Think of a question to ask. Read/listen — does it answer your question?

▶ Poster scavenger hunt. Race in pairs to find on the poster: When do they meet? Where? How much does it cost? First hands up read the answer aloud — everyone scans.

Investigate a text (PB p.15, CD1 24). «Do you love computers?» poster: find a question · exciting words · an instruction. Writing tip: Capital letters at the start of a sentence AND for days and names — Wednesday, Mr Smith!

▶ Plan a poster (Cooperative learning, AB p.12 format). Pairs choose a club (sport / art / music) and make notes: Club name · When? · Where? · What do you need? · How much? · Contact. 2 pairs share one fact aloud.

💪 Support: fill the plan template with word support. ⭐ Extension: write the full poster with a slogan: COME AND HAVE FUN!
