Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name four new wild animals (zebra · giraffe · tiger · snake), describe them — «It's black and white. It's got big teeth. It's got long legs.» — and sing the language song «What's that?» (CD2 T5).
  • Subsidiary. Ask-and-answer «What's that? — It's a (tiger)» through pretend binoculars (Teacher's tip); sing the book song «What's that?» + Genki «Animal Voices» (18) for the sounds; Phonics Hero — Magic E i-e, blend + speed-read p.58–60; listen & find the new animals, answer «What's that?» (PB p.22).
  • Personal. Model «It's got…» many times with the puppet before children say it. Keep ask-and-say short & paired — no long waiting. Praise every attempt at a full description. Start noticing initial sounds (Teacher's tip): t… tiger!
By the end, pupils can…
  • name four new animals (zebra · giraffe · tiger · snake);
  • describe an animal — «It's black and white. It's got long legs.»;
  • sing «What's that?» with the animal actions.

Structure
What's that? — It's a (snake).  ·  It's black and white.  ·  It's got (big teeth / long legs).
Form
It's + colour(s) = describe. It's got + body part = describe. What's that? + It's a/an + animal.
Pron. watch
rising What's that? ↗; linked It's got…; hiss the /s/ in snake; keep colours short.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — Magic E: i-e (again): blend l-i-ne → line, r-i-de → ride, wh-i-te → white — long i for our It's white!. Then speed-read p.58–60. Reading from zero, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“It's got big teeth — do I mean the animal has teeth, or wants teeth?” → it HAS big teeth!
“Tiger starts with… t-t-t…?” → tiger! (initial sound)

Assumptions: The six L1 animals & «What's that?» are fresh (warmed in the review). The four new animals and «It's got…» descriptions are today's stretch. Magic E reading continues. Support with flashcards, mime & the binocular game.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Look through your binoculars! What can you see? A tiger? A snake? Ooh! Make binocular hands, scan the room, gasp.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 21 · Magic E (again) — sounds Magic E: i-e — five · nine · white. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Blend with the magic e: n-i-ne → nine, t-i-me → time, wh-i-te → white — sweep the sounds + a picture. ▶ Sound hop

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.58–60 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read they · their together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.58–60 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–26′
Curiosity input · binoculars + new animals
carpet

Binocular safari. Stick the four new animals on the walls. Make pretend binoculars, scan, and gasp: Ooh… what's that? It's a giraffe! It's got long legs! Children copy — scan & answer with you.

Guess the new one. Turn the four cards face-down, mix, point: Is it a zebra, a giraffe, a tiger or a snake? Children guess; reveal + describe together.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): open the Navio App (coursebook app) on screen — the vocabulary game, the songs, and the «The surprise» story video. Children point & answer It's an elephant!. The app plays the audio/video; our Genki songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: scan & copy It's a tiger. ⭐ Extension: add a description: It's got big teeth!

26–32′
Game · Boat Race (46)
carpet, standing

▶ Boat Race Genki Boat Race: teams in lines, animal mini-cards spread in front. 🦝 Speaky calls a description It's black and white! — the front child touches the right animal, names it It's a zebra!, runs to the back; next child goes.

Calm-ish relay on describing; everyone gets a turn, nobody waits long.

32–38′
Game · Name the animal! (binoculars) ⚡
carpet / floor space

▶ Name the animal! Keep the animals on the walls. Say a description; children mime looking through binoculars at the right animal and shout it: It's orange and black! — It's a tiger! Repeat fast with It's grey. It's got a big nose (elephant) · It's got long legs (giraffe) · It's red, yellow and blue (parrot).

Everyone scans & answers every round — no watching, all binoculars up.

38–45′
Song — «What's that?» + Genki «Animal Voices»
carpet, standing

Sing the song. Lay the animal cards face-up. Play the book song «What's that?» (CD2 T5): children point to the animal in each verse, mime it, and wave their arms for Amazing!

Animal sounds. Play Genki «Animal Voices» (18) — make each animal's sound & action. The perfect sound match before the party song next time.
