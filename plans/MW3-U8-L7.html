Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have reviewed all of Unit 8 — six instruments, the four activities, What are you doing? / I'm playing the…, Do you like playing…? Yes, I do, rhythm and the story «Mimi's band» — and shown it in the flagship Music Team Game with Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41) as the reward.
  • Subsidiary. “I can…” self-assessment with happy/sad faces; every child answers (all show at once, then teams); recycle Genki «What Are You Doing?» (58) & the show song; listen & answer (PB p.87); Phonics Hero — free reading of stories, Part 2 (p.32–51); a joyful, both-teams-win finish.
  • Personal. Team game = EVERY child answers (mime it / show your card / stand up), not just the fastest. Keep the reward short & happy — it's the cherry. Both teams cheer at the end; no sore losers.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all six instruments & four activities;
  • answer “What are you doing? / Do you like playing…? Yes, I do”;
  • show what I can do with a happy/sad face (self-check).

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning, champions! Today — the BIG Music game! Drum-roll, please! Drum-roll on knees; then dance into two teams.

11–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Read together. Phonics Hero 2.0 · free reading (Part 2) — children know all 26 sounds AND the long-vowel spellings now, so today they READ whole little decodable stories, not single rows. 🦝 Speaky opens a page.

② Story reading. a different child reads each line; the group blends the tricky word and helps — ▶ Reading relay celebrate every sentence read from zero (GMF1-style).

③ Pages. open Phonics Hero 2.0 p.32–51 — each child reads a line/box to earn a reading star. Each child reads a line/box to earn a review star.

Tricky words: read every · again together as whole words.

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.32–51 (decodable stories) · Speaky
17–24′
Warm-up circuit · Play and mime! + sound sprint
carpet

▶ Play and mime! In pairs: child A says an instrument/action (I'm playing the drum!), child B mimes & sounds it; swap. Every pair working at once.

Blend it! Speaky reads a phonics word slowly; children blend it and shout it — a playful no-writing check.

 instrument cards · sound cards · Speaky
24–47′
🏆 MUSIC TEAM GAME + Genki «World Cup Soccer!» (41)
carpet, 2 teams

Flagship game. Spin the Big Wheel (app or pointer) — it lands on a pod = a challenge: name the instrument on that colour · mime & sound it · say What are you doing? I'm playing the… · answer Do you like playing…? Yes, I do · clap a rhythm (loud/fast). Challenge shown → Show me! EVERY child mimes/says it → most-correct team gets a point.

Reward: Genki «World Cup Soccer!»: draw a pitch on the board with a 2-D ball; for each team point, one player kicks the ball one space toward the goal — GOAL wins the round. Rotate the kicker every time.

🎮 Offline setup: Big Wheel mat + instrument challenge cards on the carpet; a board pitch + cut-out ball (magnet/blu-tack) for the reward kick — one kick per point, quick. (Или мягкий мяч в обруч — basketball-вариант.)

ICQ: When do you answer? (→ on Show me! only)   Finale. both teams: Well done! Give me five!

✔ Assess: across the game — who names instruments · says What are you doing? · answers Do you like…? · claps the rhythm? A picture of the whole unit.

 Big Wheel mat / app · instrument challenge cards · board pitch + cut-out ball (or soft ball + hoop)
