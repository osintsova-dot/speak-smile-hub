📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. The unit check: vocabulary (animals and describing + taking-action collocations), past simple of be (was/were, there was/were), past simple of regular verbs (affirmative/negative + spelling), reading, listening, a short piece of writing.
  • Subsidiary. The “easy first” strategy; a mini practice run before the test (to take the fear out of the format); the debrief on the same day; the unit celebration.
  • Personal. ⚠️ There is NO official Macmillan pack for Unit 6 in the tests folder (only U1 and U8 are there; Progress Test 1, Mid-Year and End-of-Year belong to a different course, Close-up A2) — the test is built to a specification taken from Quick review SB p.81 and WB pp.50–51. Keep the tone gentle, scores private, comparison only with themselves (the P3/P4/P5 mini-tests).
By the end, students can…
  • start with easy items and mark hard ones
  • apply the unit language in test format
  • check against the key and sort own slips
  • see their own growth across the unit

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Fear of “a real test”A mini practice run of 3 items BEFORE the test — the format is familiar and the fear drops.
Slower students run out of timeThe volume has room to spare; the listening has pauses; the writing follows the familiar P6 plan.
An empty feeling after the testThe unit ceremony: an award for everyone, the wall of achievements, a teaser for Unit 7.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
