🦝 Give Me Five! 1 · Unit 3 “At the circus” · Lesson 4 — Vocab+Grammar: Hair & eyes · She's got long hair! He's got green eyes!
Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., emergent readers — CONTINUING line) · 60 min · New vocab + structure together, taught as chunks (lexical approach) · carpet + desks
Week 09.11–15.11 · Lesson 21✨ VOCAB+GRAM · LEXICAL~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4 + pairs🦸 Phonics Hero ур.19a · Magic E: краткий/долгий
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to describe a person's hair and eyes in whole chunks — long · short · blonde · brown · black · red hair (PB p. 32 ex. 1) + green · brown · blue eyes — using He's / She's got (long hair) and (green eyes) and My friend has got (black hair) (PB p. 32 ex. 3).
  • Subsidiary. Revision of U1–U3 chunks in a team game, woven into today's theme (I've got a red nose · Toby's got purple hair · Be careful, Jake! · It's a big red robot → long red hair); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.19a: Magic E part 1 «Краткий ✊ или долгий 🖐?» (тетрадь стр.53); sing My friend (CD2 2) by ear; react to a friend's sentence (Wow! Me too!); value: we're different — and that's OK.
  • Personal. Drill whole chunks, never single words; give meaning in 5 seconds (photo + gesture, Russian for the whole phrase once), then spend the time on hearing & saying; after the main speaking game run a 2-min 🔁 echo reformulation; every game keeps ALL children responding.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the 6 hair chunks with a gesture (long hair, short hair, blonde hair…);
  • describe a face: She's got long hair and green eyes.;
  • talk about a friend: My friend has got black hair. — and react: Wow! Me too!;
  • show short ✊ vs long 🖐 vowels (kit — kite, ran — rain) and say the five vowel names.
Target language
Lexis
long hairshort hairblonde hairbrown hairblack hairred hairgreen eyesbrown eyesblue eyes
Structure
She's got (long hair). · He's got (green eyes). · She's got (brown eyes) and (long hair). · My friend has got (black hair).
Chunks
I've got a friend called Jake.  ·  He's fantastic! / She's fantastic!  ·  long red hair · short black hair · big blue eyes (size + colour + hair/eyes — like a big red robot)  ·  My friend has got black hair. — Wow! / Me too!  ·  Who is it? — It's Beth!  ·  We're different — and that's OK!
Recycle
I've got a red nose! / I've got big ears! (U3 L2) · Toby's got purple hair! · Be careful, Jake! · Are you OK? — Yes, I am! (U3 L3 story) · How many eyes? — Two eyes! (U3 L1) · It's a big red robot! (U2 L4 → word order for long red hair) · Where's my…? — It's here! (U1) · colours (Starter)
Form
He / She + 's got (= has got) + (size) + (colour) + hair / eyes. Two facts joined with and. My friend + has got (full form, as in the book). For the teacher only — children get it as one block: She's-got.
Pron. watch
blonde /blɒnd/ — one beat, the “e” is silent; hair /heə/ (not “her”); She's got → /ʃiːzɡɒt/ said as one word; eyes /aɪz/ — linking green‿eyes, blue‿eyes; stress the colour: She's got LONG hair.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.19 (a) первое знакомство — Magic E part 1: «Краткий ✊ или долгий 🖐?» — every vowel has two voices: short (cat, kit, hop) and long = its alphabet name (rain, kite, hope); vowel names a · e · i · o · u (тетрадь стр.53 №1–4) · sight words: other · were.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Hold up the long hair and short hair photos (PB p. 32). “Is her hair long or short?” → frame it with your hands.
Point to a girl card. “A girl — He's got or She's got?” → She's got!
Point to your own eyes. “My eyes — are they green or brown?” → class answers with the whole chunk: Brown eyes!

Assumptions: face/body words, I've got and He's/She's got (purple hair) are known from U3 L1–L3; colours and big/small/long/short from the Starter & U2 L4. Emergent readers → photos, gestures & audio, minimal print. КТП check: КТП row 21 = PB p. 32 (6 hair chunks; eyes via grammar & song) — matches the book. КТП grammar column says curly hair — not on p. 32, so it's only an optional extra for fast finishers. The book's barrier game “My clown has got short hair” is PB p. 33 = Lesson 5, so it's left for L5. Groups: max 4 per team (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Word order: “hair long”, “eyes green”The chunk is one block: long hair. Link it to last unit: A big red robot — long red hair! Back-chain: hair → long hair → She's got long hair.
Drop “got”: “She long hair”Echo the whole sentence back (no explaining): She's got long hair! → class repeats it once.
Mix up He's / She'sAlways point to the boy or girl first, then speak. Hide the word, keep the point.
blonde → “blond-ee”; hair → “her”Clap one beat for blonde; exaggerate /heə/ with an open mouth; praise self-corrections.
Relay gets wildRunners walk “like clowns” (big steps), a card only counts after the full sentence; Freeze! resets.

Speed reading — all of Part 2 (стр.53 №1). Strip ai · oa · ie · ee · or · ng · oo · ch · sh · th · ou · oi · ue · er · ar: You know ALL of them! Read there and back — fast! Whole class, choral.

Two voices (стр.53 №2). 🦝 Every vowel has TWO voices! Short = fist ✊, a little knock: a — cat. Long = open palm 🖐, the vowel says its name: a — rain. All show the hand with every example.

21–28′
Presentation · hair & eyes as chunks
carpet

Meaning — fast (PB p. 32 ex. 1, CD2 1). Listen, point, say. One photo + one gesture per chunk: long hair = hands slide down to the waist · short hair = pat your head · blonde / brown / black / red hair = touch your hair + point to the colour card. Give the Russian once, for the whole phrase: Long hair — длинные волосы. Then English only.

Say it in chunks. Never the word alone — always the block: long hair · short hair · blonde hair (one clap for blonde). Then build the sentence by back-chaining: hair → long hair → got long hair → She's got long hair! Hold the girl card: She's got long hair. Boy card: He's got short hair. Point to your eyes: I've got brown eyes! Chorus ×2, then 3 quick “solo stars”.

Stack it — like last unit. Remember? A big red robot! Now — long red hair! Short black hair! Big blue eyes! Chorus with the gestures (size → colour → hair).

▶ Stand up if… (personal chunk, everyone moves ⚡): Stand up if you've got brown eyes! Standing children say together: I've got brown eyes! Sitting ones react: Me too? — No! → Stand up if you've got short hair! · …long hair! · …blue eyes! Teach the reaction: when you stand up with someone — Me too!

CCQ: A girl — He's got or She's got? (→ She's got!)

32–39′
▶ Hair Relay ⚡
carpet → wall, 3 teams

▶ Hair Relay (card-grab race, Genki-style). Teams of 4 in lines on the carpet. At the wall: 3 identical sets of 9 cards (6 hair + green/brown/blue eyes). ICQ: Do you get a point for the card — or for the sentence? (→ The sentence!)

Round 1. You call a chunk with the girl/boy card held up: She's got blonde hair! Runner #1 from each team walks with big clown steps, grabs the card and comes back: It's here! The whole team says the sentence together: She's got blonde hair! → the card is theirs. Children waiting do the gesture while the runners go, so nobody is just watching. Next runner, next chunk.

Round 2 — harder. You only say Red! and hold up the boy card → the team must say the full chunk: He's got red hair! Round 3: two cards — She's got long hair and green eyes! Most cards wins a cheer: Fantastic team!

💪 Support: the runner may bring the card and the team helps with the sentence. ⭐ Extension: the runner adds a stack — She's got LONG blonde hair! 		3 sets × 9 cards (6 hair + 3 eyes) · boy/girl cards · board tally · ▶ Hair Relay

39–47′
▶ Guess Who? · Quiz-Quiz-Trade ⚡
carpet, mingle in pairs

Set up. Board: a poster of 6 numbered faces (girls/boys with different hair + eyes). Each child gets one secret face card (a copy of one of the 6). Model with 🦝: Who is it? She's got short black hair and brown eyes. Class points: Number 4! 🦝 Yes! It's Kate!

▶ Guess Who? — Kagan Quiz-Quiz-Trade. Music on → walk; music off → Stand up, hand up, pair up! A describes: He's got short red hair and green eyes. Who is it? B points at the poster: Number 2! → A shows the card: Yes! Swap roles. Then trade cards, high-five and find a new partner. 4–5 rounds. ICQ: After you talk — do you keep your card or swap it? (→ Swap!)

While they play, walk round and listen for 2–4 phrases to fix later (do not stop the game).

🔁 Reformulation (2 min). Class? — Yes! Everyone sits on the spot. I heard a friend say… “She long hair”. Let's make it shine: She's got long hair! → class echoes. Then 2–3 more from what you heard, e.g. “He's got eyes green” → He's got green eyes! · “Hair red” → She's got red hair! · “Is Number 2!” → It's Number 2! No names, praise the try. Then one more round with a new partner using the shiny sentences.

💪 Support: describe only the hair — She's got long hair. ⭐ Extension: add a third fact or a reaction — …and a big smile! He's fantastic!
