Give Me Five! 2 · Unit 1 “Move your body” · Lesson 4 — Vocabulary & Grammar
A1 young learners (~8–9 y.o., early readers) · 60 min · Vocab + Grammar (PPP + TPR) · carpet + desks · line: продолжающие
Week 21.09–27.09 · КТП урок 7✨ NEW~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4Present Simple 3rd person: He throws / She catches
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 ball actions (hit · jump · kick · bounce · throw · catch) and describe what a person does using the 3rd-person -s: “He throws the ball. / She catches the ball.”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle Unit 1 sports (football, basketball, tennis…) + “Do you play / go…?” from L1–L3 and stitch them onto the new actions (describe & guess a sport); listen for detail (AB “Listen & circle”); read & write the target sentence; fine motor (cut & make the action cards).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; run a stirrer→settler rhythm; make new grammar visible with I Do → We Do → You Do.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name & mime the 6 actions with a ball;
  • say & write “He/She + verb-s the ball”;
  • describe an action and guess the sport (“She bounces the ball.” → “It's basketball!”).
Target language
Lexis
hitjumpkickbouncethrowcatch (actions with a ball · PB p.12)
Structure
He (throws) the ball. / She (catches) the ball.  ·  “She bounces the ball.” — “It's basketball!”
Form
He/She + verb + -s + the ball. Positive statement, Present Simple 3rd person singular. throw → throws · catch → catches.
Pron. watch
the -s ending: voiceless /s/ in hits, kicks, jumps vs voiced /z/ in throws, bounces; extra syllable /ɪz/ in catches.
Recycled
Unit 1 sports (football · basketball · tennis · baseball · volleyball · hockey · swimming · cycling) + “Do you play/go…?” (L1–L2) + plays / goes from the story (L3) — reused to describe & guess a sport.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“He throws — one boy or many?” → one → “throws” (I say -s for he/she).
“Do I say ‘He throw the ball’?” → no → “He throws the ball.”
“She bounces the ball — which sport?” → basketball.

Assumptions: pupils know Unit 1 sports and “Do you play / go…?” from L1–L2, and met plays / goes in the L3 story — so the 3rd-person -s is noticed before, focused today. They are early readers → short printed sentences with picture support are fine. Carpet & team routines are known. Teams: max 4; 12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4.

11–19′
Presentation · Vocabulary (actions)
carpet, standing

Present with TPR. Show each action + a big mime, class copies: Hit! Jump! Kick! Bounce! Throw! Catch! Choral drill ×3, then point at 3–4 children individually. Contrast the tricky pair back-to-back: Throw (push away) — Catch (pull in).

▶ Mime & call: you say an action → every child mimes it; then flip — you mime silently → whole class calls the word. Fast, 8–10 rounds, everyone moving.

Sing it. Play the song again — this time do the actions on the chant line: Jump! Kick! Hit the ball! Throw! Catch! Bounce the ball!

ICQ: Do you say the word or do the mime? (→ both!)

19–26′
▶ Basketball toss
carpet → hoops

Controlled production — the ball games (stirrer). 3 teams in 3 lines, a paper hoop / bin per team. ▶ Basketball: the front child flips an action card, says the whole sentence — He throws the ball! / She kicks the ball! — then shoots the soft ball at the hoop. Correct sentence = allowed to shoot; a full sentence in the hoop = 2 pts, sentence but miss = 1 pt.

Everyone engaged, not “2 play — 10 watch”: while one shoots, their whole team mimes the action + chants the sentence together; then rotate to the back — fast turns so every child shoots at least once.

Rule (from Оксана): a point is only for a full sentence (He/She + verb-s + the ball), never a bare word. Reset if loud: Freeze!

 soft/foam ball · 3 paper hoops or bins · action cards · team score on board
26–35′
Grammar focus · He/She + -s
board → pairs

I Do (explicit instruction · think-aloud). On the board, two columns HE / SHE. Point to the boy: This is a boy — HE. He throw… no! He throw-s the ball. Draw a big -s. For he and she, I add -s. My little rule: he/she = + s. Note catch → catches (say “catch-iz”).

We Do. Flash an action card + point HE or SHE; class builds the sentence chorally: She bounces the ball! Do 5–6 together; hold up a big cut-out “s” each time.

You Do — Talk Partners (PB p.12, ex.3 · describe & guess). ▶ RallyRobin: A describes — She bounces the ball. B guesses the sport — It's basketball! — then swap. Recycles sports vocabulary onto the new grammar.

CCQ: Boy or girl — do I still add -s? (→ yes, for both he & she.)

 board (HE/SHE) · big cut-out “s” · action cards · PB p.12 ex.3
35–43′
▶ Memory (memo)
teams of 4, tables

Controlled practice (settler). ▶ Memo in teams of 4, each team its own set: 6 action-picture cards + 6 sentence cards (“He throws the ball”, “She catches the ball”…), all face-down in a grid. On a turn a child flips two: reads/says both aloud. A pair = picture + its matching sentence; to keep it, the child must read the full sentence correctly — She bounces the ball. Match → keep & go again; no match → turn back, next player.

All engaged: in teams of 4 everyone watches & helps their own small set; roles rotate (dealer · reader · keeper · cheerleader). Most pairs wins.

ICQ: Do you keep the cards for a picture, or for a picture AND a sentence? (→ both, said out loud.)

 3 memo sets (6 picture + 6 sentence cards each) · tables
43–48′
▶ Action relay
carpet, teams

Freer production (stirrer). ▶ Relay race: a pile of action cards at the far wall per team. On “Go!” the runner races, grabs a card, races back, and only scores by saying a correct sentence to the team — He hits the ball! — then the next child runs.

Everyone runs: fast rotation, whole team chants & mimes the sentence of the runner who's back. Most cards + correct sentences wins. Reset: Freeze!

 2–3 card piles · space to run · timer
48–56′
Write it
desks

To your tables. Let's write like superstars. On the board model one sentence; pupils copy & complete 2–3: He __ the ball. / She __ the ball. (choose the action, add the -s). Check with a neighbour.
