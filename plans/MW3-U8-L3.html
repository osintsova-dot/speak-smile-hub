Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to follow & enjoy the story «Mimi's band» (CD4 T7) and understand “Do you like playing the (maracas)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” — Mrs Cat's class gets ready for a music show.
  • Subsidiary. Predict from the pictures; listen (CD4 T7) with the story cards; play Mime the music! & the dynamic Grab the instrument! game; act tiny bits; answer story questions & circle the instruments in the show (PB p.81); Phonics Hero — the /aʊ/ & /ɔɪ/ spellings ou ow · oi oy, blend & speed-read p.79–82.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big character voices, pauses, everyone joining the instrument mimes. Don't test every word; celebrate joining in. Give every child an instrument card so all are involved.
By the end, pupils can…
  • follow the story & point to what happens in each picture;
  • understand “Do you like playing the (drum)?”;
  • name the instruments the children play in the show.

Phonics

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Mrs Cat's class — are they getting ready for a show, or for bed?” → a music show!
“I ask Do you like playing the drum? — happy yes, or an instrument you don't like?” → say what YOU think!

Assumptions: Instruments & What are you doing? / I'm playing the… are active from L1–L2; reading continues in the Phonics Hero workbook. The story-time routine (listen · point · join in) is well established. Do you like playing…? Yes, I do is new but very supported by mime.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Story audio a bit fastPlay once for fun, then again pausing on each picture; point & mime as you go.
Children lose the threadUse the six big story cards frame by frame; ask a yes/no picture question after each.
Everyone wants to run at onceGive one instrument card each; only the child with the called instrument runs.
Just names the instrument, not the phrasePuppet models Do you like playing the drum? — Yes, I do!; child echoes with a nod.
Wriggling during listeningGive a job: play along when you hear your instrument! — active listening.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 29 · /aʊ/ ou ow + /ɔɪ/ oi oy — sounds ou · ow  (/aʊ/) · oi · oy  (/ɔɪ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: ou-t → out, c-ow → cow, c-oi-n → coin, b-oy → boy — arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Blend relay — Speaky shows a word; teams blend it and race to touch its picture.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.79–82 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read there · their together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–26′
Curiosity input · Story «Mimi's band»
carpet

Predict. Show the six story cards — Mrs Cat's class is making a show. What will each child play? Take 2–3 guesses (Russian OK).

Listen 1 — for fun. Play the story (CD4 T7) with the cards. Pause on each picture — enjoy who plays what: What are you doing? — I'm playing the drum! …the recorder! …the maracas! Children point & play along.

Listen 2 — spot your instrument. Play along when you hear DRUM! children play along when they hear it — active listening.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): Navio App — play the music vocabulary game and watch the Mimi's band story video on screen; children tap the instrument they hear.

26–36′
Game · Mime the music! + Grab the instrument! (19) ⚡
carpet / floor space

▶ Mime the music! Name a character & ask Dylan! What are you doing? children respond by miming that instrument & saying I'm playing the drum! Fast, several rounds — everyone answers.

▶ Grab the instrument! Genki Steal the Bacon, re-skinned: two teams line up; instrument flashcards in the middle. 🦝 Speaky calls Do you like playing the guitar? — one runner from each team races to grab it, mimes it and says Yes, I do — the guitar! Every instrument called → everyone runs at least once.

💪 Support: run, grab & mime the instrument. ⭐ Extension: answer the whole line Yes, I do — the guitar!
