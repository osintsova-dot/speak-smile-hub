Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name six musical instruments — guitar · recorder · tambourine · piano · maracas · drum — and match each to its colour on the Big Wheel: “Where's the guitar? — It's on red!”
  • Subsidiary. Mime & make the SOUND of each instrument (TPR); play Say the colour! & Teddy Dancing (35); mime-sing Genki «What Are You Doing?» (58); Phonics Hero — read the /oʊ/ spellings oa ow o-e, blend & speed-read p.75–76; stick the music stickers on the wheel & play Memory (PB p.77–78).
  • Personal. Tiny teacher-talk, big TPR — Speaky mimes & SOUNDS every instrument, children copy. Genki recipe: disco first, phonics early, input through curiosity, energy up in the games then down for the table. Every instrument gets its own big mime, its own sound and its own colour.
By the end, pupils can…
  • mime and name six musical instruments;
  • say an instrument for a colour — “It's on red — guitar!”;
  • join in Speaky's band story & play along.

Structure
Where's the (piano)? — It's on (yellow).  ·  guitar · recorder · tambourine · piano · maracas · drum.
Form
Where's the + instrument → It's on + colour. Instruments as single naming words. No new tense — just naming what I can play.
Pron. watch
clear final sounds: guitar, drum, piano; maracas = 3 beats ma-ra-cas; keep colour words short.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 (our reading workbook) — the long /oʊ/ sound written oa · ow · o-e. Show the Jolly /oa/ action, blend words (b-oa-t → boat, s-n-ow → snow, h-o-me → home) with a meaning picture, then speed-read p.75–76. Reading from zero, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“The drum — do I hit it or blow it?” → hit it! Boom boom!
“The guitar — is it on red or on blue?” → on red!

Assumptions: Late in the year — carpet & story-time routines are automatic and all six colours are secure, so we pin the new instruments straight onto the coloured wheel. Instrument words are new but very mimeable & each has a clear sound. Children know all 26 phonic sounds; today they read the /oʊ/ spellings.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 27 · long /oʊ/ — sounds oa · ow · o-e  (all say /oʊ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds into a word: b-oa-t → boat, s-n-ow → snow, h-o-me → home — big arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Blend & play — Speaky says an /oʊ/ word slowly; children blend it and mime an air-instrument on the whole word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.75–76 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read many · any together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–25′
Curiosity input · six instruments + wheel
carpet → Big Wheel mat

Guess the sound! Slowly slide an instrument flashcard out of an envelope, mime it & make its SOUND before you reveal — What can I play? Listen… STRUM STRUM… GUITAR! Do all six (guitar · recorder · tambourine · piano · maracas · drum).

Instruments on the wheel. Lay the six flashcards on the wheel — one per colour. Point to each pod: What's on red? Guitar! What's on yellow? Piano!

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the interactive Big Wheel on screen with the six instrument pods (red = guitar · blue = recorder · green = maracas · yellow = piano · pink = drum · orange = tambourine) — children point & answer It's on red — guitar!. The wheel comes from the coursebook app; our songs & games add to it, we don't reproduce it.

💪 Support: mime & sound the instrument with the puppet. ⭐ Extension: say the instrument for a colour — It's on green — maracas!

Game · Say the colour! + Teddy Dancing (35) ⚡

▶ Say the colour! Name an instrument on the Big Wheel — children shout its colour & point to the pod (Guitar! — Red! Piano! — Yellow!). Then reverse: you say a colour, they name the instrument. Everyone points every round.

▶ Teddy Dancing Genki Teddy Dancing: instrument mini-cards spread at the back. Music plays, children dance; music stops — everyone grabs the nearest card, mimes & sounds it and shouts the instrument. Repeat fast.

Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Speaky's Band!» aloud — Speaky and his friends make a band — Speaky on the trumpet, the puppy on the drum, the bunny on the guitar. A joyful music read to open our instruments unit, right after the dancing game.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line I'm playing the drum! I'm playing the guitar!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Turn each spread — children mime & sound the instrument (strum, boom, shake) and echo the line I'm playing the guitar!; on the last page everyone plays in Speaky's band together.
