“Before/after” in the mingle
Before/after · Inside-Outside Circle
two circles

WHAT WE DO: Kagan · Inside-Outside Circle: the “In September I couldn't… Now I can…” frame — everyone tells 3 new listeners about their growth

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. The frame in chorus ×2: In September I couldn't ___ . Now I can ___ . Everyone prepares 2 of their own lines (from the I-can sheet).
  2. ▶ Kagan · Inside-Outside Circle: Two circles, face a partner! Say your lines. Partner: ‘That's growth!’ Outside — rotate! 3 rotations → SWITCH.
  3. I listen round the mingle; the best lines go up on the “growth wall”.
  4. 💪 For weaker learners: a minimum line I couldn't speak. Now I can! — plain truth and the strongest line of the day.
📺 On screen: the frame on the board · the growth wall
👦 Students do: they tell 3 new people about their growth — the couldn't/can formula sinks in along with the pride. Expected: the minimum line moves everyone. Typical error: can + to — the anchor gesture fixes it.

WHAT WE DO: an invitation for parents from a model with 2 gaps; a neighbour's check + my sign-off; sealing it in an envelope

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:

  3. A neighbour's check (Dear? date? Love?) → my sign-off → the envelope.

📺 On screen: invitation models · envelopes
👦 Students do: they write to a real addressee from a model — a manageable and important written product. Expected: they try harder than usual. Typical error: they forget the time — a neighbour's check catches it.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners have a portfolio page (the “4 squares” template) and a 5–6 sentence “My year” talk from notes, rehearsed in pairs; a parent invitation is written.
  • Subsidiary. An I-can sheet with smileys (KTP); a “before/after” using the couldn't → can frame in a mingle; review of hard spots is woven in (talk lines = the year's structures); the Showcase Day plan is accepted without fear.
  • Personal. The most heartfelt lesson of the line: we compare everyone only with their September self. For this line the contrast is huge — IN SEPTEMBER THEY DIDN'T SPEAK AT ALL. Say this to each of them personally.
By the end, students can…
  • fill the 4-square portfolio
  • say before/after lines with the frame
  • deliver a 5–6 line growth talk with notes
  • write a parent invitation from the model

Form

Pron. watch
couldn't /ˈkʊdnt/ — the l is silent · proud /praʊd/ · say it with pride, not with a question mark in your voice.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I have nothing to be proud of”? → In September you didn't speak English AT ALL. Today you made a film. That's what pride is for.
“Does best work mean no mistakes?” → It means where GROWTH is visible. A page full of corrections is the best exhibit.
“Is an invitation with a mistake embarrassing?” → That's why there's a check: a neighbour + me. It'll go home looking sharp.

Assumptions: Work has been brought in (P7 homework; those who forgot use their in-class exercise books). Showcase Day dates are agreed. Portfolio templates and the invitation model with gaps are ready.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“I have nothing to put in the portfolio”I sit down next to them, we flip through together: the September page vs the April one — growth is ALWAYS there; a fair/premiere photo is an exhibit too.
Tears from comparing themselves to strong learnersThe rule “only with yourself” + a personal line for everyone: “in September you…, today you…”. In the mingle — everyone says the same frame.
The talk won't come togetherNotes with 6 ready-made lines and gaps — we fill them in together; a minimum of 4 lines still counts as a full talk.
Mistakes in the invitationA model with 2 gaps (date/time) — there's almost nowhere to go wrong; a double check: a neighbour's checklist → my sign-off.
Parents won't comeA line in the invitation: “or watch our video” — we'll film the whole Showcase Day on a phone; nobody will be left without an audience.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
