Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow, read and act out the story «The rainy day» and talk about what to do on a rainy day: “If it's wet, we can't ride our bikes. — Let's play the musical instruments!”
  • Subsidiary. Value — being creative with your time (the children invent a water xylophone and make a music video); break Luke's code (→ DIDGERIDOO — a bridge to the New Zealand Culture block in L5); recycle conditional-with-can from L2; meet the story language Do you want to…? / I'd rather… (drilled properly in L4); story skills — predict → listen → read → Read & answer → act out.
  • Personal. Let the story breathe — big voices, pauses, prediction; the “I'd rather” idea is NOTICED today, drilled in L4 — don't over-correct. Give every child a story role.
By the end, pupils can…
  • retell 2–3 things that happen in the story;
  • read their role's lines from the book;
  • answer comprehension questions: “They make a water xylophone.”

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Can they ride their mountain bikes? Why not?” → no — it's raining, it's wet.
“Are the children bored, or creative with their time?” → creative — they make instruments and a video!

Assumptions: Conditional-with-can is active from L2; the number-code was learnt in the Starter (recycled in act 5). Readers follow the text with a finger. The act-out routine was modelled earlier in the year.

Read & answer · comprehension
carpet → desks

Read and answer (act. 3, Talk Partners). Questions: Can they ride their mountain bikes? Why? What do the children decide to do? What does Lily play at school? How do they make the xylophone? Who do they send a video to? Pairs answer in full: They make a water xylophone. Every pair speaks.

Who said it? (Showdown). Mini-whiteboards: you read a line — “I'd rather play the drums.” Who says it? — all write the name, Show me! 4 rounds.

✔ Assess: the boards are your check — who follows the story? The rest sit with you in the board game.

💪 Support: point to the right frame in the book. ⭐ Extension: add a second true fact about that moment.

28–40′
Act out
carpet, groups of 4

Model a frame. You + 3 pupils play the water-xylophone frame with big drama: Pour a different amount of water into each glass. — Wow! A water xylophone. Amazing!

Groups of 4 (Josh · Lily · Ravi · narrator). Rehearse 2 frames with books as scripts. Rotate roles once.

Showtime. Each group performs ONE frame (30–40 sec — fast carousel, no long waits). Audience task: shout the creative idea (“Water xylophone!” / “Music video!”) at the end.

💪 Support: shortest lines / the “Amazing!” reaction for the shyest child. ⭐ Extension: add an extra line from the frame.
