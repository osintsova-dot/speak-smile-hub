Give Me Five! 4 · Unit 1 “Let's eat!” · Lesson 3 — Story “The Burfi cake disaster”
A1+ pre-teens (~10–11 y.o., confident readers) · 60 min · Story lesson (listen → sequence → read → act out) · carpet + desks
Week 21.09–27.09 · Lesson 6📖 STORY~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4Grammar in context: quantifiers
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have understood and re-told a short story (“The Burfi cake disaster”) and used its language — quantifiers & cooking steps — to describe what a recipe needs and what goes wrong: How much sugar do we need? — We need a lot of. / Add the sugar and stir.
  • Subsidiary. Listen for gist & sequence 8 story frames by ear; read the text with echo/chunked reading; recycle ingredients (L1) + How many/How much (L2) inside the new story; discuss the value paying attention; act out the story in groups.
  • Personal. Keep books CLOSED until pupils have sequenced by ear (reading is a LATE step); give an ICQ before every task; press learners to justify predictions (“What makes you think that?”).
By the end, pupils can…
  • order the 8 story events and re-tell them with FIRST → THEN → FINALLY;
  • ask & answer with quantifiers (a lot of / some / a few / a little);
  • say the cooking steps (chop · mix · stir · add · pour · put in the oven) and act out the story.
Target language
Lexis
floursugarsaltnutsbutterraisinscoconutmilk · chopmixstiraddpourput in the oven
Structure
What do we need to do now? — Add the sugar and stir. · How many nuts do we need? — A few. · How much sugar? — A lot of. · Put the butter in the pan and mix it with… Then add a little milk.
Story phrases
…by mistake · Here you are · Never mind · It's salty · Mmm! Let's try a piece · Are you ready for our next trip?
Form
much + uncountable (sugar, milk, butter) · many + countable (nuts, raisins). Quantifiers: a lot of (both) · a few (count) · a little (uncount).
Pron. watch
weak do we /dəwi/ in the question; /ʌ/ in butter/nuts vs /ɔː/ in salt; stress on many / much.
Value
Paying attention — Ravi isn't paying attention → he passes the salt instead of the sugar → the cake is salty.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Can we count nuts?” → yes → How many? / a few.   “Can we count sugar?” → no → How much? / a lot of / a little.
“Did Ravi give the sugar or the salt?” → salt. “On purpose or by mistake?” → by mistake.
“Is the cake sweet or salty in the end?” → salty! “Whose fault? Why?” → Ravi — not paying attention.

Assumptions: ingredients (L1) & How many/How much + quantifiers (L2) are known — this story RECYCLES them. Pupils are confident readers, but per S&S method they meet the story by ear first, text last. Carpet/desk routines & team roles known. Teams: max 4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
much / many mixed upCCQ “count it?” → colour-code: many+count / much+uncount on the board.
Pupils open books & read too earlyBooks stay CLOSED on the text until stage ⑥ — they must sequence by ear first.
a few vs a little confusedPair gesture: a few = count on fingers; a little = pinch a pinch of sugar.
Act-out turns to chaosAssign 5 roles + a director; rehearse a freeze-frame first, one line each.
Teams grab / argue in Ask & GrabOne rotating runner per team; the rest must AGREE the question before it's asked.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → silent, tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pens down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset (also cues freeze-frames).

TimeStage / whereProcedureMaterials
0–4′
Warm-up · review
carpet, standing

Retrieval, woven into today's story. ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade (Kagan) — each pupil gets an ingredient card. Stand up, hand up, pair up! Partner A holds up the card: How much / How many [flour / nuts] do we need? Partner B answers with a quantifier: A lot of / a few / a little. Quiz each other, praise, trade cards, find a new partner. 3–4 rounds, music on/off.

Settle: Class? — Yes! Bring your card and sit in your team. (This pre-loads the exact ingredients & quantifiers the story is built on.)

 ingredient cards (1/pupil) · music · ▶ Quiz-Quiz-Trade
4–10′
① Look & predict
carpet · books CLOSED on text

Project the story pictures ONLY (or hold PB p.10–11 open at the pictures, text covered). Read the title: The Burfi cake disaster. What's a “disaster”? What do you think goes wrong?

Dialogic prediction (Talk Partners → whole class). Look at frame 1. Who's cooking? What are they making? Who ISN'T helping? Take answers, then press & build: What makes you think that? · Do you agree with [Dima]? Why? Collect 2–3 predictions on the board — we check them after listening.

Quick pre-teach (mime). by mistake · Never mind · salty. — mime tasting → Ugh! It's salty!

 story pictures (projector / PB p.10–11, text covered) · board
10–14′
③ First listen · gist
carpet · books CLOSED

Books stay closed — just listen. Was your prediction right? Play CD1 15 straight through.

Gist check — physical. Stand up if the cake was DELICIOUS… sit down if it was a DISASTER! Then quick whole-class: What went wrong? Whose fault was it — Luke, Lily, Josh or Ravi? Tick/cross the predictions on the board.

 Audio CD1 15 · board (predictions)
14–21′
④ Order by ear
carpet · teams

Sequence the SCRAMBLED frames on the second listen. Give each team a shuffled set of the 8 picture cards. Listen again. Put the pictures IN ORDER. Books closed — use your ears! Play CD1 15 again, pausing after each frame.

ICQ: Are you reading or listening? (→ listening) Do we open our books? (→ no!)

Check together — a spokesperson from each team places one card on the board line in order. Spotlight the turning point: Which picture shows the mistake? Salt or sugar?

 8 shuffled story-frame cards ×3 sets · Audio CD1 15 · board line
21–28′
⑤ Notebook · sequence
desks

📓 Interactive notebook — “The Burfi cake disaster” Story Sequence (A4 cut-and-glue). Header UNIT 1 • LESSON 3, chain LISTEN • PUT IN ORDER • GLUE • RETELL.

① Cut off the bottom strip, then cut out the 8 picture cards. ② Lay them in order (from stage ④) — don't glue yet. ③ Check in pairs — Who spots the salt-instead-of-sugar moment? ④ Glue the row in. (Comprehension questions beside it are answered in stage ⑥.)

28–29′
🎬 Bridge · TPR
at desks, standing

Stand up — let's cook! Fast cooking-verb TPR: chop · mix · stir · pour · heat · add · put it in the oven! — mime each. This wakes them up before we open the books to read.

 cooking-verb gestures
29–37′
⑥ NOW read the text
desks · books OPEN

Now open your books — page 10. Follow with your finger. Chunked / echo reading. Read a frame aloud → class echoes with expression → do the mime on the cooking lines (Add the sugar and stir!). Rotate readers frame by frame.

Comprehension — “After you read” (PB p.11 ex.3), Numbered Heads. 1 Who chops the nuts? 2 Do they add a lot of milk? 3 How much sugar do they need? 4 What does Ravi give to Josh? 5 What does Luke's friend bring? Teams confer → you call a number → that pupil answers in a full sentence (Josh chops the nuts.).

Value discussion (dialogic). Who isn't paying attention? What happens because of it? Do YOU pay attention when people talk to you? Press for reasons.

 PB p.10–11 · glued notebook row
37–43′
⑦ Phrase focus + game
carpet · teams + runner

Drill the target phrases inside a game. ▶ Ask & Grab — big ingredient cards on the floor; you are the chef. Each team AGREES a quantifier question and asks YOU: Do we need a lot of sugar? You say No… (they ask again) or, when you choose, Yes! → the team's runner grabs that ingredient card; first correct card + full sentence (We need a lot of sugar!) scores. Rotate the runner every turn — every child runs.

ICQ: Do you grab first or ask first? (→ ask first, wait for “Yes”!)

 A4 ingredient cards on floor · ▶ Ask & Grab
43–48′
📓 Notebook · retell
desks

📓 Interactive notebook — Retell strip (2-up A4). Glue the strip under the picture row: FIRST → THEN → NEXT → BUT → LATER → FINALLY with clue words (make a Burfi cake · Harbour Food Festival · chop a few nuts · butter, raisins, coconut + a little milk · add the sugar · salt by mistake · stir · the oven · it is salty · a sweet Burfi cake) + pronoun helper (Luke & the children → they · Ravi → he · Luke's friend → she).

Pupils write their OWN sentence for each connector (connectors printed, sentences theirs). Pairs re-tell with the pictures covered, using only the clue words. This becomes the script bank for the act-out.

▶ Act it out

▶ Act out the story (PB p.10 “Act out”). Groups of 5: Luke · Lily · Josh · Ravi · Luke's friend + a director. Rehearse ONE freeze-frame of the disaster first — Freeze! Then run it with lines: the recipe steps (Add the sugar and stir.), the mistake (Here you are. → salt!), the ending (Ugh! It's salty!). 1–2 groups perform to the class; audience says one thing that was great + the value in one word.

▶ Break the code
desks · teams (relay)

▶ Break-the-code relay (PB p.11 ex.5). Luke's secret message says where he's travelling next — written in a number code. Post the code key on the wall. One runner per team at a time races to the key, decodes ONE number → letter, runs back, hands over; next runner goes. First team to read Luke's destination shouts Luke is going to …! — recycles Numbers 100–1000 (Starter) inside the story finale.
