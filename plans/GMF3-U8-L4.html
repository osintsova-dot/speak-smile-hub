Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 more free-time activities and talk about the past with regular -ed verbs: “I (painted a picture) yesterday. / We didn't (watch TV) last night.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Did you watch TV?» (CD4 03); consolidate the -ed verbs noticed in the story (L3) — now drilled; discover the rule — add -ed for the past, but after didn't the verb stays plain (Grammar clue); a real “what I did” report (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Insist on the -ed ending (painted, played) AND the plain verb after didn't (didn't play, not didn't played); model with a “yesterday” timeline; keep the info-gap moving — max 30 sec per partner.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name 6 activities (play cards, climb a tree, skip, phone a friend, paint a picture, plant seeds);
  • say past sentences with -ed: “I planted seeds last week.”;
  • say negatives: “I didn't watch TV yesterday.”

Structure
I (painted a picture) yesterday.  ·  He (climbed a tree) last night.  ·  We didn't (watch TV) / We didn't (play cards) last weekend.
Form
Past positive: verb + -ed (play → played, plant → planted, climb → climbed). Past negative: didn't + plain verb (didn't skip, didn't climb — NO -ed after didn't).
Pron. watch
painted /ˈpeɪntɪd/ & planted /ˈplɑːntɪd/ (extra /ɪd/ after t/d); played /pleɪd/ & climbed /klaɪmd/ (just /d/); skipped /skɪpt/ (/t/).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“played — now or before?” → before, past (the -ed shows it!).
“I didn't play or I didn't played?” → I didn't play — plain verb after didn't!

Assumptions: -ed verbs were met in the story (L3: carried, jumped, climbed). Did you…? questions are active from L2. play cards / climb a tree / skip / phone a friend / paint a picture / plant seeds are largely new as a set. The positive-vs-negative (-ed vs plain) contrast is the key challenge. Readers read the short verb phrases.

Present (act. 1, CD4 02). Photo cards: What's she doing? Playing cards! Climbing a tree! Planting seeds! Choral ×3 + one action each.

Into the past. Board timeline yesterday → today. Turn each phrase to the past: play cards → I played cards. paint a picture → I painted a picture. Class adds the -ed with a gesture. Show the negative: I didn't skip. (plain verb!).

What did you do? (info-gap)
mingle → desks

Model first. Ask 2 pupils publicly: What did you do yesterday? — I played cards. I didn't watch TV.

▶ Talk Partners info-gap (act. 4-style). Each child has a “my week” grid (✔/✘ for 5 activities). Music → mingle; off → pair: A Did you paint a picture? B Yes, I did / No, I didn't — I planted seeds. A ticks & TRADES. 5–6 exchanges.

✔ Assess: circulate — -ed in the positive? plain verb after didn't? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: grid + sentence starters printed. ⭐ Extension: add a time word: …in the afternoon / last weekend.
