WHAT WE DO: quiet music; “one word about how you feel”; a silent 5-item mini-revision with self-check

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Quiet music before entry. At the door: One word — how do you feel? Any word is fine.
  2. I ask: Test-readiness 1–5 on fingers. Whatever you showed — you're MORE ready than you think.
  3. Silent revision: 5 lightning items (niece spelling · owner/verb apostrophe · have got gap · question scramble · short answer) on a draft, solo; key, self-check.
  4. Bridge: That was a mini-test. You just passed it. Same formats, bigger paper — strategy first, then calmly.
📺 On screen: quiet music · slide of 5 items → key · drafts (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they settle into a calm mode, solve silently, check themselves — the fear drops. Expected: 4–5 out of 5 for most.

WHAT WE DO: 4 parts, one example each; a strategy card: example → skip & return → an apostrophe sweep

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I hand out the test papers cover up: Don't open yet.
  2. I go through one example from each part, the class answers in chorus.
  3. Strategy card: 1) example first · 2) skip & return · 3) final sweep: every 's — owner or verb? + he/she = HAS.
  4. Send-off: Remember: he-she-it — HAS got it!
📺 On screen: test papers · slide of examples · strategy card (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they say the strategy out loud, open on command. Expected: “can we use pencil?” — yes.

WHAT WE DO: RallyRobin: swap papers, check with a coloured pen against the key; I keep the email

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Swap with a neighbour, coloured pens.
  2. I run the pair as Kagan RallyRobin: we alternate one answer at a time — Ping-pong it: A — one, B — one, back to A. No monologues, no lists!
  3. Key by parts: Say the correct answer quietly as you tick — marking is learning round two.
  4. I keep Part 4: Back next lesson — two stars and one wish each.
📺 On screen: slides of the key · coloured pens (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they check carefully, count the score. Expected: honest (the coloured pen + swap keep it fair).

Gentle error clinic
desks · teams

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: the top-5 error types of the unit, anonymously; “doctor teams” heal the patient sentence

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. I put up error type №1 (“he have got”) + the patient sentence.
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: everybody writes at the same time, boards face down, on the call No peeking… Boards up! they all hold them up together — I see every answer, nobody can hide.
  3. I command: Doctor teams — the cure? Fix the patient on your mini-board!
  4. I go through 5 types: he have got · Yes, I've · apostrophe in the wrong place · hair order · a question with no inversion.
  5. A calm pace, NO points — a conversation between masters, not a race.
📺 On screen: slides: type + patient (anonymous, from my notes over the unit) · mini-boards (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they diagnose and heal. Expected: “he-she-it — HAS got it!” in chorus. Typical error: they look for “whose” it is — “errors on the board belong to the whole class”.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. Assess Unit 2: family words, body/face/hair, have got (all forms), possessive 's, listening notes, a short email — in a calm, model-first format.
  • Subsidiary. Test-taking basics (example first, manage time, final apostrophe sweep); self-marking of objective parts; error log continued; «I can» line.
  • Personal. Low-stress protocol: quiet music before start, clear timings per part, no competitive games during the test. Feedback = kind, specific, forward-looking. Close with the U3 teaser.

Unit 2 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ TRC pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 8 items: family definitions → word, spelling counts (My father's sister is my ___ → aunt; My sister's daughter is my ___ → niece). B) 6 items: body/face/hair — label the picture + opposites (long/short hair, curly/straight, fair/dark).
Part 2 · Grammar have got + 's (18 pts)A) 8 gaps aff/neg with contractions (My cousin ___ (+) blue eyes. We ___ (−) a car.). B) 6 items: order the words to make Have/Has…got? questions + write a true short answer (got / any / you / Have / cousins ?). C) 4 items: apostrophe surgery — put the apostrophe in the right place and say its job (my brothers room → brother's/brothers' · Lucys cat · Hes got glasses).
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)A short description of a family/two siblings (script in the test pack; teacher-read twice if no track): 4 true/false + 4 notes to complete (hair / eyes / family members / age) — mirrors SB p.29 ex.6–8.
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A short email (6 sentences): ask someone to meet a friend + describe the friend. Marked for: have got forms correct (4) · apostrophes both jobs correct (3) · task complete: problem + description (3) — mirrors the CHECK list SB p.33 ex.7–8.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.33 — every format was met in class during the unit.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
