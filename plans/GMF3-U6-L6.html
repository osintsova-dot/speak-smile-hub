Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated Unit 6 — 12 water-sports items, be going to, adverbs of manner — and shown it in the flagship Team Quiz with reward game, then built a Treasure island together (Project 2).
  • Subsidiary. AB Review «Ready, Steady, Go!» (p.66): equipment for water-skiing/snorkelling, going-to plans, adverb sentences, break Luke's code (→ AUSTRALIA, a bridge to Unit 7); Project 2 — Let's have (a cave). We can have (a waterfall). Is the treasure behind (the waterfall)?; self-assessment for the L7 test.
  • Personal. Quiz = every child answers (Showdown boards), not captains only; keep the reward game short and joyful — it's the cherry, not the cake. Both teams celebrate at the end; the project is cooperative, not a race.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name unit words from pictures AND spell key ones;
  • use going to and adverbs of manner accurately;
  • co-design a treasure island and self-assess honestly.

24–42′
🏆 TEAM QUIZ + reward
desks, 2 teams

Flagship quiz (board or projector). Grid of tiles: picture→equipment, equipment→spelling, going-to plan, adverb sentence (how do you…?), true/false plan, break-the-code. Mechanics: tile opens → Showdown: EVERY child writes/says on their board → Show me! → majority correct = team point → reward: one player takes a bowling / penalty shot (paper ball into a box). Rotate the shooter strictly.

🎮 Offline setup: card grid on the whiteboard + a real paper-ball penalty into a box/basket — one shot per win, 20 sec.

ICQ: When do you show your board? (→ only on “Show me!”)

Finale. Count scores together in English; both teams: Give me five!

🧭 Project 2 · Treasure islands

Build an island together (Cooperative learning). Groups draw a big island. Each child adds ONE feature aloud: Let's have a cave! We can have a waterfall! Let's have a volcano! Then hide the treasure and quiz another group: Is the treasure behind the waterfall? — No, it isn't! / Yes, it is!

💪 Support: point + one-word feature (a cave). ⭐ Extension: full: Is the treasure in front of the cave or behind the volcano?
