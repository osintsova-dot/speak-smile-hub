🦝 Give Me Five! 1 (с нуля) · Unit 1 “Ready for school!” · Занятие 19 / L4 — Vocabulary & Grammar
Pre-A1 young learners (~6–7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия «с нуля») · 60 min · Vocab + Grammar (PPP + TPR + games) · carpet + desks
Week 02.11–08.11 · Занятие 19 · Ноябрь✨ VOCAB+GRAM~12 pupils · 3 teams of 4🦸 Phonics Hero ур.16 · диграфы ch·sh·th
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 6 classroom-furniture words — shelf, cupboard, bin, desk, board, chair (Pupil's Book p. 12) — and give & follow tidy-up instructions with prepositions of place: “Put the (book) on / in / under the (shelf / bin / desk).”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — the classroom objects (L1), “Where's my…? — It's here!” (L2), the story Toby goes to school where things ended up in the bin / on the shelf (L3) — woven into today's target by putting those old objects onto the new furniture; phonics — Phonics Hero ур.16: digraphs ch·sh·th — meet, blend & trace (тетрадь стр.43–45, (a) первое знакомство; “CHair” launches today's topic!); sing the coursebook song Let's tidy up (CD1 17); value: keep the classroom tidy.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; drill the 3 preposition gestures once, then hide all practice inside games; run a stirrer→settler rhythm and keep every child responding (own memo-set · own shot · own runner).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name shelf · cupboard · bin · desk · board · chair (with a gesture);
  • give & follow “Put the … on / in / under the …”;
  • ask & answer “Where's the …? — It's on the …”;
  • hear, blend & trace the digraphs ch·sh·th (chip, ship, fish…).
Target language
Lexis (new)
shelfcupboardbindeskboardchair + recycled objects: book · pencil · crayon · school bag · paper
Structure
Put the (book) on the (shelf). / in the (bin). / under the (desk).  ·  Where's the (chair)? — It's on the (…).
Form
Imperative: Put + the + noun + on/in/under + the + noun. No “I / you”. Prepositions of place — 3 fixed gestures.
Pron. watch
final /f/ in shelf; cupboard = /ˈkʌbəd/ (silent p, two beats — NOT “cup-board”); final /d/ in board; weak “the” /ðə/.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.16 (a) первое знакомство: digraphs ch /tʃ/ (поезд «ch-ch-ch!») · sh /ʃ/ (тихий пальчик «shhh!») · th («язычок между зубами») — chip, chin, shop, ship, fish, moth (тетрадь стр.43–45) · sight words: like · have.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Hold a book high on a shelf vs drop it in the bin. “Is the book on the shelf or in the bin?” (point + gesture).
“The chair is under the desk — is it on top or below?” → below (hand low).
“I say Put the book… — do I DO it, or do YOU do it?” → YOU do it! (it's an instruction).

Assumptions: the classroom objects + “Where's my…? — It's here!” are solid from L1–L3; in / on / under were already FELT in the L3 story (the book in the bin, the pencil case on the shelf) — today they get named, drilled and used to give instructions. Emergent readers → real objects, pictures & audio, minimal print. КТП also listed window/door/computer, but PB p. 12 core is these 6 → plan follows the book. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Mix up on / in / underFix ONE gesture per word: on = flat hand on top ✋ · in = fist into cupped hand 👇 · under = hand dives below ⬇️. Chant with the gesture, then hide the word — only the gesture.
cupboard said “cup-board” (2 clear words)Model /ˈkʌbəd/, back-chain: …board → cup-board → cupboard; exaggerate the silent “p”.
Children DO the action but don't SPEAK on their turnSplit clearly: the commander SAYS, the rest DO. In every game each child gets a turn as the “boss”.
Basketball / relay over-excitementSoft ball only; one runner at a time; Freeze! resets; a settler after every stirrer.
Blend reading: children name letters, not sounds; th = /d/Push sounds fast: /ch/ /i/ /p/ → chip!; picture for meaning; “tongue between teeth” for th; praise self-corrections.
Teams argue over memo cardsAssign the 4 roles inside each team; rotate every turn.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Phonics Hero — ch·sh·th (a)
board → desks

Speed reading — move (тетрадь стр.43 №1). ▶ Take Off–Touch Down: flash the review strip ai · oa · ie · ee · or · ng · oo — a long-vowel digraph → stand up tall; a short sound → crouch small. 8–10 fast — every child moves every card. Then read the strip there and back — fast!

Meet ch · sh · th (стр.43 №2) — I do → we do. The train ch — ch-ch-ch! · the quiet finger sh — shhh! · the clown th — «язычок между зубами». Tie-in to today's furniture: CHair — ch! All copy each sound + gesture; quick show-me: say chair / shop / three / chip → all do the matching gesture (every child responds).

Blend — Hub / стр.44 №1 (all together). Sound windows: /ch/ /i/ /p/… chip! /sh/ /i/ /p/… ship! Then chin, shop, fish, moth — picture appears for meaning; contrast: chip… ship — same? No!

Trace (стр.43 №3, desks — quick). By the arrows: ch · sh · th · chip · ship — whisper as you write (finish lines at home; today's MAIN aim is furniture + prepositions). Countdown: Finishing in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… pencils down.

Sight words. Flash like · have (whole-word, not sounded): chorus, air-write. Tie-in: I have a book. I like my desk!

💪 Support: gesture first, then sound; blend only chip · ship. ⭐ Extension: write fish on the spare line. 🎮 Phonics Hub (speakandsmile.ru/gmf1/phonics/) — набор ch·sh·th, ✅ готов; offline: тетрадь стр.44 №1. 22–30′
Presentation · Vocabulary
carpet → teams

Presentation — furniture (PB p. 12 ex. 1, CD1 16). Hold up each A4 card: What's this? If unknown, model: It's a shelf. Shelf. Choral drill ×3, then 2–3 children. Words: shelf · cupboard · bin · desk · board · chair.

Contrast the tricky word (hold both): This is a shelf. This is a cupboard. One gesture each. Fix cup-board? No! /ˈkʌbəd/. (back-chain).

▶ Memo (мемори) — teams of 4 on the carpet, each team its own set of 12 mini-cards (6 furniture ×2), all face-down. In turn a child flips 2 and names them in a sentence — It's a desk… it's a chair. No match! A pair → keep it & say It's a desk! again. Everyone plays every round (own set = no waiting). Retrieval practice: every flip = one word recalled aloud.

ICQ: Do you turn ONE card or TWO? (→ two).

30–37′
Song “Let's tidy up” + TPR
carpet, standing

Teach the 3 gestures first (explicit — I do). On! (flat hand on top ✋) · In! (fist into cupped hand 👇) · Under! (hand dives below ⬇️). Drill the 3, fast, mixed — all show the gesture.

Listen & move (PB p. 12 ex. 2 · CD1 17). Play the coursebook song “Let's tidy up”: Put the book ON the shelf… Put the paper IN the bin… Put the chair UNDER the desk… Let's tidy up! Children do the gesture on each preposition. Play again: Now let's SING and TIDY! — mime placing real objects.

Value moment: 🦝 Speaky — A tidy classroom is a happy classroom! Well done, superstars!

37–43′
▶ Basketball · shoot & say
carpet, 2–3 teams

Controlled → freer, active. Put the real class bin (basket) at the front — nice tie-in: This is the bin! Each child holds a scene-card (object + place: book+shelf · paper+bin · chair+desk · crayon+cupboard…). Teams line up.

▶ Basketball: to take a shot the child shows the card & says the whole sentence first — Put the book on the shelf! — THEN throws the soft ball at the bin. Right sentence = the shot counts; a basket = bonus point. Retrieval practice: the card cues the structure, so every shot = one full sentence recalled (not just a word).

Everyone plays every turn. While the shooter speaks, the whole class does the matching gesture (on/in/under) and the waiting team are judges — thumbs up 👍 / down 👎. Fast rotation so every child shoots. Model first (I do): Watch me — Put the paper… in the bin! …score!

ICQ: Do you throw first or SAY first? (→ say first).

50–56′
▶ Tidy-up Relay
open floor

Freer, fast, active. Set 3 furniture “stations” on the floor (a shelf, a bin, a desk — A4 cards or real). Each team lines up with a stack of object cards.

▶ Relay: 🦝 calls Put the book ON the shelf! The first runner grabs the book card, runs, places it on the shelf, shouts The book is on the shelf! runs back, tags the next child. New instruction each turn (mix on/in/under). First team to finish the stack & sit down wins.

ICQ: Do you run when I say GO or now? (→ on GO). Reset if loud: Freeze!
