“What if I don't review everything?” → 2 targets, not 22. A small plan works better than a big fear.

PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (12 pts)A) 6 word↔topic matches from the year’s sets (U1–U8). B) 6 collocation gaps: have got / go home / play the guitar / catch the bus / wear a uniform — traps from the units.
Part 2 · Grammar (16 pts)A) 8 gaps covering the year’s system: be · have got · some/any · present simple (3rd person!) · can · comparatives · present continuous · past simple. B) 4 order-the-words questions (Do/Does · Did · Is/Are). C) 4 “spot the mistake” items from the year’s Common errors.
Part 3 · Reading (10 pts)A short guide text in the style of U9 “A world of festival fun” (A2 Key format): 5 true/false + 5 multiple choice.
Part 4 · Listening (6 pts)Mini dialogues: picture choice a/b/c (U9/Exam trainer format) — 6 items; no track → I read the script twice.
Part 5 · Writing (6 pts)Genre of CHOICE from the year: message / email / blog post. 🚀 higher: 40+ words, 2 tenses, a connector; 🐣 standard: 25+ words using the prompt frame. Marked for: task complete (2) · grammar (2) · genre features (2).

Total 50 pts · the focus is not on the number but on the “I can” sheet next to the score. Lines: 🚀 higher (Wh-questions in writing, more traps, a story option 50+ words) · 🐣 standard (word banks + prompt frames in writing). The format of every task is familiar from the year’s lessons.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Test anxiety in weaker studentsThe test on screen IN FULL + a trial of every format with a hint; the mantra of the day: “Thursday — the same thing.”
Games without support fall flatEvery game of the day has a safety net: the answer on the back of the card, a word bank on the slide, a helper partner.
The checklist gets filled in with “I know it all”The checklist uses concrete examples (not “I know numbers” but “I can say 3:45 in English”) — checked right there in the game.
Strong students get bored with the supportThe ⭐ track: the same rounds without hints + helping a neighbour (helping = a smiley).
Overheating towards the end of the pairThe final 20 minutes are calm, seated formats; no competitions after minute 70.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
