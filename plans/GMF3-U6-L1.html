Week 01.02–07.02 · Lesson 41✨ NEW · 12 water-sports items~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.60 · AB p.58
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 pieces of water-sports equipment and say what they're wearing / ready to do: “I'm wearing a (wetsuit) and (flippers). I'm ready to (snorkel).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Fun in the sea» (CD3 07) & the vocabulary chant (CD3 06); brainstorm water sports & equipment (act. 1, Learning to learn); preview L2's future — “Are you going to go (kayaking)?”; early literacy — read the equipment words and unscramble them (AB word-order).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; place-mime/wear-mime is high-energy — plan the freezes. Insist on the article: a wetsuit, a snorkel (but flippers / gloves / water-skis = plural, no a).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 water-sports items;
  • say “I'm wearing a … / I'm ready to …”;
  • read the equipment words and match word ↔ picture.

Structure

Form
I'm wearing + a + item (singular) / no a for plurals: flippers, gloves, water-skis. I'm ready to + verb (snorkel, kayak, surf, water-ski).
Pron. watch
wetsuit /ˈwetsuːt/; snorkel /ˈsnɔːkl/; kayak /ˈkaɪæk/ (KAY-ak); life jacket — stress on LIFE; silent none, but water-skis = two words, one thing.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“flippers — do I say a flippers or just flippers?” → just flippers (it's plural, a pair!).
“I'm ready to snorkel — am I doing it NOW or ABOUT to?” → about to — I've got my snorkel!

Assumptions: Pupils know swim, sea, beach and some sports from GMF2. The 12 water-sports items are largely NEW; I'm wearing is known from clothes units — reused here. Readers — word cards work alongside the pictures.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Article/plural muddle (“a flippers”)Colour-code the board: singular items get a yellow a; plural items (flippers·gloves·water-skis) get a red “NO a” tag.
kayak / snorkel mispronouncedClap syllables: KAY-ak, SNOR-kel; one gesture each (kayak = paddle, snorkel = breathe through tube).
Long items (life jacket / water-skis)Chunk & clap: LIFE-jacket · WA-ter-skis; icon on every card.
Wear-mime gets wildSlow-motion + Freeze!; ‘put on’ the kit on the spot, no travelling.
Some read word cards slowlyIcon on every card; pair a strong reader with a developing one in card games.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

10–20′
Presentation · 12 items
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD3 06). Each card: What's this? It's… a kayak! Choral ×3 + one gesture (paddle a kayak, breathe through a snorkel, put on flippers). Sort a mini-pile: singular (a wetsuit) vs plural (flippers, gloves, water-skis).

I'm wearing / I'm ready to. Board frame: I'm wearing a wetsuit and flippers. I'm ready to snorkel! Hold each card — class chants the full line with the gesture.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if you're wearing this in the sea… a helmet! — flash cards fast, everyone moves every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + word. ⭐ Extension: full sentence — I'm wearing a life jacket and I'm ready to kayak.

A5 glue-in note — water-sports kit as picture words (mask · snorkel · flippers · helmet · life jacket · paddle · kayak · gloves · wetsuit · surfboard · water-skis · swimsuit) + I'm wearing … I'm ready to … frames and one blank personal line
  • Trim and glue the note in under today's date.
  • Play the song: pupils point along the kit as they hear each item.
  • Second play — they draw a line from each kit group to the sport it goes with (mask + flippers → snorkel).
  • Everyone fills the blank personal line: I'm wearing ___ and ___. I'm ready to ___.
  • Pairs ask each other What about you? straight off the note.

28–36′
Word ↔ picture · Memory
carpet, pairs

▶ Memory · I'm wearing… — in pairs. Each pair: 6 picture cards + 6 WORD cards (readers!) face-down in two rows. Turn one of each; a match is claimed only with a sentence: I'm wearing a mask! Wrong / no article (or wrong plural) → cards go back. Most pairs wins.

ICQ: Do you say “a flippers”? (→ no — just flippers!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair at a time — is the a / plural choice automatic yet?

 6+6 cards per pair
36–44′
Get-ready mime & guess
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Slow-motion kit mime. One player mimes putting on gear + the action (mask+flippers = snorkelling); their team guesses together: You're wearing flippers! You're ready to snorkel! Mimer confirms. Point per correct guess; rotate the mimer every round — everyone mimes once.

Whole team must guess together — no solo shouting: One voice, one team!
