Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated all Unit 5 language — the 10 farm animals, “What's this? — It's a…”, “Can you see a…? — Yes, I can / No, I can't”, the six action verbs (fly · swim · run · walk · climb · jump) and ability “(Birds) can fly. (Cows) can't climb.” — and can use it confidently in the review tasks and the quiz, including a riddle “This animal can jump. It's green. — A frog!”.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics Hero ур.28 (b) закрепление /ю:/ ue·ew·u-e (word grid → sort → dictation, тетрадь стр.78); sight words every, mother; self-assessment (My progress traffic light, AB p. 55); listening for detail (PB p. 58 ex. 1).
  • Personal. Run the flagship quiz by PIES: every child writes/shows every answer (Showdown), the responder is picked by Numbered Heads — no “captains answer everything”; keep the reward moment short so language time wins.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all 10 farm animals and answer “What's this?” without support;
  • ask & answer “Can you see a…?” with full short answers;
  • say what animals can & can't do: “Ducks can swim. Pigs can't fly.”;
  • read & sort /ю:/ words (blue · new · cube) and write them from dictation;
  • self-assess with the traffic light — ready for the Unit 5 test.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Hidden card: Can you see a horse? → No → “No, I can't.” — full answer, not just “no”.
Quiz rule ICQ: Who writes the answer — one child or EVERYBODY? → everybody, on their own board!

Assumptions: all Unit 5 language met in L1–L4; the /ю:/ houses started in L4 (ур.28a, стр.77) — today we sort & dictate them. Teams of 4 with numbered members (1–4) are routine. The flagship quiz with reward runs only on Review day — children know it's special. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

33–51′
🏆 FLAGSHIP QUIZ with reward
📺 teams · projector

Set-up (2 min). 3 teams of 4, numbered heads 1–4, one mini-whiteboard EACH. Quiz grid on screen — categories: Animals 🐄 · Can you see? 👀 · Can/Can't ✨ · Sounds 🔤. Rules + ICQ: Who writes? — Everybody! When do you show? — On “Showdown!”

Round flow (Kagan Showdown + Numbered Heads — all answer every question). Question up (e.g. picture half-hidden: Can you see a goat? · riddle: It can fly. It's small! · sound: Write the house — blue! · read a /ю:/ word). Every child writes/draws → Showdown! → all boards up → call a number: Number 3! — that child of the playing team answers aloud in a full sentence.

Predict (thinking). On a half-hidden animal, before revealing: Look — just a tail! Is it a cow or a horse? Write your guess! Then reveal — Were you right?

Reward shot. Correct = the answering child takes the shot: animated bowling / football on screen — knocked pins = team points, class counts in English: One, two, three… five! (No projector → soft-toy pins + ball, ≈ Mido №91 Bowling for Animal.) Rotate teams; over ~10–12 questions every child throws at least once.

Support ▸ an animal / word bank stays visible so weaker writers copy, not freeze; accept one correct word.   Extension ▸ when a strong pupil is “Number”, require the full sentence — No, I can't. But I can see a cow!
🔨 Flagship interactive · projector · BUILD · “Unit 5 Team Quiz + Reward”  🔨 BUILD
Screen 1 — Grid: 4×3 board of numbered tiles under the 4 category headers. Tap a tile → the question (half-hidden animal / picture / riddle / audio word / /ю:/ word). Used tiles grey out.
Screen 2 — Answer: big “Show your boards!” + a 5-sec timer; tap ✔ / ✗ to score; a full-sentence model appears for choral echo.
Screen 3 — Reward: bowling — child “rolls”, pins fall (or football penalty). One shot = +points.
Screen 4 — Scoreboard: 3 team bars, huge numbers, + a “Teamwork ⭐” toggle.
Teacher: host only — YOU tap tiles & call the number so no captain dominates. Self-contained HTML slide (S&S purple/honey). Offline reserve: printed grid + soft-toy pins + ball. Flagship quiz runs only on Review day.

Finish with totals + teamwork star: Give me five, champions!
