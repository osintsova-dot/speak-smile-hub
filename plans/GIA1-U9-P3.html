🔥 Challenge · the festivals table (exam-sim №1)
desks · crews

WHAT WE DO: ex.4 simulation: the 3-festivals table (10 gaps) with audio 59 on exam timing; check with evidence

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Simulation: Exam conditions: six minutes, the table, audio plays once, then you finish from the text. Silence = focus. GO. (audio 59; no track — the table is closed by reading, 5 min).
  2. Check with the evidence rule: an answer + a line of text. Key: tomatoes · Buñol, Spain · last Wednesday of August · water fight · Thailand · New Year in April · clean everything · dance/sing + water and paint · India · end of winter/start of spring.
  3. +1 per gap, +1 for evidence; Word work ex.5 as a finisher: hungry · diet · fun · together · unusual — definitions from memory (they're all from the year's Word work!).
  4. TB extra T/F oral blitz: Is Tomatina for locals? (F) · Thai New Year in January? (F — April).
📺 On screen: SB p.109 · audio 59 (script TB p.135) · timer · key TB p.123 (Backup: no track — I read the script aloud at listening speed.)
👦 Students do: work in exam mode — quiet, on time; answer with evidence. Expected: 8–10 out of 10. Typical error: they answer When? with a place — hack: check the gap TYPE (time? place?).

WHAT WE DO: Kagan Timed Pair Share: ex.6 choosing a festival with because, following the model + mini-pitches to the class; a vote

  2. Launching Timed Pair Share: Partner A — sixty seconds NON-STOP about your festival, two reasons minimum. Partner B listens, then repeats ONE idea back: ‘You said…’. Swap!

WHAT WE DO: a simulation of the «pictures a/b/c» format: marking the options BEFORE → two listens by tactic → a battle check

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. The marking ritual, 90 sec: Describe each picture line silently: hair types, clock times, café positions, bag/T-shirt/book, taxi/underground/bus. No play until marked.
  2. Pass 1 (audio 60): Tick or question mark. No writing words! Pass 2: finish off the «?».

  4. Follow-up with evidence (TB): Why underground? (cheaper). Layla's hair in the past? (curly).
  5. No track — I read the script TB pp.135–136 in roles twice.

👦 Students do: mark the pictures before the audio, work the two-pass tactic, answer at the same time. Expected: 4–5 out of 5 after marking. Typical error: answering on the first trap word they hear — hack: listen to the WHOLE line.

WHAT WE DO: the finale: trios put together an «exam hack map» (5 of today's hacks in their own words + when to use them) and hand it in by relay

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can apply the exam reading strategy (quick read for general idea → hunt details) to the festivals guide and solve both A2 Key listening formats (pictures a/b/c + multiple choice) using the two-listens tactic.
  • Subsidiary. The year's subskills gathered into a system (ex.1); the three-festivals table closed against the clock; a Word work review; listening hacks: pictures BEFORE, key words in the options, «?» and move on; timed exam-simulation stretches like in A2 Key.
  • Personal. We serve the exam as a sport with tactics: not «a scary test», but «we have hacks, the format has rules — we play and we win». Simulations are short and for points.
By the end, students can…
  • order the 3 reading subskill tips and use them
  • complete the 3-festivals table under exam timing
  • solve picture-based listening items (a/b/c)
  • solve the podcast multiple choice with the two-listens tactic

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
«First reading — understand every word?» → No: 1–2 minutes, general idea.
«Didn't hear the answer to No.2 — do we sit and wait?» → No: «?» and move on; the audio will play a second time.
«In the picture items a/b/c, listen when they start talking about No.1?» → Before the audio, describe the PICTURES — the words will come from them.

Assumptions: They read texts at level confidently; exam BEHAVIOUR (timing, the two-listens strategy, working with the options) is new, and it's the pair's main content. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (mixed-year items), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our revision zone before the exam formats”.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Panic at the word «exam»The word «exam» is always linked to «hack/game/points»: a 6-minute simulation — then straight into a debrief of what worked.
They read slowly and read everythingA hard, visible timer on the quick read (90 sec) — the task is NOT to finish reading, but to state the general idea.
In listening they write while it's talking and lose the threadA tactic drill: the first pass — only ticks/«?», the hand doesn't write words.
They mix up the options because they haven't read them before the audioThe ritual: 60 sec to mark the options BEFORE play; without the marking, I don't play the track.
The strong ones dominate the battle for answersAnswers only on mini-boards at the same time (boards up) — shouted-out answers don't count.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
