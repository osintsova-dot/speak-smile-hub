Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer about sports in Present Simple (I / you) using “Do you play (tennis)? / Do you go (swimming)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.” and choose play vs go correctly.
  • Subsidiary. Reactivate Lesson 1 sports vocabulary (10 words) in context; act out a model dialogue; listen for detail (Listen & tick, PB p.9); freer speaking through a mingle; writing at word/sentence level (notebook constructor).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; run I Do → We Do → You Do; give an ICQ + a colour-coded model before every task; keep a stirrer→settler rhythm with signals.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Do you play/go…?” and give the short answer “Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”;
  • say “I play (ball sport)” vs “I go (-ing activity)” with the right verb;
  • read & act out the model dialogue and build a sentence in writing.

Structure
Do you play (tennis)? · Do you go (swimming)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  ·  I play… and I go…
Form
play + ball sports (football, tennis…); go + activity ending in -ing (swimming, cycling…). Short answer echoes the auxiliary: Yes, I do — not “Yes, I play”.
Pron. watch
connected Do you /dʒə/; falling tone on the short answer Yes, I do.; clear /-ɪŋ/ ending in swimming, cycling, rollerblading.
Phonics
No new phonics block today (grammar lesson). ai / ay comes in Lesson 5. Optional 30-sec spelling flash of a sport word as a settler.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Football — is it play or go?” → play (a ball sport).
“Swimming — play or go?” → go (it ends in -ing).
“Do you play tennis? — Yes, I ___?” → do (not “Yes, I play”).

Assumptions: the 10 sports were met in Lesson 1 (Vocabulary, PB p.8) — this is grammar in context, so vocabulary is recycled, not re-taught. Pupils are early readers → short printed prompts are supported with pictures, colour-coding & a spoken model. Carpet & team routines are known. Группы: команда — максимум 4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Model dialogue (PB p.9, ex.1) 🧘. Open your books at page 9. Listen and read. Play CD1 15 — Beth & Molly: Do you play tennis and basketball? — Yes, I do. And I go rollerblading. — Do you go swimming, too? — Yes, I do.

Think-aloud (make the choice visible). Point to the Grammar box: I go rollerblading. Do you play tennis? Yes, I do. No, I don't. Say: Tennis is a ball sport — so I say PLAY. Rollerblading ends in -ing — so I say GO. And the answer copies “do”, not the verb: Yes, I DO.

Back-chain drill. tennis? … play tennis? … Do you play tennis? Choral ×3. Short answers alone: Yes, I do! / No, I don't! (thumbs up / thumbs down).

Act out ⚡. In pairs on the carpet, read the dialogue by roles (A = Beth, B = Molly), then swap. ICQ: Do you answer “Yes, I play” or “Yes, I do”? (→ Yes, I do.)

📓 Interactive notebook · «Lesson 2» — sentence constructor (colour code) · ~5 min
· Pupils glue the strip in. Colour the three sentence parts: green = you / I · blue = play / go · purple = do / don't.

27–36′
Controlled drill · game
carpet / desks

The one lesson with tic-tac-toe ⚡ (Оксана's format). ▶ Sports Tic-Tac-Toe — lay 9 A4 sport cards in a 3×3 grid on the carpet. Two teams: ✕ and ⭕ (paper chips). Teams take turns.

To claim a cell the team must say the full target sentence for that sport — I play basketball. / I go cycling. — or a full exchange Do you play hockey? — Yes, I do. Correct → place your chip on the card. A single word or the wrong verb (“I go football”) = no cell, the turn passes. First team with 3 in a row wins the round; re-shuffle the grid and play again.

Everyone in ⚡: the team decides together (whisper huddle), a rotating “speaker” says the sentence — a new speaker each turn, so all 4 get a go.

▶ Boat Race relay ⚡ (Genki). Two teams line up. On Go! the first runner runs to the card box, grabs a sport card, and produces a full sentence at speed — Do you go rollerblading? Yes, I do! or I play volleyball! — drops the card in the team's “boat” (hoop), tags the next runner. Wrong verb → run the card back, try again.

Fast rotation = every child runs & speaks. First boat full & correct wins. Settler: Freeze! → back to the carpet.
