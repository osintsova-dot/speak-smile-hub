Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“He didn't wanted — правильно?” → No: didn't ест прошедшее, остаётся WANT.
“Did you go? — Yes, I went?” → No: Yes, I DID. Короткий ответ повторяет did.
“How did it started?” → How did it START — после did всегда инфинитив.

Assumptions: Past simple affirmative (regular + irregular) прошли в U7; отрицания и вопросы знакомы по GMF4, но письменная точность хромает. Транспортный сет 1 в активе после P1. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (didn't + infinitive / question word order / superlative spelling), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
