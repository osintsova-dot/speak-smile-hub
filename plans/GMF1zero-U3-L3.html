Pre-A1 young learners (~7 y.o., first-year beginners · линия с нуля) · 60 min · Story: listen → follow → act out (drama) · carpet + desks
Week 14.12–20.12 · Занятие 30✨ STORY~12 pupils · trios for dramaPB p.30–31 · AB p.28🦸 Phonics Hero ур.21b · Magic E i-e
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow and understand the story “Fun at the circus” (Pupil's Book pp. 30–31, CD1 52) and act it out using key story language: “Let's make a circus!”, “I've got a red nose!”, “Look — a clown / an acrobat!”, “He's got purple hair!”, “Wow! Fantastic!”.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — body parts + “I've got…” (L1–L2), Genki colours — woven into the story (make a circus monster, describe the clown); phonics — Phonics Hero ур.21 (b) закрепление: Magic E i-e (match → dictation → tricky right, goes → story «Майк и воздушный змей»; the circus bike = bike!); values: joining in / “Let's make a circus!”; preview L4 He's / She's got….
  • Personal. Keep story listening active (gesture responses, prediction); manage drama noise with Freeze; every child acts in a trio at the same time — no “2 on stage, 10 watching” until the final 30-sec showcase.
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they understand the story (order the frames, answer with gestures);
  • say the key lines in role: “Let's make a circus!”, “I've got a red nose!”;
  • read, match & write i-e words (kite, bike, five, time) and chorus the story «Майк и воздушный змей»;
  • join in kindly with friends (values: “Let's make a circus together!”).

Structure

Form
I've / He's got + noun (receptive — meaning through gesture; He's / She's got gets full focus in L4). Let's + verb (invitation).
Pron. watch
keep the ’ve / ’s: I've got /aɪv gɒt/, he's got /hiːz gɒt/; acrobat = 3 beats /ˈækrəbæt/; sentence stress: a RED nose.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.21 (b) закрепление: Magic E i-e /aɪ/ — read & match kite, bike, five, time · dictation bike, five, time, ride · tricky strip right, goes · story «Майк и воздушный змей» (тетрадь стр.59–60) · sight words: right, goes.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Frame 1: “What does Jake say?” → “Let's make a circus!” (arms wide, inviting gesture).
Clown frame: “What has the clown got?” → “A red nose! Big feet!” (point on your own body).
Last frame: “Is it a good circus?” → “Wow! Fantastic!” (big cheer, clap).

Assumptions: body parts + “I've got…” known from L1–L2; children know the Magic Bike characters (Jake, Beth, Molly, Toby) from earlier units. Emergent readers → they FOLLOW the pictures while listening; the teacher points, children don't read the bubbles alone. He's / She's got is met here in context and studied properly next lesson. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
New words block the story (acrobat, juggle, circus)Pre-teach the 3 with picture + gesture (balance-walk, juggle-hands, big-top tent) before listening.
Children call answers over the audio“Listening hands” rule — hands cupped behind ears while the CD plays; talk only when it stops.
Magic E i-e read as two sounds: bike = /би-ке/Cross out the ghost e; “the e is silent, makes i say /aɪ/” — bike, kite!
Drama turns to chaos (running around)Acts are ON THE SPOT; roles fixed by colour badges; “Freeze!” resets.
Frame cards ordered by one child in the pairRule “one card each turn, take turns” (RallyRobin with cards); praise pairs where both hands work.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset (essential in drama).

Speed reading. Vowel names + Magic E strip (a-e · i-e): There and back — beat your time! Story tie-in: A circus BIKE — bike, with the magic e! (today's circus story!).

Read & match — тетрадь стр.59 №3 (all point first). Read each word together (kite · bike · five · time), everyone points to its picture in the air → draw the line. Neighbour-check: Bike? — Yes!

Dictation — стр.60 №1 (everyone writes). 4 words, once each: bike · five · time · ride → say the sounds + magic e on fingers (/b/ /i/ /k/ + e — bike!) → write → Show me! → reveal & self-check.

Story — стр.60 «Майк и воздушный змей» (settler). Chorus the 5 frames, finger under the words: Mike has a kite. The kite flies high. Five, four, three… Time to ride the bike home! ⭐ Star task: find & circle every kite. Colour the star «Я молодец!» (стр.59).

24–34′
📺 Story — listen & follow
carpet, books/screen

Before you read (PB p. 30). Big story picture on screen / books open. Pre-teach with gesture: circus (big-top tent) · clown (honk nose) · acrobat (balance-walk) · juggle (juggle-hands).

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Find it! — “Who's at the circus?” ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 find-it-u3
Screen: the U3 “Find it!” engine on the big story picture. · How: Speaky asks, a child taps a character; it lights up. · Pupils: whole class points in their book / calls it — The clown! The acrobat! Toby! The magic bike! · Teacher: 🦝 How many clowns? Point! — 4–5 things, quick. · Offline reserve: no projector → point in the open Pupil's Book (p. 30), pairs count 15 sec & chorus.

Predict (thinking) — before we listen. Jake wants to make a circus. Who will be the clown? Who will have a red nose? Guess! Thumbs vote. Let's listen and check!

Listen 1 — global (CD1 52). “Listening hands” behind ears. Teacher points frame by frame; children follow the pictures. Gist check: Is everyone happy at the circus? Show me! (thumbs) Was your guess right — who's the clown?

Detail check — True or False? (thumbs up / down, all react). Say a sentence, class shows a thumb: The clown has got a red nose. (👍) · Toby the dog is at the circus. (👍) · The acrobat has got small feet. (👎 — big feet!) · The circus is boring. (👎 — fantastic!). Re-point the frame to fix any wrong thumb.

Listen 2 — active (all respond). Play again; children do the story gestures at the right moments: honk the nose 👃, balance-walk, juggle, big cheer Wow! Fantastic! CCQs between frames (see box above).

35–43′
▶ Build the story
desks, pairs

▶ Story Builders (picture re-skin for non-readers — all pairs work at once). Each pair gets the story frame-cards, shuffled.

Order it. Put the story in order — one card each, take turns! (RallyRobin with cards.) Countdown 2 min.

Check with the audio. Replay CD1 52: pairs touch each card as they hear it — self-check, fix the order. Then choral retell, teacher holds up big frames one by one, class says the line: Let's make a circus! · I've got a red nose! · Look — an acrobat! · He's got big feet! · Wow! Fantastic!

🎮 Интерактив (проектор): Circus Story-Sequence 1–5 ✅ ГОТОВ · 🎮 u3-story-circus
Screen: the 5 key frames appear shuffled, each with an empty number badge 1–5. · How: tap two frames to swap (or drag); a “Check” button lights correct frames green with a cheer. · Pupils: the class calls the order — Number one — let's make a circus! — and gesture each frame. · Teacher: take votes (Numbered Heads), tap as they decide, then replay CD1 52 to confirm. · Offline reserve: big frame cards on the board (blu-tack), children come up in a fast rotation.

Support: hand that pair only the 3 clearest frames & number the first. Extension: after ordering, say one whole line per frame from memory before the audio check.

43–51′
Act out — drama
carpet, trios

Roles. Trios: Jake (green badge, invites) · Clown (red badge, red nose) · Acrobat (blue badge, balance-walk). Teacher narrates and models once with a strong trio.

All trios act at the same time. Narrate slowly, frame by frame; every trio plays it in their own spot (on the spot!): Let's make a circus! — I'm the clown! I've got a red nose! — I'm the acrobat! Look — big feet! — He's got purple hair! — Wow! Fantastic circus!

Create (thinking) — make it yours. Before round 2: What ELSE is at YOUR circus? A juggler? A lion? You choose! Each trio adds a line — I've got a big hat! I'm a juggler! (support: point to a card; extension: invent the whole exchange).

Swap roles, run it again (2 rounds). Volunteers: ONE trio shows 30 sec for the class — big applause. Values (PB p. 31): Jake makes the circus WITH his friends. It's fun to play TOGETHER!

Support: quieter children get the acrobat (short line + gesture) with a line card in hand. Extension: confident children play Jake / narrate and lead.
