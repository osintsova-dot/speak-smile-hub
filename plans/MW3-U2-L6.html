Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to show being kind at school (welcome, share, invite), know that activity gives us exercise, and sing & dance the traditional song «Go in and out the window» — a first taste of culture.
  • Subsidiary. Values — being kind (Harry & Zoe welcome new boy Jake, CD1 T31); Content — exercise at school + Genki «Head And Shoulders» (23); Culture — «Go in and out the window» (CD1 T33/34) with the Mimi says circle game; Phonics Hero — ng · oo (consolidation), speed-read p.39–42.
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values through DOING (welcome a friend, share a crayon) not talking-at. Every child does a kind act and joins the dance.
By the end, pupils can…
  • do a kind act for a friend at school;
  • say activity gives us exercise (heart, legs!);
  • dance «Go in and out the window» with a partner.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A new child is alone — do we welcome them or leave them?” → welcome them!
“When we run & jump, does our body get lazy or strong?” → strong! (exercise)

Assumptions: School activities & Do you want to…? are secure from L1–L5; the ‘being kind’ seed was planted in the L3 story (Mimi & Katy). Reading is at digraphs ng·oo. Content & culture stay concrete, physical & visual.

Circle dance chaosHold hands at shoulder height first, name the ‘windows’; one weaver at a time (TB).
Reading too hard for someBlend/read together; success = joining in, not solo reading.
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge (Head And Shoulders / Go in and out) moves everyone on.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 15 (consolidation) — sounds ng · oo (letter-teams). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Re-read faster: k-i-ng → king, z-oo → zoo, f-oo-d → food — sweep the team + a picture. ▶ Word-swat — Speaky says a word; children point / swat it on the page fast.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.39–42 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read review what · when · why · where together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Story about kindness. Speaky shares the Little Book of Values: a new boy, Jake, starts school. Are Harry & Zoe kind to Jake? How? (CD1 T31). Harry shows Jake where to put his coat, Zoe shares her crayons, they invite him to play.

Reading corner 📖

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Speaky's Band!» aloud — Speaky makes music WITH his friends — sharing & playing together, the heart of today's ‘being kind’ value.

Culture · «Go in and out the window» + Mimi says

Make the windows. Children make a circle, hold hands at shoulder height — These are the windows! Choose a weaver to go in and out the windows; then Go in and out the window! stand & face your partner · shake hands · dance around. Demonstrate each (TB).

▶ Mimi says Genki-style Mimi says: 🦝 Speaky calls the actions in random order — Mimi says… shake hands with your partner! speed up as they get confident. Then play the song (CD1 T33) & dance it together.
