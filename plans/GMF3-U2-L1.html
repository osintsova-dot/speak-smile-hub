Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 household chores and say what they do & don't do at home: “I (make my bed). / I don't (cook dinner).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «What a busy day!» (CD1 30) & the vocabulary chant (CD1 29); brainstorm how we help at home (Learning to learn); preview the question “When do you (dust)? — I dust at the weekend”; early literacy — read the chore phrases and match them to pictures (AB).
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — chore-mime is high-energy, plan the freezes. Insist on the whole phrase: make my BED, wash the CAR (verb + object).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 household chores;
  • say “I (make my bed). / I don't (cook dinner).” about myself;
  • read the chore phrases and match phrase ↔ picture.

Structure
I (make my bed). / I don't (cook dinner).  ·  preview: Do you (dust)? — Yes, I do. When do you (dust)? — I dust at the weekend.
Form
Verb + object phrase: make my bed · lay the table · hoover the floor. Negative: I don't + verb phrase. Watch the verb — you don't “do” a bed, you MAKE it.
Pron. watch
hoover /ˈhuːvə/ (long oo); vacuum-verb here = hoover; vegetables not needed; stress the verb (MAKE my bed, WASH the car); dust /dʌst/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I make my bed — do I do this chore, or is it my mum?” → I do it myself.
“wash the car — one action or a whole day's job?” → one chore, verb + object.

Assumptions: Unit 1 routines are back; pupils know classroom verbs and some home words from GMF2. The 12 chore phrases are largely NEW; I / I don't is known — production with new verbs is today's stretch. Readers — phrase cards work alongside pictures.

Whole phrase reduced to one word (“bed”)Insist verb + object; echo-correct with a beat; frame stays on the board.
Long phrases (water the plants)Chunk & clap: WA-ter · the · PLANTS; icon + verb-colour on every card.

Presentation · 12 chores
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD1 29). Each card: What's this? Making the bed! Choral ×3 + one gesture (wash up = scrub, walk the dog = hold a lead, dust = flick a duster). Colour-highlight the VERB: MAKE my bed — WASH the car!

I do / I don't. Board frame: I make my bed. I don't cook dinner. Hold each card — class shows thumbs up/down for THEMSELVES, then the full sentence.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if you do this chore at home… walk the dog! — flash cards fast, everyone moves every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + verb. ⭐ Extension: full sentence — I always make my bed in the morning.

28–36′
Phrase ↔ picture · Memory
carpet, pairs

▶ Memory · I … — in pairs. Each pair: 6 picture cards + 6 PHRASE cards (readers!) face-down in two rows. Turn one of each; a match is claimed only with a full sentence: I walk the dog! Wrong verb / one word only → cards go back. Most pairs wins.

ICQ: Can you keep the cards if you just say “dog”? (→ no — I WALK the dog!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair at a time — is the verb + object automatic yet?

 6+6 cards per pair
36–44′
Mime & guess
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Slow-motion chore mime. One player mimes a chore (scrubbing = wash up); their team guesses together: You wash up! Mimer confirms. Point per correct guess; rotate the mimer every round — everyone mimes once.

Whole team must guess together — no solo shouting: One voice, one team!

I help at home · speak & write
desks

Speak. ▶ RallyRobin: Take turns: I … / I don't … — chores you do and don't do at home.

Write (notebooks / AB p.16 format). Two frames from the board: I ___. I don't ___. Each child writes 3 true sentences about the chores they do, copying the chore phrases. Fast finishers add and / an adverb.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from 3 phrase cards. ⭐ Extension: add when: I make my bed every morning.
