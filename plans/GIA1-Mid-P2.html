WHAT WE DO: the Vocabulary (16) and Grammar (20) parts per the spec; silence; I walk around quietly, watching for the strategies

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Start: Thirty-five minutes. Parts one and two. Go.
  2. I walk around SILENTLY; if I see a freeze — quietly: Mark it, move on. I don't give anyone content hints.
  3. At the 10-min and 3-min marks — a quiet time announcement (no drama).
  4. Finishers: I remind them of the final check with a gesture (I write “-s?” on a sticky note), then the quiet envelope.

WHAT WE DO: a calm finish via Kagan Timed RoundRobin: a how-do-you-feel word round the circle; NO going over the answers; light homework; a teaser for the speaking checkpoint

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. A Timed RoundRobin circle: One word each, clockwise — how do you FEEL? (not “how did you do”); plus fists to five.
  2. Firmly: No answer-talk till Thursday — I check, THEN we talk. Results are a map, not a verdict.
  3. Light homework (below): preparing a 60-sec “about me” for the speaking checkpoint.
  4. Teaser: Thursday: you TALK, I listen — the nicest exam of all. Then holidays!

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. Learners complete the Mid-year test (Starter–U4, higher variant) across vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening and writing, applying the trained strategies calmly.
  • Subsidiary. Lesson A: the Vocabulary+Grammar parts. Lesson B: Reading+Listening+Writing (“a message about me”) + a calm finishing activity with no assessment. Exam behaviour (silence, time management, checking) is itself a learning outcome.
  • Personal. Test day before the holidays: my job is an even, calm atmosphere. No fuss, no “hurry up”. Whoever finishes early gets quiet tasks, not new points. Going over it is NOT today (in P3, with a fresh head).
By the end, students can…
  • work through a long test with time management
  • apply strategies: read twice, easy first, hunt the -s
  • write a 45+ word message about themselves
  • self-check before submitting

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Stuck — do we sit on the task?” → No: mark it, move on, come back.
“Finished early — do I hand it in?” → First a final check: -s, apostrophes, spelling. Then a quiet task.
“Is this the final mark of the year?” → No — a snapshot of the half-year for the plan for the second one.

Assumptions: The format of every part is familiar (Quick reviews + unit tests + yesterday's mini-mock). Anxiety is the main enemy; calming rituals matter more than last-minute cramming.

Mid-year test Starter–U4 — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ / TRC pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (16 pts)A) 4 nationality adjectives from countries (Spain → Spanish). B) 4 labels: family/body (hair: curly, blond…). C) 4 food words to sort C/UC. D) 4 times in figures → words (7:45 → quarter to eight) + 2 routine collocations (have ___ , go ___ — trap: go home with no to).
Part 2 · Grammar (20 pts)A) 5 gaps: be (am/is/are/isn't/aren't). B) 4 gaps: have got + 1 possessive 's. C) 4: some/any/there is/there are in a text about a shop. D) 6: present simple aff/neg + 3rd person spelling (watch→watches, study→studies) + order the words → a question (🚀 higher: + write 2 Wh-questions yourself).
Part 3 · Reading (8 pts)A short profile text “my day + my family” (built from the language of U1–U4, our own text, not the publisher's): 4 true/false + 4 detail questions (subskill reading for detail from U4).
Part 4 · Listening (6 pts)The teacher reads a description of a person and their day twice (our script, modelled on Quick review): 3 true/false + 3 gaps (time/number/colour).
Part 5 · Writing (10 pts)“A message about me” to a keypal: name+country (U1) · family have got (U2) · favourite food (U3) · my day + time in words (U4) + because. 🐣 standard: 30+ words using a support frame · 🚀 higher: 45+ words, no frame, + 1 question to the reader. Marked: task 3 · grammar 4 · vocabulary 2 · format Hi…/Bye 1.

Total 60 pts. The point isn't the score — it's a personal error log for the second half of the year. The children have seen all the formats in Quick review U1–4 and the unit tests. Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Test anxietyA start ritual: 3 breaths, strategies in chorus, “this is a photo, not a verdict”. No countdown timer on the screen — only a clock.
CopyingSeating with a gap between students (arranged in advance!), variants A/B with reordered items.
Fast finishers make noiseA quiet envelope on the desk in advance: sudoku-numbers in words, an alphabet code, a colouring scheme — they take it silently.
Someone's sick/absentNote it: a resit with a mini-version in P3 during the speaking checkpoint carousel (individually).
Panic during listeningI read the script TWICE with pauses; before the start: “first reading — just listen, second — write”.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» / «Showdown!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
