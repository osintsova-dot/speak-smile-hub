Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 attractions at festivals and say what they do there: “Do you ride on the (merry-go-round)? — Yes, I do. / I eat (candyfloss) and watch the (fireworks).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «There's a festival» (CD4 14) & the vocabulary chant (CD4 13); brainstorm favourite festivals (Learning to learn); preview the past question “What did you (wear)?” for L2; early literacy — read the attraction words and solve the AB puzzle.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — the mime game is high-energy, plan the freezes. Keep the two-word compounds together as ONE word-picture (big wheel · ghost train · bumper cars).
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 attractions at a festival;
  • say “Do you (ride on the roller coaster)? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.”;
  • read the attraction words and match word ↔ picture.

Structure

Form
Do + you + verb + attraction? Short answers: Yes, I do. / No, I don't. Two-word attractions stay together: a big wheel · a ghost train · a merry-go-round.

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A big wheel — one thing or two things?” → one attraction, two words.
“You eat candyfloss and toffee apples — do you EAT them or RIDE them?” → eat them (food, not a ride!).

Assumptions: Pupils know Unit 8 Past-Simple questions (Did you…?) and festival ideas from their own life. The 12 attractions are largely NEW; Do you…? is known — production is quick. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures.

Presentation · 12 attractions
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD4 13). Each card: What's this? It's… the big wheel! Choral ×3 + one gesture (big wheel = big circle, fireworks = hands explode up, clown = round nose). Contrast held together: BAND — PARADE!

RIDE or EAT? — sort. Two board columns. Hold each card: class calls Ride on it! or Eat it! then the full line: I ride on the merry-go-round. I eat candyfloss.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if you like this attraction… the ghost train! — flash cards fast, everyone moves every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + word. ⭐ Extension: full sentence — I ride on the roller coaster and I scream!

28–36′
Word ↔ picture · Memory
carpet, pairs

▶ Memory · I ride / I eat — in pairs. Each pair: 6 picture cards + 6 WORD cards (readers!) face-down in two rows. Turn one of each; a match is claimed only with a sentence: I ride on the bumper cars! / I eat a toffee apple! Wrong verb → cards go back. Most pairs wins.

ICQ: Can you keep the cards with just the word — “bumper cars”? (→ no — a full sentence!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair at a time — is ride/eat sorted correctly yet?

 6+6 cards per pair
36–44′
Mime & guess
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Slow-motion attraction mime. One player mimes an attraction (roller coaster = hold on & scream, clown = big smile & red nose); their team guesses together: It's the roller coaster! Mimer confirms. Point per correct guess; rotate the mimer every round — everyone mimes once.

Whole team must guess together — no solo shouting: One voice, one team!

At the festival · speak & write
desks

Speak. ▶ RallyRobin: Take turns: At a festival I ride on… / I eat… / I watch…

Write (notebooks / AB p.90 format). Board frame: At a festival I ride on the ___. I eat ___. I watch the ___. Each child writes 3 true sentences, copying attraction words. Fast finishers add and.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from 3 word cards. ⭐ Extension: add an adjective: I ride on the BIG wheel.
