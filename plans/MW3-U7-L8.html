Week 29.03–04.04 · Lesson 8🎨 Project · My town map · draw & present~8 pupils · make + presentPH 26 (/aɪ/ ie igh y i-e · p.72–74) · songs: «Do you want to go to the zoo?»(T19)·Where Are You Going?(56)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will have made and presented a “My town map” — drawing places on their own map and saying: “There's a zoo. There's a park. I want to go to the toy shop!” in a mini gallery.
  • Subsidiary. Recycle all ten places & I want to go to the…? through a creative product; sing the language song (CD T19) & Genki «Where Are You Going?» (56); take turns presenting; Phonics Hero — /aɪ/ ie igh y i-e review, speed-read p.72–74; a happy end-of-unit gallery.
  • Personal. Project = process & pride, not perfection. Model the making & the presenting sentences first. Keep making calm; keep presenting short (2–3 places each) so no long waiting.
By the end, pupils can…
  • draw places on my own town map;
  • present my map — “There's a zoo. I want to go to the park!”;
  • say “Well done!” to a friend's map.

Form

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — /aɪ/ ie · igh · y · i-e review (pie, night, fly, bike): proud, fluent reading, then speed-read p.72–74.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do we draw ONE place, or lots of places in our town?” → lots of places!
“Your friend shows their map — what do we say?” → Well done!

Assumptions: All Unit 7 language is consolidated (L7 review done). Children can name the ten places, invite, and say where they want to go; reading reviews /aɪ/. They know the making & presenting routines from earlier projects (mirrors the U5 concertina project).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Can't decide what to drawModel with the Big Wheel / flashcards first; lay out 4–5 place cards and copy them onto the map.
Making takes over the lessonA ready map outline (roads + empty spaces); children add 4–5 places; time-cap with a visible timer + countdown.
Freezes when presentingSay it together with the puppet first; showing the map + one place is enough.
Audience not listeningGive a job: everyone claps & says Well done! after each child.
Long waiting to presentPresent in a fast carousel / small groups at once — 2–3 places each.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello, map-makers! Today we make our OWN town map — with a zoo, a park, a toy shop… all our places! Show a finished model map, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lesson 26 · /aɪ/ — sounds ie · igh · y · i-e  (all say /aɪ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Read your favourite words from the page — proud, fluent (pie · night · fly · bike)! ▶ Star read — read a /aɪ/ word to Speaky to earn your reading star.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.72–74 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read my · why together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

17–22′
Model the product + sentences
carpet

Watch & copy. Show how to add places to the town map (draw a zoo, a park, a toy shop on the roads). Model presenting with the puppet: There's a zoo. There's a park. I want to go to the toy shop! Everyone says it together once.

22–38′
Make · My town map 🧘
tables

Make it. Children draw 4–5 places on their town map outline (zoo · park · toy shop · cinema · supermarket…) and decorate the roads. Write/trace the place words if they can.

Rehearse as they work. go round: Tell me — what's on your map? Where do you want to go? help each child rehearse quietly at the table.

💪 Support: draw & name the places with the puppet. ⭐ Extension: add I want to go to the… and There's a… for each place.

42–52′
Gallery · present & celebrate
carpet, circle

▶ Show & say. In a fast carousel, each child holds up their map: There's a zoo. There's a park. I want to go to the toy shop! The audience claps & says Well done!

Pairs → circle. pairs first (say it to your partner), then a few to the whole circle — keeps waiting short, everyone speaks.

✔ Assess: who names places on their map with There's a… & I want to go to the…? who needs the puppet? — a lovely end-of-unit picture of progress.

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Show your partner your map and say two places in your town. — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I made it! I can name places on my town map! colour one star.

What amazing work — I'm so proud of you! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
