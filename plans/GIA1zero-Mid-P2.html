WHAT WE DO: Reading → Listening (I read it twice, slowly) → Writing from a support frame (30+ words); a final check

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Part 3 Reading: the instruction out loud → 8–10 min.
  2. Part 4 Listening: First time — just listen. Second time — write. I read the script SLOWLY, twice, with pauses.
  3. Part 5 Writing: the frame is printed in the booklet (Hi! I'm… I'm from… I've got… I like… I get up at… Bye!). “Fill it in about YOURSELF, 30 words minimum; more is fine!”
  4. At 5 min left: Check time: big letters in days, -s, a/an. Collecting with a personal “thank you” for everyone.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. Learners complete the Mid-year test (Starter–U4, standard variant) in a calm, familiar-format setting: vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening and a supported writing task «a message about me».
  • Subsidiary. Lesson A: Parts 1–2 (Vocabulary+Grammar). Lesson B: Parts 3–5 (Reading+Listening+Writing from a support frame) + a calm finishing activity. Test behaviour (silence, “mark it and move on”, checking at the end) is trained as a skill.
  • Personal. The zero-level group's first big test: the atmosphere decides everything. They saw the WHOLE format in P1 — no surprises. My tone is level and warm; support the anxious ones with a look and a nod. Review isn't today (P3).
By the end, students can…
  • work through a long test calmly
  • use «mark it, move on» when stuck
  • write a 30+ word message with the frame
  • check their work before submitting

Mid-year test Starter–U4 — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ / TRC pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (16 pts)A) 4 nationality adjectives from countries (Spain → Spanish). B) 4 labels: family/body (hair: curly, blond…). C) 4 food words to sort C/UC. D) 4 times in figures → words (7:45 → quarter to eight) + 2 routine collocations (have ___ , go ___ — trap: go home with no to).
Part 2 · Grammar (20 pts)A) 5 gaps: be (am/is/are/isn't/aren't). B) 4 gaps: have got + 1 possessive 's. C) 4: some/any/there is/there are in a text about a shop. D) 6: present simple aff/neg + 3rd person spelling (watch→watches, study→studies) + order the words → a question (🚀 higher: + write 2 Wh-questions yourself).
Part 3 · Reading (8 pts)A short profile text “my day + my family” (built from the language of U1–U4, our own text, not the publisher's): 4 true/false + 4 detail questions (subskill reading for detail from U4).
Part 4 · Listening (6 pts)The teacher reads a description of a person and their day twice (our script, modelled on Quick review): 3 true/false + 3 gaps (time/number/colour).
Part 5 · Writing (10 pts)“A message about me” to a keypal: name+country (U1) · family have got (U2) · favourite food (U3) · my day + time in words (U4) + because. 🐣 standard: 30+ words using a support frame · 🚀 higher: 45+ words, no frame, + 1 question to the reader. Marked: task 3 · grammar 4 · vocabulary 2 · format Hi…/Bye 1.

Total 60 pts. The point isn't the score — it's a personal error log for the second half of the year. The children have seen all the formats in Quick review U1–4 and the unit tests. Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Test anxiety (the first big test!)A ritual: 3 breaths, “you've done all of this”, an unhurried start. A clock instead of a countdown timer; I seat the anxious ones closer to me.
They don't understand the taskI read the instructions for every part OUT LOUD in Russian before it starts; worked examples are in the booklet.
CopyingSeating with a gap, variants A/B; but the tone isn't policing: “show YOUR OWN, this is your map”.
Fast finishersA quiet envelope: a colour-by-code picture (letters→colours), a number sudoku; they take it silently.
Tears/freezing upCome over, crouch beside them, whisper: “start with the easy one, I'm right here”; as a last resort — they finish a mini-version in P3.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» / «Showdown!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
