Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask & answer about the past with irregular verbs: “What did you (wear)? — I (wore) a costume. / What did you (eat)? — I (ate) candyfloss.”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Ravi & Lily's dialogue about a festival (CD4 16); discover the rule — most past verbs add -ed, but some are irregular (wear→wore, eat→ate, ride→rode…); recycle the 12 attractions from L1; write questions & answers about the past (AB follow-up).
  • Personal. Drill the irregular pairs as chants (wear–WORE, eat–ATE); keep the interview strictly timed; model the full answer, accept short answers and recast. Note: did lives in the question only — the answer takes the irregular past.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “What did you (do)?” about a past festival;
  • answer with irregular past: “I wore… / I ate… / I saw…”;
  • match 6 irregular verbs to their past forms.

Target language
Lexis
past forms: wear→wore · eat→ate · drink→drank · ride→rode · see→saw · go→went · do→did · have→hadattractions (revision)
Structure
What did you (wear / eat / see)? — I (wore / ate / saw)…  ·  Did you (wear) a costume? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Form
What + did + you + verb…? Answer takes the irregular past (no -ed): What did you eat? → I ate candyfloss. did is in the question only.
Pron. watch
wore /wɔː/; ate /eɪt or et/; drank /dræŋk/; rode /rəʊd/; weak did you /dɪdjə/ at speed.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“What DID you eat? — is did in the question or the answer?” → question only; the answer is ate.
“eat → ate — did we add -ed?” → no! it's irregular — a new word.

Assumptions: 12 attractions are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up). Regular -ed past met in Unit 8; the NEW focus is the irregular forms + the did-question. Pupils can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar table.

did repeated in the answer (“I did ate”)Two-colour board: did in the question box only; cross it out of the answer with a gesture.
Wh-word dropped (“Did you what eat?”)Sentence-strip build: [What][did][you][eat]? — pupils physically order the strips.

Answers too short (“candyfloss”)Recast to the full sentence with a “stretch it” gesture; frame on the board.

19–27′
Tic-Tac-Toe · What did you…?
desks, 2 teams

▶ Noughts & Crosses — festival past. 3×3 grid of verb icons (wear/eat/drink/ride/see/go/have/do). To claim a cell the team ASKS the other team What did you wear at the festival? — the other team answers in chorus with the irregular past: We wore costumes! Both sides speak every turn. Rotate the asker.

🎮 Projector/board option: draw the 3×3 grid on the whiteboard; a rotating child taps the winning cell as the WHOLE team says the answer aloud.

ICQ: Is “did” in the question or the answer? (→ the question!)

Festival diary info-gap
desks, pairs

Model first. Show two half-diaries (A knows what Lily wore & ate; B knows what she rode & saw). Model publicly: What did Lily wear? — She wore a costume. What did she eat? — She ate candyfloss.

▶ Talk Partners info-gap (act. 4-style). Pairs ask & answer to fill the missing cells — What did she drink? What did she ride on? Both write. 5–6 exchanges.

✔ Assess: circulate with the class list — who keeps the irregular past (not -ed)? Recast, note names.

💪 Support: verb + past form printed on the half-sheet. ⭐ Extension: add: What did you do last weekend? — I went to…
