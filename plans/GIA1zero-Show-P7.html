WHAT WE DO: session 1: pairs of hosts receive guests, guests with passports and the deck visit every stand

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Start: The fair is OPEN! Guests: play + ask (a card is fine) → a stamp.
  2. I direct the flow and stand by the quiet stands for the first few minutes.
  3. Pairs of hosts: the first phrase in chorus, then from the card; praising the guest is compulsory.
  4. I do NOT fix mistakes — I note them quietly for the P8 stations.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, every learner has hosted with the 3 phrases and visited every stand, answering familiar questions; stamps are collected, sympathies handed out.
  • Subsidiary. The language of every unit is run through in live mini-dialogues from notes; a sympathy vote + warm feedback; points for courage and growth (KTP) — onto the season's smiley board.
  • Personal. Celebration #3: atmosphere matters more than perfection. Phrase cards in the hosts' hands are legal; my job is cheerleading and “Well done!” at every stand. I don't fix mistakes at the fair — I note them quietly for the P8 stations.
By the end, students can…
  • host with the phrase card in hand
  • play and ask familiar questions at every stand
  • give a stamp fairly (play + ask)
  • vote with a simple because

Form
a stamp = played + 1 familiar question; a sympathy vote = a category + a because-word (funny/kind/interesting).
Pron. watch
fair energy: Welcome! in chorus from the pair of hosts · praise the guest with a smile.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Forgot the host phrase?” → The card's in your hand — read from it, it's professional (tour guides use cheat sheets too).
“The guest asked something not from the deck?” → Good question! It's a… — the safety-net formula. Or call your partner: Team!
“Can I vote for my own stand?” → No: sympathies go to others. Others will vote for your stand.

Assumptions: Stands are ready, phrases and Q&A rehearsed at P6, the question deck is in guests' hands (a legal cheat sheet). Pairs of hosts work TOGETHER — the first phrase in chorus.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
A host freezes with a guestThe pair of hosts: the partner picks it up (the Team! rule); I stay near quiet stands for the first minutes of each session.
Guests run around with no languageA stamp only for play + ask; the question can be read off the deck card — that's allowed and it's English too.
Crowds/empty standsI direct the flow: “Unit 3 is free!”; the passport requires ALL stamps.
Russian takes over the fairPhrase cards in both sides' hands + my presence; a stamp — for any English attempt at all.
Someone is missing their host partner (illness)A backup: a “trainee” neighbour from another team for that session (their own stand is in the other session) — I announce this as an honour role.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
