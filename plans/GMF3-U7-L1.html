Week 01.03–07.03 · Lesson 48✨ NEW · 12 household objects + prepositions~10 pupils · pairs + 2 teamsPupil's Book p.72 · AB p.70
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 household objects and say where things are using prepositions of place: “Where's the (mirror)? — It's in the (living room). It's between the (sofa) and the (bookcase).”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «There's a mystery» (CD3 26) & the vocabulary chant (CD3 25); become detectives — spot what's missing (Kim's Game); recycle rooms of a house & Where…?; plant the past — “Where was it? It was in the living room” (bridge to L2); early literacy — read the object words in the AB diagram & break Luke's code.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — Kim's Game is calm-focus, place-mime is high-energy, plan the freezes. Insist on the article + preposition: the mirror is IN the bedroom, ON the wall.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 household objects;
  • say where an object is with in / on / under / behind / between / next to;
  • read the object words and match word ↔ picture ↔ room.

Structure
Where's the (mirror)? — It's in the (living room). It's between the (sofa) and the (bookcase).  ·  preview: Where was it? — It was in the (kitchen).
Form
It's + preposition + the + place: in / on / under / behind / between … and … / next to. Rooms: living room · kitchen · bedroom · bathroom. Watch the article the for known objects.
Pron. watch
cupboard /ˈkʌbəd/ (silent p — “cubbard”!); wardrobe /ˈwɔːdrəʊb/; bookcase — stress BOOK-case; fridge /frɪdʒ/ (soft dg); mat vs bath — short a vs long a.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“It's between the sofa and the bookcase — how many things is it next to?” → two, one on each side.
“cupboard — do we say the P?” → no! silent p — “cubbard”.

Assumptions: Rooms of a house & in/on/under are known from GMF2; the 12 objects are largely NEW; between / next to / behind are the new prepositions. Where was…? is met receptively today, drilled in L2. Readers — word cards work alongside pictures.

between vs next to muddledTwo-hands gesture for between (thing in the middle), one-hand for next to; sort on the board.

 «There's a mystery» (CD3 26)
10–20′
Presentation · 12 objects
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD3 25). Each card: What's this? It's… a wardrobe! Choral ×3 + one gesture (fridge = open door, mirror = check your hair, bath = wash). Contrast the tricky one: cupboard — silent P — CUB-bard!

Where's the…? (act. 4). Board frame with rooms: Where's the mirror? — It's in the bedroom. It's on the wall. Add between / next to: The mat is between the sofa and the armchair. Class chorus each.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if this goes in the KITCHEN… a cooker! — flash cards fast, everyone moves every time (kitchen / living room / bedroom / bathroom).

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + word. ⭐ Extension: full sentence — The cooker is in the kitchen, next to the sink.

 12 object flashcards · board frame (rooms + prepositions)
20–28′
🔍 Kim's Game · What's missing?
carpet → desks

Detective observation. Lay 8 object cards on a tray. Detectives, look carefully… 10 seconds! Cover with a cloth, secretly remove one. What's missing?

▶ Kim's Game — Showdown. Every child WRITES the missing object on a mini-whiteboard → Show me! Reveal together, self-check. Rotate: a pupil removes the card next round. 5 rounds — add prepositions: The mirror is missing! It was NEXT TO the painting.

ICQ: Do you shout, or write and wait for “Show me”? (→ write and wait!)

✔ Assess: boards are your check — who spells the object AND remembers where it was?

 object cards · tray + cloth · mini-whiteboards
28–36′
Where's the…? · Memory
carpet, pairs

▶ Memory · Where's the…? — in pairs. Each pair: 6 picture cards + 6 WORD cards (readers!) face-down in two rows. Turn one of each; a match is claimed only with a full sentence: Where's the sofa? — It's in the living room! Wrong/no room → cards go back. Most pairs wins.

ICQ: Can you keep the cards if you just say “sofa”? (→ no — a full sentence with the room!)

 6+6 cards per pair
36–44′
Mime & guess · the object
carpet, 2 teams

▶ Slow-motion object mime. One player mimes USING an object (wash hands = sink, sleep = bed/wardrobe area, cook = cooker); their team guesses together: It's a cooker! Mimer confirms + adds the room: Yes — it's in the kitchen! Point per correct guess; rotate the mimer — everyone mimes once.

Whole team guesses together — no solo shouting: One voice, one team!

 object flashcards (prompt deck)
44–52′
My room · speak & write
desks

Speak. ▶ RallyRobin: Take turns: In my living room there's a… It's next to the…

Write (notebooks / AB p.70 format). Board frame: There's a ___ in my ___. It's ___ the ___. Each child writes 3 true sentences about their home, copying object words. Fast finishers add and.

💪 Support: copy the frame + choose from picture-word cards. ⭐ Extension: add a preposition of choice: The lamp is between the bed and the wardrobe.
