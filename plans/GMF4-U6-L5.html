Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to read & say the /əʊ/ sound spelt o_e and o (the robot rolls home with a rose), talk about robot competitions in the USA, and read & plan an advert — three mini-blocks, one “machines” thread.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics: notice that o_e and o both spell /əʊ/ (recognise → say → sort, next to ow & oa); culture — RoboGames in California (robots that do sports, kung fu, breakdancing; robot dogs; made of new & recycled materials) + video, 21C: creating adverts; literacy — text type an advert, wow words (incredible · amazing · fantastic), slogan · price · opinion · special offer; plan & write an advert.
  • Personal. Three blocks — keep transitions crisp (chant/action bridges); the robot tongue-twister is play; cap each block hard so the advert actually gets planned.
By the end, pupils can…
  • say the /əʊ/ twister and sort o_e / o words;
  • name 2–3 things robots can do at RoboGames;
  • find the parts of an advert (slogan · price · wow words) and plan one with a partner.

Structure
The robot rolls home with a rose and hits its nose on a phone.  ·  Robots are made of new and recycled materials.  ·  advert: NO MORE CHORES! You need ROBOMATIC! It's fully automatic!
Form
/əʊ/ = o_e (home, rose, nose, phone) AND o (robot, rolls, no, go) — (also ow: snowman · oa: goat). Advert layout: product name · slogan · true statements · opinion · price · special offer · wow words.
Pron. watch
/əʊ/ clean in home/rose/nose/phone/robot; automatic /ˌɔːtəˈmætɪk/; incredible /ɪnˈkredəbl/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“home and no — same /əʊ/ sound? Same letters?” → same sound, different spelling (o_e vs o)!
“An advert — does it want you to BUY or just to read?” → to buy — that's why it uses wow words!

Assumptions: Machines, used for, materials & made of are active (L1–L4). Sound–letter awareness from earlier phonics is enough to enjoy the o_e / o noticing (по учебнику). Pupils can read the short advert texts with support. RoboGames links the whole “machines” theme.

Block 1 · Phonics /əʊ/ (o_e · o)
carpet → desks

Speak and spell (act. 1, CD3 15). PB p.65: The robot rolls home with a rose and hits its nose on a phone. Remember! snowman · goat. Listen, then the speed ladder: slow → fast → whisper. Pairs test each other ×3.

Sort the /əʊ/ spellings. Hoops/columns: o_e (home, rose, nose, phone) · o (robot, no, go) · (spares: ow — snowman · oa — goat). Hold up word cards — every child points/steps to the right hoop BEFORE you place each card.

Notice the rule. Home and robot — same sound /əʊ/, different letters! o_e has a magic e; o is just one letter. (notice, don't lecture).

💪 Support: say the word, teacher shows the hoop. ⭐ Extension: add one more /əʊ/ word to a hoop (bone, so).

✔ Assess: who still reads o_e as short /ɒ/? Note them — extra RallyRead next unit.

Block 3 · Literacy · an advert
carpet → desks, pairs

Read an advert (PB p.66, act. 1–3, CD3 17). «ROBOMATIC»: Where can you see adverts? What is this advert for? What can the Robomatic do? Read/listen; find the slogan (NO MORE CHORES!), the price (£499), the wow words, an opinion.

▶ Advert feature hunt. Race in pairs to find on the PB p.67 adverts (blender / MixerTronic): Find a slogan · a true statement · the price · the product name · wow words · an opinion. First hands up read the answer aloud — everyone scans.

Writing tip (PB p.67). We use wow words to make texts exciting — incredible, amazing, fantastic, delicious! Chorus the wow words with a sparkle gesture.

▶ Plan an advert (Cooperative learning, AB p.64 format). Pairs choose a machine & make notes: Product name · What it's for · What it's made of · Slogan · Price · Wow words · An opinion. 2 pairs share their slogan aloud.

💪 Support: fill the advert template with word support. ⭐ Extension: write the full advert with 3+ wow words & a special offer.
