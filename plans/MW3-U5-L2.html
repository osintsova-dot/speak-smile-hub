Week 11.01–17.01 · Lesson 2✨ Focus · +have breakfast/lunch/dinner/shower · What do you do every day?~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 25 (/iː/ ee ea e-e y · p.69–71) · Genki: This Is My Day(111)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name four MORE daily routines — have breakfast · have lunch · have dinner · have a shower — and talk about their day: “What do you do every day? — I get up. I have breakfast.”
  • Subsidiary. Order the routines through the day; sing the language song «What do you do every day?» (CD2 T19) with mimes; play Genki Karuta (31) & What's Left? (40); Phonics Hero — /iː/ ee ea e-e y (consolidation), speed-read p.69–71; listen & find the new routines (PB p.30).
  • Personal. Model What do you do every day? many times with the puppet before children say it. Keep speaking short & choral first, then paired — no long waiting. Praise every routine named.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name four new routines (breakfast · lunch · dinner · shower);
  • ask & answer “What do you do every day? — I get up”;
  • put my day in order and mime it.

Form
Question chunk What do you do every day? → answer I + routine. Sequence of I… statements = my day. have + meal / shower.
Pron. watch
rising What do you do every day? ↗; have a shower linked; the /iː/ in eat / breakfast… keep /æ/ short in have.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — /iː/ ee ea e-e y again, a bit faster: blend (t-r-ee → tree, ea-t → eat, happ-y) with a picture → speed-read p.69–71. Reading from zero, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Have breakfast — morning, or bedtime?” → morning!
“I ask What do you do every day? — do you tell me ONE thing or your whole day?” → things I do every day!

Assumptions: The six routines from L1 are fresh (checked in the warm review) and re-used on the wheel. The four have… routines are new; the question What do you do every day? is understood receptively — producing it is today's stretch. Reading continues from L1.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Good morning! What do YOU do every day? Let's mime our whole day — from waking up to bedtime! Mime a fast day, then freeze.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 25 · long /iː/ (again) — sounds ee · ea · e-e · y  (all say /iː/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: s-ee → see, r-ea-d → read, ea-t → eat, happ-y — sweep the sounds together + a picture. ▶ Sound hop — children hop to the /iː/ spelling Speaky calls (ee / ea / y), then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.69–71 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read love · another together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.69–71 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · four new routines + my day
carpet

Guess the new ones! Slide out the four new cards one by one: What else do I do every day? Guess! Reveal + mime + echo (have breakfast · have lunch · have dinner · have a shower).

Order my day. Turn the four cards face-down, mix them: Is this have lunch or have dinner? Guess! Then order ALL the routines through the day (get up → breakfast → … → dinner).

Choral first. Point to the flashcards and model your day, then ask: What do you do every day? — I get up. I have breakfast. I play! children answer chorally with you.

 routine flashcards ×10 · Speaky
25–31′
Game · Mime your day! ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Mime your day! Everyone stands. Let's mime our day! I get up… I get dressed… I have breakfast… Say your day one routine at a time; children join the mime AND the sentence with you, starting I get up. Repeat faster, add and I play! with a jump.

 routine flashcards · Speaky
31–37′
Game · Genki Karuta (31) + What's Left? (40)
carpet, seated

▶ Genki Karuta Each child has the routine mini-cards spread in front. Speaky calls a routine — children grab it FAST and shout it. Calm, seated, everyone plays every round.

▶ What's Left? Finish with What's Left? — call routines to put UP one by one; everyone holds the last card and shouts it together …have a shower!

 routine mini-cards (a set per child) · Speaky
37–44′
Song — «What do you do every day?» + Genki (111)
carpet, standing

Sing the day. Play the language song «What do you do every day?» (CD2 T19). Teach the mimes (shrug for the question, wash/brush/eat, jump for play). Sing 3× changing the volume. What else do they do in the song? Play!

Day boost. Reprise Genki «This Is My Day» (111) — a pure day-in-order boost.

Draw a routine. children draw one missing routine, then answer What do you do every day? — I get up!
