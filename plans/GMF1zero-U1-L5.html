Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will have consolidated all Unit 1 language — the classroom objects (book, pen, pencil, rubber, ruler, crayon, sharpener, pencil case, school bag, computer + desk / chair / board / bin / shelf), “Where's my…? — It's here / It's (in/on/under) the…”, “Put the (book) on the (shelf)” and “May I borrow your…? — Of course!” — and can retrieve and use it confidently in the review games and the quiz, ready for the Unit 1 test next lesson.
  • Subsidiary. Phonics Hero ур.16 (b) закрепление — ch · sh · th: sort → dictation → tricky strip they, all → mini-story «Fish and chips» (тетрадь стр.44–45); sight words live, give; formative self-check (My progress traffic light, AB p. 13 ex. 3).
  • Personal. Keep it a PLAY lesson, not a worksheet lesson: review lives inside 3 games (memo · relay · flagship quiz) + one short paired notebook task; teacher-talk low, ICQ before every game. Run all-response formats (Showdown / Numbered Heads) so every child answers every question — no “captains answer everything”. Keep each reward moment short so language time wins.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name all the classroom objects and answer “Where's my…?” without support;
  • make a full sentence with a preposition — “It's (in/on/under) the (bag)” — to claim a card;
  • say “Put the (book) on the (shelf)” and “May I borrow your…? — Of course!”;
  • sort, read & write ch/sh/th words (chip, ship, fish, this) and chorus the story «Fish and chips»;
  • self-assess with the traffic light — ready for the Unit 1 test.

18–25′
▶ Game 1 · MEMO (teams)
carpet · teams of 4

▶ Memory (memo) — Where's my…? hidden inside. Each team of 4 gets a small set: 4 pairs of school-object cards (8 cards), face-down in a grid on the carpet. Turn order rotates (Kagan RallyRobin).

How each turn works. The player flips 2 cards; as each card turns over, the WHOLE team names it — It's a ruler! (all-response — nobody stays silent). A match = the player asks Where's my ruler? and takes the pair with It's here! No match → cards flip back, next player.

ICQ: When a card turns over — who says the word? One child or EVERYBODY? (→ everybody!) Most pairs wins the team a ⭐. Reset if loud: Freeze!

Support ▸ weaker teams use picture–picture pairs.   Extension ▸ stronger teams add a preposition — hide the pair “in / on / under” a real thing and claim it with It's under the book!

▶ Game 2 · Card-Grab RELAY (⚡ speed)
open space · 2–3 team lines

▶ Genki-style card-grab relay (everyone runs). Spread all 10 object flashcards on the floor at the far end. Teams line up at the start. 🦝 Speaky / teacher calls a question — Where's my ruler? (or What's this? … the crayon!) → the first runner from each team races to the pile, grabs the right card, runs back and says the full chunk: It's here! It's a ruler!

Correct + spoken = the team keeps the card and a point; the runner hand-touches the next runner. Rotate so every child runs at least twice. Waiting team-mates cheer and count the score in English: One, two, three!

Attention reset between rounds: Class? — Yes! / Freeze!

Support ▸ call the object clearly + hold up the matching picture as a cue.   Extension ▸ ask for two things at once — the pen AND the rubber!

📓 Interactive notebook · вклейка · «Review» — 🪜 word ladder (spelling self-test)  ✅ IN NOTEBOOK
What it is: the 10 Unit 1 words — school bag · book · pen · ruler · rubber · pencil · pencil case · sharpener · crayons · glue — each on a shrinking ladder (school bag → school ba_ → school b__ → … → s_).
Pupils do: pick 3 words (their choice — agency), write each rung getting shorter, last rung from memory. Whisper-spell as you write. Finish the rest at home. Tick the strip: I can say the words · I can spell the words → Great job, superstar!
📓 Interactive notebook · вклейка · «Review перевод» — 🔗 MATCH IT (in class) + TRANSLATE IT (home)  ✅ IN NOTEBOOK
MATCH IT — здесь, на уроке (pairs, RallyCoach): two columns — Russian ↔ English — 10 whole chunks from all of Unit 1 mixed on purpose: Где моя ручка? — Where's my pen? · Вот она! — It's here! · Положи книгу на полку. — Put the book on the shelf. · Карандаш под столом. — The pencil is under the desk. · Где мой пенал? Он в портфеле. — Where's my pencil case? It's in the school bag. … + word bank. Draw a line Russian → English.
RallyCoach (all engaged): Partner A reads the Russian aloud → Partner B SAYS the English chunk → then A draws the line and praises; swap next item. Both talk every turn — nobody silently ticks. ICQ: Say it first, or draw first? (→ say it first!)
TRANSLATE IT → домой: the writing half of the sheet (copy the English chunk under each Russian) is homework — today we only MATCH & SAY in class.
Why here (retrieval + interleaving): the Russian is a cue → child reconstructs the English from memory (cued recall, testing effect); the 10 chunks interleave every Unit 1 structure (Where's my…? / It's here / prepositions / Put… / borrow), so pupils must discriminate which structure fits — the exact thinking the test needs.
Support ▸ pairs use the word bank + do the first 5 pairs only.   Extension ▸ fast pairs cover the English and re-say a chunk from the Russian alone; start TRANSLATE IT early.

🏆 Game 3 · FLAGSHIP QUIZ + reward
📺 teams · projector

Set-up (1–2 min). 3 teams of 4, numbered heads 1–4, one mini-whiteboard EACH. Quiz grid on screen — categories: School things 🎒 · Where's my…? 📍 · Sounds & spelling 🔤 · Class rules ✨. Rules + ICQ: Who writes? — Everybody! When do you show? — On “Showdown!”

Round flow (Kagan Showdown + Numbered Heads — all answer every question). Question up (half-hidden picture: What is it? · position: Where's the ruler — in / on / under? · sound: Write the word — sh-i-p! · read a digraph word: chip · ship · fish · class rule: Put your hand up to…?). Every child writes/draws → Showdown! → all boards up → call a number: Number 3! — that child answers aloud in a full sentence.

Predict (thinking). On a half-hidden picture, before revealing: Just a corner! What do you THINK it is? Write your guess! Reveal → Were you right? — guess-and-check every round.

Reward shot. Correct = the answering child takes the shot: animated football penalty on screen — a GOAL = team points, class counts in English: One, two, three… GOAL! (No projector → paper goal + soft ball, ≈ Mido №91 Bowling reskin.) Rotate teams so over ~10–12 questions every child shoots at least once.

Support ▸ word-box / picture bank stays on a side card so weaker writers copy, not freeze; accept one correct word.   Extension ▸ when a strong pupil is “Number”, require the full sentence — It's a pencil case. It's on the desk.
🔨 Flagship interactive · projector · BUILD · “Unit 1 Team Quiz + Reward”  🔨 BUILD
Screen 1 — Grid: 4×3 numbered tiles under the 4 category headers. Tap a tile → question (picture / half-hidden object / audio word / digraph word / rule prompt). Used tiles grey out.
Screen 2 — Answer: big “Show your boards!” + 5-second timer; ✔ / ✗ to score; a full-sentence model appears for choral echo.
Screen 3 — Reward: football penalty — child “kicks”, goalie dives, animated GOAL (or bowling reskin). One shot = +points.
Screen 4 — Scoreboard: 3 team bars, huge numbers, “Teamwork ⭐” toggle.
Teacher: host only — YOU tap tiles & call the number so no captain dominates. S&S purple #6C3FC5 / honey #F5C842. Offline reserve: printed grid + paper goal + soft ball works identically.

🎮 Ready-made engine for this quiz: class-quiz — the S&S bowling/basketball quiz on this unit, 1–4 teams: a picture question on screen → the team answers — Where's my bag? → It's here! → one child throws on screen; the score counts itself.

Finish with totals + teamwork star: Give me five, champions!
