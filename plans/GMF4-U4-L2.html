Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to form and use the superlative of short adjectives to compare planets: “Jupiter is the biggest planet. Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. Neptune is the furthest from the Sun.”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Josh & Ravi's dialogue about the solar-system model (CD2 18); discover the rules — add -est (small → smallest), double the last letter (big → biggest), irregular (good → best); use the before every superlative; recycle the 12 space words from L1; write true sentences about the planets (AB).
  • Personal. Drill the spelling rules until they're automatic; keep the visible before every superlative; model the full sentence, accept short answers and recast. Make the planet facts real — biggest, hottest, coldest, furthest.
By the end, pupils can…
  • add -est / double the letter / use best correctly;
  • always say “the” before the superlative;
  • make true superlative sentences about the planets.

Lexis

Structure
Jupiter is the biggest planet.  ·  Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.  ·  Neptune is the furthest planet from the Sun.  ·  Earth is the best planet!
Form
Short adj + -est (cold → coldest); double final consonant (big → biggest, hot → hottest); irregular good → best, bad → worst. Always the + superlative. furthest = far.
Pron. watch
biggest /ˈbɪɡɪst/ (double g, one /ɡ/); hottest /ˈhɒtɪst/; furthest /ˈfɜːðɪst/; the /ðə/ weak before the superlative.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“big → big___ — one g or two?” → two! double the last letter → biggest.
“the biggest or just biggest?” → THE — one winner, so we need the.

Assumptions: 12 space words are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up); planet order is on the board. Superlatives are largely NEW as production — pupils met comparatives in Level 3. They can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar table.

▶ Noughts & Crosses — planet facts. 3×3 grid of adjectives (big · hot · cold · close · far · small · good…). To claim a cell, the team makes a TRUE superlative sentence — Jupiter is the biggest planet. The other team judges true/false with the fact strip. Rotate the speaker.
