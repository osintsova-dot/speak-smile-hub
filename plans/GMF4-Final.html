📝 Give Me Five! 4 · Final Test — End-of-Year Written Control (Units 1–9)
A2 young learners (~10 y.o., readers · continuing line) · 60 min · calm written protocol, model-first · solo desks
Week 10.05–16.05 · Lesson 69📝 Final Test — calm & kind~10 pupils · solo desksYLE Movers/Flyers practice format
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. Pupils show the year’s learning on the end-of-year written test — four parts (vocabulary · grammar · reading · listening) across Units 1–9 — in a calm, supported, model-first format with no surprises.
  • Subsidiary. Exam micro-skills for the whole test, not one unit: budget your time across 4 parts, read the example first, skip & return, check your name & that nothing is blank. A proud year-end milestone.
  • Personal. Radiate calm; no competitive games; help procedure, not answers (“read the example again”). Note common errors — they feed tomorrow’s Speaking Exam warm-up.
By the end, pupils can…
  • work independently through a 4-part paper;
  • manage ~13–14 min per part with a visible timer;
  • use “skip and come back” and check their work.

Assessed language · structure of the paper
Part 1 · Vocabulary
picture / word matching across all units: food & cooking, character & past, prehistory & space, free time & instruments, machines & materials, city & transport, jobs & workplaces, countries & holiday activities.
Part 2 · Grammar
quantifiers (a lot of / some / a few / a little) · was/were · past simple & could · superlatives · first conditional with can · passive (used for / made of) · will-future · have to & who/where · present perfect (ever / been).
Part 3 · Reading
read a short text / dialogue → comprehension (true-false / match / gap) — YLE Flyers reading style.
Part 4 · Listening
listen & tick / number / match — each item played twice, point-follow the numbers. YLE Movers/Flyers listening style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“If a task is hard — stop, or skip and return?” → skip and return.
“How many times do you hear each listening item?” → twice.
“Is it OK to look at a friend’s paper?” → no — I want to see what YOU know; that’s how I help you.

Assumptions: All nine units taught, tested and reviewed (incl. Reserve 1). Pupils know the four part-types, the traffic-light self-check and the “quiet menu” routine. Source paper = GMF Level 4 Units 1–9 Higher Level Tests (course folder); assemble the four parts from it (only the Higher Level paper is provided — scale it to your group).

Likely problemWhat you do
Year-end nerves — “it’s the BIG test”Normalise: “it’s the same as our books, just quiet and longer”; smile; breathing in-2-3-out-2-3 before starting.
Running out of time on one partPre-agreed pace: ~13–14 min/part; announce softly at each switch: “Halfway — move on if you’re stuck.”
Listening — someone loses the placePoint-follow the item numbers on your held-up copy; play each item twice; pause between items.
Early finishers disturb othersQuiet menu ready ON desks before the test: mixed word-search + reading corner. No walking about.
A child freezes completelyKneel beside, whisper the example through, do the first item together, then step away.

Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🙋 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.
