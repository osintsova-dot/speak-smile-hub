Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to ask about the future city: “Will there be (ice rinks) in the future?” and answer with short answers: “Yes, there will. / No, there won't.”
  • Subsidiary. Understand Lily & Ravi's dialogue about a book about the future (CD3 27); discover the rule — Will there be…? → Yes, there will / No, there won't; recycle the 12 city places from L1; predict what our city will be like; write future questions & answers (AB).
  • Personal. Drill the short answers until automatic (Yes, there WILL / No, there WON'T — not “yes it will be”); keep the question frame visible; model the full answer, accept short answers and recast.
By the end, pupils can…
  • ask “Will there be … in the future?” correctly;
  • answer “Yes, there will. / No, there won't.”;
  • make predictions about places in our city.

Structure
Will there be (hotels) in the future? — Yes, there will.  ·  Will there be (airports)? — No, there won't.  ·  There will be (football stadiums), but there won't be any (airports).
Form
Will there be + noun + in the future? Short answers: Yes, there will / No, there won't (won't = will not). Statements: There will be… / There won't be any…
Pron. watch
won't /wəʊnt/ (long /əʊ/, NOT “want” /wɒnt/); weak there /ðə/ at speed; rising question ↗ then falling short answer ↘.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Am I asking about NOW or later?” → later — the FUTURE.
“Will there be robots? — No, there ___.” → won't!

Assumptions: 12 city places are fresh from L1 (checked in warm-up). Will met receptively before; forming the question + the short answers is today's new focus. Pupils can read the dialogue speech bubbles & the grammar clue box.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
won't / want confusedBoard split: WON'T = will not (future) with a big /əʊ/; contrast-say won't ↔ want side by side.
Short answer over-built (“yes it will be”)Anchor cards: Yes, there will. / No, there won't. — beat-drill; stop at “will/won't”.
Interview = same partner againTrade prediction cards after each exchange (new partner every time).
Question word order muddledFrame strips on the board: Will · there be · ___ · in the future? — rebuild each time.
Weaker pupils silent in teamsTic-Tac-Toe: BOTH teams speak every turn (one asks, one answers); rotate the speaker.

▶ Noughts & Crosses — future city. 3×3 grid of place pictures. To claim a cell, a team ASKS the other team — Will there be libraries in the future? — and the other team answers with a short answer + a reason: Yes, there will. Everyone reads! Both sides speak every turn. Rotate the asker.
