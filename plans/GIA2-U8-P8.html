WHAT WE DO: self-marking the vocabulary/grammar against the key; four typical errors worked through with no names

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Keys out. Mark your own — honestly. Circle every slip.
  2. The typical ones on the board: will + to or will + -s · going to without be · present continuous with no time and no second person (“we're meeting” — when? with whom?) · the apostrophe in the wrong place (its / it's, parents / parents').
  3. Your circles = your Unit 9 revision list. Do not lose that paper.
  4. Speaking (10 marks) — in pairs, in turn, while the rest check the written part.
📺 On screen: the keys · the feedback slide
👦 Students do: they mark their own work — they see the errors before the mark. Expect: everyone's circles look similar, and that is reassuring

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
