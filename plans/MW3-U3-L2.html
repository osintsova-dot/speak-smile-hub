Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to say what there is in the park with There's a (pond)! and There are (trees)!, and to name four new park things — trees · flowers · path · grass.
  • Subsidiary. Introduce trees · flowers · path · grass; sing the coursebook song «There's a pond in the park» (CD1 T39); play Guess what's in the park!; sing Genki «How Many?» (30) for plural there are; Phonics Hero — sounds ou · oi (consolidation), speed-read p.46–48; listen & tick the park objects (PB p.54).
  • Personal. Model the two frames MANY times with the puppet before children say them. One thing = There's a…, many things = There are… — feel the difference with fingers (1 finger vs many). Praise every attempt at the full sentence.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name four new park things — trees · flowers · path · grass;
  • say “There's a (pond)!” for one thing;
  • say “There are (trees)!” for many things.

Structure
There's a (pond)!  ·  There are (trees)!  ·  What's in the park?
Form
There's a + one thing (singular). There are + many things (plural, +s). Question: What's in the park?
Pron. watch
linked There's-a… · There-are…; final /z/ on plurals trees · flowers.
Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 — review sounds ou · oi (Jolly gesture) → blend (ou → shout, oi → point) with a picture → speed-read p.46–48 a bit faster. Reading from zero, GMF1-style.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“One pond — do I say There's a pond or There are pond?” → There's a pond!
“Lots of trees — There's a tree or There are trees?” → There are trees!

Assumptions: The six park items are fresh from L1 (checked in the warm review). The four new words are very picturable. The frames There's a / There are are understood receptively; producing them is today's stretch. Reading continues from L1.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Uses There's a for pluralsShow fingers: 1 finger = There's a…, wiggle all fingers = There are…; puppet models.
Drops the plural /s/Exaggerate it: treessss · flowerssss; children hiss it with you.
Long sentence to holdChunk it in claps: There's (clap) a (clap) pond! (clap).
Same partner every timeMusic on = walk, music off = new partner (Mix-Pair).
Waiting = wrigglingEveryone holds a park card and answers chorally at the same time, then in pairs.

Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Welcome back to the park! Let's look… what can you SEE? Binocular-hands, look around, then dance.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

▶ Park flash. flash the six L1 park cards — chorus + action (slide · pond · swing · seesaw · roundabout · bench).

Wheel chorus. point to a wheel pod → whole class say the colour + park item: Blue… slide! Orange… pond! Warms L1 before the new words.

 park flashcards · Big Wheel mat
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 17 (again) — sounds ou · oi. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Build the word: ou → cloud, oi → boil, oi → join — sweep the sounds + a picture. ▶ Sound hop — children hop to the sound-card Speaky calls, do the action, then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.46–48 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read live · give together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.46–48 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · four new things + the frames
carpet

Peek & reveal. Cover a park scene with a cloth; lift a corner slowly: Ooh… what's in the park? What can you see? Reveal trees · flowers · path · grass one by one with a big gesture (arms up = trees, hands blooming = flowers, walk fingers = path, wiggle low = grass).

There's a… / There are… Model the two frames with fingers: one pond → There's a pond! many trees → There are trees! Whole class copies each, chorally first.

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the Big Wheel with the park items on their colour pods on screen (blue–slide · red–bench · yellow–swing · green–roundabout · pink–seesaw · orange–pond) and play the park story video & songs from the coursebook (Navio) app. Our Genki songs & games add to it — we don't reproduce the coursebook media.

25–32′
Song — «There's a pond in the park» (CD1 T39)
carpet, standing

Sing & move. Play the coursebook song «There's a pond in the park» (CD1 T39). Teach the actions: point out to the pond, arms-up for trees, blooming hands for flowers, walk on the spot for the path. Sing 3× — normal, super-quiet, giant voice.

As you sing, point to each thing on the park poster/wheel; children join the There's a… / There are… line.

 «There's a pond in the park» song (CD1 T39) · park poster
32–39′
Game · Guess what's in the park! + Steal the Bacon (19) ⚡
carpet / floor space

▶ Guess what's in the park! Coursebook Guess what's in the park!: turn the park flashcards face DOWN; children guess There's a bench! There are flowers! — turn one over to check, whole class says the full sentence.

▶ Grab & say! then Genki Steal the Bacon, re-skinned: two teams; park flashcards in the middle. 🦝 Speaky calls a thing; one runner from each team grabs it and says the sentence There's a slide! Every item called → everyone runs & speaks at least once.

💪 Support: copy the frame with the picture. ⭐ Extension: add a colour: There's a red bench!

 park flashcards ×10 · Speaky
39–46′
Song — Genki «How Many?» + count the park
carpet, standing

How many? Play Genki «How Many?» (30) — count things in the park: How many trees? — One, two, three… There are three trees! A plural boost that feeds straight into There are (three) trees.

Count & say. point to the poster: How many flowers? — There are flowers! children answer with the plural frame.

 Genki «How Many?» (30) · park poster
46–54′
Table time · Listen & tick (PB p.54) 🧘
tables

Listen & find. Play the audio — children listen and tick (✔) the park objects they hear on the page.

Say what there is. In pairs: A points to a picture and says There's a pond!, B points to another and says There are trees! — then swap.

Fast finishers colour one thing in the park.

54–58′
Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Tell your partner one thing in the park — full sentence! — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I can say “There's a…” and “There are…”! colour one star.

Super sentences, everyone! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
