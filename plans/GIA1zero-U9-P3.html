Audio step by step: 5 dialogues
Try it · five conversations (audio 60) step by step
desks

WHAT WE DO: the “pictures a/b/c” format step by step: #1 we work through TOGETHER → #2–5 on their own; two listens, no pens on the first

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Step-model on item 1 together: Three girls — describe them! Long straight fair hair… short dark hair… curly hair and glasses. We listen to the fragment → choose TOGETHER (A).
  2. Items 2–5 on their own: first 90 sec describing the pictures in pairs (a clock! a café! a bag-t-shirt-book! transport!).
  3. Listen 1 (audio 60): pens ON THE DESK, only listening and holding a finger on an option. Listen 2: marking it. Key: 2 C · 3 C · 4 A · 5 B.
  4. Working through one trap: You heard ‘taxi’ — but the answer is underground. Why? It's CHEAPER — listen to the END!
  5. No track — the TB pp.135–136 script by roles, twice.
📺 On screen: SB p.112 ex.2 · audio 60 (script TB pp.135–136) (Backup: no track — I read the script aloud at listening speed.)
👦 Students do: they describe the pictures before the audio and decide in two listens — following the key protocol. Expected: 3–4 out of 5 (item 1 together). Typical error: grabbing the first word they hear — the trap was set on purpose.

📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can read the festivals guide chunk by chunk and complete the table with support, and solve both listening formats step by step (pictures a/b/c + a podcast) using the “secret keys”: pictures BEFORE · listen TWICE · “?” and move on.
  • Subsidiary. The year's Subskills tips are gathered (ex.1); a Word work review with pictures; the ex.1 p.112 warm-up sheets as a “cheat sheet” for the audio; discussing “where would you go” with the frame.
  • Personal. We present the Exam tips as “secret keys” — gifts, not requirements. Audio is scarier than reading for the from-zero line: two listens are sacred, the first one WITHOUT writing. Success = “I understood a real podcast”, not speed.
By the end, students can…
  • say the 3 reading steps in order
  • complete the festivals table chunk by chunk
  • solve picture items a/b/c after describing pictures first
  • answer 3+ podcast questions using two listens

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Does a quick read mean understanding everything?” → No: just what the text is about.
“Do we write the answers on the first listen?” → No: we listen and mark ticks/‘?’. We write on the second.
“Is a ‘?’ a failure?” → No, it's a plan for the second listen!

Assumptions: They read short texts at this level with support; we cut the long guide into 3 festival chunks. The exam formats are new — we talk through and rehearse every step together on one item before the students work on their own.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
They're scared of a long textCutting it into 3 chunks by festival; after each chunk — checking that chunk's row in the table. The elephant, one bite at a time.
They get lost in the audio from the first secondsKey #2: before the audio we describe the pictures in chorus — half the words are already out before the track starts.
They write during the first listen and miss everythingRitual: pens DOWN ON THE DESK on the first listen. Physically.
They can't keep up with the podcastThe pause button is legal for the from-zero line: after every answer fragment — stop, tick, go on.
“Exam” sounds scaryThe word exam always comes paired with “secret keys”: we have keys — we're not scared.
Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
