Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to value being curious & learning (go to school · look at books · play games · talk to friends · watch films), cross the road safely in town (traffic lights: red = stop, green = go; look left & right), and enjoy the culture story «The enormous carrot» — a story about helping & teamwork.
  • Subsidiary. Values — sing «I find out about my world» (CD T27) & name five ways to learn; Content — road safety (traffic-light game + look left/right); Culture — «The enormous carrot» (CD T28) + pull, pull, pull! + helping each other; Phonics Hero — the /eɪ/ spellings ai · ay · a-e, speed-read p.67–68.
  • Personal. Keep the three mini-blocks crisp with song/puppet bridges. Values & culture through DOING (mime the ways to learn, pull the carrot together) not talking-at. Every child crosses a pretend road on green, and every child helps pull the carrot.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name ways I learn about my world (books · school · friends · films);
  • cross the road safely — stop on red, go on green, look left & right;
  • join in «The enormous carrot» — pull, pull, pull, and help my friends!

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“The traffic light is RED — do we stop or go?” → stop!
“The carrot is enormous — is it tiny, or very very big?” → very big!

Assumptions: All town language is secure from L1–L5; reading is on /eɪ/. Children handle three short blocks well if each is active & bridged by a song. Traffic-light colours (red/green) are known; left/right are recycled. The pull-together folk-tale format (like The Gigantic Turnip) is easy to join.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
“Interest in learning” too abstractMake it doable: mime each way to learn — open a book, talk to a friend, watch a film — praise it live as learning!
Road-safety = just talkRun it as a game: teacher's card RED = freeze, GREEN = walk on the spot; add look left, look right heads.
Culture block = just listeningEveryone lines up behind the carrot & pulls together on pull, pull, PULL!; find a helper each time.
Over-excited pullingPull on the spot on your countdown; Freeze! resets between pulls.
Three blocks overrunHard mini-caps; a song bridge moves everyone on.
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! I LOVE to learn new things! Do you? Books, friends, films… let's find out about the world! Open-a-book, chat, watch mimes, then dance.

9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero review · lesson 24 · /eɪ/ — sounds ai · ay · a-e  (all say /eɪ/). 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds: r-ai-n → rain, d-ay → day, c-a-ke → cake — big arm-sweep + a meaning picture. ▶ Sound hop — children hop to the /eɪ/ spelling Speaky calls (ai / ay), then blend a word with it.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.67–68 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read play · today together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

Values · ways to learn + song
carpet, standing

Talk & do. Ask How do you learn about the world? Elicit ways to learn — go to school · look at books · play games · talk to friends · watch films. Mime each one.

Values song. Sing «I find out about my world» (CD T27) — the ways to learn, with mimes. How many can you count?

25–31′
Content · road safety in town ⚡
carpet → floor ‘road’

Cross safely. How do we get to places in town safely? Red — STOP! Green — GO! Hold up a RED card — everyone freezes; a GREEN card — walk on the spot. Add Look left… look right… now cross! turn heads left, then right.

Left & right. Genki Left And Right (06) — a quick left/right movement song to practise looking both ways.

💪 Support: do the stop/go & look left-right. ⭐ Extension: say Red — stop! Green — go! Look left, look right!

 red & green cards · Genki «Left And Right» (06) · Speaky
31–41′
Culture · «The enormous carrot» + pull together
carpet

Culture story. Play «The enormous carrot» (CD T28) with the story cards — a woman grows an ENORMOUS carrot and can't pull it out; more & more friends help. Pause: Pull, pull, PULL! It's enormous! Children join every heave.

▶ Pull-together chant Line up behind a pretend carrot; add a new helper each round (Do you want to help? — Yes, of course!) and heave together — out it pops! Then let's cook it together.

Helping around the world. Speaky: children in different countries help each other too — 2 joyful examples of teamwork.

 story cards · «The enormous carrot» (CD T28) · Speaky

Draw & tell. children draw one way they like to learn, then tell a partner I like to learn with… books! — recast kindly.

Cool-down · I can… + Goodbye 🧘
carpet

How do you feel? Eyes closed — Did you like it today? show a happy or sad face (an honest signal, no copying).

▶ Turn & tell: Tell your partner one way you like to learn about the world. — tell your talk-partner / show me (no writing).

“I can…” badge: I love to learn! I cross the road safely! I help my friends! colour one star.

You are all so curious and kind! Goodbye song. Sing the Goodbye song (CD1 T3) with 🦝 Speaky — wave to every friend.
