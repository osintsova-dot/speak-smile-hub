Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to name 12 jobs and identify a job from what a person does: “Who cooks your food in a restaurant? — A chef. / This person cuts your hair. — Is it a hairdresser? — Yes, it is.”
  • Subsidiary. Sing «Who is it?» (CD3 43) & the vocabulary chant (CD3 42); brainstorm the jobs of people they know (Learning to learn); link city places (Unit 7) to the people who work there; early literacy — unscramble & read job words (AB); talk about what they want to be.
  • Personal. Keep teacher-talk low; ICQ before every task; stirrer→settler rhythm — the guessing game is lively, plan the freezes. Push for the full riddle frame (“This person …”) rather than one-word job labels.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name the 12 jobs;
  • match a job to what the person does (“Who cooks…? — A chef.”);
  • play a guessing game: “This person …. — Is it a …? — Yes, it is.”

Structure

Form
Who + present simple action + object? — A + job. Guessing frame: This person + verb-s + object. Is it a …? — Yes, it is / No, it isn't.
Pron. watch
hairdresser /ˈheədresə/ (3 syll.); photographer /fəˈtɒɡrəfə/ (stress 2nd syll., not “photo-grapher”); carpenter /ˈkɑːpəntə/; mechanic /mɪˈkænɪk/ (stress 2nd); vet /vet/.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“A person who cooks your food in a restaurant — chef or waiter?” → chef (waiter brings it).
“A person who helps your pet when it's ill — vet or nurse?” → vet (nurse helps people).

Assumptions: Pupils know Unit 7 city places (hospital, office building, restaurant…) — the hook for “who works there”. Most of the 12 jobs are NEW in English, though several are internationally transparent (doctor→dentist, mechanic, artist). Readers — word cards work alongside pictures.

10–20′
Presentation · 12 jobs
carpet → board

Present with flashcards + chant (act. 2, CD3 42). Each card: What's this job? A chef! A vet! A mechanic! Choral ×3 + one gesture (chef = stir, vet = stroke a pet, hairdresser = snip, driver = steer).

Riddle frame (act. 3 language). Board: Who ___s your ___? — A ___. Model from the song: Who fixes your car when it doesn't work? — A mechanic! Who cuts your hair when it's too long? — A hairdresser! Class choruses the answer as you flash the card.

▶ Take Off–Touch Down: Stand up if this person works OUTSIDE… a driver! …a chef? — flash cards fast, everyone reacts every time.

💪 Support: answer with the gesture + the job word. ⭐ Extension: answer with the full riddle — A chef cooks your food in a restaurant.

Guessing game

▶ Guessing game (act. 4, Talk Partners). A holds a job card & gives a clue: This person cooks food in a restaurant. B guesses: Is it a chef? — Yes, it is! Swap. Point per correct guess.

ICQ: Do you SAY the job or DESCRIBE it? (→ describe it — the partner guesses!)

✔ Assess: stand behind one pair — full clue sentence + “Is it a …?” question?
