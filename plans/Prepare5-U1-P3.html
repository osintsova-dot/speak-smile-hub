Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to describe personality and friendships (12+ target adjectives + reading chunks) and to talk & write about likes/abilities using -ing forms — after verbs, after prepositions and as the subject.
  • Subsidiary. Reading for gist then specific detail (SB “Friendship in the 21st century”); speaking fluency (About you / Talking points); exam link — B1 Prelim Reading (words→meanings, C/I sentences) & Speaking Part 1; parallels to ЕГЭ personal questions.
  • Personal. Keep TTT low; ICQ before every task; teach grammar by guided discovery, not PPP; use delayed error correction; a stirrer after the long reading block.
By the end, students can…
  • use 12+ personality adjectives accurately in context;
  • read a short article for gist, then for specific detail;
  • form -ing after verbs, after prepositions and as subject — and avoid the four exam traps.

Structure
-ing forms, three routes: after verbs (enjoy/love/hate/don't mind/can't stand) · after prepositions (good at / interested in / keen on / afraid of) · as subject (Keeping in touch matters.)
Form
verb + -ing · preposition + -ing · -ing as subject. Spelling: work→working · make→making · sit→sitting · lie→lying.
Pron. watch
ending /ɪŋ/, not /ɪn/; weak at/in/of /ət, ɪn, əv/ before -ing; word stress: reLIable · SENsible · SOciable.
Exam link
B1 Prelim — Reading “match words to meanings” & “correct/incorrect” · Speaking Part 1 (personal questions). ЕГЭ — personal questions, opinion language.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“I enjoy to read.” — correct? → no → “I enjoy reading.” (verb + -ing)
“She's good at swim.” — correct? → no → “good at swimming.” (preposition + -ing)
sensible = has good judgement · sensitive = notices/feels emotions. Same word? → no.
Is reliable positive? → yes → someone you can trust / who keeps promises.

Assumptions: from Prepare 4 & Unit 1 students know present simple, like/enjoy + noun, basic determiners (some/any) and can read a short B1 article. A few personality adjectives may be known. Mixed level expected → sentence frames for weaker students, extension line for stronger. Teams: 3–4; pairs re-formed each stage.

Reading — gist → detail
desks

Predict (Ex 1). Make a quick list: how are online friends different from real friends? 60 sec in pairs.

Gist read (Ex 2) — timed. Read “Friendship in the 21st century” fast — just check: were any of your ideas mentioned? You have two minutes. ICQ: Reading every word? (→ no). Feedback: thumbs / one idea each.

Detail (Ex 3). Read again; answer the 5 questions (online support at night · memory · hugs · face-to-face · which is best for you & why). Check with ▶ Numbered Heads: teams agree, a random number reports.

Vocabulary from text (Ex 4). Match the 5 highlighted words (finding out · pal · unfriend · hugging · gestures) to meanings.

📓 Interactive notebook — reading words & chunks (readSB). Glue in the A5 sheet; sort the chunks into online vs face-to-face friendship (give honest advice · have the same interests as · provide support · build confidence · stay in touch with · disagree with sb); cover definitions and rebuild from the examples.

48–70′
Grammar — -ing forms (guided discovery)
desks

Notice (I do → we do). Put three sentences from today's material on the board: I love laughing. · Are you good at staying in touch? · Keeping in touch matters. Ask: What form is the verb after “love”, after “at”, and at the start of the sentence? Elicit the three routes.

Clarify. Build the three routes: after verbs (enjoy/hate/love/don't mind/can't stand) · after prepositions (good at / interested in / keen on / afraid of) · as subject. Spelling workshop: work→working · make→making · sit→sitting · lie→lying.

Hinge check (before practice). Fingers 1/2/3: Which is correct? 1) I enjoy to swim. 2) I enjoy swimming. 3) I am enjoy swim. (→ 2). If >20% wrong, re-model before moving on.

📓 Interactive notebook — -ing forms card (gram). Glue in; colour the three routes in three colours; fix the four exam traps aloud (enjoy to read ✘ · good at listen ✘ · Play football is fun ✘ · interested in learn ✘).

Controlled practice. ▶ RallyCoach in pairs on WB-style items (A does & explains, B coaches, then swap). Then ▶ Showdown (teams): show a trap sentence — everyone writes the fix on a mini-whiteboard, reveal together on Showdown! — every student answers every item (PIES).
