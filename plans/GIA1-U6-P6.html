Спецификация теста Unit 6 (ЧЕРНОВИК до официального пака Macmillan A1+)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: rooms & furniture по определению/картинке (You cook here → kitchen; You wash here → bathroom; It's above you → ceiling). B) 4 items: prepositions по картинке-плану (between · in front of · above · next to). C) 4 items: clothes & accessories + глагол (You ___ an umbrella when it's raining → carry; You ___ trainers to do sport → wear).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 8 gaps: present continuous aff/neg с -ing spelling (sit → sitting; write → writing; not wear → aren't wearing). «Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
