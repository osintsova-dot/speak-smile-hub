Step 1: going over the model
STEP 1 · THINK
pairs

WHAT WE DO: Timed Pair Share: ex.2–3: what makes the photos interesting + going over the Model project against 7 questions — what's answered, what's missing

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Ex.2 90 sec in pairs: Look at the four photos. Are they interesting? Why? (unusual ones: penguins in the living room, people upside down).
  2. Ex.3: we read the description of photo 3 in the Model project and check it against 7 questions. Key: it answers ALL the questions (TB p.94).
  3. Seven questions on the board (stay up until the end of the project): What can you see in the photo? · Where are the people? · What are they doing? · What are they wearing? · Who is taking the photo? · Why is he/she taking the photo? · Why do the writers like the photo?.
  4. Extra (TB p.94): pairs answer all 7 questions about photo 3 orally — 60 sec, this trains the form.
  5. Format — Kagan Timed Pair Share: A talks for their time on the timer, B is the active listener (counts pauses on their fingers, catches the best phrase), then Switch roles! The listener's response starts with My favourite bit was…
📺 On screen: SB pp.82–83 · board: the project's 7 questions
👦 Students do: they go over the model and try on the 7 questions. Expected: “we wouldn't have written that” — the model works as the bar. Typical error: they skip the questions “who took it and why” — those are exactly what makes a guide a guide.

Step 3: creating and recording the audio guide
STEP 3 · CREATE + Super skill
pairs

WHAT WE DO: Super skills Communication: rules for clear speech + Key phrases for feedback; rehearsal and recording the audio guide

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Super skills on the board: Super skills · COMMUNICATION (SB p.82): speak slowly and pronounce words clearly · speak loudly · make pauses so people have time to look at the photo.
  2. Key phrases for feedback (SB p.82) — cards handed to every pair: That's very fast. · You're very quiet. · Try again. · Try to speak more loudly. · That's better. · I can't understand what you're saying..
  3. Rehearsal in pairs 4 min: one speaks, the other HOLDS UP a card (“That's very fast” / “You're very quiet”); swap.
  4. Recording the audio guide on a phone 3 min (How to… p.120) — or a live run-through if there's no tech.
  5. Requirement: an intro + 4 descriptions, each ≥3 sentences, present continuous compulsory.
📺 On screen: Key phrases cards (one set per pair) · phones/recorder · How to… p.120
👦 Students do: they rehearse with feedback cards and write the audio guide. Expected: the cards work better than my comments. Typical error: they read off the sheet — the rule “6 keywords on the card, not text”.

Step 4: exhibition + peer assessment
STEP 4 · PRESENT + Peer review
gallery

WHAT WE DO: exhibition: pairs show their photos and play/voice the guide; peer review 3 questions; Final reflection with emojis

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Gallery format: pairs spread round the perimeter with their 4 photos; half the class walks around and listens, then swap — EVERYONE performs, not just 3 chosen ones.
  2. Peer review (SB p.83 ex.10), 3 ticks: 1 Do they describe the people, the place and what people are doing? 2 Is the photo interesting or unusual — why? 3 Is the guide CLEAR?
  3. Judging by axes (TB p.95): Visuals (4 photos, varied and “telling”) · Content (description + who took it and why + why you like it) · Language (present continuous for actions, present simple for background, prepositions, clothes, colours) — TB p.95.
  4. Final reflection (SB p.83): three emojis for task / super skill / language; whoever has a yellow or a red — one sentence, “what I'll do differently”.
  5. Points to the leaderboard; gold run = +5.
📺 On screen: walls/desks as a gallery · peer review cards · leaderboard
👦 Students do: everyone performs and everyone listens, in two waves; they fill in 3 ticks and emojis. Expected: pride in their own “museum”. Typical error: a description with no actions — peer review question 1 catches it.
🏆 The bar for the product (the judges' rubric, each pair): Fluency — pauses no longer than 3 sec (1–3) · Accuracy — present continuous in full, be is NOT dropped, -ing by the rule (1–3) · Richness — prepositions of place, clothes, colours, connectors (1–3). 9/9 = “gold run”, +5 to the leaderboard.		peer review cards

🔥 Revenge quiz · the team revenge
crews

WHAT WE DO: Showdown: going over errors as a team quiz by TYPE (no names): -ing spelling · missing be · question order · prepositions · so

👩‍🏫 Teacher does — step by step:
  1. Rule: First — count what you GOT RIGHT. Keys for parts 1–2 on the projector, self-check in pencil.
  2. I run it as Kagan Showdown: EVERYONE writes at the same time, boards face down, on the command No peeking… Boards up! they lift together — I see everyone's answer, nobody can hide.
  3. ▶ Revenge quiz: 5 rounds by error type, taken from THIS test (no names), on mini-boards, crews: 1 -ing spelling · 2 lost be · 3 word order in the question + short answer · 4 preposition (in/on · near/next to · in front of) · 5 so or because.
  4. Plus a round from the questions they wrote themselves in pair 2 (Fast finishers) — their authors judge.
  5. Revenge points go into the unit leaderboard: The test is a score. The revenge is the LEARNING.
📺 On screen: keys on the projector · mini-boards · slides for 5 rounds (Backup: everything on the slide goes onto the board before the lesson (or onto printed cards, one per crew).)
👦 Students do: they count their successes first, then fight back as a team — an error becomes a round, not a verdict. Expected: the weakest rounds will match the radar targets from pair 5.

WB Unit 6 (pp.44–51) · notebook · points to the leaderboard next pair
📋 Preparation reference — read BEFORE the lesson, not in class
  • Main aim. By the end, learners can present a video/audio guide for four exhibition photos (describing people, place, actions and clothes) and complete the Unit 6 test in familiar formats.
  • Subsidiary. Super skill Communication (speak slowly · clearly · loudly · pauses) is drilled as a measurable skill; WDYT? “What is art?” is closed with a collective answer; the test mirrors Quick review p.81; the review is a team revenge quiz.
  • Personal. The pair is packed: the project 42 minutes, the test 33, the review 15. Keep the timers strict and don't let the project “sprawl”. The test — no drama: model-first, the format is familiar, the revenge right after.
By the end, students can…
  • describe a photo: people, place, actions, clothes
  • speak clearly and loudly with pauses (Super skill)
  • give and take peer feedback on a guide
  • show unit grammar and vocabulary in a written test

Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“Do we watch a video guide or read it?” → We watch/listen — it's spoken.
“In the photo they stand or they're standing?” → they're standing (right now, in the shot).
“Why do we pause?” → so people have time to LOOK at the photo.

Unit 6 test — specification (DRAFT until the official Macmillan A1+ pack)
PartContents & marking
Part 1 · Vocabulary (14 pts)A) 6 items: rooms & furniture by definition/picture (You cook here → kitchen; You wash here → bathroom; It's above you → ceiling). B) 4 items: prepositions from a floor-plan picture (between · in front of · above · next to). C) 4 items: clothes & accessories + verb (You ___ an umbrella when it's raining → carry; You ___ trainers to do sport → wear).
Part 2 · Grammar (18 pts)A) 8 gaps: present continuous aff/neg with -ing spelling (sit → sitting; write → writing; not wear → aren't wearing). B) 6 items: order the words → Yes/No- and Wh- question + true short answer (you / are / what / wearing ?). C) 4 items: contrast with present simple (Every day I go… / Right now I ___ going…) — a trap on time expressions.
Part 3 · Listening (8 pts)Describe a photo/room (a script in the style of SB p.77 audio 43; the teacher reads it twice if there’s no track): 4 true/false + fill in 4 gaps — mirrors SB p.77 ex.6–7 (captions).
Part 4 · Writing (10 pts)A text message dialogue (6+ turns) with an invitation and a refusal. Marked for: present continuous forms (4) · so in the right position (3) · text-message format Hi… / See you! + task complete (3) — mirrors SB p.81 Skills boost.

Total 50 pts · pass focus: not the number but the personal error log. Built strictly from Quick review SB p.81 — every format was met in class during the unit. The two lines: 🚀 higher (+ Wh-questions in writing, more -ing spelling traps) · 🐣 standard.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
English-first — ideas in Russian are welcome, POINTS only for the English version — recast and repeat.
