Pre-primary / early years (~5–6 y.o., pre-readers) · 60 min · Genki recipe · Disco warm-up → phonics → curiosity input → Genki games → song → craft · TPR + puppet · Phonics Hero 2.0 (reading: recognise → blend → speed-read) · carpet + tables
Week 26.10–01.11 · Lesson 1✨ NEW · slide · pond · swing · seesaw · roundabout · bench~8 pupils · circle + tablesPH 17 (ou · oi · p.46–48) · Genki: Colours(52)·Numbers(08)
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, children will be better able to name six things in the park (slide · pond · swing · seesaw · roundabout · bench) and match each to a colour on the Big Wheel — blue–slide · red–bench · yellow–swing · green–roundabout · pink–seesaw · orange–pond.
  • Subsidiary. Listen, point & repeat the park words (CD1 T38); play the coursebook Memory game (colour → park item, CD1 T37); sing Genki «Colours» (52) & count with «Numbers» (08); learn a park safety talking-point; Phonics Hero — sounds ou · oi, blend + speed-read p.46–48; circle the park items on PB p.52.
  • Personal. Tiny teacher-talk, big TPR — let Speaky do the talking. Genki recipe: disco first, phonics early, input through curiosity, energy up in the games then down for the table. Accept a point or a single word this early — smiles first, sentences come in L2.
By the end, pupils can…
  • name six park things — slide · pond · swing · seesaw · roundabout · bench;
  • match a park thing to its colour on the Big Wheel;
  • say one park safety idea — “Hold on!” / “Sit down!”

Phonics
Phonics Hero 2.0 (our reading workbook) — sounds ou · oi with the Jolly gesture, blend (ou → out, oi → coin) with a meaning picture, then speed-read p.46–48. Park link: r-ou-nd → round…about! Children already know the 26 sounds — this is learning to READ (GMF1-style).
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“The slide — do I go UP or DOWN it? Hold on with one hand or two?” → down · two hands (safety!).
“Blue on the wheel — which park thing?” → the slide!

Assumptions: Children know colours & the Big Wheel routine from Units 1–2; the six park items are new. They can read early CVC words (Phonics Hero through Unit 2). Pre-readers of text — everything lands through pictures, the wheel, song, movement & the puppet.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Wriggling / can't sit long on the carpetKeep every stage 5–8 min; use Freeze! and the volume-arm; a movement stage always follows a sitting one.
seesaw / roundabout hard to sayClap the beats: see–saw (2), round–a–bout (3); say it big, then whisper.
Colour & park item get muddledAlways pair them on the wheel — point to the pod: blue… slide! red… bench!
Answers in RussianAccept warmly, recast in English once with the puppet — don't correct, just model.
Memory game — everyone shouts at onceEyes closed on the count; open & only the child you point to answers, then whole-class echo.

“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset.

0–4′
Genki Disco Warm Up ⚡
door → carpet

Greet each child at the door with 🦝 Speaky: Hello! How are you? — quick high-five, straight onto the carpet.

Dance it out. ▶ Genki Disco Warm Up (01) — hands up/down, jump, clap, turn around, wiggle. 🦝 Speaky bring the energy UP, then trick them: Speaky says… FREEZE! while they're already moving, then And… sit down!

Today (kid-friendly). Show today's picture: Hello! Let's go to the PARK! Big slide… wheee! Swing… whoosh! Mime a slide and a swing, then dance.

4–9′
Warm review · last time
carpet

Colour wheel. spin the Big Wheel — What colour? — Blue! Yellow! Red! chorus the colour, from Units 1–2.

▶ Count the wheel. count the pods 1→6 with fingers and one, two, three, four, five, six! a quick number warm-up before the park words.

 Big Wheel mat · colour cards · Speaky
9–17′
Phonics Hero 🔤 ⚡
carpet, moving

① Recognise / review. Phonics Hero lesson 17 — sounds ou · oi. 🦝 Speaky shows each with its Jolly action (gesture technique); children do the action and NAME the sound — they already know all 26. We don't drill bare sounds: teacher voices, children gesture.

② Blend / build. Slide the sounds into a word: ou → out, oi → coin, oi → oil — big arm-sweep + a meaning picture. Park link: r-ou-nd → round…about! ▶ Blend & jump — Speaky says a word slowly; children blend it with the actions and jump on the whole word.

③ Speed reading. open Phonics Hero 2.0 (our workbook) p.46–48 — finger under each word, read the row FAST as a group (whole words, playful, celebrate speed — real reading from zero, GMF1-style).

Tricky words: read like · have together as whole words (we don't sound tricky words out).

 Phonics Hero 2.0 workbook p.46–48 · sound-action cards · Speaky
17–25′
Curiosity input · park things
carpet → Big Wheel mat

Guess what's in the park! Slowly slide a park flashcard out of an envelope — What's this? A park thing… guess! Children guess; reveal + BIG action (slide down, swing arms, spin for roundabout, sit for bench). Do all six (slide · pond · swing · seesaw · roundabout · bench), whole class echoes.

Match to the wheel. Put each item on its colour pod — point & chorus: Blue… slide! Red… bench! Yellow… swing! Green… roundabout! Pink… seesaw! Orange… pond!

📱 Coursebook app (Macmillan digital): put the Big Wheel with the park items on their colour pods on screen (blue–slide · red–bench · yellow–swing · green–roundabout · pink–seesaw · orange–pond) and play the park story video & songs from the coursebook (Navio) app. Our Genki songs & games add to it — we don't reproduce the coursebook media.

💪 Support: point & copy the puppet's action. ⭐ Extension: say the item + its colour: blue slide!

25–31′
Safety talk + Stand up if…
carpet

Play safe! Quick park-safety chat with the puppet: On the slide — up or DOWN? Hold on — one hand or TWO? On the swing — sit down or stand up? Show thumbs-up for safe, thumbs-down for not-safe.

▶ Stand up if… Stand up if you like the SWING! …the slide! children who like it stand, do its action, then sit — everyone reacts every round.

 park flashcards · Speaky
31–37′
Reading corner 📖
carpet

Read-aloud. Read our own Speak & Smile story-book «Left and Right!» aloud — Speaky follows a treasure map along a park path — trees, grass, a winding path. A calm, curious read that drops today's park scenery into a little adventure.

Hold up each spread — children look at the pictures, do the gestures and echo the key line Look! A tree! A path! Left… and right!. Pre-readers: they listen, watch & repeat, they don't read the words.

Point to the park things on each page — children name what they can (tree · path · grass) and point left/right with Speaky; a gentle taster of the park scene before L2's new words.

 «Left and Right!» (Speak & Smile Genki story-book · lr.pdf) · Speaky
37–45′
Game · Island Hopping (23) → Dinosaur Danger! (12) ⚡
carpet, standing

▶ Island Hopping Genki Island Hopping: each child gets a little pile of park mini-cards (their “island”). 🦝 Speaky calls Show me… the pond! Show me… the swing! — children who have it hold it UP and say it; everyone else copies. All cards up every round, nobody waits.

▶ Dinosaur Danger! then Dinosaur Danger!: children on the “safe” side. 🦝 Speaky holds up a park card; children NAME it The roundabout! — when Speaky roars Dinosaur! everyone runs to the other safe side. Freeze on landing.

✔ Assess: who names all six park items? who needs the picture? Note it — no pressure, just for next time.
