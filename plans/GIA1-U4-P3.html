Structure
He watches TV after school. · She doesn't live in a city. · My friends and I don't relax at the weekend. · On Thursdays, Flora goes swimming.
Form
he/she/it + -s; -es после -s/-o/-ch/-sh/-x; согл.+y → -ies; have→has; отрицание don't/doesn't + инфинитив БЕЗ -s.
Pron. watch
-es = /ɪz/: finishes · relaxes · watches (SB p.116 ex.1) · do you = /dʒʊ/ позже в P4.
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
“He watchs — OK?” → No: watch → watchES (-ch!).
“She doesn't likES?” → No — после doesn't инфинитив без -s.
“Studies или studys?” → согласная + y → -ies.

Assumptions: Устно PS беглый (GMF2–4), письменный спеллинг 3-го лица — реальная дыра; free-time лексика частично знакома, но 16-сет как система — новый. Gate is diagnosis, not humiliation: the ceiling must be REAL (time-in-words / -s spelling / question word order), results are private tallies, the reveal is “so THIS is our growth zone”.

Classroom management — signals
«Eyes up in 3-2-1» — count down calmly → all eyes on you at «1». No shouting over noise, ever.
Visible timer — timer on screen = we work; timer stops = pens/hands down. The timer is the boss, not you.
Volume dial — arm up/down = louder/quieter; «radio whisper» for rehearsals.
«Boards up!» — mini-whiteboard rounds: everyone commits, all boards up together — nobody hides, nobody shouts first.
One clap — two claps — you clap once, class answers with two and freezes — instant reset mid-game.
