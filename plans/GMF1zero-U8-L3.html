Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. By the end, learners will be better able to follow and understand the story “Fun at the camp” (Pupil's Book pp. 84–85, CD3 47) and act it out using key story language: “What's the weather like? — It's hot / raining.”, “Let's swim / sail a boat!”, “You can't swim today. It's dangerous.”, “We can sail the magic bike!”, “Oh no! What a pity!”
  • Subsidiary. Recycle real prior learning — clothes + “I'm wearing…” (L1–L2), can/can't (Unit 5), Where's my…? — woven into the camp adventure; phonics — Phonics Hero ур.29: /ау/ ou·ow + /ой/ oi·oy (cloud, town · coin, boy — the “ау!/ой!” calls; тетрадь стр.79–82); values: staying safe & helping each other at camp.
  • Personal. Keep story listening active (gesture responses, prediction); manage drama noise with Freeze; every child acts in a trio at the same time — no “2 on stage, 10 watching” until the final 1-min showcase.
By the end, pupils can…
  • show they understand the story (order the frames, answer with gestures);
  • say the key lines in role: “What's the weather like? — It's raining!”, “Let's swim!”, “You can't swim today!”;
  • read & write ou/ow/oi/oy words (cloud, town, coin, boy);
  • say “Be careful! Let's help!” (values).

Structure
What's the weather like? — It's hot / cold / raining. · Let's swim / sail a boat. · You can / can't swim today. It's dangerous / raining. · She's riding a horse. He's sailing a boat. · Where's my swimsuit? · Oh no! What a pity! · We can sail the magic bike!
Form
can / can't + verb (ability & permission). Weather: It's + adjective / -ing (raining). Receptive present continuous She's riding… (full focus L4).
Pron. watch
“weather” = /ˈweðə/ (soft th); can't /kɑːnt/ vs can /kən/; big fanning (hot) / shivering (cold) intonation.
Phonics
Phonics Hero ур.29: /ау/ ou·ow + /ой/ oi·oy — ou/ow «ау!» (cloud, town) · oi/oy «ой!» (coin, boy) — read & sort · dictation cloud, town, coin, boy (тетрадь стр.79–82). A rain CLOUD over the camp — ou!
Concept checks (CCQs) · Assumptions
Frame with rain: “What's the weather like? — It's raining!” “Can they swim?” → “No! You can't swim. It's dangerous!”
Sunny frame: “Can they swim now?” → “Yes! Let's swim!”
Last frame: “How do they have fun?” → “They sail the magic bike! Hooray!”

Assumptions: clothes + “I'm wearing…” from L1–L2; can/can't from Unit 5; children know Toby & the magic bike. Emergent readers → they FOLLOW the pictures while listening; the teacher points. ou/ow/oi/oy met in Часть 2; ур.29 revisits. She's riding… is felt in context, studied properly in L4. Группы: команда — максимум 4; при другой численности делим на команды по 3–4 (12=3×4 · 10=3+3+4 · 9=3×3 · 8=2×4 · 7=3+4).

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
New words block the story (lake, forest, flag, dangerous)Pre-teach with picture + gesture before listening (water, trees, waving flag, cross-arms “no!”).
Children call answers over the audio“Listening hands” rule — hands cupped behind ears while the CD plays; talk only when it stops.
can / can't sound the sameExaggerate can't with a head-shake & cross arms; can with a nod; drill the pair.
Drama turns to chaos (running “to the lake”)Swim / ride / sail ON THE SPOT; roles fixed by badges; “Freeze!” resets.
Frame cards ordered by one child in the pairRule “one card each turn, take turns” (RallyRobin with cards).
Classroom management — signals
“Class? — Yes!” — you: “Class!” → pupils stop & echo “Yes!”
“WiFi” 🖐 — say “WiFi” + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
“Give me five” — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — “Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.”
“Freeze!” — statue, mid-movement reset (essential in drama).

Phonics Hero ур.29 — /ау/ ou·ow + /ой/ oi·oy
board → desks

Speed reading. Vowel-name chant + the digraph strip: There and back — beat your time! Recall the calls: hands round the mouth ou! (ау!) · surprised sailor oi! (ой!) — all ×3. Story tie-in: A rain CLOUD over the camp — ou!

Read & sort — тетрадь стр.79–80 (all point first). «ау» group (cloud · town · brown · out) vs «ой» group (coin · boy · oil · toy) — read each word together → every child calls «ау» or «ой» → sort into two columns. RallyCoach: partner checks.

Dictation — стр.81–82 (short, story day). 4 words once each: cloud · town · coin · boy → segment on fingers → write → Show me! → reveal, self-check.

Sight words. Flash the strip (want · because · other · were): chorus, air-write.

Colour the star «Я молодец!». (No long written work — full story lesson; phonics is brisk revision.)

24–34′
📺 Story — listen & follow
carpet, books/screen

Before you read (PB pp. 84–85). Big story picture on screen / books open. Pre-teach with gesture: lake (water) · forest (trees) · flag (waving) · dangerous (cross arms “no!”).

🎮 Interactive (projector): Find it! — “What's at the camp?” Screen: the campsite scene. How: Speaky asks, a child taps an object; it lights up. Pupils: whole class points in their book / calls it — The tent! The lake! The flag! Toby! Teacher: 🦝 What can you see? Point! — 4–5 things, quick. Offline reserve: point in the open Pupil's Book (pp. 84–85). · 🎮 find-it-u8

Predict (thinking) — before we listen. It's raining! Can the children swim? What can they do at the camp? Sail a boat? Ride a horse? Or… the MAGIC BIKE? Thumbs vote. Let's listen and check your guess!

Listen 1 — global (CD3 47). “Listening hands” behind ears. Teacher points frame by frame; children follow. Gist check: Is the weather good at the start? Show me! (thumbs) Was your guess right — what did they do?

Detail check — True or False? (thumbs up / down, all react). It's sunny at the start. (👍) · The children can swim in the rain. (👎 — can't! dangerous!) · There's a flag at the lake. (👍) · They sail the magic bike. (👍). Re-point the frame to fix any wrong thumb.

Listen 2 — active (all respond). Play again; children do the story gestures: sun ☀️, rain 🌧, shiver, swim, ride a horse, sail, cheer Hooray! CCQs between frames (see box above).

▶ Build the story
desks, pairs

▶ Story Builders (picture re-skin for non-readers — all pairs work at once). Each pair gets 6–8 mini frame-cards of the camp story, shuffled.

Order it. Put the story in order — one card each, take turns! (RallyRobin with cards.) Countdown 2 min.

43–51′
Act out — drama
carpet, trios

Roles. Trios: camper 1 (red badge) · camper 2 (green) · Toby the dog + Magic Bike bell (blue). Teacher narrates and models once with a strong trio.

All trios act at the same time. Narrate slowly, frame by frame; every trio plays it in their own spot: It's sunny! Let's swim! · Oh no — it's raining! · You can't swim today. It's dangerous! · What a pity! · Let's ride a horse! · Where's my swimsuit? · We can sail the magic bike! · Hooray! Fun at the camp!

Create (thinking) — make it yours. Before round 2: Your weather changes! What can you do? Each trio picks a weather + an activity: It's windy! We can't sail. Let's ride a horse! (support: point to a weather + activity card; extension: invent the exchange).

Swap roles, run it again (2 rounds). Volunteers: ONE trio shows 30 sec — big applause. Values: The rain is dangerous — what do good friends say? → Be careful! Let's help!

💪 Support: quieter children get Toby + the bell (short line + gesture); a line card in hand. ⭐ Extension: confident children narrate & lead the weather changes. 		role badges ×3 colours · weather + activity cards · bell · PB p. 85 (values)
