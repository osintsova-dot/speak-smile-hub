What this document is (RU)

🎤 Это ПРОТОКОЛ проведения итогового устного экзамена (занятие 71, после Final Test). Формат-опора — Cambridge YLE Starters Speaking: три коротких знакомых детям части — ❶ приветствие-диалог · ❷ описание картинки (называем предметы) · ❸ вопрос-ответ. Экзамен один-на-один с учителем, ~3–4 минуты на ученика. Тон — тёплый разговор со 🦝 Спики, а НЕ проверка.

Главная задача организации — чтобы, пока ты слушаешь одного ребёнка, остальные не сидели без дела: работает «тихое меню» + «уголок репетиции в парах». Ниже: тайминг на ученика, рассадка, реплики экзаменатора, критерии оценивания и как поддержать волнующихся. Подготовь заранее: набор картинок для описания (сцена с игрушками/днём), карточки-вопросы года, бланк оценивания на каждого ребёнка.

Room setup — solving «1 speaks, 11 wait»
🎤 Exam corner (with you)
  • Two chairs at a small table, side-on (not interrogation-style), your back to the room.
  • Picture prompt + question cards face-down; assessment sheet + pen; 🦝 Speaky sits on the table as a friendly co-host.
  • One child at a time, ~3–4′. A visible «now / next» name order so children know when they're up.
  • Warm body language: smile, nod, lean in. This is a chat with Speaky, not a trial.
🧠 Waiting = busy (the other children)
  • Quiet menu (desks): end-of-year colouring, app games, «draw & label your favourite unit» sheet — independent, silent.
  • Rehearsal buddies (carpet corner): pairs quietly rehearse the exam questions on prompt cards — RallyRobin, so everyone speaks while waiting.
  • A reliable child or assistant manages the «now/next» order & keeps volume low.
  • Rule up front: Whisper voices — I'm listening to your friends.
The exam — three parts (~3–4′ per pupil)
Part · ~timeWhat happens · examiner script
❶ Warm-up hello
(interview) · ~1′		Settle the child, then easy personal Qs from across the year: Hello! What's your name? How are you? How old are you? · What's your favourite colour? What's your favourite toy? Can you jump? Aim: relaxed, happy answers, no freezing.
❷ Look & tell
(picture) · ~1′		Show one busy scene (a bedroom / a park / a toy shop). Look! What can you see? Prompt if needed: How many balls? What colour is the bed? Is it a dog? Where's the cat? Aim: name things + a known structure (It's a…, There's a…, How many…, colours, prepositions).
❸ Question & answer
· ~1–1.5′		Pull 3–4 question cards mixing units: Do you like apples? What do you do every day? When do you have breakfast? Have you got a pet? Aim: understand the question + answer in a short sentence.
Assessment criteria (per pupil — 1 to 4)
CriterionWhat you listen for
Willingness & interactionAnswers without long freezing; smiles, tries, responds to your prompts. (Not speed — willingness & flow.)
VocabularyRange of unit words used correctly (toys · colours · numbers · animals · food · routines…).
Grammar/chunksTarget chunks attempted: It's a…, There's a…, I like…, I can…, I (…) every day.
PronunciationIntelligible; key sounds from the year (th, sh, ch, ou/oi, Magic E); clear enough to understand.
Simple 4-point scale
  • 4 — Confident: answers all 3 parts, short sentences, minor errors that don't block meaning.
  • 3 — Competent: answers most, some support needed, meaning clear.
  • 2 — Developing: single words, needs prompts, some meaning through.
  • 1 — Emerging: mostly words/gestures, heavy support — note for next year.
📝 Score each criterion 1–4 on the child's sheet + one warm note. Total is for YOUR records & parent feedback, not announced competitively.
Aims & objectives
  • Main aim. Each pupil demonstrates spoken English from Units 1–9 across three Starters-style parts (hello · look & tell · Q&A) in a warm one-to-one, assessed on willingness, vocabulary, chunks & pronunciation.
  • Subsidiary. Waiting pupils keep practising speaking in pairs (RallyRobin) — no idle time; every child speaks a lot today, examined or rehearsing.
  • Personal. Make it feel like a friendly chat with Speaky, not a test. Support nerves actively. Assess fairly & kindly; end every child on a positive note.
By the end, pupils can…
  • answer easy personal questions across the year's topics;
  • name what's in a picture with unit vocabulary & a chunk;
  • understand & answer a mixed question in a short exchange.

Anticipated problems & solutions
Likely problemWhat you do
Child freezes / very nervousStart with the easiest Q (What's your name?), smile, wait, give a starter (It's a…); Speaky «asks» in a funny voice; a nervous child may go last after hearing others.
One-word answers onlyPrompt to extend: Tell me more. What colour? How many? Score what they DO produce; don't over-prompt into their score.
Waiting children get loud / boredQuiet menu + rehearsal-buddy corner set up before you start; whisper rule; helper manages the queue.
Doesn't understand the questionRephrase once, simpler; gesture; point at the picture; if still stuck, move on kindly — note it, don't dwell.
Fast finishers of the examReturn to the quiet menu; can become a rehearsal buddy for someone still waiting.
Running over timeKeep a soft timer; 3 parts × ~1′; if a child is flowing, that's fine — trim prompts elsewhere.
Classroom management — signals
«Class? — Yes!» — you: «Class!» → pupils stop & echo «Yes!»
«WiFi» 🖐 — say «WiFi» + raise a spread hand → pupils silently mirror & tune in.
Volume control — raise/lower your arm = louder/quieter → settler.
«Give me five» — eyes · ears · mouth · hands · feet.
Countdown — «Finishing in 5…4…3…2…1… pencils down.»
«Freeze!» — statue, mid-movement reset.

Prime the language (all together). Quick chant of a few year-favourite question–answers so everyone hears the models: What's your favourite toy? — My favourite toy is a… What's your favourite colour? — It's…

🦸 Phonics Hero — Карта прогресса, финиш! (тетрадь стр.92). Look how far you came — from no reading to a whole book! Colour the last square on your progress map — you FINISHED! (Quiet, joyful.)

Explain the setup. I'll call you one by one for our chat with Speaky. While you wait: quiet menu at your desk, OR practise the questions with a partner in the corner — whisper voices! ICQ: Loud or whisper while I listen? (→ whisper). Show the «now/next» name order.

 question prompt cards · quiet menu sheets · «now/next» board · Phonics Hero стр.92 + crayons
10–52′
🎤 One-to-one Speaking Exam
exam corner + desks

Call pupils one by one (~3–4′ each, ~12 children in ~42′). For each child run the three parts:

❶ Hello: Hello! How are you? What's your name? How old are you? What's your favourite colour?

❷ Look & tell: Look at this picture. What can you see? How many…? What colour is the…? Where's the cat?

❸ Q&A cards: Do you like apples? Can you swim? What do you do every day? When do you have breakfast?

Close each child warmly: Thank you — that was GREAT! Well done! ✔ Score willingness / vocab / chunks / pronunciation 1–4 on the sheet + one note, right after each child (while fresh). Send them back to the quiet menu.

💪 Support: a word starter + picture prompt for nervous / weaker pupils; Speaky asks in a funny voice. ⭐ Extension: an extra «tell me more / what colour?» follow-up for confident speakers.

After the exam — records & feedback

Collect assessment sheets; transfer scores + notes to the class list. Combine with Final-Test data for a full end-of-year picture. In parent feedback: lead with a spoken strength («confidently named all the colours») + one next step («working on full sentences»). Weak spots → feed the Reserve revision (lesson 72).
